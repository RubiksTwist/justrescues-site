Meet Eden! This sweet, scruffy little girl is the definition of irresistible! At just 4 months old and 8 pounds, Eden may be small, but her personality is absolutely HUGE! With her big, beautiful eyes, adorable little nubby teeth, and a smile that will melt your heart, Eden has a way of getting whatever she wants—and honestly, we don't blame anyone for giving in! She is spunky, happy, and completely in love with life. Eden loves going everywhere with her people, playing with her puppy friends, and soaking up every bit of attention she can get. We have no doubt this little girl will be a total showstopper wherever she goes, with everyone stopping to say hello. She’s looking for someone who can coach her through her crazy puppy antics, teach her manners and someone who truly wants those puppy snuggles. If you're looking for a tiny adventure buddy with a giant personality, a contagious smile, and enough cuteness to stop traffic, Eden may just be your girl!

Eden is spayed, microchipped, and up to date on her vaccinations. For more information about her, please fill out an application at www.ALRcares.com and email Rachel@alrcares.com. We schedule meet and greets for approved adopters only, an application is the first step in our approval process. Thank you!
