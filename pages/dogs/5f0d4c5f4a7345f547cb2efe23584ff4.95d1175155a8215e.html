Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

At 6 years old and a short and stocky 60 lbs, your sixth sense should be telling you to stop scrolling and keep on reading.

Pulaski is a social butterfly who will easily swoon you with her bright,, beady teddy bear eyes, huge koolaid smile and perfectly shaped ears.

The good thing about you quickly falling head over heels in love with Pulaski is that the feeling will absolutely be mutual. At first meet, Pulaski is a wiggly, sweet, social and affectionate girl leaning her full body into you for pets, snuggles and she will never say no to a treat!

And if the fun and sweet first impression wasn't enough, Pulaski is proud to show off all the skills she's learned in her 6 years of life. Pulaski has fully mastered her 'sit, down, place and wait' commands while also being fully housebroken and solidly crate trained.

Pulaski is an adapatable queen who can stay home and hang while you knock out an 8 hour work shift but she also won't refuse a car ride, a hike or a fun game of tug.

Pulaski is such a wonderful companion who is happy to keep herself entertained at home when necessary whilst also obliging anytime she is summoned for a snuggle session.

Pulaski is studious and receptive on leash especially in familiar environments. In new environments she can initially become overwhelmed yet before you know it Pulaski's sweet and adventurous personality emerges never to be dimmed again.

Pulaski's lives her life under the mantra, "trust takes time, but love doesn't have to" so if you are also someone the all or nothing type with your heart - you should absolutley have a heart to heart meeting with Pulaski. We have a sneaky suspicion your souls may just tie on sight.
