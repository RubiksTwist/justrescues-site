Meet Andy!

This adorable three-month-old pups is ready to find a family to call his own. Andy was found in a ditch off a dirt road in Georgia with his four littermates where they were keeping each other warm and safe. Now safe and cared for, he is ready to put his difficult beginning behind him and start his next adventure.

Just like in Toy Story, and like any young puppy, Andy has plenty of growing, learning, playing, and exploring ahead of him. He is looking for a family who will show him the ropes, cheer him on as he discovers the world, and give him all the love a puppy could ask for.

Every great story needs a best friend. Come meet Andy and see if he is meant to be yours!
