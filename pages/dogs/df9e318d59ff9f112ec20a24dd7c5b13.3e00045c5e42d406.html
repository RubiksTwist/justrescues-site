Mikrocosmos is the sweetest little pup looking for a special home. She was found stray in Staten Island with a spinal injury, likely hit by a car. The trauma caused her to become partially incontinent, and though she might recover bladder and bowel control as her body heals — only time will tell.

Despite her recent misfortunes, Cosmos remains a happy dog brimming with love. She’s super affectionate with people and gets along well with other dogs. She loves to be held and snuggled, walks well on leash, and settles easily in the crate. Because of her incontinence, she needs an adopter who is mindful of her potty schedule and is willing to keep her fresh and clean.

Mikrocosmos is fostered in Brooklyn, NY, and is available for local or out-of-state adoption as long as the adopter can travel to meet her. To get the adoption process started, please send us an application at https://bit.ly/rosr-adopt-foster.
