Description provided by NYC Second Chance Rescue

This little powdered sugar piglet is SO ready to find her family! Once upon a time, Lindy was abandoned at a grooming salon, but thanks to her foster heroes, she never had to go to the shelter. We cannot believe she is still waiting for adoption; she is such a special sweet little girl!! Lindy is 4 years old, 51 pounds, housetrained, and has become great friends with her foster's resident dogs. We call her a powdered sugar donut because she can curl up into the tiniest ball when she wants to snuggle in somewhere. And, yes, occasionally, she snuggles up next to her foster's youngest, hoping for an extra snack to fall on the ground so she can (VERY HELPFULLY) offer her cleaning services. What a good girl! Lindy is an adorable, affectionate, chunky girl, and she's ready to find her forever home. Apply today to learn more about Lindy.
