Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Hazelnut is a 1-year-old mixed breed who weighs around 54 pounds, and if there's one thing you'll notice about her right away, it's just how gentle she is. She has a soft, easygoing nature and approaches everyone she meets with kindness. She enjoys meeting other dogs and is always hopeful they'll want to play too, though she's quickly learning that not every pup is as eager as she is.

At home, Hazelnut is an absolute cuddle bug. She loves being close to her people and is happiest when she's soaking up affection or curling up beside you after a day of fun. Her sweet personality makes her an easy dog to fall in love with.

She's already house-trained and crate-trained, giving her a great head start as she settles into her forever home. With her affectionate nature, gentle spirit, and playful side, Hazelnut is ready to become a loyal companion for a family looking for a best friend to share everyday life with.
