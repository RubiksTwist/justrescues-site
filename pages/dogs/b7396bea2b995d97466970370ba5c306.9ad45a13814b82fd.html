My foster writes: Sadie Baby is the absolute sweetest soul you'll ever have the pleasure of knowing! She is a total lovebug who craves nothing more than your affection and attention. I've been genuinely amazed by how quickly she masters complicated puzzle games, proving just how bright she really is. Sadie is highly motivated by praise and snacks, and if you have anything peanut butter flavored, I'd say you've officially won her heart.
Because she's such a sensitive and gentle spirit, she can be nervous around loud noises, so she'll need a patient family to help her settle into her new home at her own pace. But she's absolutely ready to be someone's new best friend.

My foster wites:

Sadie Girl aka The Baby is one of the sweetest, most loving dogs we've ever met. From day one of bringing her home she wowed us with her trust and love - she just wants to be near her people and it takes so little to make her happy. If you're settled, she's settled. She'll jump up on the bed or couch next to you, put her little head on your knee, and snuggle in with a big sigh and probably some snoring. She is an extremely polite and friendly girl- trying to say hi to every human and dog we pass on the street- always with an excited but calm body and lots of kisses. Sadie is really still a baby herself - even though she's already had puppies- which maybe explains some of her patience and tolerance getting kisses and bear hugs and baths and paw cleanings. Whoever brings Sadie home forever will get to watch this girl grow into an adult dog and her best self.
Since she is still young, someone with patience, time, and readiness to work on training with her is needed. Someone ready to meet her energy when she's excited and then help her calm her body down after. She is a FAST learner, so eager to please, very smart and treat-responsive. An energetic, patient home with people who are ready to snuggle and laugh (she is so silly) will be perfect for Sadie girl.
Sadie continues to melt our hearts everyday by showing us how much love and gentleness she has in her heart. We are excited for her to find her people and for them to find her 🧡
