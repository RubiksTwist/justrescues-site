Meet Bunzy!

One Bunzy is a 3-year-old long stay pup who came in as a stray and is now waiting patiently for her chance at a forever home. She is a longstay and her adoption fee has been reduced!

Personality

One Bunzy is a sweet, friendly, and affectionate girl who truly enjoys being close to people. Her foster describes her as calm, loving, and very people oriented, often happily resting her head on your lap for attention and cuddles. She is gentle in her interactions and seems to enjoy forming quiet, steady bonds with those she trusts.

She is also smart and eager to please, with a natural willingness to learn. Her foster notes she seems easy to train and responsive to positive attention, especially when she feels safe and included.

Out and About

One Bunzy is an easy leash walker with no pulling and no reactivity toward people or other dogs. She moves calmly through her environment and tends to stay close to her handler. She enjoys steady, relaxed walks and benefits from continued positive experiences outside the home.

Social Behavior

In the shelter, One Bunzy is highly social and approaches handlers readily with a soft body and gentle wagging tail. She accepts both soft and more active handling and remains neutral and relaxed during interaction. She enjoys being near people and shows a calm, steady interest in connection.

In a dog interaction on 09/23/25, One Bunzy showed a soft body and wagging tail but chose not to approach the gate, suggesting a polite and observational style when meeting other dogs.

Home Life

One Bunzy will thrive in a home that can offer love, consistency, and gentle structure. She enjoys being part of daily life, whether that means going for walks or simply relaxing at home beside her person. She will benefit from positive outlets for her energy, such as toys and enrichment activities, even though she is not highly toy motivated right now. Bunzy is a Level 1 dog, meaning she is suitable for the majority of households.

Things to Know

One Bunzy has medical needs that staff will discuss with adopters. She enjoys social interaction, but her history with other animals and children is unknown, so slow introductions will be important.

Fun Fact

One Bunzy is kind of dog who doesn't ask for much, just a little love, a soft place to rest her head, and someone to walk through life with by her side. 🐾

My foster writes: If you are looking for a loyal, people-loving shadow, meet Bunzy! She is an incredibly sweet, even-tempered girl who absolutely adores human company. Bunzy is the ultimate "velcro puppy"her favorite place in the world is right next to you (or literally on you!). Whether she's squeezing herself into your office chair to "help" you work, or adjusting her sleeping position at night to make sure her body is glued right next to your head, Bunzy just wants to be close.
Not only is she a total sweetheart, but she's also a dream to train! Bunzy is highly food-driven and incredibly eager to please, making her a star student who loves following commands. With just a little bit of basic puppy training, this well-balanced girl is ready to become an absolute angel in her forever home. She's ready to fill your life with chin rests, cuddles, and endless love!
