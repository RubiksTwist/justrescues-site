Meet Macadamia!

Macadamia is your typical happy puppy; he is always playing, exploring, and looking for his next snack! He's curious about everything and loves being part of whatever is going on. Whether he's wrestling with his brother, carrying around a toy that's way too big for him, or zooming around the yard, he makes every day a little more fun.

He's still just a baby, so he'll need a family that's ready for all the puppy things and can help him grow into the best dog he can be. The good news? He's treat-motivated, eager to learn, and has all the potential to become an amazing companion.

Macadamia is looking for a foster-to-adopt home. Since he is still too young to be adopted at this moment, he'll need to stay in foster until around December while he finishes his puppy vaccination series and is neutered. Foster-to-adopt is a great opportunity to bond with him from the start and watch him grow into the amazing dog he's meant to be. Then, once he's medically cleared, his adoption can be finalized.

If you're ready to welcome a goofy, sweet, playful puppy into your home and don't mind a little puppy chaos along the way, Macadamia is ready to steal your heart! Please fill out and submit an adoption application today.
