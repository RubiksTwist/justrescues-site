Description provided by Rescue City

Robinson is almost 4 months old, is growing into those adorably oversized paws, all legs, all heart, and already over 26 pounds. This boy lives to make his people proud. The second he hears the treat bag, he’s already sitting patiently, looking up with those big puppy eyes like, “Did I do good?” (Spoiler: he always does.) Robinson is smart, eager to learn, and happiest when he’s spending time with his humans. He loves puzzle toys, zooming around with his foster sibling, wrestling until everyone’s tired, then flopping down with a chew toy and waiting for the next belly rub. He’s still working on his manners (those little puppy nibbles and enthusiastic greetings!), but his willingness to listen and learn shines through every single day. He just wants someone to tell him he’s a good boy. And trust us… he really, really is. Now all Robinson needs is the family he’ll spend the rest of his life making proud.
