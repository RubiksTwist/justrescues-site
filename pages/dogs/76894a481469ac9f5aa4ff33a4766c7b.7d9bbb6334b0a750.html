Description provided by Animal Lighthouse Rescue

Introducing Harlem! She is a 6-year-old, 32-pound Lab mix with a heart as big as her love for lounging. While she enjoys playtime, she’s truly an expert on the cuddle scale. Her favorite position is sprawled across the couch with her legs in the air and her belly proudly on display, ready for endless belly rubs. Harlem adores relaxing days at the park where she can soak up the sunshine, followed by some light playtime—grabbing toys from the toy bin, gnawing on a good bone, and then settling in for a well-earned snooze. She has a gentle, balanced personality that makes her a joy to be around. She does wonderfully with other dogs and cats and could happily join a home with either. Harlem was surrendered to our sister shelter in Puerto Rico along with her daughter, and now she’s ready to start her next chapter in a loving forever home. If you’re looking for a sweet, affectionate companion who’s always down to cuddle, Harlem might just be your perfect match. Inquire about her today!

Harlem is spayed, microchipped, and up to date on her vaccinations. For more information about her, please fill out an application at www.ALRcares.com and email Lori@alrcares.com. We schedule meet and greets for approved adopters only, an application is the first step in our approval process. Thank you!
