Description provided by NYC Second Chance Rescue

Sweet Lilly Claire’s journey hasn’t been easy; she was surrendered by her previous family and found herself at risk in the city shelter. But through it all, she’s never stopped being the affectionate, happy girl she was born to be.

Lilly Claire is wonderful with people and lights up whenever someone gives her attention. She adores pets, snuggles, and will happily lean in close for as much love as you’re willing to give. She also does well with other dogs, though she’d prefer a cat-free home.

This food-motivated sweetheart has a playful side, she can get excited on leash, and she may even jump to greet you when she just can’t contain her joy. With consistency and daily activity, she’ll thrive and make a fantastic companion for someone who enjoys walks, playtime, and plenty of cuddle breaks.

Lilly Claire has so much love to share and is just waiting for the right family to open their heart to her.

Ready to meet Lilly Claire? Come discover how special she truly is.
