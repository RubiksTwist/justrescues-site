Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Meet Ollie, the handsome two-year-old gentle giant we pulled from a Long Island shelter just before he was euthanized. Now he’s ready to start a new chapter with a family who will adore him.

Large in size but overflowing with love, Ollie has a gentle heart and an affectionate personality that makes him think he’s a lapdog even if he takes up most of the couch. He knows his basic commands, enjoys making new friends, and loves following you around just to be close.

Ollie would thrive with someone who appreciates a loyal, sweet companion with endless love to give. If you’re looking for an oversized cuddle buddy who’s all heart, Ollie might just be your perfect match.
