Meet Ziggy - a gentle gem of a puppy whose searching for a forever family for keeps! Ziggy is a 5 month old, sweet puppy whose temperament is so rare to find in rescue. This well rounded, social, family pup loves all people - kids and adults alike and all animals. Despite his little sad eyes, Ziggy is a happy, soulful pup who has all the makings of a best friend. Not only is Ziggy a well mannered house guest being both potty trained and crate trained - he is also super affectionate and extremely docile. Ziggy is the type of puppy who makes your heart melt the minute he climbs in your lap who you daydream about when you are away from home. Ziggy has never known the love a family has to give yet still embraces any love he is given. If you are looking for a dog who leads his life with grace, poise and so much acceptance - Ziggy is your guy. Apply to meet him today, he's super special and if anyone is going to be lucky to adopt him, it should be you!
