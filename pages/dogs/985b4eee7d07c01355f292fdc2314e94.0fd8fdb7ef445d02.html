Kate is a sweet little diva who firmly believes she should be at the center of your universe, and she's pretty convincing! This cuddly puppy loves being close to her people and is happiest when she's getting plenty of attention and affection. She's expected to be medium-sized when fully grown.

Like any puppy, Kate is still learning the ropes and will need a patient, committed adopter who is ready to guide her through puppyhood with training, socialization, and consistency. Give her the love and guidance she needs as she grows, and Kate will happily reward you with plenty of cuddles and a lifetime of devotion.
