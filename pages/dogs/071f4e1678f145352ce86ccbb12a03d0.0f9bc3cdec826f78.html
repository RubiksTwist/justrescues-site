Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Aladdin is a 3-year-old mixed breed weighing around 46 pounds, and he is an absolute sweetheart. The kind of dog who melts into pets and leans into affection.

He’s house-trained, curious about everything, and approaches the world with an enthusiasm that’s hard not to love. Walks are one of his favorite things because there’s always something new to see, smell, or investigate. He has a delightful pep in his step and is so interested in the world around him that he’ll occasionally try to sneak a hello from a passing stranger.

At home, Aladdin has a softer side. He loves to cuddle and seems happiest when he knows his people are nearby. One of his favorite spots is sitting with his bum pressed against your leg, quietly keeping you company while staying connected.

He also has a playful streak that comes out when the toys appear. Watching him flop onto his back and entertain himself is enough to make anyone smile.

Approved adopters interested in Aladdin can email adopt@hbrdogs.com for more info. If you're not approved yet, apply today at hbrdogs.com.
