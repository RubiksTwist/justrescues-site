Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Lubec is a 1-year-old Chihuahua mix weighing around 18 pounds, and she's the perfect mix of playful, clever, and cuddly. She's always up for a game or an adventure, but when it's time to relax, she's more than happy to curl up beside you for a snuggle.

This smart little girl is incredibly food motivated and loves learning. She already knew how to sit, and she picked up "paw" in no time. She's also a huge fan of meeting new people, greeting them with an enthusiastic tail wag before sitting politely in front of them, hoping they'll stop to say hello.
