At just 5 weeks old, Hooligan and his sister Arirang were found on the streets without their mother. They were brought into rescue and have grown into happy, well-adjusted puppies. Now 6 months old, Hooligan is a gentle boy who bonds quickly to his family. He loves sharing his bed and giving kisses when he wakes up in the morning. Hes affectionate towards children, playful with other dogs and comfortable around cats — making him an easy addition to any family ready to love him.

Hooligan is fostered in Stamford, CT and is available for local or out-of-state adoption as long as the adopter can travel to meet him. To get the adoption process started, please send us an application at https://bit.ly/rosr-adopt-foster.
