Description provided by Bobbi and the Strays

Rescued from the streets of Puerto Rico, sweet little Rita is safe in our care. This lovely senior girl is now waiting to go to a loving forever home where she can live a quiet life. She is a good walker who likes other dogs and is also content to cuddle on comfy laps. She is a low energy pup who will make a wonderful companion.

Rita is a 12 1/2 year old Chihuahua who is up to date on vaccinations, microchipped, spayed and will be heartworm tested upon adoption. She is currently at our Atlas (Queens) location. If you would like to meet this sweetheart, please fill out the adoption application on our website using a laptop or desktop (no phone or tablet, please) at https://bobbiandthestrays.org/adoption-page-new/ .
