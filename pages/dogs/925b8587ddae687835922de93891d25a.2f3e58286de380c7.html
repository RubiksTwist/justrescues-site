Meet Lady G!

Lady G came to us after being found living in difficult conditions with several other dogs, and she is now ready for a fresh start. Despite her past, she is a gentle, social girl who enjoys being around people. She comes over for attention, leans into pets, and shows her sweet nature in soft, quiet ways.

My Ideal Home

Lady G would do best in a calm, adult-only home that can give her structure, patience, and time to fully settle in. A peaceful environment will help her feel safe and build confidence.

Good with Other Pets?

Lady G has lived with multiple dogs before, but her interactions can be a bit mixed. She may feel unsure in new situations and can become overwhelmed, so slow introductions and a thoughtful approach will be important. A lower traffic, more controlled environment is recommended.

Good with Kids?

Recommended for a home without children under 13 due to her sensitivity and need for a calm environment.

Things to Know

Lady G can be a bit anxious, especially in busy or high energy settings, and may pant or feel overwhelmed at times. She benefits from gentle handling, predictable routines, and positive reinforcement training. With consistency and kindness, she has a lot of potential to relax and grow more confident.

Fun Fact

Lady G lives up to her name, she's a soft, gentle girl who carries herself with a quiet, regal charm once she feels comfortable.
