Meet Lotus! 🐾❤️

Lotus is a young Husky with plenty of energy, a playful personality, and a big heart. During her time in care, she has been energetic and playful, showing off the fun-loving spirit you'd expect from a Husky. We recommend a home with older children due to reported food guarding and some handling sensitivity. A family who can provide respectful boundaries, patience, and positive reinforcement will help set this pup up for success.

Her previous owner describes her as a social and affectionate girl who loves being around people. Lotus absolutely loves car rides! She also knows several cues, including sit, stay, paw, and down, showing that she's a smart girl who is ready to keep learning. One of her previous owner's favorite things about Lotus is her ability to recognize when someone is upset and offer comfort.

And when it comes to favorite activities, toys, food, or snacks? Lotus loves literally anything! She's an affectionate, playful, people-loving girl who would make a wonderful companion for an active family ready to give her plenty of love, fun, and adventure.
