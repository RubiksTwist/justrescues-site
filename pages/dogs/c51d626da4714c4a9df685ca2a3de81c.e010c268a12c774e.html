Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Ramona is a sweet, affectionate puppy who has already mastered the art of cuddling. She's a total lap dog who wants nothing more than to curl up with her people and enjoy being close to them. She doesn't even need to fall asleep. She's perfectly happy just hanging out in your lap and soaking up the company. She's expected to be medium-sized when fully grown.

Like any puppy, Ramona has plenty to learn as she grows and will need a patient, committed adopter who is ready to provide training, socialization, structure, and lots of love along the way. With the right guidance, this cuddly little girl has all the makings of a wonderful companion.
