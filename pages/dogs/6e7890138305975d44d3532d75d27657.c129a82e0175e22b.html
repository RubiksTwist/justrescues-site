Meet Ruby Rose (Ruby Roo)

Ruby Rose, lovingly known as Ruby Roo, is as sweet as they come. She was at risk of losing her life in an overcrowded shelter in Alabama, but she was given a second chance when she was taken into rescue with a promise: that her next chapter would be safe, loved, and full of care.

Ruby has shown nothing but gentleness and heart. She is affectionate, eager to please, and has a calm, loving presence that makes everyone around her feel at ease. She doesn’t ask for much, just kindness, stability, and someone to love her back as much as she loves them.

Ruby Roo has already overcome uncertainty, and now she’s ready for something she has never truly had: a forever family of her own.

Please consider adopting Ruby Rose and giving her the life she has been waiting for all along.
