Meet Mochachino.

Mochachino was found as a stray and ended up at the ACC, where the stress of kennel life quickly began to take its toll. We knew she deserved better, so we busted her out before the shelter environment could dim her bright spirit.

Now safe in rescue, Mochachino is showing everyone what a truly special girl she is. She's sweet, beautiful, and incredibly smart, with so much potential just waiting to shine. Like many dogs adjusting to a new life, she'll thrive with a patient adopter who can provide gentle guidance, consistency, and love as she continues to build her confidence.

Mochachino has already overcome so much, and now she's ready for the one thing she's been missing: forever family to call her own.

If you're looking for a loyal companion and are willing to give her the time and understanding she deserves, Mochachino will reward you with a lifetime of love. Could you be the happy ending she's been waiting for?

All dogs will be altered, microchipped, and receive age-appropriate vaccines prior to adoption.
