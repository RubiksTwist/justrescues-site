About:

Meet Aires | Apply Here

Aires is a gorgeous 4 year old and 48lb Husky! He came to us from a local owner who surrendered - his owners had to relocate to a building that didn’t allow pups! He is just as sweet as he is gorgeous and ready to join a new family.

Being the proper husky he is, Aires is a fan of making himself known. If you get him riled up enough he’ll sing you the song of his people. Even still, he’s a dream boy both inside and outside. His leash walking skills have soared to the stars and he’s getting all ‘A’s in his crate training progress. He’s also a people’s dog AND a dog’s dog!

Aires loves playing with anyone that will give him attention, but make sure there’s a comfy spot for a nap later. He’s an outgoing boy with big dreams of his future family and forever home.
