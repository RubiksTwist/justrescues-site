Meet Arlo! This 4-month-old, 8-pound bundle of fluff is quite possibly the most adorable puppy you will ever meet! Arlo is happy ALL the time and has a smile that is guaranteed to make everyone around him smile, too. He is incredibly playful, loves going for walks, and is determined to walk himself on the leash—because clearly, he knows exactly where he’s going! He also enjoys a good game of fetch, although we make absolutely no promises that the ball will actually make its way back to you. He is eager to learn, loves being a good boy, and absolutely soaks up every bit of praise he can get. When all that puppy fun finally wears him out, Arlo knows how to relax in style. When he gets really comfortable, you'll find him completely passed out on his back, paws straight up in the air, tongue hanging out, dreaming the sweetest puppy dreams. Arlo is the kind of puppy who brings happiness wherever he goes. With his fluffy ears, playful personality, and giant smile, we have a feeling he’s going to make his forever family very happy!

Arlo is neutered, microchipped, and up to date on his vaccinations. For more information about him, please fill out an application at www.ALRcares.com and email Lori@alrcares.com. We schedule meet and greets for approved adopters only, an application is the first step in our approval process. Thank you!
