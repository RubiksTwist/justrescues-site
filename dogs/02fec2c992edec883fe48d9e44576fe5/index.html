Description provided by Bideawee

Apollo is just one year old and has so much ahead of him! After spending his whole life in a home, he’s now adjusting to shelter life, and even with all this change, he has been such a sweet and friendly boy.

Apollo is starting to show his playful side and enjoys spending time with people. He’s a big, strong boy, so he’ll need someone comfortable handling a dog his size. His former caretaker told us that Apollo has lived with both a dog and a cat, so he may be a good fit for a home with other pets with the right introductions.

If this dog is located at our Manhattan Adoption Center, head in to meet them and see if you are a match! We are a same-day adoption center, and an application does not put you on a list or hold an animal for you. If they are in Wantagh or in a foster home, please submit an online application and our team will reach out to further discuss the adoption process. Prior to adoption, all of our animals are spayed/neutered, microchipped, and given their age-appropriate vaccines.
