Meet Ocean.

Meet Ocean, a handsome New Yorker with a very special feature, one beautiful blue eye as blue as the ocean!

Ocean was found as a stray here in New York and eventually landed at the NYC ACC. When the shelter ran out of room, we stepped in to give this sweet boy the second chance he deserved.

Ocean is an energetic, happy fellow who absolutely loves his treats! He’s sweet and eager to please, but he’s also still learning and needs some guidance and direction. With patience, positive reinforcement, and plenty of yummy rewards, this handsome boy is sure to thrive.

Ocean has the looks, the personality, and that unforgettable ocean-blue eye. Now he just needs a loving home to show him how wonderful life can be.

Could Ocean be the one who makes a splash in your heart?

Want to learn more? We'd love to tell you! Our bios are just a snapshot, so please reach out to our adoption team with any questions about Ocean's personality, medical history, or ideal home. We'd be happy to help you determine if Ocean is the perfect match for your family.
