Description provided by AMA Animal Rescue

Robin's life took a heartbreaking turn when he was abandoned at our shelter. He arrived malnourished, frightened, and having clearly endured abuse. He had every reason to be afraid, so we gave him the gentle care, patience, and love he desperately needed.

With each passing day, Robin has started to feel safer, and little by little, we're getting to see the sweet boy behind those frightened eyes. He's playful, loves spending time in the backyard with his toys, and is a very smart pup who is eager to learn. In just a few days, he's already picked up basic commands, especially when yummy treats are involved!

Robin is looking for a family who will give him the guidance, gentle love, and patience he needs to continue growing in confidence. With time, positive experiences, and plenty of opportunities to socialize, we know he has so much potential. He's a friendly, loving boy who could make a wonderful addition to a family.
