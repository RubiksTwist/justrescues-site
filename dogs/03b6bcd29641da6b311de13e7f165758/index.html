Description provided by Waggytail Rescue

Lindt is is a 2-month old male kitten.

Lindt is the sweetest and most playful kitten who loves everyone he meets! Bigger than his brother and sister, Lindt is the most adoring kitten. He loves to wrestle, run around, climb, and cuddle. His favorite is playing on people's laps (like a jungle gym) and his favorite fuzzy ball he carries around in his mouth. He is a sweet boy who loves people (he likes everyone he meets) and loves playing with his siblings. He is curious and adventurous and deserves to be in a home with a lot of love. Lindt would love a playful cat or dog companion to learn wrestle!

Like the all of our kittens, Lindt must be adopted in a pair with another kitten from our rescue, OR adopted to a home with another playful cat or dog.

Lindt is is up-to-date on his FVRCP vaccine series, needs Rabies, FeLV/FIV negative, dewormed, microchipped, and is getting neutered on 11/13.

Please apply using the following instructions if you are interested in adopting one of our Waggytails: Visit our website (www.waggytailrescue.org) for an adoption application. It is important to fill out an application as soon as possible for consideration, and we require a complete application for you to meet an animal. We are currently unable to respond to general email inquiries as an all-volunteer run rescue. If the cat is on our website, they are still available.

Waggytail Rescue CANNOT guarantee the exact age of any adoptable cat, or final size of any of our adoptable kittens. We rely on the information provided to us by veterinarians, shelters, owner surrenders and other circumstances bringing the cat into our rescue.
