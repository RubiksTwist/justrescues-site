HISTORY

Tumblr was found as a stray in Georgia and taken in temporarily by a kind stranger. When her work took her back on the road, she was no longer able to care for him, and he was boarding at a local vet’s office when we met him. He traveled to Brooklyn in mid-October 2026.

DETAILED PROFILE

Tumblr is an easygoing adventure buddy who mostly just wants to be included. He greets the day ready to go, and people sometimes mistake him for a puppy, but he’s just as happy to settle in later for a Hallmark movie with the family. He’s goofy, intuitive, and quick to make friends.

He’s a social guy who showers his people with hugs and (mostly) gentle kisses. He handled a marathon crowd and packed dog-friendly bars like a champ. In a brand-new place he can be a little shy, but with his people nearby he’s glad to meet anyone offering pets or treats.

Other dogs are a highlight. He loves rough-and-tumble wrestling with willing partners and did well at daycare. His enthusiasm can be a lot for some dogs; when a bossy one corrects him, he shows no interest in a fight. A dog sibling would be a bonus, not a requirement. He’s also just short enough to walk under one of his foster sisters, who sometimes ends up riding on his back. He seems very proud of this.

He’s crate trained and pretty good on leash, though eager morning walks could use polish. He’s also learning to keep his paws off people. House training started off spotless, but lately he’s had crate accidents when the other dogs head out without him, so a steady routine and clear structure from day one will help. He’s still settling in, so expect more to unfold.

Squeaky toys, full-speed sprints, neighborhood rounds, and a couch seat for movie night. His foster has yet to find a downside.
