Description provided by NYC Second Chance Rescue

Herman was found in Georgia, abandoned, barely holding on.

Skin and bones. Wounded. Forgotten.

Most people walked away. We didn’t.

We said yes to Herman, because no dog deserves to suffer like that, and no life is beyond saving.

Today, he’s safe, healing, and showing us the sweetest, most resilient soul. Despite everything, Herman still wants love. He still trusts. He still hopes.

Now all he needs… is you.

Be the one who changes his ending.

All dogs will be altered, microchipped, and receive age-appropriate vaccines prior to adoption.
