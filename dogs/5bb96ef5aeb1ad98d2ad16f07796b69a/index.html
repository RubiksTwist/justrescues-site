Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

My Boroughbreaker writes: Willow is a total lovebug! I took her to a coffee shop and around Central Park. She's extremely sweet and friendly towards people, including children. She's unfazed by the city sounds. She's very good on the leash and patient when I wanted her to wait. She's very curious and enjoys sniffing around and watching squirrels. She does get a little nervous when other dogs get close but totally cool if they keep their distance. I thoroughly enjoyed spending the day with her and hope she finds her forever home soon!

My Boroughbreaker writes: First went for a car ride- she got in and out of the car herself. She was a nice passenger who stayed in the backseat and enjoyed sniffing out the windows. We walked around the city and she was not phased at all by the hustle and bustle and extra busy amount of people out and about for Halloween. She would be fine in a city setting. She was an easy walker but did bark at some dogs while others she passed with no issue. We sat outside for lunch and she laid down and was super chill the whole meal. She received a lot of attention from people which she loved, especially when she was being offered treats. She was lovely with all, including children! Willow is a beautiful dog with a beautiful soul and I thoroughly enjoyed our time together and hope she finds her forever family soon!
