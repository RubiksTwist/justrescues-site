Meet Red Berry!
Red Berry is a sweet, energetic girl who is always ready to get moving! This treat-loving pup is social, affectionate, and eager to be around her people. She may have a little extra pep in her step, but underneath all that excitement is a friendly girl who is ready to find someone who will help her slow down and enjoy life.

My Ideal Home
Red Berry would love an active home where she can get plenty of exercise, enrichment, and positive-reinforcement training. She'll need a family who can help her practice calm behavior when she's excited, while giving her lots of appropriate outlets for her energy. With consistency, patience, and plenty of treats, Red is ready to learn that sometimes the best things in life are worth slowing down for.

Good with Other Pets?
Red Berry's history with other dogs is unknown since she came to us as a stray. She can become tense and very interested when she sees other dogs, but she is responsive to redirection and can keep moving. Any introductions should be slow, thoughtful, and supervised while her new family helps her build positive associations with her canine neighbors.

Good with Kids?
Because Red Berry gets excited easily and can be quite enthusiastic with her greetings, we recommend a home without young children under 5. Older kids who understand how to give an energetic pup space and practice calm interactions may be a better fit.

Things to Know
Red Berry is a lively girl who loves attention and may jump up when she's excited to see you. She walks with some pulling, especially when another dog catches her attention, but she is easy to redirect and very motivated by treats. She's affectionate, eager to explore, and ready to put her energy to good use with a family who can help her blossom. With Red Berry around, life is bound to be a little sweeter!
