Keiko was found in a empty apartment unit and brought in with 4 other dogs by animal control to the NYACC shelter. We saved Keiko from the NYACC's euthanasia list on April 6, 2026.

Outside the shelter, Keiko is a social pup with humans, she is eager to learn and loves her stuff toys. She is happy spending time training with her foster and very independent; she enjoys downtime by relaxing in her crate.

She is being fostered with her brother Skipper, but has no interest him or in other dogs.

She is looking for an experience owner who can show her the ropes and shower her with love!
