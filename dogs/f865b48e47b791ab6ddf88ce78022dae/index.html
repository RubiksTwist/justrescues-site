Keiko was found in a empty apartment unit and brought in with 4 other dogs by animal control to the NYACC shelter. We saved Keiko from the NYACC's euthanasia list on April 6, 2026.

Outside the shelter, Keiko is a social pup with humans, she loves to learn, gets along with dogs and cats, and understands how to be a good girl. She is happy spending time training with her foster and giving affection; she enjoys downtime by relaxing in her crate.

All four of her siblings have found their forever home. She is the only one left which is surprising because she meets every criteria of a dream dog!
