Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

Sesame is a very cute little girl who is still getting used to her new surroundings. She can be a little shy at first and is taking some time to figure out the shelter environment, but she warms up nicely with a slow and gentle approach. Sesame was found tied up outside of a police station, so she would benefit from a patient and understanding family who can give her the time and reassurance she needs to feel safe. Once she starts to trust you, her sweet personality begins to shine. And if you want to win Sesame over, snacks are definitely the way to her heart! With patience, love, and a little time, Sesame has the potential to become a wonderful and devoted companion.
