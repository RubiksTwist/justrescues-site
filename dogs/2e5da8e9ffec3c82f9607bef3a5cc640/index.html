Description provided by ASPCA Adoption Center

Baby talk and cheese are the ways to sweet Stella’s heart!

Stella is a gentle brown-and-white girl who is learning that the world can be a kind and cozy place. With baby talk and yummy treats like cheese, Stella has been able to make many friends. She is also great on walks, keeping a loose leash while taking in the sights and smells around her.

In her time at the Adoption Center, Stella quickly blossomed into a dog who loves to lean in for pets! She spent an afternoon in a staff member’s office and was a wonderful guest. “In the office, she sniffed a lot at first, but then settled down very quickly,” the staff member said. “She solicited pets often, gave kisses to my arms and entertained herself the entire time with toys and enrichment. She is truly so amazing!”

Stella is sure to be a lovely addition to your life! To find out more about her, click the “Adopt Me” button!
