Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

A Volunteer Wrote: If eyes are the soul of a dog, all you need to do is look into precious Gigi's to know the size of her extraordinary heart and spirit. She's the gentlest, kindest dog, and I thought about her long after our time together, the majority of which she spent in my lap seeking assurance and affection. I cannot whisk her away to my home, but I'm sure hoping you can-she would make the most extraordinary addition to a life, waking up and going to sleep with that sweetness by your side!

My #BoroughBreak Buddy Writes: Do you love hugs? Well, so does Gigi! More than walking, she would prefer to just stand on her hind legs and hug you until you say, "let's do something else." Even when getting on her leash, she sat on staff's lap until it was time to go. No only does she love hugs, she gives the best hugs, burying her head into your arm. She is not dog-reactive at all. She loves looking at squirrels, but doesn't chase them in a way that is disruptive. Sometimes she can get fixated on giving love in a way that is difficult to redirect. That said, she spends most of her day in a kennel, so she may be hanging on to the human attention she can get or be overly excited. With training, trust building, and proper decompression, she can seamlessly became your right hand woman. Gigi is ready to jump into your arms. Are you ready to accept her love?
