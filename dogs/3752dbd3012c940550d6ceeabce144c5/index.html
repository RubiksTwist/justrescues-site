Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Meet Graham - yes like the crackers! This boy is savory, sweet and somehow super duper fulfilling.

Graham is a 2 year old bachelor that is just under 60 lbs. If there was one word to describe him, it would be charming. Like a typical bachelor, Graham is calm, affectionate, and wonderfully well-balanced. If Graham were a human, he would be the guy that all the girls had a crush on and all the boys were jealous of. Graham is the epitome of a chill dude who makes himself at home in any room he walks in and makes everyone around him feel like they've known him forever.

Graham's contagious personality isn't the best hing about him however. Despite being 2 yars old with a long , beautiful life left to live - he's independent enough to curl up beside you while you read or have independent play while you work. Graham is a dog who is happy to exist in a comfortable silence - happy to simply be near his people without also needing constant attention. Don't be fooled though, if you are looking for a boy who will cuddle, give and accept affection - Graham is always adaptable to the needs of his people.

Outdoors, Graham is easygoing and manageable even when someting new and exciting catces his eyes. Grahams a curious community guy who ejoys exploring new places, sniffing new smells and simply taking in the world around him. Graham is quick to redirect, disengage and focus on his human on his walks and has proven to be a dog with a healthy balance of homebody and brunch buddy.

With humans, Graham is friendly, confident and overall unphased by external stimuli such as strangers on the street or delivery people at the door. When Graham is approached by someone who wants to be his friend, he will ask and accept pets, roll over for belly rubs, give and take face kisses and soak up as much laptime as you are willing to give him.

Graham is a true companion through and through and is the type of dog that adds so much value to his humans life, he will make it hard to remember what life was like before him.
