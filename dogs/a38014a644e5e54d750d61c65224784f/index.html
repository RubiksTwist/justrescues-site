Description provided by Waldo's Rescue Pen

Louella has made great progress getting used to the hustle and bustle of the city. Once shy and even frightened--with tail tucked between her legs and occasional shivering when walked outside--Louella now walks confidently most of the time, tail wagging and even taking the lead when walked with her foster brother, Tater. She gets along well with other dogs, is generally submissive and/or playful, and loves to be near her humans. She is very food and treat motivated, but not aggressively so. She is virtually housetrained, but we have had a few accidents while she’s been decompressing. With regular walks, 2-3 times a day, I’m sure she will improve is this area. She hardly ever barks (we were surprised when she suddenly gave out two quick, strong woofs). Overall Louella’s been the easiest going foster we’ve taken in yet. She’s a delight!
