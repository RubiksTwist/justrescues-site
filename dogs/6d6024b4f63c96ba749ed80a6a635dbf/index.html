My foster writes: Hi, I'm Maspeth AKA Massy .I'm a 5 year old bully girl with a little attitude, a lot of personality, and a belly that's always available for rubs.

Life didn't exactly start out in my favor, but thanks to ACC and my foster, I'm getting my second chance at finding my forever people.

A few things about me: I know sit, down, kennel and come. I'm about 60% housebroken (hey, nobody's perfect), great with kids over 10 years old and dogs, and I walk really nicely on leash. I love people, cozy dog beds, my crate, and my toys. I do enjoy chewing, but don't worry, I stick to my own stuff. I take correction like a lady.

I like to take my time getting to know new friends. Slow introductions are my thing, but once I trust you, congratulations, you've got yourself a shadow with four legs and a serious belly rub addiction.

So, are you ready for a new best friend? Let's take over Queens together!

My foster writes: Hi, I'm Maspeth! I'm a sweet, cuddly girl with a BIG appetite and a soft spot for my favorite toys. I already know sit and down, and I'm quite the good listener! I'm still getting comfortable in my new surroundings, so I appreciate a little patience and gentle love. I enjoy cozy cuddles, tasty treats, and taking life one day at a time.

I'm having fun discovering my personality, and I can't wait to share more with you. Stay tuned for my pupdates!
