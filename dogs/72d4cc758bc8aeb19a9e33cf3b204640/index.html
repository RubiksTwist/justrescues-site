AGE / WEIGHT: 3 years old / 44 lbs

GENDER: Male

ENERGY: Moderate

GOOD WITH: Dogs - Yes, Kids - TBD, Cats - Yes

ABOUT ME: First stop in any new home: the toy basket. I test every one, then crown a winner (lately, a little squeaky horse, which feels right for a guy with my name). Other dogs like me instantly, cats get my best behavior, and every person on the block gets a hello. I’m playful but can kick back, and my manners are in progress, so I’m looking for someone who likes a project with a wagging tail.

MY FOSTER SAYS: “He instantly made himself at home testing every toy in the toy basket; his favorite is the little stuffed squeaky horse. He’s doing a great job living up to his name.”

HISTORY

Li’l Sebastian came in as a stray in Georgia and spent almost a year boarding at a veterinarian’s office, where our team met him on a rescue trip. He traveled north in mid-October 2026 and spent a few weeks boarding outside the city before moving into a foster home.

DETAILED PROFILE

Li'l Sebastian is a dog's dog. He reads other dogs with real finesse: he wrestles and plays tug with his foster's playful pup, gives the quieter resident dog his space, and even leaves that dog's favorite toys alone or brings them over as a peace offering. He passes dogs on the street calmly, and he's polite with cats, who want nothing to do with him.

He's goofy, affectionate, and playful, with a serious toy habit. The moment he arrived, he tested every toy in the basket and crowned a little stuffed squeaky horse his favorite. Between games he's happy to flop down for a nap, sometimes using a toy as a pillow. He can settle and relax at home, and short play sessions with quiet breaks suit him best.

People are his other favorite thing. He wants to greet everyone he passes, and kids get him especially excited. When he met a preteen neighbor he was thrilled, but he jumped up and got a little mouthy, so a home with young children should wait until his manners catch up. The leg-humping that seemed to be his way of asking for things has nearly stopped, and he's responding well to ""no"" and ""stop.""

Training is very much in progress. He's starting to respond to his name and hasn't learned sit yet. House training slipped as he got more freedom, so plan on a steady routine and some back-to-basics work. He's great in his crate, but anything made of fabric is fair game for chewing, so bedding and socks should stay out of reach. On leash he takes direction well but will pull toward every person he spots.

Fetch, a canine pal, and a patient teacher: that's the whole wish list.

MEDICAL HISTORY

The dogs in our care are up-to-date on vaccinations, spayed or neutered (when old enough), microchipped, and given a general health check. Immediate medical needs and medical history will be addressed with the adopter prior to adoption. Complete vet records will be sent shortly after adoption.
