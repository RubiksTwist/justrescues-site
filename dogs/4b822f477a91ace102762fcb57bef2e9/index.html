Pre-Screener Form

Pretty Bear is at risk due to behavioral reasons. Pretty Bear has been observed to be highly rope reactive. Noted to bite and tug the leash into kennel. In one instance, Pretty was tugging so vigorously that the kennel door began to be pulled inwards. This behavior is very difficult to redirect. While out of kennel Pretty is noted to jump up and bite the leash often coming close to the handler's hand. Initially when Pretty arrived to the care center, she was observed to be fearful. Noted to maintain a low tense body and tucked tail. Over time she has opened up to handlers. With familiar handlers, Pretty has a loose body, wagging tail, and allows contact. Medically, Pretty has dermatitis and otitis externa.

Pretty Bear is a sweet, affectionate, and playful girl who is approximately 2 years and 8 months old and ready to find her next loving home!

This lovely lady has a low-key personality and enjoys the simple things in life, especially relaxing on the couch and spending time with her favorite people. She can be a little reserved around strangers and visitors, preferring to warm up at her own pace, but once comfortable, her loving and playful personality shines through. She previously lived with three children and is described as tolerant, friendly, and playful with kids, although she prefers affection on her own terms.

When it comes to adventures, Pretty Bear gets excited at the sight of her leash! She enjoys going for walks twice a day, typically around 35 minutes each, and is reported to have little to mild leash pulling. She also loves car rides, bath time, and trips to the groomer. She's even a good sport about nail trims!

Pretty Bear knows some basic cues, including "sit," "come," and "no." She is partially housetrained and would benefit from a little patience and consistency as she continues learning.
