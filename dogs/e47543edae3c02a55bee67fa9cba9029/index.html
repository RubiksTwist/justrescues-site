Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Jolene is a 2-year-old Great Pyrenees mix weighing around 50 pounds, and calling her cuddly might actually be an understatement. If she could crawl under your skin to get just a little closer, she probably would. And when you hit just the right spot during a good petting session, she actually purrs like a cat. Yes, really.

Jolene is brand new to NYC and still deciding how she feels about all this noise and excitement. She may ultimately be happier in a quieter area where she can enjoy the outdoors without quite so much happening around her. With other dogs, Jolene appreciates a polite introduction. She's not a fan of dogs who come on too strong and isn't afraid to set some boundaries, but give her a little time to get to know them and she's perfectly happy sharing her space.

Registered adopters interested in Jolene can email adopt@hbrdogs.com for more info. If you're not registered yet, do it today at hbrdogs.com!
