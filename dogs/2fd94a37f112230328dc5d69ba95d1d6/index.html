Description provided by Animal Lighthouse Rescue

Meet Atlas! This handsome 4-month-old, 10-pound Lab mix is looking for a forever home where he can blossom into the amazing dog we know he is destined to be! Atlas is truly wonderful in every way, but he can be a little shy when meeting new people. If you give him a little time, patience, and a chance to settle in, and you'll get to watch his sweet personality come shining through. The quickest way to see Atlas come out of his shell to to introduce him to another dog! The moment he spots a canine friend, he jumps right out of his comfort zone and is ready to play! We think Atlas would thrive in a home with another confident, friendly dog, a quieter neighborhood, and someone who will truly appreciate watching him grow more comfortable and confident as the weeks go by. Once he feels safe and settled, we're certain he'll become everyone's best friend. Atlas is eager to please and absolutely thrives on praise—and, of course, an abundance of treats! He loves being a good boy and soaking up all the encouragement he can get. After a fun-filled day of puppy adventures, Atlas loves splashing around in his kiddie pool, soaking up some sunshine, and then sprawling out across the cool tile floor for a nice, long snooze. Although Atlas may need a little time to show you his whole heart, we promise he's worth the wait. If you're looking for a sweet puppy whose confidence and personality will continue to blossom right before your eyes, Atlas could be your perfect match!

Atlas is neutered, microchipped, and up to date on his vaccinations. For more information about him, please fill out an application at www.ALRcares.com and email Lori@alrcares.com. We schedule meet and greets for approved adopters only, an application is the first step in our approval process. Thank you!
