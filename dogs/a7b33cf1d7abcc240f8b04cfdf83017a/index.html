Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Confetti is a whole lot of sweetness wrapped up in one adorable little package! This girl has so much love to give and is just waiting for the right person to show her all the good things life has to offer.

Confetti is an absolute dream on walks! She walks beautifully on a loose leash, happily following your lead and enjoying all the sights, sounds, and sniffs along the way. Whether you're taking a leisurely stroll around the neighborhood or heading out for a little adventure, she's the kind of walking buddy who makes getting outside that much sweeter.

Back at home, Confetti loves a good chew toy and is always happy to have something to keep her busy. She's also a smart girl who's learning new tricks and showing off her skills one paw at a time (literally!). She's recently learned how to give paw, and with a little encouragement and some tasty treats, we have a feeling she'll be picking up plenty more. This girl is eager to learn, and it's so fun watching her confidence grow as she figures out new things.

And when it's time to wind down? Confetti is all about the cuddles. She's a sweet, affectionate girl who loves being close to her people and soaking up some love. There's something so special about seeing her settle in for a snuggle and start to feel at home.

Confetti does well in her crate with a little help from enrichment to keep her happily occupied. She's still learning the ropes of foster life and figuring out how to settle into her surroundings. She's also a bit of a neighborhood watch volunteer and may have something to say when she hears noises outside the door! She's learning that not every little sound requires an announcement, and with patience and consistency, she's working on finding her indoor zen.

Like any girl who's still figuring things out, Confetti may need a little time to feel fully comfortable in her new home. With kindness, consistency, and a little help finding her comfort zone, she's learning that home can be a place to relax, play, chew on her favorite toys, and enjoy all the cuddles her heart desires.

Confetti is such a sweet girl with so much potential, and she's just getting started! She's got the leash manners, the brains, the playful side, and the cuddly personality to make someone a very lucky dog parent. All she needs now is a person willing to give her the time and love to blossom into the happiest, most comfortable version of herself.

Whoever gets to call Confetti theirs is going to have a very special little shadow by their side. She deserves a soft place to land, a basket full of chew toys, plenty of treats to practice her tricks, and someone who reminds her every day that she's home. 💕
