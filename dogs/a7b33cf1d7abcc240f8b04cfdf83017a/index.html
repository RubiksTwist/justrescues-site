Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Confetti is just what her name suggests, a party girl, with her cute half speckled face and body. She's about 6 years old, but young at heart.

She's hasn’t been in our care long, but she already knows her sit and come commands and will happily perform for treats. Confetti walks like a princess on a leash, loose leashed, and taking your lead, checking in along the way. She loves being outside and enjoys a leisurely stroll- sometimes she prances with a wagging tail and floppy ears, other times she takes it slow, getting all the sniffs in and on alert for birds, squirrels and the occasional cat. Confetti has been untested around kids, and spent a straycation with another pup so most likely dog-friend, though untested here.

Confetti loves enrichment games and treats. She is a very smart girl who likes to put her brain to the test sniffing out hidden goodies. This lady is chatty, girl loves to talk! She's on her way to finding better ways to communicate, with redirection and rewards for quiet.

Confetti likes to give new people big love, and is working on keeping both feet on the ground when meeting a neighbor or new potential friend. She prefers to meet new friends outside first. She's also working on feeling safe enough to allow endless pets and cuddles, and prefers if you let her come to you and are attuned to her comfortability level and body language.

Confetti will happily go into her crate for a high reward treat and is able to settle if you give her some time to get there her own way. She loves a covered crate and calming music to help her find her zen.

Confetti is mostly potty trained, only having a few accidents in the first couples days, which we’re confident were likely just due to initial nerves. Confetti was found as a stray, but despite her past, she got glowing reviews from the shelter as being the bestest girl. She's still adjusting to foster life, but will certainly find her stride. A strong, brave, smart, resilient girl that is deserving of someone that can be patient, likes to picnic with her outside, and keeps her mentally stimulated!

Truly, whoever gets to be her person will be so lucky. She is the most deserving girl and deserves to know a soft, gentle type of love.
