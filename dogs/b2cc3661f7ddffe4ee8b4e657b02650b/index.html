Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

Meet Enrique (aka Enrique Iglesias)! If you look up the definition of bravery in the dictionary, a picture of Enrique shows up. This boy had a rough start in life but over the course of his weeks in foster care he has become a whole new dog. He arrived very shy and shut down. But after about a week in his crate, only coming out to potty on a pee pad, he started explore the apartment, play with toys and his foster siblings, and even his humans. At first he wouldn't allow any human touch, but now he chooses to sit next to you on the couch or bed. He has gone from never going outside before to being potty trained and going on walks every single day. He is 100% crate trained and he finds it to be his special safe space, so it's crucial he have one in his forever home. He can be left home alone for hours and doesn't make a sound - but he does sometimes bark at the door to alert you to potential intruders. He also LOVES other dogs and gets along great with dogs of all sizes. He is also very respectful of his foster cat-siblings and their space. Enrique is ready to find his forever home but just needs someone patient and understanding. Could that person be you?

﻿Enrique is approximately 3y 8m 9d old and is fully grown at 11 pounds. They are microchipped and up to date on all age-appropriate medical care. Their breed is unknown as we have not had a DNA test done.

Although our dogs are listed as unknown breed, buildings with specific dog restrictions typically enforce their rules based on what breeds a dog appears to resemble. It's always important to keep that in mind and know your building's policies before adopting a dog.
