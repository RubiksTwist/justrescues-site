Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

Meet Enrique (aka Enrique Iglesias)! This little man has had a rough start in life but is ready for things to turn around and be someone's forever pup. Enrique is very shy and arrived into his foster home very shut down. But after about a week in his crate, only coming out to potty on a pee pad, he started to be brave and explore the apartment and even try to play with his foster siblings. He is working on allowing human touch and going outside. But he appears to be pee pad trained and is very crate trained and doesn't mind being left alone at all. Enrique needs someone patient and understanding of his slower pace to thrive. Could that person be you?

﻿Enrique is approximately 3y 7m 11d old and is fully grown at 11 pounds. They are microchipped and up to date on all age-appropriate medical care. Their breed is unknown as we have not had a DNA test done.

Although our dogs are listed as unknown breed, buildings with specific dog restrictions typically enforce their rules based on what breeds a dog appears to resemble. It's always important to keep that in mind and know your building's policies before adopting a dog.

This dog is available for adoption through Muddy Paws Rescue. If you're interested in adopting, the first thing you should do is begin the registration process at www.muddypawsrescue.org.
