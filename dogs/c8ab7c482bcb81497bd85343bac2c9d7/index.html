Submit an adoption application a t socialteesnyc.org Our Team Says: "Sorrel would do best in a calm area. She's still working on her confidence outdoors. She wouldn't say no to a home in the burbs or beyond." Thier Foster Parent Says: " Sorrel is literally a tiny baby Living in a big, very scary world. At a whopping 9 pounds, she has decided that the safest place to be is… directly on your skin . If you’re sitting down, congratulations!!! you now have a chiweenie dog attached to you. She LOVESSSSS salmon skin treats. Like, LOVES THEM . She will absolutely bark at you for more because apparently 47 treats is never enough. She’s also learning how to sit, which is a BIG deal because, as everyone knows, chiweenie dogs have approximately zero interest in taking orders . She’s doing great and slowly realizing that listening = snacks. Outside, Sorrel is still a scaredy baby . Going out to potty is a whole production because literally EVERYTHING is terrifying. A leaf? Scary. A noise? SCARY. A person existing nearby? CALL 911 . She’s only 9 pounds, so the world is understandably HUGE to her. With patience, reassurance, and someone willing to let her take things at her own pace, she’s learning that outside isn’t so scary after all. Sorrel is crate trained and has discovered that her bed is her little safe space. When she wants peace and quiet, she knows exactly where to go. She isn’t super toy motivated yet and would much rather play with you than a squeaky toy, but who knows, maybe her inner toy gremlin is still loading. She currently has two foster sisters: one around her tiny size and one big potato. She absolutely loves snuggling with them when she’s in the mood, but Sorrel is also very good at communicating when she’s had enough. She’ll happily cuddle… until she doesn't, and then she will politely let you know that your services are no longer required. Sorrel is looking for someone who understands that she’s still a baby, who will give her time to build confidence, and who doesn’t mind having a 9 pound personal shadow following them around." Level of dog ownership experience required: BEGINNER – “Beginner” doesn’t necessarily mean easy as all dogs will require time, work, and patience, but we predict this dog will be low on the challenge scale because of their breed/age combo. Potentially a good fit for someone who has relevant dog care experience (fostering, dog sitting) and is looking to adopt their first pup. Energy level: MEDIUM – Needs a daily 60 minute walk or window of quick paced exercise (minimum) in addition to potty break strolls (10-30 min each). Also needs significant socializing opportunities outside the home and mental stimulation at home like tricks training practice, chew items, and puzzle toys. (This is regardless of dog size – many small breeds were designed for lots of activity and will exhibit unwanted behavior when they’re understimulated.) Up to date on their DHPP, Bordetella, and Rabies vaccines; heartworm tested if coming from Tennessee or Puerto Rico; spayed/neutered; microchipped; treated with dewormer upon intake How did this dog end up with Social Tees? Most of our dogs come via partner groups who help us save them from shelters where they are at risk of euthanasia in Tennessee, Los Angeles, and Puerto Rico. There are very few spay/neuter resources there and many more animals in those areas than there are people to adopt them. Rescue groups like ours bring them to the northeast where the desire for pets like these allows us to save their lives.
