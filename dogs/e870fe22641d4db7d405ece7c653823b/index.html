Meet Hercules!

Since I'm a long stay pup, my adoption fee has been reduced! Visit me today to find out my favorite treats and how you can take me home!

Hercules is a friendly and social dog who was abandoned by his previous owner and later surrendered to the shelter. He is a laid-back and affectionate dog once he feels comfortable but can be nervous in new situations. Hercules enjoys being around people and can be playful and wiggly when he bonds with someone.

My Ideal Home

Hercules would do best in an adult-only home with a calm, predictable environment. He needs a family that is patient and experienced with fearful or anxious dogs. He may display some nervousness or fear-based behaviors, so a home where he can adjust at his own pace will be ideal. His new family should be prepared to provide enrichment, structure, and positive reinforcement to help him manage his impulsivity and destructive tendencies.

Good with Kids?

No. Due to his fearfulness, especially around strangers, Hercules is better suited to a home without children under 13.

Good with Other Pets?

No. Hercules has not been tested with other animals but has shown fear-based behaviors (barking, growling, hiding) around cats. He would do best in a home with no cats or other pets.

Leash Manners

Hercules walks on a loose leash but can pull moderately. While he is generally easy to leash, he may be hesitant about collaring, and desensitization could help him feel more comfortable during walks.

Behavior Determination

Level 4. Hercules is recommended for an experienced adopter. His fearfulness and destructive behaviors, along with his poor impulse control, make him best suited for someone with experience working with anxious dogs. He will need a patient and dedicated adopter who can provide consistent training and care.

Other Fun Facts

Hercules is a sweet, affectionate dog who enjoys attention once he feels comfortable. He can be playful and social with those he bonds with, but he's also independent. He loves being near his humans, especially when treats are involved, and he seeks out attention on his own terms. Hercules is generally a calm dog but may need some extra time to adjust to his new home.
