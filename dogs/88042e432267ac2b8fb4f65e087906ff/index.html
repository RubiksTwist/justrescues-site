Description provided by NYC Second Chance Rescue

Wonder is a friendly, affectionate dog who plays with his whole body and has giant elf ears that always reveal what he's thinking. He has a great personality, gets along well with docile dogs who don't mind his pushy playstyle, and loves being showered with affection. He will proudly carry his toys down the street, as if showing off his prized posessions to the world. Some of his happiest moments are sitting outside, squeaking his toys or laying down to ask for belly rubs while he bakes in the sunshine. Wonder has a heart as big as his extra-large ears, and he isn't afraid to wear it on his sleeve. While in our care, Wonder has been very receptive to training and learning new skills. His loose-leash walking could still use some polishing up, but overall, he does very well walking in the city. He's been fine with all handling, even allowing us to put on jackets and clothes without a fuss.

We can't wait to find Wonder a great home. Adopt this big lovebug and kickstart the best friendship you'll ever have.

All dogs will be altered, microchipped, and receive age-appropriate vaccines prior to adoption.
