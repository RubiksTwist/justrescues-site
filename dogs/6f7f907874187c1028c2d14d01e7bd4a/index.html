Meet Dewey!

Sweet Dewey was found parvo-positive alongside her two siblings at a known dumpster site in Georgia. Despite such a heartbreaking start to life, Dewey kicked parvo's butt and her tail hasn't stopped wagging since!

Dewey is a happy girl with so much love to give. She's playful, affectionate, and gets along wonderfully with both dogs and cats. While she can be a little shy when meeting new faces, give her a moment and you'll quickly see her sweet, loyal personality shine through. She's the kind of dog who forms deep bonds and will be your biggest fan once she feels safe.

This goofy girl is full of personality and potential. She's sweet, smart, funny, and absolutely obsessed with playing fetch. Whether she's making you laugh with her silly antics or snuggling up after a day of fun, Dewey is sure to steal your heart.

Although she's currently in NYC, Dewey would do best in a home outside the city in a low-traffic neighborhood. A quieter environment will help her feel more relaxed, allowing her confidence to blossom and her wonderful personality to truly shine.

Dewey is ready for a forever home where she can be cherished, spoiled, and loved for the rest of her days. If you're looking for a joyful, loyal companion, Dewey might just be your perfect match.

Want to learn more? We'd love to tell you! Our bios are just a snapshot, so please reach out to our adoption team with any questions about this dog's personality, medical history, or ideal home. We'd be happy to help you determine if Dewey is the perfect match for your family.

All dogs will be altered, microchipped, and receive age-appropriate vaccines prior to adoption.
