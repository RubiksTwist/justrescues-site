Description provided by Humane Society of New York

Benoit (born: 12/12/2022), weighs 25 pounds and is a young French Bulldog who knows how to sit, loves to run and play, has fun with his toys and enjoys having a treat. Born with a congenital eye issue, Benoit had to have surgery to remove his right eye when he was just 10 weeks old and he continues to have follow-ups with an ophthalmologist for his left eye, which has limited vision, for life. Benoit also developed a head tilt and the neurologist diagnosed him with Hydrocephalus and prescribed Omeprazole – which is a medication he will have to take for life.

Benoit continues to see a Neurologist every 6 months – his last visit, the Neurologist requested Benoit see a specialty surgeon for further evaluation. Benoit was weaned off steroids, a cat scan and surgery were performed and Benoit is still taking antibiotics and he is doing well.

Benoit is a French Bulldog that will need to be checked by a neurologist and an ophthalmologist for the rest of his life.
