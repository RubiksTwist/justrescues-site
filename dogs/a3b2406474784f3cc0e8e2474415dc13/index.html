Description provided by Waggytail Rescue

Hermione is a sweet little grey kitten with a big purr and an even bigger love for humans! At 2 months old, she's a certified cuddle bug who loves being close to her people and soaking up all the affection she can get.

This curious and playful girl has plenty of favorite things. Her purple tunnel is at the top of the list, especially the dangling strings she can play with for ages! She's also a big fan of Friskies Shreds wet food and, of course, a warm, cozy heating pad for her afternoon naps.

Hermione currently lives in a home with two adult cats, another kitten, and a small dog, so she's familiar with a multi-pet household. She may need a little time and patience to warm up to a new feline friend, but with a proper introduction, she can settle in at her own pace.

A note for adopters: As part of our kitten adoption policy, we prioritize homes with a young, playful resident cat or families interested in adopting a pair of kittens for kittens under six months old. Hermione would love a home where she has a playful companion to grow up with!

If you're looking for a cuddly, curious little companion who will fill your home with purrs, Hermione might be your girl! 💜

She is up to date on shots, FeLV/FIV negative, dewormed, microchipped, and spayed.

Please apply using the following instructions if you are interested in adopting one of our Waggytails: Visit our website (www.waggytailrescue.org) for an adoption application. It is important to fill out an application as soon as possible for consideration, and we require a complete application for you to meet an animal. We are currently unable to respond to general email inquiries as an all-volunteer run rescue. If the cat is on our website, they are still available.

Waggytail Rescue CANNOT guarantee the exact age of any adoptable cat, or final size of any of our adoptable kittens. We rely on the information provided to us by veterinarians, shelters, owner surrenders and other circumstances bringing the cat into our rescue.
