Another foster writes:
If I could describe Flax in a few words, they would be loving, well-mannered, gentle, and absolutely amazing. He is truly one of those dogs who leaves a lasting impression on your heart.

Flax is incredibly well-behaved, fully house-trained, and such a gentleman. Not once did he have an accident in the house. He sleeps peacefully through the night, eats very well, and listens beautifully to commands. Tell him to sit, and he'll happily do so! He loves his treats, enjoys his walks, and absolutely loves to cuddle.

He is wonderful with children and was especially gentle and well-behaved around my nephew. He is respectful of the home, too. I never had to worry about leaving things around because he doesn't chew, destroy, or nibble on household items. He also doesn't jump on furniture and is very good about respecting boundaries.

Flax does well around other dogs and people. When passing another dog, he may stop and observe for a moment, but he remains calm and goes right back to sniffing or whatever he was doing. He also got along with my small dog, although he sometimes forgot just how much bigger he was and wanted to play!

He was equally amazing outside the home. He did beautifully on the train, was great during car rides, and handled new surroundings so well. He is observant, gentle, affectionate, and simply a pleasure to have around.

Honestly, there aren't enough words to describe how special this boy is. Saying goodbye and returning him to the shelter has been incredibly heartbreaking because he has been such an absolute joy. My greatest hope is that the next person who meets him sees what I saw: a wonderful dog with so much love to give.

Flax deserves more than just a home. He deserves a family who will love him, cherish him, and make him part of their lives forever. Whoever adopts him will be incredibly lucky.

My Foster writes: As Flax's straycation foster, I officially crown him as "Unbothered King of NYC"! During Flax's time with me, he was mistaken for a puppy so many times I lost count. He was so easy to love and offers the best of both worlds, looking like a puppy and having the easygoing manners of an adult.
Flax was friendly and gentle with everyone we met, including men, women, and children. He happily accepted pets and treats, and if someone was not interested in saying hello, he simply moved along. He showed interest in meeting other dogs and was nonreactive, even when they barked or reacted to him. He was curious about the world around him, but remains calm, even on busy streets. I was impressed by how easily he carried on with his day and did not get fixated on anything.
City life was also no big deal. Blaring sirens, rumbling trucks and buses, trash blowing down the sidewalk, and scary Halloween decorations barely fazed Flax. While he enjoyed his sniffaris and walked nicely on leash, he was also happy to spend downtime lounging and sleeping near his humans. While he was food and treat motivated, he never begged or barked with me.
Flax would thrive on routine and predictability, and with continued structure, he can learn his humans will always come back. Flax is ready to find his forever family, share all the love he has to give, and deserves to be chosen. If you are looking for a sweet, easygoing companion for city adventures, cozy nights in, and everything in between, Flax is your perfect match!

Meet Flax!
At 5 years old, Flax is a handsome, treat loving sweetheart who's ready for his happily ever after! Found as a stray, he's hoping to trade shelter life for cozy naps, leisurely walks, and a family to call his own. This social boy loves meeting people, happily approaches for treats, and has already shown us that he's a playful friend to other dogs. Flax is ready to find a home where he can feel cherished and loved every day!

My Ideal Home
Flax would love a home where he can enjoy daily walks, playtime, and plenty of quality time with his favorite people. He's an enthusiastic guy who will benefit from fun training sessions, food puzzles, long lasting chews, and games that keep his mind busy. He's also a big fan of treats, so you'll have a great way to help him learn new things while building a bond. Just be ready for plenty of happy greetings along the way!

Good with Other Pets?
Flax recently met another dog at ACC and showed off his playful side with bouncy play bows, happy circles, and lots of back and forth fun. He was comfortable taking breaks and letting the other dog sniff him, too. Since his history with other pets is unknown, slow and thoughtful introductions are still recommended, but Flax has given us some lovely glimpses of his potential as a canine companion.

Good with Kids?
Flax's history with children is unknown. His social personality and love of treats make him a fun guy to get to know, and calm, respectful introductions will help everyone start off on the right paw.

Things to Know
Flax walks with only mild pulling and was a lovely walking buddy during his Boroughbreaker outing, enjoying a long walk without barking. He can get excited and jump up when he's hoping for treats, so positive reinforcement will help him practice polite greetings. He's also working on gaining a little weight, and luckily, mealtime and treats are right up his alley! Flax is a whole lot of sweetness wrapped up in one handsome package, and he's ready to find the family who will adore him.
