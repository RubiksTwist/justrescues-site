Meet Claire!
Claire is a sweet, gentle girl who may take a little time to warm up, but once she does, she'll happily enjoy some pets and tasty treats from her favorite people. She was found as a stray, so we're still learning about her past, but this pretty girl is ready for a fresh start with someone who will give her the time and love she deserves.

My Ideal Home
Claire is looking for a peaceful home where she can relax, settle in, and take things one paw at a time. Her perfect person will be patient and kind, letting Claire set the pace as she gets comfortable with her new surroundings. With lots of encouragement, yummy treats, and a cozy routine, she may just blossom into a wonderful little companion.

Good with Other Pets?
Claire came into care with another dog, although we don't know much about their relationship. She has seemed a little unsure around another dog here, so any new introductions should be calm and gradual, with plenty of time for Claire to feel comfortable.

Good with Kids?
Claire would love an adult-only home where things can stay nice and calm while she settles in. She's a sensitive girl who will appreciate having her own space and the freedom to come say hello when she's ready.

Things to Know
Claire may be a little reserved at first, but once she feels safe, she is happy to accept gentle affection and spend time with her people. She walks nicely with only mild pulling and follows along with a soft, relaxed body. This lovely girl just needs someone willing to give her a little time to come out of her shell, and she could be a very sweet addition to the right home.
