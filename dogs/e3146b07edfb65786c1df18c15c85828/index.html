Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

The first thing you'll notice about Smokey Joe is his soulful eyes. And if those eyes could tell a story, they would tell you about a boy who has been waiting a very, very long time for someone to call his own.

Smokey is now 13 years old and has been at ARF since 2015. For more than a decade, he has watched other dogs come and go, while he continues to wait for his turn. But despite all those years, Smokey still has so much love left to give.

He has an incredible memory and never forgets the people who have taken the time to earn his trust. Once you're his person, you're his person. He forms deep, meaningful bonds, and his devotion is something truly special. He may not be the dog who runs up to greet everyone he meets, but the love he gives to those he trusts is worth every bit of patience it takes to get there.

At this stage in his life, Smokey isn't looking for a big adventure. He's looking for a soft place to land. A calm, quiet, adult-only home where he can enjoy his golden years surrounded by familiar faces, a predictable routine, cozy naps, and the comfort of finally belonging somewhere.

Smokey will need to be the only pet in the home, where he can enjoy all the love, attention, and space to himself. He will also need adopters willing to invest in at least three visits before bringing him home. Smokey can be wary of strangers, and building trust takes time. Our wonderful trainer and staff will be there every step of the way to help make the transition successful.

We know Smokey isn't going to be the right fit for every home. But somewhere out there is someone who will see past his age, understand his needs, and realize that an older dog still has so much love to give.

After spending so many years waiting, Smokey deserves to know what it's like to wake up in a home of his own. He deserves a family, a favorite spot on the couch, and someone who promises he'll never have to wonder where he belongs again.

He's been waiting since 2015. Let's not make him wait any longer.
