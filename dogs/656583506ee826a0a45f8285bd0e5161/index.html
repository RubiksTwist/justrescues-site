Description provided by PupStarz Rescue

Meet Lemon Drop – A Shy but Sweet Little Gem! Lemon Drop is an 2 year 7pound stunning black kitty with a quiet charm. She may be shy at first, but underneath that reserved exterior is a gentle soul who just needs a little time to feel at home. She’s never aggressive—just independent—and she prefers to observe the world from her favorite sunny perch on the cat tree. While she’s not the biggest fan of cuddles, Lemon Drop secretly enjoys pets when gently encouraged. She absolutely adores the company of other cats, especially her best buddy Tomato Soup, and can often be found playing, wrestling, or curling up with a feline friend. She’s also completely unbothered by dogs! Lemon Drop would thrive in a patient home, ideally with another confident cat to show her the ropes. She’s a low-maintenance, laid-back girl who will bring quiet joy to the right family. Could you be the one to help her blossom?

If you would like to give this wonderful cat a home, please email rescue@pupstarzrescue.org or visit www.pupstarzrescue.org for an application!
