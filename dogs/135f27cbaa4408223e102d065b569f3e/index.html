Description provided by AMA Animal Rescue

Meet Pooch, the giant with a heart of gold and a face that’s pure mush! This lovable Cane Corso hails from an AKC champion bloodline, descending from generations of superb, Cane Corsos known for their strength, composure, and noble temperament. His lineage is one of excellence, Pooch carries the legacy of greatness with every proud, gentle step.

This handsome boy stands tall and proud, and his regal stature reflects the championship heritage he comes from. But don’t let his impressive frame and noble background fool you-he’s a big sweetie at heart. With a face so soft and mushy, you'll want to cuddle him all day long.

Pooch isn’t exactly a bundle of energy, but that’s part of his charm. His laid-back personality and calm demeanor make him the perfect napping companion. When it's time for a walk, he moves with grace and confidence, strutting like the gentleman he truly is.

A mellow fellow, Pooch gets along well with confident (but non-dominant) male dogs, and wouldn’t mind sharing his space with a canine friend. His idea of a perfect day? A cozy nap, a leisurely stroll, and some quality time with his people-perhaps with a regal head resting on your lap.

If you're looking for a loyal, noble-hearted couch companion to bring quiet strength and endless snuggles into your life, Pooch might just be your new best friend.
