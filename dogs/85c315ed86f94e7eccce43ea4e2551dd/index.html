Meet Marisol! Marisol is still getting settled in, so we’re just beginning to learn who they are. Right now, everything is new—new people, new sounds, new routines—and we’re giving them time to relax, decompress, and show us their true personality at their own pace. Here’s what we know so far about her time in foster care: https://bestfriends.sharepoint.com/:b:/s/GRP_NYC_Foster/IQDt-a9WcEPuTbbVQvZ4hmsaAUJGBCYmXI2V2T_bBAZSKjE?e=hFZ39R Her foster says "Marisol has been an absolute dream to foster, and I absolutely adore her. Whoever adopts this sweet girl will be so lucky to have her. We bonded within hours, and from day one she seemed to love the peace and quiet of a home. During her first 48 hours, she was already keeping me company while I studied, spending hours curled up on a little self-heating mat beside my laptop and following me around. As she settled in, we developed the sweetest little routines. If I was on the couch, she was right there beside me. At bedtime, she slept next to me and rested peacefully through the night. By the end of her stay, she felt like my little companion in everything: affectionate, easy to be around, and happy simply being close without needing constant attention. She makes ordinary moments at home feel so much sweeter. She gives lovely headbutts, rubs against your legs, and loves head scratches. She also lets you know playfully when she has had enough. One of my favorite things is her welcome home: she comes straight to the door to greet you and rolls onto her back. It is such a lovely feeling to have her waiting for you. She was also wonderfully social when someone new came over, and it was lovely to see that affectionate side of her with visitors too." . She is a social girl and very loving. She has been spayed, vaccinated, microchipped, and has had preventative treatments to ensure she is ready for her next steps.
