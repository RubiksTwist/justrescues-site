Description provided by NYC Second Chance Rescue

DOG OF THE WEEK: ASUKA IS WAITING FOR YOU! If you love a girl with a little muscle, a lot of personality, and just the right amount of sass, you need to meet Asuka! At 60 pounds, this beautiful, athletic girl is quite the package. She's smart, playful, affectionate, and has a hilarious way of making her opinions known. Whether she's showing off on the treadmill, tossing her favorite toy around, or giving you those big puppy eyes for just one more treat, Asuka knows how to steal the spotlight! She's also a quick learner who knows her commands, walks nicely on leash, comes when called, and settles beautifully in her crate. She loves having a job, staying active, and spending time with people she trusts. Asuka would do well with a compatible canine companion or as the queen of her own castle. She'll need an active family who can provide structure, continued guidance, and help her channel all that enthusiasm. New situations may take a little patience, but once she feels comfortable, her wonderful personality really shines. This girl has so much going for her. She's not looking for a perfect family, just one willing to understand her, keep her engaged, and love her for exactly who she is. She's got the looks, the brains, and the personality. All she's missing is YOU! Ready to meet your new best friend? Reach out to our Adoption Team and ask about Asuka!
