Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

Ochoa is one of the best pups I've had the chance to spend time with. He was slightly energetic when we first met, but he quickly settled in and became comfortable lounging around, whether he was watching TV with my roommates or sleeping in my bed. Ochoa loves to follow you wherever you go, but he's also very patient. He'll happily wait by the bathroom door until you're finished, just so he can continue following you around afterward. Ochoa would do great in a laid-back home that also enjoys throwing toys for him to fetch and giving him plenty of treats. One of his funniest quirks is that after he pees or poops, he'll kick his back legs and then look at you proudly, as if he thinks he's successfully buried his poop and you no longer have to pick it up. Unfortunately for him, he's wrong! No matter what he was doing, my roommates and I loved watching Ochoa prance around the room. He's such a fun, sweet pup and would make a wonderful addition to a loving home.
