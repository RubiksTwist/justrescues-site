Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Chance is one of those dogs who wins people over the second they meet him. He’s friendly with everyone, loves attention, and is always ready for snuggles. Whether he’s out on a walk or relaxing, he brings an easygoing, joyful energy to every moment. He enjoys long strolls and likes to take his time, often stopping to take in the sights. If there’s a bench along the way, you can count on him hopping up and waiting for you to sit beside him—it’s his favorite way to sneak in some cuddle time! Chance is playful, but he can also get a little overstimulated when he’s really excited. He does best with calm guidance and structure, and he’s great at matching the energy of the people around him. Chance would prefer to be the only pet in the home—he’s got a lot of love to give and wants to keep all the snuggles for himself! He’s currently looking for a home with someone who can keep a close eye on him. He has a curious streak (he once decided a blanket looked like a snack), so he needs a safe space where someone can help him stay out of trouble. He’s healthy now, but because of that past incident, extra care is important for him moving forward. Chance needs someone patient, kind, and ready to help him enjoy life safely. He truly is a special dog, and if you take a “Chance” on him, you’ll be gaining a loving, loyal friend who’ll make every day better.
