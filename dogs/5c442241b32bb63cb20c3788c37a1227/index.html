Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

Waylon is a 4-month-old puppy who may be a little shy when you first meet him, but give him just a little time and you’ll see his sweet personality shine through. Once he warms up, he is the absolute sweetest boy!

This playful pup loves his toys and is always ready for a good game. He especially loves playing with his much bigger foster brother and will frequently jump all over him to initiate a wrestling session — he definitely doesn’t let the size difference stop him!

Waylon is also very food motivated, which has been a huge help with his training. He’s a quick learner and is always eager to earn a treat. In fact, if he hears a wrapper open from anywhere in the house, you can pretty much guarantee he’ll come running to investigate.

He’s already made great progress with his manners and has gotten the hang of potty training, which is a huge accomplishment for such a young puppy!

Waylon is a sweet, playful, food-loving little guy who is learning more every day.
