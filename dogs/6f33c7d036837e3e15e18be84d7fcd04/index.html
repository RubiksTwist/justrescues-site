Ford arrived at MACC as a stray with no known history. While in care, Ford has been observed to have poor impulse control. Recently it is also noted that Ford has a low threshold for arousal. Noted to jump up, mount, and mouth a handler while on leash. Due to the detailed behavior, we recommend an experienced adult only home.

Meet Ford!
At just 1 year old, Ford is a social, energetic guy who's ready to hit the road with his new family! Found as a stray, this handsome youngster has already shown us that he loves meeting people, getting attention, and being part of the action. He's always happy to bounce over for a hello, especially if you happen to have some treats. Ford is ready to leave the shelter behind and start his next adventure!

My Ideal Home
Ford would love an active home where he can enjoy daily walks, playtime, and plenty of opportunities to explore. He's a young guy with lots of energy, so food puzzles, long lasting chews, and fun training games will help keep his mind busy. He'll also benefit from positive reinforcement training to practice polite greetings and show off all the new skills he's sure to learn.

Good with Other Pets?
Ford's history with other animals is unknown since he came to us as a stray. He showed no reaction to other dogs while walking at ACC, but slow and thoughtful introductions with any resident pets are still recommended.

Good with Kids?
Ford would do best in a home without children. He's an enthusiastic young guy who loves jumping up to greet people, so an older household will be a better fit while he works on his manners.

Things to Know
Ford walks with only mild pulling, but he can get especially excited when greeting people and may jump up for attention. He's happy to accept handling and loves approaching people, especially when treats are involved. Practicing cues like Sit and Off with positive reinforcement will help him learn to keep all four paws on the floor. He may need a little time and encouragement when adjusting to new surroundings, but this sweet young guy is ready to shift gears and cruise into his forever home!
