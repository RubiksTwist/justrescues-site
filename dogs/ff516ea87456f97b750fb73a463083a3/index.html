Description provided by AMA Animal Rescue

They say that eyes are the windows to the soul, and one look into Milly's enchanting eyes will tell you she has a soul that's sweeter than any treat!

We found Milly in a box on our shelter's doorstep after she was abandoned there overnight along with another cat. We've quickly discovered that Milly is a giant, loving furball who likes to play and explore! She's a big girl, but that doesn't stop her from returning to her kitten roots and playing hide-and-seek by squeezing into whatever nooks and crannies she can! She is curious and adventurous.

For Milly, a purrfect afternoon would consist of loafing at her favorite spot near the window and birdwatching on a clear, sunny day. She'll strike some yoga poses here and there to stretch her limbs out after she's done. Then, she'll prowl around the house and see what her favorite human is up to, hoping to get a few chin scratches and back rubs. She might hop onto your lap so she can be face-to-face with you and nuzzle your nose during a cuddle session!

If you're feline earnest in having Milly's gentle presence in your home, please fill out and submit an adoption application today! Millie is waiting for her forever home.
