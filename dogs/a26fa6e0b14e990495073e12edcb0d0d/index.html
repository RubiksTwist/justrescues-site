Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

If family friendly, dog friendly, and well-rounded are a part of your adoption criteria — look no further.

Shanghai is a 45 lb, 3-year-old girl whose personality is even more breathtaking than her smile. She has the sweetest temperament, the most loving demeanor, and a truly rare, unequivocal purity for life.

Shanghai is the epitome of what it means to have a good friend in your corner, and she is the definition of genuine. She's a friend to all humans and other dogs, while also being able to bond deeply with the people who hold a special place in her heart. She has an almost unearthly appreciation for the simple things in life — like claiming the middle of the bed so she can press up against her person for a snuggle, or snoozing in a little later the first night she sleeps on a new comfy blanket.

Outside of her wonderfully human-like companionship, Shanghai is simply a good dog. She's fully crate trained and happy to hang in her space anytime throughout the day — even during a long work day! Shanghai loves puzzles and treats, but most of all she loves her toys. She'll give you a glimpse of her puppy past when she gets a new toy to chew, all while remembering she's a big girl and never destroying them. When she isn't cozied up next to her person or enjoying enrichment, her even-keeled nature pops right back in and you'll find her snoozing the day away.

Shanghai is the kind of dog who's content to just hang on a bench or a stoop with her person and watch the world go by. More than anything, it's been proven time and again that Shanghai blossoms around a furry friend — or a whole pack of them!

Shanghai is fully housetrained, walks well on a leash, and may just remind her walker to take a second to breathe in the world around them. She loves to train for treats on walks, and there's no greater joy than watching her free-run in enclosed areas where she can truly let loose. She can be eager on her harness and leash when something startles her, so a calm, patient handler and a well-fitted harness help her feel secure — and she settles quickly once she knows she's safe.

Shanghai LOVES other dogs and is an enthusiastic playmate. She's spent time with both small dogs and senior dogs, proving herself wonderful at both appropriate play and gracefully taking corrections. She'd love a social circle of friends, and we think she'd even enjoy time at a doggy daycare if her forever home is aligned with the idea!

Like many sensitive souls, Shanghai is still building her confidence around new men, especially inside the home — and she's already making beautiful progress. She responds best to people who let her set the pace and reward her with patience and calm. For the right adopter, watching Shanghai's trust bloom is one of the most rewarding journeys there is. She has never once sought out physical contact when nervous; she simply asks for a little time and reassurance, and she gives it all back tenfold.

Shanghai is extremely smart, having mastered sit, come, leave it, and okay. The "okay" command is her cue that she's allowed to exit her crate or leave her designated place! If you're in the market for a well-rounded, social girl who values the intimacy of relationship-building and is as devoted as they come — do not hesitate to apply for Shanghai.
