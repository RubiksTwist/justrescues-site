Meet Fashion Week!

Found as a stray, Fashion Week is a social girl who knows how to make an entrance! She explored the room with a loose body, happily solicited attention, and readily came over to meet her handler. She also showed a serious interest in playing with a high value ball, so this stylish girl is ready to make her debut in a forever home!

My Ideal Home

Fashion Week will love an active family who can keep her body moving and her mind engaged. Daily walks, playtime, food puzzles, long lasting chews, and fun games like hide and seek will help her thrive. She may be a little fashion forward, but when it comes to her new home, she'll be looking for a calm and patient family who lets her set the pace while she settles in.

Good with Other Pets?

Fashion Week's history with other animals is unknown, so we're still getting to know her around canine friends. Any future introductions should be slow, thoughtful, and supervised, giving her plenty of time to feel comfortable. With the right approach, she can take things one step at a time and find her own comfort zone.

Good with Kids?

Fashion Week would do best in a home without children under 5. She can be a little sensitive when she is unsure, so older children who understand how to give a dog space and let her approach on her own terms may be a better fit.

Things to Know

Fashion Week walks beautifully without pulling and has a playful side, especially when there's a ball involved! She can be sensitive to handling and unfamiliar situations, so she'll appreciate a calm, quiet start and plenty of time to acclimate to her new surroundings. Let her choose when she's ready for interaction, and this fashionable girl will be ready to strut her way into your heart!
