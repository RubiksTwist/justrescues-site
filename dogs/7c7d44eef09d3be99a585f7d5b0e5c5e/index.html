My foster writes: SPICE - SPUNKY PUPPY LOOKING FOR HIS FOREVER HOME

Backstory/Journey

After being brought in abandoned last winter, Spice has spent more than a quarter of his young life in the shelter waiting for a forever home of his own. His demeanor is that of a puppy younger than his stated two years; he is spunky, curious and very smart. In a home with previous dog experience, who can offer love, patience and security, he will thrive. In choosing Spice - you not only change his life, you gain an extraordinarily loyal companion ready to give you his whole heart.

Potty Trained?...Yes.

Toy Guarding?...Slight.

Dog Reactive?...Yes.

Personality & Energy Level

As of November 2026, Spice has spent two months in full-time foster care and is bravely adjusting to city life. He is most at ease in suburban settings, as observed during weekends in New Jersey. This slower, more quiet environment is recommended for him; the daily city life makes him skittish, but he continues to explore with bravery!

Spice is goofy and playful and has moderate energy level. Belly rubs are his standard, and he loves car rides and looking out the window! His zoomies are adorable and he is very affectionate (touch is his love language!). Spice can be slightly fearful/skittish in new environments but this is weaning over time. He is alert to sounds (great home protector!) but is also very quiet.

Social Skills: Kids & Dogs

It is unknown whether Spice has previously lived with children, so introductions should be gradual and supervised. He has demonstrated a good demeanor around elementary-aged children who have prior experience with dogs. With dogs, Spice is reactive particularly in busy environments. His leash skill can be moderate to severe pulling so we keep distance to ensure plenty of space for all. He responds well to verbal commands like: "no" and "leave it". In suburban environments, he demonstrated well on his leash with mild pulling typically reflective of his excitement to go for walks. Spice has been described as "mouthy." Mouthing is a natural way for puppies to explore their environment. In adult dogs, it may result from insufficient exercise, limited early training around appropriate play boundaries, or heightened stress associated with abandonment or shelter life.

Home Time & Separation

Spice does best with his people nearby, ideally in a work-from-home household. He may wait by the door while alone, but with time and consistency, he is becoming more trusting (and puppy-cam check-ins help immensely!). Because he fears and escapes pop-up crates, puppy-proofing is essential. He chooses to nap freely in and sleeps through the night in a partially covered crate.

Diet, Health, Exercise & Wellness

Spice is treat driven (great for training) but won't hesitate to spit out something he doesn't like (funny boy)! He loves water. Spice came in with a previous injury on his hind leg(s) so walks (after he goes potty) are kept slow/steady and large volumes of stairs avoided - current information can be discussed with his ACC team.

Spice is an amazing puppy and will thrive in a loving, consistent suburban home that provides: daily walks and regular exercise, positive, fear-free training, clear and consistent play boundaries, developmentally appropriate enrichment.

Meet Spice!

This lively, affectionate boy is full of personality and love, and he's ready for a home that can keep up with him and help him feel secure.

My Ideal Home

Spice would do best in an active, adult-only home that can give him plenty of exercise, structure, and companionship. He prefers not to be alone for long and thrives when he has his people nearby.

Good with Kids?

No children under 13, as Spice can become overexcited and benefits from an adult-only home.

Things to Know

Spice is a high-energy, affectionate dog who loves attention and will happily jump in for pets, playtime, or even a cuddle in your lap. He can get a little overexcited at times and will benefit from continued training to build calm behaviors and impulse control. He prefers company and may struggle when left alone for long periods, so enrichment, routine, and companionship are key. He also enjoys his toys and does best with positive "trade" games to keep things fun and safe.
