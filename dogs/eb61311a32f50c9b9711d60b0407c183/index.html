Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Lentil is a 2 year old Shepherd mix who weighs around 29 lbs. This sweet and curious girl has the cutest way of playing, pouncing on her toys with an enthusiasm that's impossible not to smile at! She's also a big fan of people watching from the window. No barking or commentary from this girl, she just likes to keep up with what's happening in the neighborhood.

Lentil is a little shy when meeting new people, but treats are definitely the way to her heart! She's very food motivated and will happily accept a snack even from someone she just met. It only took her about 24 hours to feel comfortable with her foster family and start showing her playful personality. She's curious about other dogs and is still getting familiar with city life. She'd love an adopter who will give her a little time to settle in and discover just how sweet and fun she is!

Registered adopters interested in Lentil can email adopt@hbrdogs.com for more info. If you're not registered yet, do it today at hbrdogs.com!
