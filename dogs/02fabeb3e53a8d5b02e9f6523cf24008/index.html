Philly Cheesesteak is a 2-year-old bulldog mix who weighs around 46 lbs and is basically the perfect mix of playful and relaxed. She has plenty of energy for walks, adventures, and playtime, but she also knows how to settle in for a very serious nap afterward.

She loves people, loves other dogs, and is happiest when she gets to be close to her humans. She’s curious, affectionate, great on leash, crate-trained, and fully house-trained. Philly Cheesesteak is the kind of dog who wants to be part of your everyday life, whether that means heading out for a walk or curling up nearby while you relax.

She’s sweet, social, and a total lover with an easygoing side that makes her pretty hard not to adore.
