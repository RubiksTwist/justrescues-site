Meet Rudy

Rudy is a little fighter who has already been through more than any puppy should.

Rudy came to us from Georgia while he was struggling with parvo, a serious and heartbreaking illness that can be especially dangerous for young puppies. He was fighting to get through each day, and all we wanted was to give this sweet boy the chance he deserved.

Rudy fought hard.

Now, we want to make sure that all of that fighting leads somewhere beautiful. We want Rudy to know what it feels like to simply be a puppy. To have a family who loves him, a soft place to sleep, toys to play with, and people who will be there for him through all of life's little adventures.

He has already shown us just how strong he can be. Now he needs someone who will show him how wonderful life can be.

Please adopt Rudy. Give this little fighter the forever home he has worked so hard to reach. He deserves a family who will cherish him, love him, and make sure the rest of his story is filled with happiness.
