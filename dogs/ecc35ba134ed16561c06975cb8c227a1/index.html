HISTORY

Dolly PAWton was at a small shelter in Texas, at risk of euthanasia, when our team received an urgent call to help. She traveled to Brooklyn in mid-September 2026.

DETAILED PROFILE

Dolly PAWton has a signature walk: a tidy little prance, perfected to a science, with a hop over every sidewalk ledge for good measure. She's a small, gentle lady with moderate energy and a curious streak. Put her somewhere new and she'll investigate every corner rather than hide under a blanket.

She's still settling in, but early signs point to an easygoing, low-drama companion. With new people she's calm and a little shy, happy to accept pets without demanding them. She's been comfortable around kids, though they'll need to be gentle with a dog her size.

Her social life is a highlight. She shares her foster home with a young puppy, and the two love to race to the door and chase each other around. She's lived with cats and is largely unbothered by them. Big, bouncy dogs can be a lot for her, so playmates closer to her size or energy are a better fit.

On leash she's a star. She enjoys a casual, easy-paced walks. She's working on house training and on daytime crate time (nights in the crate are already no problem).

And then there are snacks. Dolly will jump for food, and within minutes of arriving she had located the puppy's peanut butter jar. Pantry doors in her next home should plan accordingly.
