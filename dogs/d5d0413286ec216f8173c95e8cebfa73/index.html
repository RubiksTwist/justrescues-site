Description provided by NYC Second Chance Rescue

Xena Bloom is a true survivor with a warrior’s heart. This sweet girl was found as a stray in North Carolina after being hit by a car and left for dead. Against all odds, Xena held on, and through it all, she was never alone. Her sister, Ivy, stayed faithfully by her side, guarding her and refusing to leave.

Despite everything she’s endured, Xena Bloom is gentle, loving, and full of quiet strength. All she wants now is safety, kindness, and a family who will love her the way she’s always deserved.

Xena is ready for a home where she never has to fear being hurt again; where meals are guaranteed, beds are soft, and love is unconditional. She’s done surviving. Now it’s time for her to live.

All dogs will be altered, microchipped, and receive age-appropriate vaccines prior to adoption.
