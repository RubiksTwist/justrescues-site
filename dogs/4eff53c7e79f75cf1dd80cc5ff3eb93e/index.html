Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

"Hi, I'm Coconut, can I please give you a hug?! Hugs are my favorite. I'm a big-hearted goofball and I love everyone that I meet! Please be my forever best friend!!" — Coconut

Coco is just an absolute gem. Lovable, cuddly, and sweet—everyone she meets immediately loves her and people will stop me on the street just to say hi. She loves long walks and exploring her neighborhood (sometimes hard to get her to come back inside, but once she’s tired out and in she turns into a complete couch potato!). She really loves the outdoors, whether its rolling around in the grass or just people watching in the park, Coconut could spend her entire day outside. She's also a big fan of belly rubs, a nice bone to chew or a toy to chase around and maybe even shred if she's lucky. She behaves very well on walks, with some light pulling which has improved a ton with her double leash. She also generally does not get distracted by other dogs or noises on walks (but is always on the lookout for squirrels!). Coconut is a good listener, a best friend and a fun-loving spirit. I really can’t say enough good things about this girl. Coco will thrive in a home with a steady routine, access to outdoor space, and a lot of love and laughs!

﻿Coconut is approximately 1y 7m 18d old and is fully grown at 74 pounds. They are microchipped and up to date on all age-appropriate medical care. Their breed is unknown as we have not had a DNA test done.

Although our dogs are listed as unknown breed, buildings with specific dog restrictions typically enforce their rules based on what breeds a dog appears to resemble. It's always important to keep that in mind and know your building's policies before adopting a dog.
