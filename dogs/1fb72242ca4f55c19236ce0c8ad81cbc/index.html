Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

My foster writes: Head bumps are Lily's love language. She is affectionate but seems sensitive to some touch-while she is lovely company, she needs someone willing to read her cues with utmost care. Slow movement, light, gentle touch, and patience have begun to draw her personality out into the sunshine. She is a decent conversationalist and oddly adept at articulating what she wants. She follows her foster around the house (readily bumping against their legs while they prepare her dinner) and likes to settle in close, often pressed against her foster at bedtime. She loves to chase string and watch birds out the window. She makes fresh biscuits every morning and sometimes before bed. She is calm and stately, only getting excited when the strings come out. She is a lovely little sidekick and absolutely beautiful. Last but not least, she is a purr machine!
