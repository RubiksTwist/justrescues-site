Description provided by Rescue City

Madison is a 5 Month Old cutie who is so patiently waiting for her forever home. This smart girl LOVES fetch, chewing on carrots, solving treat puzzles, and rearranging blankets until her nap spot is just right. And when she decides she needs a little extra attention? She’ll politely request belly rubs. Obviously. For a puppy, Madison is a pretty easygoing, medium energy girl. She gets her bursts of playtime and then is more than happy to curl up next to her person for a snooze. She’s patient, crate trained, qnd loves to explore outside. And did we mention how SMART she is? . She already knows come, sit, down AND spin, is doing great with leash training, and is working on stay! Madison is a sensitive girl who can be a little unsure in busy environments or around loud noises when overstimulated; but she’s learning every day. She looks to her person for reassurance and has been gaining confidence with every new outing. She’s happiest when she knows she has someone safe by her side. Her forever home could include an older, relaxed dog to keep her company and show her the ropes, but she’s also been doing wonderfully as the only dog.

Madison is sweet, smart, snuggly, a little sensitive, and very ready to find the person who looks at those pretty eyes and says, “Yep, you’re coming home with me.”
