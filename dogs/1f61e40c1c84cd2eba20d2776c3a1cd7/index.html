Description provided by Rescue City

Madison is a 20 lb, 3.5 month old sensitive angel princess and a professional cozy girl.

She LOVES fetch, chewing on carrots, solving treat puzzles, and rearranging blankets until her nap spot is just right. And when she wants some love? She’ll politely request belly rubs.

For a puppy, Madison has a pretty easygoing medium energy level. She gets her bursts of playtime and then is more than happy to settle down next to her person for a snooze. She’s patient, never mouthy, crate trained, doing amazing with wee wee pads, and has started working on pottying outside.

Did we mention how smart she is? She already knows come, sit, down, and spin, is doing great with leash training, and is working on stay!

Madison can be sensitive to busy environments and loud noises but is learning, looks to her person for reassurance, and has been gaining confidence with every new outing. She’s happiest when she knows she has someone safe by her side.

Her ideal home could include an older, relaxed dog to keep her company and learn the ropes from, although she’s also been doing wonderfully as the only dog.

Madison is ready to find her forever family. Apply to adopt at RescueCity.nyc/adopt.
