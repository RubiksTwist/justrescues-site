Description provided by Rescue City

Madison is 7 months old and has been busy becoming the BEST dog in her foster home. 37 lbs, smart, so much fun, and has the funniest personality. Don’t let the Husky part fool you! Madison has a great balance of energy. She loves fetch, tug, peanut butter, and long neighborhood walks where every sidewalk must be sniffed and every shop investigated. She loves to take up the couch or lay on top of you, in some ridiculous position, including flat on her back like a little rotisserie chicken. And while she’s a pretty independent girlie, she LOVES her people. Her favorite ways to show affection is climbing right into your lap and covering you in kisses. If a stranger stops to say hi on a walk, she becomes goes into full body wiggles. She already knows an impressive number of commands, is still working through some puppy manners and is also sensitive to some handling. She needs a patient family who will respect her boundaries.

We’d LOVE to see her find her people before Thanksgiving. She is vaccinated.+ spayed, and can be adopted / or a foster to adopt!
