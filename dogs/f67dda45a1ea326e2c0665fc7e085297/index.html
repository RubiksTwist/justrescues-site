Meet Miley!
Miley is a sweet, gentle girl who may be a little shy when meeting someone new, but there's a very loving side to this pretty pup. Her former family says she has lived with both dogs and cats before, and during her time at ACC she's shown us that she enjoys gentle affection, even leaning in for pets and belly rubs. She's looking for someone who will give her a little time to settle in and help her feel safe and loved again.

My Ideal Home
Miley would love a calm home where she can settle into a comfortable routine and take things at her own pace. She's a medium-energy girl who will enjoy daily walks, enrichment, and plenty of quiet time with her people. A patient family who lets her build confidence gradually will help this sweet girl come out of her shell.

Good with Other Pets?
Miley has lived with both dogs and cats before, according to her former family, and she showed no response to dogs while walking at ACC. ACC recommends slow, careful introductions between Miley and any resident pets to help everyone get comfortable. We also offer two-week trial adoptions, giving families time to make sure everyone is a good fit at home.

Good with Kids?
Miley would do best in an adult-only home where things can stay calm and predictable while she settles in. She's a sensitive girl who appreciates gentle interactions and the freedom to come say hello when she's ready.

Things to Know
Miley can be a little reserved at first, but she has a sweet way of warming up once she feels comfortable. She may need some coaxing on walks and when meeting new people, but she's happy to lean into affection once she feels safe. With patience, positive reinforcement, and a little time, this lovely girl is ready to become someone's very best friend.
