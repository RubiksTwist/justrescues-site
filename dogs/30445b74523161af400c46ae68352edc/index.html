Meet Stack!

Stack was found tied up in front of a precinct with another dog. He's an independent guy who is still learning that the world and new people are safe, and he's looking for a calm, patient home to help him come out of his shell.

My Ideal Home

Stack would love a quiet, adult only home where he can settle in at his own pace. He enjoys getting out for walks and sniffing around, and he'll do best with a family that gives him time, structure, and lots of positive reinforcement as he builds confidence. Once he feels comfortable, his gentle, curious side starts to shine.

Good with Other Pets?

Stack showed brief, polite interest in another dog at the care center, but became tense during the interaction and was moved away for his comfort. His history with other animals is unknown, so any introductions to resident dogs should be slow and thoughtful. He may be happiest as the only pet in the home.

Good with Kids?

Due to his fearfulness and sensitivity in new situations, Stack is recommended for an adult-only home at this time.

Things to Know

Stack is a thoughtful, observant dog who can be a bit anxious in new environments and prefers to take things at his own pace. He is generally tolerant of handling but appreciates gentle, low-pressure interactions and the freedom to step away when he needs space. With patience, consistency, and positive reinforcement, Stack has a great foundation to grow into a trusting and loyal companion.

Fun Fact

Stack has a soft, curious side that comes out more each day. Once he feels safe, he enjoys exploring and quietly taking in the world around him.
