Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Meet Tabitha - a little masterpiece in tortoiseshell with a personality just as lovely as her one-of-a-kind coat.

There's something special about a tortie, and Tabitha is a pretty wonderful reminder of that. She's sweet, affectionate, and has that unmistakable tortie charm that makes you feel like you've been personally chosen. She's got a gentle way about her, the kind of girl who makes you want to slow down and spend some extra time with to see what she's thinking (and what she's meowing about, as she has quite the voice!).

Between her beautiful patchwork coloring, her endearing little personality, and the sweetness she brings to the table, she's got a way of quietly stealing the spotlight. Of course, she wouldn't be a tortie without a little bit of that signature sass and individuality sprinkled in!
