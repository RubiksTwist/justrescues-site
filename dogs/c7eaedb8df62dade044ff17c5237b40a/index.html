Berri is at risk due to behavioral reasons. Berri was brought to the care center following a bite incident in the home. His previous owner stated that he will snap and bite whenever someone touches his collar or attempts to remove it. They also state that he does not allow nail trims and will growl and attempt to bite. While on walks Berri will explore his surroundings and is receptive to treats from handlers. Medically, Berri has CIRDC.

Meet Berri! This playful, energetic boy loves staying active and exploring the world around him. He enjoys going for walks and is a great leash walker, with no pulling reported. He's used to 3-4 walks a day, about 20 minutes each, and would love a home that can keep up with his adventurous side. When it's time to relax, Berri is happy doing his own thing around the house.

Berri isn't crate trained and hasn't learned any formal commands just yet, but he's ready to start learning with his new family! One thing to keep in mind is that Berri is uncomfortable with nail trims and may growl or attempt to bite when handled this way, so he'll need a family prepared to work with him on grooming sensitivities using appropriate care and handling.

Berri previously enjoyed a wet-food diet and is looking forward to finding a family to share plenty of walks, car rides, and fun adventures with!
