Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Meet Coal, a little guy with a big personality and a stunning black-and-white coat that makes him impossible to overlook.

His name might bring to mind something dark and bold, but Coal has a softer side that shines through in all the best ways. Beneath that striking coloring is a sweet puppy who is ready to discover everything the world has to offer - from exciting new smells and playful adventures to the simple joy of being close to his people.

Coal has that special puppy charm that makes you want to see what he’ll get into next. Rescued from Louisiana, in collaboration with our rescue partner BISSELL, he’s curious, playful, and full of potential, and every day is another opportunity for his personality to grow.

Coal may have a bold name, but his heart is made for loving.
