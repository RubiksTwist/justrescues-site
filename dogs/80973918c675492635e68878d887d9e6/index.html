Meet Dahlia, a gentle soul who loves stopping to smell the roses!

For Dahlia, happiness can be as simple as following an interesting scent, chasing a tennis ball or enjoying some tasty cheese! She loves spending time outdoors and discovering all the exciting smells along the way.

Dahlia enjoys getting to know new people at her own pace, and having a dog friend nearby helps her feel more confident. As she gets comfortable, there’s a sweet, playful personality to discover!

Ready to get to know Dahlia? Click the “Adopt Me” button to connect with Dahlia’s team in Pawling, New York!
