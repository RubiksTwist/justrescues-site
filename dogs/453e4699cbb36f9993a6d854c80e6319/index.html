Mister Toosa is currently in foster care and ready for adoption via our virtual process! Mister Toosa, an 11-year-old male, is still getting settled in, so we’re just beginning to learn who they are. Right now, everything is new—new people, new sounds, new routines—and we’re giving them time to relax, decompress, and show us their true personality at their own pace. He is a total charmer. His foster says "Mister Toosa is the sweetest, cuddliest boy! Almost immediately upon coming to his foster home, he was exploring and making himself comfortable. He warms up quickly and loves to be near you, whether it's climbing into your lap for a nap or lying by your feet while cooking dinner. As a senior citizen, he is definitely more low-key and really just enjoys the finer things in life like his naps, belly rubs, and treats. He's a cuddle bug! He loves to be either right in your lap, on your legs or at least right beside you. When he's comfy on the couch, he loves to flop over on to his back and show off his belly with his paws in the air! He loves chasing his toy mouse, chattering at birds from the window, and showing off his sweet, quirky personality. Toosa is a loyal, cuddle-loving companion who brings so much warmth and joy to any home." He is neutered, vaccinated, microchipped, and has had preventative treatments to ensure he is ready for his next steps.
