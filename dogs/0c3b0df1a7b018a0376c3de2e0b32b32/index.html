Meet Chi Chi!

This sweet tabby is looking for her forever home after overcoming a rough patch. Raised by her original owners since she was just two weeks old, Chi Chi was sadly misunderstood when she developed a UTI, causing her to have a few accidents. Instead of seeking medical help, her owners kicked her out. After we provided the proper care, Chi Chi is now back to being a healthy, litter-box-trained kitty, who is ready to move on to brighter days.

Chi Chi is the definition of a foodie! She’s especially fond of squeeze treats (who can blame her?), and will happily entertain you with her playful personality as she snuggles up for some fun and food. Whether it’s chasing toys, pouncing around, or curling up for a nap after a snack, this girl’s got it all.

Looking for a sweet companion who’s playful, loving, and ready to be spoiled? Chi Chi just might be the perfect fit!
