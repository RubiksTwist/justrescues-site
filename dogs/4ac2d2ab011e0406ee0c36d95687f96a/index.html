Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Not looking for a senior dog but a gentle and affectionate personality is top on your list? Look no further than Capri

At 3 years old and 58 lbs, Capri is an absolute gem. At first meet, you can't help but realize how simply happy Capri is with her ear to ear smile that turn her beautiful eyes into squinty little stars. Capri is the dog who captivates you to the point where you just have to stop and meet here and then you'll immediately be swooned by her darling, timid yet accepting personality.

Very quickly you will see that basic human kindness wins Capri's loyality over and you will have the privilege of being best friends with the pretty freckled face and freckled ear girl there ever was. Truly, Capri is everything great. She is a cuddly, she is calm and her most special trait of all is that when someone loves her and chooses her, Capari has no problem expressing her deep devotion and loyalty to them. Transparently, Capri desires nothing more in life than to spend time with her peple - regardless if that means curling up next to or on top of you while you read a book or watch a movie or simply just stare out you lovingly while you meet a deadline as you work from home.

Capri's most endearing quality is how adorably excited she gets when you grab her leash and it's time to go for a walk. Capri also walks beautiflly on a loose leash 95% of the time and even in those intense moments when she see's another dog and becomes very excited to make a new friend, you can help but be grateful that her excitement comes from a place of love for friendship and a love for life. Capri would absolutely benefit from an owner who is excited to continue teaching Capri that she can both love dogs and show her love for them in a calmer way. She would also benefit from an owner who equally loves to soak up slivers of sun, standy by bright green grass patches while she gives them a good sniff and simply is open to enjoying the world around you.

Capri deserves to belong to an adopter who will love her as deeply as she loves them — and knowing how special she is, her expectations are more than fair.

Capri is a once-in-a-lifetime dog, so if your search is greater than simply looking for a pet but instead a real desire to feel complete in life - Capri is worth applying for. If she sounds too good to be true, meet her in person and see for yourself.
