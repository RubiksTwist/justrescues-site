Description provided by Bideawee

Mr. Smith is a sweet, affectionate cat who loves nothing more than getting cozy with his person. He’ll happily tuck himself into bed, sometimes even sneaking under the covers to curl up beside you for the night. He settled into his foster home quickly and feels comfortable in his surroundings, with a playful side that still comes out in bursts of zoomies now and then.
He can be a little nervous at first, but with some time and patience, his loving personality really shines through. Mr. Smith will do best as the only cat in the home, where he can soak up all the attention and continue to grow into the cuddly, devoted companion he’s meant to be.

If this cat is located at our Manhattan Adoption Center, head in to meet them and see if you are a match! We are a same-day adoption center, and an application does not put you on a list or hold an animal for you. If they are in Wantagh or in a foster home, please submit an online application and our team will reach out to further discuss the adoption process. Prior to adoption, all of our animals are spayed/neutered, microchipped, and given their age-appropriate vaccines.
