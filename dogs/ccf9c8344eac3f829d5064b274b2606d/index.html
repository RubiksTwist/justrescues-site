Meet the sweetest treat this Halloween season, Candy Corn! 🧡

Candy Corn is a young 3 years old boy weighing around 11 lbs. He has been a treat in foster care, getting along with dogs, cats, and friendly to children who want to pet him on the street as well as different guests in the home. He is master of all things cuddle related, and is happy to chill out in your lap for hours or go on a walk around town with you or to brunch. He is truly a city dog, doing well with loud noises, crowded streets, subways and taxis. He is truly a go anywhere, do anything dog who is just happy to be with you, his best friend in the world. He will give you all his love and will be thrilled to receive your love in return. Come meet your future soul dog! 🧡
