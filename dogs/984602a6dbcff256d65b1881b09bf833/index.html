Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Vixen is a 7-month-old shepherd mix who weighs around 31 lbs. This sweet, curious girl never lets being a tripod slow her down! She has the most positive, adventurous personality and loves exploring the world around her.

Vixen is settling in beautifully with city sounds and noises, and she’s super friendly with everyone she meets, both people and dogs. As a teenage working breed, she has a good amount of energy and will thrive with adopters who can provide the physical and mental stimulation, structure, and consistency she needs.

Registered adopters interested in Vixen can email adopt@hbrdogs.com for more info. If you’re not registered yet, do it today at hbrdogs.com!
