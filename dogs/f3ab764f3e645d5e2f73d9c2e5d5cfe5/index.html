Meet Sourdough! 🍞

Sourdough is a 5-year-old, 7-pound luscious-looking lady who is as stunning as she is sweet. Her gorgeous coat is a rich shade of gray that can almost look dark blue in certain lighting—hence her Russian Blue good looks!

Sourdough may be shy at first, but she is clearly warming up more and more each day. While she can be tentative around unfamiliar people, once she begins to trust you, her sweet and loyal personality starts to shine.

In fact, Sourdough has become quite the little shadow to her primary caregiver! She loves following her person from room to room and will chirp for attention when she wants a little extra love. 🥹 Her adorable meow sounds more like a chirp, and when she's feeling comfortable, she happily accepts—and enjoys—head pets and chin scratches.

And she's starting to discover that playtime might actually be pretty fun! Sourdough has recently begun playing with bouncy balls, and we're loving watching her playful side emerge. 🎾

Sourdough is friendly with other cats of all ages, shapes, and sizes and has wonderful litter box manners. She has a healthy appetite and will happily gobble up treats with gusto.

Just like the bread she's named after, Sourdough needs a little warmth, patience, and appreciation to bring out her very best flavors. Give her the time to settle in, and you may just find yourself with a loyal little companion who follows you everywhere and chirps her way straight into your heart.

And the best part? Sweet, fluffy, and absolutely delicious... without all the carbs. 😉
