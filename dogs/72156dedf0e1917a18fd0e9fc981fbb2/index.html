Meet Cloudberry!
Cloudberry is a sweet, sensitive boy who is still getting comfortable with all the changes around him. He was brought in as a stray, so we're still learning about his past, but he's shown us that he may just need a little time to find his footing. Give him a peaceful place to land and a person who will let him take things one paw at a time, and Cloudberry may surprise you with how much he blossoms.

My Ideal Home
Cloudberry would love a calm, quiet home where he can settle in at his own pace. His ideal person will be patient and understanding, giving him plenty of choice while using treats and positive reinforcement to help him feel more confident. Walks, enrichment, and cozy time together will give him a comfortable routine as he gets to know his new world.

Good with Other Pets?
Cloudberry's history with other dogs is unknown, and during a recent introduction he became a little unsure when the other dog wanted to play. He'll do best with slow, thoughtful introductions and plenty of time to get comfortable. For now, a quiet introduction is much more his speed than a busy dog park.

Good with Kids?
Cloudberry would do best in an adult-only home where things can stay calm and predictable while he settles in. He's a sensitive boy who will appreciate having the freedom to approach new people and experiences when he's ready.

Things to Know
Cloudberry can be reserved in new situations and may prefer to hang back while he takes everything in, but he tolerates gentle handling and follows along on walks with only mild pulling. His new family should let him set the pace and avoid rushing him into interactions before he's ready. He's a quiet little guy with a whole lot of potential, and we're hoping he finds the peaceful home where his true personality can finally shine.
