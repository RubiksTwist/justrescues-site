Description provided by ASPCA Adoption Center

Meet Shadow, a playful and loyal dog who will always want to be by your side!

Shadow has beautiful brown eyes that are sure to draw you in and a tummy that is just made for giving good scritches.

One of our favorite things about Shadow is how much she adores spending quality time with her human companions. Whether she’s adventuring around the city or having a lazy day on the couch, this Shadow is ready to follow you everywhere.

Shadow is also a fashionista. You can see for yourself — check out her Halloween costume in her photos. Wonder Woman or Shadow, who wore it better?

As for hobbies, Shadow enjoys playing with her canine pals almost as much as her humans. She loves doggy playgroups and doesn’t shy away from some rough-and-tumble fun with her doggy friends. When she’s done playing, nothing’s better than relaxing with her human and snacking on some delicious cheese to round off a perfect day.

Do you think the sensational Shadow could be the doggy companion for you? Come to the Adoption Center or click the “Adopt Me” button to get in touch with us!

*You can meet animals located at the ASPCA Adoption Center during our walk-in hours (daily, noon to 5 p.m.). We encourage a $150 suggested adoption fee when adopting an animal. This adoption fee allows the ASPCA to continue our lifesaving work and help even more animals in need. We are grateful for any amount given at the time of adoption.*
