Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

This Shakira is no She Wolf—she’s a lover girl. She’s a cuddle bug who loves curling up by you with the softest, sweetest puppy eyes and just wants to be by you. She’s also an independent girlie who keeps herself busy with chew toys, loves naps, can’t resist a fuzzy dog bed, and quickly understands boundaries, especially as she learns self-control in her puppy teething era.

While Shakira is still too little for walks beyond brief potty breaks, she happily follows your lead and will take any chance to sit next to you or between your legs. She enjoys being in her carrier (she’ll sometimes sit in it herself!) and loves to quietly observe passersbys, making her a perfect brunch or coffee date companion. She’s also incredibly proud of learning how to climb steps and will happily show off her new skills with her tail wagging.

Shakira can be a little shyer and more reserved at first, but underneath that gentle nature is a wonderfully confident and curious little girl. Give her a moment to take things in, and her friendly self will soon be right there beside you, ready to play, explore, or settle in for a cuddle.

Shakira would make the sweetest companion for someone looking for an affectionate little sidekick. Whenever, wherever—you just might be the person she’s meant to be together with.
