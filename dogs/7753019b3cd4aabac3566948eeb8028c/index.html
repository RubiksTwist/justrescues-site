Meet Miss Pancakes!

Miss Pancakes came to ACC as a stray with limited history. She can be a little shy and cautious at first, but she warms up with treats and gentle encouragement. Once comfortable, she has shown a soft body and enjoys leaning into gentle attention.

My Ideal Home

Miss Pancakes needs a patient home that will let her take things at her own pace. Slow introductions, positive reinforcement, and allowing her to choose when she is ready to interact will help her feel safe.

Good with Kids?

No. A home without children under 13 is recommended due to her history of fearful and defensive behaviors.

Good with Other Pets?

Miss Pancakes should be the only pet in the home. She has a reported history of attempting to bite another dog and has shown tension around other dogs. Dog parks are not recommended.

Overall Health

No significant medical concerns noted in the available information.

Behavior Determination

Level 3. Miss Pancakes can give clear warnings when uncomfortable and may retreat when given the opportunity. She should never be forced to interact and will benefit from patient, reward-based training.

Other Fun Facts

Miss Pancakes is food motivated! Give her some treats and a little time, and she may approach you with her sweet, soft side.
