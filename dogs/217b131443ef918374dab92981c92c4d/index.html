About:

Meet Popo, a spirited Jindo mix puppy with a big personality and an even bigger enthusiasm for life!

Rescued from a dog meat farm in South Korea, Popo is getting a fresh start and making the most of every moment. Despite his difficult beginnings, he's a happy, confident, and energetic boy who loves to run, play, and explore the world around him.

Popo is full of personality and isn't shy about expressing himself. Curious and adventurous, he'd much rather be out discovering something new than sitting around waiting for the excitement to begin!

With his playful spirit and boundless enthusiasm, Popo may be a great match for an active family who can keep up with his energy and give him plenty of opportunities to run, explore, and enjoy life.

After everything he's been through, Popo deserves a loving home where he can continue to grow, discover new adventures, and experience all the joys of being part of a family.
