Description provided by ASPCA Adoption Center

Meet Junebug, an adorable girl with a heart full of love!

Junebug is a beautiful lady with the sweetest face and a personality to match! She loves being around her people and has a wonderful way of making every day a little brighter. Junebug is a big fan of petting, especially around her head and cheeks.

When Junebug isn’t soaking up affection, her playful side comes out! She loves playing with pipe cleaners and can happily entertain herself with them, or you can bring out a wand toy for her to chase. She will curl up at your feet after for a cozy nap, making her the perfect mix of playful and snuggly.

And, of course, Junebug has a soft spot for yummy treats! Whether she’s playing, relaxing, or enjoying some snacks, this kind girl is happiest when she gets to share her day with you.

If you’re looking for a gentle and affectionate companion, Junebug is ready to start an amazing chapter with you! Click the "Adopt Me" button to get in touch now.

*You can meet animals located at the ASPCA Adoption Center during our walk-in hours (daily, noon to 6 p.m.). We encourage a $150 suggested adoption fee when adopting an animal. This adoption fee allows the ASPCA to continue our lifesaving work and help even more animals in need. We are grateful for any amount given at the time of adoption.*
