Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

A volunteer writes:
Peaches is a pretty cow print kitty girl who appears a little standoffish(I love her face, though) in her kennel but I think this is because she is kind of fearful after all what recently happened in her life. Not much is known about her except that she might have been affected by the passing of her housemate, acted differently over the last few months and had a recent and single scratch/bite incident after having been startled. Peaches' whole world fell apart from that moment on...Peaches is rather quiet in her kennel, rarely in hiding and usually not coming forward to meet her visitors. She appreciates treats and after some "pep" talk, leans slowly into pets, head bunts my hand and even purrs. She'd rather prefer to be elsewhere, mostly when her room is filled with adopters. She likes a quiet adoption ward. Lovely Peaches would open up and do better outside the shelter environment, in a quiet adult only home where she would feel safe and loved. Come and meet this cutie at the Manhattan Care Center!
