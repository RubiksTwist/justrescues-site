Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

June is a 3-year-old Husky mix weighing about 65 pounds, and she's as lovely as she looks. Joyful, curious, smart, and deeply loving, she has a regal presence that's hard to miss, truly, photos don't quite capture how beautiful she is in person.

She's house-trained, crate-trained, and walks great on a loose leash. June takes a little time to warm up to new people and places, usually a day or two, but once she trusts you, she's incredibly sweet: she'll cuddle up on the couch, rest her head in your lap, or curl up right next to you in bed. She gets the cutest zoomies in the park when she spots a squirrel (though she's picky about which ones earn a chase!).

June does best in a calm home rather than a loud, high-traffic area. She's had some big wins lately: her separation anxiety has improved significantly, and while she can still be a little hesitant with new men at first, she warms up quickly, especially with women. She's a smart, affectionate girl who just needs a patient adopter willing to give her a little time and a peaceful place to settle in.

Likes 🐾

Cuddling up close and resting her head in your lap

Chasing (very particular) squirrels in the park

Quiet mornings and calm environments

Getting to know new people at her own pace

Dislikes 🚫

Loud, chaotic streets and crowds

Being rushed into new situations

Busy weekend energy in high-traffic neighborhoods
