Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Edmond is a gentle, shy kitten who is slowly coming out of his shell. Each day he grows a little braver, beginning to show his sweet nature and quiet affection to those he trusts. With a little patience and kindness, Edmond is sure to blossom into a loving and devoted companion. Rescued from Brookhaven though our Operation Cat program, Edmond is now hoping for a home where he can continue to gain confidence at his own pace. Edmond has Feline Leukemia (FeLV), which means he will do best as the only cat in the home and has a more delicate immune system, so a caring environment will help him thrive. Many cats like Edmond live happy, love-filled lives with just a bit of extra support. He is so deserving of a chance, and the right person can truly make a difference in his life. If you have a big heart and a little patience, Edmond will be worth every moment.
