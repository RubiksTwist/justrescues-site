Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

My foster writes: Eucalyptus is a little ball of energy! He never met a feather wand, ribbon, or ball he didn't want to chase, and treats are his love language. He started out shy, but a little playtime and a few snacks won him over - now he follows his people from room to room angling for pets, and he's not afraid to chirp and ask for them.

Fiesta is the calmer half. He's happy to settle in next to you on the couch, though he likes space on his own terms, and he saves his playful side for games with Eucalyptus. Once having a couple weeks to warm up, he has settled into being the steady, easygoing one of the pair.

My foster writes:
Eucalyptus is an energetic ball of fun who loves to chat, while Fiesta is his calm, shy shadow who prefers to stay out of trouble and observe from a cozy hiding spot. Eucalyptus is always ready for action and will happily chase strings and long feather toys, or independently bat around springs and fuzz balls. While neither are big fans of cuddling or being petted just yet, they can both be tempted and won over with a tasty treat like a Churu.

A volunteer writes:
Young Eucalyptus comes from a house full of cats, all of them as shy as he is. He lived with one adult only, knows a lot about cats but has no experience what so ever with strangers, children or dogs. Imagine landing on another galaxy.. This is how Eucalyptus must feel at the care center, unsure, fearful and worried about what could come next.. He is stationed mainly in his cubby. To avoid any hissing, open gently his door, speak softly to him and you might get slow blinks in return. Attempts at petting send him to the back of his cubby but a feather wand is not seen anymore as a foe. Eucalyptus can actually play with his guest and by himself too. His former owner told us that Eucalyptus was high energy, quite playful and very gentle with his housemates. Eucalyptus is making very slow progress and needs a low traffic home, may be with another kitty and an understanding and patient new owner in order to bloom into amore confident and happy pet. Will you give Eucalyptus a chance. He is waiting for you at the Manhattan Care Center.
