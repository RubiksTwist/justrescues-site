Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

We picked Stella up from the ASPCA and all of the staff there were so sad to see her go. They called her auntie Stella because she loved playing with a litter of puppies who happened to look just like her 🥹 Now Stella has been in Brooklyn for less than 24 hours and has been a dream. First of all she is so unique and gorgeous, we call her a cow pig bunny, and utterly sweet. She loves squeaky toys and tough toys she can gnaw on, peanut butter and cheese, and most of all snuggles. She let us give her a bath right away and has the softest white coat with a perfect baby pink belly, paws, nose, eye and ear. She loves to sniff everything outside but hasn’t tried to eat stuff. It takes her a little time to go to the bathroom but that’s probably just because she needs to sniff this new place out first (priorities!). No accidents yet too! She slept peacefully in her crate. She’s an incredibly special girl.

﻿Stella is approximately 5y 0m 10d old and is fully grown at 52 pounds. They are microchipped and up to date on all age-appropriate medical care. Their breed is unknown as we have not had a DNA test done.
