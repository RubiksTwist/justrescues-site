Description provided by Animal Rescue Foundation — Beacon

This is Mischif the American Bulldog. He is 61 pounds and approximately 10-12 years old. He was recently surrendered to us due to no fault of his own. He is deaf and follows basic commands by hand signal.

Mischif is very sweet with people, ignores other dogs, and loves to play ball! He is a lovable senior pup who wants to take nice walks and hang out with his people.

Mischif is intact. Due to his age, it was decided that it’s in his best interest to not neuter him.

Help make Mischif’s senior years special in a loving home!
