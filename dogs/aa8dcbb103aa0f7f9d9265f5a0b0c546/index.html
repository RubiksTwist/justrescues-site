DETAILED PROFILE

Owen Thiele is a dog who reads the room. Bring the energy and he'll bring it right back, running laps, flopping over for belly rubs, and racing after whatever toy you throw. Sit down to work and he'll simply curl up and go to sleep. Cuddly, sweet, and very chill is a rare combo in a dog who also loves a game.

That game is usually fetch. He isn't much for playing with toys on his own, but throw one and he'll chase it down and bring it back. He enjoys a round of tug, too, and early signs point to good manners about it: he treats it as a game and lets go without fuss. He also has a serious soft spot for a good chew bone; when it went missing, he kept checking his toy bin in case it turned up.

Owen just arrived, so everything here is a first impression. So far he's friendly with people, happy to accept pets from strangers who offer, and otherwise content to ignore passersby. He was a bit barky around other dogs on his very busy first day, but since then he's been calm and mostly uninterested on walks, even when a tiny, hyper dog jumped on him. He's living with cats; after some early chasing, he's learned to give them space and turns away when they hiss. His experience with kids isn't known yet.

He's settling into his crate (a treat helps), taking care of business outside, and walking pretty well on leash, with a little zigzagging and plenty of sniffing. He'll eye snacks on the coffee table but usually backs off when told no.

After his first big outing in the city, he crashed on the couch and snored loudly enough to crack everyone up. Consider yourself warned.
