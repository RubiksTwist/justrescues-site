Description provided by PupStarz Rescue

When is adopting two cats a better idea than adopting just one? Meet Shadow and Ash, a bonded pair of sister cats who are 11 months old and around 8 pounds a piece( as of 10/10) . Shadow is the kind, caring, sweet leader, with Ash being the sillier, more curious and playful of the two. Together, Shadow and Ash complete each other, and the way they look out for one another is nothing short of amazing.

These two beauty queens currently live outside, but they are both friendly, approachable, affectionate, and happy. Easy-going and upbeat, they get along well with other cats, kids, and adults. In fact, they absolutely lovvvve playing with other kitties.

Not at all fearful of being handled, Shadow and Ash enjoy being pet, cuddled with, and scratched under the chin. Sporting a healthy appetite, these girls are not fussy eaters, and they gobble up treats like there’s no tomorrow. Churus are a sure crowd pleaser with this group!

Shadow and Ash would welcome a loving home where they can slowly learn to enjoy the safety, warmth, and comfort of indoor living. How nice it would be for the sisters to have shelter from the rain, heat in the winter, a couch for napping, a window to gaze out of, and a bowl of fresh kibble that’s always available. Yeah, these two deserve at least that much…and for that, Shadow and Ash will be as loyal and loving to you as they have always been to each other.

If you would like to adopt Shadow and Ash, comment ADOPT and we’ll send you an application.
