Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

My foster writes:
Meet Lucy-Belle, the low maintenance companion with a world class wiggle while she politely carries her own toys. She is a chill soul famous for her side saddle sit where she flops her legs to the side to make sure her belly is fully exposed for rubs. Whether she is snoring through her signature deep naps or people watching at a restaurant, she is a master of the quiet life.
She is very respectful of other dogs and adjusts beautifully to their energy. If a dog wants to play, she goes right into a play bow, but she is just as happy to coexist peacefully if they aren't interested. She is a total dream on leash (although a but zig-zaggy lol) walking alongside her humans with a nice slack leash, making her a very calm and easy walking partner.
Lucy-Belle gives actual hugs and loves carrying a favorite toy in her mouth while her whole body moves in happy S curves around the apartment. She is a gentle, low to medium energy girl who is gaining confidence every day. Her cute cleft lip makes her uniquely charming and all she needs is a quick face wipe after her meals to stay looking her best. If you are looking for a sweet, soulful best friend, with positive energy, Lucy-Belle is ready to meet you!
See more on Instagram @nycacc_foster
