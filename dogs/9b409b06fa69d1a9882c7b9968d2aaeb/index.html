Meet Travis Kelsit

Travis is a simple guy with a very important philosophy: if there’s a toy, it’s time to play.

This boy loves chasing toys almost as much as he loves convincing you to chase him while he has one. Add a squeaker and you’ve officially made his day. He gets so focused on playtime, and watching him have fun is pretty irresistible.

But once the toys are put away, Travis is just as happy to be your cuddly companion. He’s a sweet, happy-go-lucky guy who loves his people and enjoys being close to them.

Travis is the perfect combination of playful and affectionate. He’s ready for a family who will toss the toys, share the cuddles, and make him part of their everyday lives.

If you’re looking for a happy, loving guy with a playful side and a beautiful heart, Travis is waiting to meet you.
