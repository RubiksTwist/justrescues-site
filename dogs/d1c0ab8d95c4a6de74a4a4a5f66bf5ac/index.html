Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

Flo Rida will absolutely “welcome you to her house”.

Flo is the outgoing, curious little explorer of the duo. She loves discovering new things, checking out her surroundings, and is almost always the first one to trot over and say hello. She approaches the world with confidence, curiosity, and a very waggy tail.

This girl has two great loves in life: food and playtime. Flo gets the cutest bursts of puppy energy and throws herself wholeheartedly into whatever fun is happening, whether that’s playing with her sister Shakira, investigating something new, or giving puppy eyes for treats. She’s usually a quiet girl but if you hear a peep from Flo, there’s a very good chance it’s snack related.

For such a playful and adventurous girl, Flo also has an impressively independent side. She absolutely loves her crate and zonks out for the night. She’s comfortable having her own space and is perfectly happy entertaining herself when it’s time to wind down. She also seems to notice the TV and zones in when Paw Patrol is on.

Flo has become a lot more of a cuddle bug since we first met her. She absolutely loves to roll over for belly rubs and it doesn’t take much to find her spot and have her little feet kicking with scratches,

Flo Rida would be the perfect little sidekick for someone looking for a confident, playful, food-motivated adventurer who is always ready to explore — and will happily be the first one to greet you.
