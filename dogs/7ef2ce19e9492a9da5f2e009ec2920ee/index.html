Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

Hi there, I'm Baby Bean! While I'm not exactly a true baby anymore at ten years old, you wouldn't know it when you meet me. I have a loving and spunky attitude, twirling for attention and talking to you with my sweet little chirps! I love to chat and spend time together. My world changed when my owner was no longer able to care for me, and it took me some time to adjust to the shelter. I've been described as lower energy, talkative and sassy, and have a funny quirk of eating food with my paw. You might say I am a princess set in my ways! I'm looking for a loving, calm home with no very young children and no dogs. I have made so much progress with my friends at ACC, but I've been here since August, and I'm really hoping to spend the holidays cozy with my forever family. Please come and meet me if that might be you!
