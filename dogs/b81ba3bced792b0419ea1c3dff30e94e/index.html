Biscuit is a 1-year-old Great Pyrenees mix who weighs around 48 lbs, and he is one seriously sweet guy. He’s house-trained, super friendly, and very affectionate. His foster says he loves being petted, cuddling up, and staying close to his people.

He’s also great with other dogs and has that lovable, social personality that makes him easy to root for. Biscuit is still young, so he’s continuing to learn his leash manners and settle into a routine, but he’s already showing what a wonderful companion he can be.

If you’re looking for a big softie who loves affection and being part of the action, Biscuit may be your guy.
