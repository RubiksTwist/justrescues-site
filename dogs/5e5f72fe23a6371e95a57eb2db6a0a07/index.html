Description provided by Wags & Walks

Meet Finn! Sweet little boy Finn came into our care along with his sibling, Floro and Forge, from a crowded shelter in the south. Now safe and ready for a fresh start, Finn is looking for his forever home in the Hamptons! Like most young puppies, Finn is full of curiosity and loves spending the day exploring, playing with toys, and making new friends. This little boy is a total snuggle bug! After playtime is done, he will happily curl into a ball in your lap for a good cuddle. Finn is doing well in a foster home with other pups, but he would also be more than happy as a solo dog in his forever home. As Finn continues to grow, he will thrive with a family that's excited to provide plenty of training, socialization, and love while experiencing all of the joys and occasional challenges of puppyhood. If you're looking for a happy, affectionate pup to add to your family, Finn is ready to meet you!
