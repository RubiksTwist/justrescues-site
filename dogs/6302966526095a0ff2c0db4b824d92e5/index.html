Meet Mogwai!

Mogwai is a 3-year-old with an irresistibly cute face and a goofy personality to match! He’s a smart little guy who learns quickly and loves showing off how clever he is. When he’s not being his silly self, Mogwai is happiest snuggled up with his people. He’s affectionate, cuddly, and ready to bring lots of laughs and love into his forever home.
