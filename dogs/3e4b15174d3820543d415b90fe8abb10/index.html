Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

My foster writes: Gypsy's a really cute, food-oriented, playful cat who loves exploring and playing with toys! He's not picky and can entertain himself with any toy. He loves chicken, freeze dried salmon, napping (he sleeps during the night and during the day, so that whenever he is away, he has lots of energy), and looking out the window. When he's in a snacking mood (he almost always is), he'll sit patiently next to you until you give him a treat and bump your body with his head or rub his body against your legs. He's gentle, will politely pat you with his paws for food, and is sometimes a bit silly (sometimes he's a bit uncoordinated and falls ungracefully off the bed or a cabinet). I've been able to pick him up (he tolerates this) and pet him / touch him almost whenever, and he's a bit clingy so wherever I go, he follows to sit down next to me and lay down next to me. He's pretty tolerant of actions he doesn't like (I can always get a good few paw rubs in or hold his tail for a good while before he moves his limbs away). When he's mad or dislikes something, he usually just turns away and then comes back half a minute later. He definitely doesn't hold grudges. He's willing to wear little accessories like collars too!
