Lady is a sweet, easy going girl who is excited for her new life! At pick up, Lady was nervous and unsure of herself, but after a nice meal and a good sleep, she woke up so happy and ready to take on the day. She is learning how to play with toys and run in the backyard. She is not bothered by other dogs that walk past her on the sidewalk and will keep walking forward. While walking, she sometimes will zig zag to sniff around but will follow you when redirected if needed. Once inside, she likes to cuddle up beside you on the couch. Lady is a real foodie and will happily eat anything you give her.
