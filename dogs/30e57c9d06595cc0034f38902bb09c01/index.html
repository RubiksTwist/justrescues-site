Meet Isis, a big hearted girl with an even bigger head to pet :)

Isis is a Rottweiler/Bully mix who is definitely large in size, but she’s even larger in love. She’s incredibly people-friendly and lives for affection, especially kisses and long sessions of head scratches. If you’re looking for a dog who truly adores her humans, Isis is your girl.

Because of her size, Isis will do best with a confident, patient leader who can help her feel secure and supported. Isis would thrive with an active adopter, such as someone who enjoys regular walks or runs, because nothing is more exciting for her than a long jog in the park. Isis would love a home where she can stay active, explore new places, and spend quality time with her person.

If you’re looking for a loyal companion who will shower you with love and devotion, Isis is waiting to meet you ! Please fill out and submit an adoption application today.
