Description provided by AMA Animal Rescue

Meet Isis, a big girl with an even bigger personality and absolutely no idea that she's not a lap dog!

Isis is a Rottweiler/Bully mix who believes there's nothing better than being close to her humans. She's extremely sweet, affectionate, and always ready for some extra love, especially when it comes to kisses and long head scratches. She's also quite the conversationalist! Expect plenty of talking, little sass, and the occasional answer back when she has something to say. She's a girly who knows what she wants, and she's not afraid to tell you!

Despite her big personality, Isis is a pretty laid-back girl. She's happiest relaxing alongside her favorite people, soaking up the sunshine, and enjoying the simple things in life. She's smart, listens well, and is eager to learn, making her a wonderful companion for someone who enjoys spending time together and continuing to teach her new things. With her calm, happy demeanor and incredibly sweet nature, she's just an all-around lovely girl to have around.

Isis would love a home where she can be part of the family, enjoy leisurely walks, find the sunniest spot in the house, and sneak in as many cuddles as possible. Just remember, personal space isn't really her thing when she thinks she belongs in your lap!

If you're looking for a sweet, silly, affectionate companion with a little sass and a whole lot of love, Isis is ready to make herself right at home.
