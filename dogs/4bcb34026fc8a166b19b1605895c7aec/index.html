Meet Dalkong | Apply Now

This sweet boy is the full package: gentle, resilient, and ready for a quiet place to call home~ ✨ Dalkong ✨ is a 1.5-year-old, 28lb pup who has won over all of our hearts!

Rescued from the Gangneung shelter from their euthanasia list, Dalkong has overcome so much to get here, and he is officially ready to start his soft life.

Dalkong is a sensitive soul who is taking the brave transition to indoor life one step at a time. Left to his own devices, he treats his crate like a safe haven. He happily hangs out inside all cozy and relaxed, even during non-crate hours with the door wide open!

Stepping outside and putting on his leash can feel a bit scary for him right now, but once he’s out on the pavement, he finds his groove and walks like a dream.

He strolls along with a calm stride, quietly fascinated by the front doors of houses and looking up at people as they pass. While sudden loud noises, busy traffic, or strong gusts of wind can make him freeze or pull momentarily, he is already showing incredible resilience re-doing scary walks (like facing down the giant garbage truck!) and growing braver around passing bikes every single day. He’s also a total pro in elevators, politely sitting or lying down for the ride.

Dalkong would thrive in a patient home that will let him explore his new surroundings at his own pace.

If you’re ready to open your heart to a sweet, brave pup who will love you forever, come meet Dalkong this weekend! We strongly encourage submitting your application and interviewing before the event. Pre-approved adopters get 30 minutes of exclusive access before the general public.
