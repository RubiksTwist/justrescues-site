Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

We are very lucky that Big Foot the re scue dog is an actual being and not just an old wives tale. In fact, Big Foot is a sweet, slow moving mush of a boy whose heart is bigger than his 70 lb frame. At just over 5 years old, Big Foot is a well rounded gentlemen who is extremely adaptable and who would do well in any home.

In his maturity, Big Foot has mastered many commands such as sit, come, stay, go back and lay down. Big Foot is easily adapable to new routines, understands the expectations people have of him while also being extremely humble and graciously accepting of praise, pets and companionship.

Out doors, Big Foot is a polite and enjoyable walking buddy. He is completely unbothered by any chaos in the community and in the early days when he might feel nervous in a new environment but once he adjust, he morphs into his true cool as a cumcumber personality. In his foster home, Big Foot is a neighborhood favorite - greeting all humans and dogs for appropriate interaction and affection.

In the home, Big Foot is the biggest cuddler you could ever meet. His favorite place is pressing his body onto your legs - both giving and receiving comfort and affection. Big Foot loves being close to you and is happy to soak up all the pets or just curl up next to you for some quiet time together. When alone and is non destructive, happy to snooze on the sofa until his people return.

Big Foot is completely housebroken and is happy to respectfully communicate his needs when he requires a potty break. Big Foot overall is a house guest with impeccable mannerisms. Big Foot is happy to love and trust people quickly in spaces where mutual respect and safety exist. Big Foot is a dog who is simply waiting for someone to see how rare of a dog he is in rescue and realize the gem they have sitting in front of them. Once you meet Big Foot, you will for sure know it's finally forever and wouldnt be able ot fathom how you ever lived life without him.
