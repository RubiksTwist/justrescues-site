Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Rachel is an 8-month-old mixed breed who weighs around 55 lbs and is a total cuddle bug. She loves being close to her person, whether that means curling up beside you or somehow ending up right on top of you at night. She’s affectionate, sweet, and very happiest when she gets to stay close to the people she trusts.

Rachel can be timid when meeting new people and dogs, but with a little time she warms up and shows her friendly side. She’s also still getting used to city life, and all the unfamiliar sounds outside can be a lot for her right now. She’ll likely do best in a quieter area with adopters who can give her the time and reassurance she needs to keep building confidence.
