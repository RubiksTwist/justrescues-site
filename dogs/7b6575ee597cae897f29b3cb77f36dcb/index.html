⌚ Meet SMART WATCH! (aka Jenell) ⌚

Looking for a companion who will keep you moving AND remind you when it's time to slow down? Smart Watch might just be your perfect match!

At 5 years old and just 13 pounds, this adorable Chihuahua is a little lady with a big independent streak and a serious love for her walks.

Smart Watch isn't your typical velcro dog. She's perfectly happy doing her own thing, but loves having her person nearby. Her favorite place is right beside you, keeping an eye on the world, and she's always happy to accept some face rubs and scratches behind her ears. She even enjoys being carried!

Now, let's talk about her walking skills. This girl MEANS BUSINESS! She loves long walks (30–75 minutes!), trots along beautifully on leash, and doesn't spend the entire time stopping to investigate every leaf. She's house-trained, crate-trained, sleeps through the night, and travels well in the car.

Smart Watch gets along with people of all ages, men and women, and is comfortable around cats. When it comes to other dogs, however, she's more of a solo act. She prefers not to play or socialize with other pups and isn't a fan of having them sniff her. She'd do best as the only dog in the home.

She's a little timid when first meeting new people, but give her a little time and she'll come out of her shell. One thing to know: her foster suspects she may have some hearing loss, so she'll benefit from a patient family willing to learn how she communicates best.

She's calm, easygoing, and doesn't need a house full of toys or constant entertainment. Just a loving home, a comfy spot to relax, and someone who appreciates an independent little companion.

If you would like to adopt this dog, please visit www.pupstarzrescue.org for an application!
