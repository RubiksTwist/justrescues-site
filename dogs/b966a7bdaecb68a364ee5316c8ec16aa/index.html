Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

My foster writes: Miles is patiently waiting to meet his new best friend! He's a very friendly and loyal guy who just wants to cuddle on the couch or sit with you outside. Or sit with you in the kitchen or bedroom or poke his head in to keep you company in the shower. He's not a barker but you'll know he's there because of the paw or head or tail he has touching you at all times.
He knows sit, down, paw, stay, and come and loves practicing and learning new tricks.
On walks Miles ignores anything and anyone until he senses they want to say hi to him, then he's excited and wags his tail until someone pets him. He is friendly or apathetic around other dogs but really soaks up attention from people.

My #BoroughBreak Buddy Writes... If you're looking for an easygoing, affectionate companion, Miles might just be your perfect match. This sweet boy is as chill as they come - nothing seems to faze him. He showed no signs of reactivity around other dogs or loud noises and took everything in stride with a calm, happy demeanor.
Miles absolutely loves both people and other dogs. He was excited to say hello to everyone he met, from adults to children, and greeted other dogs with friendly curiosity and zero aggression. He's the kind of dog who just wants to make friends wherever he goes.
He seems to have a lower-to-moderate energy level - despite our walk being a fairly relaxed pace, he was perfectly happy to settle in and relax afterward. An affectionate guy, he gave a few gentle hand kisses throughout our time together. The only times Miles does pull on the leash a bit when he needs to go to the bathroom or when someone is giving him attention and he wants to say hello back, but otherwise he's a relaxed, easy companion who is a pleasure to be around.
One of the sweetest moments was taking him to Starbucks for a pup cup - Miles patiently and gently licked the whipped cream right from the cup I was holding for him, showing just how gentle and well-mannered he is.
Miles is truly an angel. Whether you're a single person, a couple, or a family, anyone would be lucky to welcome this lovable boy into their home. He has so much love to give and is ready to be someone's best friend for life.
