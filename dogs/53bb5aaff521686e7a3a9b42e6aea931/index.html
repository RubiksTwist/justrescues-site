Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Meet Ollie - your future cuddle buddy, personal comedian, and very best friend.

Ollie is the kind of dog who makes you wonder how you ever lived without him. This sweet boy has a heart as big as his personality, and he’s not shy about sharing it! He’s incredibly affectionate, loves to snuggle up close, and has the gentlest soul. Rescued from Louisiana, thanks to our rescue partner BISSELL, Ollie is ready to leave the life of living on the streets behind him and trade it for a life in the Hamptons.

But don’t let his cuddly side fool you - Ollie has a goofy, playful streak that makes him extra special! He’ll keep you smiling with his silly antics and adorable personality, then happily switch gears and melt right into you for some quality snuggle time.

There’s something so endearing about Ollie. He has that special way of making you feel loved just by being near you. He doesn’t need grand adventures or fancy things to be happy - just a little playtime, a cozy spot beside his favorite person, and someone who will love him as much as he loves them. Ollie is ready to give his whole heart to someone lucky enough to call him theirs - could that someone be you?
