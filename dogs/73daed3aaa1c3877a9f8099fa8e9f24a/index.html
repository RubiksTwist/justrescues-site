Meet Texas!

Texas was found as a stray, and now this young, enthusiastic guy is looking for a place to call home. He enjoys exploring and sniffing around, and once he's comfortable, he'll happily lean into gentle affection. He's still getting to know new people and situations, but with a little patience, Texas is ready to discover just how good life can be outside the shelter.

My Ideal Home

Texas would love an active home that can give him plenty of physical exercise and mental enrichment each day. Food puzzles, long-lasting chews, hide and seek, and reward-based training will help keep his enthusiastic mind busy. His new family should let him approach new people at his own pace.

Good with Other Pets?

Texas's history with other animals is unknown. Any introductions with resident pets should be slow, thoughtful, and supervised once he's had time to decompress and get comfortable in his new home.

Good with Kids?

Texas would do best in a home without children under 13. He's still learning how to feel comfortable with new people and situations, so older, dog-savvy children should have an in-depth interaction with him before adoption and be prepared to respect his boundaries.

Things to Know

Texas is a young dog with lots of enthusiasm who will benefit from patient, positive training. He's sensitive about handling and has shown discomfort during veterinary care, so his family should use treats and other positive experiences to help him feel more comfortable with touch. He's also been tense around toys and may hover or body-block when he has something he values, so he'll need to be left alone while enjoying his toys and taught positive trade-up and drop cues. Nothing should ever be taken directly from his mouth.
