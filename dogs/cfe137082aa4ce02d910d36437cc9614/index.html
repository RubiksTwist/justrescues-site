Description provided by Waldo's Rescue Pen

Scrubs is a sweet, gentle, and very timid young pup who is slowly learning that the world can be a safe place. She is very observant and cautious, always watching her surroundings and the people around her before deciding whether she feels comfortable. She loves other dogs and is very friendly towards them. New people and unfamiliar experiences can be overwhelming for her, so she needs a little extra time and patience to feel safe.

She is the easiest, sweetest and most gentle little pup ever! Since arriving at her foster home, Scrubs has already made meaningful progress. At first, she was too nervous to be touched and would run under any dark surface, but now she loves to cuddle up next to people she’s comfortable with and take long naps. She enjoys having her own safe space and prefers to approach people and new experiences at her own pace.

Scrubs is being exposed to so many people and it may take time but she gets comfortable pretty easily. She absolutely loves running in the grass and leaves and eating some treats! At first when you put the harness on her she may shake and be scared but once she gets outside she has a smile on her face

One thing that definitely wins her over is food! Scrubs loves to eat and is very food-motivated, which has been a wonderful way to help her feel more comfortable and introduce her to new things.

She would do best with a patient, gentle adopter who won’t rush her and will let her build trust at her own pace. Underneath that timid exterior is a sweet and curious little pup, and watching her slowly become more comfortable is incredibly rewarding. 🐾

She’s pee-pad trained, but she still needs to be trained to poop on the pad. She also sleeps through the night.
