Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

If your home could use a little more laughter, a little more mischief, and a whole lot of personality, Finian is ready to report for duty!

This playful little guy is basically a tiny whirlwind wrapped in fur - he came in through our Operation Cat program, ready to leave life on the streets for cozy blankets and a family to keep constantly entertained. He has a talent for turning absolutely anything into a toy, whether it’s a crumpled piece of paper, a dangling string, or something you were pretty sure wasn’t meant to be played with.

But what makes Finian special isn't just his endless supply of kitten energy. It's the way he makes ordinary moments feel a little less ordinary. He's the kind of kitten who will keep you entertained with his ridiculous antics, make you smile when you least expect it, and remind you that life is much more fun when you don't take it too seriously.
