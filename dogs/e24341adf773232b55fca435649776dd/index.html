Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

She's squishy, she's silky, she's Sky Girl!

Sky Girl is a teeny tiny 40 lb, 6 year old lady who is as demure as she is compact.

In addition to being a little lady, Sky Girl is also a girl with brains. Sky Girl is crate trained, knows her basic commands and is always eager to show off those skills.

On leash, Sky Girl is an absolute gem. Sky Girl walks on a loose leash, checks in with her person and even when Sky Girl remembers that she is a friendly and social butterfly and becomes excited, she's easy to refocus.

When Sky Girl is not meet and greeting friends on the street she is a perfect homebody with wonderful mannerisms. Sky Girl is both fully housebroken outdoors and on peepads for those unexpected emergency moments! When she's home with you, she also loves to snuggle up close and watch reality tv too!

In new environments Sky Girl can be timid for a short while until she realizes new scenary and new sounds are not so scary and she can show off how brave she really is.

Overall, Sky Girl is a lovely, smushy faced girl whose personality and demeanor makes her true best friend material. Sky Girl would love a home that loves her as much as she loves them and understands that rarity like her is hard to find in rescue!

If you've been searching for the girl of your dreams, look for Sky Girls unique, smushy face in the crowd - we are nearly certain she is yours to keep.
