Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Miss Poppi Seed is a four year old sweet and social girl who legitimately lives to brighten your day. Poppi is such an easy going, going with the flow type lady who is always down to match her humans vibes. Want to play with her? Lets do it! Do you fancy a walk in the park She’s ready to go! Is it a cuddle up and watch movie type of day? Awesome, she'll ask for belly rubs during the preview before cuddling up under the blanket with you.

At 4 years old, Poppi has learned all she needed to about loving people and living in her home. She is fully crate trained and happy to sleep in her crate throughout the night and when she needs to be left alone while her humans run errands or go to work.

Miss Poppi seed is the kind of girl who lets you know the relationship is very mutual. When you are close by, she will kindly ask you for pets by placing her paw on your arm or knee or gently nudging you with her teddy bear shaped head.

Miss Poppi Seed is also exceptionally smart already having mastered her "sit" and "paw" command.

Miss Poppi Seed is a wonderful brunch buddy or bar date, always happy to meet new people and never turning down the opportunity to be the center of attention.

Miss Poppi Seed's personality stems from a place of curiousity and expressiveness. Some of her favorite hobbies include staring at her reflection in the mirror, sniffing new areas outside and watching the neighborhood wildlife. If you need to get Poppi's attention during a walk she will quickly redirect and pay attention to her handler and is both happy for a quick potty break or some extra time in the fresh air.

In foster care, Poppi has yet to directly interacted with other pets or very young children however, given social and happy personality, one can only assume she'll be a really good companion with the normal proper slow introductions all dogs require.

Miss Poppi Seed is a turnkey ready houseguest, who is fully house trained, unbothered by the majority of outside noises and prefers to find a sunny spot in the home to sunbathe or a window sill to peer out of whenever she isn't snoozing on the couch.

True to Miss Poppi Seed's confident nature, she loves a game of tug-of-war and has no qualms about being a touch cookie to beat. At 4 years old, Miss Poppi Seed is happy to be entertained with all sorts of toys and she is thrilled to be enriched with mentally stimulating treats like frozen lick mats or a long lasting chew toy.

Miss Poppi Seed is such an adaptable girl and would be the perfect regardless if you work from home or need to spend time out of the house. Whatever your situation - she'll be sure to greet you with her koolaid smile and her wagging tongue - proof that she is legitimately the happiest girl alive. If you have space in your heart and your home for endless joy and companionship - come meet this girl, the proof is honestly in the poppi seed this time, no need for pudding.
