This pet needs emergency placement. Please click here to go to our emergency placement page for more information.

Thursday is on the At Risk list due to behavior. He is not thriving in the care center and continues to display challenging behavior during interactions. He is extremely intelligent and high energy, and needs to expend energy constructively with interactive play sessions 3-4X/day. In addition to playtime, this cat would also benefit from mental exercise from activities such as puzzle toys/games, lick mats, or clicker training. Without providing activity for his mind and body, the cat may react with rough play, potentially resulting in bite/scratch incidents. It is in his best interest to move out of the kennel environment and into a stable home as quickly as possible. He would do best in a home with adopters who are willing to give him/her plenty of time and space to adjust, and who have experience with feline body language, overstimulation, rough play, and who have the time to invest in managing these behaviors on a consistent basis to avoid further bite/scratch incidents.

A volunteer writes:
Thursday is a young and very attractive ginger who can be quite pleasant to spend time with. He incites potential visitors to request a meet and greet by rolling on his side behind his door and looking in a very friendly way right into their eyes. As one comes in, Thursday leans into pets, head bunts, purrs and plays, plays, plays with a feather wand. A perfect encounter. Thursday, though does have a bite/scratch history(one quite serious) in the both households he lived in and he has shown in few occasions overstimulation translating into swatting and hissing at the care center, rather toward the beginning of his stay. This is why Thursday needs to be in an adult home only with a very experienced owner able to provide a quiet(while stimulating as Thursday is quite energetic), safe, stable while nurturing environment. Thursday is hoping that you will give him a chance at getting better and being a great pet!
