About:

Meet Haneul, a sweet and curious Jindo mix who's discovering just how wonderful the world can be!

Before her rescue, Haneul spent her life tied to a short rope in an empty yard, isolated from people and other dogs. Thanks to a compassionate person who recognized her situation and stepped in to help, Haneul finally has the chance to experience a life filled with love, freedom, and new adventures.

Despite her difficult past, Haneul has maintained a bright and curious spirit. Rather than letting unfamiliar surroundings hold her back, she approaches new experiences with wide-eyed curiosity, eager to explore the world around her.

Haneul is still learning what it means to receive affection and interact with other dogs. She can be a little unsure as she navigates these new experiences, but she's making progress at her own pace. She tends to feel especially comfortable around women, often warming up to them more quickly and showing her affectionate side.

When it comes to other dogs, Haneul is gradually building her social skills. With patient introductions and positive experiences, she's learning how to interact with her canine companions.

Haneul also enjoys going on walks, taking in all the new sights, sounds, and smells along the way. She's becoming more comfortable in urban environments as she continues to explore life beyond the small yard she once knew.

Haneul would benefit from a patient, understanding family who can give her the time and encouragement she needs to continue building confidence. With love, consistency, and plenty of opportunities to explore, she'll have the chance to discover everything she's been missing.

After spending so much of her life confined to a small space, Haneul is ready for a world full of possibilities. Could you be the one to help her discover it?
