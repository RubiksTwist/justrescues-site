Rice cake came into ACC as a stray; because of this, we do not know how she would be in a home environment. She was found in an abandoned building with 3 other dogs and 2 puppies. During her time in the care center she has been noted to fixate on toys and treat pouches, being difficult to redirect her attention away from them. She has been noted to fixate on a handler's treat pouch, then jump up and latch onto the treat pouch, treats do not redirect her, and the handler had to toss their entire treat pouch onto the floor. Rice cake has also been noted to engage in training with handlers. We recommend placement with a New Hope partner who can provide any necessary behavior modification (force-free, positive reinforcement-based) and re-evaluate behavior in a stable home environment before placement into a permanent home.
