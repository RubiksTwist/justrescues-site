Description provided by Waggytail Rescue

Skrimp is a 10 y/o, 8 lb, female chihuahua mix from Mexico.

She has settled into her foster mom's home super quickly in Brooklyn. She is very affectionate, calm and easy to please with a few treats throughout the day.

She isn't much for toys just yet but she loves to be by her person, snuggled up on the sofa next to them in her blanket or on their lap - and will take all the head rubs she can get. As for bedtime, she loves to bury under the sheets with her person and will cuddle up close if you let her.

Skrimp is a little shadow and will follow you around your home to ensure all is well. She will greatly appreciate it if you can leave the shower curtains open a crack so she can check and make sure you haven't slipped and fallen - 9 or 10 times / per shower. She's very diligent and makes sure her people are safe! When she isn't checking in she will happily nap the day away.

She is doing great on potty pads - hardly any accidents. And tries to save a poo for a walk. She walks great on the leash and enjoys a slow, short walk.

She is ok with dogs in the home as long as they mostly leave her be and will give a little growl if she needs them to backup a bit. She hasn't been tested with children but due to her relaxed, friendly nature we think she would be fine as long as they are gentle with her. The more company the better for this one.

Skrimpt would do best with a low key/ medium energy family who is around more often then not and will need continued support with her crate training as she has some separation anxiety and isn't keen on her crate.

Skrimp is on the skinnier side and needs some TLC getting to a healthy weight! She had a detected heart murmur at her last visit and it is recommended she get a cardiac enzyme test in 6 months.

Vaccinated for rabies, DHPP, and bordetella, microchipped and neutered. Heartworm negative and current on flea, tick, and heartworm prevention.

To apply for Skrimp, visit our website (www.waggytailrescue.org) for an adoption application. It is important to fill out an application as soon as possible for consideration, and we require a complete application for you to meet an animal. We are currently unable to respond to general email inquiries as an all-volunteer run rescue. If the dog is on our website, they are still available.
