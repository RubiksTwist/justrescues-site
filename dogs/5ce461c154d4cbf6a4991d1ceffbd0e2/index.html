Meet Dior!

Dior was surrendered when her owner could no longer care for her, and she's ready to find a loving home for her next chapter. She enjoys exploring and following interesting scents, and when she's ready for affection, she'll happily climb into your lap and lean right in! Dior may be a little independent at first, but she has a wonderfully cuddly side waiting to shine.

My Ideal Home

Dior will do best with a family who can give her plenty of enrichment and help her feel settled in her new surroundings. Long walks, puzzle toys, and fun activities will give her something positive to focus on and help keep her happy and engaged. A patient family using force free, reward based training will help Dior feel confident and comfortable.

Good with Other Pets?

Dior has lived with both cats and dogs in her previous home. She was described as tolerant and gentle with cats, and while we don't know all the details of her relationships with her previous canine housemates, this gives us a helpful glimpse into her experience living with other pets. Any introductions to new animal friends should still be slow, thoughtful, and supervised.

Good with Kids?

Dior's history with children is unknown, so introductions should be thoughtful and supervised. She can be enthusiastic when greeting people and may jump up to say hello, so respectful family members who can help her practice calm greetings will be a good fit.

Things to Know

Dior walks with mild pulling and can be quite interested in all the fascinating scents around her. She may jump up when passing her people, but she is also a big fan of cuddles and has been happy to lean into affection during handling. Her previous owner shared that she can feel anxious and have a hard time settling, so a consistent routine, plenty of enrichment, and positive reinforcement will help her feel right at home.
