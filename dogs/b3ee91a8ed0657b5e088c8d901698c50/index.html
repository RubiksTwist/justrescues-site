Please note: Any profiles with a "Pawling, NY" icon are located at the ASPCA Recovery & Rehabilitation Center, which is a 90-minute drive outside of the city. All other dogs are located in New York City.

Meet Vesper, who is ready to be your best friend and your go-to foodie sidekick!

Vesper loves meeting people and is always eager to say hello. She happily seeks out attention and while she’s making the rounds, she’ll certainly investigate whether anyone happens to have a snack to share. Vesper takes snacks very seriously, but she isn’t much of a food critic — even the simplest treat gets her stamp of approval! With her enthusiasm for good company and just about anything edible, Vesper is the kind of friend who would appreciate checking out all the best food spots in town right alongside you.

At the end of the day, Vesper is happiest spending time with people. If you’re looking for a new best friend to share your adventures and maybe a snack or two with, click the “Adopt Me” button to connect with Vesper’s team in Pawling, New York!

*We encourage a $150 suggested adoption fee when adopting an animal. This adoption fee allows the ASPCA to continue our lifesaving work and help even more animals in need. We are grateful for any amount given at the time of adoption.*
