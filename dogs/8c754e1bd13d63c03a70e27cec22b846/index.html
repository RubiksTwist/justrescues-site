Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

Lilly is the sweetest little lap dog in a 40lb dog’s body. There is no place she would rather be than snuggling next to (or on top of) her people. Lilly can be wary of new people, but feed her some treats and she’ll become your new best friend. In fact she’ll do almost anything for treats, and loves exercising her brain to extract every ounce of kibble or peanut butter from an enrichment toy. Equally at home in the city or the suburbs, Lilly is happy to go wherever you go. She will be your companion for life.

﻿Lilly is approximately 2y 4m 6d old and is fully grown at 39.2 pounds. They are microchipped and up to date on all age-appropriate medical care. Their breed is unknown as we have not had a DNA test done.

Although our dogs are listed as unknown breed, buildings with specific dog restrictions typically enforce their rules based on what breeds a dog appears to resemble. It's always important to keep that in mind and know your building's policies before adopting a dog.

This dog is available for adoption through Muddy Paws Rescue. If you're interested in adopting, the first thing you should do is begin the registration process at www.muddypawsrescue.org. If you are already a registered adopter with Muddy Paws Rescue, please email us at adoptionmeetings@muddypawsrescue.org.
