Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

Lilly is the sweetest little lap dog in a 40lb dog’s body. There is no place she would rather be than snuggling next to (or on top of) her people. Lilly can be wary of new people, but feed her some treats and she’ll quickly become your new best friend. In fact she’ll do almost anything for treats, and loves exercising her brain to extract every ounce of kibble or peanut butter from an enrichment toy. Equally at home in the city or the suburbs, Lilly is happy to go wherever you go. She will be your companion for life.

﻿Lilly is approximately 2y 3m 1d old and may be 30 to 50 pounds when fully grown. They are microchipped and up to date on all age-appropriate medical care. Their breed is unknown as we have not had a DNA test done.
