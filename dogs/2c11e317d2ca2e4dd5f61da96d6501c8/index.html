Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

Meet Marilyn, a sweet medium-sized girl who just needs a little time and patience to show you her wonderful personality! When Marilyn first arrived at the care center, she was very fearful and needed time to feel safe and build trust with staff. She spent her first few days socializing indoors before she felt comfortable enough to venture out into the yard. Since then, Marilyn has made wonderful progress and is now enjoying walks outside! As she has grown more comfortable, more of her personality has begun to shine through, including her love for being close to people and sitting right in their laps. Marilyn has shown that with patience, understanding, and a little time, she can learn to trust and let her affectionate side shine. She will do best with an experienced owner who understands that she may need additional time to adjust to a new home and settle into her surroundings at her own pace. In return, Marilyn has the potential to become a loving and devoted companion. If you have a gentle heart and are willing to give her the time she needs to feel safe, Marilyn could be the perfect friend for you!
