Marilyn is a very fearful dog who may not have had many opportunities for positive socialization in her previous home, where she lived with many other animals. Since arriving in our care, Marilyn has been extremely nervous and overwhelmed by her surroundings. When taken out of her kennel, she will often run to the nearest corner and hide. However, Marilyn has shown that she is capable of forming connections when given time and patience. When staff sit quietly with her and allow her to approach at her own pace, she has sought comfort by resting her head against them and allowing gentle pets. Marilyn will need a patient, understanding adopter who can give her the time and space she needs to build confidence and learn that she is safe. A calm, predictable home and a slow approach to new experiences will be important as Marilyn continues to come out of her shell.
