Description provided by Louie's Legacy Animal Rescue — Northeast

Clarissa is a 1-year-old, 20-pound mixed-breed pup with the perfect combination of playful energy and a gentle, affectionate heart. She is super curious and endlessly sweet. She's just as happy exploring the neighborhood as she is snuggled up beside her favorite person. She is also house-trained and loves being close to her human, whether she's napping nearby while you work or watch TV. She enjoys looking out the window, playing with toys, and especially playing with her foster sister.

Going outside is one of Clarissa's favorite things. She enthusiastically sniffs her way through every walk, taking in all the sights and smells on the way to the park. She's friendly with both people and other dogs, greets everyone happily, and takes treats very gently. She's also a good car passenger, making her a fun companion for adventures and weekend outings.

One of Clarissa's superpowers is her joyfulness. She is genuinely happy when she wakes up and sees her person, heads out for a walk, meets other pups, sniffs the grass, and, of course, for treats and meals.

She enjoys city life and walks confidently through Brooklyn, happily investigating every tree and interesting scent. She's unfazed by traffic and city noise. At home, she's calm and content, happiest simply being near her person.

Clarissa will melt your heart with her adorable Dobby ears and one-of-a-kind eyes—they're impossible to resist. If you're looking for a joyful little sidekick who's just as happy exploring the neighborhood as she is curling up beside you at the end of the day, Clarissa is your gal!

Clarissa is currently being fostered in Manhattan, NY and her adoption fee is $550.

For more information on the adoption fees and the adoption process, please complete our adoption application here: https://www.louieslegacy.org/adopt - an application allows us to get to know your family better so we can assess whether the animal you are interested in is a good match for your home. An application does not obligate you to adopt. Please understand that due to the volume of applications some animals receive, we are unable to contact all applicants. If your application is considered a good fit for the animal you are interested in, or even a different pet, a volunteer will give you a call as soon as possible.

We are a foster based rescue and do not have a shelter location where animals can be seen during the week. Please see our Facebook page at https://www.facebook.com/LouiesLegacy for our weekly adoption album if you are interested in meeting an animal in person. Not all animals attend every adoption event, please check our Facebook page for animal event times and locations before you make a trip to meet a specific animal."
