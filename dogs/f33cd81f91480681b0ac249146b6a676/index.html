Description provided by AMA Animal Rescue

Meet Cass, a gentle cat who’s ready to write her next chapter in a forever home. Cass was found bravely sheltering beneath a car, protecting her four kittens from the dangers of the outside world. Despite the hardships she’s faced, she has shown her strength and a sweet, nurturing spirit. Her kittens have since found safety, and now it’s Cass’s turn to receive the love and security she so deeply deserves.

Cass is looking for a patient and understanding adopter who can offer her the calm, stable environment she needs to feel safe and begin to truly thrive. She may be shy at first, but with time, kindness, and a few tasty treats (one of her favorite ways to warm up), she comes out of her shell. Cass is the kind of cat who builds trust slowly, but once she does, the bond is incredibly rewarding.

She would do best in a quiet home where she can decompress and grow comfortable at her own pace. With a soft voice, a gentle touch, and a few snack incentives, Cass will show you just how sweet and soulful she really is.

Cass is good with other cats and is currently thriving in a foster home with a young child!

If you’re someone with a kind heart and a little extra patience, Cass is ready to meet you. Apply to adopt her today and give this brave girl the second chance she’s been waiting for!
