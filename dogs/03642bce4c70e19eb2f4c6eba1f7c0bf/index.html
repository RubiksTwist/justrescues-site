My Boroughbreaker writes: Meet Marshmallowsita (264996)!

Marshmallowsita greeted me with so much energy and excitement that I could tell right away what a sweet girl she is. She left the shelter easily, and we headed to Central Park for a walk that ended up being more than five miles long!

She was easy to walk from the start: curious about her surroundings, responsive to gentle leash guidance or a sound, and never pulling toward people, children, or other dogs. Squirrels definitely caught her attention, though! Once we reached the park, she loved exploring the grass and wooded paths. She has a beautiful trot when she gets moving and seems like she'd be a wonderful companion for long walks or hikes.

Marshmallowsita is still young and full of life, but she also knows how to settle. During our breaks, she sat beside me or climbed onto my lap on a bench, calmly watching the world go by. She welcomed pets all over, made lovely eye contact, and returned affection with happy licks and excitement. She didn't show much interest in the dogs we passed, though she seemed comfortable around them.

I didn't see her respond to any commands yet, but she enthusiastically accepted every kind of treat I offered, which should make training together a joy. She struck me as smart, loving, and wonderfully sweet. I hope this beautiful girl finds her person soon-she has so much love to give.
