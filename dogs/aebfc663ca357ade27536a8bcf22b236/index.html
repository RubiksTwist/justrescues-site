Description provided by Waldo's Rescue Pen

The cutest personality ever! He's like a little toddler wobbling around, full of playful energy and so much love to give. He's incredibly affectionate, gives the sweetest kisses and cuddles, and has the most adorable little puppy waddle. He already knows "sit" and does it SO cutely. And did I mention he has THUMBS on his back paws?! (Yes, actual thumbs!)

He's good with other dogs and kids. We're unsure how he does with cats.

Personality: He's a happy, playful, loving little guy! He can be a bit shy when meeting new people and may bark initially until he feels comfortable. Once he warms up, his sweet personality shines. He's not much of a barker otherwise.

Potty Training: He's mostly pee-pad trained. He hasn't had the opportunity to learn to potty outside yet, as he's been an indoor-only puppy. He'll need some patience and consistency as he learns this new routine.

Crate Training: We're currently working on crate training. It takes him a little while to settle down, but once he does, he curls up and falls right asleep.

Training: He's very food-motivated, which makes learning fun! He knows "sit," and we're currently working on "lay down."

Favorite Things:

  • Peanut butter stuffed Kongs
  • Licking treats off his lick mat
  • Playing tug-of-war
  • Giving kisses and getting cuddles
  • Playing with his people

Energy Level: Typical puppy energy! He loves to play hard, but he also takes plenty of naps throughout the day. He alternates between playful bursts of energy and adorable little puppy snoozes.

He's a floppy, goofy, affectionate little puppy who is going to bring so much joy to his forever family!

ALL adoptions require a completed application and final contract. The application can be found at WaldosRescue.org and once you are approved to adopt you will be put in touch with the foster to see if it's a good match. We are a RESCUE not a shelter. We conduct veterinary and personal reference checks during the application approval process. We are unable to contact every applicant for every dog. We will select the best applicant for each dog, based on the dogs needs. Waldos Rescue Pen is a 501c3 whose primary focus is rescuing dogs from the euthanasia list from high kill shelters throughout the United States and Korea.
