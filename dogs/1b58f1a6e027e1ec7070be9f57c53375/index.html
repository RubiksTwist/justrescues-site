Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Hope is ready to fill your home with adventure, laughter, and plenty of cuddles. This beautiful five-year-old Great Pyrenees/Husky mix came from Georgia in search of a loving family. With her striking good looks, fluffy cream colored coat, and bright smile, she turns heads wherever she goes. As sweet as she is stunning, Hope loves people, enjoys the company of other dogs, and has a heart full of affection. In fact, she is convinced she is a lap dog. If you sit down, do not be surprised if she happily climbs into your lap for hugs. She already knows some basic commands and is eager to keep learning. An active home that can match her energetic spirit will be the perfect fit. Hope would do best in a home without cats, where she can be the center of all the love and attention. If you are looking for a loyal best friend who is equal parts playful and affectionate, Hope is waiting to meet you.
