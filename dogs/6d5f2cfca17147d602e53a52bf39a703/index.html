🖤 Meet Bastet! 🖤

Beautiful Bastet is a petite black beauty who deserves nothing less than a fresh start and a lifetime of love. She was rescued from our city shelter’s euthanasia list simply because they didn’t have the resources to give her the care she needed. Thankfully, we were able to step in, provide her with thorough medical attention, and give her the second chance she deserved!

Today, Bastet is in good health and officially ready to find her forever home! Beyond her beautiful appearance, she’s a sweet, loving little girl with so much affection to give. She gets along wonderfully with other cats, especially young kittens, and would make a lovely companion in a home with feline friends. That said, she’s also perfectly happy being your one and only.

Bastet’s story could have ended very differently, but now she’s ready for the chapter she’s always deserved — one filled with comfort, security, and unconditional love.

Could you be the fresh start Bastet has been waiting for? 🖤🐾
