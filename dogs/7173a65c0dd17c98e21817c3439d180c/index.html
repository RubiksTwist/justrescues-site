Description provided by Animal Lighthouse Rescue

Introducing Maisie! At just 5 months old and 15 pounds, this adorable Lab mix is ready to steal your heart! Her big brown eyes are impossible to resist, and her sweet, snuggly personality makes her even more lovable. Maisie has a playful side, too! She loves romping around, wrestling with her littermates, and making the most of every opportunity for fun. Whether she's tagging along for a morning coffee shop stop, joining you for an afternoon at the park, or curling up beside you after a day of adventures, Maisie would love to be your little sidekick. She's still working on her puppy manners and training, but she's food motivated and eager to learn. With a consistent routine, positive reinforcement, and a little patience, she's sure to blossom into an amazing companion. If you're looking for a sweet, playful pup with big eyes, an even bigger heart, and a lifetime of love to give, Maisie might just be your girl!

Maisie is spayed, microchipped, and up to date on her vaccinations. For more information about her, please fill out an application at www.ALRcares.com and email Kate@alrcares.com. We schedule meet and greets for approved adopters only, an application is the first step in our approval process. Thank you!
