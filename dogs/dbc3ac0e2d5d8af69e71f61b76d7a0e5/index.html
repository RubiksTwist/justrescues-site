Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Meet Honey Bun — we couldn’t think of a more perfect name for this sweet girl. Honey Bun is a 7-month-old Great Pyrenees puppy who weighs 48 pounds. There are few things Honey Bun loves more than being with people. New friends and old, Honey Bun will greet you with full body hugs and puppy eyes that are sure to capture your heart. She is adjusting well to New York life and walks well on a leash, calmly greeting other dogs who are interested in making a new friend. Honey Bun is still a puppy and will thrive with a forever family that can continue to teach her the ropes of being a confident companion. Give this girl a chance and you’ll be rewarded with the sweetest little bun to wake up to everyday.
