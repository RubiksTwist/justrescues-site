Description provided by NYC Second Chance Rescue

Meet Romilly! This sweet girl has been through a lot, but she hasn’t let that stop her from looking for love. Found in Mississippi with a serious eye injury, Romilly is believed to have been attacked by another animal, leaving her permanently blind in one eye. But don’t let that fool you, this girl still has so much love to give! Romilly is sweet, affectionate, and ready to leave her difficult past behind for a future filled with cozy naps, happy moments, and a family to call her own. She may see the world a little differently, but she deserves all the same love and happiness as any other pup. Could you be the one to give Romilly her happily ever after?
