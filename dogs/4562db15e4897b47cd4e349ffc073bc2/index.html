🤎 Meet Mizuky— a true hidden gem! 🤎

This beautiful girl was an owner surrender from our cities municipal shelter. She was being placed on the at-risk euthanasia list because she was extremely overstimulated by the shelter environment. When we saw her face, we knew we had to change that fate. We knew she deserved a chance, so we brought her into our rescue — and she has absolutely blossomed. ❤️

She adjusted beautifully and quickly showed us her true personality. She is sweet, affectionate, friendly, and full of love. She’s not shy at all — in fact, she’ll happily come right over to greet you and will almost always find a way to rub against your legs for some attention and affection. 🥰

Sadly, despite how wonderful she is, she somehow continues to be overlooked. With more than 40 cats in our rescue, this isn’t uncommon — this gorgeous girl has been waiting far too long for someone to notice her.

She is the kind of cat who will make herself part of your everyday life, happily weaving around your legs and reminding you that she’s there. She deserves a family who will appreciate her affectionate, loving personality and give her the forever home she’s been waiting for. 💕

She was given a second chance when she was rescued from the euthanasia list. Now she just needs someone to give her the final piece of the story: a forever home. 🏡🤎

Could you be the person who finally chooses her?
