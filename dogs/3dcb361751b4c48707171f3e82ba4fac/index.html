Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Some folks find a rainbow after the storm, but we got something even sweeter - miss Eden! This playful little pup traveled all the way from Alabama, after being flown to New York out of harm's way of Hurricane Isaias. This helped make room for more pups who would need shelter in the aftermath of that strong storm. Now that Eden’s made it safely through the storm, she’s ready to start her next adventure, and honey, she’s got PLENTY of energy for it!

Eden is a happy-go-lucky little firecracker who believes life is one big invitation to PLAY! Whether she’s chasing toys, bouncing around with her puppy pals, or finding new ways to keep the fun going, this girl has enough pep in her step to brighten even the cloudiest day.

After a journey that started with a storm, Eden’s ready for a future filled with blue skies, open spaces, and a family who’ll make every day feel like a fresh start. The hurricane may have changed Eden’s course, but now she’s headed toward her very own happily-ever-after!
