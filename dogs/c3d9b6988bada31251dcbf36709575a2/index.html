Description provided by Animal Lighthouse Rescue

Introducing Leni! She is a sweet, gentle, and playful 4-month-old, 10-pound Lab mix who is ready to find her forever family! Leni loves a good game of fetch, although she's still working on that little detail about bringing the ball back! When she's not chasing toys, you can find her tossing her plushies into the air, squeaking them with pure joy, or happily chewing on a bone while she enjoys some downtime. If she's looking for a little extra attention, just watch her flop right over and offer up that adorable belly for rubs! This sweet girl loves romping around with her littermates and would be thrilled to have a family who enjoys playtime, leisurely walks to the park, and sunny afternoons spent soaking up some rays. She's got that perfect puppy mix of playful energy and gentle sweetness that makes it impossible not to fall in love! Leni is looking for a home where she can grow, explore, play, and soak up all the love her little heart can handle. If you're dreaming of puppy kisses, silly toy antics, belly rubs, and a loyal companion to share life's everyday adventures, Leni might just be your girl!

Leni is spayed microchipped, and up to date on her vaccinations. For more information about her, please fill out an application at www.ALRcares.com and email Lori@alrcares.com. We schedule meet and greets for approved adopters only, an application is the first step in our approval process. Thank you!
