Hi everyone, my name is Zayne, but I also go by "good boy" because that's what everyone calls me when they meet me! Don't be deceived by my silver beard, I still act young at heart and can keep up with any adventure whether outdoors for long walks or wanting to play both with my fosters or entertaining myself. I'd like to think the saying is true that "Dog's faces turns white because I've received so many kisses that the color's been wiped off my fur over the years".

Some would say I am the epitome of what it means to be 'Man's best friend', I am loyal, love companionship, cooperative and have unconditional love to give. I am up for any adventure you throw my way, as long as someone is there with me to share the experience with.
