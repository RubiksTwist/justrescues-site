Meet Dee Dee!

Dee Dee was found with her companion Giulia (260987) and is ready to find a home where she can feel safe, loved, and supported. This playful girl has a big personality and lots of energy to share! While shelter life can feel overwhelming for her, Dee Dee is looking for a patient family who can help her learn that the world is a fun and safe place.

My Ideal Home

Dee Dee would do best with an adopter who understands that she may need time and patience as she adjusts to a new environment. A predictable routine, plenty of enrichment, daily exercise, and positive reinforcement training will help her feel more comfortable and confident. She is ready to learn and would love a person who can help her channel her enthusiasm into fun activities.

Good with Other Pets?

Dee Dee arrived with another dog, Giulia (260987), but her history with unfamiliar dogs is unknown. Slow introductions are recommended when meeting new furry friends.

Good with Kids?

Due to Dee Dee's anxiety and difficulty settling in the shelter environment, she would do best in an adult-only home or a home without young children. Older children may be considered after a proper introduction.

Things to Know

Dee Dee is an active and enthusiastic pup who will benefit from daily physical activity and mental enrichment. Puzzle toys, long walks, and training games can help her stay engaged and build confidence. With patience, structure, and plenty of love, Dee Dee is ready to become someone's playful and devoted companion.
