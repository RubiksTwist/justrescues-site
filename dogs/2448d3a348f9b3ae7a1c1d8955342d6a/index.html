Meet Finley, your new best friend! Sweet as they come, she is currently living happily with two kids in the middle of Downtown Brooklyn, where she’s taken all the city noise, busy sidewalks, and everyday chaos completely in stride. In fact, we haven’t heard her make a single sound or bark since she arrived!
She absolutely loves her crate and seems to view it as her own cozy little safe space. But her favorite thing might just be a good belly rub which she will happily submit to 100% of the time. Affection is her middle name!
On walks, Finley is curious about the world around her and enjoys checking out both people and other dogs. She greets everyone with a relaxed body, a wagging tail, and a gentle, easygoing attitude. She’s not bothered by the sounds and activity of the city and seems to be taking her new surroundings in stride.
Finley is affectionate, gentle, curious, and incredibly easy to love. She’s the kind of dog who quietly wins you over and makes herself right at home. We feel very lucky to be her foster family and we can’t wait for her to find her forever home.
