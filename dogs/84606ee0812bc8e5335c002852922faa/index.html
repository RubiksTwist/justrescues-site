Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Meet Rhylee! This 3 year old girl is a whole lot of joy in one petite pup and everyone she meets is instantly a friend.

Rhylee is ALWAYS happy, is potty trained, and was a total volunteer favorite at the shelter. She’s been collecting fans everywhere she goes.

She responds to her name, knows “sit,” goes into her crate so easily, and snoozes once inside. Rhylee likes other dogs and loves people. We haven’t tested out how Rhylee does with kids yet but so far she hasn’t met a person she doesn’t like. She just wants to be close to her person and is a true cuddle bug!

Rhylee walks well on the leash but will sometimes pull towards other people to say hi. She is doing so well with treat training and redirecting! She is curious and learns very quickly.

Rhylee is an energetic girl and would do best with some time to run around, play, and engage in enrichment. She does great in an apartment setting with a nice long walk to get her day started:)

Rhylee has been at the shelter for over four months - most of that time in a pop-up crate - waiting to be seen. Even with her hardships, she has persevered so well and is a shining example of leading with hope and joy. Now she’s getting her chance to settle into foster care, and we can’t wait to keep learning more about her!

If you are looking for a sweet, loving, and CUTE addition to your life, look no further than Rhylee. If you adopt Rhylee, she will bring lots of laughs and love into your home, guaranteed!
