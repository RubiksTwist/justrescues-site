Description provided by Bideawee

Obi is what we lovingly call a gentle giant, and he has been waiting much longer than he should for someone to finally take him home. This big, handsome boy has completely captured the hearts of the staff and volunteers who know him best, and he has become a familiar, beloved presence around the shelter.
One of Obi’s favorite things to do is wander through the offices to say hello, making his quiet little rounds like he works here too. Those visits always brighten our day. He is playful, incredibly sweet, and happiest when he is spending time with his people. Obi enjoys a good chew toy, a cozy place to relax, and he is absolutely convinced that he belongs in someone’s lap. Truthfully, none of us have the heart to argue with him.
During his time in foster, Obi showed just how loving and devoted he can be. His foster family described him as a champion cuddler who simply wants to be close and soak up affection.
Now in his senior years, Obi is not asking for much. Just a soft place to rest, familiar faces to love, and someone willing to finally choose him after all this time. He would do best in a home with older children and as the only dog. Obi is not a fan of cats, but he has so much love left to give. We hope someone will see the wonderful companion he is and give this very good boy the home he has been patiently waiting for.
