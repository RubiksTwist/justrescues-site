Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

Hi, I'm Gretchen! I am a 69-pound lap dog who hasn't quite gotten the memo that I'm not a puppy anymore. My absolute favorite place in the whole world is draped right across my human on the couch, preferably with a belly rub in progress. Don't let my size fool you; I am a total snuggle bug at heart.

I originally came into foster care after my first family could no longer care for me. While it took me a little time to settle in, these past few months have been amazing. Now that I know my foster humans are here to stay, I have completely relaxed into my true self. I am a wonderfully goofy, affectionate, and slightly dramatic girl who will happily follow you from room to room just to be near you. My signature move is sleeping flat on my back with all four legs straight up in the air!

I am currently working on my excitement around other dogs, as they can make me a bit nervous, so I would do best as the only pup in the home. If you are a patient human who is willing to keep up with my training, I promise to reward you by being the most loyal, couch-warming companion you could ever ask for.

﻿Gretchen is approximately 7y 2m 6d old and is fully grown at 69 pounds. They are microchipped and up to date on all age-appropriate medical care. Their breed is unknown as we have not had a DNA test done.
