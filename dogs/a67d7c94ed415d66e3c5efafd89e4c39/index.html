Meet Peaches!

Peaches was found as a stray, and now she's ready to find the sweet life she deserves. True to her name, she's a peach of a pup who enjoys exploring her surroundings and taking things at her own pace. With a little patience and a lot of love, she's sure to blossom into a wonderful companion.

My Ideal Home

Peaches would love a calm, patient home where she can settle in comfortably and build confidence at her own speed. Gentle introductions, positive reinforcement, and a predictable routine will help this sweet girl feel safe and secure.

Good with Other Pets?

Because Peaches arrived as a stray, her history with other animals is unknown. Slow, thoughtful introductions to any resident pets are recommended.

Good with Kids?

Peaches would likely do best with older, respectful children who understand that she appreciates gentle interactions and the chance to warm up at her own pace.

Things to Know

Peaches walks nicely on a loose leash and enjoys exploring while checking in with her people. She has medical needs that ACC staff will be happy to discuss with you when you meet her. If you're looking for a gentle companion to spoil rotten, Peaches is ready to make life a little sweeter.
