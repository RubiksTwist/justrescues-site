Description provided by ASPCA Adoption Center

Dunaway is at the very start of her journey with you!

Dunaway is so incredibly brave, learning to trust and love with the courage of a lion. Shelters can be intimidating, but Dunaway is taking cues from her furry friends. While in our care, she has learned so much about herself, including that she loves pets! She especially loves having her head rubbed.

Dunaway is a gentle soul who is starting to ease into her true self. She’s relaxed and happy with her feline friends, even sharing her yummiest treats. Every day, we see her come into her own. Dunaway is hoping to find a loving home that will continue to show her how wonderful life can be.

Come meet Dunaway at the Adoption Center or click the “Adopt Me” button to learn more!

*You can meet animals located at the ASPCA Adoption Center during our walk-in hours (daily, noon to 6 p.m.). We encourage a $150 suggested adoption fee when adopting an animal. This adoption fee allows the ASPCA to continue our lifesaving work and help even more animals in need. We are grateful for any amount given at the time of adoption.*
