Meet Dilly!

Dilly is a gentle girl who is still finding her confidence in the shelter. Her history is a mystery, but she has shown that she is receptive to treats, approaches people readily, and is comfortable with gentle handling. She is looking for a patient person who can give her the time and space she needs to feel safe.

My Ideal Home

Dilly would do best in a calm, patient home where she can settle in at her own pace. With gentle encouragement and plenty of positive experiences, she can begin building her confidence and discovering all the good things life has to offer.

Good with Other Pets?

Dilly's history with other animals is unknown, but she has shown interest in other dogs and did not react negatively during a recent introduction. She was tense and communicated that she wanted some space, so any future dog introductions should be slow, gentle, and supervised.

Good with Kids?

Dilly has no known history with children and, because she is fearful right now, she would do best in a home without very young children under 5. She may be open to older children once everyone has had a chance to meet and get comfortable with one another.

Things to Know

Dilly has a loose, easy leash and has shown no reactivity toward people or dogs while walking. She may need some coaxing in new situations, but she is willing to follow her handler and is receptive to treats. Food puzzles, long lasting chews, hide and seek games, and gentle daily exercise can help her stay engaged while she continues to gain confidence.

A Volunteer Says

This gentle girl is so scared in her pop up crate, but once outside she immediately found comfort around the dogs nearby. She eventually settled down so sweetly at her volunteer's feet, lying right on top of them while calmly watching the other dogs play. Dilly is a sweet pup who just needs a soft place to land so she can start showing the magic that is already there.
