Description provided by NYC Second Chance Rescue

Arlo’s story began in Mississippi, where life had clearly not been kind to him. Despite everything he has endured, Arlo has held onto his sweet, gentle spirit and is ready to finally experience what it means to be loved. This handsome boy is affectionate, loyal, and happiest when he is near his people. Arlo deserves a family who will show him patience, kindness, and all the love he has been missing. After a difficult start, he is more than ready for his happily ever after.

All dogs will be altered, microchipped, and receive age-appropriate vaccines prior to adoption.
