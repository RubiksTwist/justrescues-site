Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

Mist (aka Misty aka Misty Moo aka Moo Moo aka Miss Moo) is a spunky, spirited girl who is pure sunshine in dog form (not to mention, she has the most adorable bunny ears).

Originally from Queens @nycacc and then @aspca, Mist has seamlessly settled into her foster home, acting like she's lived here forever and immediately making herself at home. She's convinced she’s a 20-pound lap dog and has never heard of personal space (seriously, there's no such thing as *too* close for this girl, she would crawl into your skin if she could). The fact that she is, in reality, a fully grown 60-pound house hippo is merely a technicality (don't be fooled by the number on the scale, she's a low rider). But once she looks at you with those sweet eyes and attempts to climb into your lap, you won't be able to say no.

Mist loves exploring new places (and following her foster around like a little shadow), taking long neighborhood walks with plenty of sniff breaks, and investigating everything the world has to offer. She’s also an excellent work-from-home coworker: happy to snooze beside you while you work (keep an ear out for her little snores!), provided you honor her policy of frequent play breaks.

Mist is potty trained, comfortable being on her own, knows “sit” and “paw,” loves other dogs (she became instant besties with her foster sister), and is nearly a perfect walker. She can be cautious when trying new things, but her adventurous spirit always wins out.

All she really needs is a cozy couch, some humans to adore, and a lap that she can insist is hers.

﻿Mist is approximately 4y 9m 4d old and is fully grown at 60 pounds. They are microchipped and up to date on all age-appropriate medical care. Their breed is unknown as we have not had a DNA test done.

Although our dogs are listed as unknown breed, buildings with specific dog restrictions typically enforce their rules based on what breeds a dog appears to resemble. It's always important to keep that in mind and know your building's policies before adopting a dog.
