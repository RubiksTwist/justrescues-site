Meet Blackjack, a distinguished young gentleman looking for a loving home!

Our favorite thing about Blackjack is how seriously he takes his self-care. You will often find him lounging in a cozy spot, curled up relaxing! When rest time is over, he’ll hop down and take in what’s around him before deciding on his next “to-do” for the day.

If you’re looking to become Blackjack’s best friend, the way to his heart is tasty treats! When food is around, Blackjack will become your best friend and rub against you to say thanks. He even likes to express how happy he is by trilling gently.

Once he can get back to his perch to relax, Blackjack enjoys tracking a mouse toy or laser around the room before stretching out for another lounge session.

Are you ready to meet a cat that will be an amazing reminder to relax and take things easy? Click the “Adopt Me” button to learn more about Blackjack!

Blackjack is bonded to Chess, so they need to go home together!
