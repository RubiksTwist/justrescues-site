Description provided by Waggytail Rescue

From Tijuana to the subway in three days flat, Chiquilina (aka Chicken Nugget) is already on her way to becoming a true New Yorker. 🗽

This 1-year-old, 12-lb Chihuahua/Dachshund joined us just last week, and is now looking for her forever family in NYC.

With her floppy ears, nougat coat and soulful brown eyes, she is the perfect little Fall companion. A few days in, she rides the subway like a pro, walks busy streets with confidence and has truly awesome leash manners. In the office, she is the most patient little coworker: no whining, no chewing, just napping in her bed and enjoying an occasional treat.

Little nugget is a champion snuggler on the couch, who’s also happy to chill next to you while you're busy working. She adores other dogs and is incredibly gentle with her boisterous 9-month-old foster sister. She lets her wrestle, then curls up with her for naps.

Nugget is spayed, treat-motivated and doing great with her potty training. She just needs a forever family to keep that consistency going. Just in time for the holidays, she’s ready for cozy nights and tiny sweaters.

Vaccinated for rabies, DHPP, and bordetella, heartworm negative, spayed and microchipped. Current on flea, tick, and heartworm prevention.

To apply for Chiquilina, visit our website (www.waggytailrescue.org) for an adoption application. It is important to fill out an application as soon as possible for consideration, and we require a complete application for you to meet an animal.
