Description provided by ASPCA Adoption Center

Diawara is a gorgeous boy with a curious mind and a playful heart.

He enjoys taking in the world around him, often observing the room and keeping an eye on all the action before deciding where he'd like to join in. When his besties are around, he’ll run up to them meowing to say hello and rub against their legs. When it’s time for pets, he’ll arch his back into them for more!

When it's time to play, Diawara is ready! Wand toys are a favorite, and he loves the chance to chase, pounce, and show off his playful side. Tasty treats are another way to win him over and make his day a little brighter.

With his thoughtful nature and fun-loving personality, Diawara offers the perfect balance of companionship and entertainment. If you're looking for a cat who can keep himself amused but also enjoys interactive play and time with his people, Diawara could be your perfect match! Interested in meeting Diawara?
