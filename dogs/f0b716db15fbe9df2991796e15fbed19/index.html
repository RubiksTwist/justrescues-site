Leon was surrendered to the care center by his previous owner due being unable to care for him for health reasons. Leon has been highly fearful in the care center, showing reluctance to leave his kennel, and trembling, baulking, and freezing in novel environments. In calm settings and with known handlers, he has shown social and affiliative behavior, soliciting petting and play. Due to his level of fear, we recommend placement with a New Hope partner who can allow for decompression, provide any necessary behavior modification (force-free, positive reinforcement-based), and re-evaluate behavior in a stable home environment before placement into a permanent home.
