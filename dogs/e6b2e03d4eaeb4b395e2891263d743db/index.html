Description provided by AMA Animal Rescue

Meet Echo, a senior sweetheart with a silly side, a playful heart, and plenty of spark left to share!

Don't let his age fool you. Echo still has so much life and personality in him! He loves playing with string toys, showing off his goofy side, and, above all, spending time with his favorite pickle stuffy. He's hoping his new family will spoil him with a pickle of his very own! Fancy toys? Not necessary. Give this boy an Amazon box and he'll be entertained for hours. It's the simple things that make Echo happy!

Echo previously suffered from diabetes, but we're so happy to share that he is now in remission! He's ready to enjoy his golden years surrounded by comfort, love, and all the little things that make life special. Whether it's playtime, cozy afternoons, or simply having someone to call his own, Echo has so much love left to give.

If you believe senior cats deserve just as much excitement, happiness, and love as anyone else, Echo is waiting for you. He's not done having adventures just yet, and he'd love to start his next chapter with you!
