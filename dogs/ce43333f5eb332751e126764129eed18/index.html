Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Nori is a happy-go-lucky 1-year-old mixed breed who weighs around 32 lbs and is very committed to making friends with absolutely everyone.

He loves people, loves other dogs, and approaches the world with the kind of enthusiasm that makes every walk feel like an event. He’s energetic, playful, curious, and always ready to sniff everything, explore somewhere new, or convince someone to stop and say hi.

Nori is still young and will do best with a patient adopter who’s excited to keep teaching him the ropes. The good news is that he’s eager to learn and genuinely interested in everything around him, so training should be a fun way to channel all that curiosity. He’s a sweet, social little guy with a lot of energy and even more personality.
