Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Looking for a massively adorable, loyal, and goofy? If so, meet Franny (otherwise known as Franny Banany).

At 5 years old and 65 lbs, funny little Franny is the perfect mix of both joy and humor.

Everyday Franny's top and most important priority is making sure her people are as happy as she is. She loves meeting new people and showering with them as much love and kisses as they will endure. Once she calms down and gets over realizing that she loves you so much, even if she see's you everyday, Franny loves to cuddle up and relax. While Franny can be incredibly goofy and playful, she is also laid-back, loves a good nap, and can often be found sleeping in the silliest positions. Above all else Franny loves her people. Her preference is to be as close as possible to lean into you for pets and snuggles and enjoying a few moments of reinforcing how much you two love one another.

In addition to being a social, friendly butterfly Franny, is also an amazing house guest. Franny is well behaved, house-trained and eager to please. Once she realizes she is safe in a new place, she doesn't mind being home alone and will greet you at the door with an equal amount of excited if you've been gone for hours or minutes. Franny is so fun to train and learns new tricks and skills so quickly. She's happy to learn new things for yummy treats or even words of affirmation. Franny absolutely adores other dogs and lives happily beside her foster sister, who she loves to cuddle up to on the couch and play with.

Besides getting super excited when she sees a potential dog friend, on walks Frany is wonder on leash. She stays at your side, always looking to you for encouragement.

Franny is full of zest for life in the way that makes humans really acknowledge what we have to be grateful for. Franny lives everyday with an incredible ampunt of joy, she wears her heart on her sleeve and she's taken the time to lay a really solid foundation into the dog she is growing to be.

Every day Franny is thankful to live a happy life - a life that's ready to be a part of a home that appreciates and fully accepts her, is that home yours?
