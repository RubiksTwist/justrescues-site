Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Looking for middle aged, mature and massively adorable? If so, meet Franny.

At 5 years old and just under 50 lbs, funny little Franny is the perfect mix of both joy and humor.

Everyday Franny's top and most important priority is meeting new people and showing with them as much love and kisses as they will endure. Once she calms down and gets over realizing that she loves you so much, even if she see's you everyday, you would have the privilege of sitting front row to Franny morphing into quirky girl with the most endearing playful habits. Don't be mistaken however, after Franny's wiggly, excitable and funny first impressions, you will find that her true peresonality is soft, gentle and demure side. In fact, when Franny is at her most vunerable her preference is to lean into you for pets, snuggles and a few moments of reinforcing how much you two love one another.

If being a social, friendly butterfly Franny is also an amazing house guest. Franny is well behaved, house-trained and eager to please, regardless if it be for yummy treats or kind words of affirmation. Franny is an extremely adaptable girl who will do wonderful in any home. Franny absolutely adores other dogs and even tolerates children contrary to how she barks at them in excitement on walks. On the contrary however, Franny is cool as a cucumber with small creatures and has been extremely unbothered and respectful of her guinea pig foster siblings.

Franny's ideal adopter is simply someone who agrees that its okay to continue to grow into your best self and being 5 years old and silly doesn't define Franny's value as a middle aged girl . Franny is full of zest for life and although its not in the typical energetic way, its in the way that makes humans really acknowledge what we have to be grateful for. Franny lives everyday with joy, she wears her heart on her sleeve and she's taken the time to lay a really solid foundation into the dog she is growing to be and her adopter would be privileged to journey along Franny's side as she reaches her full potential.

Also, if you haven't noticed, Every Last One rescue has helped Franny's outer beauty match her inner beauty. Franny originally joined the rescue with cherry eye which has been completely resolved and every day Franny is thankful to live a happy, pain free life - a life thats ready to be apart of a home that finally appreciates her, is that home yours? a
