Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

My foster writes:
Stand up, lower your hand, and wriggle your fingers at Lara, and you might just earn one of her signature paw taps! You'd never guess this petite young lady with the adorable white face has already been a mom, and she's ready to steal your heart right meow.
A shy gal, Lara is a purr-fessional daytime napper who tucks herself away. Around midnight, she comes out of her shell-meowing for quick pets, rolling adorably on the floor, exploring, and batting at her favorite worm ball. This sweet, gentle girl prefers keeping all four paws on the floor, has paws-itively great litter box manners, and loves wet food, dry food, and Delectables squeeze-ups right from your hand.
If you have a calm, quiet home where she can blossom at her own pace, Lara is ready to be your peaceful daytime shadow and your purr-fect midnight companion!

A volunteer writes:
Lara came to us with quite a number of cats, brought little by little from a house full of felines. At her young age of one, Lara had already had three litters. I am so glad she was brought in to put an end to further gestations and find her a safe, stable and loving home. Lara is very shy, I would even say fearful. She stays at the back of her cubby(coming out only to use her litter box), opening big eyes as my hand advances toward her to pet her. She complies with petting without committing. No a hiss, not a swat. Making herself small and not "reacting" one way or another to my "advances" is all she knows to do. I had two cats like her in my lifetime. It took time, patience and belief that I could turn them around. They eventually did after a while and are now the most lovely kitties, still a bit skittish with outsiders but lovely with me and my family. Lara needs out of a shelter environment. A low traffic home and an experienced new owner are needed to make her feel safe and allow her to thrive as a happy pet!
