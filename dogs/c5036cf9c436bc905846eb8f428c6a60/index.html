Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Taurus is a 7-month-old shepherd mix who weighs around 49 lbs and is an affectionate, playful goofball. He has a particularly charming way of saying hello: flopping himself right into you like personal space is optional.

He loves people, does great with other dogs, and is always happy to be part of whatever is going on. He’s also house-trained and good on the leash, which is a great foundation for a pup his age.

As a young shepherd mix, Taurus will do best with an adopter who’s ready to keep him mentally and physically engaged as he grows. He’ll benefit from training, enrichment, play, and plenty of opportunities to use that busy brain, and he’ll repay the effort with lots of affection, silliness, and full-body greetings.

Approved adopters interested in Taurus can email adopt@hbrdogs.com for more info. If you're not approved yet, apply today at hbrdogs.com.
