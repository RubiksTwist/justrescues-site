Description provided by Bobbi and the Strays

Given up by owners (along with his son, Casper) who could no longer care for him, Spikey is currently in foster and waiting to meet his new family! His foster mom said he is quite the cuddle bug and enjoys a nap under the covers! He’s not much of a talker and hasn’t barked once…even when he hears other dogs barking! The one thing Spikey doesn’t enjoy is sharing his meals with cats!

Spikey is an 12 year old Chihuahua who is up to date on vaccinations, microchipped, heartworm negative and will be neutered upon adoption. If you would like to meet this cutie pie, please fill out the adoption application on our website using a laptop or desktop (no phone or tablet, please) at https://bobbiandthestrays.org/adoption-page-new/ .
