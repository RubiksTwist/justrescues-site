Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Meet Noble - a gentleman as distinguished as his name.

At 2 years old Noble is the whole package - a true trifecta of handsome, well mannered and extremely well tempered.

In the home, Noble has wonderful house manners - being a quiet boy who rarely barks and an independent king - happy to hang out in his crate.

Throughout the day Noble does not need much to keep him occupied, often shifting between cuddling in his crate, to the foot of your bed and sometimes finding a sliver of sun from the nearest window.

Noble is always happy to respond when you call his name and reciprocate any affection you give however is also extremely low maintenance understanding that there is a time and to live life as well as relax.

Noble's baseline temperament is friendly and curious albeit he can initially be shy when meeting new people. Additionally, Noble is a dream come true on leash showing no reactivity, doing his business quickly and leisurely walking along side his handler. When Noble spots a furry friend he finds very intriguing, he may stop and stare in awe for just a moment before continuing on with his walk.

Noble has a past history of living with multiple dogs of all sizes and is completely unphased by the 3 cats in is foster home. The best way to describe Noble is cool, calm and collected and he'll thrive in a home that matches his laid back lifestyle.

Noble is not only easy on the eyes, he's truly easy on life being such a low maintenance companion whose happy to be by your side through the thick and thin of friendship and truly be a unwavering figure in the life of the lucky folks who adopt him.
