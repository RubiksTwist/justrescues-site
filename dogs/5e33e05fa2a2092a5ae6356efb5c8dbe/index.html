Lamu loves to be curled up on or next to you. If you move to a new room, she is not far behind, finding a new spot to get your attention or take a nap. She is a very vocal cat, chirping to announce herself when she comes to say hello. When playing, you can find her on her hind legs swatting a stick toy or dolphin diving at a toy mouse or spring. If you want a cat that shows you unconditional love, Lamu is the cat for you.
