Biggie Baby has not acclimated well to the shelter environment and has only been allowing for minimal handling from staff. In the kennel, he is moving away or retreating to a back corner of his kennel, trembling as he presses himself against the wall. Due to these concerns, we recommend that Biggie Baby be placed in an adult-only home through a New Hope Rescue partner who can provide any necessary behavior modification using force-free, positive reinforcement-based training to safely manage/modify any behavior White Lightning presents with outside of the Care Centers.
