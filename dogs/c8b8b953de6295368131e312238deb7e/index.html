Meet Brazil!

Brazil is a sweet, affectionate, and easygoing French Bulldog mix who loves being wherever her people are. She's friendly with new people, enjoys leisurely walks, and is happiest relaxing by your side. Brazil is house-trained, knows "come here" and "lay down," and has experience living with adults, teens, dogs, and cats. She can be a little shy in new environments, but once she settles in, she's a quiet and loving companion.

My Ideal Home: A calm, patient home where she can take her time adjusting and enjoy plenty of cozy downtime.

Good with Kids? She has lived with teens and is friendly and outgoing with children.

Good with Other Pets? She has lived with dogs and cats and has generally been tolerant. She may be shy with unfamiliar animals and prefers to interact on her own terms.

Overall Health: Brazil has brachycephalic (short-nosed) needs and will need a family prepared to provide appropriate care and monitoring for her breathing and heat tolerance.
