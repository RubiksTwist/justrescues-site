Meet Pete!
Pete was found as a stray, and this curious boy is ready to find a place to call home! He enjoys sniffing around and exploring his surroundings, and he'll happily approach people once he feels comfortable. Pete can be a little unsure when meeting someone new, but with some patience and a gentle approach, he's got the chance to make a wonderful companion. He's ready to find his person and start his next chapter!

My Ideal Home
Pete would love a calm home where he can settle in at his own pace. He enjoys exploring and follows along nicely on walks, with only mild pulling when something catches his attention. His ideal person will give him time to warm up, let him approach new experiences when he's ready, and use plenty of encouragement to help him build confidence.

Good with Other Pets?
Pete's history with other dogs is unknown since he came to us as a stray. During a recent introduction, he remained fairly neutral around another dog, but they did not complete a full greeting. Slow, thoughtful introductions will be important when meeting potential furry friends.

Good with Kids?
Pete may be intimidated by young children, so he would do best in a home without children under 5. He's a sensitive boy who appreciates a gentle approach and time to feel comfortable around new people.

Things to Know
Pete can be a little nervous when being handled, but he has shown that he can approach people readily when given the chance to do so on his own terms. He may need a little extra patience around his collar and during new experiences, so gentle handling and positive reinforcement will help him feel more secure. With a quiet place to land and someone who understands his pace, Pete is ready to put his best paw forward and find his perfect match!
