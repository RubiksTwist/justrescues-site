Pre-Screener Form

Nala came into the care center as an owner surrender due to her owners moving out of state and not being able to take her with them. Nala is noted in the prior home to be very affectionate and loves quality time with family. She is noted to become anxious when left alone and has destroyed household items. Nala has not acclimated well to the kennel environment and has allowed only minimal handling since intake. We recommend placement with a New Hope partner who can provide any necessary behavior modification (force-free, positive reinforcement-based) and re-evaluate behavior in a stable home environment before placement into a permanent home.

A volunteer writes: When I learn Nala is only a puppy, at 11 months old, her hesitancy to leave the building and explore the outdoors breaks my heart just a little bit more. Nala has been living in a crate in an overflow room since she arrived at the care center in late July. Since then, she's incrementally gained some more courage each time I take her out, happily accepting pets and treats when we spend time together.

Nala was surrendered when her guardians were moving and couldn't take her with them. They described her as an affectionate dog who loved quality time with her people. She even lived well with two cats. Her former family also says she was playful and outgoing with both familiar and unfamiliar dogs.

While she can be frightened of long walks on the street and loud noises, I see her sprightly puppy energy start to reveal itself when we're in the play yard. Recently, she's even started galloping in excitement when I toss around some toys! I can only hope that with some time, patience and encouragement, she'll continue to blossom into the confident pup I see she has the potential to be - ideally in the loving comfort of a foster or adoptive home.

Due to her fearfulness at the care center, Nala is currently available via a New Hope partner.
