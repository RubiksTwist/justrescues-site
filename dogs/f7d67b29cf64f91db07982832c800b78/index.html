Bentley is a sweet, friendly guy who loves being around people and getting lots of pets. As he gets comfortable with you, he’ll even lean his head against you for a little extra affection. His foster jokes that Bentley should carry a sign that says, “Pet me, I’m friendly,” because he stops in front of just about everyone he meets hoping for some attention!

Bentley can be energetic and playful but also knows when it’s time to relax! He’ll make a great, adorable, loving companion.
