Meet Renn!
Renn is a sweet, sensitive girl who may need a little time to feel comfortable, but once she does, she really appreciates gentle affection. She was found as a stray and came into care with two other large dogs, so we're still learning about her past. One thing we already know is that there's a very soft side to this beautiful girl, especially when she's getting quiet pets from someone she trusts.

My Ideal Home
Renn would love a peaceful home where she can settle in at her own pace and enjoy a predictable routine. Her ideal person will be patient, gentle, and happy to let Renn decide when she's ready to come say hello. Daily walks and enrichment will give her opportunities to explore, build confidence, and enjoy life at her own pace.

Good with Other Pets?
Renn came into care with two other large dogs, although we don't know much about their relationship. During a recent introduction at the shelter, she was quiet and cautious around another dog before choosing to head back out, so any future dog introductions should be slow and thoughtful.

Good with Kids?
Renn would do best in a home without young children under 5, where she can enjoy a calm environment and have plenty of choice about when she wants attention. She's a sensitive girl who will appreciate people who understand that trust is built one little step at a time.

Things to Know
Renn can be nervous in new situations, but she has a sweet way of showing when she's ready for affection. During her assessment, she leaned her head against her handler's leg and happily accepted pets, which gave us a glimpse of the lovely companion she can be when she feels safe.
