Meet Dunkin!

Dunkin arrived at ACC as a stray, so his past is a bit of a mystery. While he may need a little extra time to warm up, this sweet boy has already shown that he is willing to connect with people and happily approaches for treats. Dunkin is looking for a patient family who will help him feel safe, build confidence, and discover all the good things life has to offer.

My Ideal Home

Dunkin would thrive in a calm home with an adopter who understands that trust takes time. He will benefit from slow introductions, positive reinforcement training, daily exercise, and enrichment activities to help him feel comfortable and confident. With patience and plenty of encouragement, Dunkin is ready to become your loyal companion.

Good with Other Pets?

Dunkin's history with other animals is unknown. Since he arrived as a stray, we recommend slow and thoughtful introductions to any furry friends in the home to help everyone feel comfortable and set up for success.

Good with Kids?

Due to Dunkin's fearful behavior and need for gentle interactions, a home without young children under 5 is recommended. He would do best with people who can respect his boundaries and allow him to build confidence at his own pace.

Things to Know

Dunkin is a sensitive sweetheart who may need a little time before showing off his full personality. He enjoys treats, exploring, and taking things one step at a time. If you're looking for a pup who will reward your patience with trust and companionship, Dunkin could be the perfect blend of sweet and special.
