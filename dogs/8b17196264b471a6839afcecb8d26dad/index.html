Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

This beautiful girl came to us in 2024 after her owner was evicted and could no longer care for her. With her stunning eyes and gorgeous markings, Charlotte prefers to watch the world from the safety of her bed or curled up under a blanket, truly enjoying the quiet and simple joys in life.

While unsure of new faces at first, with a little patience and lots of soft pets, and maybe even some tasty treats, Charlotte is sure to come around quickly and offer companionship that is deeply meaningful and rewarding. If you’re ready to offer patience, kindness, and a safe place to land, Charlotte will reward you with a bond that’s as special as she is.
