ABOUT ME: Put the book down. I’m right here, and my head fits perfectly under your hand. I’m a friendly guy who loves people, loves a good cuddle, and will nudge you until the petting resumes. Treats are fine, I guess, but attention is my real currency. On walks I’ve got my own ideas about which way we’re going. Mostly, though, I’d rather be on the couch with you.

MY FOSTER SAYS: "We have a toy hedgehog that was positioned in such a way that it looked like it was staring at Kevin. Kevin kept grumbling at it, thinking that the toy was staring at him."

HISTORY

Kevin came to us from Selma, Alabama, where he was abandoned by his owner. He traveled to Brooklyn in late September 2026.

DETAILED PROFILE

Kevin is a sweet, gentle adult dog with moderate energy who loves people and asks for pets by rolling belly-up beside you. He's calm when meeting new people and has done well with the school-age kids and teens he's met. He plays nicely with his foster's dog, though he sometimes barks at certain dogs on walks. His experience with cats isn't known. He's house trained and settles in his crate with a treat. On leash he likes to sniff everything and can be stubborn about his route. He's been in foster about two months and is settling in well.

MEDICAL HISTORY
