Meet Mac - the missing piece to your Mac & Cheese!

Say hello to Mac, an adorable little pup with a name as comforting as his personality! Brought in by local animal control with his brother after no one claimed them - Mac can be a little shy and reserved when he first meets you, so he may need a little time to warm up and feel comfortable. But once he does, you’ll discover what a sweet, gentle, and loving boy he truly is.

He’s the kind of puppy who doesn’t need to be the loudest in the room to steal your heart. Give him some patience, a little encouragement, and the chance to come to you at his own pace, and you’ll get to watch this sweet boy blossom into the loving companion he’s meant to be.

He may be named after mac and cheese, but Mac is the real comfort food for your soul.
