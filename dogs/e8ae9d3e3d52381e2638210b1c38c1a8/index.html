Description provided by AMA Animal Rescue

This is little Octavius, a spunky guy ready to find his forever family.

Octavius was surrendered by his previous owner as a puppy, and the moment he was brought to us it was clear that he needed medical help. After a few weeks of care, Octavius showed so much improvement, and now he is ready to find his forever home.

This little guy has a lot of spark and personality! When Octavius finds his human, he loves them without reservations and looks to protect them. He's also a bit of a foodie and has an ambitious palette. Octavius loves sunbathing, and absolutely loves outdoor time.

We believe he would thrive in a home that can continue to provide the basic training he has been receiving, and a family with that can provide all the playtime and love he truly deserves. Octavius would benefit most from an adopter who has a home with a backyard, or someone who is often available to take him outside for walks.

Octavius also gets along with kids, cats, and other dogs!
