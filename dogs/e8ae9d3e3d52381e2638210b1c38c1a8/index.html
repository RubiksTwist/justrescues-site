Description provided by AMA Animal Rescue

Octavius came to us at just two months old from a terrible backyard breeding situation. He was tiny, severely malnourished, and very sick. He needed plenty of medical care, patience, and love to get where he is today. But his journey isn't over just yet. He's still waiting for the family who will finally give him the forever home he deserves.

This silly, lovable guy has a personality that brings joy to everyone he meets. He's a total velcro dog who absolutely loves his people and would happily spend his days soaking up attention, cuddles, and affection. He's been in a foster home with dogs, cats, and children, and we truly believe he has all the makings of a wonderful family dog!

Octavius just needs a family willing to give him the right guidance, continued training, and consistency to help him become the very best version of himself. He also loves his outdoor time, whether it's stretching out in the sunshine, sunbathing, or having room to run around and be his silly self.

After spending so much of his life waiting, Octavius is more than ready to be someone's beloved pup. He's given so much of himself already. Now it's his turn to be picked, loved, and never left behind again.

Could Octavius be the missing piece of your family? Apply to adopt him today!
