Meet Grandpa!
Grandpa is a mellow gentleman who believes life is best enjoyed at a leisurely pace. After his owner could no longer care for him, this sweet senior is looking for a cozy home to spend his golden years. His former family says some of his favorite activities are sleeping, enjoying a tasty treat, and playing with tennis balls. But his most adorable quirk? The moment he hears ice cubes clinking into a cup, he'll come right over hoping to snag a few of his own! Grandpa is ready to bring his easygoing charm to a family who will treasure him.

My Ideal Home
Grandpa would love a comfortable home where he can enjoy plenty of rest, short strolls, and time with his favorite people. He's used to one or two short walks a day and walks beautifully on a loose leash without pulling. He knows Sit and Go to Your Bed, and he'd be perfectly happy spending much of the day snoozing nearby. If you're looking for a relaxed companion who appreciates the quieter side of life, Grandpa could be your perfect match.

Good with Other Pets?
Grandpa has lived with both cats and dogs in his previous home. He also showed no response to other dogs during his assessment at ACC. Slow, thoughtful introductions with any resident pets will help everyone settle in comfortably.

Good with Kids?
Grandpa's history with children is unknown. His calm, easygoing nature makes him a lovely senior to get to know, and gentle, respectful introductions will help everyone start off comfortably.

Things to Know
Grandpa is a true couch potato who enjoys a peaceful routine and accepts gentle handling without fuss. He's had occasional indoor accidents a few times a month, so keeping him on a consistent bathroom schedule will help him stay comfortable. His former family also mentioned that he can get into the trash, so keeping tempting goodies out of reach is a good idea. Grandpa may be an old soul, but he's got plenty of little joys left to share, especially if ice cubes and Beggin' Strips are involved. Could you be the one to give this sweet senior the cozy retirement he deserves?
