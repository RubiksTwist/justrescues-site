Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Fax is a playful, sweet, energetic puppy who currently weighs around 27 lbs and is very serious about two things: learning everything she can and getting as much affection as possible.

She is extremely cuddly and loves being close to her people. Fax will happily accept pets pretty much anywhere, including her belly, paws, and ears, and she has a very funny way of asking for more. She’ll rub her head into you, slowly melt her whole body toward the floor, and somehow end up on her back waiting for belly rubs.

She loves toys, loves sniffing her way through walks, and is already making great progress with her leash manners. Fax picked up “sit” incredibly quickly and is eager to please, which makes training with her especially fun. She’s also doing really well in her crate, where she settles nicely, especially when someone is nearby.

Other dogs are very exciting to Fax, and she approaches new canine friends like a true social butterfly. She’s affectionate, curious, smart, and very ready to keep learning her way through puppyhood.
