Description provided by AMA Animal Rescue

Almond has already done the hardest job of her life. Rescued from a high-risk shelter while pregnant, she was given the chance to safely bring her beautiful puppies into the world. She poured all of her love into raising them, and now that her babies are beginning their own journeys, it's finally Almond's turn. After everything she's been through, this gentle mama is ready to trade worry for belly rubs, peaceful naps, and the unconditional love of a family who will cherish her just as much as she deserves.

As a large breed girl, Almond is looking for a home with experience handling big dogs and a family that's excited to help her continue building confidence through training and routine. She's taking this next chapter one step at a time as she settles back into simply being a dog, and she's hoping to find patient, loving people who will let her bloom at her own pace. If you're ready to give a deserving mama the fresh start she's been waiting for, Almond is ready to spend the rest of her life showing you just how grateful a rescued heart can be.
