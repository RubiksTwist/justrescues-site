Description provided by Waldo's Rescue Pen

Hi! Marvel is the biggest love bug you'll ever meet. He is absolutely a gentle giant—calm, loving, and sweet. Truly kind-natured. He loves to say hello to other dogs and people. He is house-trained and does not bark at all in the house. He loves curling up in his bed to take a nap, enjoys lots of love and kisses, and is the best cuddler!

Check out more pictures and videos of Marvel here:
https://www.instagram.com/dog_parents_of_nyc?srtk=MTI4amo3MG1lZGk1Zg==

Key things you need to know:
- Energy: 4. He is pretty calm at home and loves sniffing around (but remember, he is a total 10/10 on the handsome scale, just a beautiful, soft-furred boy who gets compliments and attention every time we go out for a walk).
- Noise: Quiet dog and only ever heard him bark when he wants to say hi to other dogs during our walks. He sleeps throughout the night in his bed.
- Walking manners: in familiar spaces, he heals, doesn't pull, and walks at your pace. But in unfamiliar spaces and around new dogs, he gets excited and still pulls on the leash. We're working on it!
- Appetite: He eats breakfast around 9am and dinner at 5pm and gobbles it all up. He is patient to wait for his food and gentle to take any treats.
- His favorite things are belly rubs. He also plays with his toys on his own—a rope and fish chew toy. He can sit, come, and knows to leave it.

Just a note: His ideal home would be spacious and have a big yard where he can roam freely. He is a couch potato who doesn't care for long walks each day.

We would have adopted him in a heartbeat, but sadly, we just don’t have the space he deserves. We can’t wait for him to find his loving forever family. Whoever you are, you’ll be incredibly lucky to have all the love he has to give.

ALL adoptions require a completed application and final contract. The application can be found at WaldosRescue.org and once you are approved to adopt you will be put in touch with the foster to see if it’s a good match. We are a RESCUE not a shelter. We conduct veterinary and personal reference checks during the application approval process. We are unable to contact every applicant for every dog. We will select the best applicant for each dog, based on the dogs needs. Waldos Rescue Pen is a 501c3 whose primary focus is rescuing dogs from the euthanasia list from high kill shelters throughout the United States and Korea.
