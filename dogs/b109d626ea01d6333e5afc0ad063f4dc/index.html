Meet Coffee! ☕🐾
Coffee is a sweet, playful girl who has spent the last 9 years in a home, so it's understandable that she is feeling fearful and anxious in care right now. She is adjusting to a lot of change and will need time, patience, and a calm environment to feel safe and let her personality shine.

When comfortable, Coffee is active, playful, and loves being around people. She enjoys chasing balls and sticks, going for walks, and riding in the car. She is a great leash walker and knows several cues, including sit, stay, down, "stay home," and "go sleep."

Coffee is looking for a patient family who will give her the time she needs to decompress and trust again. With a little patience, this playful girl could become a wonderful and loving companion! ❤️
