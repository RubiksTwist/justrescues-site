Description provided by Wags & Walks

Meet Jones! This 4-year-old Poodle mix is a smart, affectionate boy with plenty of personality. Jones is very playful, and has a mischievous streak that keeps life entertaining. One of his favorite party tricks is giving the cutest little wave when you hold a treat above his head and his adorable smile is guaranteed to brighten your day. Jones loves being with his people and is happiest when he's included in whatever you're doing, whether that's relaxing at home or spending time together throughout the day. He's already potty trained and eager to continue learning, especially when treats are involved. If you're looking for a sweet companion with a fun personality and just the right amount of charm, Jones is ready to meet you.
