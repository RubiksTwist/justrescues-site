Description provided by Rescue City

Dixie is a 1 year old, 48 pound Poodle mix who was found alongside the road with her mom, Dani, and littermate, Denver, by a worker from our partner shelter. These are some of the sweetest dogs we have ever met! Dixie is gentle, loving, and happiest getting all the affection and attention from people and other dogs. The three share such a sweet bond, and each is now learning the ropes in their fown oster home as we get to know their individual personalities. She has been spayed and vetted and has started a nutritious diet to help her gain some healthy weight. We can’t wait to watch this girl continue to blossom and find a forever family to love!
