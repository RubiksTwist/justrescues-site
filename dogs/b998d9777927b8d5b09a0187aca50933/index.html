My foster writes: Jack-Jack is an adorable, adventurous cat that loves to find comfy places to lounge. Another foster writes: Jack-Jack is a friendly, vocal, and playful companion. She loves greeting houseguests, pulling up a chair at gatherings, accompanying her humans around the house, and saying hello with morning greetings.
