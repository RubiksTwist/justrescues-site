Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Meet Amber! She just turned 2 and is slowly but surely remember what it's like to live in a home with her people. The first thing you'll notice about Amber is her markings, she's got such striking coloring on her petite little frame. She is quiet and polite, and walks amazing on leash minding her own business-- like a good new yorker! On walks, Amber loves to jump on and 'perch' so you're forced into taking a break to give her some pets. She loves affection and she loves giving hugs! She's young so she definitely has some energy to burn, but she settles so nicely whether you're out with her catching some sun or working from home and she's passed out on the couch nearby. She's housebroken and does well with a schedule, and will definitely alert you if someone is at the door but is easily redirected. Her interest in other dogs is typical, and she can easily walk by. She'd do best meeting a dog of similar size and temperament with a proper intro and knows basic commands like 'sit' and 'paw', but don't worry as she's a very quick study. She truly loves her people and is just 'happy to be here'.

She's the best of both worlds, a 'pocket princess' with a personality 10x her size. She's unphased by city life and as long as she's surrounded by her people, she'll do just fine.
