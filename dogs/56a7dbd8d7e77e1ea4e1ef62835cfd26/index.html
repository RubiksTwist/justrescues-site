Bessie is on the at-risk list due to behavior concerns. In the care center, Bessie has shown fearful behavior, including flinching, cowering, whale eye, head whipping, and attempting to flee from sudden sounds or movements. She has also shown discomfort with handlers being too close, becoming tense and avoidant during leashing and walks at times. Bessie has shown improvement over time, becoming more comfortable with familiar handlers, seeking attention, leaning into petting, and exploring her surroundings, but is still generally fearful in the care center. Medically, Bessie is apparently healthy.

Bessie came into the care center as a stray; due to this, her behavior in a home environment is unknown. She has not acclimated well to the kennel environment and has allowed only minimal handling since intake. We recommend placement with a New Hope partner who can provide any necessary behavior modification (force-free, positive reinforcement-based) and re-evaluate behavior in a stable home environment before placement into a permanent home.
