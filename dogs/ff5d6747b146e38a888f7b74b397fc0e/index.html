Meet Meatloaf

Meatloaf is a sweet little senior who has already been through far more than he ever should have.

He was found as a stray in Georgia, and it was clear that he had been neglected for a very long time. He was missing fur from chronic, untreated allergies, had painfully overgrown nails, ear infections, and dental issues. Looking at this little guy, it was hard not to wonder how long he had been struggling without anyone stepping in to help him.

But when we saw Meatloaf’s face, we knew we couldn’t let his story end there.

This sweet boy deserved to know what it feels like to be loved. He deserved to be comfortable, cared for, and surrounded by people who would see him as precious instead of letting him suffer. Especially now, in his senior years, we want Meatloaf to know that he matters.

And despite everything he has been through, Meatloaf is sweet and loving. He has so much tenderness left to give, and there is something incredibly special about seeing a senior dog finally get the chance to experience the love and care he should have known all along.

We may never be able to change Meatloaf’s past, but we can help make sure his future is very different.

Please consider opening your heart and home to this special little senior. He deserves to spend his golden years knowing exactly what it means to be loved.
