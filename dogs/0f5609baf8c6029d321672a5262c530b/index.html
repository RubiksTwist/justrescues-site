Description provided by NYC Second Chance Rescue

Meet Jiggy, a sweet boy who was at risk in a municipal shelter in Alabama before we stepped in to give him a second chance.

Now, Jiggy is ready to put his shelter days behind him and focus on the important things in life; finding his people, getting plenty of love, and finally having a place to call home.

There’s so much potential in every dog who comes through our doors, and Jiggy is no exception. He deserves someone who will give him the patience, consistency, and love every dog deserves while he settles into his new home.

An adoption bio is only a snapshot of who an animal is. For more information about Jiggy, including his personality, needs, and what type of home may be the best fit, please reach out to our Adoptions Team.

All dogs will be altered, microchipped, and receive age-appropriate vaccines prior to adoption.
