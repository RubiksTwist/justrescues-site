Description provided by PupStarz Rescue

Meet 55 Inch TV (aka Max), the sweetest, gentlest and quietest boy around! Max is a proud couch potato who would happily spend all day lounging on the sofa. If you sit down next to him, he’ll rest his head in your lap and soak up the pets and cuddles. His foster absolutely adores him, and it’s easy to see why.

Max loves the couch so much that getting him off it for a walk can take some convincing. Once he’s outside, though, he’s great. He walks well on a leash and happily greets the people and dogs he meets. He’s good with dogs, cats, kids and adults of all ages, though he isn’t very interested in playing with other pets, toys or treats. He’s fully house trained, doesn’t bark, travels well, knows “sit,” and has no resource guarding or bad habits at all. He can be a bit timid, so he’ll do best with someone patient and gentle who gives him time to feel safe.

Max would be perfect for a calm, loving home that wants a low-key companion for Netflix nights and easy strolls around the block. Give this gentle giant a cozy couch and a little patience, and he’ll give you all his love.

If you would like to adopt this Rockstar, please visit www.pupstarzrescue.org for an application!
