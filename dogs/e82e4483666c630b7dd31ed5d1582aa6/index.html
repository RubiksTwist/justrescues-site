Gumdrop, gumdrop, I can be your gumdrop! Meet our sweetest new adoptable, Gumdrop! Gumdrop is a 7-month-old and 13 pound Chihuahua mix. Gumdrop loves nothing more than staying close to his people. He is curious about what goes on behind closed doors, what you’re cooking, and the many wonders that New York City had to offer! Gumdrop would love nothing more than a forever family to continue to teach him the ropes of being a pampered puppy and solidify his already solid training foundation. Friendly with pups and people, Gumdrop is ready to be a loving companion to anyone who will shower him with love in return.
