Description provided by Bobbi and the Strays

Are you looking for a best friend who is very friendly, loving and super sweet? Well, then Griselda is your gal! Found as a stray, she is safe in our care and has been waiting to go to her forever home. She walks beautifully on leash and is completely housebroken. She will flourish in an active, adult only home. This beauty needs time to warm up to new people and will require a few visits while she gets to know you. She will do best as the only dog in the home.

Griselda is a 8 1/2 year old Husky mix who is up to date on vaccinations, microchipped, heart worm negative and spayed. She is currently in Freeport. If you would like to meet this beautiful dog, please fill out the adoption application on our website using a laptop or desktop (no phone or tablet, please) at https://bobbiandthestrays.org/adoption-page-new/ .
