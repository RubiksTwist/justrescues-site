Meet Lily Pad, a 1-year-old girl whose joyful energy and bright expressive eyes are guaranteed to light up any room. This sweet, playful girl brings pure sunshine wherever she goes, whether she is exploring outdoors on a walk, lounging happily in the grass, or settling down indoors to shower you with affection.

As a young pup, Lily Pad balances her eager, curious energy with a gentle, attentive nature that makes her the ultimate companion for everyday adventures and cozy hangouts alike. She’s great with other dogs and love the outdoors; one of her favorite activities is running! She can be a little shy with strangers initially but very affectionate with her people and always eager to learn new things.

She will thrive with an adopter who can guide her consistency, and provide her with the outlets for her energy.
