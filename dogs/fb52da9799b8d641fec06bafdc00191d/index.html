Lily Pad is an 8-month-old retriever mix weighing around 35 pounds, and she's at that wonderful age where she's ready to learn, explore, and soak up everything life has to offer.

Like many young dogs, Lily has plenty of puppy energy and will thrive with an adopter who's excited to continue her training and help her become the best version of herself. She can be a little shy when meeting new people, but once she feels comfortable, her sweet personality shines. She loves being close to her people and is always happy to roll over for belly rubs whenever the opportunity presents itself.

Lily Pad is a loving, affectionate pup who's looking for someone ready to invest in her during this important stage of her life. With a little guidance, plenty of patience, and lots of love, she's sure to grow into an incredible companion.
