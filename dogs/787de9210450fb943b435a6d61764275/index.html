Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Cornmeal is a 3-year-old mixed breed who weighs around 16 lbs and is the definition of a little sweetheart. She’s shy and timid at first, but there’s a very affectionate girl underneath who gives the sweetest kisses once she starts to feel comfortable.

She’s also very food motivated, which is a great way to help her build confidence and make new experiences feel a little easier. Cornmeal is house-trained, crate-trained, and seems happiest when she can take things at her own pace with people who are willing to let her warm up naturally.

She may not be the first dog to rush over and introduce herself, but give her a little time, a few treats, and some patience, and you’ll get to see just how loving she really is.
