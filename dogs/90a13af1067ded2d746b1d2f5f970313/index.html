Maple is a goofy, lovable puppy who has a special talent for making everyone around her smile. Whether she's being silly or simply toddling around on her tiny little legs, there's something about this little girl that's guaranteed to brighten your day. She's expected to be medium-sized when fully grown.

Like any puppy, Maple has plenty to learn as she grows and will need a patient, committed adopter who is ready to provide training, socialization, structure, and lots of love along the way. With the right guidance, Maple is ready to grow into a wonderful companion while keeping her family laughing in the process.
