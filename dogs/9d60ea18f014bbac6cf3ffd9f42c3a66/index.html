Meet Sierra!

Sierra arrived at ACC as a stray, so her history is a bit of a mystery, but this sweet girl is ready to start a brand new chapter. Sierra is a curious pup who enjoys exploring her surroundings and happily approaches people with a soft body and wagging tail. She may still be learning about the world around her, but she is showing that she has plenty of potential to become someone's perfect companion.

My Ideal Home

Sierra would do best with an adopter who can provide daily exercise, enrichment, and positive reinforcement training to help her continue learning and growing. Activities like food puzzles, long lasting chews, and fun games will help keep her engaged and give her plenty of opportunities to show off her smart side. With patience and consistency, Sierra is ready to become your new adventure buddy.

Good with Other Pets?

Since Sierra arrived as a stray, her history with other animals is unknown. She has not shown any concerns around other dogs while in our care, but slow and thoughtful introductions are always recommended when meeting new furry friends.

Good with Kids?

Due to observed behaviors and Sierra's need for continued training and guidance, an adult only home is recommended at this time. She would do best with people who can help her practice polite interactions and set her up for success.

Things to Know

Sierra is a sweet and curious pup who loves exploring, accepting treats, and spending time with people. She is still working on learning appropriate ways to show affection and would benefit from positive reinforcement training to help her practice good manners. If you're looking for a dog with a lot of potential and a heart ready to grow, Sierra may be the perfect match!
