Description provided by Waldo's Rescue Pen

Hi, I'm Tesslee, lovingly called Tessie! I came up from Georgia and now I'm a city girl in NYC. I'm a super sweet puppy and I love everyone I meet. Dogs, kids, strangers, I'm friendly with all of them. I'm also really smart, and I pick up tricks fast, especially if there's a treat involved.

I nap a lot - especially on someone’s lap! - but when I'm awake I like playing with my toys and going for walks. I sleep in my own bed at night, but during the day my favorite nap spot is on my foster mom's tummy. Right now I'm teething, so I nip sometimes when we play. I'm working on it! I'm still settling in, but I'm so ready to find my forever home.

Personality & Characteristics

My foster dog is good with: Dogs (she's great with them) and kids (calm). Not tested with cats.

My foster dog is: Mostly house trained (sleeps through the night in her own bed with no accidents), low shed. Not crate trained but learning.

3 Keywords for your pup: Sweet, Smart, Playful

Energy Level (1 = calm, 10 = high energy): 8 when she's awake, but she naps most of the day.

Barker? Or Quiet Pup?: Quiet. She hardly ever barks.

Walking Manners: Still learning, but she loves going outside

Appetite: Very food motivated

Favorite Things: Toys, cuddles, belly rubs, attention, napping, playing, walks, treats

Commands/Tricks: Sit and paw. She's working on leash skills now.

Ideal Home
Tessie needs someone patient who understands that she’s a young puppy. She's smart and loves treats, so training is easy. She does fine alone for 3 to 4 hours during the day and longer overnight once she's settled, so a home where she isn't left all day would be best. She'd be great with another dog who can play with her, and she's already doing well with city life. Mostly she wants lots of attention when she’s awake and a lap to nap on the rest of the time
