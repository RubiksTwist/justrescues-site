Pre-Screener Form

Maple arrived at MACC as a stray with no known history. While in care, Maple has been observed to be fearful noted to be frozen when handlers interact, and will have a tucked tail and pinned ears. During one instance when a staff member attempted to untangle Maple from her harness, Maple rushed towards the handler and bit them on the ankle. Due to the detailed behaviors, we recommend an experienced adult only home.

Meet Maple!

Maple has had a pretty big change of scenery after being found tied to a bench, and she's still figuring out that people can be trusted. She's a gentle girl who starts out timid but warms up with a slow, patient approach. Once she feels comfortable, she'll come closer for treats and attention and even wag her tail for you.

My Ideal Home

Maple is looking for a calm, patient home where she can take her time settling in. She'll do best with someone who understands that trust takes time and will let her choose when she's ready to interact. Predictable routines, positive reinforcement, and plenty of quiet time will help her feel safe.

Good with Other Pets?

Maple has been observed barking at dogs passing her kennel, so she would benefit from careful introductions and plenty of distance from other dogs while she gets comfortable. Her history with other pets is unknown.

Good with Kids?

Because Maple is currently fearful and sensitive to new experiences, ACC recommends a home without children under 13.

Things to Know

Maple can become anxious when she's overwhelmed, but she responds well to a gentle approach and treats. She's going to need someone who can give her the time and space to come out of her shell at her own pace. If you're looking for a quiet companion and are willing to let a sweet girl write her own happily ever after, Maple may be your perfect match.
