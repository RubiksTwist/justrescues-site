Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Meet Ladybug! Just like a Ladybug, this 7 week old girl isnt only dainty and precious, she seems to be a good luck charm too!

She's sparkly, spunky little puppy with a big personality and a sweetness that will steal your heart in an instant!

Ladybug is affectionate, energetic, curious, and confident, with a wonderful independent streak. But make no mistake, this girl is a total people pup! She loves being cuddled, held, and showered with affection. She has the sweetest, most focused eyes and really locks in on her humans, making you feel like you're her whole world.

She’s an alert and inquisitive puppy who loves exploring everything around her. She’s also perfectly happy entertaining herself with some independent playtime, but her absolute favorite activity? Romping around with the big dogs! Ladybug absolutely loves other dogs and is learning so much from her foster siblings. She has great puppy manners and a respectful, appropriate play style. Her confidence is seriously impressive, she happily joins in with her two 45-pound pittie foster sisters and seems to have no idea that she’s still a baby! She's also incredibly respectful of her 5 pound, 16-year-old Chihuahua foster sister, who has absolutely no interest in entertaining puppy antics. We love a respectful queen. She would also do wonderfully in a home without other dogs. As much as she enjoys playing with her foster siblings, Ladybug has that special “I’m your girl” personality. IF she has you, she's a happy camper.

Her foster mom says some of her favorite things about Ladybug are:

That tail! It NEVER stops wagging. She’s one of the happiest puppies she’s ever met.

Her sweet, focused eyes. Ladybug really connects with her humans and has a way of making you feel very special.

Her prancing walk and bunny hops. She struts around like she owns the room!

Her confidence and independence. This little girl thinks she can keep up with everyone, and she absolutely does!

How loving she is. Ladybug melts right into your arms and gives the sweetest puppy kisses.

How smart and trainable she is. She’s picking things up quickly! She’s working on SIT and STAY, knows COME, and is making great progress with her crate training.

Ladybug is a truly special puppy with a huge personality packed into one adorable little body. She’s playful, loving, confident, curious, and full of joy and she’s going to bring so much life and happiness to her forever home.
