Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Meet Ladybug! She's a 10 week old pup who is happy, sassy, playful girl who is just starting out in life and is extremely excited for all it has to offer!

Ladybug is affectionate, energetic, curious, and confident, with a wonderful independent streak. She LOVES to cuddle, give you kisses and soak up all of the pets you have to offer. She has the sweetest demeanor, is so inquisitive and loves to learn new things.

Ladybug loves other dogs and is learning so much from them! She plays respectfully and seems to understand how each dog likes to engage from the moment she meets them. We think she would do great in a home with another dog! Ladybug is also incredibly smart. She is listening well, knows to come when She's incredibly smart and is quickly picking up on new things, working hard on her pee pad training (she is too young to walk the city streets yet) and is a crate trained queen!

This girl has become such a great sleeper through the night! When she wakes up in the morning her tail is wagging and she is happy from the moment her eyes open. She is a great eater and doesn't scarf down her meals like most puppies. She likes to take her time and enjoy it! During the day she is fine being left alone in her crate for a few hours and will happily snooze in her bed, play with a toy or enjoy some enrichment. And when the day is done Ladybug loves to curl up and cuddle with you while you watch TV or clean up the house.

Ladybug is a truly special puppy with a huge personality packed into one adorable little body. She’s playful, loving, confident, curious, and full of joy and she’s going to bring so much life and happiness to her forever home.

If you want to make her your forever pup, submit an application!
