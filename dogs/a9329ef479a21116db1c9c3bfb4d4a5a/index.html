Meet Monie!

Monie was found in an apartment and is ready to find the loving home she deserves. This sweet girl is a total people person who lights up when she gets attention. With her wagging tail, happy personality, and love for cuddles, Monie is the kind of companion who will happily be your new best friend.

My Ideal Home

Monie is looking for a home that can help her channel her excitement into good manners. She is an energetic, playful girl who would love daily walks, enrichment, and positive reinforcement training. She's eager to engage with people and loves learning, making her a fun partner for someone ready to continue building her skills.

Good with Other Pets?

Monie's history with other animals is unknown. Please speak with our staff about the best way to introduce her to any resident pets.

Good with Kids?

Due to Monie's mouthy behavior and enthusiastic play style, we recommend an adult-only home at this time. She would do best with people who can help her practice polite greetings and respectful play.

Things to Know

Monie is a social butterfly who loves making connections with people. She is eager to engage, enjoys affection, and has a playful personality that makes every interaction fun. She can get a little overexcited and may jump up or use her mouth when playing, but with consistent positive reinforcement and fun training games, she is ready to learn.
