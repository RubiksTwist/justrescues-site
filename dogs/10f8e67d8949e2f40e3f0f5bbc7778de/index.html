Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

Bugs is the chillest husky you'll meet. He's a city dog through and through — nothing phases him. On Labor Day, a million people descended on our neighborhood for the West Indian Day Parade, but when we took Bugs out for his walks he didn't eat any of the (loads of) trash left on the sidewalks, ignored the (frankly, overwhelming) crowds of people, and didn't react to the intensely loud volume. He will try to say a friendly hello to dogs while on walks, but we're working on training/redirecting him out of that. In the apartment, he just likes to be near his people, happily lounging around, napping, and cuddling up to us while we work. He has only gotten vocal and barked when alerting us that he needs to go out or wants some play time. He has no destructive tendencies that we've seen, doesn't beg for food, doesn't jump up on folks, and sleeps through the night. He's a total sweetheart.

﻿Bugs is approximately 6y 0m 28d old and is fully grown at 44 pounds. They are microchipped and up to date on all age-appropriate medical care. Their breed is unknown as we have not had a DNA test done.
