Description provided by NYC Second Chance Rescue

Meet Mama Maude Pie, a gentle soul with a heart full of love.

She was found wandering the streets of New York as a stray,and to everyone’s surprise, she was expecting. We don’t know how or why Maude ended up alone, pregnant, and without safety, but our hearts ache for what she must have endured.

She landed at ACC, where unfortunately the resources weren’t available to support her and her future litter. That’s when we stepped in. Now Mama Maude Pie is safe with us, cared for, and finally able to rest.

Maude is sweet, loving, and so deserving of a home where she never has to worry about her next meal or facing the world alone again. She’s ready for a fresh start with a family who will cherish her for the rest of her days.

Please adopt Mama Maude Pie and give her the security and love she’s always deserved.

All dogs will be altered, microchipped, and receive age-appropriate vaccines prior to adoption.
