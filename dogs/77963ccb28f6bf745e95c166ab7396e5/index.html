Description provided by NYC Second Chance Rescue

Say hello to Salish, a tender-hearted girl who got her second chance in the nick of time. She was surrendered to a crowded Georgia shelter and placed on the euthanasia list simply because there was no room left. Once rescued, Salish blossomed into a sweet, affectionate companion who just wants kindness and a place to belong. She’s ready for her forever home, one that will finally give her the love she’s been waiting for.

All dogs will be altered, microchipped, and receive age-appropriate vaccines prior to adoption.
