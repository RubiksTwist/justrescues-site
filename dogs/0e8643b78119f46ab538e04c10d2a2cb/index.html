Description provided by Animal Lighthouse Rescue

Introducing Lucy! At 2 years old and 35 pounds, Lucy is the perfect-sized bundle of affection. She absolutely adores being around people and gets along wonderfully with adults, children, other dogs, and even cats! Lucy can be a little shy when first meeting someone, but give her a little time to warm up, and you'll discover the sweetest, most loving companion. Once she feels comfortable, she'll happily plop her whole body right next to you, soaking up all the love and reminding you that personal space is overrated. Lucy is a girl who appreciates the simple things in life. Her idea of a perfect day is lounging on the back porch, basking in the sunshine, and enjoying every little moment outdoors. And if you're someone who likes to spend time working in the garage, Lucy is more than happy to be your sidekick. Just give her a cozy blanket, and she'll happily settle in nearby, content just to be wherever you are. Lucy's journey to find her forever home started in Colombia, where she was discovered by a group of tourists. They saw something special in this sweet girl and brought her to the United States, hoping to give her the opportunity to find the loving family she deserves. Now, Lucy is ready for the next chapter of her story. She just wants a family to love, a cozy spot beside her favorite people, a little sunshine, and a place she can finally call home.

Lucy is spayed, microchipped, and up to date on her vaccinations. For more information about her, please fill out an application at www.ALRcares.com and email Julie@alrcares.com. We schedule meet and greets for approved adopters only, an application is the first step in our approval process. Thank you!
