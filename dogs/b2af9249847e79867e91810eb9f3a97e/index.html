Description provided by Louie's Legacy Animal Rescue — Northeast

Meet Bessie! Bessie is a super sweet, smart little girl with a heart full of love!

At just 12 pounds and 6 months old, this Chihuahua/Dachsund mix is expected to stay on the smaller side, but she has a big personality! Her warm brown eyes give her the soulful, expressive face of a much bigger dog.

She’s happiest when she’s close to her person, and once she makes it onto the couch, she’s not going anywhere! Her adorable little Jack Russell-style legs are still figuring out how to get her up there, but once she settles in, she’s ready for puppy kisses, cuddles, and all the love you can give.

Bessie is house-trained, thanks in part to her foster mom working from home and keeping her on a consistent outdoor routine. No pee pads needed! She knows “come” and is learning “gentle” when taking treats. She’s a quick learner, especially when treats are involved, and is also working on “sit” for food and treats. She’ll happily sit and wait when she knows a snack or dinner is coming!

Still a puppy, Bessie loves running around the yard, carrying her squeaky toys, and playing with her sister. She’s also been very sweet with her three smaller Pomeranian foster siblings, even though they mostly prefer to keep to themselves.

Bessie has a playful, rambunctious puppy side, but she’s also one of the easiest dogs to train. She’s smart, eager to learn, and very motivated by treats! She’s still learning her manners, so her future family should be prepared to provide consistent, positive training, patience, and guidance as she grows.

Bessie has a little protective streak, too! She initially barked at a friend's dogs when they arrived but settled down quickly. She also likes to announce when deer or squirrels are visiting her yard.

Leash training hasn't started yet, so Bessie will need a patient family to introduce her to walking on a leash and help her build confidence. She’s a very easy puppy to have in the house, and treats are a great way to help her learn and stay engaged. Like any puppy, she’ll need plenty of toys, treats, and chew bones to keep her happy and out of mischief.

With her affectionate personality, playful spirit, and eagerness to be near her person, Bessie is sure to bring plenty of joy, laughter, and snuggles to her forever home.

Bessie is fostered in Spring Lake Heights, NJ. Her adoption fee is $575.

All of our animals come spayed or neutered, up to date on age appropriate vaccinations, are microchipped, heartworm negative, current on flea/tick and heartworm prevention, dewormed, and treated for any ailments found upon veterinary examination. The adoption fee covers a portion of these services, and makes it possible for us to continue rescuing animals.

For more information on the adoption fees and the adoption process, please complete our adoption application here: https://www.louieslegacy.org/adopt - an application allows us to get to know your family better so we can assess whether the animal you are interested in is a good match for your home. An application does not obligate you to adopt. Please understand that due to the volume of applications some animals receive, we are unable to contact all applicants. If your application is considered a good fit for the animal you are interested in, or even a different pet, a volunteer will give you a call as soon as possible.

We are a foster based rescue and do not have a shelter location where animals can be seen during the week. Please see our Facebook page at https://www.facebook.com/LouiesLegacy for our weekly adoption album if you are interested in meeting an animal in person. Not all animals attend every adoption event, please check our Facebook page for animal event times and locations before you make a trip to meet a specific animal.
