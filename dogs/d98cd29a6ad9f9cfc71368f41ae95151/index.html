Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Looking for a sweet chick? Look no further than Duckie herself 🐥 Duckie is 10 months old girl who is truly the ideal mix of calm and curious. In foster care, Duckie has shown herself to be a quirkly, quiet queen with so much love to give. Her foster parents have yet to hear her bark and she seemingly has no issues with being left alone as of current. Since arriving into her foster home, we've learned that Duckie is very smart adn eager to learn. She has mastered her sit command very quickly and is excited to continue practicing any basic commands you ask of her. Duckie also is a fetch connoisseur who will play all day if you keep tossing her toy for her and she knows to bring it back and drop it without prompt! Duckie walks beautifully on a leash, never pulling and is her happiest self walking alongside you - being close to her human is her most content place on the planet. Upon first meet, Duckie can be a bit timid however she warms up quickly and will show you that she's a professional cuddler, especially if you provide a soft blanket, cozy bed, chew toy and a human to keep her company or follow arm. In new environments Duckie may look to her person to keep her safe if she feels nervous while aslo showing you just how brave she gets every day. Duckie is weepad trained for those long days when her human is away and with a proper walking schedule, she is happy to go potty completely outside. Truly,Duckie has a heart as big as her floppy ears, and she’s the kind of dog who reminds you how special it is to watch a shy pup discover her confidence and still be willing to love you hard from the very beginning until the bitter end. Watching Duckie blossom in her forever home would be a privilege for anyone to have and that privilege could be yours.
