Meet Andor (aka Andy)! 🐾

Andor, affectionately known as Andy, is a 6-month-old American Bully mix with a heart as big as his puppy paws. This sweet boy is the definition of a social butterfly — he absolutely loves people and gets along wonderfully with other dogs and pets. We haven’t had the opportunity to see how he does with cats yet, so that’s still a mystery!

Andy is a quiet, gentle pup who rarely makes a peep. His one exception? When he sees another dog and wants to PLAY! Playing with other dogs is one of his absolute favorite things in the world, so a home with a friendly canine sibling would be a wonderful match for him.

Although Andy enjoys going for walks and exploring, he isn’t what we’d consider a high-energy dog. His biggest bursts of energy are reserved for playtime with his four-legged friends. Once playtime is over, he is more than happy to settle down and enjoy some quality time with his humans.

And speaking of humans… Andy is a professional cuddler. ❤️ He absolutely adores being close to his people and will happily snuggle up for affection. Give him kisses and you’ll be rewarded with the sweetest little tail wags!

He’s also doing really well with his potty training. Most of his pees and poops happen outside, and when accidents do happen indoors, he generally uses his wee-wee pads. With consistency and patience, he’s well on his way to being fully house-trained.

Around the house, Andy is surprisingly well-behaved for a 6-month-old puppy. He isn’t destructive and is happy to hang out with his people. He does, however, have a very particular fascination with shoes — so his future family may want to keep their footwear safely out of reach while he works through that puppy curiosity! 👟😂

Andy is looking for a family who will continue helping him grow into the wonderful adult dog he is destined to be. A home with another friendly dog would be a huge bonus, but what he really needs is a family ready to give him love, guidance, patience, and plenty of cuddles.

If you’re looking for a sweet, affectionate, social puppy who loves everyone and has a serious talent for cuddling, Andy might be your perfect match! ❤️
