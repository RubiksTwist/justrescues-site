Meet Moosey Gooobert!

Moosey Gooobert was found as a stray, and this sweet, social guy is ready to leave his mystery past behind and start his next chapter. He loves being around people, happily approaches for attention, and will lean right into his handler for some affection. With those soulful eyes and his wonderfully goofy name, Moosey Gooobert is already pretty hard to forget!

My Ideal Home

Moosey Gooobert would love a calm home where he can take his time settling in and get plenty of daily exercise and enrichment. He's an enthusiastic guy who can get a little excited around his people, so he'll benefit from positive-reinforcement training to help him practice polite greetings. Walks, food puzzles, long-lasting chews, and fun games will give this Moose plenty of good ways to use his energy.

Good with Other Pets?

Moosey Gooobert has no prior dog history, and his recent introduction to another dog showed some mixed feelings. He initially showed interest and followed her playful movements, but became uncomfortable when she approached his handler and needed some space. Any introductions with resident pets should be slow, thoughtful, and supervised, with plenty of attention to Moosey's comfort.

Good with Kids?

Moosey Gooobert's history with children is unknown. Since he can initially be a little tense and fearful in new situations, he'll do best with calm, respectful interactions and people who can give him the time and space he needs to feel comfortable.

Things to Know

Moosey Gooobert may need a little time to warm up when something is new, but once he feels safe, his affectionate side comes out and he seeks contact from his people. He's a moderate leash puller and has a tendency to jump up when he's excited to say hello, so positive-reinforcement training will help him learn polite greetings and walking manners. Moosey doesn't need to be rushed into new experiences, just given the chance to take things one step at a time and discover that the world can be a pretty wonderful place.
