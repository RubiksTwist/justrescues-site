Description provided by ASPCA Adoption Center

Wriggle into a new friendship with Wriggley!

Wriggley is a 4-year-old girl with a playful and affectionate personality. She is a social dog who loves being around people and will approach you for attention and pets. Her name is an accurate description of how her body reacts when she gets excited! She will wiggle with joy and she has a particularly enthusiastic response to petting. Wriggley enjoys playing with toys, either on her own or in a game of tug with you!

Wriggley is super motivated by food, and knows that tasty treats make learning new things worthwhile. She will sit for treats and is eager to work with her people, making training a fun way to build your bond. She also really likes getting outside to sniff and explore. This sweet pup is happiest when she has plenty of opportunities for activity, play and time with her favorite people.

This smart and playful girl may be your perfect match! To learn more about Wriggley, click the “Adopt Me” button.
