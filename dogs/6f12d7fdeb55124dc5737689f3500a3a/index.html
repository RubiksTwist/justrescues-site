Meet Nakyeop | Apply Now

Meet Nakyeop, our beautiful brindle survivor from the Andong wildfires in 2025. While the countrysides of Korea were taken over by flames, pups like Nakyeop were abandoned with little to no hope. Luckily our team and rescue partners managed to save this handsome boy and so many others just in time. He’s five years old and 35lbs, and with his puppy face and charming personality he’s sure to make you swoon.

Though he just arrived this weekend, Nakyeop is taking to the city with ease. He’s finding reprieve in his crate for decompression, but is very curious of his new life. At our center in Bundang, he was a favorite of staff and other pups! He loves a good cuddle or neck scratch, always enjoys an adventure, and best of all plays happily with friends or solo.

This babe will be making his big break on Saturday at our pantry, swap, & adopt event and we hope to see you there !
