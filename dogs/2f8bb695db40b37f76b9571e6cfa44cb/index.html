Meet Princess Pat!
Princess Pat was found as a stray, and this sweet girl is ready to find a kingdom of her own. She's an easygoing explorer who likes to sniff around, check in with her people, and happily accept a tasty treat or two along the way. She's got a lovely wiggly side, too, especially when she's getting some attention.

My Ideal Home
Princess Pat would love a home where she can enjoy leisurely walks, fun adventures, and plenty of opportunities to explore. She walks beautifully on a loose leash and seems to enjoy doing her own little investigations before checking back in with her people. A family who can offer her some enrichment, patience, and plenty of love would be fit for this royal sweetheart.

Good with Other Pets?
Princess Pat's history with other dogs is unknown since she came to us as a stray. She had a brief introduction to another dog and seemed a little unsure, so any future dog introductions should be slow, thoughtful, and supervised while she gets comfortable.

Good with Kids?
Princess Pat's history with children is unknown. Since she's a girl who likes to take in her surroundings at her own pace, calm and respectful introductions will help her feel comfortable.

Things to Know
Princess Pat is a nice loose leash walker who enjoys exploring and is happy to approach with a little encouragement. She can be easily distracted, but she remains responsive to her handler and is receptive to treats. She may not need a castle, but this lovely girl is definitely ready to be treated like royalty in her forever home.
