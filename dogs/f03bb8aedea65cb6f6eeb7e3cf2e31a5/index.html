HISTORY

Louie-Bloo Raspberry was born in a foster home in early May 2026, one of a litter of nine puppies after his mother was found heavily pregnant at a shelter in Selma, Alabama. He traveled to the Brooklyn area in late July 2026.

DETAILED PROFILE

Louie-Bloo Raspberry is a young puppy with a big, goofy heart and the social instincts of a dog who has decided the world is full of friends. He walks right up to new people for a sniff and a round of pets, wags through introductions, and treats a sidewalk full of strangers as an opportunity. Sweet, playful, and a little clumsy, he is happiest when someone he loves is nearby.

He has plenty of puppy energy to burn and loves a good game of fetch, a peanut butter Kong, and any plushie with a squeaker inside. He is also a devoted shadow who follows his person from room to room and settles in for a cuddle the moment things slow down.

Louie-Bloo has been friendly and gentle with the adults and kids he has met, and he does well with school-age children, though his comfort with very young kids hasn't been observed yet. He currently shares a home with a cat and is calm and easy with her, alternating between inviting her to play and politely ignoring her. He has also played nicely with resident dogs, reading their cues and backing off politely when an older dog set a boundary, so he would be happy with a canine housemate, though he doesn't need one.

Like any puppy settling into a new city, he is a work in progress on the basics. He knows sit, comes when called, and answers to his name, and he is picking up his crate and potty routines with the help of treats, which motivate him enormously. He is still learning his manners around people and food.

He is an eager, fast learner who mostly just wants to be near his people and figure the rest out as he goes.
