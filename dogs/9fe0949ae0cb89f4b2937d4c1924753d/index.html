Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Franky is a 7-year-old terrier mix who weighs around 17 pounds, and he approaches life with endless enthusiasm. Whether he's heading out for a walk, meeting someone new, saying hello to another dog, or simply discovering an interesting smell, Franky is all in. He's the kind of dog who reminds you that there's excitement to be found in the little things.

He's incredibly friendly and affectionate with people, and meeting strangers is one of his favorite activities. He'll happily introduce himself with lots of enthusiastic sniffing before settling in once he realizes you've become a friend. His warm, outgoing personality makes it easy for everyone he meets to fall for his charm.

Franky also enjoys the company of other dogs and loves having opportunities to explore and socialize. Once the initial excitement wears off, he's happy to relax and enjoy being close to his people. If you're looking for a cheerful, affectionate companion who will greet every day with a wagging tail and a sense of adventure, Franky is ready to bring that joy into your home.
