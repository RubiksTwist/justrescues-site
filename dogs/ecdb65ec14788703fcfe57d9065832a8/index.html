Description provided by NYC Second Chance Rescue

Suzy is a spirited little force of nature wrapped in fur. Feisty and full of personality, she keeps life interesting with her playful energy and curious streak. But beneath that sass is an incredibly loving and affectionate companion—she’ll happily follow you from room to room just to be near you. When it’s time to wind down, Suzy transforms into the ultimate cuddle buddy, curling up beside you and drifting off to sleep like she’s right where she belongs.

She has a special talent for making mornings sweeter, gently waking you with her soft purring and signature “biscuits,” kneading away like a tiny baker hard at work. Whether she’s zooming around in a burst of energy or snuggled up close, Suzy brings warmth, charm, and a whole lot of heart to every moment.
