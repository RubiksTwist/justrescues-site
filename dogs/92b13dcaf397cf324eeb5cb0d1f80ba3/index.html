Meet Bearstein!

Bearstein was found tied up on the street and is now looking for a patient adopter who can show him that the world can be a safe place. While he's understandably cautious in new situations, this gentle boy has already started making steady progress and is slowly learning to trust the people around him.

My Ideal Home

Bearstein would do best in a quiet, predictable home with someone who understands that trust takes time. He'll thrive with gentle encouragement, a consistent routine, and positive reinforcement as he continues to build confidence. Letting him move at his own pace will help him settle in and truly shine.

Good with Other Pets?

Although his history with other dogs is unknown, Bearstein has shown promising social skills at the shelter. He has enjoyed relaxed greetings, engaged in playful interactions with another dog, and has generally ignored dogs while out on walks. As always, slow introductions to resident pets are recommended.

Good with Kids?

Due to his fearful nature and need for a calm environment, ACC recommends an adult only home.

Things to Know

Bearstein can be shy around new people and may retreat or become uncertain when approached too quickly. He's made wonderful progress during his time at the shelter, now walking beautifully on a loose leash, calmly passing people and dogs, and allowing gentle handling with patient, low-pressure interactions. He'll do best with an adopter who understands that confidence is built one positive experience at a time and is willing to let him come out of his shell at his own pace.

Fun Fact

Once he's out on a walk, Bearstein is a true gentleman, strolling politely on a loose leash and taking in the world one sniff at a time.
