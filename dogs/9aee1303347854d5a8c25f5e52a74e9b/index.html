HISTORY

Adele was adopted in Georgia and later surrendered to our rescue partner when her owner’s living situation changed and she could no longer keep her. She stayed in our partner’s care until she traveled to Brooklyn in mid-October 2026, then boarded outside the city briefly before moving into a foster home.

DETAILED PROFILE

Adele has a signature move: she climbs onto the couch, settles in for a cuddle, then gently corn-cobs your face. It’s sweet, it’s goofy, and it’s a pretty good summary of who she is once she trusts you.

She’s still early in her city life and still finding her confidence. Sudden movements and unfamiliar situations can make her timid, and she’s quick to roll over for a belly-up hello. Each week she’s a little braver, more relaxed at home and more willing to come ask for attention. And she will ask: she throws her face right into your hands for scratches, whether you’re free or on a call. Coming home earns a full-body wag.

With people she’s friendly and outgoing, sometimes a touch skeptical at first before opening up for cuddles. Other dogs are a strength. During testing before she joined us, she answered an overeager pal with calm, clear manners, then played happily and kept coming back for more. On city walks she’s curious about dogs and can get overexcited. She shared a room calmly with cats during a test. She hasn’t lived with kids yet.

Adele is still working on house-training. She has accidents indoors, especially when she’s nervous or excited, and she’s working toward a reliable outdoor routine. On leash she pulls, stops to sniff everything, and has strong opinions about passing bikes, which she’ll bark or lunge at. She settles in her crate with a treat and knows sit.

She’ll do best with a patient, consistent adopter ready to keep up the house training and confidence building. Bring a ball: she loves to chase one, and a game of fetch is where her goofy side really shines.

MEDICAL HISTORY

The dogs in our care are up-to-date on vaccinations, spayed or neutered (when old enough), microchipped, and given a general health check. Immediate medical needs and medical history will be addressed with the adopter prior to adoption. Complete vet records will be sent shortly after adoption.
