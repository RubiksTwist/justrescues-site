HISTORY

Adele was surrendered to our rescue partner in Georgia when her owner moved and couldn't bring her along. She has been in our partner's care since, and she will travel to Brooklyn in mid-October 2026.

DETAILED PROFILE

Adele's party trick is dog diplomacy. When she was introduced to a dog with a little too much enthusiasm. She answered with calm, clear manners and no fuss, and once they had room to interact, the two had a blast. When we dropped her leash, she kept circling back to him for more. The next day she met another dog and was just as playful.

People get the same easy treatment. She bursts out of her kennel with plenty of excitement, settles quickly, and gets down to the business of saying hello to everyone in sight.

Adele was surrendered to our rescue partner when her owner's living situation changed and there was no room for a dog.

The rest of her résumé (house and crate training, leash skills, energy at home, kids and cats) gets filled in by her foster after she lands in Brooklyn in October.

Anyone hoping for a dog who can hold her own with other dogs and still greet the neighbors politely should put Adele on repeat.

MEDICAL HISTORY
