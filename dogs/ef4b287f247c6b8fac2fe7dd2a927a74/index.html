Description provided by PupStarz Rescue

Meet Coffee Maker ☕ (formerly known as Carter!)

A 4-year-old, 15-pound Chihuahua/Dachshund mix who is looking for something many of us take for granted: a place where he can finally feel safe.

Coffee Maker came to us from a hoarding situation, and it's clear that the world is still a very scary place for this little guy. He's not the kind of dog who will run up to greet you, ask for belly rubs, or immediately curl up in your lap. In fact, he's still learning that people can be trusted.

Right now, Coffee Maker spends much of his time tucked away in his safe space, watching the world around him and trying to figure things out. He isn't comfortable being touched and will typically retreat when someone approaches. He's nervous around unfamiliar visitors and isn't ready for walks or outdoor adventures just yet. Even stepping outside can be overwhelming for him.

But Coffee Maker deserves a chance, too.

He'll need a very special adopter who understands that progress may be slow, that trust cannot be rushed, and that loving a dog sometimes means giving them the space to come around on their own terms. He needs someone who will celebrate the little victories, respect his boundaries, and never expect him to become a social butterfly overnight.

Coffee Maker is crate-trained, takes treats, and has shown no resource guarding. His experience with other dogs has not been positive so far, and he is not currently comfortable sharing his space with them. His experience with children and cats is unknown.

Coffee Maker isn't a project to be fixed. He's a little soul who deserves patience, understanding, and the opportunity to learn that life can be good.

We're not looking for someone to change Coffee Maker overnight. We're looking for someone willing to let him be himself and give him the time he needs.

Could you be the person who finally shows him that he's safe?

Please note, Coffee Maker would do best in a home outside of NYC.

If you would like to adopt this dog, please visit www.pupstarzrescue.org for an application!
