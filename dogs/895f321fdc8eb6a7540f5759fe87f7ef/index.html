Leonard - 5 years & 20lbs, Doodle Mix - Shnoodle?, Neutered Our Team Says: " Leonard is a doodle, poodle, shnoodle - dream! He's got a wonderful personality. And a dreamy vbe - always down for a good time. Please note his adopter does need to be ready to deal with the classic small dog dental issues. He is in need of a dental post adoption and he does have one cracked tooth - it's not an emergency but somethng his adopter will need to be ready to take on." Thier Foster Parent Says: "Leonard is an adorable Poodle/Doodle mix, around 5 years old, with the most expressive eyes that seem to make you smile the moment you look at him! He can be a little shy when you first meet him, but he warms up quickly and lets his sweet personality shine. He walks nicely on a leash and loves saying hello to other dogs he meets along the way. He knows how to sit when asked, and when you stop to pet him, he may pop up on his hind legs and dance around for your attention! Leonard is a sweet, well-mannered boy with a playful side. He loves a big, tasty bone, yummy treats, and running around with a teddy bear in his mouth. He still gets the occasional case of the zoomies, which makes him even more adorable! He has been spending time with a resident dog and is doing very well. When playtime is over, Leonard enjoys resting in his crate, curling up in his cozy bed, and relaxing. Leonard is housebroken and does best with several walks throughout the day to help him maintain his good habits. He does understand the word sit and No. Car rides can make him a little anxious, but looking out the window seems to help him feel more comfortable. With patience and reassurance, he can continue building his confidence. Between his soulful eyes, happy little dances, playful zoomies, and love of cuddles, Leonard has a way of making you smile without even trying." Up to date on their DHPP, Bordetella, and Rabies vaccines; heartworm tested if coming from Tennessee or Puerto Rico; spayed/neutered; microchipped; treated with dewormer upon intake How did this dog end up with Social Tees? Most of our dogs come via partner groups who help us save them from shelters where they are at risk of euthanasia in Tennessee, Los Angeles, and Puerto Rico. There are very few spay/neuter resources there and many more animals in those areas than there are people to adopt them. Rescue groups like ours bring them to the northeast where the desire for pets like these allows us to save their lives.
