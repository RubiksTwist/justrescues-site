Bourbon Fries is at risk due to behavioral reasons. While in care, Bourbon has been observed to be highly on leash reactive. Noted to lunge at people in the hallway and while on the street. In one instance Bourbon attempted to grab a member of the public's cane. While with familiar handlers, Bourbon is noted to be loose and wiggly. Medially, Bourbon has CIRDC.

Bourbon Fries came into ACC as a stray after being found in a park. Because of this, we do not know how he is in a home environment. During his time in the care center, Bourbon F. has been observed to be on leash reactive. In one instance Bourbon Fries was reported to lunge and attempt to grab a passerby's cane. He has been noted to lunge at people in the hallways when returning from his walk. He has been noted to greet familiar handlers with a loose and wiggly body. We recommend placement with a New Hope partner who can provide any necessary behavior modification (force-free, positive reinforcement-based) and re-evaluate behavior in a stable home environment before placement into a permanent home.
