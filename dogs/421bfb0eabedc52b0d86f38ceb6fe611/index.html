Meet Nova | Apply Now

This girl is the full package: sweet, resilient, and down to Earth~ ✨ Nova ✨ is a 7-year-old, 55lb Jindo mix who has captured all of our hearts! For years, she survived on the harsh streets of Pyeongtaek alongside her son, Noah, fiercely watching over him through the seasons while being fed daily by two caring locals. When her health began to rapidly decline from a painful prolapse, our team stepped in on Christmas Day to bring both mama and son to safety. After urgent surgery, medical rehabilitation, and endless TLC, this brave mama is completely healed, thriving, and ready for the soft life she has always deserved.

Even after everything she’s endured, Nova is a bright ray of sunshine who has adjusted seamlessly to indoor life. She is a remarkably sweet, calm, and chill gal whose tail is almost constantly wagging to let you know just how happy she is. She adores soaking in affection and leaning in for gentle pets, but she’s just as content snoozing peacefully on her dog bed while you go about your day. To make things even better, she is an absolute dream in her crate. She’s quiet, cozy, and settling right in without a peep.

She is fantastic on leash, strolling along with a relaxed, steady pace and taking in the neighborhood scents with quiet curiosity. While busy streets can sometimes feel intimidating to rescue dogs, Nova takes everything in stride with her signature chill, well-adjusted demeanor. Her foster family reports she is pure joy to walk and an easygoing presence in every setting.

If you’re ready to give this deserving mama the forever love she’s waited a lifetime for, come meet her this Saturday! We strongly encourage submitting your application and interviewing before the event. Pre-approved adopters get 30 minutes of exclusive access before the general public.
