Meet Endzone Ellie

Endzone Ellie is a sweet little girl who was given a second chance when she needed it most.

Ellie and her sister, Hail Mary, found themselves in a Georgia shelter where they were at risk simply because there was no space. At such a young age, they should have been busy being puppies, discovering the world and dreaming about their forever families. Instead, their lives were in danger through no fault of their own.

Thankfully, we couldn’t let that be the end of Ellie’s story.

Now this adorable girl is ready for the part of her life she should have had all along. Ellie is playful, curious, and full of puppy sweetness. She is just beginning to discover the world, and we hope she gets to experience it surrounded by people who will love and protect her.

There will be toys to play with, things to learn, adventures to share, and plenty of cozy moments along the way. Most importantly, Ellie deserves a family who understands that bringing home a puppy is a commitment to loving her through every stage of her life.

Her name may be Endzone Ellie, but the only touchdown she needs now is finding her forever home.

Please consider giving this precious girl the second chance she deserves.
