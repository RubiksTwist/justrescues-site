Description provided by NYC Second Chance Rescue

Sid's story is one of survival, resilience, and a heart that never stopped loving. Rescued from rural South Carolina after surviving on his own, Sid was starving, frightened, and unsure who to trust. One woman refused to give up on him, saved his life, and gave him the chance to finally feel safe. He was named Sid in her honor.

Because of his rescuer's help, Sid was adopted by a family. For more than three years, Sid thought he had found his forever home. Sadly, through no fault of his own, he is searching again.

With love and patience, Sid blossomed into the happy, affectionate, incredibly smart dog he is today. He knows basic commands, is crate and house trained, loves people, and enjoys making dog friends.

After everything he has been through, Sid still loves with his whole heart.

He deserves a family that will choose him for good.

Could you be Sid's forever?

All dogs will be altered, microchipped, and receive age-appropriate vaccines prior to adoption.
