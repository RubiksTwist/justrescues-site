Meet Sid!

Sid's story is one of resilience, hope, and second chances. Rescued from rural South Carolina after surviving on his own for far too long, Sid was named after the woman who refused to give up on him and ultimately saved his life. With patience, love, and determination, he transformed from a starving, frightened dog into the happy, healthy companion he is today.

Sid is incredibly smart, affectionate, and eager to please. He knows basic commands, crate training, and house manners. He loves people, enjoys making dog friends, and is always ready to show off how clever he is.

After spending more than three years in a home, Sid has found himself looking for a family once again through no fault of his own. Through every challenge, he has never lost his spirit or his ability to love. Now, this sweet boy is hoping for the forever home he's waited so long to find.

Could you be Sid's happy ending?
