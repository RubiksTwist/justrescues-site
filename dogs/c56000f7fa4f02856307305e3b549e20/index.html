Meet Gobi!

Gobi’s story began in the most heartbreaking way; she, along with her mom and two siblings, was found discarded near a dumpster in Mississippi. Left behind as if they didn’t matter. But Gobi’s spirit tells a very different story.

Despite her rough start, Gobi is incredibly sweet and tender-hearted. She seeks connection, comfort, and the reassurance that she is safe. She is the kind of dog who quietly leans in when she trusts you, as if she’s beginning to believe that love might actually stay this time.

Gobi is now looking for a home that will show her, every single day, that she was never “trash” or forgotten, only waiting for the right people to see her worth. She doesn’t need perfection, just patience, kindness, and a steady hand to help her blossom into the confident, joyful dog she is meant to be.

She has already survived the worst kind of start. What she deserves next is simple: dignity, safety, and a family who will never give up on her again.

If you’re looking for a dog who will quietly change your heart, Gobi is ready to meet you.

All dogs will be altered, microchipped, and receive age-appropriate vaccines prior to adoption.
