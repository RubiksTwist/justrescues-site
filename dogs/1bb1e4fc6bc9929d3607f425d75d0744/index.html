Meet Ren

Ren is a sweet boy who has already experienced far more hardship than any dog should. He and his companion, Stimpy, were found abandoned inside a Bronx apartment after the building’s former superintendent left, leaving the dogs behind with no one to care for them.

They were eventually discovered by the new superintendent, who found them in terrible condition. Both dogs were heavily infested with fleas, and they had been left without the basic care they needed.

Despite being abandoned and neglected, Ren is now getting the care, attention, and kindness he should have received all along. His past may explain where he came from, but it does not have to determine where he goes next.

Ren deserves a safe home, a soft place to sleep, and someone who will make sure he is never forgotten again. We hope his next chapter is the one where he finally gets to be someone's beloved companion.

Ren and Stimpy need to be adopted together. After everything they have been through side by side, their bond is incredibly important to them, and they deserve to begin their new life together in a loving forever home where they can finally feel safe, cherished, and never alone again.
