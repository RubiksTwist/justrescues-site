Meet Panda Lynn! 🐼🤍

Meet Panda Lynn, our 3-year-old, 39-pound spotted sweetheart with the cutest pink nose and a whole lot of love to give!

Panda is a playful, toy-loving pittie who also happens to be deaf. This special girl is perfectly capable of learning, connecting, and communicating in her own way. She's still settling into foster life, but we're already discovering what makes her so lovable. Give her a cozy place to nap and a few of her favorite toys, and she's one happy panda bear!

COMMANDS KNOWN: Panda is still learning how to communicate through hand signals and visual cues. Since she's deaf, her future family will need to use consistent visual communication and positive reinforcement.

LEASH MANNERS: Panda gets excited when she sees her leash and may try to grab or chew on it in anticipation of going outside! Once she's out the door, though, she's a wonderful walking companion. She walks beautifully on leash and frequently checks in with her person, showing just how attentive and connected she is despite not being able to hear.

KIDS/DOG MANNERS: Panda can be mouthy when she's excited or playing, so a home without young children is recommended. She's still learning appropriate boundaries and would benefit from continued positive reinforcement and guidance. Her manners around other dogs are still being evaluated, however, she does not pay attention to dogs on her walks.

SOMETHING PANDA LOVES: TOYS! 🧸 This girl loves her toys almost as much as she loves a good nap. She enjoys relaxing and having her favorite things nearby.

CRATE TRAINING: Panda Lynn is crate trained! She does well having her own designated space to relax and unwind. This is a great foundation as she continues learning her potty routine and adjusting to life in a home.

WHAT SHE'S WORKING ON: Panda is learning to communicate through visual cues and hand signals while building confidence in her new environment. She's also recovering from her recent spay, so we're taking things slowly and helping her establish a consistent daily routine.

POTTY DETAILS: Panda is not yet potty trained. She's still learning that outside is where she's supposed to do her business! Her future family will need to continue working with her through consistency, patience, and positive reinforcement.

We don't know everything about Panda Lynn's first three years of life, but we do know that she deserves a future filled with love, patience, and plenty of toys! Panda is looking for someone willing to learn alongside her, celebrate her progress, and give her the stability every dog deserves. With a little patience, consistency, and a whole lot of love, this spotted sweetheart has so much potential to become someone's very best friend! 🤍🐾
