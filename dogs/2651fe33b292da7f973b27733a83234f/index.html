🐶 Emma | Female | Approximately 3 Months Old
🐾 Dachshund/Lab Mix
Meet Emma, a sweet, lovable little girl with a BIG personality packed into one petite puppy! ❤️
💕 A LITTLE LOVE BUG! Emma absolutely LOVES being around her person! Her favorite place is curled up in a nice, warm lap, soaking up all the love and attention she can get. 🥰
🎭 AND THE OSCAR GOES TO... EMMA! This little girl has quite the dramatic side! If something scares her, she lets out a scream that will have you thinking the world is ending! 😂 And when she gets overly excited or frustrated? Get ready for her signature HOWL! Emma definitely knows how to make her feelings known!
🐾 SHE'S GOT SPRINGS FOR LEGS! Don't let her petite size fool you! Emma is quite the little jumper and has mastered the art of escaping her playpen. This clever girl likes to keep everyone on their toes!
🧸 TOYS, TOYS & MORE TOYS! Emma loves playing with just about any toy she can get her paws on! She's also a big fan of spending time outdoors, exploring and enjoying the fresh air!
🐶 FURRY FRIENDS! Emma has been exposed to older dogs and cats, giving her experience sharing her space with other animals.
🏠 CRATE TRAINING? WELL... Emma is crate trained, although she would much rather be hanging out with her people! She may put on a little howling performance when first crated, but thankfully, she settles down pretty quickly. 😂❤️
💕 Emma is sweet, affectionate, playful, a little dramatic, and absolutely full of personality! She's looking for a forever family who will appreciate her funny little quirks, give her plenty of playtime, and always have a warm lap waiting for her!
