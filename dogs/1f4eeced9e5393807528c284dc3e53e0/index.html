Meet Kibou! Kibou means hope in Japanese and this sweet girl is hoping that she finds her forever family soon! She's the perfect companion if you work from home, but she's also perfectly content napping the day away on her own if not! She's housebroken, crate trained, loves walks and is great on leash. She will even happily entertain herself playing with her toys. Kibou also gets along well with her foster dog sibling and loves meeting new people too!
