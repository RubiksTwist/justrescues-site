Meet Kali!

Kali is an owner surrender who is now looking for a fresh start and a home where she can settle in and feel secure. She has a calm, easy presence and does best when life feels predictable and kind of low-key, which helps her confidence really shine through.

Personality

Kali is a steady, people-oriented girl who likes being part of whatever her humans are doing. She's not pushy for attention, but she enjoys staying close, checking in, and quietly taking in her surroundings. Her foster describes her as an easy dog in the home who settles well once she understands the routine and enjoys just being included in daily life.

She responds really well to calm energy and kind handling. Nothing overly complicated, just a consistent, relaxed environment where she knows what to expect.

Out and About

Kali is a super nice leash walker with no pulling and no reactivity toward people. She moves along at an easy pace and stays soft even when passing other dogs, often with a loose body and a friendly, wagging tail. Walks with her feel pretty straightforward and relaxed, like she's just happy to be out and about with you.

Social Behavior

Kali is moderately social when meeting new people and will usually approach when called. She tends to stay nearby and then drift around the room a bit before coming back to check in. She's comfortable with gentle handling and does best when interactions stay calm and unforced.

At the shelter she greeted another dog with a loose, wiggly body, then casually disengaged and went back to focusing on her handler, showing a nice ability to take things in stride.

Home Life

Kali would do really well in a home that has a steady routine and a calm vibe. Her foster describes her as an easy companion who enjoys being part of everyday life without needing constant stimulation. She's happiest when things feel balanced and predictable and she can just hang out with her people.

Things to Know

Kali is comfortable with normal handling and day to day environments, and she does best when people are gentle and not overly intense with her. She's the kind of dog who settles in nicely once she understands the rhythm of a home.

Fun Fact

Kali is a quiet "stick by your side" kind of dog. She doesn't ask for much, she just likes being near her people, keeping things calm, and being part of the moment. 🐾
