Description provided by NYC Second Chance Rescue

Meet Brad Pitt! This handsome, affectionate, and loyal pup is ready to find his forever home! Brad is the perfect mix of active and easygoing—he loves walks, hikes, and a good game of fetch, but he’s just as happy zipping around the yard or rolling in the grass. And at the end of the day, his favorite place to be is curled up next to his favorite humans, soaking up all the love.

Brad has an incredible rescue story—he survived a dog attack in an overcrowded shelter, which left him a bit anxious around unfamiliar dogs. He can happily live as your one and only, but if there’s another dog in the home, a calm female with slow introductions would be the best match. This remarkably smart and well-trained pup knows all his commands and is fully house-trained. He’s eager to please, deeply devoted, and even more charmer than his namesake. If you’re looking for a loving, loyal companion with an inspiring story, apply today to learn more about Brad.

All dogs will be altered, microchipped, and receive age-appropriate vaccines prior to adoption.
