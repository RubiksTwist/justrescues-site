Meet Story!

Story is a sweet Alabama girl who is ready to leave her difficult beginnings behind and start the happy chapter she has always deserved.

Story and her siblings, Palmer, Booker, and Major, were found wandering the streets of Alabama without their mom. At such a young age, they should have been safe beside her, learning about the world from the comfort of a loving family. Instead, they were left to fend for themselves.

Thankfully, their story changed when they were rescued and given the care and protection they needed. Now Story is ready to discover just how wonderful life can be. She is looking forward to a home where she can feel safe, loved, and spoiled, with a family who will help her grow into the amazing dog she is meant to be.

Her past may be sad, but Story's future is wide open, and we cannot wait to see where her story takes her.

Could you be the family who gives Story the happily-ever-after she deserves? Apply to adopt this sweet girl today!

Please remember that adoption bios are only snapshots of who an animal is. For more information about Story, including her personality, needs, and what type of home may be the best fit, please reach out to our Adoptions Team.

All dogs will be altered, microchipped, and receive age-appropriate vaccines prior to adoption.
