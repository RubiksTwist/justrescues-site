Meet Poker, a curious cat!

With her soft, beautiful black coat, Poker is a real ace. Poker has spent some time in foster care, showing more of her great personality every day by following her foster around whenever they can! She is also a massive fan of treats – having some yummy treats are the way to this girl’s heart! Some of her favorite pastimes are lounging near her people and occasionally cuddling against their legs.

Would you like to meet Poker? Click the “Adopt Me” button to learn more. Poker has all her cards on the table at play time – she loves to chase a wand!

*You can meet animals located at the ASPCA Adoption Center during our walk-in hours (daily, noon to 6 p.m.). We encourage a $150 suggested adoption fee when adopting an animal. This adoption fee allows the ASPCA to continue our lifesaving work and help even more animals in need. We are grateful for any amount given at the time of adoption.*
