Pre-Screener Form

Kenzo is at risk due to behavior concerns. His previous owners report that he has bitten multiple times, in each case leaving scratch-like wounds. These bites have occurred when family members, have been too close to his face or walked or run past him too fast, startling him. His previous owners also report that he is sensitive to handling, and may attempt to bite when being groomed. Outside of these incidents, they describe him as a quiet, independent, playful dog who can be social and affectionate though prefers contact on his own terms. Kenzo has not acclimated well to the care center environment and has shown fearful behavior, trembling and hard barking at staff who approach his kennel. Medically, he has Brachycephalic Syndrome and an ocular abnormality that will require follow up.

Kenzo was surrendered to the care center due to behavioral concerns. Kenzo's previous owner reports that he has bitten more than five times. All bites were reported to be bite-and-releases that left scratch-like wounds. The owner described them as fear-based, occurring when family members have been too close to his face, attempted to kiss him, or walked past him too fast. The most recent reported bite occurred when the owner's young child was running past Kenzo, startling him and causing him to bite, which left a scratch on the child's foot. They also report that he may attempt to bite when being groomed or having his nails trimmed. Outside of these incidents, his previous owners describe Kenzo as a quiet, independent couch potato, who knows several cues. He loves chew toys, squeakers, playing in the park, and car rides. Due to his bite history and fearful and defensive behavior, we recommend placement placement with a New Hope partner who can provide an experienced, adult only foster home, any necessary behavior modification (force-free, positive reinforcement-based), and re-evaluate behavior in a stable home environment before placement into a permanent home.
