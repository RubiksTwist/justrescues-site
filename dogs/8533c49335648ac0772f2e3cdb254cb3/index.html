This tinly little thing has the confidence of a large dog and loves people as much as a golden retreiver. She hasn't met a human she doesn't love. She does need a home in a calmer area - she's not an East Village girlie. She likes to do her business in a calm neighborhood. Has zero people fear but needs a calm area to pee and poo." Her Foster Family Says: "If you’ve ever wanted a Chihuahua without the Napoleon complex, Malibu is your girl. She is incredibly sweet, affectionate, and just an all-around fun little dog. Malibu absolutely loves people and would happily spend her day curled up in your lap or snuggled against you. She’s also wonderfully social with other dogs. She’s very sweet and submissive when she plays, and she genuinely seems to get along with everyone and everything she meets. And she LOVES toys. She’ll play with them by herself, with her people, or with other dogs. Watching her entertain herself with a toy is one of the cutest things ever. She has a fun amount of energy without being overwhelming, and she’s just an easy, happy little companion. She’s also a really nice leash walker but city life has been a little overwhelming for Malibu, and she hasn’t quite gotten comfortable enough to consistently potty outside. We take her out 5+ times a day and she will hold her pee, but thankfully we’ve found a spot in the apartment where she reliably uses a pee pad. It likely means she knows she's not supposed to go to the bathroom wherever and whenever she wants, she just is too uncomfortable in our very busy neighborhood to go. We think she would likely thrive much more in a quieter neighborhood or calmer environment where she can relax and build confidence. One thing her future family will want to continue working on is boundaries. Malibu LOVES being on you and would prefer to be permanently attached to your chest. We’ve been working on teaching her that she can settle beside you or lower on your lap, and that when she’s invited up, she gets all the cuddles she wants. She’s been doing really well with this and is very receptive to gentle structure. She also has some separation anxiety but it's only her crying for 10-20 minutes before she settles so continuing to leave her alone every day is crucial. Malibu is just a really, really fun Chihuahua. She’s affectionate, playful, social, cuddly, and full of personality. If you’re looking for a little dog who wants to be your best friend and make you laugh every day, Malibu might be your girl." How did this dog end up with Social Tees? Most of our dogs come via partner groups who help us save them from shelters where they are at risk of euthanasia in Tennessee, Los Angeles, and Puerto Rico. There are very few spay/neuter resources there and many more animals in those areas than there are people to adopt them. Rescue groups like ours bring them to the northeast where the desire for pets like these allows us to save their lives.
