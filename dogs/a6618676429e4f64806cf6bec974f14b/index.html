Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Beautiful, sweet, and absolutely irresistible, Mai Tai is a gorgeous calico girl who has all the right ingredients for a perfect companion. Just like her namesake, she’s a little bit of sunshine, a whole lot of sweetness, and guaranteed to leave you feeling warm and happy.

Mai Tai came to ARF as an owner surrender, of no fault of her own - she loves attention and affection and is happiest when she’s getting plenty of love from everyone around her. With her beautiful calico coloring and charming personality, Mai Tai is as unique on the outside as her special name is.

She’s the kind of girl who makes every day feel like a vacation - no beach required!
