Playtime and treats make Uno a happy kitty!

Uno is an adorable 1-year-old tabby who has been showing us more of his playfulness every day! He enjoys interactive wand toys and is thrilled for a chance to chase them around the room. He also has a soft spot for tasty treats — don’t we all?

Uno has been showing off his affectionate side, happily rubbing against nearby objects and leaning in for attention. Uno also loves finding the perfect spot to survey his kingdom. Two of his favorite spots are on the windowsill and on top of the refrigerator! When he’s ready for some downtime, you might find him tucked away in a quiet hidey spot, enjoying a peaceful nap.

Come meet Uno and see what a wonderful boy he is! To learn more about Uno, click the “Adopt Me” button.

*You can meet animals located at the ASPCA Adoption Center during our walk-in hours (daily, noon to 6 p.m.). We encourage a $150 suggested adoption fee when adopting an animal. This adoption fee allows the ASPCA to continue our lifesaving work and help even more animals in need. We are grateful for any amount given at the time of adoption.*
