Meet Hail Mary

Hail Mary is a little girl with a big second chance ahead of her.

She and her sister, Endzone Ellie, were in a Georgia shelter where they were at risk because there simply was not enough space. It is heartbreaking to think about two young puppies facing such an uncertain future through no fault of their own.

We knew Hail Mary deserved the chance to grow up.

Now she is ready to leave that scary chapter behind and start discovering what life is really supposed to be like for a puppy. Hail Mary is sweet, curious, and full of that wonderful puppy spirit that makes it impossible not to fall in love.

She has so much ahead of her. New things to discover, new experiences to learn from, toys to play with, naps to take, and a family to love.

We want Hail Mary to grow up knowing that she is safe and that she matters. She deserves a family who will be there for the puppy days, the growing-up days, and all the wonderful years that follow.

This little girl was given a second chance. Now she just needs someone to give her a forever home. Please consider opening your heart and home to Hail Mary.
