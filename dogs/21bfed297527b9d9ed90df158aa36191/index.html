Meet Brooklyn!

Brooklyn was found as a stray, so we don't know much about their life before arriving at ACC. What we do know is that once they get some one-on-one time with their people, this sweet pup is eager to make friends, happily approaching for attention, wagging their tail, and leaning right into affection. Brooklyn may have arrived as a bit of a mystery, but they're already showing us plenty to love.

My Ideal Home

Brooklyn would love a home where they can settle in at their own pace and enjoy plenty of activities with their people. Since we don't have a history on them, a calm and quiet beginning will help Brooklyn feel comfortable while they get to know their new surroundings. Once settled, they'll benefit from daily walks, playtime, long-lasting chews, food puzzles, and fun games like hide-and-seek to keep their body and brain happy.

Good with Other Pets?

Brooklyn hasn't shown any reactivity toward other dogs while walking, but their history with other animals is unknown. Any introductions to resident pets should be slow, thoughtful, and supervised while everyone gets acquainted and Brooklyn has time to settle in.

Good with Kids?

Brooklyn's history with children is unknown, so we can't say for certain how they'll feel about sharing a home with kids. Their social and affectionate personality is encouraging, and their new family should keep interactions calm and respectful while Brooklyn gets comfortable.

Things to Know

Brooklyn can be a little tense and low to the ground while out on the street, so they should never be forced to approach something that makes them uncomfortable or pushed into handling before they're ready. Once inside, Brooklyn shows a much more relaxed side, seeking contact, leaning into their handler, and approaching people with a loose, wiggly body. They also enjoy their toys and can get delightfully enthusiastic about a favorite.
