Some puppies arrive with a little extra sparkle, and then there’s Colette.

With her beautiful black-and-white coat and those irresistibly expressive eyes, Colette has the kind of presence that makes you stop, smile, and wonder how anyone could resist her. Her name feels perfectly suited to her - a little elegant, a little charming, and completely unforgettable.

Colette came to ARF after she was rescued from Louisiana, thanks to our rescue partner BISSELL, and she is still discovering the world, one curious sniff, playful bounce, and cozy cuddle at a time. She has that wonderful puppy mix of sweetness and silliness, with a personality that is already beginning to shine through.

She isn’t just a black-and-white puppy - she’s Colette, a one-of-a-kind little girl waiting for a one-of-a-kind family to love her unconditionally.
