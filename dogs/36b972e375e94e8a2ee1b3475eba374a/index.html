Hi there! My name is Molly, and I'm a sweet, loving girl looking for a forever home where I can shower my humans with affection!

I might be a little shy when we first meet, but don't let that fool you! Give me a little time, and I'll quickly warm up. Once I trust you, get ready, because I'll be your little shadow! I love being around people, snuggling on the couch, playing with my toys, and, of course, enjoying some delicious peanut butter!

I'm a good girl in the house! I'm potty trained, sleep soundly in my crate all night, and do well when my humans leave home. I also know commands like "sit" and "stay."

I'm still learning a few things. Loud noises can make me nervous, and I get pretty excited when I spot squirrels on walks! I can also be a little enthusiastic when visitors arrive, but with patience, reassurance, and love, I'm continuing to build my confidence.

What I'm really looking for is someone who will give me a little patience as I settle in, lots of snuggles, and a lifetime of love. In return, I promise to be your loyal companion, your cuddle buddy, and your very best little shadow.

Could you be my forever person? Love, Molly.
