Description provided by Animal Lighthouse Rescue

Introducing Beauty! She is a 2-year-old, 25-pound Chihuahua mix who has a whole lot of love to give—and this girl absolutely melts for her men! She has the male vet techs at El Faro wrapped around her little paw. The moment she sees them, she rolls right over, belly up, ready for all the belly rubs she can get! But Beauty’s love isn’t limited to the guys—she truly adores her people and seems to have a permanent smile on her face. She’s one of those dogs who makes you happy just by being around her. At a small 25 pounds, Beauty is the perfect size for adventures. She’s easy to take along, always photo-ready, and would love nothing more than to stroll through the park with her favorite person. Whether you’re headed out for a walk or looking for a travel buddy, Beauty is ready to be your little sidekick. She also knows how to have a GREAT time! Give her her favorite plush toy and she’ll fling it into the air like she just won the lottery—then take off for a round of the zoomies! Once she’s gotten all that energy out, Beauty is ready for the best part of the day: couch cuddles. She’ll stretch out with her little feet in the air, and if she drifts off into a dream, don’t be surprised if you see those adorable paws wiggling away. Beauty is sweet, silly, affectionate, playful, and always ready to make memories with her people. She’s looking for a forever family who will give her a life full of love, adventures, belly rubs, and plenty of plush toys to fling!

Beauty is spayed, microchipped, and up to date on her vaccinations. For more information about her, please fill out an application at www.ALRcares.com and email Laura@alrcares.com. We schedule meet and greets for approved adopters only, an application is the first step in our approval process. Thank you!
