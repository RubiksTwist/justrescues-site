Hi, I'm Brutus! 🐾

I'm 2 months old, a big boy, and still figuring out what to do with these giant paws of mine. Walking and running are a work in progress, so I may trip over myself every now and then. I'm a little stoic, but don't let that fool you. I'm actually a silly, playful guy who loves to wrestle, chase balls, and say hello to everyone I meet.

I'm also quite the curious observer. I like to watch how things are done before joining in, and birds and cats are especially interesting to me. Loud noises can be a little scary, but I'm learning about the big world around me every day.

My favorite thing? Being with you. I'm your little shadow and love to follow you from room to room just to see what you're up to. I also love going on adventures in my backpack so I can take in all the sights and sounds.

I'd love a home with another friendly dog to show me the ropes and keep me company, or someone who works from home and enjoys being outside. I'm looking for a person who wants a little sidekick to grow up with, explore the world with, and share lots of puppy cuddles and adventures.

I've got a lot of growing to do, but I promise I'll make it fun! ❤️
