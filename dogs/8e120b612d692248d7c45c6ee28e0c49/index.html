Meet Chip 🐾 — 7 Pounds of Pure Love!

At 7 years old and just 7 pounds, Chip is proof that the biggest hearts come in the tiniest packages! This sweet little gentleman is ready to find his forever home and shower his lucky person with love.

Chip came into rescue after arriving at the shelter scared and refusing to eat. With a little comfort, care, and love in foster care, his adorable personality is finally shining through! He’s incredibly sweet with people, dogs, and every animal he’s met. Basically, he’s a tiny best friend who seems to make friends wherever he goes!

Chip loves being close to his person and soaking up all the pets and affection he can get, but he’s also a wonderful little companion who’s happy to relax at home while you’re out. When it’s time to head outside, he’s ready to go! He loves his walks and is excellent on leash, making him the perfect pint-sized sidekick for exploring the streets of NYC.

And the good news keeps coming: Chip is both house-trained and crate-trained!

When Chip arrived at the shelter, his fur was very matted, so he’s currently rocking a freshly shaved look. But don’t worry — his fluffy glow-up is on the way, and we have a feeling he’s going to be an absolutely adorable little puffball!

Whether you’re looking for a sweet addition to your family or a devoted little companion to share life’s everyday moments, Chip might just be your guy. He’s been through a lot, and now it’s his turn to feel safe, loved, and cherished forever.

All 7 pounds of Chip are ready to steal your heart. Could you be the one to give this little lovebug his happily ever after? ❤️
