Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Meet Chip! 🐾 The tiniest roommate with the biggest heart!

At around 7 years old and just 7 pounds, Chip is proof that the biggest hearts come in the tiniest packages. This little guy is about as easy as they come! He sleeps through the night, spends his days happily lounging around, and is always up for an adventure when it's time to get out and explore. He's great with other dogs, good with kids, fully house-trained, and practically a dream roommate. Oh, and if you're looking for a quiet companion, you're in luck — Chip isn't much of a barker!

But don't let his laid-back lifestyle fool you. Chip has a personality that's been getting bigger by the day, and we're having so much fun getting to know the little guy underneath it all.

Chip came into our care after deteriorating in the shelter. Since coming into foster care, he's been putting on some much-needed weight, getting his spunk back, and reminding us just how much a little love and security can change everything. It's been so rewarding to watch him come out of his shell and start enjoying life again!

And now that he's feeling better, Chip has made one thing very clear: he LOVES his people. This little shadow wants to be part of whatever you're doing, and he's never going to turn down a little extra attention. If you happen to be busy and haven't gotten around to petting him, don't worry — Chip has a very effective way of reminding you. He'll give you a little paw to get your attention, followed by a dramatic sigh that practically says, "Excuse me, I believe you forgot about me." Honestly, it's hard to argue with that logic.

He's also a big fan of mealtime and treats, so you'll have no trouble convincing him that you're his favorite person when snacks are involved. Between his affectionate nature, his adorable little demands for attention, and his easygoing attitude, Chip has a way of making himself right at home in your heart.

At the end of the day, Chip doesn't ask for much. A cozy place to snooze, a full belly, a few adventures, and someone who doesn't mind having a tiny supervisor following them around the house will make him one happy little guy. He's been through a lot, but he's showing us every day that he's ready for the good stuff: a home of his own, a person to love, and a lifetime of being someone's very spoiled little companion.

If you've been looking for a small, sweet, easygoing best friend with a little sass and a whole lot of love to give, Chip might just be your guy. ❤️
