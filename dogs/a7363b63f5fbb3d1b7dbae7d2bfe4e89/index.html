Pre-Screener Form

Peluchi is at risk due to behavioral concerns in care. Peluchi has been observed to display low threshold for arousal around other dogs, resulting in hard barking, fixating, lunging, and jumping while passing kennels. This behavior is hard to redirect using treats, toys, or voice. Peluchi has been observed to display kennel stress behaviors such as vibrating his body, whale eyeing, forcing his body against the kennel door, muzzle punching or head butting the kennel door. Peluchi has been noted to engage in training and is responsive to handlers at times. Medically, Peluchi is apparently healthy.

Peluchi arrived at MACC as a stray with no known history. While in care, Peluchi has been observed to be reactive towards other dogs. Noted to dart forward on leash, pull hard, and bark at the other dogs in pop up crates. On the street Peluchi will pull hard towards other dogs. With handlers when they attempt to interact, Peluchi will bark at them. Due to the detailed behavior, we recommend an experienced adult-only home.
