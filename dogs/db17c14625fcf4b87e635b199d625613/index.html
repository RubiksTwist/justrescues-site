Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

My foster writes: Stark is a sweet, energetic, vocal boy! Don't let his cuteness fool you - he takes playtime very seriously and quickly earned the nickname "shark." If Stark could play 24/7, he absolutely would, and he makes catching wand toys look like an olympic sport with all his flips and jumps. He's not shy about telling you when he wants to play, and he's looking for a home where someone can match his energy and give him lots of play time. Stark can be slow to warm up to new people, but once he trusts you he will follow you around everywhere (it's hard to walk because he's always standing between your legs)! While he loves being near his people, he's still figuring out how he feels about pets and cuddles. He's also still learning that hands, arms, and legs are not toys, so he's looking for a patient person who can help redirect his playful energy. Stark will make a perfect companion for someone who needs some adorable energy in their life!
