Pre-Screener Form

Lavender is on the at-risk list due to behavior concerns. In the care center, Lavender has shown high arousal, including repeatedly jumping on handlers, nipping at hands, biting and pulling on clothing, and leash biting. These behaviors have been difficult to redirect with treats or toys and have required additional staff assistance during walks. Lavender is social and playful, engages in fetch, responds to basic cues, and shows loose, appropriate play behavior with her resident sibling in playgroup. Medically, Lavender is apparently healthy.

Lavender was surrendered into care by her previous owner due to having too many animals in the home and other personal circumstances. Her previous family described her as a social, affectionate, very active, and playful dog. She loves playing with toys and chasing handballs. She is noted to be allergic to chicken. During her stay in care, Lavender has been social with handlers and staff, but she very quickly becomes aroused and begins mouthing and nipping at handlers' clothing, limbs, or their person. She intially could be redirected with treats or toys, but recently, she has not been taking these items reliably. Due to these concerns, we recommend placement in an adult-only home where she can have time and space to acclimate at her own pace.

Meet Lavender!
A little energy, a lot of love, and a whole lot of personality! Lavender came to ACC when her owner could no longer care for her, and now this playful girl is ready to find her next adventure buddy. She's friendly, outgoing, and always happy to make a new human friend. She can get so excited about saying hello that she might forget all about keeping her paws on the floor. Lavender is looking for a family who will embrace her enthusiasm and show her all the fun things life has in store!

My Ideal Home
Lavender would love an active home where there's plenty to do! Think neighborhood walks, sniffing adventures, food puzzles, long lasting chews, and games of hide and seek. She's a high energy girl with lots of enthusiasm to share, so she'll be happiest with a family who can give her both physical exercise and fun ways to keep her clever mind busy. A little help settling into her routine will go a long way, too!

Good with Other Pets?
Lavender's previous family shared that she enjoyed playing with familiar dogs, while she could be a little shy around new ones. She's shown plenty of interest in meeting dogs at ACC, sometimes even offering a play bow to invite them to join the fun! Not every dog has been quite as eager to play, though, and she's sometimes chosen to hang back when another dog approaches enthusiastically. Any potential dog siblings should meet her slowly so everyone has a chance to get comfortable.

Things to Know
Lavender is an enthusiastic girl who may greet you with all four paws leaving the ground! She's ready to practice her Sit, Stay, Down, and Leave It, with plenty of praise and yummy treats along the way. Her previous family also mentioned that loud noises can make her nervous, so she'll appreciate a little patience as she settles into her new routine. She can pull on leash when she's excited, so she'll benefit from some practice walking nicely with her person. Lavender has so much love and zest for life to share. She's just waiting for someone to help her channel all that happy energy into wonderful new adventures!

A volunteer writes: Lavender is the kind of dog we, respectfully, would describe as a stage-five clinger. You know the type. The first time I meet her, she's eager to give hugs: While we're standing just outside the shelter's entrance, her legs draped around my arms, a person on their way to look at adoptable dogs smiles and asks, "Who's that?" With a goofy smile and puppy-dog eyes (she is, after all, just a puppy of 10 months), her presence is easily captivating.

Unfortunately, for a young, energetic pup, life in a crate is already starting to take a toll on her, just two weeks into her stay at the shelter. Lavender was surrendered along with her brother Andre (#266661), after their person's dog had an accidental litter; they could not find homes for all the puppies and did not have the resources to care for them. Lavender appears to have been loved during her first nine months of life. Her person asked if she could be kept with toys in her crate, and she is said to be a social, affectionate, very active and playful dog, a description that has been consistent with her behavior at the shelter. They also said she loves playing with toys and other puppies or dogs, enjoys running around chasing after handballs, and knows the cues "sit", "down," and " stay." At the shelter, Lavender has done well with other dogs in playgroup, greeting dogs with a loose and wiggly body and enjoying playtime.
