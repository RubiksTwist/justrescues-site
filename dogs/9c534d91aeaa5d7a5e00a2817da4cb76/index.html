Description provided by PupStarz Rescue

Meet Ducky, a super handsome 3-and-a-half year old, 10-pound green-eyed kitty who keeps tabs on his humans all day long and will plead for attention whenever he’s in the mood for lovin’…which is pretty often!

Ducky is litter-box trained and always keeps a neat appearance. Although he tends to spend most of his time lounging around, gazing out the windows, and keenly observing his surroundings, he is very adept at chasing and capturing his favorite toy mouse, his fuzzy string toys, or his small jingly balls. Toys are a big hit with Ducky, and he seems to enjoy them all. This dude is the ideal blend of playfulness and mellowness, with lots of cat naps in between.

A wee bit shy at first, Ducky has become bolder and more daring with each passing day. He will actually seek out his trusted sources for butt rubs, chin scratches, and head pets. He’ll also never turn down the offer of a treat. Whoever said cats were finicky never met Ducky!

This sweet guy will make a wonderful pet for anyone who appreciates the beauty and wonder of felines.

*** Ducky is FIV+, but don’t let that hold you back—cats with FIV can live long, healthy, happy lives with the right care. He’s just as loving and deserving as any other kitty! ***
