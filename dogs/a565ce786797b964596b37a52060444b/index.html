She has been feeling melancholy since being abandoned, but is slowly coming out of her shell since moving into a loving foster home. She doesnt demand attention but enjoys affection from people, and coexists peacefully with her foster dog and cat siblings. Shes a calm, mellow girl with a gentle heart ready to love again.

Shades is fostered in Brooklyn, NY and is available for local or out-of-state adoption as long as the adopter can travel to meet her. To get the adoption process started, please send us an application at https://bit.ly/rosr-adopt-foster.
