Description provided by Animal Lighthouse Rescue

Meet Mickey, a 3-year-old, 17-pound Chihuahua mix who is ready to find his forever home! This happy-go-lucky little guy may be small in size, but he has a BIG personality and a Chihuahua attitude that is tried, true, and absolutely adorable. Mickey likes to think he’s the boss of the house—and honestly, he may have a point! He loves lounging around, playing with his favorite toys, going for walks, and keeping tabs on everything happening around him. When Mickey gets curious, he gets creative! If his taller doggy friends are blocking his view, he has no problem bouncing off them to get a better look. Mickey is the perfect combination of playful, fun-loving, curious, and cuddly. He may be little, but he has enough personality to fill a room! He is the perfect companion to take with you to a local coffee shop, a trip around the park, on a plane or a train. He was found walking around with a puppy-friend looking for food. They were both in need of some TLC but his friend has already found a loving home. Now, it is Mickeys turn for a life full of love and laughter. If you’re looking for a pint-sized companion with a larger-than-life personality, Mickey might just be your guy!

Mickey is neutered, microchipped, and up to date on his vaccinations. For more information about him, please fill out an application at www.ALRcares.com and email Julie@alrcares.com. We schedule meet and greets for approved adopters only, an application is the first step in our approval process. Thank you!
