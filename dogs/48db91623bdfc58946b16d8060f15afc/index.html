Meet Maybach! At just a year old, this young mama has already been through a lot. She and her five kittens were found as strays hiding in a shed, but Maybach has handled motherhood with grace and is now ready for a fresh start of her own.

Maybach is still practically a kitten herself, with so much life, love, and comfort ahead of her. After caring for her little crew, it is finally her turn to be spoiled with soft beds, sunny windows, gentle attention, and a family who will make her feel safe.

With a name like Maybach, she is clearly made for the luxury life. Now she just needs a forever home where she can leave her rough road behind and enjoy the smooth ride ahead.
