Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Jaden is a mellow fellow who believes the best things in life are the simple ones: a cozy spot to relax, a good toy to bat around, a tasty snack, and the quiet company of a friend nearby. He came to ARF when his former family faced housing challenges, and since then he has been gently winning hearts here in his own understated way.

Jaden may take a little time to get to know new people, but he is well worth the wait. Once he feels comfortable, his sweet personality begins to shine and those big, beautiful Jade(en)-green eyes do most of the talking. He is the kind of cat who forms a real bond with his people and shows his affection in soft, meaningful ways.

One thing Jaden is sure about: he loves other cats. He truly enjoys feline company and would likely be happiest in a home with a cat friend. He is also wonderfully good at entertaining himself and can spend ages happily batting away at his favorite ball tower toy as if it were the greatest invention ever created. And while Jaden may be mellow, he gets very enthusiastic when snacks are involved. Treat time is easily one of his favorite parts of the day.

With his calm spirit, playful side, and gentle heart, Jaden is looking for a patient adopter who will give him the time and space to settle in. Once he feels safe, you will discover a quietly charming companion who makes every little moment feel special.
