Meet Funnel Cake!

Funnel Cake was recently found as a stray and brought back to ACC, but this isn't her first stay with us. We know a little more about this sweet girl from her previous time at the shelter, including that she was described as excited and playful with other dogs. She's young, energetic, and highly social with people, readily approaching handlers and happily leaning in for affection. She's got plenty of sweetness to share, and we're hoping this stay is the one that leads her to her forever home!

My Ideal Home

Funnel Cake would love an active home that can keep her body and brain busy every day. Long walks, food puzzles, long-lasting chews, and games like hide and seek will help her channel all that youthful energy in positive ways. She's still working on her manners and confidence, so a patient family who uses reward-based training will help her rise to the occasion!

Good with Other Pets?

According to her previous owner, Funnel Cake was excited and playful with other dogs. She also showed friendly, loose body language when introduced to another dog at the care center, though the interaction was kept brief. Her new family should continue introductions slowly and supervise play as she gets comfortable.

Good with Kids?

Funnel Cake would do best in a home without young children under 5. She's enthusiastic and can jump up when seeking attention, and she can also become appeasing when she feels unsure, so a calmer environment will give her the space she needs to feel comfortable.

Things to Know

Funnel Cake can be a little nervous when she's unsure, and may roll onto her back, tuck her tail, or even have an accident when feeling overwhelmed. It's important to let her approach people and new experiences at her own pace and allow her to walk away when she needs space. She's also reported to lunge at some triggers on walks and may need continued help with leash skills, impulse control, and house training.

A Little Something Sweet

Funnel Cake may have had a bit of a sticky start, but she's ready for a fresh beginning! Give her patience, plenty of enrichment, and lots of positive experiences, and this sweet girl might just be the icing on the cake of your family.
