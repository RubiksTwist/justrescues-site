🍫 Meet S'more! 🍫

S'more's story didn't start out very sweet. She was found tied to a tree, and the shelter environment has been a scary place for this sensitive girl. She's very fearful right now, but with a slow, gentle, and considerate approach, S'more is beginning to let her guard down and warm up to the people around her. ❤️

S'more will need a patient, understanding adopter who can give her the time and space she needs to feel safe. She may not be the kind of girl who rushes into your arms right away, but with kindness, consistency, and a little extra love, she has the potential to blossom into a wonderful companion.

Her ideal home will be one where she can decompress at her own pace and discover that the world can be a kind place after all.
