Description provided by Bideawee

Sweet Sapphire is looking for a second chance at a loving home. After spending a year and a half with her previous adopter, she sadly found herself back in our care when her owner became ill. Sapphire is a loving girl who can be shy at first, but once she feels comfortable, she enjoys chatting with her favorite people and curling up on a warm lap. She can be easily startled and sometimes needs a little space when she’s feeling unsure, so a home where she can take things at her own pace would be ideal. She’s hoping to find a patient, understanding family who will give her the time and love she needs to feel safe again.

If this cat is located at our Manhattan Adoption Center, head in to meet them and see if you are a match! We are a same-day adoption center, and an application does not put you on a list or hold an animal for you. If they are in Wantagh or in a foster home, please submit an online application and our team will reach out to further discuss the adoption process. Prior to adoption, all of our animals are spayed/neutered, microchipped, and given their age-appropriate vaccines.
