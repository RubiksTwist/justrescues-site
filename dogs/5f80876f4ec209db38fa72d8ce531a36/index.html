Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Dusty might need a little time to decide you're her kind of person, but trust us, she's worth the wait! She's a girl who takes a little while to warm up to new faces, and she'd much rather get to know you on her own terms than rush into a friendship. After a few meet-and-greets and some time to get comfortable, you'll start seeing her walls come down and her sweet personality come through.

And once Dusty decides she likes you? Get ready for the sweetest greetings! She'll be so happy to see you, her tail will be wagging, and she'll make sure you know she's excited you're there. There's something extra special about earning the love of a dog who didn't immediately trust you, and Dusty has the potential to make that bond incredibly rewarding.

She's looking for an adopter with some experience, a little extra patience, and a willingness to understand her needs as she settles into a home. Dusty receives a little extra support through medication to help keep her anxiety at ease and set her up for success, and she'll need someone committed to keeping her on track.

Dusty isn't asking for perfection - she's just asking for someone willing to give her a chance, let her take things at her own pace, and see the wonderful dog she can be. Her future person may have to work a little harder to win her over, but those happy tail wags and excited greetings will make it all worthwhile.
