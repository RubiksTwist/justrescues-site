HISTORY

Mabel Mora was adopted as a puppy (from another rescue). After her family had children and moved to the New York area, they were no longer able to give her the time she needed and asked one of our boarding partners, where she was a regular, for help rehoming her. She came into Badass’s care in early October 2026.

DETAILED PROFILE

Mabel Mora greets company like she’s been waiting all day for you personally. Expect a lap of zoomies around the room, then a full-body wiggle as she cozies right up. She can come on strong, but there’s nothing behind it except a very sweet, very friendly girl who’s thrilled you’re here.

She’s goofy, alert (nothing on the block gets past her), and occasionally stubborn, with moderate-to-active energy, and exercise is the key to her happiness. Her previous family shared that she loves long walks and accompanying her people in their everyday adventures. Once she got that exercise out of her system, she was relaxed and easygoing at home. Her favorite thing is a full-speed run through the woods. She’s also a talker who will let you know what she thinks. An active adopter who loves long walks, hikes, or trails is her ideal match.

She’s lived with a toddler and a baby and was patient with them, even when things got grabby, so kids of all ages are on the table. She does well with dogs at daycare and boarding and could live with another dog with the right match. Her experience with cats isn’t known.

She’s house trained, knows her basic commands, and heels nicely on leash. She’s very food motivated, which makes training easy. A crate refresher may be beneficial. Like any dog changing homes, she may show some regression as she settles in.

Lace up the hiking boots. Mabel already has the trail picked out.

MEDICAL HISTORY

The dogs in our care are up-to-date on vaccinations, spayed or neutered (when old enough), microchipped, and given a general health check. Immediate medical needs and medical history will be addressed with the adopter prior to adoption. Complete vet records will be sent shortly after adoption.
