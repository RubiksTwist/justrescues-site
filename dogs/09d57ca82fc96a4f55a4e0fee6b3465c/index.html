🖤 Meet Panda! 🖤

Panda’s story is one that reminds us just how much a little love and attention can change a cat’s life.

This handsome tuxedo boy came to our rescue all the way from Staten Island, where he had been living in a dark basement with someone who was only able to stop by once a day to feed him. He had food, but what he really wanted was so much more. He wanted companionship, affection, a cozy place to nap, a windowsill to call his own, and someone to cuddle with.

And finally, Panda got his chance! He’s now enjoying life in a loving foster home, and we couldn’t be happier watching him soak up all the attention and affection he’s been missing.

Now he’s ready for the next step: finding a forever family who will give him all the love he deserves, not just for a little while, but for the rest of his life.

A little important information about Panda: he is FIV positive. FIV-positive cats can live long, happy, fulfilling lives with proper care and routine veterinary attention. His diagnosis does not define him, and it certainly doesn’t make him any less deserving of a loving home.

Panda spent too long living without the companionship he craved. Now that he’s discovered how wonderful home life can be, we want nothing more than to see him experience it forever.

He’s had enough of dark basements. Panda is ready for sunny windowsills, cozy cuddles, and a family to call his own. 🐼🤍🐾
