Meet Wasabi! 💚🐾

Wasabi is a friendly, social boy who loves attention and is happy to come over for pets! He walks nicely on leash with only mild pulling and tends to ignore other people and dogs while out and about. He's comfortable with handling and enjoys playing with his toys.

This big, silly guy has lots of energy and will thrive with plenty of exercise, mental enrichment, fun chew toys, and continued positive, reward-based training. Wasabi can get a little excited and jumpy, and he may become anxious or destructive when left alone, so he's looking for a patient adopter who can help him learn and feel secure.

Wasabi is recommended for a home without children under 13. With structure, patience, and lots of love, this playful pup is ready to find his forever home! 🏡💚
