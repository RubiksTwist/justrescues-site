Description provided by ASPCA Adoption Center

Meet Zuna, an affectionate girl who is always ready for fun!

Zuna is incredibly friendly from the start and absolutely loves being around people. She’s a big fan of pets, and she’ll happily get as close to you as possible. Zuna loves cuddling and hugs in a way that many dogs don't! It's such a treat to embrace her.

Zuna also loves getting outside to explore, especially on walks where she can take her time sniffing and checking out her surroundings. She is unphased by the hustle and bustle of the busy streets, making her a true city girl - perfect for NYC!

Her other hobbies include playing tug, running, practicing tricks, and chewing on her toys. Zuna also enjoys the company of other dogs and has made some great doggy friends in playgroup. Whether she’s playing alongside them or simply relaxing nearby, she enjoys sharing her time with her canine pals.

Give Zuna adventures and cuddles, and she’ll happily fill your days with fun and love! Click the “Adopt Me” button to learn more.
