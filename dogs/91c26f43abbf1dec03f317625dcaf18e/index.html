Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Bean is an adorable mixed breed puppy who was born deaf, which she seems to consider a very minor technicality. She is far too busy playing, cuddling, making friends, and squeezing every possible bit of fun out of puppyhood to worry about it.

She loves other dogs, is eager to learn, and somehow manages to be both a very enthusiastic little puppy and an excellent sleeper. Bean is affectionate, curious, playful, and completely ready to take on the world in her own way.

Bean will need a committed adopter who is excited to put in the time for training and help her learn how to navigate the world confidently. She has all the makings of an incredible companion for someone ready to grow with her.

Registered adopters interested in Bean can email adopt@hbrdogs.com for more info. If you're not registered yet, do it today at hbrdogs.com!
