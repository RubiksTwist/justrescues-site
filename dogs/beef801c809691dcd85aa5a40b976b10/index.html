Hi everyone, I'm Bobby / Buddy! Don't let my age fool you, I am absolutely full of life and have SO much love to give.

I love nothing more than following my foster parents around from room to room, smelling every crevice, playing with my toys, belly rubs, walks in the park and snoozing in my bed at the foot of my foster parents couch. I am friendly but unbothered by other dogs, love pats from anyone who'll offer them.

From foster parents - we have adored taking care of Bobby. He has all the benefits of an older dog (house-trained, self-soothes, sleeps through the night, knows commands, respects owners, great on leash) with all the personality, love and playfulness of a dog much, much younger. He is a perfect doggo. We would do fantastic in any environment that can give him lots of love, affection and walks.

﻿Bobby is approximately 10y 1m 23d old and is fully grown at 20 pounds. They are microchipped and up to date on all age-appropriate medical care. Their breed is unknown as we have not had a DNA test done.
