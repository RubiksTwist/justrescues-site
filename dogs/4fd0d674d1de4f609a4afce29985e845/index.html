Meet Yoshi, a survivor of South Korea's devastating wildfires in 2025.

When the fires swept through her community, Yoshi was chained outside, unable to escape. She survived, but suffered painful burns to her back and legs.

After months of medical care, rehabilitation, and lots of love, look at her now!

Today, this beautiful 2-year-old Jindo mix is blossoming in her foster home, discovering what it means to feel safe, loved, and free.

Her foster mom says she's playful, curious, and growing more confident every day. She loves her toys, gets excited for walks, and has the biggest ears that make the funniest flapping sound when she shakes!

She's still learning that loud noises and unfamiliar people aren't something to fear, but every day she's making progress.

From surviving a wildfire while chained outside to sleeping peacefully in a soft bed, Yoshi has come such a long way.

Now all she needs is a forever family to continue her beautiful journey.
