Cash is at risk due to behavior concerns, she arrived at the care centers after being involved in a bite incident which when on a walk with the owners child. Cash picked something up from the floor and when the child attempted to remove it from Cash's mouth she bit them on their finger, this did not need medical attention. Upon arrival at the care centers she displayed a fearful disposition needed time to acclimate before allowing handling, she has also displayed moments of object fixation, particularly traffic cones. Cash has displayed social behavior with handlers in care and has been reported to be affectionate with familiar handlers. Medically Cash is apparently healthy.

Cash came into the care center through the police after a bite in the prior home. On arrival a police officer mentioned that Cash was on a walk and picked something up off the ground and the kid went to grab it, that is when Cash bit the kid's finger. In the care center, Cash is observed fixating on traffic cones, frequently grabbing and holding onto them and proving difficult to redirect. During walks, she often carries a tennis ball in her mouth and displays leash pulling toward novel dogs. Cash readily allows handlers to pet her and is very social and affiliative with staff and handlers. Cash is available for rescue through one of our new hope partners.
