Meet Maybelle!

Maybelle came to ACC as an owner surrender. She can be shy at first and appreciates having the time and space to feel comfortable. Once she feels safe, she has a playful side and enjoys affection on her own terms.

My Ideal Home

Maybelle would do best in a calm, patient home where her boundaries are respected. She'll need slow introductions, positive reinforcement, and support with separation anxiety. Her family should be comfortable managing resource guarding and handling sensitivity.

Good with Kids?

Maybelle previously lived with young children, but a home without children under 5 is recommended due to her handling sensitivity and resource guarding.

Good with Other Pets?

Maybelle has lived with familiar dogs but can feel uncomfortable with unfamiliar dogs. Slow introductions are important, and dog parks are not recommended. Her history with cats is unknown. She may guard food, toys, and other valued items.

Overall Health

Maybelle has medical needs that staff will discuss with you when you meet her.

Behavior Determination!

Level 2: Maybelle can be sensitive to handling and may guard valued items. Give her space, let her approach at her own pace, and avoid taking items directly from her.

Other Fun Facts

Maybelle knows how to use wee-wee pads or go potty outside on walks. She's ready to find someone who understands that the best friendships grow with patience and trust!
