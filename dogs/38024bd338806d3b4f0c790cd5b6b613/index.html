Description provided by Rescue City

4 Month Old Toffee is as sweet as her name! This playful Lab mix weighs approximately 20 pounds and has a joyful, confident personality. She loves a good sniff session, rolling her favorite puzzle toy around the apartment, and chasing down an ice cube. After playtime, she’s ready for a snooze and some couch cuddles. Smart and very food motivated, Toffee has mastered “sit” and is working on “stay,” “down,” and “come.” She sleeps through the night in her crate and takes crate naps during the day, too. She’ll need a family excited for a puppy, who is ready to continue her training with patience and consistency. Toffee has been friendly and welcoming with new people. With her playful little jumps, affectionate nature, and enthusiasm for learning, Toffee brings plenty of joy to everyday life. She’s looking for a family with the time to help her learn, grow, and enjoy being a puppy! She has finished her puppy vaccines, and is spayed!
