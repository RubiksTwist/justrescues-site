Description provided by Animal Lighthouse Rescue

Meet Jon Snow! At just 7 months old and weighing a tiny 8 pounds, Jon Snow is proof that the biggest personalities can come in the tiniest packages! This adorable domestic shorthair is incredibly playful, affectionate, and has a special talent for making everyone around him smile. Jon Snow’s signature sleeping position says it all about his personality — you’ll find him sprawled out on his back, feet in the air, completely relaxed and somehow intertwined with whichever lucky person or kitty happens to be sleeping closest to him. He absolutely loves his treats, adores his littermates, and is always ready for a little fun. But once playtime is over, this sweet boy will happily settle in for some cuddles and purr his way straight into your heart. If you’re looking for a tiny companion with a huge personality, a playful spirit, and a heart full of love, Jon Snow may be your perfect match!

Jon Snow is neutered, microchipped, FELV/FIV negative and up to date on his vaccines. For more information about him, please fill out an application at www.alrcares.com and email Grace@alrcares.com. We schedule meet and greets for approved adopters only, an application is the first step in our approval process. Thank you!
