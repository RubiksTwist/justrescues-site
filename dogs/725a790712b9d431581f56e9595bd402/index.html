Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Gnocchi is a 1-year-old pug who weighs around 14 lbs and has absolutely no interest in minding his own business. He wants to say hi to everyone, investigate everything, and be included in whatever you’re doing. He’s endlessly curious, never shy, and brings very good vibes everywhere he goes.

He has a hilarious personality and somehow manages to be both incredibly playful and a professional lap dog. Gnocchi loves watching TV, checking out anything new around him, playing with his people, and then climbing straight into your lap when he’s ready to settle down. He’s great with people and other dogs and seems to approach pretty much everything with enthusiasm.

Gnocchi is crate-trained and making good progress with his house-training. He’s affectionate, funny, social, and extremely committed to being involved in your life, preferably from the closest possible spot.
