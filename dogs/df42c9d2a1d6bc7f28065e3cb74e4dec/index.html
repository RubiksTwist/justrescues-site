Meet Buddy, a lovable, 3 year old affectionate young cat with a big personality and an even bigger heart! Buddy absolutely adores people and will greet you with happy purrs, sweet snuggles, and the cutest air biscuits when he’s feeling loved. He’s a confident, curious boy who enjoys exploring his space, watching birds from the window, and playing with interactive toys and food puzzles. Buddy has a playful, energetic side and thrives with attention and enrichment, but he also loves curling up for cozy naps near his favorite humans. He bonds quickly and would do best in a home that enjoys both cuddle time and playtime. Fully vetted and healthy, Buddy is ready to bring tons of love, laughs, and companionship to his forever home!
