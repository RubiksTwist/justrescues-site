Meet Cashew!

Cashew is a sweet, energetic puppy who is ready to fill your life with fun, laughter, and plenty of puppy adventures! He's curious about the world around him and loves checking out new things, whether that means exploring a new space, playing with toys, or following along to see what everyone is doing.

This little guy is full of puppy personality. He loves treats, is eager to learn, and is at the perfect age to start building good habits with a family who can guide him along the way. Like all puppies, Cashew will need patience, consistency, and lots of love as he learns the ropes- potty training, puppy manners, and all the important skills that help him grow into a wonderful adult dog.

Cashew is currently looking for a foster-to-adopt home where he can grow and learn while finishing his puppy vaccinations and neuter surgery. He will be fully ready to officially join your family around December. In the meantime, you can welcome him into your home, help him grow, and be there for all of his puppy milestones while he gets ready to officially become part of your family.

If you're looking for a fun-loving, affectionate puppy to share your adventures with, Cashew can't wait to meet you! Please fill out and submit an adoption application today.
