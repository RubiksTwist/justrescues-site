Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

The secret ingredient is Goulash! At 9 years old, this 32-pound terrier mix is a pretty chill guy who doesn't demand constant attention, but is always happy to get some pets. One of his favorite activities is rolling around on a blanket, so be sure to have a few handy! (And yes, don't tell him but he could certainly lose a few pounds...)

Goulash is fully house-trained and has no problem heading into his crate to relax. When meeting new people, he likes to take a moment to check things out before coming over for a sniff.

While Goulash loves his time indoors, heading outside brings out his adventurous side! He's an enthusiastic explorer who likes to follow his nose and investigate everything around him. He's still working on his leash skills and learning how to channel his excitement during walks.
