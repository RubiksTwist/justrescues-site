Description provided by ASPCA Adoption Center

Nana is meant for the quiet life. She’d love to lay next to you as you snuggle up with a good read on the couch. If you have a hand free for petting, even better! The city is a fun place to visit, but Nana prefers a slower-paced life. Her ideal neighborhood is nice and mellow.

But Nana has a playful side. She loves her toys and is full of tail wags and just the right amount of sass. Her foster mom says she got along with her cat siblings, staying calm even when the cats tried to intimate her. We’re so proud!

The world can be large and frightening, but Nana is learning that with good people by her side, the world is also beautiful and loving. She has a lot of love to give to the right family.

Come meet Nana at the Adoption Center during walk-in hours (daily, noon to 6 p.m.), or click the “Adopt Me” button to learn more!

*You can meet animals located at the ASPCA Adoption Center during our walk-in hours (daily, noon to 6 p.m.). We encourage a $150 suggested adoption fee when adopting an animal.
