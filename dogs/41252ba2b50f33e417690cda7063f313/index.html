Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

My foster writes: There will never be a dull moment with Jupiter around! This little bundle of energy is always on the move, whether he's chasing his favorite toys, zooming through his tunnel, or exploring every nook and cranny. A curious little soul, he loves following you around like a tiny shadow, keeping tabs on everything you do. He's also quite the sweetheart, showing his affection by rubbing against your legs and making the sweetest little chirping sounds when you pet him.

Jupiter is very food-motivated, so treats are an easy way to win his heart! He's a clever little guy who loves to put on his thinking cap and solve puzzle toys to uncover his favorite treats. Once he's had his fill of adventures, he enjoys winding down by watching the world go by from the window or curling up right beside you. With his playful personality, curious nature, and affectionate side, Jupiter is sure to bring lots of love, laughter, and entertainment to his forever home!

Jupiter tolerates attention and petting but appears fearful or stressed in the shelter. He may be a little more independent, and may need time to warm up to his new home. We recommend that this cat go to a home with experienced cat parents.
