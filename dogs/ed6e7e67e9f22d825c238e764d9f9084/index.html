Description provided by AMA Animal Rescue

Meet Chuck and Blaire, a devoted bonded pair. These sweet souls had a heartbreaking start when they were left outside our shelter doors in a cramped crate, scared and neglected. During their time with us, they have leaned on each other and are blooming more every day. Because of their deep connection, this inseparable duo must be adopted together into a gentle, loving home.

Blaire is a delightfully cheerful soul who lives purely in the moment with a blissfully unbothered heart. She is quick to offer unconditional love and thrives on simple affection, happily wandering through life without a single worry in her head. Her bright, gentle demeanor instantly warms up any room and brings an effortless sweetness to everyone she meets. Her innocent enthusiasm serves as a comforting anchor for her brother, making them a perfectly balanced team. Her approximate DOB is 00/17/2019. Weight: 3 lbs.

Chuck is a bit more thoughtful than his carefree sister, though it doesn't take too long for him to warm up to new people. While he likes to take a moment to observe his surroundings, a few soft words and a gentle touch go a long way to help him feel secure. His approximate DOB is 09/17/2020. Weight: 4 lbs.

Because of their strong bond, Chuck and Blaire must be adopted together. Blaire’s outgoing nature helps Chuck feel safe, and Chuck’s gentle presence keeps Blair content. They are truly the ultimate team!

They are seeking a calm, loving home with a family ready to give them the safe haven they’ve always needed. If you have room in your heart and home for double the love, Chuck and Blair are ready to meet you! Please fill out and submit an adoption application today.
