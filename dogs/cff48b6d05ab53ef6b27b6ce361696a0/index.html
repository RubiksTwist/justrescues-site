Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Meet Lola, the sweetest girl waiting for her happy ending! ❤️

Lola's story begins with a heartbreaking note. She was left outside a cat rescue in Brooklyn, wearing a sweatshirt that read, "My name is Lola, please help me." We don't know much about what her life looked like before that night, but we do know that whoever welcomes this incredibly sweet girl into their home will be very lucky.

Lola is estimated to be between 3 and 5 years old and weighs 63 pounds. Despite everything she's been through, she's one of the gentlest, sweetest dogs you could hope to meet. She's still settling into foster care, and we're getting to know her personality a little more each day, but so far, she's been an absolute pleasure to have around.

And this girl has some seriously impressive house manners! Lola is house-trained, does beautifully in her crate, and can be left home alone without issue. She's also a lovely walking companion who walks nicely on leash and doesn't react to other dogs or people she passes. She's perfectly content to mind her own business and enjoy a peaceful stroll.

Lola is a quiet, gentle soul who asks for very little. After all the uncertainty she's experienced, she's taking her time getting comfortable in her new surroundings, and we're letting her settle in at her own pace. It's clear that underneath it all is a very special dog with so much love to give.

Lola's next chapter deserves to be so much better than her last. We're hoping to find her a loving home where she'll feel safe, cherished, and never have to wonder where she belongs again.
