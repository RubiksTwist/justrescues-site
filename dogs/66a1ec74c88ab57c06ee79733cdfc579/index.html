Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Meet Sesame, this delightful, two-year-old, 14.5-pound Terrier-mix! He is a certified very, very, good boy who loves everyone he meets. He is especially found of giving kisses and cuddling up with his people. He has the biggest, most beautiful brown eyes that he loves to use to lovingly stare up at you. He is a super athlete and loves to show off his jumping ability while playing a riveting game of fetch with his bouncy ball! Sesame gets along well with almost every dog and has yet to meet a person who he didn’t adore. He is already crate trained and walks well on leash too! He has an adorable smile he loves to put on full display when he’s happy and as long as he can be by your side, he’s always happy. Sesame is especially found of climbing into your lap and cuddling. He is such an easy-going boy and enjoys both long walks and long naps. He is the sweetest pup and he cannot wait to find his forever home!
