Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Meet Faith, our sweet Southern sunshine in the making!

This precious pup hopped on a special transport all the way from Alabama, flying to safety ahead of Hurricane Isasis. Thanks to ARF’s ability to make space for Faith and her friends, we were also able to open our doors to even more pups in need after the storm. Now Faith is safe on solid ground, and she’s taking her very first steps toward a brand-new life!

Faith is a gentle, sweet soul who is still getting comfortable with the big, exciting world around her. Like a flower that’s just beginning to bloom, she’s blossoming at her own pace, and every little bit of progress is something to celebrate. With a soft touch, a cozy spot to rest, and a family who loves her patiently, we know her true colors will shine through.

She’s looking for someone who sees the magic in quiet moments and is ready to share a journey of trust, snuggles, and tail wags to come. The hardest part of her adventure is already behind her!

Faith has weathered the storm, and now she’s ready for a lifetime of sunny days, curled up safe and sound with a family who adores her. Because sometimes, all it takes is a little faith to find your sunshine.
