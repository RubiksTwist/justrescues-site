Description provided by AMA Animal Rescue

Meet Rocky, the ultimate lover boy with a heart as big as his paws! This Rottweiler/Australian Shepherd mix is a true charmer, always ready to shower you with affection and bask in all the love you can give.

Rocky has a little gait that gives him a distinctive walk, but don’t let that fool you, he’s fully mobile and still loves to explore and enjoy life to the fullest. He would do best in a home where there aren't too many stairs, just to keep him feeling his best.

Rocky is a true people lover, always ready for a cuddle or a little affection from his human friends. He prefers to be out in the open and isn’t fond of being crated, he’s perfectly content just hanging out by your side. Rocky has met a few pups in the neighborhood, and he seems to enjoy the company of other friendly dogs.

If you’re ready for a loyal companion who’s all heart, Rocky might just be the one to steal yours. Please fill out and submit an adoption application today.
