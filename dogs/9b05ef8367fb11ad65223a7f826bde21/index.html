Meet Palmer!

Palmer is a sweet Alabama girl who has already learned that sometimes the smallest paws can overcome the biggest challenges.

Palmer and her siblings, Story, Booker, and Major, were found on the streets of Alabama without their mom. Instead of growing up safe and sound with their family, these puppies were left to navigate the world far too young and without the protection they needed.

Thankfully, Palmer's story didn't end there. She was rescued alongside her siblings and is now getting the chance every puppy deserves: to grow, explore, play, and learn what it means to be loved.

Palmer is ready for the next chapter of her life, one filled with soft beds, plenty of affection, fun adventures, and a family who will promise to always be there for her. After such an uncertain start, she deserves nothing less than a forever home where she can feel safe and cherished.

Could Palmer be the missing piece to your family? Apply to adopt this beautiful girl today!
