Milo Man is on the at-risk list due to behavior concerns. In the care center, Milo Man has shown fearful and defensive behavior around leashing and handling, including retreating into his crate, stiffening, whale eye, paw lifting, growling, flinching, lunging, and snapping toward handlers. His defensive behavior escalated during attempts to clip onto his harness and use a slip lead, making it unsafe for the handler to handle him safely. When he was removed from kennel for cleaning, he is very social with staff but is struggling to acclimate. His previous family reports Milo Man is very outgoing, enjoys attention, was mostly kept indoors so has limited experience on a leash but does enjoy car rides. Medically, Milo Man is apparently healthy.

Milo Man was surrendered to the care center by his previous owners due to their personal circumstances. Milo Man's previous owners describe him as an active, playful, and outgoing dog who brought them a lot of joy and loves being petted and receiving attention, playing with balls, and running around. Milo Man has not acclimated well to the care center environment; while he has been social and affiliative with staff in calmer environments, he has shown increasingly defensive behavior around being leashed, including growling, lunging, and snapping, that makes it difficult to safely remove him from kennel and handle him. Due to these concerns, we recommend placement with a New Hope partner who can provide any necessary behavior modification (force-free, positive reinforcement-based) and re-evaluate behavior in a stable home environment before placement into a permanent home.
