Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

David is a 2 year-old chihuahua weighing around 19 lbs, with a gentle, sweet soul through and through. He’s still adjusting to life in the city, but he’s gaining confidence every single day, and his foster says he’s getting braver by the minute.

David is house-trained, crate-trained, and heads into his crate to sleep like a champ every night. He’s a quiet guy who rarely makes a sound, and he does well on leash. He gets along nicely with his foster dog brother and is becoming more curious about the other pups he meets on walks. He loves having his head scratched, has the most unique, adorable ears, and is very motivated by food, especially anything with meat in it!

David will thrive with an adopter who’s happy to let him warm up at his own pace. Give him a little time, a few tasty treats, and some head scratches, and you’ll have a loyal, loving companion.

Registered adopters interested in David can email adopt@hbrdogs.org for more info. If you’re not registered yet, you can do it today at hbrdogs.org.
