Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

So Dottie is fun to have around. After every walk (4 a day) she has a mild case of zoomies. She likes to run around and look for her toys to play with. Dottie likes tug ropes and hard rubber or plastic chew toys. She likes to play for 5-10 minutes then likes to go off on her own with her toys.
Dottie is always curious about my whereabouts when she doesn't see me. But when she finds me she respecfully sits close by.
Given the opportunity Dottie likes to run around outside and play on the grass. And when playtime is over she winds down with a little petting time often going belly up.
She enjoys her nap time but she looks to spend time sleeping peacefully with her best friend Draco. As a matter of fact Dottie has shown her care for Draco as she grooms him numerous times a day. Though she is definitely the leader of the two.
Dottie has had 1 indoor soiling accident and a urine accident as well in the 1 month I've had her. Both were because unfortunately I had left her a little time past her scheduled walk. But surprisingly both time she went on the weewee pads I left by the entrance exit.
Dottie also loves to cuddle on the couch and bed but also respects space knowing she will have to share it with Draco.
When Dottie is outside with Draco and both are sitting or laying down, she and Draco can tend to be dog reactive by barking if more than one dog passes. Redirecting them seems to be the best method to avoid these incidents.
As far as meeting new people Dottie likes slow meetings to let her ease her way to whoever she might have interest in. The same goes if she's to meet another dog. Usually meet a couple of feet away and let her show you when she is ready.

﻿Dottie (Bonded with Draco) is approximately 1y 9m 21d old and is fully grown at 25 pounds. They are microchipped and up to date on all age-appropriate medical care. Their breed is unknown as we have not had a DNA test done.
