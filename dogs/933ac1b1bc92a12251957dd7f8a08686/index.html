Submit an adoption application a t socialteesnyc.org Their foster says: "Bear is exactly what his name suggests: a big, lovable teddy bear who would happily spend his days curled up next to you. When he isn’t asking for cuddles, you’ll usually find him curled up like a little donut on a soft bed or couch, taking one of his many naps. At night, he loves sleeping right by your side, and in the morning, he is more than happy to roll over for belly rubs. He has a wonderful balance of playful and relaxed, making him a great companion for someone who wants a dog who enjoys both adventures and cozy days at home. He is also a fast learner and has already picked up several commands, including sit, lay down, and place. He is currently working on learning his name, and with his ability to learn quickly, I think he will continue making great progress with consistent training. He is comfortable being left alone and has had free range of the house without displaying destructive or mouthy behavior. One of the cutest things about Bear is how curious he is about the world around him. He absolutely loves looking at his own reflection, whether it’s in a mirror, a window, or even a puddle! He is also a true social butterfly and has been fantastic with dogs of all sizes and ages. Despite being a big boy, Bear still very much thinks he’s a puppy. He loves to play, roll around, and wrestle with his canine friends before eventually settling down for a nap. Because he enjoys canine companionship so much, he could be especially happy in a home with another friendly dog, ideally one who enjoys playing with a bigger dog. Bear is still figuring out the world of toys. He is not particularly interested in squeaky toys but enjoys soft plush toys and balls. If you approach him while he is relaxing, do not be surprised if he immediately rolls onto his back, belly up, asking for scratches. He also loves having his floppy ears cleaned and is very comfortable being handled. Overall, Bear is a sweet, affectionate, goofy, and loving companion who has so much love to give. He would make a wonderful addition to a home looking for a big cuddle bug with a playful side and a heart just as big as he is." Up to date on their DHPP, Bordetella, and Rabies vaccines; heartworm tested if coming from Tennessee or Puerto Rico; spayed/neutered; microchipped; treated with dewormer upon intake How did this dog end up with Social Tees? Most of our dogs come via partner groups who help us save them from shelters where they are at risk of euthanasia in Tennessee, Los Angeles, and Puerto Rico. There are very few spay/neuter resources there and many more animals in those areas than there are people to adopt them. Rescue groups like ours bring them to the northeast where the desire for pets like these allows us to save their lives.
