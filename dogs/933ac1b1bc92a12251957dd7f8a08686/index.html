Submit an adoption application a t socialteesnyc.org THEIR FOSTER SAYS: "Bear is exactly what his name suggests: a big, lovable teddy bear who would happily spend his days curled up next to you (or on top of you!) simply because he doesn't quite realize how big he is. He is the ultimate cuddle bug and will gladly rest his big head in your lap whenever he gets the chance. And if you get close enough, you're officially in the face-kissing zone! When he isn't asking for cuddles, you'll usually find him curled up like a little donut on a soft bed or couch, enjoying one of his many naps. At night, he loves sleeping right by your side, and in the morning, he's more than happy to roll over for belly rubs. In fact, if you approach him while he's relaxing, don't be surprised if he immediately flops onto his back, belly up, asking for scratches. He also loves having his floppy ears cleaned and his coat brushed, and he's very comfortable being handled. One thing to keep in mind is that he can be a little nervous around blow dryers and other similar loud noises. On the other hand, he's an expert traveler and does wonderfully in the car, even on longer drives, happily settling in for the ride. Bear is also a very quick learner, especially when treats are involved! He's already picked up several commands, including sit, lay down, place, and paw, and his recall continues to improve every day. With his eagerness to learn, he'll likely continue making great progress with consistent training. He's also an incredibly curious guy who loves exploring his surroundings, whether that's sniffing every corner of your apartment or investigating interesting spots along his walks. He even enjoys looking at his own reflection! Outside, he loves bird-watching and gets especially curious whenever he spots one nearby and his hunting instincts kick in. Although he's three years old, Bear can sometimes act like a giant puppy, especially when he's outside! He's a true social butterfly and has been fantastic with dogs of all sizes and ages. He's incredibly friendly with both people and other dogs and is always excited to meet new friends. He especially LOVES playing with his furry friends, particularly those around his own size who can keep up with his playful energy. Bear truly thrives outdoors. He loves rolling around in the grass, soaking up the sunshine, and simply watching the world go by. At home, however, he's a much more relaxed guy who spends most of his day napping. That doesn't mean he doesn't enjoy keeping his mind busy! He loves brain games, learning new tricks during training sessions, chewing on his favorite bones, and playing with his beloved stuffed toy, "Happy Capy" (a capybara). Happy Capy goes just about everywhere with him. Bear carries him around, takes naps with him, and treats him like his very own emotional support toy. Bear is happiest when he's surrounded by love and affection, and he would likely enjoy having another furry sibling in the home to keep him company. Of course, he'll never turn down extra belly rubs, attention, or a cozy spot next to his favorite person!Overall, Bear is a sweet, affectionate, goofy, and loving companion with so much love to give. He's the perfect combination of playful outdoor adventurer and relaxed indoor cuddle bug. If you're looking for a big teddy bear with an even bigger heart, Bear might just be your perfect match!" Up to date on their DHPP, Bordetella, and Rabies vaccines; heartworm tested if coming from Tennessee or Puerto Rico; spayed/neutered; microchipped; treated with dewormer upon intake How did this dog end up with Social Tees? Most of our dogs come via partner groups who help us save them from shelters where they are at risk of euthanasia in Tennessee, Los Angeles, and Puerto Rico. There are very few spay/neuter resources there and many more animals in those areas than there are people to adopt them. Rescue groups like ours bring them to the northeast where the desire for pets like these allows us to save their lives.
