14.5 pounds of happiness, curiosity, and the cutest little snores. Meet CHLOE!

This 1.5-year-old Boston Terrier loves her toys, meeting new people, making dog friends, keeping cozy, and investigating everything. She’d even love to befriend the neighborhood pigeons and street cats, although they haven’t exactly returned her enthusiasm.

Chloe has little bursts of playful energy, but she’s an overall calm, easygoing girl who is just as happy snoozing the afternoon away. She’s friendly and respectful with other dogs, enjoys meeting children, and is always ready to make a new friend.

She’s a wonderful walking buddy who’s still getting used to the sights and sounds of the city.
She’s affectionate, curious, and very sweet.
She’s learning her house-training routine and making progress with her crate.
She loves her toys, cozy naps, and having a special little spot to call her own.
She’s not much of a barker, but she might serenade you with some adorable snores!
