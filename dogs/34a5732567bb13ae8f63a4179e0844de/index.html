Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

Just like the wine she's named for - Moscato is sweet, bubbly and perfect for any occasion!

Her stunning bright blue eye, gorgeous merle coat and perfectly speckled nose are what stops people in their tracks, but her personality shines just as bright. Moscato has mastered the art of being balanced - she loves her naps but will make you laugh with reckless abandon when she catches her evening zoomies. Her foster home is a revolving door of pre-teens and she soaks up all the attention she can get from them. She's still learning that her teeth are sharp and that feet/hands/ankles/noses are not, in fact, toys but with consistent reminders she'll get the hang of it soon. She loves actual toys and will collect them all into her bed so she has all the options for appropriate playtime. Her much larger canine foster brother has no interest in her and while she desperately wants to earn his love, she respectfully observes him from outside his self-prescribed bubble. A toasty cuddling sesh is probably her favorite pastime. Give her a lap, or crook of an arm, or a large stuffy and she melts. It's cozy girl season and Moscato is here.for.it.

Moscato wine is known for it's bright flavor, gentle sparkle and approachable taste. The puppy version will make any dark day brighter, give sweet little kisses to make you smile, and be the perfect companion for life.
