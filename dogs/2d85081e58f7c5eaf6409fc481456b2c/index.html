Description provided by Waldo's Rescue Pen

Hello, world. I am a professional chocolate-colored heart thief disguised as a puppy.

When I first arrived at my foster home, I was pretty sure elevators were portals to another dimension and city streets were loud, confusing obstacle courses designed specifically to test my courage. The good news? I'm figuring it out quickly. Every day I get a little braver, a little more confident, and a lot more adorable.

At home, I've already proven myself to be quite the gentleman. House training? Basically a valedictorian. I've only had one accident, which I firmly blame on a clerical error and would prefer not to discuss further.

I am sweet, playful, very quiet and powered by what appears to be an endless supply of puppy energy. My hobbies include chasing toys, investigating absolutely everything, giving out unlimited kisses, and making sure my human never has to sit alone. If you're looking for personal space, I respectfully suggest adopting a goldfish instead.

One of my greatest accomplishments so far has been winning over the resident dog—or at least trying. The resident dog is a bit of a grump and has informed me on several occasions that my enthusiasm is excessive. Personally, I disagree. Fortunately, I am incredibly patient and loving. When my canine roommate gives me side-eye, I simply offer friendship again five minutes later. Persistence is key.

After a busy day of being a fearless explorer, I transform into a world-class cuddle expert. My foster mom has learned that beneath my zoomies is a snuggly little sweetheart who just wants to curl up next to his people and dream about his next adventure.

If you're looking for a puppy who will make you laugh, keep you active, love you endlessly, and grow alongside you as he discovers the world, I might just be your guy.
