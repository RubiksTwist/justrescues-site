Meet Miami — the sunshine girl with a heart full of love!

Once a stray in Florida, Baby Miami ended up in a shelter that sadly didn’t have the resources to care for her. Her life was at risk, so we stepped in and brought her all the way to New York. It may not be the palm-tree paradise she came from, but Miami is thriving in the big city.

This sweet girl has adorable puppy energy, loves to cuddle, and is completely unaware of her own size, which only makes her more lovable. Miami is a good girl through and through, and she’s ready to shower her forever family with joy and affection.

If you’re looking for a happy-hearted, cuddly companion who radiates sunshine wherever she goes, Miami is your girl.

All dogs will be altered, microchipped, and receive age-appropriate vaccines prior to adoption.
