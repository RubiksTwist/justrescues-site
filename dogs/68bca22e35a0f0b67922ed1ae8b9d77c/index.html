Meet Hamhock, a girl who puts her whole heart into everything she does!

Whether she’s learning something new, chasing after a favorite toy or spending time with our team, Hamhock brings plenty of enthusiasm to the occasion. She already knows cues like “sit” and “place” and is always happy when a training session is on the agenda.

Fetch is another favorite, especially when her beloved squeaky toys come out to play! After the fun, Hamhock is just as happy to soak up some attention and enjoy time with the people around her.

Hamhock has brains, playfulness and plenty of affection to share. Ready to find out what the two of you could do together? Click the “Adopt Me” button to connect with Hamhock’s team in Pawling, New York!

*We encourage a $150 suggested adoption fee when adopting an animal. This adoption fee allows the ASPCA to continue our lifesaving work and help even more animals in need. We are grateful for any amount given at the time of adoption.*
