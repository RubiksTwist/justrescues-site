Meet Surf!

Surf is a gentle, sweet boy who is still getting his bearings after being found as a stray. He can be a little shy when meeting new people, but give him some treats, a slow approach, and a little time, and he quickly starts to warm up. Once he feels comfortable, he'll happily lean in for some gentle affection, and we're sure there's a lot more to this sweet boy beneath the surface!

My Ideal Home

Surf would love a patient home where he can take his time getting comfortable with his new surroundings. His new family should let him set the pace and use treats and positive experiences to help him build confidence. With a little patience, Surf may just catch the right wave and settle right into his new life!

Good with Other Pets?

Surf ignored other dogs while walking, which is a promising start, but his history with other animals is unknown. Any introductions should be slow and supervised once he's had time to settle into his new home.

Good with Kids?

Surf would do best in a home without young children under 5. He can be intimidated by new people and needs a slow approach and time to warm up, so a calmer environment will help him feel safe and comfortable.

Things to Know

Surf is a great loose leash walker and happily follows along with a loose, relaxed body. He may be wary of unfamiliar objects or new situations, but he becomes more comfortable with gentle handling and a little encouragement. Give this sweet guy some time and he'll be ready to ride the wave into his forever home!
