Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Meet Kehlani whose real name should be Mama Meatball.

Kehlani is an insanely social, curious and happy pup - the epitome of a dog being a good vibe and a good time.

Luckily for humans, Kehlani loves physical affection in all forms because she's so cute and her velvet fur is so soft it's hard to keep your hands off of her..

Kehlani is an 8 year old, 40 lb compact girl whose full of life and quriks. In the most endearing ways, Kehlani will back into a new friends legs asking for bum scritches or will sit in front of you with her left paw raised high - an ask that she be pet and loved on.

Outdoors, Kehlani is a social butterfly happy to meet and greet with all humans and other dogs. On the hot and rainy days - she's a gem to walk since she knows to do her potty quickly and on the nice weather days - she's always down for a stroll, walking beautifully on a leash.

After her walks, Kehlani is happy to sit patiently while you wipe her paws before resigning to her dog bed for snoozes and the inevitable snoring.

Kehlani is curently being fostered with a suspicious resident cat and has been able to respect his boundaries whilst being respectfully curious.

Kehlani is a super adaptable and adjustable dog who will do swimmingly in any home that loves her and she will surely love them. If you are looking for a sweet girl who cosplays as a puppy potato that will fit into your home seamlessly - Kehlani is your girl and you should apply to meet her ASAP, she will very soon become some lucky persons soulmate.
