DETAILED PROFILE

Toss a ball anywhere near Lightning McQueen and watch him light up. This young yellow lab mix is fun, happy, and excited by pretty much everything, and fetch sits near the top of the list. When we met him, he soaked up every pet we offered and then went right back to looking for the next throw.

He turned up as a stray at a home in Georgia and has been boarding at a local vet's office since.

Other dogs get a friendly, relaxed hello from him. But it's clear this is a dog who would love to play.

His foster home will be the test track for house and crate training, leash skills, energy at home, and how he does with kids and cats. Updates to follow once he arrives in Brooklyn.

Like most young dogs, he'll need some training to channel all that horsepower. His ideal person has a good throwing arm, likes to stay on the move, and thinks a dog who's thrilled about everything is a feature, not a bug.
