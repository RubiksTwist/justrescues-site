Meet Brownie! 🐾

Brownie is a medium-sized girl who is still finding her footing and getting comfortable in her new surroundings. She wasn't feeling her best when she first arrived and has received care while she continues to recover. As she feels better, we hope to see more of her personality shine through.

Brownie is a shy, gentle girl who is still warming up to staff. She allows handling for outings and enjoys going for walks, which is a great start! She may need a little extra time, patience, and understanding as she learns that she is safe and cared for.

We recommend that Brownie's new home start out calm and quiet, giving her the space she needs to decompress and build confidence at her own pace. With time, patience, and a little encouragement, we hope Brownie will continue to come out of her shell and become the loving companion she is meant to be.

If you're looking for a sweet girl who would benefit from a patient and peaceful home, Brownie may be the one for you! ❤️
