Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

If a sporty and muscular aesthetic with the typical retriever personality is your thing - keep reading and meet Billie! Billie is a 1 year old beautiful boy with a big smile and an even bigger heart that he wears on his sleeve proudly. Billie is a lover to the depths of his soul and is the epitome of pure - believing that every person he meets is a long-lost loved one he’s been yearning to meet. Billie is such an affectionate, playful, gentle and social soul with the perfect combination of enthusiasm for life and an impressive ability to just chill. Billie will never turn down a good cuddle and appreciates any time he is given affection while also being super independent - totally fine not being a velcro dog. Billie is also super self-aware, voluntarily going to his crate when he’s ready for some one-on-one time with his own thoughts. Billie also sleeps soundly in his crate whenever his humans are busy as well as through the night. Billie previously lived in a home with four other dogs, and it shows. Billie is extremely dog friendly and genuinely seems to enjoy having canine company. Billie has proven to be playful and affectionate with other pups, happy to share both his toys and his personal space while also being happy just coexisting around another pup when playtime is over. Billie is friendly and affectionate with everyone he meets, has lived with many kids and seems overall unaffected by their kidish ways. With Billie’s gentle yet playful personality, he will likely be a wonderful fit for a social family that is ready to bring a young dog into their home to share the rest of their lives with. On leash, Billie continues to make amazing progress with practicing his loose leash skills. The majority of the time Billie will be found calmly walking alongside his person and is completely unfazed by the chaos of NYC including large swarms of people, dogs, strollers, skateboards, roller skates and even helicopters. Billie is also a pro at riding the subway or hopping in the car if that’s your preferred means of transportation and will happily follow you anywhere dogs are allowed. He regularly joins his foster parents at restaurants, bars and cafés and loves being part of the conversation or just calmly splooting under the table for however long the outing takes. There is no doubt that Billie is also a very bright boy who genuinely enjoys learning. Billie picks up on new skills quickly and has already mastered his sit, down, paw, and touch commands. Billie has currently graduated to practicing his “go to your bed” and “stay” commands as well. In the home, Billie has excellent door manners, happy to sit patiently while waiting for the “ok” command before entering or exiting a doorway. Overall, Billie is highly food-motivated and engaged, making him an excellent training partner. If you’re looking for a dog to explore training classes, tricks, or even dog sports with, Billie would absolutely be game. Billie’s most entertaining quirk is that he believes any flat surface is an appropriate place for a sploot regardless if it be the city park, a building lobby or the floor of the local bodega. Billie is a respectful house guest who is able to adapt to the rhythms of unfamiliar home life quickly and with growing confidence. Billie makes it clear that he is in tune with his people and loves being included in his family’s day-to-day life while also understanding sometimes certain activities are for humans only. Billie has the enthusiasm and playfulness of a very young dog while also having the maturity to understand when playtime is over and when his humans have expectations of him. If you have been searching for a devoted, affectionate soul dog, missing out on Billie will be a huge mistake that you will likely regret forever. Still on the fence? How about you apply to meet this boy and find out how absolutely amazing he is for yourself?
