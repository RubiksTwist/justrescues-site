Description provided by Animal Lighthouse Rescue

Introducing Betsy! She is a 1-year-old, 18-pound Chihuahua mix who is ready to find her forever person and fill their life with love! The moment Betsy sees one of her favorite people, she melts into a puddle of happiness and rolls right over, proudly showing off her belly and asking for rubs. She is a true little love-bug who would happily spend the entire day curled up on your lap if you let her. With that in mind, Betsy would make the perfect work-from-home companion. She wants to be wherever you are, every minute of the day, and she’s more than happy to curl up beside you while you work. When the workday is over, she’ll gladly snuggle up with you in bed and remind you that the best part of the day is simply being together. But don’t let her cuddly side fool you—Betsy is also ready to take on the world! She loves playing with other dogs (when she gets the zoomies you will instantly smile), joining her favorite people for a relaxing trip to the coffee shop, and hanging out at the park while you work away on your computer. If you’re looking for a loyal little sidekick who will follow you everywhere, shower you with love, and happily accept belly rubs whenever possible, Betsy may be your girl! She was found wandering the streets in Puerto Rico and came up to a good samaritan asking for help. Since her rescue, she has been showered with love and has never missed a meal!

Betsy is spayed, microchipped, and up to date on her vaccinations. For more information about her, please fill out an application at www.ALRcares.com and email Lori@alrcares.com. We schedule meet and greets for approved adopters only, an application is the first step in our approval process. Thank you!
