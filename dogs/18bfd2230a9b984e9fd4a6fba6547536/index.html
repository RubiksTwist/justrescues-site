Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

A volunteer writes:
Coricopat is one of thirty cats brought to us as their apartment caught fire. No-one was hurt, thankfully! Coricopat deserves well his name picked from the famous musical CATS. After a period of initial shyness, Coricopat is now a much more animated character who loved to play, is social and a bit naughty too, snatching the treats I reserved for his friend Tantomile, a handsome but quite shy Ginger(see his video). I really enjoys my time with Coricopat, a cute petite oreo nugget, full of life, just a tinsy winsy timid at time(remember he went and is still going through difficult times) and I am so happy he is coming out of his shell in order to meet you, charm you and go home with you. Beside a little cold, Coricopat is apparently healthy and could go home today. So, ask about our friend Coricopat when you visit us at the Manhattan Care Center!
