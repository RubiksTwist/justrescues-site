Witty Aisy is at risk due to behavioral reasons. While in care, Witty has been observed to be fearful and dog reactive. Noted to have a tense body and tucked tail. In kennel Witty has been observed to growl and dodge the leash when handlers were attempting to leash her. While out of the kennel, Witty becomes frantic when seeing other dogs and will begin pulling towards them. When in a closed yard, Witty will begin to seek exit and pull back into the building. This behavior is not easily redirected. Before arriving at a Witty bit, a person who picked her up to place her into a car. This bite did break skin. During intake and her medical exam, Witty was noted to be social with handlers. Medically, Witty has CIRDC.

Witty Aisy arrived at MACC as a stray with a limited history. While being brought to the care center via the police, Witty bit one of the officers on the hand when they picked her up to place her into the car. This was a bite and release that broke skin. While in care, Witty has been observed to be fearful. Noted to have a tense body and tucked tail. In kennel she is observed to dodge the leash and growl when handlers approach to leash her. Witty has also been observed to be reactive to dogs. Witty will frantically pull towards other dogs while on leash. This behavior is not easily redirected. Due to the detailed behavior, we recommend an experienced adult only home.
