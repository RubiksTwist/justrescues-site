Meet Honey Daniels!
Honey Daniels was found as a stray, and this sweet girl is ready for her next chapter! She has a lovely way of leaning into people for affection, and her wagging tail makes it clear she's happy to make a new friend. Now she's hoping to find a family who will give her a cozy place to call home.

My Ideal Home
Honey would enjoy a home with a steady routine, daily walks, and opportunities to explore. Food puzzles, long lasting chews, and hide and seek games will give her fun ways to stay mentally engaged. She doesn't need a packed schedule, just a family ready to include her in life's everyday adventures.

Good with Other Pets?
Honey's history with other animals is unknown, and her recent dog assessment was inconclusive. Slow, thoughtful introductions will help her get to know any potential furry housemates comfortably.

Good with Kids?
Honey's history with children is unknown. Calm, respectful introductions will help her get comfortable with new family members.

Things to Know
Honey walks beautifully on a loose leash and readily follows her handler. She's not particularly interested in toys, but she does enjoy human company and gentle affection. We're still learning about her life before ACC, and a loving home will give her the chance to settle in and show us more of who she is. With a name like Honey Daniels, this sweet girl is ready to bring a little extra sweetness to someone's life!
