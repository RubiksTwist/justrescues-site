Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Sora is an 8-year-old corgi who weighs around 22 lbs, and this sweet lady still has plenty of spunk. She ended up in the shelter after her previous owner moved and couldn’t bring her along, and we knew that couldn’t be where her story ended.

She’s super sweet, house-trained, great on the leash, good with other dogs, and mostly indifferent to cats. Sora also has a very funny habit of making intense eye contact when she wants something. If you stop petting her, she’ll turn her head around and look right at you like she’s politely reminding you that you weren’t finished yet.

Sora is silly, affectionate, and ready for a home where she can enjoy all the attention and comfort she deserves.
