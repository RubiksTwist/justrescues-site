Meet Hercules!

Hercules came to us as an owner surrender after his family member became unable to care for him. This young, energetic pup may be small in stature, but he has a big personality and a lot of curiosity about the world around him. In the shelter, Hercules can feel overwhelmed at first, but once he settles in, he enjoys walks, treats, and spending time with people he trusts.

My Ideal Home

Hercules would thrive in a calm, adult-only home with an experienced adopter who understands fearful or sensitive dogs. He benefits from routine, patience, and positive reinforcement to help him build confidence and feel secure. A home that can provide daily mental and physical enrichment will help him channel his energy in a healthy way.

Good with Kids?

No. Due to his sensitivity and mouthiness with treats, Hercules is best suited for a home without children.

Good with Other Pets?

Hercules has lived with another dog in the past, but he can be reactive toward unfamiliar dogs, especially on leash. Slow, thoughtful introductions and guidance from an experienced handler will be important if there are other pets in the home.

Leash Manners

Hercules can walk nicely and enjoys sniffing along the way, though he may pull at times and react to dogs or busy surroundings. He does best with steady handling, distance from triggers, and reward-based training to help him stay focused and calm.

Behavior Determination

Level 3 - Hercules needs an experienced, adult-only home that can support him as he works through fear-based behaviors and builds confidence using force-free training.

Other Fun Facts

Hercules enjoys exploring on walks, accepts treats eagerly (sometimes a little too eagerly!), and has shown that with patience and consistency, he can relax and show his sweet side.
