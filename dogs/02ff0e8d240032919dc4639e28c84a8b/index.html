Description provided by AMA Animal Rescue

Meet Noodles, a lovely black-and-white cat with a classic tuxedo coat and bright, expressive eyes.

Noodles is an independent little lady who likes to do things her own way and at her own pace. She's perfectly happy entertaining herself, finding the coziest sunny spot for a nap, or keeping an eye on all the neighborhood happenings from her favorite window. Think of her as the perfect little roommate who loves sharing your home while still doing her own thing!

Noodles enjoys affection on her own terms, but when she's in the mood, a good head or neck scratch will quickly turn her into a little purring machine!

For someone who appreciates a calm, independent cat, Noodles is a wonderful presence to have around the house. Please fill out and submit an adoption application today.
