Darla arrived at MACC as a stray with no known history. Darla has been observed to be highly fearful while in care, noted to balk and thrash on leash when handlers attempt to return her to kennel. Due to the high level of fear, stress, and anxiety, we recommend an experienced adult only home.

I love to be loved, but on my own terms! Let's brush up on some canine body language together!
