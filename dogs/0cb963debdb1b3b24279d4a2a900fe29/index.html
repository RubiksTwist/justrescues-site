Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

My Boroughbreaker writes: We took Sheba, an adorable, sweet, and very social pup, to Central Park. She began the day as if she was seeing the world for the first time. She walked exuberantly to the park, anxious to see what was around every corner, darting back and forth, and taking in everything around her.
Sheba is a very active and happy dog and she showed this is multiple ways. Once we got to the park, she was interested in all the people, dogs, and wildlife around her. Of all the dogs we have taken on a BoroughBreak to the park, she was the only one who paid attention to the birds and pigeons. She especially enjoyed running into the flocks of pigeons around her in order to disperse them.
Sheba's cute little high-pitched squeal of a bark had everyone thinking she was a puppy and this garnered a lot of attention throughout the day.
After lunch and a game of fetch, Sheba settled down on the grass in the shade and chewed on a toy for a while, then promptly fell asleep under the tree. She would occasionally awaken, notice that she was a little too far away from us for her liking, and do the "army crawl" to get closer before falling asleep again.
Sheba is such an adorable and sweet girl and would make a wonderful family pet. She would benefit from leash training, learning some basic commands, and controlling her desire to jump up on those around her.
