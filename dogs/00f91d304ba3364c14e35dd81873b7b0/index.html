Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Tater Tot is a 1-year-old mixed breed who weighs around 25 lbs and has a lot of enthusiasm for pretty much everything happening around him. Playing? He’s in. Going somewhere? Also in. Doing something that has absolutely nothing to do with him? He’d still very much like to be involved.

He’s energetic, curious, super smart, and incredibly loving. Tater Tot has the sweetest face and is very happy to make friends with both people and other dogs. Under all that youthful enthusiasm is a really affectionate boy who just wants to love his people and be loved right back.

Tater Tot will do best with a patient adopter who’s excited to give him structure, teach him boundaries, and keep that busy brain working with training and plenty of stimulation. He’s eager to learn, eager to participate, and definitely not interested in sitting life out.
