Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Meet Hachi - he's the kind of dog who makes an impression the moment he walks into a room. This handsome Akita has a big presence, an even bigger personality, and enough energy to keep life interesting! Beneath that impressive Akita exterior is a genuinely sweet boy who has plenty of affection to give and a zest for life that's hard not to love.

Hachi isn't looking to sit on the sidelines and watch the world go by - he wants to experience it! Whether it's getting out for an adventure, exploring his surroundings, or spending time with his favorite people, he's happiest when there's something to do and someone to share it with.

Rescued from China, thanks to Hope4Fur with support of the Andrew Sabin Family Foundation, Hachi has made quite a journey, and we'd love for his next chapter to be the one where he finally gets to settle in and be someone's beloved companion.
