Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

If you walk down the street with Porsha, you mine as well be driving on the highway with the fancy car itself.

Beautiful Porsha is a 1 year old, tall and slim 50 lb girl who is the epitome of happy - the key words to describe her are soft, wiggly and absolutely full of affectionate.

In the home, Porsha loves to play and she's not picky about what play time looks like - she's happy to play a game of fetch or hide and seek, whatever you prefer! In the home, Porsha loves to meet strangers and will immediately treat them like her best friend.

Outside, Porsha is happy to greet everyone that passes her by on walks and is extremely confident in her native New York City. Porsha is a young girl, with appropriate 1 year old pup energy and when she gets excited - she's happy to redirect and fun bouncing around into treat training. Since Porsha is out of her very young puppy stage, she also never misses an opportunity to curl up on the couch with her cozy blanket or favorite toy when its time.

Additionally, Porsha however, is not only beauty but she is brains too. Porsha may be particular about her walking devices, much rather preferring collars over harnesses - a true fashoin queen who wants to show off her silky cow spots, how can you blame her? In regard to her big brain, Porsha has mastered her sit command and is now practicing the skill at each crosswalk while waiting to cross the street. She is also working on relearning positive association with the crate after spending so much time living in one in the city shelter - listening to jazz music has really supported Porsha on her crate training progress. Porsha is fully housebroken - a true demure queen.

Overall, Porsha is equal parts silly, affectionate, curious, athletic and more than anything - she is so loving. Porsha's modelesque frame is packed with personality and would thrive in a home that is excited to have a a young pup to grow with and build an unbreakable bond by embracing her goofy side as she matures into the wonderful adult dog she is destined to be.
