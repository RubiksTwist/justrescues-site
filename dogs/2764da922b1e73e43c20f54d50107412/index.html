Meet Sandy!

Sandy arrived at ACC as a stray and is a very social and affectionate dog. She can be anxious in new situations but is always friendly.

My Ideal Home

Sandy is a very high-energy dog who needs daily mental and physical activity to keep her engaged and exercised. She would thrive with an adopter committed to force-free, reward-based training techniques. Long-lasting chews, food puzzles, and hide-and-seek games are great ways to positively direct her enthusiasm. She has experience in a crate and on car rides.

Good with Other Pets?

Sandy does best as the only resident dog in the home and would not be a good candidate for dog parks. She can get worked up around other dogs and benefits from continued training in controlled settings to help her associate other dogs with positive experiences.

Good with Kids?

Sandy is recommended for an adult-only home.

Things to Know

Sandy is still learning gentle play manners and can jump up often. She tends to pull hard on the leash and can get excited around people and animals she sees on walks. She is generally healthy and ready for her new home.
