Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

My foster writes: Miss Pepper is coming out of her shell (or the bathroom sink)! Still being loyal to her favourite spot, Pepper is coming out to explore and investigate more each and every day. Being bribed with a lot of churuus also helps. She is very sweet just a little nervous. She is starting to show interest in toys! Can't wait to see her blossom!

A volunteer writes:
Pepper is a pretty lady known to be a shy and fearful cat in our shelter environment but also in a home setting as described by her initial and second owners. She is very sensitive in general and likes to just observe what is going out around her. If interaction needs to happen, Pepper favors a slow and gentle approach, a low voice and the use of treats like churrus she with lick eagerly. She will then lean into pets and accepts caresses as long as no sudden noises or moves disturb the peace of the encounter. Pepper would do better in a home environment, one that is quiet, without boisterous children and with a very patient, experienced and understanding new owner. No doubt, there is such a wonderful adopter out there who can take on the amazing task to bring a shy cat out of her shell!
