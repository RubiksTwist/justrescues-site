Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Sweet Dominic has never met a moment he couldn't make a little more interesting. This boy has his own agenda, his own ideas, and absolutely no intention of being a background character in anyone's life.

He's the kind of kitten who seems to be investigating something at all times. One minute he's busy inspecting a corner of the room like he's uncovered a major conspiracy, the next he's off chasing an invisible enemy or getting himself into some harmless mischief. Dominic brings a certain kind of entertainment that you really can't plan for - he's leaving life on the streets to bring countless fun and laughter to his new home, and our Operation Cat program has given him that chance.

There's something wonderfully funny about a kitten who takes his little adventures so seriously. Dominic has places to be, things to investigate, and toys that need thoroughly defeating. He isn't just looking for a home - he's looking for his next big adventure, preferably with someone who appreciates a little chaos and a lot of charm.
