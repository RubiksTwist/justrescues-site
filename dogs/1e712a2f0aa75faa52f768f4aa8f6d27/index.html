Description provided by Bideawee

Meet Lola! This sweet, quirky girl is a little different from your average cat, and that's part of what makes her so lovable! She's a big fan of cuddles, being petted, and lounging on the couch with her favorite people. She's also quite the foodie and is always happy when treats are involved.

Lola has an independent streak and can sometimes be a little skittish, so she'll appreciate someone who understands her quirks and lets her settle in at her own pace. Once she's comfortable, she's an affectionate companion who enjoys spending time with her people. If you're looking for a sweet cat with a personality all her own, Lola might be your girl!

If this cat is located at our Manhattan Adoption Center, head in to meet them and see if you are a match! We are a same-day adoption center, and an application does not put you on a list or hold an animal for you. If they are in Wantagh or in a foster home, please submit an online application and our team will reach out to further discuss the adoption process. Prior to adoption, all of our animals are spayed/neutered, microchipped, and given their age-appropriate vaccines.
