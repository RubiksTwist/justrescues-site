Description provided by PupStarz Rescue

Meet Espresso Machine, a stunning, plush-coated 1-year old, 41-pound Labrador/Shepherd mix who is a true nature enthusiast and loves going for long walks. This sweet girl has a thing for putting her little toes on the grass and dirt...not necessarily to do her business...but just to touch the soft earth.

Quiet and well-mannered, Espresso Machine gets along well with humans young and old, as well as other dogs. She is slightly reserved, but always warms up quickly, and is even tolerant of strangers petting her on the head or scratching her ears. Espresso Machine never begs for food, she doesn’t jump on beds, she is not at all destructive in the home, and she’s non-reactive toward other dogs, even if they’re a bit aggressive with her. She will simply shy away from them. Espresso Machine is a no-drama kind of gal!

Espresso Machine has a healthy appetite and gobbles up great with gusto. She enjoys toys, being pet, and sitting next to you on the couch, oftentimes with either her head or paw resting on your lap. Espresso Machine energy level is anything you want it to be. If you’re looking for someone to hike with, Espresso Machine is happy to oblige. If you wanna hang out and watch TV, she’s equally content to chill at home with you. She’s also very comfortable being left alone. While Espresso Machine would naturally prefer having someone around, she is neither clingy nor demanding. She is just happy to be at peace, knowing that she’s safe and knowing that she’s loved.

Espresso Machine would make an ideal pet for anyone seeking the companionship of a young, easy-going, kind-hearted dog. She’s not just fluffy and pretty on the outside...she’s also got an absolutely beautiful soul.
