Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Pecan is a 9-month-old Terrier mix who weighs around 37 pounds, and she's a wonderfully sweet and affectionate young pup. She loves being close to her people and is always happy to soak up some attention and cuddles.

True to her age and breed, Pecan has a good amount of energy and enjoys getting out, exploring, and staying active. But she also knows how to match the mood at home and is perfectly capable of settling in for some quiet time and cuddles when everyone else is relaxing. She's house-trained and already has a great foundation for life with a family.

Pecan is still learning the ins and outs of walking nicely on leash in the busy city, where there are understandably a lot of interesting things competing for her attention. With continued practice and guidance, she'll keep getting the hang of it. Sweet, fun, and full of affection, Pecan is ready for a family who will enjoy helping her continue to learn and grow.

Registered adopters interested in Pecan can email adopt@hbrdogs.com for more info. If you’re not registered yet, you can do it at hbrdogs.com.
