Balsam is at risk due to his behavioral concerns. NYPD reported that a members of the public found Balsam and guided him into a handball court, but Balsam lunged at one of them, resulting in the dog tearing off a piece of clothing. Balsam was sedated from ESU to avoid further escalation. In the care center, Balsam displays flinching during leashing and backing away when returning to his kennel. He has required two handlers to assist to return to his kennel. On walks, he has pulled toward passing people and a small dog, has displayed whining when observing dogs, as well as barking and lunging at a dog inside the shelter. He can be difficult to redirect with treats or squeakers at times. He is noted to have allowed petting from handlers. Medically, Balsam is currently overweight, would benefit from dental cleaning in the future, but otherwise healthy.

Balsam came into the care center as a stray. Due to this, his behavioral history in a home environment is unknown. Officers were called to pick up Balsam, who was sedated and brought to the care center.In the care center he has shown hesitation with leash handling, flinching when the leash makes contact with his body. He can be hesitant to enter and exit areas of the shelter and has required assistance from a second handler, including use of a sling or belly band, to return to his kennel. He is not consistently responsive to treats or squeakers. Balsam is available for rescue through one of our new hope partners.
