Description provided by ASPCA Adoption Center

Gladstone is a handsome gray and white pup with an adventurous spirit and a playful heart! Whether he's exploring the outdoors or enjoying a game with his favorite people, Gladstone brings enthusiasm and joy wherever he goes!

One of Gladstone's favorite places to be is outside, taking in everything the world has to offer. He loves leisurely walks where he can stop to soak in the fresh air, sniff the flowers, and appreciate every interesting scent along the way. His excitement for adventures is contagious, making every outing a fun experience.

When it's time to play, Gladstone is all in! He loves a good game of tug and is a big fan of two-toy fetch with tennis balls and rope toys. He especially enjoys trading his toys for tasty pieces of chicken, making playtime both interactive and rewarding.

With his playful personality, love of the outdoors, and affectionate nature, Gladstone is ready to find a loving family who will enjoy exploring and making memories together. If you're looking for a fun-loving best friend with a zest for life, Gladstone may be the perfect addition to your home!
