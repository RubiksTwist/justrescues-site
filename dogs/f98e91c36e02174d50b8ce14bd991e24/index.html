Description provided by Waldo's Rescue Pen

Alias (“Ally”) is the sweetest, cuddliest little girl and such an easy foster to have around. She loves affection, morning kisses, and playing with her foster brother, but she’s not overly high-energy and is perfectly happy relaxing at home.

Ally is doing really well with potty training and is great about going outside, though she still needs regular potty breaks every 1–2 hours while she’s learning. She can currently stay home alone for at least an hour, and we’re gradually building up her independence so she grows into a confident, well-adjusted pup.

On walks, she takes care of business quickly and is often ready to head back home afterward! She can be a little timid when first meeting new people or dogs, but she’s learning to warm up and gain confidence.

Overall, Ally is an incredibly loving, gentle, and easygoing girl who would make a wonderful addition to a family looking for a sweet companion.
