Meet Stormili!
Stormili is a social, playful girl who is ready to bring some fun into her future family's life! Her former owner describes her as friendly and affectionate, and she's shown us that she enjoys meeting other dogs and inviting them to play. She's an enthusiastic gal who loves to get out and explore, and she's looking for a family who will make room for her big personality and all the adventures ahead.

My Ideal Home
Stormili would love an active home where she can enjoy daily walks, playtime, and plenty of activities with her people. Food puzzles, long lasting chews, and fun training games will give her great ways to put her energy to good use. She's a bright, enthusiastic girl who will benefit from positive reinforcement training and a family who enjoys helping her learn new things.

Good with Other Pets?
Stormili's former owner describes her as friendly and playful with other dogs, and she has shown plenty of interest in making canine friends at ACC. She enjoys inviting other dogs to play, though she can let them know when their play gets too intense. She also tends to pull toward dogs on walks, so a front clip harness and positive reinforcement training can help her keep her attention on her person. Thoughtful introductions will help her find the right playmate.

Good with Kids?
Stormili's history with children is unknown. Calm, respectful introductions will help her get comfortable with new family members as she settles into her next home.

Things to Know
Stormili accepts handling and has a comfortable, relaxed side, even with all her enthusiasm for exploring. She can get distracted by other dogs and pull toward them on walks, so she'll benefit from some guidance and rewards for checking in with her handler. With exercise, enrichment, and consistency, Stormili will have plenty of opportunities to show off her playful personality. She's ready to bring a little extra sunshine to someone's everyday life!
