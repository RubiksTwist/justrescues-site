Meet Thomas!

Thomas is a handsome senior Persian with the sweetest face, and so much love left to give. Life hasn’t always been kind to this gentle boy, but he’s still here, ready to find someone who will make his golden years feel safe, cozy, and full of love. Now, all Thomas needs is a special person to show him what it means to truly be home.

Thomas has been spending time in foster care, where he’s had the chance to enjoy the comforts of home and socialize with other cats. He can get along with feline friends, making him a wonderful addition to the right family. Thomas is currently doing well and enjoying his days like any other happy cat, with just a daily liver supplement to help support him. He has been diagnosed with liver cancer, and while there is no cure, he deserves to enjoy every day surrounded by comfort, kindness, and love. We’re hoping to find a compassionate family who will open their heart to this special senior and cherish the time they have together. Thomas may be a senior, but he still deserves a forever home of his own, a soft place to land, a loving family, and someone to remind him every day how much he matters.
