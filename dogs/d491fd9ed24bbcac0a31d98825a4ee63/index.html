Meet Juan Pawblo!

Juan Pawblo came to ACC with five other dogs after their owner sadly passed away. Despite such a big change, this young guy has shown himself to be a social, sweet pup who enjoys being around people and is always ready to come over and say hello. He's affectionate, curious, and already showing us what a lovable companion he can be!

My Ideal Home

Juan Pawblo is a young and enthusiastic dog who will thrive with a family ready to keep his mind and body busy. Daily walks, playtime, food puzzles, long lasting chews, and fun games like hide and seek will help him stay happy and engaged. With plenty of positive outlets for his energy, Juan Pawblo will be ready to be your new best pal!

Good with Other Pets?

Juan Pawblo came into care with several other dogs, although we have limited history about his life before arriving at ACC. He has recently shown friendly, loose body language and interest when meeting new dogs, including mutual sniffing and playful interactions. He can also be polite when another dog communicates that they need space, so introductions with any future canine siblings should be slow and supervised.

Good with Kids?

Juan Pawblo's history with children is unknown, so any introductions should be thoughtful and supervised. He can be enthusiastic when greeting people and may jump up to say hello, so he'll do best with family members who can help him practice polite greetings using positive reinforcement.

Things to Know

Juan Pawblo walks beautifully on leash with no pulling or reactivity, and he happily approaches people for attention. He enjoys being petted, stays engaged with his handler, and has a curious side that comes out when exploring his surroundings. His history in a home is limited, but this sweet boy has plenty of potential to blossom with a family ready to show him all the fun that life has to offer!
