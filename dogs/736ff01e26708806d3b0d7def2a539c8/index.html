Meet Caramel! A Little Kitten With a BIG Personality! 🍬🐾

At just 3 months old and 3 pounds (as of 11/1), Caramel may be tiny, but this little girl is a whole lot of personality wrapped up in one adorable, multi-colored tabby package!

Caramel is the perfect combination of playful kitten and professional cuddle bug. One minute she's zooming around chasing balls and batting at stuffed toys, and the next she's melting into your arms, soaking up kisses, and purring up a storm. And don't forget the air biscuits! This girl makes sure you know just how happy she is to be loved. 🥹💕

Caramel loves being held, cuddled, kissed, and showered with affection. She's comfortable with handling and absolutely adores being pet. Her purr motor seems to turn on the second you give her some attention!

When she's not busy snuggling, she's off exploring, playing with her toys, or keeping herself entertained with all the fascinating things a curious kitten can discover. She's also litter-box trained and gets along wonderfully with her siblings.

Caramel is social and would love a home with people who have plenty of affection to give. She would also enjoy having another friendly feline around to play with. She's been around children, adults, and seniors, making her a wonderful little addition to a variety of homes!

🍬 If you're looking for a kitten who will keep you laughing, fill your home with purrs, and happily turn your arms into her favorite nap spot, Caramel is your girl!

If you would like to give this wonderful cat a home, please visit www.pupstarzrescue.org for an application!
