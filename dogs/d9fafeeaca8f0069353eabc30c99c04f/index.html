Looking for a little bundle of sweetness to brighten your life? Meet Glitter, an adorable 3-month-old female kitten looking for her forever home!

Glitter is a precious little girl with so much love and potential to share. Like her brother Sparkles, she may be a little shy at first and need some time to warm up. With patience, a gentle touch, and a loving home, she'll have the chance to blossom into the happy, confident kitty she's meant to be.

At just 3 months old, Glitter has her whole life ahead of her! She's looking for a special family who will give her the time, attention, and affection she deserves. Every shy little kitten deserves a chance to feel safe, loved, and cherished.
