Moana was surrendered by her previous owner because her previous family was evicted from their home. Her previous family described her as a gentle, social, affectionate, very active, and playful dog who can be very friendly around new people or situations. They reported that she will initially hide at first and may actively avoid strangers or retreat, and needs time before she warms up and becomes social. She was noted to spend most of her time in the living room when she is not napping in her crate. Moana enjoys playing with rope toys, a game of tug-of-war, and playing with balls. Her favorite treats are cucumbers, Tomatoes, lettuce, and any other form of dog treats. During her stay in care, Moana has been highly fearful and avoidant of handlers and interaction, remaining tense-bodied while trembling. Due to these concerns, we recommend for wanted to be placed in an adult-only home through a New Hope Rescue partner where she can decompress and acclimate at her own pace.
