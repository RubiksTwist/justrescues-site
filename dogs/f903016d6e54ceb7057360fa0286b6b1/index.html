My foster writes:
Hazel Bunny is the perfect combination of Sweet and Sassy! At times, she's a huge cuddle bug who only wants to snuggle in a lap, but at other times, she loves playing fetch or chasing my other dogs around the house! She absolutely loves walks around the neighborhood and shows zero reactivity to other does, regardless of size. She is totally unfazed by city sounds and doesn't let anything get in the way of her sniffaris!! When passing other dogs, she will pull to greet them. Sometimes, she will get jumpy (which might be annoying to other dogs), so we are working on setting some boundaries. Hazel loves to eat and really enjoys treats. Inside, she hasn't shown any signs of being destructive and her accidents are becoming a thing of the past! She was probably housebroken before entering the shelter, but like many shelter dogs, she probably regressed a bit. If I didn't have three dogs, I wouldn't hesitate to adopt her! She's a beautiful, lovely dog who will fill your home with so much joy.
