Description provided by PupStarz Rescue

Meet Electric Toothbrush, a 1 year old, 32- pound Husky/Shepherd mix, who is alert, adorable, and possesses the perkiest ears this side of the Mississippi. It kinda looks like she can fly! Who knows…maybe she can! ;-)

Friendly beyond belief, Electric Toothbrush knows no stranger. She greets anyone and everyone, and covers them in kisses. She’s the same way with other dogs. She wants to play with them all. Gentle, loving, and extremely sweet, it’s impossible to ignore this girl’s charisma. Electric Toothbrush is also very smart. She learned how to use pee pads almost immediately, she’s starting to do her bathroom business outside, and she’s well on her way to being fully housebroken.

Electric Toothbrush can use some practice when it comes to her leash manners, but she’s making steady progress every day. she is responsive to correction and wants to earn your praise. And treats? Oh boy, food always gets her attention. She already sits on command!

For an active person, Electric Toothbrush would be a dream come true. This high energy gal can hike for miles without tiring, can trot alongside you while you jog, and can walk seemingly forever. She loves her toys, but does not engage in any resource guarding. She does beautifully when left alone, and you come back home, she is thrilled, but does not jump all over you.

As active as she is, Electric Toothbrush is an excellent sleeper as long as she’s had an adequate amount of exercise. She’s also very mushy, cuddly, and affectionate. She makes the absolute warmest, fuzziest, best snuggle-buddy! If you’ve got the time to walk her, run her around, and continue to train her, she will be the happiest, healthiest, best furry companion you can ever imagine having.
