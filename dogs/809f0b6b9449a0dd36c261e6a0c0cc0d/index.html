Meet the lovely. laughing Mango Lassi!

Mango Lassi is a young boy with the most pure, youthful puppy joy and exuberance! Mango Lassi has never met a human, animal or activity he absolutely did not love and is truly happiness embedded.

Mango Lassi is a one year old dog who is young enough dog to mold into your perfect companion while being old enough to have some foundational skills such as being housebroken and crate trained! Some of Mango Lassi's foundational skills also include the mastery of his basic obedience commands such as sit, come, lay down, shake and paw. Mango Lassi is currently reinforcing his leave it and stay commands along with his excitable leash manners and greetings. Mango Lassi is a rambunctious friend to all dogs, small and large alike and would be absolute perfection once he masters the approrpiate ways to show his joy and excitement to other humans and dogs!

Mango Lassi's most prized possession tie at both his people and his fur friends. Mango Lassi loves to play, walk, cuddle, swim and beg for pets and belly rubs. Mango is an affection boy whose both super sweet and entertainingly outgoing. Mango Lassi also enjoys the company of respectful and mature kiddos only showing fear when they approach chaotically.

Mango Lassi would be an excellent companion for anyone who wants an enthusiastic dog whose entire world will revolve around thier humans. Mango doesn't mind a calm home environment and will love for there to be lots of toys and enrichment for independent play and if there is a fur friend - one that can tolerate his little brother nagging behaviors and teach him thats its okay to just chill out sometimes!

Overall, Mango Lassi is a wonderful teenage houseguest who can use a reminder or two to remember his daily manners before quickly getting back to his good boy safe. If you are looking for a soul son who is very much worth some time and effort - apply to meet Mango Lassi today and see for yourself how much happiness he can bring to your life.
