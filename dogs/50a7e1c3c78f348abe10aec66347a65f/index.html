Meet Daisy!

Spunky, sweet, and a little leader, Daisy has a playful spirit and the softest side. This six-month-old Chiweenie weighs approximately 12 pounds, but her personality and capacity for love are much bigger.

Once Daisy warms up to you, she is a very social little dog who enjoys your company. She loves to be held and loved on. Beneath that confident little exterior is a complete mush who is happiest sharing affection with her people. She enjoys chewing on toys, playing, and then settling in close for a cozy snuggle. When it is time to sleep, she loves curling up right beside you.

Daisy is a clever little girl with a wise way about her. Her foster mom finds her very trainable, and she is responding well to pee-pad training as she continues to learn. Daisy also adores her sister, Moo Moo. Their foster mom describes them as a wonderful pair, and their special connection is a lovely part of Daisy’s story.

Daisy is still building confidence with stairs and the outdoors. She is hesitant to go both up and down stairs, and her foster mom is patiently helping her practice. Outside, she becomes frightened and wants to return indoors quickly; outdoor potty training is still a work in progress. She will benefit from a patient family who can help her feel secure and learn at her own pace.

Once she is indoors and comfortable, her personality comes to life: she wants to snuggle, play with her sister, and enjoy being close to her people. Her foster mom calls her the sweetest and kindest little girl—a puppy who brings both fun and tenderness to everyday life.

Daisy has been spayed and microchipped and is looking for a family ready to offer love, patience, and gentle guidance as she grows. If you are hoping for a playful little companion with a cuddly heart, Daisy may be your girl

She is currently being fostered in Stamford, Connecticut. Her adoption fee is $575.

To adopt this animal, please visit https://www.louieslegacy.org/adopt

All of our animals come spayed or neutered, up to date on age appropriate vaccinations, are microchipped, heartworm negative, current on flea/tick and heartworm prevention, dewormed, and treated for any ailments found upon veterinary examination. The adoption fee covers a portion of these services, and makes it possible for us to continue rescuing animals.

For more information on the adoption fees and the adoption process, please complete our adoption application here: https://www.louieslegacy.org/adopt - an application allows us to get to know your family better so we can assess whether the animal you are interested in is a good match for your home. An application does not obligate you to adopt. Please understand that due to the volume of applications some animals receive, we are unable to contact all applicants. If your application is considered a good fit for the animal you are interested in, or even a different pet, a volunteer will give you a call as soon as possible.

We are a foster based rescue and do not have a shelter location where animals can be seen during the week. Please see our Facebook page at https://www.facebook.com/LouiesLegacy for our weekly adoption album if you are interested in meeting an animal in person. Not all animals attend every adoption event, please check our Facebook page for animal event times and locations before you make a trip to meet a specific animal.
