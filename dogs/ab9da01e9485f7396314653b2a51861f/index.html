Shelby is a super goofy, silly girl who is ready to find her forever home and a family to call her own! She’s got personality for days and is guaranteed to keep you laughing with her lovable antics.

Now, let’s get one thing straight: Shelby is a PRINCESS. And princesses don’t share the spotlight. Shelby is looking to be the one and only pet in her castle, where she can soak up all the love, attention, and adoration she deserves. No siblings stealing her thunder, please!

Shelby is also looking for an adults-only home where she can settle in and enjoy her royal lifestyle without the chaos of little humans. She knows what she likes, and she’s ready for a home that’s the right fit for her fabulous self.

If you’re looking for a fun-loving, hilarious companion who will keep life entertaining and happily rule your household, Shelby might just be your girl. She’s ready to trade shelter life for the royal treatment she’s been waiting for!
