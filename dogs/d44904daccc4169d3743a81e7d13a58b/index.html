Meet Wesley

Wesley is a beautiful boy who has already been through a nightmare, but his story does not have to end with what happened to him.

Wesley was found with Winston and Willow after the three dogs were abandoned together in a carrier outside during a Nor’easter in New York. While the storm brought brutal conditions, these three innocent dogs were left outside with nowhere safe to escape and no one there to care for them.

When they were rescued, they were in heartbreaking condition. Their hair was severely matted, they were suffering from skin infections, and they had maggots. They had clearly been neglected, and it is difficult to imagine the pain and fear they experienced before someone finally came to their aid.

Wesley survived.

And now, he deserves so much more than survival. He deserves a life where he never has to wonder if someone will come for him. A life with a warm bed, a safe home, loving people, and the comfort of knowing he is finally cared for. We cannot erase the night Wesley spent outside in that storm, but we can give him a future that looks nothing like his past.

Please consider opening your heart and home to this special boy. Wesley has already weathered one terrible storm. Now he deserves to spend the rest of his life surrounded by love.
