Description provided by Best Friends Animal Society — NYC

Meet Brock! He is just over one year old and weighs in at about 65 pounds. He is a gorgeous black and brown Shepard mix who is eager to please. He can take a while to warm up to new friends but once he does, you’ll be best friends! Brock shows his love by being playful with big goofy movements, and an intense love for tug or war. Brock has shown that he can do well playing with other canine friends and says he’s best suited for a furry friend who can match his rough and tumble play style. While Brock hasn’t fully mastered being house trained yet, he’s eager to please and receptive to training! Brock can sit, give paw and lay down on command. We are sure that once Brock enters a more consistent environment he would love to learn lots of new life skills! Brock would thrive in a home that is a bit more active than regular as he loves his exercise and sniff time outside! Some of Brock's other skills are walking on leash and checking in with his handler. If you would like to meet Brock please stop by our Soho location at 307 West Broadway and hopefully become his new loving home! He is neutered, vaccinated, microchipped, and has had preventative treatments to ensure he is ready for his next steps. If you’re open to a little patience and a lot of possibility, come meet Brock! You might be the person who helps them feel safe, confident, and right at home.
