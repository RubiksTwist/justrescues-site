Hiiii! I’m Honey Boo Boo! 🐾💗

Well, hello there, friends! It’s me, Honey Boo Boo! I’m so happy you’re here because I have LOTS to tell you about myself!

First things first, I’m a sweet little girl with a BIG personality. Now, I should probably mention that the word little is used very loosely around here. I’m a whole 66 pounds of chunky, chubby, muscular goodness, with short little legs and a body built like a tank. But don’t let that fool you, I’m just a big ol’ cuddlebug!

I LOVE my people and I love attention. If I want something, I’ll make sure you know about it! Sometimes I’ll give you a little bark, sometimes I’ll stomp my feet. I have to make sure my requests aren’t getting lost around here. A girl has needs, you know!

When I’m not busy getting everyone’s attention, I enjoy the finer things in life: sunbathing, cozy naps, cuddles, and finding the comfiest spots in the house. I’m 100% potty trained, and I’m a pretty good girl when left home alone. I usually just curl up and take a nice little nap until everyone gets back. I also do well in my crate, although if I know you’re home, I might let out a few cries because I’d much rather be where you are!

Now, let’s talk about FOOD! I enjoy my meals, although I can be a teensy bit picky about my kibble. A little topper makes everything better! I also LOVE my lick mats with Ollie, bully sticks, cow knee caps, and all the yummy treats. And toys? OH MY GOODNESS. I LOVE THEM! Just don’t get too attached because I have a little talent for taking them apart. My forever family is going to need a toy budget if I move in with you!

I also have a playful side and get little bursts of energy. And when I say zoomies, I mean ZOOMIES! Remember, I’m 66 pounds, so when these little legs get moving, everybody better watch out! 😂

Okay, okay… I do have one thing I’m still working on. My leash manners! Sometimes I get SO excited or overstimulated on walks that I start biting my leash and pulling. Once I get going, I can be pretty persistent! My foster family is helping me learn to redirect that excitement and keep my focus. I’ll need a patient family who is willing to keep practicing with me. I’m still learning, and I know I can get better with some time and consistency!

I haven’t had any direct introductions to other dogs yet, so we’re still figuring out how I might do with them. An adult-only home would be best for me right now, since I’m a big, energetic girl and still learning to manage my excitement around fast, unpredictable movements.

But here’s the thing… I’m actually a really good girl! I’m sweet, affectionate, happy to be home, and I have so much love to give. I just need someone who will love my silly little personality, be patient with the things I’m still learning, and give me a place to call my own.

So, what do you say? Do you have room for a chunky little cuddlebug who loves snacks, destroys toys, and occasionally forgets how to behave on a leash?

I promise there will never be a dull moment with me!

Lots of love and little stomps,
Honey Boo Boo 💗🐾
