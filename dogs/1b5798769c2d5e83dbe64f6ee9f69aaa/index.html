Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Ramen is a beautiful 5-year-old husky who weighs around 34 lbs, and those eyes are very hard to resist. Her life hasn’t always been easy, but she’s still incredibly loving and sweet with the people around her. One of her favorite moves is looking up at you with those gorgeous eyes, then rolling right onto her back to ask for a belly rub.

She can be a little shy at first and is still getting used to the city, but she’s making progress as she settles in. Ramen is also doing great with house-training and is showing more confidence along the way. She’ll do best with someone patient who can give her the time she needs to keep adjusting at her own pace.

Registered adopters interested in Ramen can email adopt@hbrdogs.com for more info. If you're not registered yet, do it today at hbrdogs.com!
