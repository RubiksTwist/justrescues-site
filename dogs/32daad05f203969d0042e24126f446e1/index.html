Meet Bronco!

Bronco was found tied up in a park and is now safe in our care, slowly showing that behind his soft, thoughtful demeanor is a social and playful boy who just needed a chance to settle in. Once he's comfortable, his personality starts to shine through in gentle, engaging ways.

My Ideal Home

Bronco would do best with a patient adopter who can help him build confidence and feel secure in new environments. He benefits from gentle structure, predictable routines, and positive reinforcement training. Daily enrichment like walks, sniffy adventures, and food puzzles will help him stay engaged and relaxed.

Good with Other Pets?

Bronco has shown social, playful behavior with another dog in care, engaging in loose, bouncy play. As with all strays, slow and supervised introductions are recommended if he is to live with another pet.

Good with Kids?

Due to his sensitive nature and need for a calm environment, Bronco would do best in a home without very young children at this time.

Things to Know

Bronco is a social but initially cautious boy who takes a little time to warm up in new situations. He may need a few minutes to feel comfortable with handling or collaring, but once settled, he becomes more engaged and receptive. He enjoys interacting with people at his own pace and will thrive with continued gentle encouragement and confidence building. With patience and consistency, he has great potential to become a loyal and affectionate companion.

Fun Fact

Bronco's idea of a good day is simple: a little patience, a little play, and someone who lets him set the pace.
