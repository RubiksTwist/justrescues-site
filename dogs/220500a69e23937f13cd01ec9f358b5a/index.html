Description provided by Pawsome Squad

On a very frigid night in February, Torpedo was found with his leash tied up to a door knob of a Brownstone on Tompkins Avenue across from Herbert Bon King Park in Bed-Stuy.

Who could do such a cruel thing to this innocent dog?

He was scared but slowly warmed up to us. Once he became comfortable, he went through training and shown us was a smart boy he is!

He’s still learning but has been great with leash walking, crates training, good with dogs he's familiar with, and understands basic commands. He is a very affectionate and seeks pets.

Torpedo is looking for an owner that doesn’t mind him being by his side and going on adventures together.
