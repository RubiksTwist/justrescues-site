Let us introduce you to Sam. He is a 2 1/2 three year-old American short hair orange cat. Sam is fun, energetic lovable. It takes time for him to get used to you but when he does, you're his person, he loves sitting at windows or the front glass door. He waits for the occasional squirrel or rabbit to pass by and he has a very low crying voice to alert you to that happening. He has a loud cry when he wants your attention. He loves his little soft toys being thrown towards him. He'll catch a few and return it to you in an hour later he likes to sleep under the comforter on the bed all afternoon you will know he's there by the little bump on the bed, he is a grazor for his dry food. Wet food is put out two times daily. He usually eats most of it. He will occasionally get the zoomies and somehow wind up on top of the fridge. It will take time for him to sleep with you, but he will eventually get there. Also walk across you while you are reading Sam is a good boy. You will love him. PS he's very talkative and will answer you if you ask him a question. he will nod his head to tell you he wants you to come with him.
