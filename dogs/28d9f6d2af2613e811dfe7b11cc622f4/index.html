Description provided by Rescue City

Don't let his tiny size fool you, Bing Bong has enough charm packed into his 7.5 pound frame to light up an entire room. This adorable 6 year old Terrier mix is settling into foster care and proving that middle aged dogs really are the best kept secret in rescue. Past the puppy antics but still full of life, Bing Bong is the perfect companion! So far, he's shown himself to be a big fan of people and enjoys making canine friends too. As he continues to decompress, we're getting to watch his personality blossom more each day in his foster home after arriving from the shelter. Bing Bong does have chronic dry eye that requires lifelong medication and daily eye care. But this little guy takes it all in stride and is ready to trade medical appointments for belly rubs, cozy beds, and a family to call his own.
