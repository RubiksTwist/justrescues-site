Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

This beautiful Siamese girl came to ARF after being found as a stray raising her kittens. And now that her kittens have all found loving homes, it's her turn to find her forever home where she'll be cherished and adored.

While she is timid and shy when you first meet her, give her some time to come out of her shell and she is sure to win you over with her charm. She would do best in a calm and quiet household, where she can blossom and flourish at her own pace.

After spending so much of her life caring for others, she's ready to trade in motherhood for cozy naps and a sunny windowsill - ready to be pampered and spoiled.
