Description provided by ASPCA Adoption Center

Wednesday is proof that the middle of the week can be the happiest part of it!

Meet Wednesday, a radiant, tan-coated sweetheart whose joyful spirit lights up every room she enters. Her happy, wiggly dance greets everyone like an old friend, and her warm, affectionate nature makes her instantly easy to love.

Full of enthusiasm, Wednesday thrives on playtime and is always thrilled to make new four-legged friends. She is also eager to learn and quick to show off her best manners, especially when a tasty treat is on the line. Whether she is bounding through a game or settling in for a cuddle, Wednesday brings out the best in everyone around her.

Wednesday is ready to share her boundless love, loyalty and bright personality with a family who wants a little extra joy into their lives. Click the “Adopt Me” button to learn more!

*You can meet animals located at the ASPCA Adoption Center during our walk-in hours (daily, noon to 6 p.m.). We encourage a $150 suggested adoption fee when adopting an animal. This adoption fee allows the ASPCA to continue our lifesaving work and help even more animals in need. We are grateful for any amount given at the time of adoption.*
