Meet Bernie!
Bernie is a curious, energetic boy who loves taking in the world around him! He was found as a stray, so we're still getting to know him, but he's already shown us that he's happiest when he has something to explore. He's an adventurous guy with plenty of personality and is ready to find someone who will enjoy discovering all his favorite things together.

My Ideal Home
Bernie would love an active home where he can enjoy daily walks, fun adventures, and plenty of things to keep his clever mind busy. Food puzzles, long-lasting chews, and playful enrichment will give him great outlets for his enthusiasm. He'll do best with someone who enjoys an energetic companion and can help him practice his manners along the way.

Good with Other Pets?
Since Bernie came to us as a stray, we don't know his history with other dogs. He can get very excited when he sees them and has a hard time settling down, so for now we'd love for Bernie to be his family's one and only pet. He's got plenty of personality to fill the whole house!

Good with Kids?
Bernie can get pretty excited in the busy care center, so we recommend a home without young children. A family who understands energetic dogs and can give Bernie time to settle into a routine would be a wonderful fit.

Things to Know
Bernie is an enthusiastic explorer who can get distracted by all the exciting sights and smells around him. He walks with some pulling and may jump up when he's excited, but he's also quick to approach his people with a loose, friendly body once he feels comfortable. With plenty of exercise, enrichment, and positive-reinforcement training, Bernie is ready to put his best paw forward and sniff out his forever home!
