Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Sweethear is a 1-year-old retriever mix who weighs around 68 lbs, and her name really does suit her. She is incredibly sweet, gentle, and happiest when she can be right by your side. She’s perfectly content lounging with her person, sleeps through the night, and has a very soft, affectionate way about her.

She’s house-trained and loves a good sniffing session, especially if it ends with her flopping over to show you her belly for pets. Sweethear isn’t a big fan of being confined in a crate, but she seems comfortable being left alone outside of it. She’ll be a lovely match for someone looking for an affectionate dog who enjoys being close and taking life at an easy pace.
