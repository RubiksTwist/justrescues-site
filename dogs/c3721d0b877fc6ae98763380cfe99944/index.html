Description provided by PupStarz Rescue

Meet Stick Vacuum, a playful and spirited 6-month-old, 20-ish-pound (as of 10/5) Lab/Terrier mix who is as cute as a button and sweeter than sugar. This outgoing girl makes friends with everyone—from kids to adults to old folks to other dogs! She’s as charming and irresistible as they come!

Now that Stick Vacuum is old enough for walks, she’s learning all about this whole leash thing! She’s still a puppy, so she’s figuring it out as she goes, but she’s ready to explore the world beyond the backyard and discover all the exciting smells and sights that come with being a big girl!

Stick Vacuum has lots of dog pals and absolutely loves being part of a pack. She enjoys tussling and playing with her canine friends, following the bigger dogs around, or snuggling up to them like it’s one big cuddle party.

Stick Vacuum reliably uses pee pads and is crate trained, although this social butterfly would much prefer to be loose and free. A true people dog, Stick Vacuum will happily follow her humans from room to room and will make sure you know when she’d like to be held, petted, or kissed. A more affectionate puppy you’ll never meet!

Playful, confident, and clever, Stick Vacuum is a quick learner and will absolutely keep you entertained. She loves figuring things out and thinks nothing of climbing, squeezing into tight spaces, or investigating anything that catches her attention. This curious girl is always ready to see what’s happening next!

And of course, she’s still a puppy—which means she’s continuing to learn her manners, build her confidence, and figure out the world around her. But with the right family willing to give her the exercise, enrichment, training, and attention a growing puppy needs, Stick Vacuum is going to make an amazing companion.

If you’ve got room in your heart and home for a sweet, social, playful puppy, Stick Vacuum is ready to jump right in—probably into your heart, your home, and definitely your bed if you let her. And she’s SO soft and snuggly, it’s going to be pretty hard to say no

If you would like to adopt this Rockstar, please visit www.pupstarzrescue.org for an application!
