Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

🐚 Meet Seashell! A little beachy beauty with a whole lot of love to give! 🐚❤️

Seashell doesn't believe in strangers, just friends she hasn't met yet! Found as a stray wearing nothing but a seashell necklace (yes, really!), this sweet girl quickly wiggled her way into our hearts. We may not know much about her past or how she ended up on her own, but one thing is certain: Seashell is ready to make up for lost time with a whole lot of love, cuddles, and new friendships.

At just 1 year old, Seashell is still a baby herself and learning all about the world around her. But don't let her age fool you; this girl is one smart cookie! She's already mastered "sit" and is making great progress with "down," crate training, and house training. While she's not quite 100% potty trained yet, she's come a long way and now heads straight to the door when she needs to go outside. She's a quick learner, and we can't wait to see what else she picks up!

🐾 A Social Butterfly (With Fur, Whiskers, or Two Legs!)

Seashell is an absolute dream when it comes to making friends. She LOVES other dogs and is such a thoughtful, respectful playmate. She knows how to have fun without being overwhelming, takes breaks when needed, and shares beautifully. In fact, she's even been known to bring her toys over to her doggy friends in hopes they'll want to play together. Sharing is caring, after all!

She's also cat-friendly! Now, she does find her feline friends pretty exciting and sometimes thinks they'd make excellent playmates, but she's quickly learning to respect their boundaries and give them space when they ask.

And kids? Seashell has met a handful and has been wonderful with all of them! She's incredibly gentle, friendly, and genuinely happy to meet just about anyone, regardless of age.

💕 A Girl With So Much Potential

Seashell is the kind of dog who makes you smile just by being herself. She's affectionate, social, eager to learn, and clearly loves having friends around. She's still figuring out the ropes of being a family dog, but she's off to such a wonderful start!

We're having so much fun getting to know her little quirks and watching her personality blossom. Now, all that's missing is a forever family to help her continue growing, learning, and discovering just how wonderful life can be.

If you're looking for a sweet, social, endlessly lovable little companion who believes the whole world is her friend, Seashell might just be your girl! 🐚❤️
