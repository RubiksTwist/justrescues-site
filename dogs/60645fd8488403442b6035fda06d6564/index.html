Laffy Taffy is at risk due to behavioral concerns. Laffy Taffy has not acclimated well to the shelter environment. When attempting to conduct a handling assessment, he approaches handlers with a tense body and does not allow collaring. In the kennel, Laffy Taffy is experiencing a high level of kennel stress. He is throwing his water bowl around the kennel while screeching. On one occasion, when a handler checks on him, Laffy Taffy is seen standing on the water bucket, body tense, and escalated to lunging at the handler while hard barking. He then returns to throwing the bucket around while vocalizing. Medically, Laffy Taffy is apparently healthy.

Laffy Taffy was brought in as a stray, so his behavior in a home environment is unknown. In the Care Centers, Laffy Taffy has displayed fearful behavior, including maintaining a tense posture when staff interact with him. He has also been observed to lunge from his kennel when staff approach. With slow and patient approaches, Laffy Taffy has shown some social behavior. Laffy Taffy should be placed in an adult-only home through a New Hope Partner who can appropriately manage his behavior using positive reinforcement.
