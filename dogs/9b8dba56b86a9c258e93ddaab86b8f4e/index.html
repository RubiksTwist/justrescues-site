🌸🐾 MEET TIGER LILY! 🐾🌸

This adorable little Rugrat is Tiger Lily—and underneath all that puppy cuteness is one seriously sweet little girl. 💕

Tiger Lily's story didn't have the best beginning. She came from an animal control center weighing only about 4 lbs. and absolutely covered in fleas. It took THREE baths just to finally get all of them off her. 💔

At first, Tiger Lily was very quiet and shy. She wasn't quite sure about everything happening around her.
