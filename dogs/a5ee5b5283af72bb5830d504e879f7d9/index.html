Description provided by ASPCA Adoption Center

Playtime and treats make Pinocle a happy kitty! Pinocle is bonded to Fish.

Pinocle is a sweet cat who is happiest when she’s being active. Bring out a mouse wand and you’ll see her running across the room, and if you’re sitting on the floor she’ll jump over your legs and run across your lap as she tries to catch the mouse! After a hunting session, Pinocle is the first in line for treats. She is a food motivated girl and loves yummy food as well as crunchy and soft treats. If you toss a few dry treats, she’ll run to catch and eat them!

Pinocle also has a calmer side. She enjoys relaxing and sitting near her people, taking in the activity happening around her. She’s an affectionate cat and will let you know she is ready for pets by giving you head butts. She may even rest her paws on your legs!

Get that mouse wand ready for Pinocle! To learn more about Pinocle, click the “Adopt me” button.
