Meet Beauty!
Well, hello gorgeous! Beauty is a social, affectionate girl who loves making new human friends. Found as a stray, she's still waiting to find out what her next chapter will look like, but she's already shown us that she has plenty of love and personality to share. She's a playful pup who enjoys toys, treats, and being close to her people. With her happy wiggles and big personality, Beauty is sure to make quite an impression!

My Ideal Home
Beauty would love an active home with plenty of opportunities for walks, playtime, and fun adventures. She's got energy to spare, so food puzzles, long lasting chews, and games of hide and seek will help keep her busy. She's looking for a family who can keep her entertained, teach her new tricks, and give her lots of affection along the way!

Good with Other Pets?
Beauty came to us as a stray, so her history with other pets is unknown. She can be a little watchful when she spots other dogs on leash, so any introductions to potential furry friends should be slow and thoughtful.

Good with Kids?
Beauty would do best in an adult only home at this time. She's an energetic girl who can get quite excited during play and greetings, so she'd appreciate a family who can help her channel all that enthusiasm.

Things to Know
Beauty is still learning her leash manners and may get excited enough to grab and tug on her leash during walks. Carrying a favorite toy and using tasty treats can help keep her focused on you. She also likes to keep special toys close, so trading for an equally yummy treat or another toy is the way to go rather than taking things directly from her. With some positive reinforcement and a little practice, Beauty will be ready to put her best paw forward. She's got a whole lot of love to give, and she's hoping you'll find her irresistible!
