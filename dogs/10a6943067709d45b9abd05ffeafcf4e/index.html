Max | 6 y/o | 15 lbs | Terrier Mix

Max is a 6-year-old, 15-pound small mixed breed with a distinguished beard, a big personality, and a heart full of enthusiasm. He’s loving, sweet, and spirited, with all the charm of a true terrier. 🐾

Since entering his foster home in Brooklyn, he’s adjusted beautifully and his true self has blossomed. (His foster nicknamed him “Merlin” because of that wizard-worthy beard, but he will always be Max again to us!).

Our favorite things about Max:

  • 🐾 Walks, every single time: He’s thrilled about them, whether it’s the first of the day or the fourth.
  • 😴 Sleeping on his back with his feet in the air, like a very content little bug.
  • 🌪️ Rolling and tumbling across the floor like the Tasmanian Devil. He entertains himself in the most bizarre and wonderful way.
  • 🗣️ Chatting. When he’s happy or excited, Max mumbles and makes tiny whistling sounds to tell you all about it.
  • 🤗 Hugs. He’ll wrap his little paws around your leg and rest his head against you.
  • 🔎 Exploring. Max loves to investigate everything, and every outing is an adventure. Watch him in action! (videos)

The practical stuff

  • Housebroken: Fully house-trained. A wee-wee pad is kept out at his foster’s just in case, but he has never had an accident in the home.
  • Dog-friendly: He’s great with other dogs and very well behaved on walks.
  • City-ready: He’s a perfect apartment dog. He gets chilly easily, but is happy to sport a coat on walks. 🧥

His ideal home: Max’s forever family will be patient and consistent, willing to keep building on his progress, and comfortable respecting his boundaries while gently working on his manners. Max appreciates his space and hasn’t lived with young children, so we recommend a home with older children (12+) only.

Ready to meet this bearded little character?

Vaccinated for rabies, DHPP, and bordetella, heartworm negative, neutered and microchipped. Current on flea, tick, and heartworm prevention.

To apply for Max, visit our website (www.waggytailrescue.org) for an adoption application. It is important to fill out an application as soon as possible for consideration, and we require a complete application for you to meet an animal. We are currently unable to respond to general email inquiries as an all-volunteer run rescue. If the dog is on our website, they are still available.

Waggytail Rescue CANNOT guarantee the exact age of any adoptable dog, or final size of any of our adoptable puppies. We rely on the information provided to us by veterinarians, shelters, owner surrenders and other circumstances bringing the dog into our rescue.
