Meet Amma Cocoa

Somewhere along the way, Amma Cocoa's life became about producing puppies rather than being loved and cared for herself. Found in Mississippi in a hoarding and breeding situation, this beautiful girl had already given so much of herself. She was a mama to seven little puppies, but who was looking out for her?

Now, it's finally Amma Cocoa's turn.

Look at that sweet face and those beautiful, soulful eyes. It's hard not to wonder what this girl has been through, but we'd much rather focus on everything wonderful waiting for her. No more being just another dog in a crowded situation. No more being valued for what she could produce. Amma Cocoa deserves to be someone's beloved companion, to have a cozy place to rest, and to know what it feels like to be cared for simply because she is loved.

She's already done her part raising her seven little ones. Now we hope someone will step forward and give this devoted mama the chance to be someone's baby.

Her next chapter should be about long naps, gentle affection, little adventures, and a family who chooses her every single day.

Amma Cocoa has spent enough time being a mama to everyone else. Isn't it time someone took care of her?
