Description provided by ASPCA Adoption Center

Alpha is ready to bloom!

Alpha is a sweet and gorgeous cat! She likes to lounge and observe the world around her, relaxing to the sound of music or the TV. Alpha enjoys watching the world around her, making sure each connection is meaningful.

With her foster family, this sweet girl has shown her affectionate side, actively seeking out cuddles and comfortably falling asleep on the couch and bed. She is a purring machine, especially when head scratches are involved! Alpha loves to chase the automatic laser and will come sit on your lap anytime you call her. She also has the sweetest little chirp when she wants attention.

Alpha is ready to become your loyal companion! To learn more about Alpha, click the “Adopt me” button.
