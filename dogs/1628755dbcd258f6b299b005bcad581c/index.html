Description provided by Waldo's Rescue Pen

Hi! This sweet boy’s name is Tracker! Tracker is a bundle of joy with a tail that never seems to stop wagging! He has a kind and loving personality and loves to give a hug and a kiss to everyone he meets. Tracker is a smart boy and a great listener! He’ll happily sit for a treat, and he quickly learned to go potty outside. He was surrendered by his previous owner, but that hasn’t stopped him from being full of love and hope for life!

Tracker is energetic and enjoys a nice walk where he can explore and exercise. He loves being outside! He does best with at least a 30 minute walk, although he’s happy to go longer if you have the time. He is still working on his leash manners, but he’s getting better every day. He’s interested in meeting other dogs while out on walks and has been a good boy as he learns how to socialize.

Although Tracker is energetic, he also has a calm and cuddly side. He enjoys relaxing and taking naps on the sofa. And if you’re on the sofa, Tracker comes right over to join you for some cuddles!

Tracker loves having others around and isn’t comfortable being alone. He whines if left by himself. He is working on crate training and is slowly learning that his crate is a safe and comfortable space. At night, he will sleep in his crate with the door open, although he’d rather sleep in the bed by your side!

Tracker loves toys and enjoys playing fetch and having toys to chew on. His recent introduction to a Kong toy has been helpful in keeping him happy and occupied.

Tracker has a normal appetite, but enjoys company while he eats. He does best with an elevated, slow feeding bowl.

Tracker is still young and will need patience and guidance as he learns and grows. In return, you’ll get a sweet boy who will love being by your side. Tracker is a sweetheart, and I hope you’ll consider being the one who gives him the forever home he deserves!

ALL adoptions require a completed application and final contract. We conduct veterinary and personal reference checks during the application approval process. We are unable to contact every applicant for every dog. We will select the best applicant for each dog, based on the dogs needs. Waldos Rescue Pen is a 501c3 whose primary focus is rescuing dogs from the euthanasia list from high kill shelters throughout the United States and Korea.
