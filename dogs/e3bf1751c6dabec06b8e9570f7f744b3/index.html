Description provided by Waldo's Rescue Pen

Hi! I’m Jaq - my foster family renamed me Wilson. I'm a 42lb, nine month old brindle and white puppy who loves nothing more than romping, playing with my toys, snuggling with my foster family, and falling asleep wrapped up around my sibling puppy Jessica Rabbit. I was found abandoned in Georgia and taken to a shelter, but now I’m loving my life in New York. I’m currently sharing my house with 2 cats, 2 dogs, my sister Jessica, and another foster dog, and I am absolutely having the time of my life. I make my foster family laugh nonstop with my silliness, then I crawl into their laps for well-deserved naps. I love every animal who crosses my path and want snuggles from every person I meet. I love to play and wrestle with my sister - I'm a little shy and reserved when I first meet people, but warm up quickly. I'm pee pad trained, crate trained, and getting the hang of housetraining. I’m up to date on all of my shots, and I’m looking to bring the same joy, sunshine, laughter, and love to my forever home that I’ve given to my foster home.
