Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

This pet needs emergency placement. Please click here to go to our emergency placement page for more information.

Queens is on the at-risk list due to medical concerns. Queens is a shy sweet boy who has developed a severe URI while in care. Queens also has a high grade heart murmur making it difficult to manage his current respiratory infection with fluids. He is very stressed in the shelter environment and so is having a difficult time healing. Queens needs individual attention and a lower stress environment.

A volunteer writes:
Queens came to us about a month ago with three of his friends as his house counted too many pets. They were all quite weary and thrown off by this sudden and unexpected turn of events. Each of them had lost everything and everyone....their identity too. Slowly but surely, Queens(and some of his housemates) gained some trust in us and we mutually enjoy each other company in the isolation ward where he resides for a bad cold. Queens comes forward for petting, leans, head bunts and appreciates very much churus. Quite a change from the time he arrived at the care center! Beside his cold, Queens does have a 4/6 heart murmur(with an abnormal proBNP)and being outside the shelter environment in a quiet home and with an understanding and caring new owner would increase his chance at a longer and happy life. Will you be Queens'Hero?
