Meet Cheeks!

This sweet girl was pulled from the ACC with just hours before she was scheduled to be euthanized. Upon meeting her, she was timid, sick, and unsure what her future would look like.

After some time under our care she became the sweetest and cheekiest dog. Cheeks absolutely adores people and kids, and loves being the center of attention at home. While she can be a little shy on walks, her affectionate nature shines through, making her the perfect cuddle companion, always looking to be by your side.

She wants a home that can continue to help her build confidence and someone to share her sweet personality with, and to finally show her what a loving family is. Could you be the one to give Cheeks the loving home she truly deserves?
