Meet Little Basil!

Basil is an absolutely adorable 5-month-old Bombay kitten weighing just 4.5 pounds (as of 10/5)! This tiny girl has the sweetest personality and is full of love. Her favorite place to be is right in your lap, soaking up all the pets and affection she can get!

When she’s not cuddling, Basil loves playing with her toys and enjoying all the fun that comes with being a kitten. She also gets along wonderfully with other cats and would be a great addition to a home with a feline friend.

Basil is a tripod, but don’t let that fool you—being a three-legged kitty doesn’t slow her down or affect her ability to enjoy life! She gets around just fine and is ready to run, play, cuddle, and do all the things kittens love to do. She is also housetrained and ready for her forever home.

Sweet, playful, affectionate, and absolutely precious, Little Basil is ready to find a family who will love her as much as she’ll love them!

If you would like to give this wonderful cat a home, please visit www.pupstarzrescue.org for an application!
