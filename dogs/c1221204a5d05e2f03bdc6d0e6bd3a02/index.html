Meet Basil!
Basil is a sweet, affectionate boy who loves spending time with his people and has a playful side that really shines around other dogs! His former family described him as a low energy companion who can be a little nervous around loud noises and certain types of handling. Here at ACC, though, he's been an absolute rockstar in our dog playgroups, showing off his playful bows, bouncy moves, and ability to take breaks before jumping back into the fun. This handsome herb is ready to put down roots in a loving home of his own!

My Ideal Home
Basil would love a calm, comfortable home where he can enjoy the best of both worlds: relaxing with his favorite people and getting out for walks and playtime. He enjoys toys and knows how to Sit, so fun training sessions and food puzzles will give him great ways to stay engaged. His family should be patient with new experiences and let him settle in at his own pace, helping him feel safe and secure.

Good with Other Pets?
Basil has been a rockstar with other dogs in dog playgroups! He's enjoyed games of chase, playful bows, and bouncy wrestling, and he has shown that he can take breaks and return to play appropriately. He can occasionally get a little carried away, but he's been redirectable. Since his previous family reported no experience with other dogs, slow and thoughtful introductions will still be important when meeting potential housemates.

Good with Kids?
Basil would do best in a home without children under 5. He's a sensitive boy who appreciates having a choice about when he wants attention, so calm and respectful interactions will help him feel comfortable.

Things to Know
Basil is happy to lean in for affection and enjoys being close to his people, but he can feel unsure around loud noises, baths, and certain types of handling. Giving him time, respecting his boundaries, and using positive reinforcement will help him build confidence. He'll benefit from daily exercise, enrichment, and plenty of opportunities to show off his playful side. With a little patience and lots of love, Basil is ready to grow into a wonderful lifelong companion!
