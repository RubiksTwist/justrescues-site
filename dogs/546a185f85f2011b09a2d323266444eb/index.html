Meet Billy Ray!
Billy Ray is a sweet, sensitive boy who is hoping to find a quiet place to call home. After being surrendered when his owner could no longer care for him, he's had a lot of changes to navigate and may need a little extra time to feel comfortable. With some patience, kindness, and plenty of encouragement, Billy Ray is ready for a fresh start.

My Ideal Home
Billy Ray would love a peaceful home where he can settle in without too much hustle and bustle. He'll do best with someone who lets him take things at his own pace and uses treats, praise, and positive reinforcement to help him build confidence. Daily walks and gentle enrichment will give him a nice routine while he gets to know his new family.

Good with Other Pets?
Billy Ray showed no response to other dogs while walking at ACC. His history with other animals is limited, so any introductions with resident pets should still be slow and thoughtful.

Good with Kids?
Billy Ray would do best in an adult-only home where he can enjoy a calm environment and take his time getting comfortable. He's a sensitive boy who will appreciate people who understand that trust takes time.

Things to Know
Billy Ray can be a little shy when meeting new people and may need some coaxing before he's ready to interact. Once he's feeling more comfortable, he'll tolerate gentle handling and follow along on walks with some encouragement. This handsome guy may need a little time to come out of his shell, but the right person could be just what he needs to feel at home again.
