My foster writes: If you're looking for a sweet and sleepy senior cuddle bug, look no further! After having this guy for a few months, we can definitely say that he treasures his beauty sleep. He is a professional daytime napper and, if you get up too early, he'll come get some pets and head right back to sleep! But don't let his sleepy demeanor fool you - he also has a playful side. When the mood strikes, he'll do the spins (jump around in a circle) and demand a whole 10 minutes of tussling before promptly returning to his bed for a snooze. He's housebroken, well-mannered, and an overall sweetie. Although he has no separation anxiety (and sometimes seems to appreciate a long, uninterrupted nap), he is more than happy to cuddle on the couch for movie marathons.

My foster writes: Flint is an absolute sweetheart and joy to be around. He's a professional snoozer, looking for love between naps. Even though he's a senior, he's got a puppy-like energy when it comes to getting excited for walks. It's very clear he looks to please, and may just need a little patience when it comes to his loss of hearing and figuring out how to communicate with him.
