Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Blanche is the definition of a little lovebug. This 3-year-old mixed breed weighs around 13 pounds and is happiest when she's close to her people, whether she's curled up beside you for a cuddle or greeting you with her signature happy smile. She's house-trained and has the sweetest personality, always ready to soak up love and return it right back. She also has the most unique facial coloring, giving her an unforgettable look that's just as special as she is.

People are definitely Blanche's favorite, and she seems delighted to make new human friends wherever she goes. Around other dogs, she's a little more reserved at first and appreciates having a bit of time to get comfortable before showing her true self. Once she settles in, her affectionate, gentle nature shines through. If you're looking for a loyal companion who will happily keep you company on the couch and fill your days with cuddles, kisses, and unconditional love, Blanche is ready to steal your heart.

Blanche will do best in a home where she doesn't need to spend much time alone and who is happy to have a velcro cuddlebug that just loves to hang around close.

Registered adopters interested in Blanche can email adopt@hbrdogs.com for more info. If you’re not registered yet, you can do it at hbrdogs.com.
