Meet Ellie - a seasoned lady with a side of sassy!

Ellie is a 42 lb, sweet, loyal girl who wakes up ready to match your energy. Depending on your vibe, Ellie s always down for either an adventure or cuddle session - you take the lead and she will follow

Ellie is a true lover - believing every human - kid and adult she crosses paths with is a potential new best friend. Ellie absolutely loves to meet new people and shines her brightest when being covered in attention and affection. She is a true people pleaser and is happy to spend as much time with her people as they would allow.

Not only is Ellie a big fan of spending time with her humans, she's also so eager to please them. Ellie has mastered her sit and stay commands and shows up excitedly for training sessions at any time - especially when yummy treats and sweet praise are involved..

Ellie is an absolute wonderful walking companion and she has amazing leash skills. Even in moments when Ellie becomes briefly excited at the sight of a squirrel or a bird, checking in with her person is a much higher priority and she will happily continue on her way.

Ellie is becoming more confident with playing independently and would be a dream dog around kids given her gentle, friendly and affectionate nature - especially with little ones who are equally respectful. Ellie is fully housebroken making her an adoption ready pup for any home that is looking for a happy go lucky, all around friendly, pint sized pretty girl to share their life with.

If Ellie sounds like the soulmate you've been searching for - apply to meet her today, we have no doubt they'll be an instant connection.
