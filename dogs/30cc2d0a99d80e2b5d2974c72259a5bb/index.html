Meet Refrigerator!

At 4 years old and 69 pounds, Refrigerator is a big, handsome black Pittie mix with an even BIGGER heart! If you're looking for a loving, affectionate companion who doubles as a professional couch snuggler, Refrigerator would like to submit his application.

Refrigerator is an absolute sweetheart. He's incredibly gentle, loving, and cuddly, and his favorite place to be is right next to his people. He loves crawling up beside you for pets, giving kisses, and soaking up all the affection he can get. He may even throw in a little hug every now and then! He's the kind of dog who just wants to be near his humans, and once he's settled in, he's perfectly happy to snooze the day away.

Inside the house, Refrigerator is about as easygoing as they come. He's housebroken, well-mannered, non-destructive, and does just fine having the run of the house when left alone. He doesn't bark, isn't a resource guarder, and even though he's not particularly fond of being crated, he does go into his crate at night. He knows sit and paw, is treat-motivated, and is always happy to show off what he knows.

But don't let his couch-potato ways fool you! Once Refrigerator heads outside, his social butterfly side comes out. He LOVES people and wants to say hello to everyone he meets. He's also VERY interested in other dogs and would love nothing more than to run around and play with some canine friends. He can get excited and pull toward other dogs on walks, so leash manners are still a work in progress. His foster is working with him, and his love of treats is definitely helping!

Refrigerator previously lived in a home with cats in Tennessee, although he has not been tested with cats in his current foster home.

Refrigerator is looking for a home that appreciates a big, affectionate lovebug with a playful side. Someone who enjoys walks, doesn't mind continuing to work on leash manners, and has plenty of room on the couch for a 69-pound cuddle buddy would be right up his alley!

Sweet, gentle, people-loving, and full of affection, Refrigerator is ready to trade his foster couch for a forever couch. Could yours be the one?

If you would like to adopt this dog, please visit www.pupstarzrescue.org for an application!
