Meet Omar! This 3-year-old, 8.5-pound chihuahua mix is a sweet and gentle companion. He loves relaxing in your lap, receiving pets, and showering you with an endless supply of kisses. He also absolutely lights up around other dogs! Hanging with a canine buddy really brings out his happy, playful side.

Omar is a fan of his crate and is happy to hang out in there with no issues. He is still getting used to the sights and sounds of going outside on a leash, but he is learning to be more confident every day at his own comfortable pace.
