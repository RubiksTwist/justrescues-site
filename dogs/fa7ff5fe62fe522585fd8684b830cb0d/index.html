What my friends at ACC say about me:

I love getting pets and - you guessed it - snuggles!

I'm ready to learn! I need a patient person who has the time to work on training with me.

I don't always like to share my food, toys or bedding with other animals.

I will need daily physical activity to keep me healthy and happy!

I would do best in a home without very tiny humans, although I could be open to older human children once I meet them.

My #BoroughBreak Buddy Writes: We had such an incredible Boroughbreak day with Swan! She is absolutely perfect and her forever family will be so lucky to have her. She is super affectionate, cuddly, sweet, and easy going. She still has that silly puppy goofiness, but she really matched our energy all day and was very easy and responsive. Her disposition is extremely gentle and we could see her doing well with a lot of different types of families. She was happy and relaxed meeting new people on walks and although we didn't interact with other dogs on our walks, she definitely seemed like she would do well being social with other dogs and wanted to say hello to all that we passed. As a bonus, she was also so well-behaved in the car. Swan will likely need a little support with potty training as well as confidence building on walks (understandably, she's a little nervous/intimidated with all the city noises and such). Overall, Swan was an absolute dream - the sweetest girl who will be an incredible best friend to a lucky family!
