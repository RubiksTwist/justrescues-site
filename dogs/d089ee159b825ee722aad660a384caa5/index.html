Meet Billy! 🐾

Billy is a 7-year-old sweetheart who has mastered the art of living his best life: long walks, cozy company, and snacks. Billy does well on the leash. He walks right beside you on a loose leash, just a steady, happy stroll with his favorite person. Whether it’s a quick loop around the block or a long wander, he’s the kind of walking buddy who makes you want to go the extra mile. His energy is the best kind of balance: some days he’s happy to snooze the afternoon away, and other days he’s out in the backyard soaking up the sun and sniffing every corner. And treats? Billy has never once said no to a treat. Keep a pocket stocked and you’ll have a friend for life (though, to be fair, he’ll probably be your friend anyway). What Billy loves most is YOU. He is an affection sponge. Scratches on his head, belly rubs, a lap to lean on: he soaks up every bit of attention you give him and happily asks for more. Billy is a true Velcro dog. If you’re in the kitchen, he’s in the kitchen. If you move to the couch, he’s right behind you. He has a big heart and a need to be close to his people. Billy has had a tough road, returned to the shelter more than once at no fault of his own, all he really wants is someone who will stay. He’s looking for a home where he can be part of the family and know he’s not going anywhere. He’s the kind of dog who wants to be where his people are. Billy would thrive with a family who has time and love to give, whether that’s someone who is home often or a household that’s happy to take things slowly and help him build confidence during time apart. With patience, reassurance, and a little time, he’ll learn that when you leave, you always come back.

Could you be the one to give Billy the forever home he deserves? 🧡

﻿Billy is approximately 7y 4m 7d old and is fully grown at 64 pounds. They are microchipped and up to date on all age-appropriate medical care. Their breed is unknown as we have not had a DNA test done.
