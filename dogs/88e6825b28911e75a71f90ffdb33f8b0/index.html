This sweet Georgia boy made his way to NYC, and now he’s hoping his next chapter is with a family who will show him that he is safe, loved, and finally home. Fraiser aka Bear, is a playful, loving boy with the sweetest soul. He’s the kind of pup who will make you laugh with his goofy personality, then melt right into you when he’s ready for cuddles. Bear has been through some things in his past, and he carries a little trauma with him. He may need some patience and understanding while he learns that he is finally safe. But once Bear trusts you, something beautiful happens — he opens up his big heart and shows you just how much love he has to give. He loves to play, loves attention, and absolutely treasures the people he feels safe with. Watching him come out of his shell and become more confident is incredibly rewarding. Bear doesn’t need someone looking for a perfect dog. In return you’ll have a loyal, goofy boy .
