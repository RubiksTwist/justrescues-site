Meet Bella, a one-year-old female weighing a bit under 35 lbs! She is currently in foster care and ready for adoption via our virtual process! Here’s what we know so far about her time in foster care: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1YQ-mU7SnBjoccy-Eiqn9jtFdgZWR7bjE/view?usp=drive_link Her walker said "Bella is the sweetest dog. She would be perfect for a young couple or a family. She is curious, playful and nice to other dogs, children and adults. She walks well and doesn’t pull when she walks and she checks in every 10 minutes for a hug!" She settled in nicely at foster. "Bella is the sweetest playful girl. She is playful but will melt on your couch as soon as you starting petting her becoming your couch companion. Bella loves cuddles. She will play and come to cuddle or kiss every few mins." Her foster says "Bella is a sweet, loving, curious bundle of energy. She loves playing with her toys, exploring her environment, getting pets & cuddling with her humans. Bella is only a year old so she is still learning and up for the task as she is smart and tends to pick up on learning cues pretty quickly (such as sit, etc.). Bella is already potty trained and will walk to the door to let you know she needs to go out, but still has some work to do as she still an adolescent like jumping when overly excited, mouthing when playing, and not bothering the squirrels on her walks when they are just trying to get a nut and live. Bella gets along with other animals and has yet shown any sign of aggression, gentle and doesn't resource guard her belongings. Bella is so sweet she just gives so much love. And she is so beautiful every walk someone comments how pretty she is and questions what is her breed I assume she is a hound mix with her lean body and long legs. Bella is so silly just looking at her I start to smile, I call her my little shadow as she goes where I go". She is spayed, vaccinated, microchipped , and has had preventative treatments to ensure she is ready for her next steps.
