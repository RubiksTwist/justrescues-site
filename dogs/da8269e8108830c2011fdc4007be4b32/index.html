Description provided by Louie's Legacy Animal Rescue — Northeast

My name is Fuchshia, and I’m a 40lb, 12-year-old Tibetan Terrier who is absolutely obsessed with her people.

Don’t let my age fool you — I’m still a playful pup with a whole lot of love to give! 🐾 If you’re looking for a loyal companion who will be your little shadow, your biggest fan, and your personal cuddle buddy, I might just be your girl!

One look into my big, beautiful brown eyes and you’ll see just how much love I have to give. 🥹💕 I have a way of gazing up at my favorite people that makes you feel like you’re the most special person in the whole world because, to me, you are!

I’m a social sweetheart who gets along well with other dogs and kids, so I’m happy to make new friends of all sizes. I’m also potty trained and crate trained, which means I’ve already got some important life skills down!

My favorite things include going for walks, playing with my toys, and spending quality time with my favorite humans. Of course, even a girl as active as me needs her beauty sleep, so I make sure to fit in a few cozy snoozes and cuddle sessions throughout the day. 💕

My love language? Scratches, snuggles, and absolutely ALL of your attention! And if you dare to stop petting me before I’m ready, don’t worry, I’ll give you a little paw to remind you that we are definitely not finished yet.

I may be a senior in years, but my heart is full of love, my playful spirit is still going strong, and I have so much affection left to share. All I need is a loving home where I can enjoy my walks, playtime, cozy naps, and endless cuddles with my favorite people.

Could you be the one to give this sweet girl her happily ever after? 🏡💗

Fuchshia is being fostered in Clark, NJ and her adoption fee is $400.

To adopt this animal, please visit https://www.louieslegacy.org/adopt

All of our animals come spayed or neutered, up to date on age appropriate vaccinations, are microchipped, heartworm negative, current on flea/tick and heartworm prevention, dewormed, and treated for any ailments found upon veterinary examination. The adoption fee covers a portion of these services, and makes it possible for us to continue rescuing animals.

For more information on the adoption fees and the adoption process, please complete our adoption application here: https://www.louieslegacy.org/adopt - an application allows us to get to know your family better so we can assess whether the animal you are interested in is a good match for your home. An application does not obligate you to adopt. Please understand that due to the volume of applications some animals receive, we are unable to contact all applicants. If your application is considered a good fit for the animal you are interested in, or even a different pet, a volunteer will give you a call as soon as possible.

We are a foster based rescue and do not have a shelter location where animals can be seen during the week. Please see our Facebook page at https://www.facebook.com/LouiesLegacy for our weekly adoption album if you are interested in meeting an animal in person. Not all animals attend every adoption event, please check our Facebook page for animal event times and locations before you make a trip to meet a specific animal.
