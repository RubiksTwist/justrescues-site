HISTORY

Jagger was found as a stray in Georgia after he turned up at a family’s home. The family kept him for a while before he moved to our rescue partner. He traveled to the NYC area in mid-October 2026.

DETAILED PROFILE

When Jagger wants to go out, he fetches one sneaker and sets it at your feet. He doesn’t chew it; he doesn’t chew anything but his toys. He just makes his point, politely, the way he does most things.

He’s a big, easygoing adult with moderate energy. When our team first met him in Georgia, he kept to himself. These days he’s friendly and affectionate, though he may take a moment to size up a stranger. Some greetings come with front paws up, but he calms quickly and redirects well. Meet him with enthusiasm and he’ll hand it right back, usually as face kisses.

He’s been gentle with kids, including a school-age boy in the Georgia home that took him in and a young visitor who sat and brushed him. He hasn’t spent time with very young children yet, but he’s expected to do well with kids of all ages. With other dogs he’s a mellow roommate rather than a playmate, happy to share space without much interest in wrestling. His experience with cats isn’t known yet.

He’s still settling into foster life, but the early report card is impressive. He loves his crate and never fusses in it, hasn’t had an accident in the house, and walks politely at your side with only minor corrections. He’s not much of a treat guy. His stuffed toys, on the other hand, live on their own shelf, and he picks out exactly one at a time to carry to wherever he’s decided to park himself.

Moves like Jagger? Mostly face kisses and sneaker delivery.
