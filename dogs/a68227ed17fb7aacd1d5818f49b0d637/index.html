Part couch potato, part dramatic king, part “oops-sorry-I forgot I’m strong.” This handsome hunk is searching for an experienced, adult-only, pet-free kingdom to rule (mostly from the comfort of a couch). Hot Dog is affectionate, loyal, and deeply committed to giving you his whole heart. Hot Dog is looking for an experienced owner who can give him the guidance and structure he thrives from. He doesn’t know his own strength and can get a bit overwhelmed. With proper training techniques, you can quickly re-direct his attention to something positive, TREATS! Hot Dog is as silly as his name implies and simply loves people.
