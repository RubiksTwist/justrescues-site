Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Stew is a 2-year-old mixed breed who weighs around 21 lbs and is equal parts sweet, silly, and a little shy. He may take a minute to warm up, but once he does, he has one very reliable move: flop onto his back and request belly rubs immediately.

He loves being around other dogs and is very into the full canine friendship experience: playing, cuddling, sleeping together, and handing out plenty of smooches. Stew is also house-trained, does great in his crate, and can relax there quietly for a few hours without barking or crying.

He walks nicely on leash and likes to stay close to his person, which fits him pretty perfectly. Stew is a gentle, affectionate little guy with a goofy side and a lot of love to give.

Registered adopters interested in Stew can email adopt@hbrdogs.com for more info. If you’re not registered yet, do it today at hbrdogs.com!
