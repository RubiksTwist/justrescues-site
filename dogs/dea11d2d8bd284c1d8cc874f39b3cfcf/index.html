Oona is a happy, enthusiastic puppy who seems to find absolutely everything exciting. Her tail rarely stops wagging, and every new sound, movement, or discovery is enough to get her bouncing around like a little bunny. She's a joyful, curious girl who is expected to be medium-sized when fully grown.

Like any puppy, Oona has lots to learn as she grows and will need a patient, committed adopter who is ready to provide training, socialization, structure, and plenty of opportunities to explore the world. With the right guidance, Oona is ready to bring her infectious enthusiasm and lots of fun to her future family.
