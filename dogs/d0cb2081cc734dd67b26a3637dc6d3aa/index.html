Meet Reader!

Reader is an adorable 6-month-old, 7-pound (as of 8/26) brown tabby with the sweetest little face and a playful personality that is beginning to shine. He is a gentle, curious kitten who is learning that people are pretty wonderful—especially when Churu and treats are involved!

Reader is a little shy at first and is still building his confidence, so he would do best with a patient family who will give him the time and space he needs to settle in and feel comfortable. Once he does, his playful side really starts to come out. He absolutely loves other cats and enjoys having feline friends around, which helps him feel confident and gives him plenty of opportunities to play.

Reader is also comfortable around dogs and does well sharing his space with them. He is litterbox trained and ready for a loving home where he can continue to grow, learn, and blossom into the wonderful companion we know he can be.

With patience, playtime, and plenty of Churu, Reader is sure to become a very special part of his forever family!

If you would like to give this wonderful cat a home, please email rescue@pupstarzrescue.org or visit www.pupstarzrescue.org for an application!
