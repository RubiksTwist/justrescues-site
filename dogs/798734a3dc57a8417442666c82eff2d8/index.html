HISTORY

Moneyball was found as a stray in Georgia after wandering up to a home as a young dog. The people there couldn't keep him but fostered him until our rescue partner had space for him. He traveled to Brooklyn in mid-October 2026.

DETAILED PROFILE

Moneyball's love language is the full-body hug. Given the chance, he'd wrap himself around every person he meets, and since he's a big, beefy guy, that's a lot of affection arriving at once. He's goofy, entertaining, and eager to please, the kind of easygoing housemate who's simply nice to have around.

He's still settling into city life, and early signs point to a dog who takes a beat to size up a busy room full of strangers before switching into happy-go-lucky mode. Once he's warmed up, he's friendly with anyone who wants to say hello. His time around kids so far has been at adoption events; until his greetings are more polite, he's probably not the match for very young or nervous kids.

He lives with his foster's two dogs and plays with dogs at daycare. He loves a rough-and-rowdy wrestle, though his style can be a lot for some dogs, and he doesn't always notice when a playmate isn't into it. There's no fight in him: when other dogs start to squabble, he leaves the scene. Cats are still a question mark.

Training is early days. He settles in his crate with a treat, has the occasional accident indoors, and knows sit, place, and off in concept, with plenty of practice ahead. On leash he can be calm and polite one walk and a strong, zigzagging explorer the next. He also has a serious weakness for paper and cardboard. He'll hand over a box at home without fuss, but napkins on the sidewalk are harder to talk him out of, so "leave it" is high on the list.

His ideal adopter enjoys fetch and an active, playful routine and is ready for steady work on greetings and leash manners. A recycling bin with a lid is strongly recommended.

MEDICAL HISTORY

The dogs in our care are up-to-date on vaccinations, spayed or neutered (when old enough), microchipped, and given a general health check. Immediate medical needs and medical history will be addressed with the adopter prior to adoption. Complete vet records will be sent shortly after adoption.
