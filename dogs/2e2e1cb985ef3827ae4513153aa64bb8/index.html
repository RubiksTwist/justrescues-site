Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Dolly Polly is a 4-year-old chug who weighs just 8 lbs and has an underbite that deserves its own fan club. She’s house-trained, incredibly sweet, and very serious about staying close to her person.

She may need a couple of minutes to warm up when meeting someone new, but once she’s comfortable, she is all in. Dolly Polly loves cuddling, being carried, curling up beside you, and generally making sure there is as little space between the two of you as possible.

She’s tiny, affectionate, funny without trying, and just ridiculously lovable. Dolly Polly is the kind of dog who wins you over quickly and then spends the rest of her time making sure you know exactly how much she likes you.
