Meet Nebula 🌙 – A Sweet & Spicy Little Shadow

Nebula is a petite girl with a big personality. She may look like she’s just out of kittenhood, but she knows exactly who she is—and she’s not afraid to show it.

This beauty forms strong bonds with her person. She knows her name and will come running when called, greeting you with affection and weaving herself around your legs. Once she trusts you, she loves your attention, happily making biscuits and curling up close. She enjoys window watching, high perches, and occasional zoomies. She has excellent litter box manners and is a tidy girl who takes pride in her grooming. She enjoys both wet and dry food, with a preference for wet.

Nebula is what we lovingly call a sweet and “spicy” cat who can become overstimulated and may swat or hiss, so she needs an experienced cat adopter who understands feline body language and respects boundaries. She does best in a calm, quiet, adult-only home where she can feel safe and let her purrsonality shine!

Because she can be very stressed during travel or vet visits, she will need an experienced adopter prepared to handle her carefully, potentially with veterinary support for transitions.

Nebula has been treated for bronchitis and has been diagnosed with asthma and is using an inhaler, so a smoke/incense free home is required.

Nebula isn’t for everyone—but for the right person, she’s a deeply rewarding companion: loyal, expressive, and full of personality.

If you’re experienced with cats and looking for a unique, devoted companion, Nebula just might be your perfect match.
