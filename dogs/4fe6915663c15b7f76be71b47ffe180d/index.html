Description provided by PupStarz Rescue

Jerry isn’t exactly petite! This handsome hunk is a 2-year old, 12-pound tiger-striped stud who is super chill, very easy-going, and adores all forms of life, from humans to dogs to other cats. Yup, this cat is one friendly fella! Despite his large frame, Jerry is easy to pick up, and he doesn’t mind being carried around like a sack of potatoes.

Jerry’s favorite toy is a fishing line and he will happily chase it around for as long as you care to entertain him with it. A professional snuggler, Jerry enjoys cuddling on the couch and will contentedly hang out with you while you watch TV, read a book, or spend time on your iPad. A big fan of human companionship, Jerry is the type of cat who will follow you around the house to keep track of everything you’re doing. And just in case you have time for a quick head pet, Jerry will be right there waiting, with an adorable little smile on his face. Yes, Jerry has a smile! For real!

Fully litter-box trained, Jerry’s bathroom habits are pure perfection. He has a solid appetite, he goes nuts over treats, and he naps like a champ. He’s just soooo easy!

Jerry is sweet, adaptable, a pleasure to have around, and a dream to get along with. If you’re looking to add a cat to your life, Jerry is ready to be your new best friend.
