Cosmo is at risk due to behavioral concerns. Cosmo came into the care center with bite wounds caused by another dog in the home. In the care center, Cosmo displays fearfulness and handling sensitivity, including flinching when leashed, trembling, lowered body posture, tucked tail, whale eye, and lip licking. He has a history of leash biting and tugging, particularly during kennel entry and leash removal, but is able to be redirected with treats. Cosmo has shown some social behavior with familiar handlers. Medically, Cosmo has dental disease (mild to moderate) and is a DOH-Victim and on a 6-month quarantine.

Cosmo arrived at MACC as a stray with no known history. While in care, Cosmo has been observed to be highly fearful. Noted to have a tense body and low tail. During their assessment, Cosmo allowed minimal handling and remained tense throughout the interaction. In kennel, Cosmo has been observed to bite and tug the leash into kennel. This behavior is not easily redirected. Due to the detailed behavior we recommend an experienced adult only home.
