Meet Milo!

Milo was brought back to ACC when his previous owner could no longer care for him due to medical issues. This isn't Milo's first stay with us, and we're hoping his next chapter will be in a home where he can finally settle in for good. He's having a tough time with the shelter environment right now, spending much of his time pacing and panting, but his previous care has shown us a friendly, tolerant side of Milo that we're hoping to see more of once he's feeling comfortable.

My Ideal Home

Milo needs a patient home that understands that shelter stress can make it difficult for him to show his true personality. His family should give him plenty of time to decompress and use positive reinforcement to help him feel safe and confident. He'll also benefit from daily physical and mental enrichment, including walks, appropriate chew toys, food puzzles, and other activities that give his busy brain something positive to focus on.

Good with Other Pets?

Milo has had some positive experiences with other dogs. During a recent introduction, he approached a female dog with a soft body and slow tail wag before taking breaks on his own, and during a previous playgroup he enjoyed running with another dog and was easily called away by handlers. His new family should still introduce him to resident pets gradually and supervise their interactions.

Good with Kids?

Milo would do best in a home without children under 13. His history includes some sensitivity around handling and guarding valued items, so older, dog-savvy children who understand canine boundaries may be a better fit.

Things to Know

Milo can be a little overwhelmed in the shelter environment, so he will do best with time, patience, and a calm routine as he settles in. He has previously shown some sensitivity around food, treats, and toys, so his new family should give him space while he is enjoying his special things and use positive reinforcement to help him feel comfortable. Milo has also had some accidents in the past and will benefit from a consistent routine as he gets settled in his new home. Staff will be happy to talk with you about his medical needs and everything we know about this sweet boy when you meet him!
