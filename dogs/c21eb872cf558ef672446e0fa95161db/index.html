Description provided by Waldo's Rescue Pen

This little cutie you never knew you needed would be a great addition to the family. Maysilee may be small but this girl has so much love to give. She is smart, calm, playful, food motivated and just the silliest girl you'll ever meet. There are times were she does get scared and skiddish by sudden moments but I mean who wouldn't be.

One of my favorite things about Maysilee is that when you return her kisses she'll either place her paw on you, overload you with kisses or give you some playful nibbles.

A quirky little thing she does is when she greets you she'll jump on the back of your leg and when you try to turn around to greet her she'll move to the opposite side of you and jump on you. So you two will just be going around in circles trying to catch each other. It's so silly but its cute to see how excited she is to see you.

Maysilee does well around other dogs and cats (who's ages range from young and senior). She likes to initiate play but most of the time she'll be relaxing around you or exploring the house.

When it comes to new people she will bark at them but then settles down. She will also at times whine/bark when you leave but then calms down.

We are still working on house training. She is a very smart girl and is doing well with occasional accidents. If she has someone who is willing to continue her training and help guide her, she'll have no problems in the future.

Overall Maysilee is a sweet and wonderful girl who deserves the absolute best and a family that will her give as much love as she'll give them.
