Description provided by Animal Lighthouse Rescue

Introducing Twinkle! At just 4 months old and 6 pounds, Twinkle is a tiny Chihuahua mix with a personality that shines as brightly as her name. The first thing you'll notice is her absolutely adorable ears—and just wait until she gives you her signature head tilt when she hears something interesting. It's impossible not to fall in love. Twinkle is the perfect mix of playful puppy and lovable companion. She spends her days wrestling with her brothers and sisters, chasing toys, and soaking up every bit of fun she can find. When playtime is over, she's more than happy to be scooped up and carried wherever life takes you. Whether it's a trip to the coffee shop, a stroll through the park, a visit with friends, or even a train ride, Twinkle is convinced the best place to be is right by your side. This little girl is also incredibly food motivated, making training both fun and rewarding. With consistency, plenty of treats, and lots of love, she'll be eager to learn all the puppy basics and grow into an amazing lifelong companion. If you're looking for a tiny best friend with endless charm, a playful spirit, and a heart full of love, Twinkle is ready to make every adventure a little brighter. All she needs now is a forever family to call her own.

Twinkle is spayed, microchipped, and up to date on her vaccinations. For more information about her, please fill out an application at www.ALRcares.com and email Lori@alrcares.com. We schedule meet and greets for approved adopters only, an application is the first step in our approval process. Thank you!
