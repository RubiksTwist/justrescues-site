Meet Squish!

How could anyone resist a puppy named Squish?

Rescued from Georgia, Squish is ready for the next chapter of his life, and this time, he wants a family of his very own.

This adorable boy is full of puppy potential and ready to learn everything the world has to offer. He's looking for someone who will guide him through puppyhood with patience, consistency, and lots of love. From training and exploring to cuddling up after a busy day, Squish is ready to experience it all.

He's got a lifetime of memories waiting to be made, and we can't wait to see him grow into the wonderful dog he's meant to become.

Squish has made it this far. Now he just needs someone to make him part of the family.

Please give this sweet boy the forever home he's waiting for!

Want to learn more? We'd love to tell you! Our bios are just a snapshot, so please reach out to our adoption team with any questions about this dog's personality, medical history, or ideal home. We'd be happy to help you determine if Squish is the perfect match for your family.

All dogs will be altered, microchipped, and receive age-appropriate vaccines prior to adoption.
