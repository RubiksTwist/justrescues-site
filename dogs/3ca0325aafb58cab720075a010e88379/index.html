Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Wonton is a 1-year-old mixed breed who weighs around 45 lbs, and she is an absolute cuddle champion. She truly cannot get enough snuggles and is happiest when she can be close to her people.

She’s also very smart and such a good listener. Wonton sits patiently while people eat, responds well when redirected, and has been picking up new things really quickly. She doesn’t seem fazed by loud city noises and has already shown how adaptable she can be. The elevator was scary at first, and now she walks in like she’s been doing it forever.

Wonton is also doing great with house-training and is showing us that she’s affectionate, bright, and very eager to figure things out.
