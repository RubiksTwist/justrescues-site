Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Meet Otis! This one-year-old poodle weighs 35 pounds and might just be the happiest pup in the city! House- and crate-trained, Otis's foster says he's "such a love bug with people! He loves to be included, loves the attention, and LOVES to play. He's always bringing out his toys, and he is so sweet and full of puppy-like energy." His foster is even teaching him how to channel his energy into a good game of fetch! Otis also loves the water and has been known to jump into the tub and wait for his foster to turn on the tap so he can splash around.

Otis is still working on his leash skills and is looking for a patient adopter who will take their time and work with him so that his good boy skills really shine. If that could be you and you're ready to welcome a true bundle of joyous energy in your home, you won't want to miss this very cuddle pup!

Approved adopters interested in Otis can email adopt@hbrdogs.com for more info. If you're not approved yet, apply today at hbrdogs.com!
