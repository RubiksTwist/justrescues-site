Meet Chanel

Chanel has known heartbreak, but she hasn’t let it harden her heart. This gentle, sweet girl is ready to leave her past behind and open her heart to love again. She may take a little time to trust, but once she does, she’ll look at you with those soulful eyes that say, “Thank you for giving me a chance.”

Chanel is kind, affectionate, the kind of dog who just wants to be near you, soaking up the safety and warmth she’s been missing. She’s not asking for much just a patient, loving home where she can finally exhale and know she’s home for good.

If you believe in second chances and have room in your heart for a truly special soul, Chanel is waiting to meet you.

All dogs will be altered, microchipped, and receive age-appropriate vaccines prior to adoption.
