Submit an adoption application a t socialteesnyc.org Their foster says: "Potato is an absolute sweetheart and everyone who meets him instantly falls in love with him. Small in stature but big in personality, this little guy is the ultimate cuddle bug and best friend. He loves to burrow, take naps, and accompany his human with everything they do. Not afraid of other dogs or city noises, he mostly keeps to himself on walks and will let other dogs sniff him, but doesn't pay them much attention. Potato loves positive praise (he can definitely tell when he's getting a compliment), and wants to be close by at all times. The perfect dog for someone working from home, he often watches over me while I'm in meetings, in between his nap schedule. He will sleep in as long as I let him, and can be bribed to come out from under the covers when he hears the can of wet food opening. Potato is easy to adore and will make the best addition to anyone's family!" Most of our dogs come via partner groups who help us save them from shelters where they are at risk of euthanasia in Tennessee, Los Angeles, and Puerto Rico. Rescue groups like ours bring them to the northeast where the desire for pets like these allows us to save their lives.
