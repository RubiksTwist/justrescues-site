Meet Winnie!

Winnie is a beautiful 13-year-old gray and white Domestic Shorthair who is looking for a quiet, loving home where she can enjoy her golden years in comfort and peace.

Winnie is a calm, low-key girl who enjoys a good balance of lounging and playtime. She loves napping, playing with toys, and being near her people—especially adults and seniors who appreciate a relaxed companion. She’s also a big fan of treats and is very food-motivated, though she’s polite and not an over-eater

Affection with Winnie is very much on her terms. She enjoys gentle scratches on her head and under her chin and will happily purr while being petted—but she will let you know when she’s had enough. Because of this, Winnie would do best with cat-savvy adults who respect feline boundaries.

Winnie has never been outside (except for vet visits), is litter box trained, She prefers to live out her golden years as the queen of her castle in a home without other animals or young children. She would thrive a calm household where her space and routine are respected.

If you’re looking for a sweet, independent senior kitty who enjoys companionship without being overly demanding, Winnie could be a wonderful match

Please note: This is a courtesy post. Interested adopters should email us, and we will forward inquiries directly to Winnie’s owners.
