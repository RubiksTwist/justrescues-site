Introducing Salem! She is a 3-year-old, 35-pound Lab mix with a heart as big as her sweet, incredible smile. This gentle girl is all about love and togetherness—whether that means snuggling up on the couch, curling beside you during a sunny park picnic, or tagging along to a coffee shop hang with friends. She’s affectionate, calm, and incredibly loving, the kind of dog who makes every moment feel a little warmer just by being there. Salem gets along wonderfully with other dogs and cats, making her an easy fit for many homes. Truly, she’s one of those once-in-a-lifetime dogs whose sweetness you feel the moment you meet her—and that smile? It’ll melt your heart in under a minute. Salem was surrendered to our sister shelter in Puerto Rico along with her mom, and they are both now ready to start their next chapter. Salem is patiently waiting for a forever home where she can give all the love she has to offer—and she has so much to give. Inquire about her today!

Salem is spayed, microchipped, and up to date on her vaccinations. For more information about her, please fill out an application at www.ALRcares.com and email Julie@alrcares.com. We schedule meet and greets for approved adopters only, an application is the first step in our approval process. Thank you!
