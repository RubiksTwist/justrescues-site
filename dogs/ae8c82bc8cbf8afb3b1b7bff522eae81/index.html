Description provided by PupStarz Rescue

Give a warm welcome to April, a 4-month old, 3-pound brown tabby sweetheart who goes bananas over toys and loves to climb all over her cat tree. Playful? You betcha!

Although still somewhat shy, April gets along beautifully with kids, cats, and adults. If you’re sitting on the sofa, she will eventually make her way over to you and plop herself right down. The minute you start petting her, her body relaxes, her eyes close, and you’ll hear the loveliest-sounding purr everrrr.

April has a healthy appetite, she gobbles up Churu treats like there’s no tomorrow, and she neatly and reliably takes care of her bathroom business in her litter box. When April is feeling spunky, she is energetic and kitten-like. When she wants to chill out, she finds herself a cozy spot to relax in. Pretty predictable, ehh?

April is a super attractive kitty with a heart of pure gold and a coat full of stripes. All she needs is a home of her very own and a human she can count on.

If you are interested in adopting April, comment ADOPT and we’ll send you an application.
