Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Bobbin is a very special little puppy who has already figured out that doing things differently is no reason to miss out on the fun. She has some coordination challenges believed to stem from a non-degenerative neurological condition known as caudal fossa, so she moves through the world in her own unique way.

She’s happy, curious, and full of puppy personality. Bobbin may be a little unsteady on her feet, but she’s already adapting in some pretty remarkable ways and clearly isn’t letting her quirks get in the way of enjoying life.

She’ll need a patient, committed adopter who understands that she may always do some things a little differently and is ready to support her as she grows. Bobbin isn’t looking for sympathy. She’s looking for someone who will appreciate exactly who she is and give her the chance to keep thriving.

Registered adopters interested in Bobbin can email adopt@hbrdogs.com for more info. If you’re not registered yet, do it today at hbrdogs.com!
