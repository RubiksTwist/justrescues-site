Meet Buttons!

Buttons is a little girl with a big future ahead of her!

Rescued from Georgia, this sweet puppy is ready to leave her difficult beginning behind and start a life filled with love, fun, and family. She's looking for a forever home where she can grow, learn, and experience all the wonderful things puppyhood should bring.

Buttons will need a family who understands that puppies are a commitment and is ready to guide her with patience and positive training. In exchange, you'll get to watch this little girl grow into the amazing dog she's meant to be—and there's nothing more rewarding than that.

Buttons is ready for her very own person, her very own couch, and most importantly, her very own forever family.

Could you be the happy ending she's been waiting for?

Want to learn more? We'd love to tell you! Our bios are just a snapshot, so please reach out to our adoption team with any questions about this dog's personality, medical history, or ideal home. We'd be happy to help you determine if Buttons is the perfect match for your family.
