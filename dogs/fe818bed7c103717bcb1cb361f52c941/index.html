Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Tom Nook is a 7-year-old, 67-pound gentleman that has been auditioning for the role of "World's Most Perfect Dog" and frankly, we're tired of the casting directors sleeping on him.
Tom walked into foster care and immediately chose peace. No drama, no chaos, no "who chewed the couch cushion" energy — just pure, tail-wagging, soul-warming vibes from day one. If Tom Nook were a person, he'd be the friend who shows up with snacks, asks how you're really doing, and never once checks his phone.

The Daily Vibe: Tom's routine is beautifully low-maintenance: he sleeps in until you're ready (yes, a dog who respects the snooze button), enjoys a morning walk to check his pee-mails, and happily holds down the fort while you handle your human responsibilities. No separation anxiety. No furniture casualties. Just a gentleman lounging on the couch, dreaming about — you guessed it — balls. Soccer balls, tennis balls, basketballs — if it's round, Tom has already written it a love letter. His dedication to fetch and keep-away is honestly aspirational.
Evenings are dealer's choice: a long walk where he takes in the scents like a sommelier at a wine tasting, a spirited game of tug (reigning champion, undefeated), or just vibing on the couch while you binge your shows. He's not picky. He's just happy to be included.

City Dog, Country Heart: A native New Yorker, Tom handles city streets and bustle like a pro. But would he say no to a yard? A hiking trail? A grassy field where he can sprint after a ball with reckless joy? Absolutely not. Tom's not here to judge your square footage — he's here to judge whether you're going to throw that ball or not. On walks, Tom is easygoing with solid loose-leash manners. He's still working on his cool when bikes or other dogs pass by — picture "excited fan at a concert".

The Kid-Tested Seal of Excellence: Tom is currently fostered with a 3-year-old human toddler and has been nothing short of extraordinary. Patient. Gentle. Loving. He leaves her toys alone — a level of restraint most adults can't claim around a cheese board. Strangers? Tom has never met one. Guests? The more the merrier. This is a zero-stranger-danger, maximum-love-output kind of dog.

The Bottom Line: Tom is fully housetrained, non-destructive, knows his sit and down, walks beautifully on a leash, and is neutered with a clean bill of health. He is, in every sense, a turnkey companion — the kind of dog you bring home and wonder how you ever lived without.

If you're a busy family with love to spare, an active couple looking for a hiking buddy who also excels at couch time, or someone who's been scrolling profiles waiting for the one that makes you laugh and tear up — Tom Nook has been waiting for you.
