Meet Tilly!

Tilly is the sweetest girl who would be perfectly happy spending her days right by your side. Her favorite thing is simply being with her person. Whether you’re relaxing on the couch, working from home, or getting ready for bed, Tilly is happiest when she can curl up nearby and keep you company.

Tilly is extremely easygoing. She is friendly when meeting new people and is non reactive to other dogs on walks (just wants to sniff everyone and everything!) making her a lovely companion for getting out and exploring. She absolutely loves a long walk and enjoys taking her time to sniff and see the world, but once she gets home, she is more than ready to settle in for a cozy afternoon snooze.

At home, Tilly is all about comfort and cuddles. Give her a spot on the couch and a person to snuggle with, and she is in heaven. She is not much of a toy girl, so you probably will not find her demanding that you play fetch for hours. She would much rather lounge around, nap, and pretend to be a lap dog.

Tilly is not a food hound, but she can be a little picky about what she considers worthy of a treat, she does have one very important weakness: cheese. Cheese will definitely get her attention!

And perhaps one of the best things about Tilly is how quiet she is. She never barks and is just an incredibly calm, sweet presence to have around.

Tilly is 5 years old, microchipped, and up to date on all of her vaccinations.

Tilly does not need much to be happy. A nice long walk, a cozy couch, some cuddles, and most importantly, her person nearby are pretty much her idea of the perfect day. She is the kind of girl who will quietly follow you around the house just because she wants to be wherever you are.

If you are looking for a gentle, easygoing companion who would love nothing more than to be your little shadow and snuggle up beside you, Tilly could be your perfect match.
