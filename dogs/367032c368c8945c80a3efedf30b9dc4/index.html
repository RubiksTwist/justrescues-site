Description provided by AMA Animal Rescue

Meet Wiggles! A little puppy with a BIG personality!

Wiggles may see the world a little differently, but that certainly doesn’t stop him from enjoying it! Born without both eyes, Wiggles is permanently blind, but he has learned to navigate his surroundings remarkably well with a little patience and the right accommodations.

He also has a congenital neurological condition that affects his balance and coordination, which means this adorable boy can be a bit wobbly on his feet. He may tip over, bump into things, or take a few extra moments to figure things out but Wiggles just keeps going! And honestly, those little wiggles and tumbles are part of what makes him so incredibly lovable.

Wiggles is sweet, playful, curious, and absolutely adorable. He has so much joy to share and doesn’t let his challenges define him. He’s looking for a special family who will give him the patience, understanding, and safe environment he needs to thrive.

This little guy may need some extra accommodations, but he has so much love and puppy fun to give in return.

If you’re looking for a truly special pup who will wiggle his way straight into your heart, Wiggles might just be your perfect match!
