Meet Kellan.

Kellan is the kind of dog who would score a perfect 100 on the Buddy Meter! He’s the bestest boy: sweet, affectionate, and always eager to show his people just how much love he has to give.

Kellan is happiest when he’s with his humans and is ready to become someone’s loyal companion and best friend. He has so much love to give and is just waiting for a family who will give him the same in return.

If you’re looking for a sweet, loving buddy to be by your side, Kellan may just be your perfect match!
