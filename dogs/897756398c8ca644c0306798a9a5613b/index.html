Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

What about Bob? Well, we're glad you asked! At just 5 months old and 18.4 pounds, Bob is a lovable little terrier mix who believes every day should be filled with fun and exploration.

Whether he's heading out for a walk, taking in new sights and smells, or playing with his favorite people, Bob approaches everything with endless enthusiasm. He's an active, curious pup who loves having things to do, and he'd make a fantastic match for someone who enjoys getting outside and is up for an adventure.

Bob also knows the importance of quality time with his loved ones. If you're heading to the kitchen, Bob would be happy to accompany you. Moving to the living room? What a coincidence, he was headed there too! He loves cuddling just as much as he loves playing. When meeting new people, he may have a few things to say at first, but he warms up quickly and is happy to make new friends.

Bob is also working on his house-training and continuing to practice his leash skills. He's still a young pup with lots to learn, and he's looking for a family excited to help him along the way.

Registered adopters interested in Bob can email adopt@hbrdogs.com for more information. Not approved yet? Apply today at hbrdogs.com!
