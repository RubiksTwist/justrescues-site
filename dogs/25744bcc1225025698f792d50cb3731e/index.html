Meet me! 🐾

Hi! I’m a 13-week-old little sweetheart with a big personality just waiting to shine. I may be small, but I’m one smart cookie! I’m learning new skills and tricks quickly, eager to please, and discovering just how much fun the world can be.

My favorite things include cozy blankets, treats like frozen blueberries in my Kong , and chewing my toys. But don’t let my love of lounging fool you! I also love to play, run around, explore, and show off my playful side. I’m still figuring out this whole game of fetch thing, but I’m having fun learning!

I’m a sweet, gentle girl with a gorgeous face, beautiful eyes, and an affectionate personality that’s pretty hard to resist. I’m the perfect combination of playful and cuddly — happy to join you for an adventure or spend the afternoon curled up under a blanket.

I’m still a puppy with lots to learn and so much love to give. I’m looking for a family who will help me grow, keep teaching me new things, and give me all the snuggles I could ever want.

Could I be your new best friend? ❤️

ALL adoptions require a completed application and final contract. The application can be found at WaldosRescue.org and once you are approved to adopt you will be put in touch with the foster to see if it’s a good match. We are a RESCUE not a shelter. We conduct veterinary and personal reference checks during the application approval process. We are unable to contact every applicant for every dog. We will select the best applicant for each dog, based on the dogs needs. Waldos Rescue Pen is a 501c3 whose primary focus is rescuing dogs from the euthanasia list from high kill shelters throughout the United States and Korea.
