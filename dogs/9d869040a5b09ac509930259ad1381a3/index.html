Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Meet Mystic! Proof that age is just a number and the best things in life come with a little extra wisdom. ❤️🐾

At a perfect 39 pounds and 8 years old, she's the ideal companion: sweet, easygoing, and equally happy joining you for a walk or spending the afternoon curled up at home.

Mystic really is a dream houseguest. She's fully house-trained (and familiar with wee-wee pads), does wonderfully when left home alone, and has no interest in chewing, getting into trouble, or making a mess. Her preferred activity while you're away? Napping, of course! She's also a quiet girl who doesn't bark or whine much, making her a wonderful fit for apartment living.

She's a social girl, too! Mystic has lived with three dogs of different sizes and is dog-friendly. She's also lived with cats and children, so she's no stranger to a busy household. She's comfortable navigating apartment stairs and enjoys getting out for 2–3 walks a day. She's not asking for marathon adventures, just a chance to stretch her legs, get some fresh air, and enjoy the world with her person.

Mystic can be a little shy when you first meet her, but give her a moment to warm up and you'll quickly see what a special girl she is. Once she's comfortable, her sweet and affectionate personality shines through.

And now for one of her most important qualities: Mystic is NOT an early riser! If your ideal morning involves sleeping in, enjoying a leisurely cup of coffee, and easing into the day before heading out for a walk, Mystic is absolutely your girl. ☕️

Whether you live in a city apartment or a home in the suburbs, Mystic is ready to fit right into your routine. She's past the puppy chaos and has mastered the art of being a wonderful companion. All she really wants is a cozy place to call home, a few daily walks, and someone to share life's simple pleasures with.

If you're looking for a sweet, low-key companion who loves a good walk but appreciates a lazy morning just as much, Mystic might be your perfect match! ❤️
