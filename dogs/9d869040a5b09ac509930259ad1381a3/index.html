Meet Mystic! Your new best friend and queen of cozy living. ❤️🐾

This sweet 12-year-old, 39-pound girl recently made her way into foster care with Every Last One Rescue, and we couldn’t be happier to have her here. Now, she’s looking for a forever home where she can spend her golden years surrounded by all the love and comfort she deserves.

Mystic is the best of both worlds: a loving, affectionate companion who wants to be wherever her people are, and an independent girl who appreciates her downtime. She’s friendly, outgoing, and still has a playful side, but she’s equally happy curling up on the couch and enjoying a peaceful afternoon. She’s mastered the art of balance!

When it’s time to get out and about, Mystic enjoys one or two walks a day and walks nicely on a leash. She’s also crate-trained and quite the clever girl! She knows sit, stay, paw, and lay down—and she even knows how to get into the bathtub. Add in her love of showing off her belly, giving kisses, and never turning down a snack, and you’ve got one pretty irresistible companion.

Mystic has so much love left to give, and she deserves a home that will cherish her just as much as she’ll cherish them. Whether you’re looking for a walking buddy, a couch companion, or a sweet soul to keep you company through life’s quieter moments, Mystic is ready to fill that role.

Senior dogs have a special kind of magic, and Mystic is no exception. She’s not asking for much—just a soft place to land, a few extra treats, plenty of affection, and people who will remind her every day how loved she is.

Could Mystic be the missing piece of your home? She’s ready to make your life a little sweeter. ❤️
