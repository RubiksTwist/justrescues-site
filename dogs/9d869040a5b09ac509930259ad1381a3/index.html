Meet Mystic

This sweet 12 year old, 39-pound girl recently made her way into foster care with Every Last One Rescue, and we’re so happy she’s here.

Mystic is a loving, affectionate girl who enjoys being wherever her people are. She’s friendly, outgoing, playful, and also a certified couch potato who appreciates her independence and a quiet place to relax. Basically, she’s got the balance down.

She enjoys 1–2 walks a day, walks nicely on leash, and is crate-trained. She’s also a smart girl who knows sit, stay, paw, lay down, and even how to get in the bathtub! She loves showing off her belly, giving kisses for attention, and, of course, snack time.

Mystic has so much love to give and is ready to spend her golden years surrounded by comfort, kindness, and people who adore her!
