Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Meet Chanel No. 5! 💕 Chanel recently entered foster care with Every Last One Rescue, and we can confidently say she's an absolute LOVE. At 4–5 years old and 47 lbs, this sweet girl is the perfect mix of affectionate, easygoing, and just plain adorable.

Chanel is a total lover girl. She adores people, loves giving kisses, and would happily spend her entire day pressed up against you getting pets. She's gentle, incredibly tolerant of handling, and just wants to be included in whatever you're doing. Whether it's an adventure around the city or a lazy afternoon on the couch, Chanel is happy to be along for the ride.

Chanel is a medium to lower energy level girl who is happy to relax or play! Chanel has fun little bursts of energy but knows how to settle beautifully. She recently spent an afternoon exploring the busy Staten Island Mall, where she was an absolute superstar! Crowds, screaming kids, elevators, busy stores — nothing seemed to faze her. She happily relaxed in the middle of stores, soaked up attention from everyone who stopped to pet her!

Chanel is wonderful with adults and children. She comfortable being handled from head to toe, takes treats ever so gently, and rides beautifully in the car (although she'd much prefer to be your copilot!). Chanel is house trained and crate trained.

What makes Chanel so special is how effortlessly she seems to fit into whatever is happening around her. She's affectionate without being overwhelming, playful without being overly energetic, and always ready to lean in for a little extra love!

She's a lovely walking companion who generally walks on a loose leash, although squirrels and small dogs can catch her attention, but she keeps on walking!

Chanel No. 5 is ready for a family who will love her as much as she'll love them. And trust us, that's going to be a whole lot of love!
