Meet Biscoff!
Biscoff is a sweet, wiggly girl who loves saying hello to people! Found as a stray, she's still a bit of a mystery, but she's already shown us her social side by happily approaching her handlers and accepting tasty treats. She's got plenty of enthusiasm to share and is ready to find a family who will help her feel safe, loved, and right at home. Fair warning: this little cookie may be extra sweet!

My Ideal Home
Biscoff would love an active home where she can enjoy daily walks, playtime, and fun adventures with her people. Food puzzles, long lasting chews, and little games will help keep her busy and give her plenty of positive outlets for her energy. She'll also benefit from patient, reward based training to help her practice calmer greetings and learn some new skills.

Good with Other Pets?
Biscoff's history with other animals is unknown, but she showed no reaction to other dogs while walking at ACC. Any introductions with resident pets should be slow and thoughtful, giving everyone time to get comfortable.

Good with Kids?
Biscoff would do best in a home without children under 5. She's an enthusiastic greeter who can be a little unsure in new situations, so she'll appreciate a family who can help her feel comfortable while teaching her to keep all four paws on the floor.

Things to Know
Biscoff is a social girl who can get extra bouncy when she's excited to see someone. She can also feel unsure when she's encouraged to do something before she's ready, so it's important to let her approach new people and experiences at her own pace. With gentle encouragement, tasty rewards, and positive reinforcement, Biscoff can work on her manners while building confidence. This sweet cookie is ready to find the home where she can feel safe and let her personality shine!
