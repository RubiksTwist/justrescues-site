AGE / WEIGHT: 3.5 years old / 72 lbs

GENDER: Male

ENERGY: Moderate

GOOD WITH: Dogs - Yes, Kids - Yes, Cats - Yes

ABOUT ME: Couch? Bed? Stairs? I take them all with a running start, because that’s just more fun. I’m a big, mellow, affectionate guy who loves everyone: people, kids, dogs, and cats (I grew up with three, and one of them liked to boss me around). With dog friends I play loud and rowdy; at home I’m all cuddles. Toys aren’t really my thing. You are.

MY FOSTER SAYS: “Sometimes I walk him with another foster who has a habit of randomly barking at people. Whenever she does, he starts barking at her, almost like he’s telling her to stop being so dramatic.”

HISTORY

Prince Zuko lived in one home from the age of eight weeks. When his owner experienced housing instability and could no longer keep her dogs safely, Zuko was surrendered in June 2026, along with another dog who had previously been adopted through the rescue.

DETAILED PROFILE

Prince Zuko is a big, gentle goofball with a talent for making people fall hard. His foster calls him the best and easiest foster yet, and the evidence keeps piling up. He launches onto the couch, the bed, or up the stairs with a running start, practically dances when it’s walk time, and gets more snuggly by the week.

His energy runs low to moderate: mostly a cuddle-and-relax guy with the occasional burst of play. Toys and fetch don’t do much for him; he wants to be included in whatever you’re doing. He rarely barks, though he once barked right back at a noisy dog friend on a walk, as if telling her to tone it down.

He loves people and greets everyone with a wagging tail. Kids are a big yes, sometimes a little too big: he may lick and jump at first before calming down, so little ones should be prepared. With dogs he’s neutral on the street and rough, rowdy, and vocal in play off leash. He grew up with three cats and is calm around them. A dog sibling would be a bonus, not a requirement.

Prince Zuko is house trained, fine in a crate, and in foster has been trusted loose at home, where he mostly naps. He walks beautifully on leash, though he becomes a bit dramatic when encountering textured pavement: he crouches low, tiptoes up, then hustles past. He nails his cues at home but can lose them when overstimulated somewhere new.

Toys are optional. A spot next to you on the couch is not

MEDICAL HISTORY

The dogs in our care are up-to-date on vaccinations, spayed or neutered (when old enough), microchipped, and given a general health check. Immediate medical needs and medical history will be addressed with the adopter prior to adoption. Complete vet records will be sent shortly after adoption.
