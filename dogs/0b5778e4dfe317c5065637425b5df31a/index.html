🐾 Meet Burberry! 🐾

Burberry is a young, energetic, silly, and wonderfully goofy guy weighing in at 87 lbs! 💙 This big boy is a total love bug. He may need a moment to warm up, but once he feels comfortable, he'll be leaning into pets, soaking up attention, and rolling over for belly rubs.

If you're a big-dog lover, Burberry could be the perfect match! He's got a huge heart to match his size and plenty of personality to keep you smiling.

Burberry does have a limp that we're currently monitoring, and he may need follow-up veterinary care. His future family should be prepared to continue supporting him as needed.

If you have room in your heart-and home-for a big, goofy, affectionate guy, Burberry is ready to meet you! 🐾💙
