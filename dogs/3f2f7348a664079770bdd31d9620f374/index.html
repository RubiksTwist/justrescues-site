Meet Kobe!

Kobe is a fun-loving, affectionate guy who has unfortunately found himself back at ACC after first coming through our doors as a puppy. Most recently, he was found roaming loose on his own looking for help, and now he's hoping his next home will truly be forever. Kobe is full of personality, absolutely adores people, and never says no to cuddles, treats, or a chance to learn something new.

My Ideal Home

Kobe is looking for an experienced, active adopter who can continue building on the training he's already started. He's incredibly food-motivated, eager to learn, and has made great progress with cues like "sit," "off," and "leave it." He'll thrive with someone who enjoys reward-based training and can provide plenty of exercise, enrichment, and structure to help him channel his enthusiasm.

Good with Other Pets?

Kobe previously lived with other pets. At the shelter, he enjoys meeting other dogs but can become a little overexcited and sometimes struggles to read when another dog wants space. Slow introductions are recommended, and ACC advises against dog parks so he can build appropriate relationships at his own pace.

Good with Kids?

Although Kobe previously lived with children and was described as wonderful with them, his current shelter behavior shows he's still working on his impulse control. Because of his jumping, mouthiness, and resource guarding, ACC recommends an adult-only home where he can continue his training successfully.

Things to Know

Kobe is an energetic, affectionate dog who loves toys, treats, and being close to his people. He's currently enrolled in ACC's Shelter Scholar program, where he's making fantastic progress learning new skills, including relaxing on a mat and settling himself. He's highly responsive to positive reinforcement and continues to improve his leash manners every day. Like many shelter dogs, Kobe can feel anxious in the kennel environment, but with patience, consistency, and continued training, he's already showing just how much he's capable of learning.
