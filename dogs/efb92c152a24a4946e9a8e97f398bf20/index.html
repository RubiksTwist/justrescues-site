Meet Chocolate Elmhurst!
At just 9 months old, Chocolate Elmhurst is still a little puppy figuring out this big, wonderful world! This handsome boy came to ACC as a stray, so we're still getting to know all the little things that make him special. He's a curious explorer who likes to check out new things at his own pace, and he can be a little shy when the world gets too busy. Give him some time, a cozy place to settle in, and a few tasty treats, and you'll be well on your way to winning his heart. He's got so much puppy potential just waiting to bloom!

My Ideal Home
Chocolate Elmhurst would love a calm, patient home where he can take his time settling in and learn that the world isn't such a scary place after all. He's still a young pup with lots to learn, so a family who enjoys teaching new skills with treats, praise, and plenty of encouragement would be just his cup of cocoa. Let him set the pace, celebrate his little victories, and watch his confidence grow!

Good with Other Pets?
Chocolate Elmhurst came to us as a stray, so his history with other dogs is a mystery. During a recent introduction, he preferred sticking close to his human friends rather than mingling with the other pup. We'll need to learn more about his preferences, so any future introductions should be slow and gentle.

Good with Kids?
We recommend a home without children under 5 for now. Chocolate Elmhurst can be a little unsure when people move around unexpectedly, so a quieter home with patient family members who understand when he needs some space will help him feel safe.

Things to Know
Like many puppies, Chocolate Elmhurst is still working on his manners! He can get excited and jump up to say hello, and sometimes those tasty treats are just too tempting to take gently. He's ready to learn cues like Sit, Stay, Down, and Leave It, along with practicing gentle greetings. He's also a sensitive little guy, so it's important to let him approach new people and things on his own terms. No rushing, no pressure, just plenty of patience and positive reinforcement. Chocolate Elmhurst may be a little shy at first, but with the right person by his side, we're excited to see him come out of his shell!
