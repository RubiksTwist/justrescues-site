Meet Kory

Kory is a sweet pup who has already had to learn a little too much about navigating life on his own.

He was being offered for rehoming on Facebook in Alabama, and thankfully, his story brought him to us before he was left to face an uncertain future. We may not know everything about what Kory has been through, but we do know that this sweet boy deserves the chance to finally feel safe and loved.

Kory has had to figure things out along the way, but despite whatever challenges he has faced, his sweet nature has remained. He is a lovable pup who is ready to discover what it feels like to have people he can count on.

Now we want to give Kory the opportunity to stop worrying about where he belongs and start learning what it means to be home.

He deserves a family who will be patient with him, help him continue learning, and give him all the love and reassurance he needs. In return, Kory will give them something truly special: the love of a sweet dog who is simply grateful to have someone by his side.

Please consider opening your heart and home to Kory. He has already had to navigate so much, and now he deserves a family who will help him feel safe, cherished, and finally home.
