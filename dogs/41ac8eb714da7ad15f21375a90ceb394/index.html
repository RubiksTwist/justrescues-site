Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Some dogs, like Rain, force you to remember why they are called mans bestfriend.

At 2 years old and a slim and statuesque 50 lbs, Rain is the epitome of sensitive and soulful. Rain is the a girl who walks in a room and her aura exudes magnificence.

Rain's amber eyes are a portal to her soul and when you lock eyes with her it's an instaneous meeting of the minds and hearts.

Rain is a true partnership in the relationships with her humans and doesn't mind shifting between being the one who takes care of her humans and being the one who allows herself to be cared for. Since arriving in fostere care, Rain has experienced the soft girl life all dogs deserve. Rain quickly realized that people are safe and now never hesistates to show off her soft and wiggly body - she'll even smother you in kisses if you let her.

Rain truly lets loose however, when she is training for treats and playing with her toys. Rain has a rolodex of basic commands and cues mastered, she voluntarily hangs in her crate throughout the day and is actively learning that her crate can also be a fun place overnight.

Rain is a pleasure to walk on leash and can always be found along side you on a beautiful loose leash. Rain is non reactive to other dogs, sounds or stimuli allthough she does fancy a street rat or a stray cat.

Rain is an amazing dog and she will absolutely thrive with an adopter that values her being both emotional and logical, also being happy to provide her with as much love, joy and safety as she will provide to them.
