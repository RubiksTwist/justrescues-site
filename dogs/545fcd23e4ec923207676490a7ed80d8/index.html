Description provided by Waggytail Rescue

Meet Pablo, an 11-pound package of pure sweetness ready to steal your heart and take NYC by storm! At just 2 years old, this charming little guy is looking for a fresh start, a warm lap, and a family to call his own.

Pablo recently found himself at a Texas shelter, as a stray with little known history, and has been receiving the TLC he deserves. Thanks to our amazing Texas Rescue Partner, Operation Kindness, and a generous flight chaperone, Beni is trading the Lone Star State for the Big Apple and flying high toward a bright new future!

*Note: Pablo will likely be arriving to LaGuardia Airport (LGA). Date and time are TBD and dependent on flight/chaperone availability.*

With his gorgeous tan-and-cream coat and Shih Tzu charm, Pablo is 100% irresistible. Medically he is healthy and recovered from a recent bout of a doggie cold, but he will need eye drops daily for tear production due to dry eyes. At a petite 11 lbs, he is the ultimate compact companion, perfectly sized for NYC apartment living, cozy couch snuggles, and strolls through the neighborhood.

Pablo has so much love to give and is completely turn-key ready to bring endless joy, soft head-tilts, and happy tail wags to a lucky individual or family. Whether you are interested in opening your heart to foster or offering him a forever home, Pablo is ready to meet you!

Want to welcome Pablo to NYC? Apply to foster or foster-to-adopt him today!
