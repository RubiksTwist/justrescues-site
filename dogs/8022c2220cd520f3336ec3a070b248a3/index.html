Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

This little lady is HOPING you’ll fall in love with her! Cute as a button and sweet as can be, Hope came to ARF when her owner could no longer care for her.

Hope can be a little reserved when she first meets you, but give her a little time and she’ll be right in your lap, soaking up all the snuggles she can get. She enjoys the company of other dogs, though she may do best with canine friends around her own size.

Because of her petite size and quiet personality, Hope would do best in a home with older children.

If your idea of the perfect day is hanging out with your best friend, curling up with a good book, and watching TV together, Hope is your girl!
