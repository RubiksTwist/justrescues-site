Description provided by PupStarz Rescue

Meet Albert! A 5.5-year-old, 69-pound Pittie mix with a big personality, an even bigger heart, and plenty of love to give. Albert is a people-loving, happy, wiggly guy who absolutely adores his humans. He's affectionate, playful, and always up for an adventure. Walks, hikes, exploring the outdoors, or simply spending an afternoon curled up on the couch are all right up his alley!

Albert has a lot of wonderful qualities. He's fully house-trained, crate-trained, walks nicely on leash (although he may occasionally pull), and knows several commands, including sit, down, come, paw, and speak. He's treat-motivated, eager to learn, and has pretty impressive recall. He also travels well, rarely barks, and is a very easygoing companion around the house.

Now for what Albert needs in his next home. Albert has struggled with reactivity and aggression toward other dogs, including incidents with the dog in his current home. He does not do well with on-leash greetings and should not have interactions with unfamiliar dogs. While he may occasionally tolerate very submissive or smaller dogs, his ideal home would be one where he can be the only dog and doesn't have to share his space.

Albert has also shown nervousness around young children, including snapping when startled by a toddler who approached him from behind. For that reason, a home without children is best. His experience with cats is unknown.

What Albert does want is pretty simple: a person or family who will give him love, consistency, and a place to call his own. He's a fun, energetic adventure buddy who also knows how to settle in for a lazy afternoon. He loves treats, toys, belly rubs, and being close to his people.

Albert isn't looking for a home with a packed social calendar or canine siblings. He's looking for someone who appreciates all the wonderful things about him and understands the importance of setting him up for success.

If you're looking for a loyal, affectionate companion who wants to be your one and only, Albert might just be your guy!

Albert is a courtesy post, if interested in giving him a forever home please send us an email and we'll connect you to his owners.
