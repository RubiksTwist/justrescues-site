Meet Myles!
Myles is a friendly, affectionate young guy who loves being around people and soaking up attention! After his owner could no longer care for him, this sweet boy found himself looking for a new family and a fresh start. He's had quite a journey lately, but he's still the same social pup who enjoys making friends and spending time with his favorite people. Myles is ready to find someone who will help him settle into his next chapter and show him just how loved he is.

My Ideal Home
Myles would love a home where he can enjoy quality time with his people, daily walks, and plenty of fun activities. He's a medium energy guy who knows how to enjoy himself, but he'll also benefit from some practice with leash manners and polite greetings. He's comfortable spending time in a crate, though he has had occasional accidents while crated, so regular bathroom breaks and a consistent routine will be helpful. Food puzzles, long lasting chews, and positive reinforcement training will give this clever young guy plenty of ways to learn and grow.

Good with Other Pets?
Myles has been described as playful with familiar dogs and tolerant of unfamiliar dogs on leash. He can get protective of his toys around other dogs and may growl if they try to take something he values, so he'll need careful supervision and management around shared toys. Slow introductions will be important, and he should have his own space to enjoy his favorite things in peace.

Good with Kids?
Myles has been described as social with children, but ACC recommends a home without children under 5 due to his history of guarding toys around other dogs and possible sensitivity with handling. Older family members should give him space when he's resting and allow him to feel comfortable at his own pace.

Things to Know
Myles came to ACC with a severe leg injury, and his leg was amputated at ACC. He's been recovering from surgery and was observed getting around comfortably on three legs. Our staff will discuss his recovery and any follow up care he may need when you meet him. Myles can also feel unsure when startled or held, so gentle handling and positive reinforcement will help him feel secure. This resilient young boy has already been through a lot, and now he's looking for a loving home where he can keep healing, learning, and enjoying life with his very own family.
