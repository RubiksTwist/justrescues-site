Meet Flicka!

Flicka is a sweet, playful girl who's ready to gallop right into your heart! Found as a stray, she's been making the most of her time at the shelter by exploring, making new friends, and soaking up attention. She may be a little shy when first entering a new space, but it doesn't take long for her to warm up and come looking for cuddles. Flicka is quite the social butterfly once she gets comfortable, and she especially loves a good game of chase!

My Ideal Home

Flicka would love an active family who can keep her busy with walks, playtime, and fun adventures. She's a bright, enthusiastic girl who would enjoy learning new tricks and practicing her manners with plenty of treats and encouragement. Food puzzles, hide-and-seek, and long-lasting chews would also be right up her alley!

Good with Other Pets?

Flicka has enjoyed meeting several dogs at the shelter and has shown us that she loves a good game of chase! She can get a little frustrated when meeting dogs on leash, so introductions should be slow and thoughtful. She may enjoy having a canine companion with a compatible play style, but a proper introduction is important.

Good with Kids?

Flicka would do best in a home with children over 5. She can be a little nervous when first meeting new people, but warms up quickly with some patience and gentle encouragement.

Things to Know

Flicka is still working on her manners and can get excited enough to jump up when greeting people. She's also learning to keep her paws on the ground and walk nicely on leash. With a little patience, positive reinforcement, and plenty of love, this sweet girl is sure to keep blossoming!
