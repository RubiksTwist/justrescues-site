Meet Esteban, your future best friend!

Esteban is a true survivor. This brave boy was initially adopted from the ACC, but unfortunately faced a heartbreaking situation shortly after when he was hit by a car and left without medical treatment for an entire week. Esteban was left alone, scared, and severely injured- not knowing what his fate would be. We stepped in to help and provided extensive medical treatment just hours before he was scheduled to be put down.

After enduring a long journey of recovery, with over two months of daily treatments and procedures, Esteban is now fully healed and ready to find his forever home! His strength and resilience are truly inspiring; he has shown us how much he loves life and being with people.

Esteban is full of energy and has a playful, hyper spirit that will keep you on your toes! He’s very friendly, and as some may say, the ultimate mush! He is looking for a family who will cherish him and give him all the love he deserves.

If you’re ready to welcome a loyal companion who has fought hard for a second chance, Esteban is waiting for you. Please fill out and submit an adoption application today.
