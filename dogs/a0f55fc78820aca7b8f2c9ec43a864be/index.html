Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Meet Princess Buttercup! A whole lot of love wrapped up in one very happy girl. 👑❤️🐾

Princess Buttercup is a true lover of all creatures, big and small! Whether it's humans, dogs, or even cats, this social butterfly is happiest when she's surrounded by friends. She's got a big heart, an even bigger personality, and enough love to go around for everyone she meets.

This girl has plenty of energy and is always ready for her next adventure. Long walks, outdoor exploring, playtime with her four-legged friends, or simply tagging along with her favorite person are all right up her alley. She would thrive with an active family or in a home with another playful companion to help keep her entertained.

Princess Buttercup is still learning how to manage all those BIG feelings when excitement takes over. Sometimes her enthusiasm gets the best of her, and she may jump up during play because she just can't contain herself! The good news? She's incredibly smart, eager to learn, and very food-motivated, making training a fun and rewarding experience.

She's also already got some wonderful manners under her belt! Princess Buttercup is fully crate-trained and potty-trained, and the hustle and bustle of city life doesn't bother her one bit. Traffic, crowds, and everyday city noises? No problem! She's just happy to be out exploring the world with her person.

At the end of the day, Princess Buttercup is looking for a family who can match her zest for life, continue helping her polish her manners, and give her all the love and adventures her heart desires.

If you're looking for a social, playful, smart-as-a-whip best friend who believes everyone is a potential new friend, Princess Buttercup is ready to steal your heart! 💕
