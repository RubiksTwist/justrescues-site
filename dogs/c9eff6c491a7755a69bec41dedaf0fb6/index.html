Larry is at risk due to behavioral concerns. Larry has a reported history of attempting to bite his finder, though no further details were provided. In the care center he was reported to lunge at strangers during walks, resulting in walks being restricted to the driveway/backyard. During care, Larry has displayed strong leash pulling and has occasionally fixated on other dogs with a forward posture and staring, though he has been redirectable. He is generally social with familiar handlers, accepts petting and treats, and responds to cues such as sit and paw. Medically, Larry was recently treated for CIRDC and is healthy at this time.

Larry was brought to MACC as a stray with limited information. Larry's finder stated that Larry attempted to bite them and that he is reactive to dogs. While in care, Larry has been observed to have an alert forward body when seeing other dogs while on leash. Due to the detailed behavior, we recommend an experienced adult-only home.
