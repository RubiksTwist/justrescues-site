Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Annette is a 2 year-old lab-mix weighing around 65 lbs, and she’s pure love from nose to tail. She’s sweet, trusting, and always down for some affection. Most of all, she just wants to be with her person.

If you’re looking for a dog park pal, Annette is your girl! She loves every dog she meets, big or small, and is always excited to say hi and play. She’s just as friendly with people, greeting everyone with the same happy, positive energy. Annette is crate-trained, does well on leash, and is making great progress on house training as she gets more comfortable in her new neighborhood. She’s also a big fan of food, so treats go a long way with her (and she’d love a little help brushing up on her table manners!).

Annette would be a great match for someone who wants a loyal, affectionate sidekick to share their days with. With a little consistency and lots of love, she’ll make the sweetest addition to any home.
