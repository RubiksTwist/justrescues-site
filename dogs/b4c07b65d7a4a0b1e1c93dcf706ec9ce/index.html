Meet Coco Fred

Coco Fred is a big, beautiful Lab with a heart just as big as his paws. His journey to rescue began in Texas, where he was saved from a heartbreaking hoarding situation outside a trailer park.

He was found confined to a small cage with nothing but newspapers beneath him for comfort. He was severely underweight and covered in fleas and bugs, living in conditions no dog should ever have to endure.

But Coco Fred’s past doesn’t define him. Despite everything he went through, he has remained an incredibly sweet, gentle, and loving boy. He’s intelligent, affectionate, and happiest when he can simply relax and enjoy the good things in life.

And Coco Fred has discovered two of his favorite things: water and the couch! Whether he’s enjoying a splash or stretching out for a well-deserved afternoon nap, this handsome boy knows how to appreciate the simple pleasures.

Now, Coco Fred is ready for the part of his story he’s been waiting for; a family who will love him, spoil him, and show him that he never has to worry about being forgotten again.
