A volunteer writes:
Goldiepot is a pretty and petite silvery lady who was brought to the care center with her offspring as their family moved to a place not allowing pets. No story was attached to their relinquishment... Goldiepot is a lively little gal, curious, welcoming and friendly who likes her cuddles, purrs loudly, makes biscuits and absolutely adores to play with catnip toys and feather wands. She has the greatest appetite and one wonders where she does "put" all this food! Although she seems otherwise healthy, Goldiepot had about a month ago a seizure like episode that never recurred. She was spayed under anesthesia without any problem. Lovely Goldiepot did very well on her behavior evaluation and is dreaming to go home with you. Come and meet her very soon at the Manhattan Care Center!
