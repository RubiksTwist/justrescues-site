Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

Hi everyone, my name is Daisy! I'd describe myself as a soft, gentle and quiet lady. I love nothing more than to cuddle up with my foster parents on the couch and receive lots of pets and kisses. I never make a sound, I happily put myself to bed in my crate and am very well trained to sit when asked. I respect my foster parent's space while they eat and love going for walks outside. I am friendly to new people and will happily let children pat me, but if there are too many people around or a busy street, I get a little nervous and hide behind my foster parents. I don't bark when I'm left home alone. To be honest, I'm not even sure I know how to bark or what my voice sounds like. But one thing I do know, I am my happiest while stealing body heat and receiving lots of pets.

﻿Daisy is approximately 7y 5m 5d old and is fully grown at 20 pounds. They are microchipped and up to date on all age-appropriate medical care. Their breed is unknown as we have not had a DNA test done.
