Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

Hi, I’m Verity! My foster mom calls me a potato because I love lounging and am the calmest pup you’ll ever meet. I like to be around my human but can be a little shy to warm up to you. I enjoy getting pets and will try to lick your toes while you do so. While I don’t usually like to cuddle on you, I’m most affectionate in the morning or after a nap. I’ll run straight to you and give you kisses for as long as you pet and scratch me.

I enjoy wrestling with my brother but also enjoy playing by myself. I like to chew on bones and toys and solve treat puzzles while you work. I enjoy finding dark, cozy spots like under the couch to take naps throughout the day.

I’m a smart pup and am highly food motivated. I’ve already learned “sit,” “come,” and am working on “lay down,” “stay” and “place.” I’m house trained to pee and poo on the pad and crate trained as well.

I’m not allowed to walk outside yet but I enjoy being carried around the city in a tote. I never bark but like to look at birds while I’m out and about. I have the superpower of being able to fall asleep anywhere. I’ve ridden the subway and do well in a car, too.

I can’t wait to meet my forever family!
