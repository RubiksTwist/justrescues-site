Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Meet Pippa - a precious, eight-month-old, 39 lb pup whose pocket size is positively packed with playful curiousity and pure sweetness!

Upon first meet, Pippa is a reserved soul who has historically taken about an hour to realize everyone is meant to be her longtime friend. Once Pippa realizes you are friendly and safe she will eager seek out affection, happily smothering you with kisses, hugs and cosplaying as your shadow - curious about everything you do.

As a playful puppy, Pippa thoroughly enjoys her walk being initially excitable and then quickly settling in to a nice leash walker. In the home, Pippa is a lover of all things toys - happy to keep herself entertained by throwing her own stuffies in the air and catching them as they come back down. For interactive play, Pippa will never turn down a game of fetch.

In foster care, Pippa has quickly picked up both crate training and potty training and is well on her way to mastering both.

Outside of being initially cautious of new people, Pippa trusts with her whole heart and loves with her entire being. She is a love of women, men and children and other dogs!

Although small, the love Pippa packs is mighty and she exudes best friend vibes. Pippa would do wonderfuly in a home with someone who appreciates her sweet puppy spirit, curiosity, and willingness to learn as she settles as she continues to grow and mature in her forever life.
