Meet Erling, a playful little adventurer with a big personality and an even bigger love for snuggles! Erling is a 2-3 month old Shepehrd Mix who he loves running around, exploring everything he can find, and playing with his foster buddy Ozzy, but once he’s tired himself out, he’s more than happy to curl up close for a nap. Erling is curious and excited about the world around him. He loves meeting new people, soaking up attention, and has been wonderful with another dog in his foster home. He’s already getting the hang of puppy life, working on his wee wee pad training and learning basics like “sit” and “lay down.” He also sleeps comfortably in his crate. His ideal family will be ready for all the fun that comes with raising a puppy — plenty of playtime, continued training, new adventures and, most importantly, lots of love. Erling is ready to grow up with a family of his very own!
