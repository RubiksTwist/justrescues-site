Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

Meet Jackson, a gentle giant with an even bigger heart! This handsome man may be a little shy at first, but underneath that giant exterior is a total sweetheart just waiting to come out of his shell. Once comfortable, Jackson enjoys head butts, affection, and showing off his impressive motorboat purr. He's also quite the little entertainer, whether he's chasing toys, hunting imaginary bugs, or striking his signature sitting pose that his previous family lovingly described as looking like a forklift! Jackson appreciates having cozy places to retreat to and would love a quieter home with no young kids where he can settle in at his own pace. He's a gentle, lovable guy with plenty of personality, and he's hoping to find someone who will appreciate all the sweetness he has to offer!

A volunteer writes: Some personalities start shining the moment they walk into the room and others blend in until they find their group. I'm in the latter camp myself and I'm pretty sure Jackson is as well. His previous family said their favorite thing about him is how lovable he is and how his determination for pets comes across in some very heartfelt headbunting. Jackson is reportedly excellent at catching bugs and when he sits, he looks like an adorable little forklift. Though they describe sides of Jackson I haven't yet seen, I understand there's more to this little seal cat huddled up in the corner. Jackson is such a sweet guy but Care Center life is not his speed. He quickly explained that he wasn't looking for any pets and snuggled himself in the corner for emphasis. Jackson's hoping to find a loving and patient family to meet him exactly where he is.
