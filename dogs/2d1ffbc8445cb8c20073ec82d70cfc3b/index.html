Pre-Screener Form

Pop was surrendered to the care center by his previous owner due to his behavior towards a child in the home. In two instances, the most recent a year ago, Pop is reported to have bitten the child when they ran into a room where he was sleeping, in one case on the arm and in another the leg. In both cases, this was a bite and release that left small punctures and was treated at home. The owner otherwise describes Pop as an independent dog who is good on the leash and likes be where his people are, but is happy to nap or chew on a bone by himself. At the care center, in calmer environments and with time to warm up, Pop has shown social behavior with staff. Due to noted concerns displayed in a home environment, we recommend placement with a New Hope partner who can provide an experienced, adult-only foster home, a period of decompression, and any necessary behavior modification before placement into a permanent home.
