HISTORY

Lucy Liu was born into rescue in early 2021, after Badass rescued her pregnant mother, who gave birth in a New York City foster home. She was adopted as a young puppy and returned to rescue in October 2026 when her adopter’s circumstances changed and they could no longer care for her.

DETAILED PROFILE

Lucy Liu has a wardrobe, and she knows how to use it. Hold up an outfit and she’ll step right into it herself. For Halloween she went as a snail and collected attention all over the sidewalk. Off the runway, she’s a goofy, gentle, entertaining adult dog with moderate energy and a strong attachment to her people.

Her favorite game is tug-of-war, with whatever toy she can get her paws on. When she has energy to burn, she’ll bring you a toy to start a round or chase a ball, and once she’s worn out, she wants to snuggle. She’s smart and very treat motivated: she knows sit, down, and crate, and she’s working on place.

People are a big yes. She’s curious about everyone she meets on the street or in the elevator. When someone new comes into the home, she may bark at first, then settles within a few minutes and turns friendly. She’s been comfortable with kids she’s met outside the home. On walks she mostly minds her own business around other dogs but sometimes gets a little excited to say hi; her foster can’t picture her being anything but sweet with them. A dog sibling could work but isn’t required. Cats are unknown.

At home she’s close to perfect: house trained, and calm in her crate after a few minutes of settling. Walks are her big project. Busy streets frighten her, and she still freezes and pulls back hard once she reaches a certain point. She’s pulling less and getting a little farther from home every day, but she’ll need an adopter ready for patient, confidence-building leash work.

The park is five minutes away. She’s working on it, one block at a time, and she plans to arrive in style.
