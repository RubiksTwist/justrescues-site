ABOUT ME: After every bathroom break, I kick up dirt and grumble like a grizzly, because a job well done deserves a show. Indoors I’m a cuddle bug with a cushy crate mat, a pile of stuffies, and a window to watch. I just got here, so new faces get a slow once-over, and sidewalk dogs get a piece of my mind. Once I’ve picked my person, though, I’m a lapdog through and through.

MY FOSTER SAYS: “Backpack is a wonderful combination of sugar, some spice, and everything nice. I’d die for him.”

HISTORY

Backpack was found tied up outside our rescue partner’s offices in Dallas, Texas, freshly bathed and left with toys and treats. He was taken to a local shelter for medical care and traveled to Brooklyn in late October 2026.

DETAILED PROFILE

Backpack has a signature move. Every time he finishes his business outside, he kicks up dirt (or just air) while grumbling and growling like a grizzly defending his title. Indoors, the drama melts away. He’s a snuggly little guy who curls up with his people, loves a cushy crate mat, and recently discovered the joy of being wrapped in a blanket burrito and sleeping like a rock.

He’s affectionate and attached once he knows you, but he’s still new to Brooklyn and cautious with strangers, so he does best when new people let him make the first move. He’s particular about handling, too: baths, grooming, and even putting on his harness or clipping his leash can make him growly, wiggly, and mouthy (no biting). Chihuahua lovers, take note: he has the big personality, strong opinions, and world-class snuggle game you already know and love, and someone who gets the breed will read him easily. For now, we’re recommending a home without young children.

At home he’s easygoing with other animals. In an earlier foster home he lived with three dogs, ranging from unbothered to genuine friends, and he’d tell them to back off or take a break when play got rowdy. He passed a cat test and now lives with a cat. Out on the sidewalk it’s a different story: he can be loudly reactive to other dogs, even across the street, and to some passing people. He does better walking with a dog buddy and when he keeps moving.

He knows sit, place, stay, off, and come, and he’s eager to please. House training is close; the bigger project is his enthusiasm for leg-lifting on things like lobby furniture.

Squeaky-toy fetch and tug-of-war are his favorite games. Fair warning: the post-potty victory dance comes standard.

MEDICAL HISTORY

The dogs in our care are up-to-date on vaccinations, spayed or neutered (when old enough), microchipped, and given a general health check. Immediate medical needs and medical history will be addressed with the adopter prior to adoption. Complete vet records will be sent shortly after adoption.
