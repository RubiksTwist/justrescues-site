DETAILED PROFILE

Gene Parmesan is on the case. Every bird in Brooklyn is a person of interest, and he keeps a close eye on all of them. Indoors, though, he’s off duty and about as easy as roommates get. He stays off the furniture, doesn’t beg at the table, and his foster has yet to hear him bark.

He’s a gentle, friendly adult with moderate energy and a goofy side that’s just starting to show. He’s still decompressing after his move north, so expect more personality as he settles. He loves a good run in his foster’s fenced yard and zooms loops with pure joy. He’s still getting used to the sights and sounds of city walks, and leash manners are a work in progress.

New people take him a little time. He’s shy at first and doesn’t connect right away, but he warms up. He lives with another dog and mostly ignores it, so a canine sibling could work with the right match but isn’t required. His experience with kids and cats isn’t known yet, and given his strong interest in birds, any cat introduction would need care.

He’s crate trained and house trained. Aside from a little marking when he first arrived, there have been no accidents, though some regression is normal in a new home. He’s starting to learn his name and doesn’t know commands yet, but he isn’t breaking any rules either. His foster is working on helping him settle.

A quiet home and a patient person would suit him perfectly. The birds will just have to live with the attention.
