HISTORY

Gene Parmesan was found as a stray on a rural county road in Alabama; the person who found him pulled over, he ran to the car and climbed in on his own. He may have been a discarded hunting dog, though that is unconfirmed, and he arrived with a rough coat, poor skin, and an old, healed ear injury. He traveled from Selma to Brooklyn in late September 2026.

DETAILED PROFILE

Gene Parmesan has one great passion, and it has feathers. On walks and in the yard, his attention belongs to the birds. He can watch them in the backyard for ages, and on city sidewalks that focus turns into strong pulling. It looks like pure instinct, so his adopter should be ready to keep working on leash manners.

Indoors, he’s a gentle, friendly goof with a clumsy streak. He stays off the furniture, doesn’t beg, and plays a little with toys. He’s still learning to fully relax in a house, and his foster is helping him settle. His energy varies from day to day, so plan on regular outdoor time. He’s also a fan of water.

With people, Gene plays it cool. He doesn’t rush to greet strangers, and the birds often win his attention. When he does connect with someone, he leans in and rubs against them. He’s spent time around school-age and older kids with no concerns reported, though his experience is limited. Other dogs are where he lights up: he did very well in playgroups while boarding, plays happily with dogs at home, and loves a crowd of them. Cats haven’t been tested, and given how intently he tracks birds, any introduction should be careful.

He’s comfortable in his crate and hasn’t had accidents in the house, but he doesn’t ask to go out yet, so a regular potty schedule is a must. Walks can be so interesting that he forgets to go, and rain or cold may take some coaxing. He responds to his name, “come,” and “off,” and seems to know some field-style cues, but basic obedience training is still ahead.

Gene would thrive with a patient, training-minded adopter and, ideally, a dog friend to play with. Brooklyn’s pigeons remain under investigation.
