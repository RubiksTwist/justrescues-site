Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Do senior dogs have your heart? They have ours too!

Tourmie may look like a young pup with her bright brown coat and floppy ears but she is a well seasoned and sultry 10 year old lady.

With a decade of lived experience, Tourmie wants nothing more than to spend her days laying around leisurely, sitting on park benches with her humans, being invited to picnics and even having sleepovers with famil and friends when her people need to travel!

On leash, Tourmie is an all star and every once in a while she'll cosplay as a spring chicken and become excited to see other dogs - don't let he full you though, she actually prefers to spend her golden years being a families one and only.

Tourmie also loves everyone she meets including human strangers and young children. Tourmie is extremely respectful, gentle and indepedent understand when its time to be involved and when it's time to not be the center of attention.

Tourmie may call out to you for a few minutes when left alone and once she realizes you are always coming back for her she settles quickly. Truly, all Tourmie wants to remind you when you leave is how much she values spending time with you and how eager she is to have you back home. When you do return home - Tourmie will always become your built in bestie always aspiring to be your shadow!

Overall, Tourmie is an extremely low maintenancfe, easy going, snuggling and housebroken girl who spends her days not turning away from any treats offered and looks forward to spending her evenings cuddling in bed.

Last year, Tourmie was found to have a malignant tumor however her prognosis remaings good! If you take the time to meet Tourmie, you will see for youself that she still has alot of like left to live and she wakes up every morning feeling blessed to live it.

Tourmie is such a content dog, never asking for much however, our rescues deepest wish is that Tourmie gets to spend the rest of her life in a real home that finally picks her for once.

Tourmie's adoption fee is fully sponsored as we understand that the special human who welcomes Tourmie into their home, has more love in their hearts than any amount of funding could compare to.

If your hearts desire is to gift Tourmie with a lifetime of memories she'll keep with her forever - she will repay you with unconditional love and devotion- like Tourmie, everyone deserves to be chosen. Even you!.
