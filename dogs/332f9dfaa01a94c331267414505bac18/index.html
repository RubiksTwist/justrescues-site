☀️ Everything You'd Want to Know About Tourmaline — Her Foster Already Does.

Here's the gift of adopting Tourmie: she comes to you fully known and deeply loved. For the past year, she's lived with a devoted foster who can tell you exactly who she is — no guesswork, no unknowns. And who she is, is wonderful.

Who Tourmie is, every single day

Tourmie is ten years old, with a bright brown coat, floppy ears, and the settled, soulful presence only a decade of living gives a dog. She is low-maintenance, easy-going, fully housebroken, and content in a way that will slow your whole world down in the best way. Her idea of a perfect day? Lounging leisurely, holding court on a park bench beside her humans, being invited along to picnics, and curling up in bed for the evening — never turning down a treat along the way. She's a snuggler and a shadow: come home, and she becomes your built-in bestie, happiest simply being near you.

On leash, she's an all-star. Every now and then she'll cosplay as a spring chicken and get giddy at the sight of another dog — but don't be fooled. Tourmie would genuinely prefer to be your one and only, spending her golden years as the center of your world (and letting you be the center of hers). She adores everyone she meets — human strangers and young children included — and she's remarkably respectful and gentle, with an easy sense of when to join in and when to let the moment be.

About being left alone

Her foster knows this one well: Tourmie may call out for a few minutes when you first head out the door. It's not distress — it's devotion. Once she learns that you always come back for her, she settles quickly. All she's really saying is how much she treasures your time together and how eager she is for your return.

Her health — honestly

Last year, Tourmie was found to have a malignant tumor. Here's the truth, and it's a hopeful one: her prognosis remains good. Her foster has watched her thrive every day since. Spend ten minutes with her and you'll see it for yourself — she has so much life left to live, and she wakes up every morning blessed to live it. This is a dog who is thriving, not fading.

Her adoption fee is fully sponsored.

We believe the right human for Tourmie will arrive with more love in their heart than any amount of funding could ever measure. So we've removed that barrier entirely. All that's left is you.

If you've been waiting for a gentle, devoted companion who asks for little and gives everything — a dog whose personality is already an open book, who will repay a warm bed and a steady routine with unconditional love — Tourmie is ready, and her foster is ready to help you both begin. Our wish is simple: that this content, easy-to-love girl gets to spend the rest of her life in a home of her very own.

Everyone deserves a home to call their own.
