Meet Blueberry!

Blueberry has endured more heartbreak than any dog ever should.

This beautiful boy was seized from his owners after a horrific case of cruelty left him with devastating injuries, ultimately resulting in the loss of one of his legs. Despite the unimaginable pain he experienced, Blueberry never gave up.

Today, Blueberry is proof that resilience comes in the wag of a tail and the kindness of a heart. He has adapted beautifully to life as a tripod and doesn't let his past define him. What defines Blueberry is his gentle spirit, his loving nature, and his unwavering belief that people can still be good.

After suffering for far too long, Blueberry is finally safe. Now, all he needs is a family who will show him the love, compassion, and security he has always deserved. He isn't looking for sympathy: he's looking for a best friend, a cozy place to call home, and someone who will love him for the incredible dog he is.

Blueberry has survived the worst of humanity. Now it's time for him to experience the very best. Could you be the one to give this remarkable boy the forever home he's been waiting for?

Want to learn more? We'd love to tell you! Our bios are just a snapshot, so please reach out to our adoption team with any questions about this dog's personality, medical history, or ideal home. We'd be happy to help you determine if Blueberry is the perfect match for your family.
