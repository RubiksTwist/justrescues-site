🤍 Meet Lance! 🤍

Lance is a 2 year old, gentle, sweet-natured boy who has been making wonderful progress in his foster home, and we’ve learned so much about his personality along the way!

He’s been doing well with other cats following slow introductions, with no aggression or fighting. However, we’ve noticed that Lance really comes out of his shell when he has space to himself. He’s much more confident, outgoing, and comfortable without other cats around.

We believe Lance would thrive as the only cat in the home, where he can enjoy all the attention and have a space of his own. He may also potentially do well with a kitten, given the right introduction.

He’s a kind, gentle boy who loves interactive play, elevated spaces, and one particularly adorable thing… drinking from his water fountain! 🥹

Lance doesn’t need much. He just needs a home where he feels safe, secure, and free to be himself.

Let’s find this sweet boy the forever home where he can truly blossom! 🤍🐾
