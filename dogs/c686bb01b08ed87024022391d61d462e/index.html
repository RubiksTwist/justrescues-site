Chico was surrendered to the care center by his previous owner due to behavioral issues. The owner reports that Chico displayed highly defensive and fearful behavior in the home, including growling and snapping when family members attempt to approach or touch him, escalating to bites on three occasions-- to the owner, their mother, and their two-year old son. All three bites were bite-and-releases that broke skin and were treated at home. Chico has not acclimated well to the care center environment, growling, lunging, and snapping at staff and allowing only minimal handling. Due to noted concerns displayed in both the home and care center, we recommend placement with a New Hope partner who can provide an experienced, adult-only foster home, a period of decompression, and any necessary behavior modification before placement into a permanent home.
