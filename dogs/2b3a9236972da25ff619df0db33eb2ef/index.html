Ringo | 9 years young | 20 lbs | Terrier Mix

Ringo has been waiting for someone to choose him for nearly five months. We truly don’t understand why. 💔

Ringo spent almost eight years in a loving home, a lifetime of routines, familiar voices, and his favorite spot beside his people. A family change requiring an unexpected relocation landed Ringo back in Waggytail care, the place that first helped him find a home. His family trusted us to make the best decision for his future. We’re doing everything we can to find Ringo's perfect fit!

Ringo is a love bug at heart. All he wants is to cozy up to his person, on the couch, at your feet, or pressed against your side during a quiet afternoon. He bonds deeply, and once he feels safe, his affectionate, goofy personality shines through. 🩵

He is also a funny, quirky guy with opinions. Ringo is a little vocal and has boundaries, and if something isn’t to his liking, he’ll tell you: “Hey! I don’t like that!” He’s not aggressive in any way. He just communicates clearly, and with a little patience, he adjusts beautifully. He’ll bring you many laughs for all of his remaining days. 😄

More about Ringo

  • Easy to live with: Fully house-trained outside and on pads. He loves his walks and is just as happy relaxing at home.
  • Good with others: Does well with other dogs, cats, and older children.
  • Slow to warm, quick to love: He can be a little nervous meeting someone new, so give him time and you’ll see his sweet side come through.
  • Signature charm: Yes, his little tooth comes included. 🦷

Ringo doesn’t need anything fancy. He needs a patient, loving home where he can feel secure, settle into a routine, and know that he belongs. After nearly his whole life in one home, starting over hasn’t been easy, but he has shown us just how resilient and loving he can be.

Vaccinated for rabies, DHPP, bordetella, spayed/neutered and microchipped. Heartworm negative and current on flea, tick, and heartworm prevention.

To apply to adopt Ringo, visit our website (www.waggytailrescue.org) for an application. It is important to fill out an application as soon as possible for consideration, and we require a complete application for you to meet an animal.
