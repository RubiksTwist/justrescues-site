Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Edsel is a bit of a character, and honestly, he wouldn't have it any other way. There's something about this boy that makes you want to stop whatever you're doing and see what he's up to. He's got that unmistakable kitten curiosity, the kind that makes every box worth investigating, every toy a potential new obsession, and every unexpected noise a reason to come running.

He's still figuring out the world - rescued and brought in through our Operation cat program, he's trading a hard life in the great outdoors for a forever home where he can keep his people constantly laughing. Whether he's busy entertaining himself, getting into a bit of harmless trouble, or simply finding another way to keep life interesting, Edsel has a knack for making you laugh without even trying.

We're hoping his next big discovery will be a family of his own. One that appreciates his quirks, encourages his little adventures, and understands that living with a kitten means occasionally wondering what on earth he's gotten into now.
