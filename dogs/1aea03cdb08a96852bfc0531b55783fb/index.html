Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

If you’re looking for a puppy who will keep you smiling, make you laugh, and happily claim your lap as her favorite place in the world, you need to meet Lainey!

This beautiful German Shepherd came all the way from Georgia - she was rescued after she had found with a serious wound to her front leg, which ultimately cost her the leg. But Lainey isn't a puppy who feels sorry for herself, having three legs definitely doesn't slow her down - she's too busy enjoying life, soaking up love, and being the happy, affectionate girl she is.

Lainey has that wonderful puppy spirit - she's full of energy, curiosity, and excitement for everything around her. But underneath all that puppy enthusiasm is a total lovebug who just wants to be close to her people. Personal space? Not really her thing! If there's a lap available, Lainey believes that's exactly where she belongs. She has so much love to give and is going to make an incredibly devoted and wonderful companion.
