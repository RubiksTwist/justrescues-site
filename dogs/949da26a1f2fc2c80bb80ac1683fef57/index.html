Meet Jefe!

Jefe arrived as an owner surrender to the Manhattan Animal Care Center on December 22, 2025. He is a friendly, attentive companion who is looking for a calm, patient person to help him settle into a new routine. Staff describe him as having some behavior challenges to work through, but he quickly shows that he wants to connect and learn when given consistent, gentle guidance.

My Ideal Home

Jefe will thrive in a quiet, predictable home with a patient adopter who can provide daily interaction and consistent training. He does best without other animals in the household and would do well in a single-adult home where he can get focused attention while he adjusts to life outside the shelter.

Good with Kids?

No - Jefe was surrendered for not being good with kids, so he should be placed in a home without children. He does best with experienced adults who understand his needs and boundaries.

Good with Other Pets?

No - Jefe needs a home with no other dogs, and he should not live with cats or small animals. Staff observations indicate he does not do well in multi-pet homes.

Leash Manners

Jefe is making progress but will need ongoing training and practice on leash. He benefits from a calm handler and clear, consistent cues; patience and positive reinforcement will help him become more comfortable and confident on walks.

Overall Health

Healthy overall and neutered. He is receiving routine care at the shelter and is comfortable now that he's been settled in.

Behavior Determination

Experienced Adopters - Jefe has some behavior needs that staff will discuss with you. He's best suited to someone prepared to work patiently with him, follow shelter guidance, and provide consistent structure and training.

Other Fun Facts

Jefe loves interaction and responds well to calm, consistent attention. He enjoys spending time close to his people and will do best in a home where he gets regular companionship and gentle, positive training to build his confidence.
