Meet Olive (Olivewood)! 🐾

Olive is a 4-year-old Shepherd mix and your next true love – endlessly affectionate, loyal, well-behaved, smart as a whip, wanting to learn and to please. According to her Embark DNA test, Olive is 50.7% German Shepherd, 33% Belgian Malinois and 16.3% “Supermutt,” which may explain why she’s so smart and loyal! 🧬

Olive loves to cuddle while you pet her favorite spots on her ears and nose, and she especially loves rolling over for a good tummy rub. When you stop, she will reach out and tap you for more or rest her chin on your leg. ❤

At home, Olive is low maintenance and easygoing. While she adores her people, Olive is also happily independent. She’ll play with her toys on her own or curl up in her bed and relax, which makes her an easy, low-key companion. She is fully house-trained and never has accidents. She’s crate trained and can be left for hours, either in her crate or out of it, and she stays calm and well behaved. She does like to steal shoes, but doesn't chew or eat them, just collects them, so her future family may want to keep footwear out of reach. 👟😂

Outdoors, Olive comes alive, sniffing through the grass and playing with other dogs at the park. She loves a good roll in the leaves and is an excellent swimmer. She appreciates her outdoor time and has learned to navigate busy city streets around other dogs and distractions on her way to and from the park. Back indoors, she is low energy and content to settle down.

Olive is leash trained and very responsive to treats, which makes training a joy. She’s smart, eager to please, and genuinely loves to learn. She already knows sit, down, crate and come, and she’d love a person who will keep her training going, continue her calm leash walks, and teach her new things.

Olive loves meeting and playing with other dogs and has lived with other dogs before. If she’s joining a home with a resident dog, she would need proper, slow introductions so everyone can settle in comfortably.

Olive is spayed, fully up to date on her vaccinations and preventatives, and has tested negative for heartworm and parasites, so she’s healthy and ready to go home! 🩺

Olive has been so patient waiting for her forever home. She’s looking for an adopter or a long-term foster-to-adopt who will give her the love, care and affection she deserves, and she’ll give it all back many times over.

If you’re looking for a smart, affectionate, loyal and sweet companion, Olive could be your perfect match! ❤
