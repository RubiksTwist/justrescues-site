My foster writes:
Meet Enamor - Your New Little Shadow 💛
Enamor is the sweetest, most easygoing girl you'll ever meet - and she wants nothing more than to be wherever you are. Cooking, working, working out... doesn't matter. She'll be your quiet little shadow, content just to keep you company.
Despite being gentle and calm, Enamor is SO smart - she learned "sit" in just a few tries, and picked up fetch in about 10 minutes flat (she now proudly brings the ball right back to you!). Give her a job to do and she'll surprise you with how fast she catches on.
She's wonderfully steady with people, other animals, and kids alike, and adapts easily to new situations. A tennis ball met its match with her - RIP to one very well-loved chew toy 🎾 - and she also loves puzzle-style toys where she gets to "hunt" for hidden treats.
Speaking of treats: this girl has ZERO off-switch when it comes to food. She's been known to sneak a snack left within reach, so a watchful eye at snack time is a must - she just doesn't know when to stop!
Enamor came from a shelter stay and is still building confidence with alone time, but she's already made huge strides - going from anxious to calmly walking into her crate on her own. With patience and consistency, she just keeps growing more secure every day.
If you want a sweet, smart, endlessly affectionate companion who'll be by your side through it all, Enamor might just be your perfect match. 🐾
