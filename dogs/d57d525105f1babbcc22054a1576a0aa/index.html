Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Celine Dion may sing “All By Myself,” but our Celine is hoping that era is officially over.

At just a year and a half old, this soulful sweetheart has stolen plenty of hearts at ARF since she arrived from Animal Control. Celine loves meeting new people, walks beautifully on leash, and is always eager to learn, especially if treats are involved. She already knows sit, down, and come, and her happy, playful personality makes her a joy to be around.

While she loves a good game of fetch, Celine also has a softer side. One of her favorite things to do is relax beside her people and quietly watch the world go by.

Celine would thrive in a calm, structured home where she can be the only pet. She’d do best with kids ages 8 and older, and with a little patience and consistency, she’ll blossom into an incredibly loyal companion.

Equal parts playful sidekick and devoted best friend, Celine is ready for her next chapter.
