Meet Goalie 🐾

Found stray in Georgia and transported to NYC, Goalie’s foster family got a surprise soon after: she was pregnant. Not long after, she welcomed 5 puppies of her own — and just like that, this little girl became a mom before she’d even finished being a puppy herself (she’s still under 2!).

Don’t let the “mom” title fool you, though. Goalie is pure goofball energy — constantly trying to rope her two foster brothers (each about 3x her size) into play, and if her babies will let her, she’s got all 5 of them roped in too. Mornings are her best look: wiggly, snuggly, and giving off serious sea otter vibes with that sweet face of hers.

Underneath the silliness is a genuinely fearless, independent little girl who isn’t scared of a thing. Goalie’s proven she can handle whatever comes her way — now she just needs a family (and maybe a comfy couch) to call her own.
