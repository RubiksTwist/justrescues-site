Meet Companion!

Companion was found wandering loose on the street and is now safe and looking for a steady, understanding home. He's a calm, independent boy who takes a little time to warm up, but once comfortable, he moves through the world with a gentle, easygoing presence.

My Ideal Home

Companion would do best in an experienced adult-only home that can support him with structure and patience, especially around outdoor distractions. He'll thrive with calm leadership and predictable routines while he builds confidence.

Good with Other Pets?

Companion can become stiff, vocal, and tense when seeing other dogs on walks, so he will need slow, controlled introductions and careful management in shared spaces.

Good with Kids?

Not recommended for young children due to on-leash reactivity and his need for a calm, structured environment.

Things to Know

Companion is a more reserved dog who prefers to take in his surroundings before engaging. He may appear aloof at first, often focusing on sniffing and observing rather than interacting. While he accepts handling and walks politely without pulling, he can become reactive when he sees other dogs and will need guidance and distance to feel comfortable. With time, patience, and consistent routines, he has the potential to become a steady, loyal companion.

Fun Fact

True to his name, Companion is the kind of dog who quietly grows into your best sidekick once he decides you're his person.
