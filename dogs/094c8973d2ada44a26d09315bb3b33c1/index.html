Meet Dream!
Dream is a sweet, social girl who loves being wherever her people are! Her former family says she's affectionate, enjoys spending time in the living room, and absolutely loves bath time. She's also a clever girl who knows plenty of tricks and is ready to bring her charm, smarts, and big personality to her next home. Now all Dream needs is someone to make her dream of a forever family come true!

My Ideal Home
Dream would love a home where she can enjoy her daily walks, fun adventures, and lots of quality time with her favorite people. She's used to going on three walks a day and has experience with crates and car rides, so she's already familiar with many parts of home life. She's a medium energy girl who will enjoy plenty of enrichment and chances to show off her tricks. She'll also benefit from some positive reinforcement training to help her practice staying calm when she's excited.

Good with Other Pets?
Dream showed no reaction to other dogs while walking at ACC, although we don't have a complete picture of her history with other pets. Slow, thoughtful introductions with any resident animals will help everyone get comfortable.

Good with Kids?
Dream would do best in a home without children under 5 because she can get very excited during interactions. Older children who are comfortable around an enthusiastic dog may be a better fit, with adults helping her practice calm greetings and giving her breaks when she needs them.

Things to Know
Dream is an affectionate girl who enjoys attention, but sometimes gets so excited that she starts bouncing around and zooming off to burn off her energy. She can feel a little anxious during certain handling situations, so patience, positive reinforcement, and rewarding her when she's calm will help her feel more comfortable. She walks with only mild pulling, follows along with a loose body, and is always ready to learn something new. With Dream around, you'll have sweet dreams of all the adventures you'll share together!
