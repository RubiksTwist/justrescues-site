Winston looks like a tiny little old man in a 5-pound puppy body, and it's one of the first things people notice about him. Someone told me he looks like Albert Einstein, and now I can't unsee it. But don't let his scruffy little face fool you-he's still very much a puppy. He loves playing and chasing toys around the apartment, and chewing on his favorite chew sticks. Once he's gotten his playtime in, he's perfectly happy curling up next to his people for a nap or rolling onto his back for a belly rub. He is a total love bug with people and will happy wiggle over for kisses and pets. He would love nothing more to be your sidekick and go with you everywhere throughout the day. Winston came from the ACC with his mom, Lottie, and brother, Alfie, and now he's ready to find a family of his own. Winston has a gentle heart and a sweet personality. He enjoys playing just as much as any puppy, but the happiest place he will ever be is in your lap. ﻿Winston is approximately 0y 2m 25d old and may be 22 to 26 pounds when fully grown.
