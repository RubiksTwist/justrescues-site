DETAILED PROFILE

Tim Curry has a signature sleeping position: flat on his back, belly to the ceiling, paws in the air, completely unbothered by the world. When he’s awake, he’s a goofy, gentle, entertaining guy with moderate energy and a soft spot for his people, whom he likes to stick close to.

He’s friendly and outgoing with new people and has been comfortable with kids he’s met. Other dogs are a highlight: he gets along with every dog he meets on the street and with his dog housemate at home. He loves toys and, remarkably, shares them, happily playing together without ever getting territorial. His experience with cats isn’t known yet.

He’s house trained and working on crate training and leash manners. More details will come as his foster gets to know him, but the early verdict is clear: Tim is a sweet, easy-to-love companion who plays well with others and naps like nobody’s watching.
