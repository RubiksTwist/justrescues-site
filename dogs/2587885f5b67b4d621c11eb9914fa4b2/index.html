Description provided by Bobbi and the Strays

Simply put, Misty is an AMAZING girl! She was found on the street and has been waiting for her forever family to give her the wonderful home she deserves. This super sweet and friendly pup is very energetic and will thrive given lots of exercise, walks and love! She really enjoys playing, sunbathing and going for a dip in the water! Most of all this beautiful pup is happiest when with her people. She will do best as the only pup in an adults only home. Her owner must have breed experience.

Misty is a gorgeous 7 year old Pit Bull Terrier mix who is heartworm negative, up to date on vaccinations, microchipped and spayed. She is currently in Freeport. Please fill out the adoption application on our website https://bobbiandthestrays.org/adoption-page-new/ if you are ready to meet Misty.
