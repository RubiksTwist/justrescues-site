Description provided by Bideawee

October (my foster family calls me Rosie) is a super sweet, affectionate pup who gets along with pretty much everyone including kids and other dogs! She is potty trained and happiest snuggling, giving kisses, and soaking up belly rubs. She’ll have a couple of short zoomies a day, but otherwise she’s relaxed and happy to just hang out with you. She loves following her foster parents around to see what they’re up to but is also content playing with her toys on her own. She is a happy girl and has the cutest smile!

Like many pups especially in NYC, October can get overstimulated in new places, so walks can take some patience, training, and redirection. A quiet neighborhood would likely be her best fit. She is learning crate training and can get nervous when left alone; she previously lived with another dog, so a home with other dogs could work well for her. She’s very food motivated and works hard to improve - she’s become totally comfortable on our regular walking loop in our busy neighborhood, which shows how much progress she can make with some time! She’s very smart and already knows “come”, is making great strides with “wait” and “sit,” and loves working on puzzles.

October is a beautiful sweet girl looking for her forever family with lots of love and treats!
