Meet Fitz

Fitz’s life began with more hardship than any puppy should ever have to endure. Found discarded in Georgia with a collar embedded deep into his neck, he suffered a painful, infected wound that required extensive veterinary care. Thankfully, Fitz was rescued just in time. With treatment, patience, and love, he healed and got his chance at a better life.

Today, Fitz is ready for the next chapter, and there is so much to love about this resilient boy! He’s smart, affectionate, loyal to his people, and eager to please. He loves treats, enjoys a good game of fetch, and will happily bring his ball right back to you when he’s ready for more. He has an active, curious mind and enjoys learning, making him a rewarding companion for someone who wants to keep him engaged.

Fitz has moderate energy and would love a home with a fenced yard where he can play and enjoy his favorite games. He walks nicely on leash and continues to make progress with distractions and new experiences. He can be cautious when meeting unfamiliar people and dogs, so slow introductions, patience, and consistent guidance will help him feel comfortable. He’s affectionate with his trusted people and responds well to clear boundaries.

After everything Fitz has endured, he deserves a family who will see just how special he is, continue helping him grow in confidence, and give him the security and love he’s always deserved.

His painful past is behind him. Now, it’s time for belly rubs, cozy naps, games of fetch, and a forever family to call his own. After surviving the unimaginable, Fitz is ready to find his forever happy place. Could that place be with you?
