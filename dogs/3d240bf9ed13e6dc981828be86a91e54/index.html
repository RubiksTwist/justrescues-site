Meet Meye!

Meye is a lovely girl who is looking for a forever home where she can be cherished and loved. She recently arrived at our shelter and has quickly won over staff and volunteers with her sweet nature and gentle personality.

Meye enjoys spending time with people and appreciates a calm, comfortable environment where she can relax and feel secure. She has been adjusting well and continues to show what a wonderful companion she can be.

Meye came to our shelter when her previous family could no longer care for her. Now, she is ready for a fresh start with a family that can provide her with the love, stability, and lifelong commitment she deserves.

If you are looking for a loyal feline friend to share your home and heart, Meye may be the perfect match. She is ready to begin her next chapter and can't wait to meet her new family.
