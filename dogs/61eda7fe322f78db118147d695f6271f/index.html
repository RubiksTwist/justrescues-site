Meet Luna!

Luna is a medium energy girl who is looking for a calm place to land after being surrendered when her family could no longer care for her. Her previous family describes her as friendly, and she has lived happily with two other dogs. Luna can be fearful in new situations, so she will need a patient person who can let her settle in at her own pace.

My Ideal Home

Luna would do best in an initially calm and quiet home where she can decompress and build confidence. She enjoys being active and will benefit from daily walks, enrichment, and force free, reward based training to help her learn and feel comfortable.

Good with Other Pets?

Yes! Luna lived with two other dogs in her previous home, and she has been social with dogs at ACC. She enjoys playful interactions, while also being able to explore independently and take breaks when needed. As always, introductions should be gradual and supervised.

Good with Kids?

Luna would do best in an adult only home. She can be fearful and sensitive about certain types of handling, so she needs people who will respect her boundaries and allow her to choose when she is ready to interact.

Things to Know

Luna's previous family reports that she may hover over food she considers valuable, so adopters should use positive reinforcement and never take items directly from her. She can also jump up when excited, have occasional potty accidents, and may bark or growl if her paws are touched or she is held or restrained. She will do best with patient, reward based training and a family who understands that trust is built through positive experiences.
