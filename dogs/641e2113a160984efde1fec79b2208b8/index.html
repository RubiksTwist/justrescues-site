Andre was surrendered to the care centers by his previous owners due to their housing situation. His previous owners describe him as curious, gentle dog who enjoys quality time with his family, is crate trained, is always leaning in for pets, and loves to cuddle in laps despite his size. Andre has not acclimated well to the care center environment, and has allowed only minimal handling since intake, showing fearful behavior during intake, medical examination, and handling assessment. Due to his level of fear, we recommend placement with a New Hope partner who can provide decompression, any necessary behavior modification (force-free, positive reinforcement-based) and re-evaluate behavior in a stable home environment before placement into a permanent home.

A volunteer writes: Andre has a puppy-like curiosity that is beyond endearing to witness-he is, after all, but a puppy of only 11 months. On a recent walk, he discovered the joys of a discarded paper cup, picking it up like a toy and proudly trotting around with it for a beat, but quickly losing interest. Despite being just a baby, not even a year old, he has the soulful demeanor of a dog beyond his years. On walks, he'll check in, mostly hoping to snag some treats, and being successful in his quest (only a monster could deny his pleading eyes). This young boy has already perfected his leash skills, walking loosely beside me at all times. I'm surprised to learn how fearful Andre was when he first arrived at the shelter, because the moment I meet him, two weeks in, he's eager to get to know me and solicit attention. It pleases me to see that he's gained some more confidence in these strange new surroundings.

Andre was surrendered along with his sister Lavender (#266660) and another dog who has since been adopted, after their person's dog had an accidental litter. Their guardian could not find homes for all the puppies and did not have the resources to care for all of the dogs.
