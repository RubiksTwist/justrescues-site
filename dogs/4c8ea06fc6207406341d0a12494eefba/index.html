Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

Yuna is the sweetest wee pup looking for a forever home! She's love incarnate. The absolute friendliest, sweetest, most loving pup! She's excellent with dogs (and dying to play with them!) and very food motivated. Yuna has so much love in her heart and wants to snuggle and lick everyone she meets. She is working on her potty training and her very few accidents (when given regular potty breaks) are usually on a pee pad. She has been sleeping alone in a pen and is not very whiney. When left alone, she whines for 2 ish minutes and then self-soothes. Any home would be lucky to have Yuna. She is eager to learn and so full of love!

﻿Yuna is approximately 0y 3m 8d old and may be 33 to 41 pounds when fully grown. They are microchipped and up to date on all age-appropriate medical care. Their breed is unknown as we have not had a DNA test done.
