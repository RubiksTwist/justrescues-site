Meet Mercury!

Mercury is a gentle girl who is still trying to make sense of her new surroundings after being found as a stray. She can be nervous around new people, but she'll explore her surroundings and occasionally check in with her handlers. She's not quite ready to show all her cards yet, but with patience and a little time, we're hoping her personality will start to shine.

My Ideal Home

Mercury would love a calm, quiet home with a patient person who understands that trust takes time. She'll need the freedom to explore at her own pace and plenty of positive experiences as she settles in. She may be taking things at her own pace, but with time, Mercury could be ready to orbit right into your heart!

Good with Other Pets?

Mercury's history with other animals is unknown. Her new family should give her plenty of time to settle in before considering introductions.

Good with Kids?

Mercury would do best in a home without very young children under 5. She can be easily intimidated by new people, so she needs humans who will approach slowly, respect her boundaries, and let her choose when she's ready.

Things to Know

Mercury walks with mild pulling and may try to make a quick exit when it's time to leave her crate, but once she's out, she follows her handler and explores. She's comfortable with gentle contact even though she can remain tense, so her new family should focus on slow decompression and let her build confidence at her own pace. Give this Mercury a little space and patience, and you might just discover a truly stellar companion.
