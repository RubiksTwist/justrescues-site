Description provided by AMA Animal Rescue

Lola's story began with heartbreak. Found alone at night, tied to a fence and severely malnourished, she was discarded and desperately in need of someone to save her. We gave her the care and love she deserved, but despite everything she's overcome, Lola is still waiting for a family to call her own. She's been with us for far too long, and it's time for her to finally have a place to belong.

Lola is a very sweet girl who forms deep bonds with her people. She'll happily spend her days soaking up belly rubs, enjoying cozy naps, and basking in the sunshine. She can be friendly with other dogs and would thrive with an experienced owner who can offer her the patience, structure, and guidance she needs. Lola is looking for an adult-only home with no children, where she receives the training and care she deserves.

After all this time, Lola deserves to know what it feels like to be home. Will you be the one to give her that chance?
