Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

My foster writes: Bella is a sweet and loving cat! She loves to hang out and watch you during any activity. She likes the attention of new people after she's gotten comfortable. Closed doors and closets are not allowed! Once the door is opened, she will go about her day. She is talkative and will let you know what she wants, especially if it's breakfast or dinner!

A volunteer writes:
"She is playful, lively and cuddly"...Bella's owner's words. Bella unfortunately has not shown to us this side of her. She is having a hard time at the care center as she is also a shy cat who needs time to get used to new things and people. The hustle and bustle of a busy shelter does not help, of course and makes the situation worse. Bella is starting to trust and accepts gentle petting on her head, even leans and moves a little forward in her cubby. She slow blinks at me when I talk to her softly. If someone suddenly enters her ward or makes a loud noise, Bella quickly goes back to square one, the back of her cubby. Bella is a sweet cat who does not fit well in a shelter environment. She needs a home sweet home, a quiet one, low traffic and with a new owner who loves and understands shy kitties. Bella was surrendered with Butter as their owner lost their sight and became unable to care for their pets. She is six year old, apparently healthy, already spayed and could go home ...Today! Ask to meet sweet Bella when you visit us at the Manhattan Care Center!
