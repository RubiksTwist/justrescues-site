Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Mishka means "little bear" and have you ever seen a more appropriate name?

At 3 years old, Mishka is a smart, social, funny and incredibly sweet girl. Mishka is the equal medium of cuddle buddy and adventure partner and is always happy to match your energy. At first meet, Mishka may be a tad bit shy but very quickly after you will see that she is a total peoples pup with a goofy side and the most endearing personality.
.

In fact, Mishka abosolutely ADORES her humans and never misses an opportunity to be wherever they are wether that mean she's following them around, checking in with them during play or looking to them for guidance when uncertain. Mishka is more than a dog, she is a being who genuinely wants to be her persons best friend.

Even outside of her intimate people, Mishka is a social butterfly! Mishka ideal meet and greet scenario is being enaged with politely, being allowed to approach a new person which leads to her warming up very quickly to men, woman and children.

Mishka is also a born and bred city girl being completely unbothered by loud kids, scooters, bikes, and all the sights and sounds of city life and in the rare event she does become excitable, all she needs is a treat to refocus!

Walking with Mishka on leash is super enjoyable especially because she is also friendly, social and happy to play with other dogs. Mishka will do especially well with a rough and rugged playmate who is not overtly submissive given her playful enthusiam.

Mishka is doing swimmingly in her crate and doesn't mind hanging out inside however sometimes she'll let you know she much prefers to be next to you rather than away from you.

Mishka is also 100% housebroken as well as having excellent house manners - being non-destructive and sleeping soundly through the night.

Mishka's favorite hobbies on earth are getting belly rubs, going for walks, hanging with her people and any experience that allows her to show her inner mermaid - MISHKA LOVES WATER and will not miss an opportunity to submerge herself in a pong, a pool, a lake, a sprinkler!

The super human who adopts Mishka will be incredibly lucky to experience all the love and joy she will bring to them and her forever family will undoubtedly have the time of their life discovering the long list of little things that make Mishka perfect. If easy going and joyful is what you are seeking, look no futher, this little bear is exactly who you need.
