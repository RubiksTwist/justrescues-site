Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Bisque is a 4-year-old shepherd mix who weighs around 35 lbs. She's incredibly sweet, smart, curious, and definitely likes to see what she can get away with, but once she understands the rules, she respects them.

She loves a good brushing session and is a big fan of sniffing her way through the world, taking everything in without trying to eat or chew whatever she finds. Bisque is also house-trained and crate-trained, which makes her pretty easy to settle into a routine with.

She’d do best with adopters who appreciate a clever dog with a little personality and are happy to give her the structure she responds so well to. Underneath that slightly cheeky streak is a very sweet girl who clearly loves being close to her people.

Registered adopters interested in Bisque can email adopt@hbrdogs.com for more info. If you’re not registered yet, do it today at hbrdogs.com!
