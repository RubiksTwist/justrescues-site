Kong is a quiet, independent dog who would benefit from a rescue partner willing to give him the time, patience, and support he needs to thrive. He is currently experiencing a skin condition that is likely causing significant discomfort and may be contributing to his behavioral sensitivities. When feeling uncomfortable or overwhelmed, Kong has escalated to higher-level warnings, so it is important that his boundaries and communication are respected.

Kong does best with a hands-off approach and minimal handling. When given space and allowed to keep to himself, he is generally content to quietly coexist without seeking much interaction. He is not comfortable with traditional grooming, including bathing and brushing, and will need a thoughtful, patient approach to any necessary handling.

We are seeking an experienced rescue partner who can help Kong address his medical needs, pursue appropriate treatment for his skin condition, and provide behavioral support before considering placement in an adoptive home. Our hope is that, once his discomfort is better managed and his needs are understood, Kong will have a better opportunity to succeed in a safe and appropriate home.
