Description provided by NYC Second Chance Rescue

Briar Millie has a beautiful smile, a sweet personality, and a story that deserves a much happier ending.

Millie was found by an off-duty NYPD detective locked inside a hot car in New York. Another dog was with her and had suffered serious injuries. Thankfully, that dog made a recovery and was adopted shortly afterward. Now it is Millie’s turn to find the loving home she deserves.

Despite everything she has been through, Millie is playful, affectionate, and full of love. She is the kind of girl who can make you smile just by looking at you. Her beautiful smile is only one of the many things that makes her special, and her sweet, loving nature makes her a joy to spend time with.

Millie deserves a family who will keep her safe, cherish her, and show her what it means to be loved. She has so much happiness to share, and we would love to see her finally have a home of her own.

Please consider adopting Briar Millie. She is ready for her next chapter, and we hope it leads her straight to you.

Please remember that adoption bios are only a snapshot of each animal. They are meant to give you a glimpse into their personality and story, but there is so much more to learn. Please reach out to our Adoption Team for additional information about Millie and to learn if she may be the right fit for your home.

All dogs will be altered, microchipped, and receive age-appropriate vaccines prior to adoption.
