Meet Hibiscus, a silly, sweet girl who knows how to enjoy the good things in life!

Hibiscus is a big fan of cuddle time, whether she’s curled up with you on the couch or rolling onto her back for a good tummy rub. Full-body scratches are always appreciated, and a little cheese or a tasty treat certainly doesn’t hurt either! Outside, Hibiscus loves following her nose, sunbathing and rolling around in the grass. When she gets especially excited, her goofy side takes over. She’ll zoom around, make her signature little snorts and playfully pretend to run right into you for a snuggle break! From couch cuddles and sunny naps to grass rolls and zoomies, Hibiscus has a knack for making the everyday moments more fun. Click the “Adopt Me” button to connect with Hibiscus’s team in Pawling, New York!
