Description provided by AMA Animal Rescue

Lady's story began when we spotted a plain cardboard box tucked behind the trash bins, and our hearts sank. Boxes left like that rarely mean good things. But when we carefully lifted the lid, out stepped Lady, bright-eyed, calm, cute and ready to charm. There were no notes or clues about where she came from, just a sweet cat someone had decided to leave behind. What started as a sad discovery quickly turned into the best rescue.

Now that she’s safe and loved, Lady’s true colors shine, and oh, does she have personality! She’s the first to greet you, the queen of head bumps, and a total pro at chasing string toys. Her little gray mustache gives her a one-of-a-kind look (we think she totally rocks it). Lady is affectionate, friendly, and always up for a cuddle or a play session. She may have been left behind once, but she’s ready to find her forever home, one filled with love, cuddles, and plenty of toys to chase. Apply to adopt Lady today!
