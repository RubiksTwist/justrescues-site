Meet Marron, a tiny bundle of sweetness with a personality that’s impossible to resist!

Marron is equal parts adorable, curious, and playful. She was found as a stray with her siblings in East Hampton, and loves discovering new things, chasing anything that dares to move, and turning even the simplest moment into an adventure fit for a kitten with a big spark. And when the zoomies are over? She’s more than happy to curl up and enjoy some cozy downtime.

There’s something extra special about this little girl - she has that perfect combination of sweetness and personality that makes you want to scoop her up and never let go. Her special move? One look, one purr, and your heart is officially hers.
