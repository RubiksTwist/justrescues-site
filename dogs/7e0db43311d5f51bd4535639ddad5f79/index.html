Meet Tilly – Your New Best Friend! 🐾

Tilly is a city-savvy sweetheart who’s ready to trade uncertainty for unconditional love. Rescued just in time from NYC ACC, this gentle soul once faced an uncertain future, but now, with us, she’s all wiggles and hope, dreaming of a cozy couch to call her own.

Tilly is a true people-lover! Whether you're young or young at heart, she’ll happily snuggle up beside you. She enjoys leisurely strolls through the city, calmly walking on leash without pulling, and soaking in the sights like the classy city girl she is. With her medium-to-low energy, Tilly is just as happy stretching her legs as she is curling up for a nap. Apartment living? She’s already a pro. Tilly thrives in a calm home environment and brings a warm, grounding presence to any space. She’s affectionate, well-mannered, and endlessly sweet; the kind of dog who wiggles her whole body just to say hello.

If you're looking for a loyal companion with a gentle spirit and a heart full of love, Tilly is ready to meet you. Open your heart, and let her brighten your life forever.

All dogs will be altered, microchipped, and receive age-appropriate vaccines prior to adoption.
