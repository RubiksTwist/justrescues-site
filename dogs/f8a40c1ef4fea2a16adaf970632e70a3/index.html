Named after Aang’s adorable, mischievous flying lemur from Avatar: The Last Airbender, Momo is a seven-month-old black kitten who is ready to embark on an adventure of her own!

Momo came to ARF from Sag Harbor as part of our Operation Cat program. Now, this sweet girl is looking for a forever home where she can be loved, spoiled, and make plenty of memories of her own. If you’re looking for a playful, lovable companion with a name as fun as she is, Momo might be your girl!
