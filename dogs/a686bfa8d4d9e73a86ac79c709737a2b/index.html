Meet Friends (aka Fran)! 🐾

Friends is a 10-year-old dachshund with the sweetest personality and the most wonderful temperament. She is happiest when she’s with her people and loves being part of the action. She is incredibly friendly, calm, and gentle, and she has never met a stranger she didn’t want to say hello to.

Friends absolutely loves her walks and exploring NYC. She takes her time and enjoys a leisurely sniff walk, stopping to investigate all the interesting smells along the way. She doesn’t need to rush anywhere and is perfectly happy taking in the world at her own pace.

When she’s home, Friends loves being close to her people, getting lots of pets, chewing on her favorite bone, and of course enjoying her treats and food. She is a quiet girl overall and communicates in her own adorable way whenever she wants or needs something. She is house trained and does best with regular potty breaks throughout the day.

Friends also loves to burrow under blankets and make herself a cozy little nest when it’s time to relax. She is affectionate and easygoing, and happiest when she can simply hang out with her people. She has such a sweet presence and would make a wonderful companion for someone patient who is looking for a gentle senior pup to share their days with.

Follow @fran.the.doxie on Instagram to see daily updates of her adventures! 💕
