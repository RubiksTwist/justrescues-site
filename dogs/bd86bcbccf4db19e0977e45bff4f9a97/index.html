Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Big fluff, even bigger love! Noodle is a handsome Samoyed with a heart full of affection and a personality that’s just as big as his fluffy coat.

Noodle originally made his way here from China with the help of Hope 4 Fur and the Andy Sabin Family Foundation, and he’s hoping his next chapter will finally be the one that lasts forever.

Noodle has been adopted twice, and while both homes ultimately weren’t the right fit, we know there’s someone out there who will understand him and help him succeed. This lovable guy can get overly excited and sometimes struggles to manage all those big feelings. He needs a dedicated, experienced adopter who understands his needs, can recognize when he needs a break, and is committed to helping him learn appropriate ways to navigate the world. A structured environment, consistent boundaries, and continued positive-reinforcement training will be important for his success.

But here's the thing about Noodle: he LOVES his people. He is incredibly affectionate and sometimes forgets that he’s a big, fluffy Samoyed and not a tiny lap dog. If you’re looking for a companion who wants to be close to you and shower you with love, Noodle has plenty to give!

Noodle needs more than just a home; he needs someone willing to invest in him, advocate for him, and help him build the skills to be successful. He may not be the easiest dog, but for the right person, he could be an incredibly rewarding companion.
