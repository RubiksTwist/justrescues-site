Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Adonis is a lanky, goofy, teenage boy ready for forever! At 50lbs and 5 months our team expects him to grow into a big boy but thay just means there will be more of him to love!

Long legs and an equally long snoot define his look but soulful eyes and goofy energy define his personality! Adonis loves people and is superb and making friends and handling city energy. He is learning about socializing with other animals and currently makes a big impression when he meets other dogs (he is known to bark his "hellos!" to other dogs). He would love doggie friends, he is looking for a human who can help teach him the best way to make them!

True to his age, Adonis is a great learner while navigating all that comes with being a puppy. He is working on his house training, has learned to be in his crate when he is alone, but he shows off best when you see what a great walker he is! He would love help learning that barking is not a good communication style. However, he is more than happy to teach you that 50lbs IS lap dog size!

Adonis is looking for a family that can be routine, predictability, and match his fun and sweet side
