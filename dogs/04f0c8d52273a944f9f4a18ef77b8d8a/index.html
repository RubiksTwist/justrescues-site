Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Meet Doni! 8 month old Doni is lanky, goofy, and everything you want in a best friend and companion. Through watching Doni learn and grow we have discovered what an incredible and special dog he is.

Doni is proven to be an incredibly smart, social, and driven boy. He has never met a stranger, and thrives being with his people and getting the most out of the human-dog relationship. He absolutely loves to learn, and locks in for his training sessions quickly picking up an arsenal of cues; sit, down, wait, paw discrimination, leave it, and drop it but is eager to keep working and to learn more. When he's not learning new things, he's eager to teach you that 50lbs IS lap dog size!

Doni hit the genetic lottery for drive! He would thrive in a home that wants to get him on a barn hunt journey, sees his long lanky legs as the dock diving dog of their dreams, or has another dog sport that they're dreaming of the perfect training companion for! He wants a job to do and people who are excited to do it with him!

Adonis LOVES adventures! He would be the perfect hiking, biking, or running buddy for an active owner who also loves to cuddle on the couch at the end of a long day.

Adonis is looking for a family that thrives on routine and predictability, and match his fun and sweet side.
