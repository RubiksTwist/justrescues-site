Meet Ashoka, a gorgeous tabby with a heart full of love and affection!

Her day starts with joyful chirps to greet you the moment you walk into her room, and she graciously gives you a tour of her whole kingdom. Ashoka is a true sun-worshipper who melts into warm, sunny spots for the coziest sunbathing sessions. She'll happily accept yummy treats and a fun wand play session, and when she's ready to recharge, she lounges in her cozy bed like the independent queen she is. Sweet, chirpy, and endlessly charming, Ashoka is ready to fill a home with warmth and purrs. Click “Adopt Me” to learn more!

*You can meet animals located at the ASPCA Adoption Center during our walk-in hours (daily, noon to 6 p.m.). We encourage a $150 suggested adoption fee when adopting an animal. This adoption fee allows the ASPCA to continue our lifesaving work and help even more animals in need. We are grateful for any amount given at the time of adoption.*
