Hi! I'm Wednesday aka a total love bug!! I love to snuggle and will happily be your shadow, I'm just as content joining you for dinner out or a round of errands as I am curled up at your feet while you fold laundry.

My demeanor is calm and go-with-the-flow, yet always eager and curious for an adventure. Truly the type of dog who keeps up with city life without missing a beat. Nothing phases me, from honking traffic to crowded sidewalks. I love to say hi to everyone I see, whether it's another dog, a child, or a bird. I'm a natural in cars, elevators, and even baths. I use my floppy ears to expresses myself.

At home, I entertains myself with my toys, tiring myself out with independent play before settling in for a cuddle. I'm very quiet and not a barker, but I do make an adorable groan whenever I'm waking up / stretching from a nap.

At the end of the day I'm just an easygoing, social, adventure-ready, and endlessly affectionate gal!
