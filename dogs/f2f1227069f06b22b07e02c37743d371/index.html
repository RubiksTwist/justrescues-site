Meet Jojo!
Jojo is a sweet, affectionate boy with a playful side and a whole lot of love for his people! His former family describes him as a quiet, well mannered companion who loves spending time wherever his favorite humans happen to be. He's also quite the little athlete when it comes to tug of war, and his family says you might have to let him win every now and then because he could play all day! Jojo is a baby at heart and is hoping to find someone who will make him feel just as loved as he deserves.

My Ideal Home
Jojo would love a home where he can enjoy daily walks, playtime, and plenty of quality time with his people. He's already a clever boy who knows Sit, Stay, Lay Down, Give Paw, Wait, and Don't Move. He enjoys a good game of tug, bath time, and being brushed by people he trusts. He's looking for a family who will appreciate his playful personality while also giving him reassurance and a little extra companionship as he settles in.

Good with Other Pets?
Jojo's former family describes him as playful with other dogs, although he prefers to take his time getting comfortable. He's met several dogs at ACC and has generally been neutral and polite, often choosing to explore or check in with his handler rather than engage in play. He can become protective of his food and toys when other dogs approach, so any shared home would need careful management, including separate spaces for meals and special belongings. Slow, thoughtful introductions are recommended.

Good with Kids?
Jojo would do best in a home without children under 13 due to his resource guarding around other dogs and the need for thoughtful management around his belongings. He'll appreciate a family who understands his boundaries and gives him space to enjoy his favorite things.

Things to Know
Jojo can be a little shy when meeting new people, but he warms up with a gentle approach and plenty of encouragement. He walks on a loose leash, although he may feel nervous in unfamiliar surroundings. His former family also shared that he can become anxious when left alone and may chew or get into things when bored or stressed. He'll do best with a gradual transition to alone time, plenty of enrichment, and a predictable routine. Jojo is a loving little companion who simply wants to be where his people are, and he's ready to find someone who will let him be their very best buddy.
