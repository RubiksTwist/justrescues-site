Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Cream Puff is a 9-month-old mixed breed who weighs around 35 lbs and is basically sweetness with legs. She loves company, gives lots of kisses, and has a very cute habit of resting her paw on your arm or leg like she’s making sure you know she’s there.

She’s also a big fan of belly rubs and will happily roll onto her back to make that request very clear. Outside, Cream Puff is curious about everything. She loves sniffing her way through walks and saying hi to strangers, so the world is still one big interesting place to her.

She’s doing great with house-training and is an affectionate, social young dog who seems happiest when she’s close to people and involved in whatever is going on.
