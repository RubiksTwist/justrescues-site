Meet Sky! This sweet 5-month-old Lab loves nothing more than sprawling out on the couch, tucking under warm blankets, and resting her head right on her favorite humans. She takes a brief moment to warm up when meeting new people, relaxing comfortably once she has had a little time to say hello, and she enjoys playful yard time alongside her resident doggy foster sibling.

In her foster placement, Sky is a typical energetic puppy who is actively working on her house training, crate routine, and leash manners. She is making great progress as she learns the ropes of her daily schedule and builds her confidence on walks.
