Description provided by Bobbi and the Strays

This beautiful girl has not had an easy life. Abby’s mom was no longer able to care for her and so we stepped in to help. With her sweet and easygoing nature, Abby loves everyone she meets including children. She’ll sit closely with a human friend and simply enjoy the companionship. Having a squeaky toy nearby is a plus! One look into this beauty’s eyes and you will fall in love…

Abby is a 10 year old Pitbull Terrier mix who is up to date on vaccinations, heartworm negative, microchipped and spayed. She was recently diagnosed with cancer. Four mast cell tumors were removed. It is possible they could come back but as of now she is doing well. She is currently in foster and her foster parents love her! If you would like to meet Abby , please fill out the adoption application on our website using a laptop or desktop (no phone or tablet, please) at https://bobbiandthestrays.org/adoption-page-new/ .
