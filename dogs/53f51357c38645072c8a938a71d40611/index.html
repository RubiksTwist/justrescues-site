Meet Black Robin!
Black Robin is a total people pup who loves being close to his favorite humans! This wiggly, affectionate boy happily approaches for attention and may even try to climb into your lap for a face full of kisses. He was found as a stray, so we're still learning about his past, but it's clear that Black Robin has plenty of love to give.

My Ideal Home
Black Robin would love a home where he can spend plenty of time with his people and get lots of opportunities for walks, play, and enrichment. He's an enthusiastic boy who will benefit from some positive-reinforcement training to help him practice calmer greetings. In return, you can expect a very affectionate companion who is always happy to be by your side.

Good with Other Pets?
Black Robin's history with other dogs is unknown since he came to us as a stray. During a recent introduction, he briefly greeted another dog before choosing to walk away and give himself some space. Any introductions should be slow, thoughtful, and supervised, allowing Black Robin to decide when he's comfortable.

Good with Kids?
Black Robin's history with children is unknown. Since he's an enthusiastic greeter who loves jumping up for attention, his new family should help him practice polite greetings and make sure interactions are calm and respectful.

Things to Know
Black Robin walks with only mild pulling and happily follows along with his people, even when he's feeling extra bouncy. He's incredibly affectionate and seeks out contact, often trying to climb into his handler's lap for kisses. He's still working on keeping all four paws on the floor, but with some tasty rewards and a little practice, this lovable guy will be ready to put his best paw forward. Black Robin may be named after a bird, but this boy is definitely ready to fly straight into someone's heart!
