Description provided by NYC Second Chance Rescue

Meet Chance ❤️ — Chance’s story is one of survival, resilience, and hope.

At 11 years old, Chance was rescued in Brooklyn, where he had spent his life as an indoor/outdoor cat. The only indoor shelter he knew was a dirty basement — far from the comfort and safety every cat deserves. One day, the doors to the only place he knew as home closed on him, leaving him to fend for himself outside.

He continued returning to that same familiar spot, but when he was finally found, he was in heartbreaking condition. One of his eyes was bloody and turned, and his body was covered in what our veterinarians described as one of the worst flea infestations they had ever seen. He was severely anemic, weak, and lethargic. We feared he might not make it.

But Chance fought. Against the odds, and just when we were beginning to lose hope, he made a remarkable recovery. And that’s how he got his name — CHANCE.

Today, Chance is still adjusting to the many changes he has experienced over the past few months. After everything he’s been through, it’s understandable that he’s shy around new people and needs a little time to feel safe.

He’s an older boy who has already endured more than his share of hardship. What he needs now is someone willing to be patient, give him space to settle in, and show him that home can finally mean comfort, security, and love.

Chance has been given a second chance at life. Now we’re hoping someone will give him a second chance at a real home.

At 11 years old, he deserves to spend his golden years knowing what it feels like to truly belong. ❤️
