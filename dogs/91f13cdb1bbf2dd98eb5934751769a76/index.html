Meet Lady: Professional Ball Chaser, Bird Watcher & Full-Time Sweetheart 🐾

Lady is a sweet, playful, and incredibly smart 11-month-old girl with a big personality and an even bigger heart! She's equal parts adventurous athlete and professional couch potato. One minute she's sprinting after a tennis ball, and the next she's curled up next to you for a well-deserved nap.

Don't let her athletic abilities fool you - Lady is a total sweetheart who loves being close to her people. Whether you're watching a movie on the couch or lounging in the sun, she's loves to cuddle and nap next to you.

Lady is incredibly smart and eager to learn. She picked up potty training in just THREE days! She's also a huge fan of toys, especially anything that lights up, squeaks, or can be used for a good game of tug-of-war. Fetch might just be her favorite sport, and she's convinced she could go pro.

On walks, Lady is a dedicated sniffer and an enthusiastic bird watcher. She's curious about the world around her and loves exploring new places. She's interested in other dogs, though she can sometimes get a little too excited and is still working on staying calm during introductions.

Why You'll Fall in Love With Lady

Lady is the kind of dog who makes every day a little more entertaining. She's goofy, curious, athletic, and so incredibly sweet. Watching her experience new things is pure joy, whether it's discovering a new squeaky toy (or chicken jerky), chasing a ball across the yard, or finding the perfect sunny spot for her afternoon nap.

She's already learned so much in such a short time, and with the right family, we know she'll continue to blossom.

ALL adoptions require a completed application and final contract. We conduct veterinary and personal reference checks during the application approval process. We are unable to contact every applicant for every dog. We will select the best applicant for each dog, based on the dogs needs.
