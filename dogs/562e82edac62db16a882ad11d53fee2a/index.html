🖤 Meet Little Liberty! 🤍

Liberty’s story began on the Fourth of July, when she and her siblings were rescued in Jamaica, Queens. They all needed a little extra TLC, and Liberty quickly showed us she was the bravest of the bunch!

While she can still be a little shy at times, she’s also got quite the personality. Liberty is a vocal girl who isn’t afraid to speak up when she wants attention or when it’s time for dinner. And trust us, she likes things on HER schedule! 😂

She gets along wonderfully with other cats, but we know she’d absolutely thrive with a family of her own. She’s come such a long way, and now it’s her turn to experience the comfort, love, and stability of a forever home.

Liberty has already shown us how brave she can be. Now she just needs someone to give her a reason to feel right at home. 🐾
