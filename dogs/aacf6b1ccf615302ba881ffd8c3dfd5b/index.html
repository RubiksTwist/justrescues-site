Description provided by Rescue City

Meet adoptable Blueberry, our 13 lb, 2-month-old Terrier mix who might just be the sweetest little girl EVER! When Blueberry first arrived in her foster home, she was a shy little thing, hiding between legs and snuggling into coats for comfort. Fast forward, and she’s blossoming into the happiest, cuddliest, most curious little puppy! She loves following her humans EVERYWHERE, playing with her toys, collecting treats for being a good girl, and cuddling with absolutely anyone willing to hold her. She’s even convinced her foster family’s not so cuddly Husky to become her snuggle buddy! Blueberry is one smart cookie, too! She’s learning commands like sit, stay, and leave it, doing great with her wee-wee pads, and sleeping through the night in her crate. She’s still building confidence around unfamiliar sounds and learning all the things that come with being a puppy, but she’s making the sweetest progress! She’s dreaming of her forever home with lots of love, patience, a cozy spot to cuddle, and humans who don’t mind having a tiny shadow following them around.
