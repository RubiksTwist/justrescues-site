Truffle is the happiest and most loving dog! He is amazing with all humans and would make a great family dog. He loves to give kisses, enjoys belly scratches, playing tug-of-war and fetch while also being able to lay and watch a movie with you. He's great in his crate during the day and very polite at home. He will follow you around like he's your shadow. With a routine and consistent positive reinforcement he will thrive!

Truffle is a super loving and happy dog. He wants nothing more than to be by your side. He loves attention and lots of belly rubs. He's happiest even being in the same room with you while he plays with his toys. He's your perfect work from home companion. He would do best in a home with an outdoor space to let out some of his energy, but can also do well with someone who has an active lifestyle and can give him the exercise and stimulation he needs. He gets very excited when heading for a walk and is on full alert of his surroundings when outside. When he sees another dog he gets excited, but a quick tug will get his attention again. He needs structure and consistency, but with positive reinforcement and training he'll continue to learn and do great. His sweet face will completely steal your heart!
