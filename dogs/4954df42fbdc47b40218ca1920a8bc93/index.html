Description provided by Bideawee

Kobe is one of those dogs everyone falls in love with. He’s the absolute nicest boy; gentle, affectionate, and so well-behaved both at the shelter and in his foster home. Kobe loves his toys, loves his people, and especially loves to cuddle (he’ll do his best to fit on your lap, no matter the size!). He’s been with us for quite some time now, and while we adore him, he truly deserves a loving home of his own.
Kobe has made a few doggy friends and would make a great furry sibling to a well-matched pup. If you’re looking for a dog who’s as sweet as they come and ready to fill your life with love, Kobe’s your guy.
