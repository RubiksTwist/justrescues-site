Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

With her mesmerizing light green eyes and sweet pink nose, Julie is a quiet beauty who’s come a long way. Rescued from a hoarding situation, she’s slowly learning what it means to feel safe and loved. Once obsessed with feather wands, Julie now prefers the calmer things in life and has become a very chill companion. While she can be a bit shy around new people, she gets along well with other cats and has recently discovered the joy of being petted—giving in to those happy purrs more and more each day. This once under-socialized girl has made incredible progress at ARF. Just imagine how much more she could thrive in a home of her own. Julie is ready for her next chapter—could it be with you?
