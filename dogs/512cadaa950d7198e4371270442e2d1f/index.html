Meet Sansa, an adorable, spunky 7-month-old, 8 pound domestic shorthair kitten who is ready to find her forever home! This little girl is full of love, curiosity, and playful kitten energy. She loves to snuggle, especially with her littermate, but she’s just as happy pouncing on anything and everything that catches her eye. When she’s not busy perfecting her hunting skills, you can find her perched by the window, watching the world go by like it’s her own personal television show. Sansa has a special way of showing her love, too! The moment she spots her favorite person, her purr motor kicks into high gear, and she’ll weave in and out of your legs as she follows you around the house. If you work from home, that’s even better! Sansa fully believes she belongs on your lap—or curled up on your leg—providing all the cuddles and companionship you never knew you needed. With her playful energy, affectionate heart, and irresistible kitten charm, Sansa is sure to bring endless smiles, laughter, and love to her new family. Inquire about her today!
