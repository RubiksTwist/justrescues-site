Meet Drama!
Drama is a social, affectionate guy with plenty of energy and a whole lot of personality! His former family describes him as friendly, outgoing, and playful, and says he absolutely loves the water. Whether he's going for a walk, splashing around, or hanging out with his people, Drama is happiest when he's part of the action.

My Ideal Home
Drama would love an active home where he can enjoy plenty of walks, playtime, and adventures with his people. He's a smart guy who may already know some cues like Sit, Stay, Down, and Wait, and puzzle toys and food games will give him fun ways to keep his brain busy. After a good day of activity, he'll be ready to relax and enjoy some quality time with his favorite humans.

Good with Other Pets?
Drama's former family says he can get excited about other dogs and may bark or try to play with them. He showed interest in another dog during a recent introduction, but also became a little too enthusiastic, so any new dog friendships should be introduced slowly and in a controlled setting. For now, dog parks aren't his scene, but positive reinforcement can help him learn to have fun around canine friends without all the drama.

Good with Kids?
Drama would do best in a home without young children under 5. Older kids who understand how to give a dog space and respect his boundaries may be a better fit.

Things to Know
Drama is a very affectionate, energetic boy who walks beautifully on leash and happily approaches people for attention. He can be a little sensitive about having his paws handled, so his new family should take a patient, reward-based approach to grooming and build positive associations with touch. His former family also described him as generally anxious, so plenty of exercise, enrichment, and fun things to focus on will help keep this playful guy feeling his best. There's plenty of personality packed into this handsome boy, and he can't wait to land a starring role in someone's everyday life.
