Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

Miss Roxanneis the goodest girl on the block! A certified lover girl and an absolute sweetheart who will steal anyone’s heart. She’s a ball of energy, loves her walks, playing with her toys, and diving into your lap for snuggles. If she could crawl into your skin she would. Despite her energy, she still has quiet hours, loves a nap, and is easily entertained by her Kong toy. Shes still learning her manners but is easily directed, and is making lots of improvements very quickly! She’s very quiet and almost never barks, except a small bark if someone knocks on the door. She really is an angel, never mischievous and really seems to want to make you happy. She’s a curious girl, loves being your shadow, and loves people and bird watching on her walks. On her walks she can be a bit of a tugger in new environments but has been easing into her routine walks. She is great in her crate, settles in a few minutes and sleeps through the night. If you’re looking for a goofy love bug to jump into your arms, this sweetheart is for you!

﻿Roxanne is approximately 6y 1m 12d old and is fully grown at 43 pounds. They are microchipped and up to date on all age-appropriate medical care. Their breed is unknown as we have not had a DNA test done.
