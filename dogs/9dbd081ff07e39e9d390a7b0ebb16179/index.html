Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

Mochi is a very good boy with a lot of love to give. You'd never guess he's almost six years old given how playful and energetic he is. He'll do best in a home where he gets regular walks, outdoor playtime, and mental stimulation. He likes tug, fetch, and doing lots of sniffing. He also loves being with his humans and feeling like part of the pack. If he gets the activity and attention he needs, he's happy to spend the rest of his time (most of his time) napping on the couch or in a patch of sunlight on the floor. We're working on his training — he's smart, good natured, and very food motivated, which helps. We always have kibble on hand, indoors and out, to reward his good behavior. On our regular walking route, Mochi is good on the leash, keeping pace alongside us. He doesn't lunge at other dogs, though sometimes he'll play bow or even lie all the way down in anticipation when he sees them coming down the street. He does do some scavenging, so we're working on "leave it" and "drop it." When walking in new places and/or with new people, he will pull on the leash. Also in new places and with new people, he can be skittish and fearful. You'll know he's anxious when both of his ears are down — 99% of the time, one of them is standing at attention! TLDR: he's a total sweetie.

﻿Mochi is approximately 5y 11m 10d old and is fully grown at 68 pounds. They are microchipped and up to date on all age-appropriate medical care. Their breed is unknown as we have not had a DNA test done.

Although our dogs are listed as unknown breed, buildings with specific dog restrictions typically enforce their rules based on what breeds a dog appears to resemble. It's always important to keep that in mind and know your building's policies before adopting a dog.
