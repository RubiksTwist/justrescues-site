Meet Phoenix | Apply Now

Meet Phoenix! At just 11 months old and 75 pounds, this purebred Golden Retriever is a big, goofy, sweet-natured boy with quite an interesting backstory.

Phoenix was surrendered to us by a foreign worker living in South Korea who had brought him over from France. According to his previous owner, he is a fifth-generation French Golden Retriever from a breeder. While his pedigree makes for an interesting story, what matters most to us is finding him the right forever home.

Phoenix has all the wonderful qualities you would expect from a Golden Retriever. He's friendly, affectionate, playful, and has a fantastic personality. He gets along well with other dogs, children, and even cats, and has the potential to be an incredible family dog.

Although Phoenix is gentle and sweet, he's still a big puppy who doesn't quite realize his size! He can get excited and jump up on people, so he'll need someone committed to helping him develop good manners and continue his training.

We are looking for a foster-to-adopt home for Phoenix, ideally with someone who has experience with Golden Retrievers or similar large breeds, can comfortably accommodate his size, and is genuinely invested in training and providing him with the structure and exercise he needs.

We will be thoughtful and selective about his placement. Phoenix has so much potential, and we want to make sure his future family is prepared to meet his needs, not just excited about bringing home a Golden Retriever.

He's a truly wonderful young dog with a gentle disposition, a big personality, and an even bigger heart. With the right guidance and commitment, Phoenix is going to make an exceptional lifelong companion.
