Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

Times Square can’t shine as bright as Delilah. Meet the silliest and sweetest lady. This gal might be a senior citizen, but she has so much to give! We have loved being her foster parents. She can be a little shy when meeting new people, but give her a day or two and she will be your new favorite shadow.

She has pretty severe arthritis in her back right leg, but that does not keep her from sharing her spunky spirit! She LOVES walk time and is active around the home. She follows me from room to room (and then snoozes in that room - but she’ll get up and move herself to the next room if I move!). Watch her video of her park zoomies! With pain management meds, this gal thrives!

She lives in a home with a rabbit. Thus far they can see each other but not directly interact and it’s been fine! Delilah doesn’t pay him any mind. We live in bustling midtown Manhattan and she has also been just fine with the environment. Cars, bikes, people - they don’t bother Delilah! Sometimes she wants to say hi to dogs, but she is very easy to usher along. She’s just the best.

﻿Delilah is approximately 12y 1m 22d old and is fully grown at 78.5 pounds. They are microchipped and up to date on all age-appropriate medical care. Their breed is unknown as we have not had a DNA test done.
