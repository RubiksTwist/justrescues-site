Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Webster is a 1-year-old chihuahua mix who weighs around 12 lbs, and he is an absolute sweetheart. He’s an easy, laid-back little guy who’s just as happy heading out for a walk as he is hanging out near you on the couch.

Webster can be quite timid at first, so he needs a little time to feel safe and get to know you. Once that trust starts to build, his sweet side really comes out, including a serious appreciation for belly rubs. He’s also house-trained and crate-trained, making him a pretty easy companion at home.
