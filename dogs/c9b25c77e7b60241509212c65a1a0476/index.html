Paisley is ready for some fun!

Paisley is a 1-year-old girl who loves to explore and spend time with her people. When it's time to take on the world, she enjoys outings to a quiet local park or peacefully lounging in the backyard with some sunshine.

Here at the Adoption Center, Paisley has been having a great time with her friends in doggy playgroup! She loves getting the zoomies, and she especially enjoys a good game with a favorite toy. A cute quirk about Paisley is when she's feeling sassy, she loves to let you know! This sweet girl won't shy away from giving a gentle howl between running around and playing. Paisley is also a cuddle bug who seeks out affection from people and will happily follow along when there are pets and attention to be had!

Come meet Paisley and start your adventures together! To learn more about Paisley, click the “Adopt Me” button.
