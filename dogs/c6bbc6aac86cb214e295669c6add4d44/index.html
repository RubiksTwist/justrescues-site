Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

At just 8 months old and 35 lbs, Lexi is the sweetest girl with the softest velvet fur. Although Lexi has a perpetual pouty face, she is a asoft and wiggly puppy with an insatiable desire for pets and affection. Lexi is a true lover girl and can almost always be found where her people are - she's basically a built in shadow.

Lexi is happily crate trained for up to 4 hours of which she will nap or chew on a dog bone to keep herself busy. Her foster mom hasn't returned home to any accidents, or noise complaints and simply returns home to a little girl that is happy her human is back!

Lexi is nearly fully housebroken with the occasional puppy accident. Outdoors, Lexi is also mastering leash walking. This good girl is completely non reactive to dogs and people and she could not care less about the noisy city chaos and sounds. Lexi doesn't pull on the leash and is simply learning how to walk nicely next to her person and not zig zaggy or behind them.

Lexi is extremely trainable and really loves making her people proud. With training, Lexi has been semi-food motivated but is highly motivated by physical affection and kind words. In fact, at any given time Lexi may just plop down in between your legs, where she feels the safest and fall into a deep slumber.

Lexi is currently in foster care with a resident dog. They have been sweet, affectionate and happy to co-exist in a shared space with one another. The more time they spend together, the more respectfully interested the become with one another and in one weeks time, they have graduated to sniffing and wiggly tail wags. In no time, we expect to see them playing and napping together. Lexi is absolutely a pup who can live with another dog happily however she is also that pup who will always prioritze love from her humans over anything else in the world.

Overall, Lexi is simply intuitively a good dog. She is gentle, loving, affectionate and respectful and can easily acclimate into any home that provides her the soft love and the soft life that she deserves.
