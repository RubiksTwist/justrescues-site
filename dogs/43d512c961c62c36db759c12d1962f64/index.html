Meet Robot Vacuum!

75 pounds of love, 6 years young, and still waiting for her happily ever after.

Robot Vacuum is a beautiful brindle pittie with a heart just as big as her blocky head. She's dog-friendly with pups of all sizes, has lived in a foster home with up to FIVE other dogs, and is wonderful with the children in her foster home. She's house-trained, crate-trained, and a medium-to-low energy girl who enjoys the simple things in life.

Her favorite activities? Going for walks, soaking up the sunshine on the deck, giving hugs, and snuggling up on the couch with her favorite people. With the colder weather ahead, Robot Vacuum would make the perfect cozy companion!

As a strong, 75-pound girl, Robot Vacuum would do best with an adopter familiar and comfortable with the breed. She can be protective, but she's responsive to correction and redirects well. We believe she'd thrive in a home outside of the city where she can enjoy a little more space.

Robot Vacuum has been waiting far too long for someone to see just how amazing she is. There's so much more to her than her size or her beautiful dark brindle coat. She's loyal, loving, affectionate, and ready to be someone's very best friend.

Could you be the one she's been waiting for?
