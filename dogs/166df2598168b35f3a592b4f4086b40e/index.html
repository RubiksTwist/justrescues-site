Star is a 2-year-old Jindo Mix weighing 31.7 lbs. Rescued from a city shelter as a tiny puppy, Star spent his early life in rescue, but a recent foster stay proved he's more than ready for a real home. Social, affectionate, and always ready to come running the moment you call his name, Star is a boy who wears his heart on his sleeve. He can't wait to find his forever home in NYC!
