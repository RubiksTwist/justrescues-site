Description provided by NYC Second Chance Rescue

Mo is a goofy guy who’s equal parts snugglebug and energizer bunny. He came to us underweight but with good food, meds and TLC, he’s gained weight and his zest for life is off the charts.

This playful guy loves toys, balls and just about any excuse to have fun. He’s dog-friendly and very social. He’s also a quick learner and picks up new cues fast - especially if there’s a snack involved.

Mo is affectionate, funny and full of personality. If you’re looking for a companion who will keep you laughing, keep you moving and brighten every single day, Mo’s your guy.

All dogs will be altered, microchipped, and receive age-appropriate vaccines prior to adoption.
