Meet Winslow! He is currently in foster care and ready for adoption via our virtual process! Winslow is an 8-month-old medium mixed-breed male weighing approximately 34 lbs. Winslow is the sweetest boy who loves spending time with his people! He’s calm and playful at home, enjoys playing with toys, and likes to follow his favorite people wherever they go. He already knows some basic obedience cues and is eager to keep learning. Winslow is still working on his housetraining and learning to stay calm around other dogs. If you’re open to a little patience and a lot of possibility, come meet Winslow! You might be the person who helps them feel safe, confident, and right at home.
