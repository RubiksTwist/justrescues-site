Meet Delilah!
At 12 years young, Delilah is ready to spend her golden years surrounded by love and comfort. Found as a stray, this sweet senior girl is still getting used to her new surroundings, but she's shown us a softer side with her wagging tail and willingness to greet people. She's looking for a cozy place to call home and someone who will give her the time and patience she deserves.

My Ideal Home
Delilah would love a calm, comfortable home where she can settle in at her own pace. She'd enjoy leisurely walks, sniffing around the neighborhood, and spending quality time with her favorite people. Enrichment like food puzzles and long lasting chews can keep her mind busy, too. Most of all, she's looking for someone who will make her feel safe and cherished.

Good with Other Pets?
Delilah showed no reaction to other dogs while walking at ACC, but we don't know much about her history with other animals. Slow, thoughtful introductions with any resident pets will help everyone get comfortable.

Good with Kids?
Delilah's history with children is unknown. Calm, gentle interactions and giving her space to get comfortable will help her feel at ease around new family members.

Things to Know
Delilah walks with only mild pulling and readily approaches people with a soft body and wagging tail. She can feel a little unsure during hands on interactions, so it's important to let her choose when she's ready for attention and never rush her. With a peaceful home, a gentle approach, and time to settle in, this lovely senior has the chance to enjoy the comfort and companionship she deserves. Delilah's golden years could be golden indeed with you by her side!
