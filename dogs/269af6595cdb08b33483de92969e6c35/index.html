Description provided by PupStarz Rescue

Digital Picture Frame is a beautiful rosebud waiting to blossom into the most sensational flower ever. All this tender-hearted 5-year old, 17-pound Rat Terrier mix needs is someone to show her the way and treat her like the sweet, loving, gentle creature that she is.

Digital Picture Frame is docile and kind around humans and other dogs. She isn’t quite sure of the world around her, but if you calmly sit with her, she likes being pet and massaged. She hasn’t been walked outdoors yet, but she dutifully does her business on the pee pads inside. She eats well, she gobbles up treats, and she isn’t at all possessive when it comes to her food, her bed, or her toys. Digital Picture Frame is trying so hard to be brave, and with each passing day, you can see clear signs of progress.

This lovely angel might be a bit quiet and initially shy, but she absolutely loves being spoken to. Does she understand English? She sure seems smart, so ya never know! Digital Picture Frame is as pretty as a picture, and it’s just a matter of time before she becomes the playful, curious, and confident dog she was always meant to be. Wanna give this girl the home she deserves? You won’t be sorry.
