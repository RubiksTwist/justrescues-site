Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

All 10 months and 35 lbs of Owen is packed full of sweetness. 🐾

Despite still be a young lad, Owen is the total package! Owen is crate trained, potty trained, ridiculously dog friendly and an utter love of all people.

In his foster home, Owen enjoys spending time playing with his furry foster sibling and would be thrilled to have a canine companion. More than anything however, Owen's absolutely favorite place to spend time is cuddled in your lap.

Owen is a dream on leash, does not pull and checks in with his walker consistently. He know's his basic commands such as sit, lay down and stay. Owen is also a very quick learner and really wants to make his people proud. After Owen's walks and any training practice, he is happy to spend his days cuddled up next to his person.

Owen has wonderful manners, an affectionate personality , a gentle nature along with an immense love for people and dogs making him an incredibly special pup.

Owen will do well in anyhome regardless if his new family is searching for a cuddle buddy or an adventure partner - Owen is happy to be both in exchange for the love of a forever family
