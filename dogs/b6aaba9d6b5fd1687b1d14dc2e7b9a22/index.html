Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

Mossie is a sweet and quirky pup who loves nothing more than a good cuddle! She was found as a stray in New York, and has been absolutely loving her new luxurious life as a couch potato.

Mossie was quite nervous and shy when we picked her up, but just a few hours later she was passed out on my shoulder, snoring loudly with her tongue hanging out. In the days since she has truly shown us her personality! She loves to play with toys, race around the living room, and get up to your typical puppy mischief. She is very eager to befriend our resident dog (who is still deciding how she feels about a bouncy puppy). She also truly knows how to get comfortable.

Mossie is making great progress adapting to apartment living. At first, she didn’t want to eat regular meals and was too scared to go down the stairs, but we conquered both of these challenges in no time! Being outside still makes her nervous, and we are now working on feeling safe enough to use the bathroom outside.

Give this beautiful girl a chance and you'll have a best friend for life!!

﻿Mossie is approximately 0y 4m 27d old and may be 45 to 55 pounds when fully grown. They are microchipped and up to date on all age-appropriate medical care. Their breed is unknown as we have not had a DNA test done.

Although our dogs are listed as unknown breed, buildings with specific dog restrictions typically enforce their rules based on what breeds a dog appears to resemble. It's always important to keep that in mind and know your building's policies before adopting a dog.
