Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Miley is a 3-year-old mixed breed who weighs around 29 lbs, and she is one seriously smart girl. She responds beautifully to training and structure, picks up new skills incredibly quickly, and is eager to learn. Positive reinforcement goes a long way with Miley, and she seems to genuinely enjoy figuring out what you’re asking of her.

She can be quite timid with unfamiliar people at first and does best when given the space to approach on her own terms. Once she feels comfortable, though, she is incredibly sweet and affectionate. Miley also absolutely loves other dogs! She’s social and playful, frequently inviting them to interact, and has had lots of positive encounters with dogs of different sizes and ages. She can be a little cautious with larger dogs initially, but usually relaxes quickly and is soon ready to join the fun.

The city and all of its distractions can still feel a little overwhelming for Miley, so she’s been working on her leash skills and making a lot of progress. Her excitement around other dogs can sometimes lead to some pulling, but she responds well to redirection. She’s crate-trained and mostly house-trained, and will do best with someone who enjoys continuing to work with a bright, responsive dog and helping her keep building confidence.
