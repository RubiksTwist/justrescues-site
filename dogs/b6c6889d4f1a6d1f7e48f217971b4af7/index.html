Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Miley is a 3-year-old mixed breed who weighs around 29 lbs and is the kind of dog who picks her person and then takes that job very seriously. She’s a total cuddle bug, follows her foster from room to room, and seems happiest when she can be close to someone she trusts.

She does well with other dogs and loves to play, although she’s still learning that not every dog is always in the mood to join her. She’s also making good progress with house-training and starting to settle into city life.

Miley will need a patient, empathetic adopter who understands that she’s still figuring out what it means to feel safe and just be a dog. Humans haven’t always been kind to her, so she can take some time to warm up to strangers and will benefit from continued training and confidence building. She’s not looking for someone expecting perfection. She’s looking for someone willing to give her the time and consistency she needs, and in return, she’ll be an incredibly loyal and loving companion.

Registered adopters interested in Miley can email adopt@hbrdogs.com for more info. If you’re not registered yet, do it today at hbrdogs.com!
