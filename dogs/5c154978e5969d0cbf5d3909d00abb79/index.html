📸 Meet CAMERA (aka Buddy)! 📸

Forget fancy gadgets and complicated settings. Camera is all about keeping things simple: a comfy couch, a cozy bed, some good food, and a human to snuggle with.

At 4 years old and 23 pounds, this Chihuahua/Terrier mix is basically a living, breathing stuffed animal. He's a professional couch potato who isn't looking for hiking trails, marathon walks, or games of fetch. His idea of a perfect day? Being close to his people and enjoying the good life.

Camera is house-trained, walks nicely on leash, knows SIT, loves car rides, and gets along with small dogs. He's also been wonderful with a 10-year-old child. Toys aren't really his thing, and he's not much of a barker. He'd much rather spend his time cuddling than playing!

Now, we should mention that Camera is a bit of a foodie. Dry kibble hasn't impressed him, and he prefers wet or home-cooked meals. He's not crate-trained, but he's an easygoing little guy who would love a home where someone is around to keep him company.

If you're looking for a low-key companion, a work-from-home buddy, or someone to keep your couch warm, Camera might just be the perfect shot!

He's ready for his close-up and hoping his next stop is FOREVER!
