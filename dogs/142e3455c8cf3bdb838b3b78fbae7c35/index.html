Meet Konfetti!

Konfetti may have arrived as a stray, but she certainly knows how to make an entrance! This wiggly, affectionate girl loves being close to her people and will happily seek out pets, attention, and treats. She leans into affection, gives kisses, and is always ready to sprinkle a little joy wherever she goes.

My Ideal Home

Konfetti would love a family who can keep her busy with walks, enrichment, and reward based training. She is still working on her manners and can get a little enthusiastic when asking for attention or treats. With consistency and positive reinforcement, she'll be ready to party like it's her job!

Good with Other Pets?

Konfetti's history with other dogs is unknown. She has shown some mixed feelings during dog introductions, becoming tense and barking during one interaction while choosing to walk away and seek out her people during another. Any future dog introductions should be slow and carefully managed.

Good with Kids?

Because Konfetti can be mouthy when taking treats and seeking attention, ACC recommends a home without children under 5 at this time.

Things to Know

Konfetti is still learning that good things don't have to be grabbed at full speed! She can take treats with a hard mouth and may jump up when she's excited, so she'll benefit from practicing calm ways to ask for what she wants. If you're looking for a sweet, affectionate girl who is ready to shower her new family with love, Konfetti is ready to make your life a little more festive.
