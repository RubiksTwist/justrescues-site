Description provided by Waggytail Rescue

Ollie could so use a foster home, or even better a foster- to- adopt home! Ollie's a young adult kitty with the most gentle heart.

Needless to say, Ollie is a very handsome gray-and-white cat who believs the best friendships are worth taking a moment to discover.

Ollie is the kind of boy who quietly watches the world, chases a toy with surprising enthusiasm, and happily leans in for a few gentle head scratches. He’s curious, easygoing, and has the sweetest, soulful eyes that seem to say, “I think I’d like to be your friend.”

Ollie is one of those cats who blossoms a little more every day. We’re sure that with a cozy home and maybe even a feline buddy to show him the ropes, we think his silly, cuddly side is just waiting for its happily ever after.

If you’ve been looking for a gentle companion whose story is only just beginning, Ollie can’t wait to write the next chapter with you.

Ollie is up to date on his vaccines, dewormed, microchipped, and neutered.

Please apply using the following instructions if you are interested in adopting one of our Waggytails: Visit our website (www.waggytailrescue.org) for an adoption application. It is important to fill out an application as soon as possible for consideration, and we require a complete application for you to meet an animal. We are currently unable to respond to general email inquiries as an all-volunteer run rescue. If the cat is on our website, they are still available.

Waggytail Rescue CANNOT guarantee the exact age of any adoptable cat, or final size of any of our adoptable kittens. We rely on the information provided to us by veterinarians, shelters, owner surrenders and other circumstances bringing the cat into our rescue.

Adoptions: adopt@waggytailrescue.org
