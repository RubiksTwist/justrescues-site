Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

My foster writes: When you first meet Tazzie she is shy and reserved, but don't let that fool you. Once you get to know her you will feel as if you've known her for ages! Tazzie is a sweet and caring dog who is a sucker for cuddles, belly rubs and chin rubs. She is a very loyal girl who will give you all the love in her heart and is sure to stay by your side. She is the perfect balance between chill and energetic: She can handle long walks in the park, playing fetch until her tennis ball is worn out, but is also down for a chill day at home where she can just lay down and snore away! She also does very well in car rides and will either sleep the whole ride or silently watch out the window. Whatever the plan is with Tazzie, she is the perfect dog to have by your side and will mold perfectly into a calm, loving home!

What my friends at ACC say about me:

My history is a mystery and my friends here do not know much about me yet!

I love to be loved, but on my own terms! Let's brush up on some canine body language together!
