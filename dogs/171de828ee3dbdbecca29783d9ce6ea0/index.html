Description provided by Bobbi and the Strays

This adorable pair of BONDED pups is ready for the wonderful life they deserve TOGETHER! Fannie and Harley, who we believe are siblings, will need plenty of exercise, healthy meals, and lots of love! These cuties are very playful, super active, and enjoy being held and petted. They are good with most other dogs but are not fans of cats.

Our dynamic duo are 2 year old Shih Tzus who are up to date on vaccinations, microchipped, and altered. They are currently at our Atlas (Queens) location. If you would like to meet them, please fill out the adoption application on our website using a laptop or desktop (no phone or tablet, please) at https://bobbiandthestrays.org/adoption-page-new/ .
