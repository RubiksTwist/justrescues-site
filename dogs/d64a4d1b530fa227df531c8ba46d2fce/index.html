Description provided by AMA Animal Rescue

Meet Meeko, a silly senior gentleman who is finally discovering just how good the sweet life can be!

After years of being confined to a small crate, without proper medical care, affection, or the chance to experience what it means to be loved, Meeko is finally getting the fresh start he has always deserved. Surrendered by his previous owners, this sweet boy missed out on so much, but he's making up for lost time one cozy day at a time!

Meeko has come such a long way and has built so much confidence. Once a shy and uncertain boy, he's now showing everyone his silly side! He's discovered that life has some pretty wonderful perks, like finding the comfiest spot in the house (preferably right underneath a sunny window), enjoying a delicious meal, and spending his afternoons relaxing. He's also a smart cookie who loves a good puzzle, especially when there's a tasty reward involved. Who says you can't teach an old dog new tricks?

Now in his golden years, Meeko isn't asking for much. He would love a quiet, peaceful home where he can enjoy his favorite comforts and be surrounded by people who understand him. He gets along with other cats and could happily share his home with feline friends. He would still prefer a calm environment without young children, where he can enjoy his routine and settle in at his own pace.

Like any senior, Meeko will need a family prepared to provide the extra care and attention that comes with his age. In return, you'll get a wonderfully sweet, silly companion who has so much personality left to share. Give him a cozy bed, a sunny window, a good puzzle, and something yummy to eat, and you've pretty much made his day!

Meeko has spent enough time waiting for the good things in life. Now it's his turn to enjoy them. Could your home be the peaceful retirement he's been dreaming of?
