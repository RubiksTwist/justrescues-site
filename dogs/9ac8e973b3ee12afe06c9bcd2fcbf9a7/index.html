🐾 Meet Snappy (Islander)! Your Future Best Friend! 🐶❤️

Hi! My name is Snappy, also known as Islander! I’m a fun-loving, silly, affectionate boy who is ready to find my next foster home while I wait for my forever family. ❤️

I’m a big cuddle bug who loves being around my people. I enjoy cuddling in bed, getting lots of attention, going on adventures, visiting dog parks, and spending time with my furry friends. I’m a social boy who has lived with multiple dogs and would love a home where I have another friendly dog to play with and keep me company.

🏡 A little about me:

  • I’m potty-trained and crate-trained.
  • I can stay in my crate while my foster is at work and have been comfortable being home for a full workday.
  • When my foster is home, I enjoy having more freedom around the house.
  • I know commands like sit, stay, and lie down, and I understand cues for going outside and eating.
  • I LOVE food and treats!
  • I enjoy kids, other dogs, water, outdoor adventures, and dog parks.
  • I can pull a little on the leash and may bark at dogs I don’t know or particularly like, so I’ll benefit from continued leash training and positive guidance.

🐕 My ideal foster home:

I would love a loving, patient home that can give me attention, exercise, structure, and opportunities to socialize safely. A home with another friendly, compatible dog would be wonderful because I enjoy having canine companions. Slow introductions and proper supervision are important, just like with any dog.

I’m still learning and growing, so I need someone willing to keep working with me and help me become the best boy I can be.

❤️ Why I need a foster:

Through no fault of my own, I need a new foster home while my current foster works on finding stable housing. I’ve been loved, cared for, and given a place to grow, and now I need someone willing to open their heart and home to me temporarily.

Fostering me means giving me a safe place to land while I wait for my forever family. You could make a huge difference in my life!

Could you be my next foster? 🐾

If you have room in your home and heart for a sweet, goofy, cuddly boy like me, please consider fostering Snappy (Islander). I have so much love to give, and I’m just looking for someone to give me a chance. ❤️🐶
