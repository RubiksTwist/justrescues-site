Meet our handsome 9-month-old foster boy!
This sweet, loving, and charming pup is truly one of a kind. He has the best personality—affectionate, gentle, and full of joy. He absolutely loves being outside and thrives on adventures, long walks, and exploring the world around him.

He is great with kids and other dogs and would do best in a home with another dog sibling, as he doesn’t enjoy being the only dog. He’s very social and happiest when he has a furry friend to share life with.
He is well-behaved and non-destructive in the home. While I’m at work, he stays safely in his kennel, and once I’m home, he roams freely with no issues. He is fully potty trained, loves his food and snacks, and is eager to please.
At night, he prefers to sleep on the bed and does not like sleeping in a kennel—he’s definitely a cuddle bug. He loves water, knows how to swim, and even enjoys riding on my husband’s boat! A home with a backyard would be a big plus for him.

This boy is incredibly loving, loyal, and ready to be someone’s best friend. He would make an amazing family dog and is truly the best pup you could ever have. His nickname is Snappy Doodlebutt, because his happy wiggles and playful personality always keep us smiling. He’s full of love, charm, and puppy joy.
