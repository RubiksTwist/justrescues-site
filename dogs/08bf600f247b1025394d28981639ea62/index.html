Meet Nurse Jackie! 🐾

Affectionately known as Lucy in her foster home!

Short Bio:

Meet Nurse Jackie, a sweet, playful, and gentle little girl with a big love for other dogs! Her foster family calls her Lucy, and she’s already stealing their hearts.

Nurse Jackie may be a little shy when first meeting humans, but give her some time and you’ll see her adorable personality start to shine. She absolutely LOVES other dogs! Meeting her foster family’s resident dogs was a turning point for her, and she quickly came out of her shell, running around, playing, and making new friends.

While she’s still building confidence around people, Nurse Jackie is learning that human affection can be a wonderful thing, and she enjoys being petted! She’s a sweet girl who is slowly discovering how nice it feels to receive love and attention from her humans.

Nurse Jackie is still very much a puppy, with plenty of playful energy, but she also has a gentle, respectful side. She loves spending time with her sister and other dogs, and having furry friends around really helps her feel comfortable and confident.

She’s making wonderful progress every day, and with patience, love, and a little encouragement from a furry friend, Nurse Jackie is sure to continue blossoming into the amazing dog she’s meant to be!

Personality & Characteristics:

My foster dog is good with:

  • Dogs: YES! Absolutely loves other dogs and wants to be everyone’s friend.
  • Kids: Yes! Currently living with children ages 6 and 9. She’s comfortable around them but doesn’t actively seek out their attention just yet.
  • Cats: Not yet tested.

My foster dog is:

  • Crate trained: Does great sleeping overnight in a pen with her sister.
  • House trained: Work in progress! She’s doing well with frequent outdoor breaks and can hold it overnight, but she’s still learning.
  • Low shed: Yes!

3 Keywords for your pup: Playful, Gentle, Sweet

Energy Level (1 = calm, 10 = high energy): 7/10 like with any puppy or JRT she’s got some energy . She can also be a nibbler of things so watch your socks and shoes and rugs

Barker? Or Quiet Pup?: Very quiet so far! She hasn’t shown much interest in barking, although she may find her voice as she becomes more comfortable.

Walking Manners: Still building confidence on the leash. She’ll benefit from patient encouragement and continued practice. She does not enjoy wearing a collar but we are working on it !

Appetite: Good! She’s eating well and settling into her routine. So far doesn’t show any signs of food aggression

Favorite Things: Playing with her sister, making friends with other dogs, running around, and enjoying pets from her humans.

Commands/Tricks: Still learning! Nurse Jackie is currently working on building confidence and mastering new experiences.

Additional Notes: Nurse Jackie would love a home with patient humans who understand that she’s still learning to feel comfortable in her new surroundings. She enjoys being petted and is discovering the joys of human affection at her own pace. A friendly, playful resident dog could be a wonderful companion and confidence booster for her. She’s a sweet little girl with so much potential, and watching her blossom has been such a joy!
