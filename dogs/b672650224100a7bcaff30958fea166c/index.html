Meet Lucy Goosey!
Lucy Goosey is a happy, affectionate girl who loves making new human friends! She's a social butterfly with a wiggly personality and is always happy to lean in for some attention. Found as a stray, we're still learning about her past, but she's already shown us what a fun and loving companion she can be. She's got a little curiosity in her step and a whole lot of love to share!

My Ideal Home
Lucy Goosey would love a home where she can enjoy daily walks, explore the outdoors, and spend plenty of time with her favorite people. She loves investigating interesting things along the way, especially rustling leaves and fluttering birds, so she'll appreciate a little guidance on her walks. A home with plenty of enrichment, cozy places to relax, and opportunities for fun adventures would be right up her alley.

Good with Other Pets?
Lucy Goosey's history with other dogs is unknown. During a recent introduction, she seemed a little unsure and preferred to hang back rather than approach the other dog. She'll need slow, thoughtful introductions if meeting potential canine housemates. Lucy also has a strong interest in birds and other small animals, so a home without cats or small pets would be best for her.

Good with Kids?
Lucy Goosey's history with children is unknown. She's a friendly, affectionate girl with the people she's met here, and calm, respectful introductions will help her get to know new family members.

Things to Know
Lucy Goosey walks nicely on leash overall, although she can get enthusiastic when something catches her eye. She enjoys toys, but prefers to have a little space around her favorite things, so positive reinforcement and giving her room to enjoy them will be important. She's a big fan of human attention and happily leans in for pets, often greeting people with a wiggly body and plenty of enthusiasm. This sweet goose is ready to find her forever flock!
