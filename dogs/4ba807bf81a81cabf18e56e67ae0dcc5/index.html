Description provided by Animal Lighthouse Rescue

Introducing Teddy! If happiness had four paws and a wagging tail, it would be this boy! This adorable 4-month-old, 8-pound Chihuahua mix is searching for the forever family he's been dreaming of. Teddy is the definition of puppy joy. His tail never seems to stop wagging, and his contagious smile has a way of making everyone's day a little brighter. Whether he's running around with his siblings, splashing in the puppy pool, or zooming around the yard, Teddy is always ready for his next adventure. Like any growing pup, Teddy is busy learning his puppy manners. He's discovering how fun walks can be—although he still thinks it's more entertaining to walk you on the leash! He also loves a good game of fetch... well, at least the chasing part. Bringing the ball back is still a work in progress, but he promises to keep you laughing while he figures it out. With puppy energy for days and a heart full of love, Teddy is looking for a family ready to help him continue learning, exploring, and growing into the amazing dog he's destined to be. And fair warning—wherever you go, be prepared to make a few extra stops because everyone is going to want to say hello to this precious little guy! If you're looking for a loyal companion who will fill your home with laughter, love, and endless tail wags, Teddy is ready to meet you.

Teddy is neutered, microchipped, and up to date on his vaccinations. For more information about him, please fill out an application at www.ALRcares.com and email Shruti@alrcares.com. We schedule meet and greets for approved adopters only, an application is the first step in our approval process. Thank you!
