Meet Cosmo | Apply Here

This little lady is Cosmo, sister of Conan! She is 8 months old and 18lbs and has most definitely nailed the puppy eyes already. She’s everything you’d hope and dream of from a puppy - sweet, playful, a little cautious, and just so silly.

Cosmo was rescued alongside her siblings and mom from a dog meat farm not too long ago. Luckily since she was rescued young, most of her life has been filled with encouragement and play. To say Cosmo is a fan of her toys is an understatement - she will meticulously pull multiple toys out of the bin and take turns playing with each of them independently. As she gets more comfy in her foster home, her antics have become goofier!

She’s also a gem with her crate training and loves taking long naps in there while her fosters run errands or work from home. On walks she can be a little weary while exiting the building, but has quickly turned nervous walks into happy trots. A loud boom can still startle her but hey we’re all a little jumpy from time to time.

You don’t have to wait for an adoption event. In-person meet-and-greets are available in the NYC area, with virtual meet-and-greets available for adopters outside NYC.
