Meet Patti, one of three dogs rescued from severe neglect in Washington Heights, NY. After being used for breeding and denied basic care, Patti was left suffering with severe, painful matting and a urinary tract infection. Thankfully, NYC Second Chance Rescue stepped in to help secure her surrender, and she is finally safe in our care. Patti deserves a chance to leave her painful past behind and discover what it means to be loved.
