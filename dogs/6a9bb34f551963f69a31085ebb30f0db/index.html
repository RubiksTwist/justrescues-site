Meet Patio Furniture!

A 1-year-old, 13-pound tricolor Terrier mix with an adorable face, a playful personality, and a whole lot of love to give!

Patio Furniture is a happy little guy who loves making friends, whether they have two legs or four! He's playful, sweet, and always ready for some fun. He absolutely loves other dogs and would be thrilled to have a playful canine sibling who enjoys a good game of chase or a little wrestling match. He's also a big fan of human attention and is always happy to soak up some affection!

Patio Furniture is looking for a home that can keep up with his playful side. While he gets along with other dogs, he can be a little much for older or more laid-back pups who prefer their personal space. A home with another fun-loving dog or kids could be a great match!

Patio Furniture is still figuring out some of the finer details of being a well-rounded pup! House training, crate training, and leash walking are all works in progress, so he'll need a family ready to help him along the way with patience, consistency, and lots of encouragement.

Patio Furniture is a little bundle of fun wrapped up in 13 pounds of cuteness. If you're looking for a sweet, social pup who will bring plenty of playtime, cuddles, and personality into your home, Blair is ready to meet you!

If you would like to adopt this dog, please visit www.pupstarzrescue.org for an application!
