Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Meet Sticky! At around 2 years old, Sticky is a handsome Cane Corso mix with a heart that is somehow even bigger than he is. Seriously, just look at that face! Those sweet eyes, those big floppy ears, that adorable face… how could you possibly not fall in love.

Sticky is literally the definition of a gentle goofball. He's the sweetest, silliest, most lovable boy with so much love to give. He's playful, affectionate, easygoing, and just an all-around precious soul. He's completely potty trained, dog friendly, has shown no signs of resource guarding, and is even perfectly happy to let you wipe his paws clean after coming inside. What a gentleman!

He absolutely loves his walks and does wonderfully on the leash. And considering he's a big, floppy boy with plenty of enthusiasm for life, that's saying something! He's a joy to take out and explore with.

Don’t let that sweet face fool you… Sticky does have a little bit of goofy puppy energy left in him! He can get excited and occasionally show it through some jumping and mouthing. He also seems to be under the impression that he's a tiny lap dog, having absolutely no concept of his size. And when you tell him to stop? Well, he might just interpret that as an invitation to play! The best part is that he's very easy to redirect. He also does wonderfully in his crate, where he'll happily settle right down and snooze.

While Sticky is a friendly boy, the hustle and bustle of the city can sometimes be a little overwhelming for him. He'd much rather enjoy a peaceful stroll through a quieter neighborhood or a relaxing afternoon at the park. He can be a little shy when meeting people out and about, so he appreciates being approached gently and given a moment to warm up. Once he feels comfortable, his sweet personality really shines.

What Sticky wants most in this world is pretty simple: a human best friend to love, play with, go on adventures with, and curl up beside. He would absolutely thrive with a loving family and, ideally, a big backyard where he can stretch those big legs, play to his heart's content, and just be his goofy self.

There is so much love packed into this big boy. He's the kind of dog who will make you laugh every day, melt your heart with the smallest things, and remind you just how special it is to have a best friend waiting for you at home.

So, if you've been searching for the most handsome boy to steal your heart, a giant goofball to make you smile, and a loyal best friend to fill your days with love, Sticky might just be your guy! He's ready to give someone all the love in the world. All he needs now is a family to call his own and a forever home where he can be loved as much as he'll love them. Could that be you?
