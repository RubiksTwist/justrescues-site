Maddie is doing her best to settle in after so many changes in her life, and we’re giving her the time she needs to feel comfortable. Her former caretaker told us she’s a calm, sweet girl who loves people and absolutely loves attention. She enjoys being included in whatever is going on, getting belly rubs, playing, and especially being babied. In fact, they said she sometimes acts more like a baby than a dog!

We know Maddie has so much love to give, and we can’t wait to see her settled into a loving home where she can feel safe, comfortable, and completely adored.
