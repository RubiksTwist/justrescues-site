Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Meet Edith Lynn - a 39 lb girl full of love, life and an immense desire for friendship.

Edie is 8 years old so it is no surprise that she is the perfect combination of silly, sweet and extremely snuggly. As a long time family dog, Edith loves being wherever her people are and is happy to reimburse you for your company with a full on comedy show - this girl is so quirky she will keep you laughing anytime you lock eyes with her.

In the moments that Edie isn't slivering her way into inverstigating what you are up to, you can happily find her chewing on one of her toys or napping not too far away.

Edith's wonderful house manners extends beyong her award winning personality and mature ways. Edith is also fully potty trained making life with her just that much easier.

In her true bullyesque ways, Edith often likes to decide which direction she prefers to walk in and is actively becoming more willing to bargain a change in direction for treats and other forms of positive reinforcement.

Edith has been very neutral towards other dogs and has a long history of living with children as well. Unsurprisingly, as a small girl in a big world, Edith can be nervous when new people enter the home unknowingly and is eager to broadcast her social butterfly ways outside before venturing in doors as a pack.

Edith is a nosy little sidekick in the best possible way and truly one of the only times you won't find her in your business is if she is frequenting her crate, which is her favorite, safe space to relax, snooze and settle when she desires cozy and calm!

Overall, Edith Lynn is a puo with such a humanlike personality. Edith is equal parts goofy, curious, a tad bit stubborn and quite nosy - qualities which you can't help but love. Ediths ideal forever home is with a human or a family who wants to truly include her as a family member and actively spend life together, even if that means simplying hanging besing you while you work or snoozing at your feet after a busy day.

Edith Lynn is a true bestie for the restie and as she transitions into a forever home, an adopter who will help her build her confidence while she decompresses will be gifted with a lifetime of friendship in exchange.
