Pinky is ready to be your walking buddy!

Pinky is a lovely 4-year-old girl who enjoys being around people and getting attention. She is especially fond of pets and will happily lean against you to soak them up. Snuggling up next to her favorite human is Pinky’s idea of paradise! Think of how cozy you’ll be as winter sets in.

This sweet pup is also a pleasure to take out for a walk! She walks on a loose leash, stays close to her person and regularly checks in along the way. She has done well around other dogs while out and about, generally choosing to mind her own business. Whether you're heading out for a neighborhood stroll or enjoying some fresh air together, Pinky makes a lovely companion.

Come meet Pinky and see if she's the walking buddy you've been looking for! To learn more about Pinky, click the “Adopt Me” button.
