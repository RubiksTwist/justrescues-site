Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

Hello future family. Here is a little about me. I am a very calm and friendly girl. I make friends with anyone who wants to say hello to me. I love belly rubs and below the ear scratches, oh man those just put me in a great mood. I only do my business outside. I've gone places by car and tend to just nap during the car rides. I prefer plushy toys rather than balls.

While I may be a bit on the bigger side, my foster mom say I walk amazing on leash. I walk by her side and only get distracted by interesting smells but I quickly move on after a quick sniff. I have been around children and birds and I am very neutral around them. I usually sleep where the wind takes me, meaning one second I'm asleep on the kitchen floor, than next second I might be asleep in the living room or hallway. My foster mom always tells me I act like I am carrying the world on my shoulders due to the constant sighing but maybe I am because I make everyone's day brighter. I know commands like, sit, down and leave it. I don't jump on people but if you pat your chest and say up, I will gladly put my front paw on your chest and accept pets from there. I have trigger words like are you ready, come on, outside, and walk. I get excited because that means I am going outside. If I hear a knock on the door I wait for you to open and let me know if I can say hi, I especially react if you say, "who is that" and I excitedly go check if it is someone I know or met before.
