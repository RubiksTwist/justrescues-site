Pumpkin is the sweetest little snuggle companion who just wants to be near her people. At only 11 months old, she has such a gentle, loving personality and is happiest when she’s getting pets, relaxing nearby, or curling up with you. In the morning, when you let her out of the crate she immediately sits down to get pets. One of Pumpkin’s most endearing qualities is how affectionate she is. She’ll wag her whole tail just because you walked over to say hello. She loves attention and seems happiest simply knowing her person is close by. She’s also a big fan of treats, but her biggest motivation is truly love and affection. Pumpkin is an easygoing girl who enjoys hanging out in the apartment and being part of whatever you’re doing. Whether you're relaxing on the couch, working from home, or having a quiet night in, she just wants to be your little shadow. She’s looking for a forever home where she can give lots of love.
