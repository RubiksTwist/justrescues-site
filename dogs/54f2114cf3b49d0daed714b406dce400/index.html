Pre-Screener Form

Mister Max was surrendered to the care center by his previous owners due to their family circumstances. He is described by his previous owners as an active, playful, and independent dog. During his time at the care center, Max has shown fearful behavior around handling, particularly on returning to kennel when he head whipped at a staff member. In calmer contexts, though, he has shown social behavior with staff. His previous owner's report that he is sensitive to handling; he is fearful when his collar is grabbed has snapped when his paws are touched. He is also reported to resource guard, and may escalate to growling if his initial signals of hovering and tensing are not respected. Due to these concerns, we recommend placement with a New Hope partner who can provide a decompression period, any necessary behavior modification (force-free, positive reinforcement-based), and re-evaluate behavior in a stable home environment before placement into a permanent home.
