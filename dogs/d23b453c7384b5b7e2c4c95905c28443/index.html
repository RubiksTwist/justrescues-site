Meet Kazooie!

At 10 years old, Kazooie is ready for a comfy home where she can enjoy her golden years. She was found in a vacant home and is now looking for someone who will give her the patience and security she deserves. Once she's comfortable, she enjoys exploring, greeting her people, and leaning into affection, and she still has a playful side too!

My Ideal Home

Kazooie would love a calm, predictable home where she can take things at her own pace. She's affectionate once comfortable, but may need some time to decompress and build trust in a new environment. Gentle, positive reinforcement will help her feel right at home.

Good with Other Pets?

Kazooie has limited history with other dogs and came into ACC with another dog. She has shown interest in other dogs and even offered play bows, but can become a little too enthusiastic, so slow introductions will be important.

Good with Kids?

Because Kazooie can be fearful and defensive when stressed, ACC recommends an adult-only home at this time.

Things to Know

Kazooie loves gentle affection and has a sweet side that shines when she feels comfortable. She can be protective of her toys, so she'll need some space around her favorite things. Give this senior girl a little patience and she'll have the chance to show you just how special she is!
