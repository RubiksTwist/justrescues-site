Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Meet Android, a tiny girl with a personality that’s every bit as memorable!

Android may have a powerful-sounding name, but this little lady’s greatest strength is her ability to completely melt hearts. She’s curious, playful, and always ready to see what kind of trouble she can get herself into. Every toy is a new challenge, every corner is something to investigate, and every person is a potential new best friend.

Rescued as a stray with her four siblings after being found alone in East Hampton, she has a wonderfully mischievous kitten spirit, but once the adventures are over, she’s more than happy to power down and enjoy some cozy time. Forget super strength or energy blasts - Android’s real superpower is stealing hearts and making the best biscuits around.
