Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Looking for a little mischief to brighten up your Halloween? Meet Pugsley, an adorable kitten with a spooky name but an incredibly sweet heart - he was brought to ARF with his sibling, Wednesday, after being found by Good Samaritans in East Hampton.

This gentle boy has all the spooky-season charm you could ask for - with an extra helping of sweetness. Pugsley may have an adorably mysterious name, but his personality is anything but scary - he's loving, affectionate, and happiest when he gets all the pets and attention, even if he has to share it with his sibling sometimes.

Pugsley will happily trade creepy old mansions for cozy blankets, haunted hallways for warm laps, and Halloween mischief for lots of love. Adopt Pugsley and bring home your very own little piece of Addams Family magic!
