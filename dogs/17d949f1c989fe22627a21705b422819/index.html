🔔 Meet SMART DOORBELL (aka Coraline)! 🔔

Ding dong! Someone adorable is at the door, and she's hoping you'll let her into your heart! ❤️

At 7 years old and 17 pounds, Smart Doorbell is a precious Chiweenie mix with big brown eyes, a sweet little face, and a talent for making herself right at home on the softest couch she can find.

Coraline is a gentle, affectionate soul who wants nothing more than to be near her people. Her favorite place? Burrowed into the crook of your arm, preferably under a cozy blanket. She's basically a built-in heating system with four legs!

She's also a BIG foodie. Think happy little dinner dances, adorable puppy eyes from the kitchen, and a willingness to work for chicken breast. Just be warned: if there's something tasty on your plate, she'll be keeping a close eye on it. 😂

Cora enjoys leisurely walks and sniffing around outside, although the big world can still be a little intimidating. She's making progress with her leash skills and building confidence every day. She's house-trained in progress, uses puppy pads, and is learning that going potty outside earns her favorite reward: CHICKEN!

She's calm, polite, gentle with other dogs, and perfectly happy relaxing while her humans are away. She doesn't need a house full of toys or endless adventures. A soft couch, a loving human, and a predictable routine are pretty much her dream come true.

Cats and children are still unknown, and car and subway rides are works in progress. But with patience, encouragement, and a little chicken, this girl is blossoming beautifully.

So, who's ready to answer the door and welcome Coraline home? She promises to greet you with the sweetest eyes and the warmest snuggles!

And thanks to BARK's incredible generosity, her adoption fee is WAIVED through 12/2 or the first 50 adoptions, whichever comes first!
