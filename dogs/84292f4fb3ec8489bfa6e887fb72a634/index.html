Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Sweet, calm, and brand new to NYC, Luna is taking big-city life one step at a time. This 2 year old lab mix is still adjusting to life in the city, but she’s already proving she’s braver than she looks. Her first elevator ride made her a little nervous, and now she walks in like a pro!

Luna is a gentle, quiet girl who’s happy to take things at her own pace. She’s house-trained with no accidents, settles right into her crate, and does well on leash. She’s calm around people on the street and seems comfortable with the hustle and bustle, even if she’s not the type to rush over and say hello just yet.

Her foster can’t wait to see more of her personality come out as she settles in, and we’re guessing there’s a real sweetheart waiting to shine. Luna would be a lovely fit for a patient adopter who’s happy to give her a cozy, low-key home and the time she needs to blossom.
