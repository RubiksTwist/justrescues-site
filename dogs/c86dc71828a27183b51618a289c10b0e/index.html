Simba was surrendered to the care center as a stray, and we have no history on his behavior in a home environment. During his stay at the care center, Simba has displayed some defensive and conflicted behavior around handling. His was reported to have growled at his finder, and was tense during his medical exam though warmed up and was soliciting attention by the end. In his handling assessment, he showed escalating arousal as he approached and jumped on handlers with a stiff body and flagged tail, though did not escalate further. Due to these concerns, we recommend placement with an experienced New Hope partner who can provide a period of decompression, any necessary behavior modification (force-free, positive reinforcement-based) and re-evaluate behavior in a stable home environment before placement into a permanent home.
