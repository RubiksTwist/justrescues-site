Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Thimble is an adorable little puppy who is expected to stay small when fully grown, but her personality has absolutely no intention of following suit. She’s spunky, curious, feisty, and very interested in investigating everything around her.

She approaches the world like there’s always something new to figure out, which means she keeps her foster on their toes in the most entertaining way. And then, after all that exploring, she’ll pass out in some ridiculous little sleeping position looking impossibly sweet, as if she wasn’t just busy running the show five minutes earlier.

Like any puppy, Thimble will need a patient adopter who’s ready to teach her the ropes, give her structure, and help channel all that curiosity as she grows. She has a lot of character packed into a tiny package and is going to be a very fun little companion.

Registered adopters interested in Thimble can email adopt@hbrdogs.com for more info. If you’re not registered yet, do it today at hbrdogs.com!
