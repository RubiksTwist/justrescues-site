My foster writes: Meet Diva, the pup who has the best personality! Sweet, observant, and wonderfully easygoing, Diva would be perfect for someone looking for a calm companion who is loving but also independent. Diva loves being part of your day. Compared to toys, Diva put all her attention to people. She will follow you from room to room, check in on what you are doing, and then settle into her own spot for a nap. When craving for human food, she likes to sit nearby and watch every bite with hopeful eyes, but she is never demanding. When you come home, she will greet you with enthusiastic tail wags, tiny happy whines, and plenty of affection. She rarely makes a sound, but she is an excellent communicator-she is an expert of using her body language to express her needs. She learned "paw" in only two days, so there is more potential to explore!
