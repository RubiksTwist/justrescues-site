Description provided by NYC Second Chance Rescue

Meet Yellow, a handsome Lab mix who found himself at risk in a municipal shelter in Alabama. Thankfully, he made his way into rescue and now has the chance to find the loving home he deserves.

With his sweet face and lovable Lab-mix charm, Yellow is ready to leave his shelter days behind and start a new chapter. He’s the kind of boy who just needs someone to give him a chance and show him how wonderful life can be.

We can’t change the way Yellow’s story started, but we can make sure the rest of it is filled with love, safety, and a family of his own.

An adoption bio is only a snapshot of who an animal is. For more information about Yellow, including his personality, needs, and what type of home may be the best fit, please reach out to our Adoptions Team.

All dogs will be altered, microchipped, and receive age-appropriate vaccines prior to adoption.
