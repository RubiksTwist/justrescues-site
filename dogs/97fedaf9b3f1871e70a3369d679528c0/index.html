Meet Pixel Rocky.

Pixel Rocky is a big guy with a goofy personality, a friendly heart, and a little puppy still living inside that big body.

This handsome boy came to us all the way from Florida, and he has a way of making you smile. He can be a little aloof at first, but once he gets comfortable, he is wonderfully accommodating and happy to go with the flow. Sal describes him as a wonderful dog, and we can see why.

Pixel Rocky is friendly and has plenty of energy. He is still working on his leash walking, and because he is a big, strong guy, he will need an experienced owner who understands how to help a large dog continue learning and growing into his best self.

But beneath that big body is a sweet, goofy boy who is ready to have fun, make friends, and find his people. He has so much personality, and we would love to see him in a home that appreciates all the wonderful things that make him who he is.

If you’re looking for a big-hearted companion with a playful side and plenty of love to give, Pixel Rocky may be your guy.
