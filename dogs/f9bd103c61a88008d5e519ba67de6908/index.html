Cody is a sweet, gentle girl who just needs a little time and space to feel comfortable in a new environment. She was understandably unsure during her first couple of days with us, but it has been so rewarding watching her come out of her shell!

Once she feels safe, she’s affectionate, curious, and gets adorably excited to see her people. She’s now comfortable lounging all around the apartment, whether it’s on her bed, the carpet, or wherever she decides is her spot for the day. She is incredibly well house-trained and has settled nicely into our daily routine of walks, meals, treats, and plenty of naps.

Cody is a total sweetie who seems to thrive with a calm, patient home and gets more comfortable and trusting every day.
