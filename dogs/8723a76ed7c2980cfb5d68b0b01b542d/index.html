Description provided by AMA Animal Rescue

This little black kitten was found all alone at just one month old, left at the doors of our shelter. She was covered in fleas and sick, but thankfully, Freya received the medical care she needed and is now showing us just how much life she has to give!

Freya is a fiesty little firecracker who loves to play. Her favorite pastime? Chasing things and battling her beloved bee toy! She also has a hilarious habit of flipping herself over whenever you reach for her, ready to “fight” your hand with all four paws.

But underneath all that spunk is a sweet, affectionate kitten with an enormous personality packed into one tiny body. She’s playful, energetic, curious, and ready to find a family who will love every ounce of her big attitude.

Freya may have had a rough start, but she’s ready for the happy ending she deserves.
