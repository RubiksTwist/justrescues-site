Description provided by Waggytail Rescue

Willow and Meadow are sweet twin sisters. Willow is the tuxedo, and Meadow is the black long-haired kitty. They are both a little shy to start but warm up with Churu and wand toys. They are not vocal at all, but are quick to gobble up some fancy feast. They love to be scratched on their back and will pretty immediately start loudly purring. They love to wrestle and play tag with each other. When eating, they like to eat out of one bowl together even if you give them two bowls. Very sweet and demure little girls

We think they will do great in a home with other cats or children.

They is up to date on shots, FeLV/FIV negative, dewormed, microchipped, and spayed.

Please apply using the following instructions if you are interested in adopting one of our Waggytails: Visit our website (www.waggytailrescue.org) for an adoption application. It is important to fill out an application as soon as possible for consideration, and we require a complete application for you to meet an animal.
