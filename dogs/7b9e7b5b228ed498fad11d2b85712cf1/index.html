Description provided by PupStarz Rescue

Meet OLED TV! 📺❤️

He's 8 years old, 17 pounds, and comes with all the features you could want: built-in cuddle mode, excellent treat reception, outdoor adventures, and a low-energy setting that's always on.

OLED TV didn't have the easiest start to life, but this handsome dachshund mix is ready for a much brighter picture. He's house-trained, walks nicely on a leash, travels well, and gets along with dogs and cats. He's not much for toys or playing with other pets. He's more of a cozy-night-in, snuggle-on-the-couch kind of guy.

He's sweet, gentle, trusting, and incredibly tolerant. He may occasionally provide some dachshund commentary in the form of moans and wails, but hey, even the best TVs have a little background noise!

OLED TV isn't looking for a complicated setup. Just a loving home, a comfy place to land, some tasty treats, and people who will remind him every day that his best days are still ahead.

Ready to bring OLED TV home? This is one upgrade you won't regret!

These pups are part of a hoarding case PupStarz Rescue is helping with. While many are adjusting well, they may need a little extra time, patience, and understanding as they settle into their new lives.
