Meet Einstein

Einstein is a sweet senior who has already been through more than any dog should. He was surrendered to a Georgia shelter at a time when he needed help the most. He was dealing with a medical condition that needed attention, and being blind made an already frightening situation even harder.

He was scared. He was sad. And we can only imagine how confusing it must have been for him to find himself in a shelter, wondering what had happened and whether he had done something wrong to deserve being left behind.

But Einstein did absolutely nothing wrong.

He did not deserve to be abandoned. He did not deserve to face his senior years without the care and comfort he needed. And he certainly did not deserve to feel forgotten.

So we made him a promise. We would do everything we could to right the wrongs he had experienced and help erase some of the heartache. We would make sure he knew he was safe, loved, and worthy of being cared for.

Now, we are looking for someone who will help us keep that promise.

Einstein deserves a soft place to land, a patient and loving family, and a home where he can spend his golden years feeling secure and cherished. Being blind does not mean he has any less love to give. He simply needs someone willing to see him for the wonderful dog he is.

Please consider opening your heart and home to this special senior. He has already been through so much, and he deserves better. He deserves love, comfort, and a family who will never make him question whether he matters.

Please reach out to our Adoption Team for additional information about Einstein and to learn if he may be the right fit for your home.
