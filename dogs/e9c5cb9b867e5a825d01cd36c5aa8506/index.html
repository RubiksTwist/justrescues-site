Meet Casper!

Casper was surrendered when his owner could no longer care for him, and now this sweet, low energy boy is looking for a soft place to land. His previous family describes him as social, affectionate, house trained, and a big fan of car rides and spending time close to his people.

My Ideal Home

Casper would do best in a calm adult-only home where he can continue healing and settle in at his own pace. He's looking for patient adopters who can give him stability, kindness, and lots of cozy downtime while he regains his confidence.

Good with Other Pets?

According to his previous owner, Casper has been friendly and outgoing with other dogs both on and off leash. At the shelter, he showed interest in greeting another dog through the gate, though interactions have remained limited while he focuses on healing.

Good with Kids?

We recommend an adult-only home for Casper at this time due to his discomfort with novel situations and handling while recovering medically.

Things to Know

Casper is currently recovering from ongoing skin issues and has been unable to complete a full handling assessment while receiving treatment. Even so, he has remained gentle with staff, takes treats softly, and continues to tolerate care and medical handling well. He can become a little timid in unfamiliar situations and may bark or growl at strangers approaching his family on walks, but overall he is described as an affectionate, easygoing companion who enjoys being near his people. Casper also loves car rides and already has great house manners.

Fun Fact

Casper may be named after a friendly ghost, but this Casper prefers cozy naps, car rides, and sticking close to his favorite humans.
