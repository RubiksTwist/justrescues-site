Description provided by Waggytail Rescue

Meet Vinny Van Gogh! 🐾 A petite, young gentleman who is ready to be your cat's BFF and your dedicated playtime pal!

Sweet Vinny unfortunately had a rough start in life before coming into our rescue. Originally thought to be an outdoor cat, he was fixed by local rescuers (and given his handsome ear tip), only for it to be discovered that he was dumped outside when his owners moved and that he was longing to have a real home and family of his own.

Now that Vinny is with us at Waggytail and has been fully vetted, the only thing left to do is find him a foster (or better yet, an adopter!) who can make him feel safe and loved again.

🎨 Vinny's Masterpiece - Playing with Toys!

Want to know the ultimate shortcut to becoming Vinny's absolute favorite human? It’s simple: the wand toy. The moment you pick up that wand toy, his inner tiger takes over and all his shyness evaporates into thin air. He'll go from lounging in the window to leaping, pouncing, and chasing that feather around the room with pure joy. Playing together is Vinny's absolute favorite way to bond, shed his worries, and show you his sweet personality. After a play session, it's not uncommon for him to snuggle up at his foster's feet and purr himself to sleep (so cute!)

🐱 Vinny's Favorite Thing - His Kitty Pals!

Ultimately, Vinny's happy place is truly in company of other cats. He adores having the companionship of another cat or kitty around and loves to play, cuddle and curiously explore the house with his foster's resident cats.

🌃 Vinny's Starry Night & Ideal Home

Every great friendship takes time, and all we can say is that building trust with Vinny is SO worth the wait. While he may start off as a gentle wallflower, living in foster care has allowed his curious, playful side to fully emerge. Give him a little time and love, his shy exterior melts away to reveal a playful, curious, and relaxed pal.

Vinny's perfect home is one where his people are patient and comfortable letting him acclimate in his own time and where there is another cat (or two!) who is seeking a kitty friend to spend their days with.

If you’re looking for a BFF for your kitty and you understand that a true masterpiece takes a little time, Vinny is your perfect guy!
