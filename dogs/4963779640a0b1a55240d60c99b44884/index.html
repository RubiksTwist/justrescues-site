Pre-Screener Form

Moko is at risk due to behavioral concerns. Moko has not acclimated well to the shelter enviornment despite best efforts to keep him comfortable. In his kennel, he is jumping to the top of his kennel with saliva and feces smeared across the glass. Out of the kennel, Moko has remained loose and wiggly with handlers but has displayed a low threshold for arousal, which has manifested mainly as jumping up towards staff and handlers' faces. In calmer settings, he has difficulty settling but will intermittently engage with training games. Moko recently became aroused once removed from the kennel and jumped up towards a staff member's face, and his teeth made contact with their cheek. A second handler had to assist with Moko as he attempted to continue, but they were able to quickly separate them. This did not break the skin or draw blood. Medically, Moko is reported to have chronic ear infections but is otherwise apparently healthy.

Moko was surrendered by his previous owner due to a family member being allergic. Moko was described as a social, affectionate, active, playful, and vocal dog. He is noted to prefer contact on his own terms, enjoys lounging in the living room, but has never been in a car. He enjoys playing with squeaky toys; his previous owner noted that he is shy around new people and needs time to warm up. During his stay in care, Moko has been loose and wiggly with handlers, but he has a concerning level of arousal which has manifested as persistently jumping on handlers while aiming/muzzle punching towards their face, and is intermittently mouthy. Due to these concerns, we recommend that Moko be placed in an adult-only home through a New Hope partner that can address his challenges with the use of a certified professional trainer that utilizes positive reinforcement methods.
