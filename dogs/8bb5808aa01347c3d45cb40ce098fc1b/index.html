Handsome Choppa was initially surrendered to the care center as a stray, and was later returned by his adopter after one week due to mouthy behavior. During his time at the care center, Handsome Choppa has shown a low threshold for arousal, persistently jumping up on handlers, fixating on treat pouches, mouthing at hands and clothes, and attempting to mount. In calmer settings, he has shown social behavior with handlers and engaged in training sessions with a focus on settling and building relaxation. Due to his escalating arousal in care, we recommend placement with a New Hope partner who can allow for decompression, provide any necessary behavior modification (force-free, positive reinforcement-based) and re-evaluate behavior in a stable home environment before placement into a permanent home.
