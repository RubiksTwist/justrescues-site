He will benefit from a home with a family that is committed to training him to use his intelligence and protective qualities to everyone's advantage. He will benefit from long walks and puzzles to help keep him happy, healthy, and intellectually stimulated. Kingston is a great dog that needs a fantastic owner to help him meet his potential.
