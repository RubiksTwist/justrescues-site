A volunteer writes:
Two friendly cats, Mac and his friend Cheese(already adopted) were dropped at a police precinct. No explanation give....Mac, a handsome five year old Tuxedo was on a timid side for a few days but quickly came out of his shell, welcoming visitors as they present at his door. Mac is quite a friendly kitty, quick to head bunt my hand, lean into pets and even purr...He does appreciate treats and gourmet food but being entertained is what he really prefers. Mac did well on his behavior evaluation, pointing at his shy and reserved side at the beginning of his stay and advising his new owner or family to give him time to acclimate to his new settings. Mac is a really nice cat so worth your attention and the promise of a loving and faithful four legged friend. Come and meet Mac at the Manhattan Care Center!
