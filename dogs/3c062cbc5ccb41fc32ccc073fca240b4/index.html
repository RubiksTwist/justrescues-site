Description provided by Bideawee

The only home Aubrey has ever known is gone, and suddenly everything familiar to her has been replaced by the sights and sounds of a busy shelter. She’s scared and overwhelmed, but a soft voice and a little baby talk help her feel safe enough to come out of her shell.

Aubrey will take short walks, but busy sidewalks are a lot for her. She would be happiest in a quiet, calm home where she can settle in and feel safe again. She has lived with children in the past and did wonderfully with them. We can’t wait to see Aubrey back in a home where she belongs.

If this dog is located at our Manhattan Adoption Center, head in to meet them and see if you are a match! We are a same-day adoption center, and an application does not put you on a list or hold an animal for you. If they are in Wantagh or in a foster home, please submit an online application and our team will reach out to further discuss the adoption process. Prior to adoption, all of our animals are spayed/neutered, microchipped, and given their age-appropriate vaccines.
