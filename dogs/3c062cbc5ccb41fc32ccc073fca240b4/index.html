Description provided by Bideawee

Eight-year-old Aubrey has lost the only home she’s ever known, and adjusting to shelter life hasn't been easy. This sweet girl is feeling overwhelmed and is hoping to find a family who will help her feel safe again.
Aubrey is affectionate and responds well to baby talk and reassurance. She previously lived with a child and was a wonderful companion. Busy streets can be scary for her, so she'd do best in a calm, quiet home, ideally with a yard where she can enjoy the outdoors at her own pace. She just needs a little patience and a place to call home again.
All of our animals are spayed/neutered, microchipped, and up to date on vaccinations. Animals at our Manhattan location are available for same-day adoption. For animals at our Wantagh location or in foster care, please submit an online application at www.bideawee.org to schedule a meet and greet.
