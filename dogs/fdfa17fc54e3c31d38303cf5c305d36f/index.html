Description provided by Waggytail Rescue

AJ is a 4-year-old, 18-pound, small mixed breed whose ancestry is a true mystery. She’s a real Heinz 57, with the tri-color coat of a spaniel, a white bib and a white sock, and the expressive eyebrows of a Rottweiler. We don’t know what’s in the mix, but we know the result is adorable. 🐾

A slow burn, worth the wait

AJ is a gentle, sensitive girl who warms up quietly and gets sweeter the more safe she feels. Since coming into care, she’s already shown us how easy she is to live with, and with continued decompression we expect her to keep opening up. 🩵

  • Home life: AJ is non-destructive when left on her own and is about 95% house-trained! She’s a cozy girl who appreciates a soft place to nap and takes happily to her crate. A little crate or confinement time helps her nail the last 5%, and she hasn’t shown any separation anxiety.
  • On leash: She loves walks and is great once she finds a quiet street. On a busier block she may pause for a moment and lean against your legs for encouragement, and a little patience gets her moving again. She loves popping into stores and cafes and is a well-mannered little lady. She’s food motivated and eager to please, so treats go a long way!
  • Brain games: We’ve recently discovered how much AJ loves enrichment and engagement activities. Puzzle toys, sniff games, and learning new things light her up. 🧩
  • Other dogs: Outside the home or on leash, she’s not reactive to other dogs, regardless of size. Indoors, she prefers not to share her people, so we recommend a home without another resident dog.
  • Curious about critters: AJ has a natural prey drive and is very interested in small, furry creatures and birds. She’d be happiest in a home without small pets, and on walks, an attentive handler who can redirect her with treats will keep her focused. 🐦
  • Adventurer at heart: AJ was originally found in a park, and in care she’s always on the lookout for a chance to explore the great outdoors. She’s a bit of an escape artist, so she should be leashed at all times when outside, unless she’s in a fully fenced enclosure that’s 6 feet or taller. Secure doors, gates, and windows are a must, and a well-fitted harness will help keep her safe. 🚪

What AJ needs

  • A quiet, low-traffic home. She’s still building confidence and does best in a calm household without heavy foot traffic. She seems most comfortable with women and female-presenting people.
  • No small children. A calm, predictable environment is the best fit for her.
  • An alert, security-minded adopter. Someone who will keep doors and gates closed, stay leashed up outdoors, and keep a watchful eye on those small furry distractions.
  • Training and consistency. AJ is still learning about the big world, so she’ll shine with an adopter who’s excited to continue leash and general obedience training. With routine, treats, and gentle encouragement, she’ll blossom. Training also makes a great bonding activity!

Vaccinated for rabies, DHPP, and bordetella, heartworm negative, and microchipped. Current on flea, tick, and heartworm prevention. AJ will be spayed in Long Island City when a December appointment becomes available. She can be adopted beforehand, but her adopters will be responsible for transporting her to/from the appointment and a refundable $100 spay/neuter deposit.
