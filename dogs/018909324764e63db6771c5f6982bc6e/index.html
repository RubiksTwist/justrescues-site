Hi! My name is Mika! Since I'm a long stay pup, my adoption fee has been reduced! Apply for me today to find out my favorite treats and how you can take me home! Here's what my friends at ACC say about me:

My foster writes:
Mika is a very loving and sweet dog. She wants nothing more than to lay with you on the couch or bed and snuggle up to you or to bring her ball to you and play. She has been learning to walk nicely on the leash and absolutely loves being outside. She is friendly to people and kids and greets everyone with her tail wagging. Mika does snore when she sleeps and can even snore when she's awake! She takes treats very gently and knows how to sit, stay or give paw for treats. She will benefit from plenty of exercise, food puzzles and interaction with you. She should be the only pet in the home and, because of her strength and excitability, a home with no small children.

My foster writes: If I had to describe Mika in a single word, it would be exuberant! She is a joyful and loving companion who wants to become friends with every living creature she meets. While she is certainly energetic she is also equally cuddly, and is happy following a good round of exercise with cuddling close on the sofa to watch a show. She's also so eager to please that she learns quickly, having picked up several new commands with minimal effort. She would be certain to brighten any home she enters!
