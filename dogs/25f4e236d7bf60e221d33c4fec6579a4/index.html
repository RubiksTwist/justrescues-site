Description provided by Animal Lighthouse Rescue

Introducing Primrose! She is a 6month-old Lab mix weighing just 15 pounds, and she's ready to find the forever family she's been waiting for! With her compact size and adventurous spirit, she's the perfect little companion to take wherever life leads you. Whether it's a vacation, a trip to the park, a day at the beach, a train ride, or a cross-country road trip, Primrose is happiest simply being by your side. She doesn't just want to be part of your life—she wants to share every moment of it. This sweet girl is full of personality and will keep you smiling every single day. She loves tearing around the yard with the cutest puppy zoomies, tossing her toys high into the air and trying her very best to catch them, and wrestling with her puppy pals until everyone is happily worn out. Treat time is one of Primrose's favorite parts of the day! She gets so excited that she'll bark at her treats, toss them into the air, and turn snack time into her own little celebration before finally enjoying every bite. Her silly, joyful personality is impossible not to love. When the adventures are over, Primrose turns into the ultimate cuddle buddy. She'll happily curl up beside you on the couch, ready to watch your favorite movie while soaking up all the love and affection she can get. Primrose is searching for a family that will include her in life's adventures, continue helping her grow into an amazing companion, and love her for the sweet, goofy, and affectionate puppy she is.

Primrose is spayed, microchipped, and up to date on her vaccinations. For more information about her, please fill out an application at www.ALRcares.com and email Lori@alrcares.com. We schedule meet and greets for approved adopters only, an application is the first step in our approval process. Thank you!
