Since I'm a long stay pup, my adoption fee has been reduced! Visit me today to find out my favorite treats and how you can take me home!

My foster writes: Mercy is a spunky and loving dog that will melt your heart the minute she enters your home. She loves cuddles, belly scratches, and when you call her with kissing sounds. Be ready to have your heart stolen with this precious girl. Mercy has a lot of energy, that stems from her being home alone while I'm at work, so she needs to go on several walks through out the day. If you can squeeze a long walk than even better! At home she takes long naps, plays with her toys, or goes back and forth with the puzzles I leave her. She's a sucker for Greek yogurt and peanut butter, so make sure to have extra jars on hand! She's indifferent to cars and bikes, which is great when going on walks, but can get excited when spotting another dog and/or bird. Mercy is a curious pup, so make sure you have a good grip on her leash or your walk will turn into a spontaneous run. She loves to sniff around and occasionally will sit and enjoy the view while on walks.

I am excitable and energetic! I will need positive outlets for my energy!

I don't always like to share my food, toys or bedding with other animals.

I would do best in a home with only adult humans.
