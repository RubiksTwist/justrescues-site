Meet Bella! This girl is a little bundle of joy and love. Bella lights up every room with her extremely friendly and easy-going nature. She literally makes friends anywhere she goes - on walks people stop to ask for pets because she is just so cute and happy. She may be a senior lady but she has lots of spunk! She loves a good snooze and cuddle but also gets so excited for her walks and whenever a new human or dog comes home. She is great with other dogs and cats both in the home and on leash. She is fully potty trained! She is working hard on being okay being left alone. She does bark and paw at the door when her favorite people leave but she settles within an hour or so in her crate. This girlie deserves a home for her golden years and she is so ready for it!
