Meet Jack Jack!

Jack Jack was surrendered when his owner could no longer care for him, and now he's searching for a patient person to help him start his next chapter. This energetic, affectionate boy loves quality time with his favorite people, whether that's cuddling on the couch, heading out for adventures, or joining you on a car ride.

My Ideal Home

Jack Jack is looking for an adult-only home with someone who will give him the time and guidance he needs to build confidence. He has lots of energy and will thrive with daily exercise, enrichment, and positive reinforcement training. Once he's had a chance to burn off some steam, he's more than happy to settle in for snuggles.

Good with Other Pets?

Jack Jack prefers not to share high-value items like food or toys, so careful management and slow introductions are important if there are other pets in the home. Our staff can help determine whether he'd be a good fit for your household.

Good with Kids?

Jack Jack would do best in an adult-only home.

Things to Know

Jack Jack is a loving dog who enjoys affection on his own terms and forms strong bonds with his people. He's smart, food motivated, knows "sit," and enjoys car rides and staying active. He can be sensitive about handling around his neck and during nail trims, and he prefers to eat without interruption. With patience, gentle handling, and reward-based training, Jack Jack has all the ingredients to become an incredible lifelong companion.
