Meet Colada, a cuddly girl who knows the best seat in the house is your lap!

Colada is an affectionate pup who loves being close to her people. When she’s ready for some attention, she may crawl right into your lap and settle in for plenty of pets. Don’t let her cuddly side fool you, Colada knows how to have fun too! Give her a tasty treat and a fun squeaky toy, and you will see her playful personality start to shine.

Whether she’s enjoying a good play session or climbing in close for affection, Colada has a sweet way of making time together feel special. Click the “Adopt Me” button to connect with Colada’s team in Pawling, New York!

*We encourage a $150 suggested adoption fee when adopting an animal. This adoption fee allows the ASPCA to continue our lifesaving work and help even more animals in need. We are grateful for any amount given at the time of adoption.*
