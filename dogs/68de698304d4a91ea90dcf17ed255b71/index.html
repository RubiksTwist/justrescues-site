My foster writes:

Meet Skyla, a dog made purely of love and happiness. Our nickname for her is PiggyWorm: Piggy for how cute and pink she is, and Worm for how she loves to wiggle her way into us when she snuggles. Skyla's favorite things are sleeping on the couch, belly rubs, and peanut butter. She is a simple girl; as long as she is with her humans, she is at peace. Every one of our friends who has had the privilege of meeting Skyla has instantly fallen in love with her calm demeanor and kissable face.
As someone who works from home, I have found Skyla to be the most delightful companion. When she is around, I am never lonely! Aside from a few short potty breaks each day, she can usually be found resting her head on my lap while I work, serving as somewhat of an "emotional support dog" during moments of stress. She has an incredible awareness of human emotions and seems to react accordingly, often coming over for extra cuddles when she senses that we need them.
It has been truly special to watch Skyla's big personality continue to flourish as we get to know her better each day. Whoever adopts Skyla should consider themselves lucky, because they are in for years of affection, loyalty, and genuine friendship.

Since I'm a long stay pup, my adoption fee has been reduced! Visit me today to find out my favorite treats and how you can take me home!
