ABOUT ME: Every morning I bounce out of my crate like it’s championship day, because honestly, it might be. I’m gentle, goofy, and a little clumsy, and I’m a total dog person: I aced my first day of daycare and I’ll happily play with your pup. Once a day I raid the toy bin and prance around, not caring when a toy whacks me in the face. I’m also a fast learner. Sit on the elevator, get a treat? Already mastered.

MY FOSTER SAYS: "He figured out that when he sits on the elevator he gets treats. Now whenever he gets on the elevator he comes up and sits in front of me. Yesterday there was a stranger who got on behind us and OG sat in front of him waiting for his reward."

HISTORY

OG Anunoby is from Georgia, where he showed up as a stray at a rescue volunteer's home and was so friendly that the volunteer took him in right away. He traveled to Brooklyn in mid-September 2026.

DETAILED PROFILE

When something catches OG Anunoby's interest, his neck stretches up, his head tips to one side, and his face stays completely serious, a poker face worthy of his namesake. It fits a dog who watches closely, learns the rules, and waits for permission. Once he figured out that sitting on the elevator earns a treat, he began offering a sit to every rider, strangers included. He also has a mischievous streak, once caught with his paws on the counter, scouting for snacks.

He's goofy, gentle, and eager to please, with moderate energy. Mornings start with a cheerful bounce out of the crate and some play; toss a few tennis balls and he'll chase and tackle each one in his clumsy way. After dinner, he's off duty. The last bathroom trip of the night is a favor he grants reluctantly before making a beeline back to bed. He loved hiking in the woods and rides calmly in the car, but he is not a running buddy.

Other dogs are his favorite thing. He plays happily with his foster's dogs, gets high marks at daycare, and keeps his cool when another dog is rude: when one snapped at him, he simply walked the other way. He'd love a dog sibling. With people he's calm, friendly, and increasingly outgoing, and no one has ever seen him jump up. He was gentle with the kids he's met; he hasn't lived with children but is expected to do well with them. Cats are unknown.

He's a gem on leash and now house trained. He sleeps happily in his crate, but being crated while his people are out is still a work in progress. He's relaxed home alone uncrated.

Ask him to sit, and he'll do it with a perfectly straight face.
