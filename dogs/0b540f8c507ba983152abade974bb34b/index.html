HISTORY

From Georgia; showed up as a stray at the home of a rescue volunteer and was so nice they immediately took him in. OG traveled north to Brooklyn in mid-September 2026.

DETAILED PROFILE

OG Anunoby greets every morning like it’s opening night at the Garden. He bounces out of his crate ready to go, and his good mood is contagious. He’s goofy, gentle, a little clumsy, and eager to please, with moderate energy split between cuddling up to relax and a daily burst of play. That burst usually starts at the toy bin, where he picks something he can shake and prances around while it smacks him in the face. Given the chance, he’ll empty the whole bin, one toy at a time.

He’s a quick study. Once he worked out that sitting on the elevator earns a treat, he started offering a polite sit to every rider, strangers included. He’s food motivated, learning his name and sit, and just starting on place. On leash he’s impressively polished, walking right at your side for much of the walk.

Other dogs are his favorite thing. He loves running and playing with his foster’s dogs, and his first day of daycare earned great reviews for playful, gentle group play. A home with another dog would suit him, but it’s not required. With people he’s calm and polite rather than over-the-top, and he’s been gentle and relaxed with the kids he’s met, even showing quiet curiosity about babies in strollers. His experience with cats isn’t known yet. He’s also good in the car and loves the water.

He’s still settling in and working on crate comfort and house training, so his adopter should be ready to keep practicing. So far, he’s been relaxed when left home alone. For anyone who wants an underrated, do-it-all teammate with a championship-level morning attitude, OG is ready for tip-off.
