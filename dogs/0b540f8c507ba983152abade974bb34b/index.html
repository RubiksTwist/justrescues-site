ABOUT ME: Every morning I bounce out of my crate like it’s championship day, because honestly, it might be. I’m gentle, goofy, and a little clumsy, and I’m a total dog person: I aced my first day of daycare and I’ll happily play with your pup. Once a day I raid the toy bin and prance around, not caring when a toy whacks me in the face. I’m also a fast learner. Sit on the elevator, get a treat? Already mastered.

MY FOSTER SAYS: "He figured out that when he sits on the elevator he gets treats. Now whenever he gets on the elevator he comes up and sits in front of me. Yesterday there was a stranger who got on behind us and OG sat in front of him waiting for his reward."

HISTORY

From Georgia; showed up as a stray at the home of a rescue volunteer and was so nice they immediately took him in. OG traveled north to Brooklyn in mid-September 2026.

DETAILED PROFILE

When something catches OG Anunoby’s interest, his neck stretches up, his head tips to one side, and his face stays completely serious, a poker face worthy of his namesake. It fits a dog who watches closely, learns the rules, and waits for permission. Once he figured out that sitting on the elevator earns a treat, he began offering a sit to every rider, strangers included. Polite as he is, he has a mischievous streak: his foster recently caught him with his paws up on the counter, scouting for snacks.

He’s goofy, gentle, and eager to please, with moderate energy. He spends much of the day snoozing or curled up beside his people or a dog friend, and yes, he snores. Mornings start with a cheerful bounce out of the crate and a burst of toy-tossing play. He loved hiking in the woods and rides calmly in the car, but running is a no; on his one attempt, he lay down on the sidewalk.

Other dogs are his favorite thing. He plays happily with his foster’s dogs, earns high marks at daycare, and stays polite around dogs on leash. He moped when his canine housemates went away, so he’d love a dog sibling. With people he’s calm and friendly and never jumps. He was gentle with the kids he’s met; he hasn’t lived with children but is expected to do well with them. Cats are unknown.

He’s a gem on leash and very close to fully house trained. He heads into his crate on his own for meals, naps, and bedtime, but staying calm when crated while his people are out is still a work in progress. He’s relaxed home alone uncrated, and crate practice should continue.

Ask him to sit, and he’ll do it with a perfectly straight face.
