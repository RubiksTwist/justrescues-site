Meet Oreo Cookie!

Oreo Cookie was surrendered when her owner could no longer care for her and is ready to start a new chapter with a loving family. She is an affectionate girl who enjoys leaning in for pets, spending time with her people, and exploring the world by your side. With a little patience and guidance, Oreo Cookie is sure to be one sweet companion.

My Ideal Home

Oreo Cookie would thrive in a patient home that can provide a consistent routine, positive reinforcement training, and plenty of enrichment. Daily walks, puzzle toys, and fun training sessions will help keep both her mind and body busy. She may need a little extra support as she adjusts to being left alone, but with time and reassurance, she'll continue building confidence.

Good with Other Pets?

Oreo Cookie previously lived with a small dog and was described as friendly with familiar canine companions. She can be uncomfortable around unfamiliar dogs while on leash, so slow introductions and continued positive reinforcement training will help her succeed. Dog parks are not recommended, as she is happiest meeting dogs in calmer, more controlled settings.

Good with Kids?

Because Oreo Cookie can be sensitive in new situations and is still building confidence, a home without very young children is recommended. Older children who understand how to respect her space may be a great fit.

Things to Know

Oreo Cookie loves attention and happily leans in for affection, though she may jump up when she's excited to say hello. She can be startled by unfamiliar situations and may need slow introductions to new experiences, but she responds well to gentle, reward based training. With a little patience and a lot of love, this sweet cookie is ready to become the best part of someone's day.
