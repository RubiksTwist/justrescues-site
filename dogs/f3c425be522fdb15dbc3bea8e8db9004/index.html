Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

Knight is a handsome 5-year-old mixed breed with a big personality and an even bigger heart. His previous family describes him as social, affectionate, playful, and active, while also being happy to relax nearby after a day of fun. He loves car rides, playing with his ball, and spending time with his people, and he's reported to be friendly with strangers, children, and dogs he knows.

Knight is housetrained, enjoys regular walks, and has lived successfully with a child. He can be anxious at times and may jump up when excited. He is uncomfortable around unfamiliar dogs that approach him, especially off leash, so he'll do best with thoughtful introductions and an adopter who can help him feel secure on walks. He's also reported to have a chicken allergy, so his new family should keep that in mind when choosing treats and food.

If you're looking for an affectionate, fun-loving companion who will happily join you on adventures and then curl up nearby for a well-earned nap, Knight may be your perfect match!
