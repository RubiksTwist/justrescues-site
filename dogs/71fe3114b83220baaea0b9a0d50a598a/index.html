Description provided by ASPCA Adoption Center

This profile is intended for walk-in adopters who may be uncertain about which dog to adopt. Our main objective is to ensure the best match between you and a pet that suits your lifestyle. To achieve this, we engage in a conversation to gather information, ensuring it's a good fit for both the animals at our Adoption Center and their potential adopters.

How to Prepare to Bring a Pet Home Today:

Our suggested adoption fee is $150.

All adult cats must leave in a carrier, which is available for purchase here for $40. Kittens can go home in one of our free cardboard carriers.

You must present a valid government-issued ID and proof of address.

We practice same-day adoptions and can only place short holds on adult animals.

Other Information:

We update our website regularly as animals become available for adoption.

Our hours of operation are daily from 12:00 PM to 5:00 PM.

The adoption process typically takes between 30 minutes and 1.5 hours.

Please note that we do not offer open tours to view available animals.
