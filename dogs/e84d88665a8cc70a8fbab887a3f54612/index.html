Brownie | 11 months-old | 22-pounds | Terrier Mix

Meet Brownie (also known as Alphy!)—an 11-month-old, 22-pound bundle of goofy charm with legs for days and floppy ears that could melt any heart. Originally from Mexico, this sweet, loyal pup is looking for a forever family to call his own!

Brownie is the ultimate balance of playful puppy energy and cozy cuddle bug. He loves chasing his zoomies out, chewing on his favorite bones, sniffing every leaf on his path, and shadowing his favorite humans from room to room. When playtime is over, his favorite spot is burrowed right into the couch beside you (or spilled halfway out of his bed in the funniest sleeping positions!).

He is a smart boy who already knows “sit” and is making great strides with his house training and obedience. Because he’s still adjusting to life in a busy city, Brownie thrives in quieter environments and loves peaceful, late-night strolls. He’s looking for a patient, moderately active home—ideally with a private yard where he can safely play and build his confidence at his own pace.

Brownie’s Ideal Home:

  • A Cozy Routine: He loves having his people around and does great in his crate during the day when you're nearby, settling in after a bit of adjustment when left alone.
  • Canine Companions: He does well with smaller, gentle dogs who match his polite energy.
  • Family Dynamics: Because he’s still an enthusiastic puppy who loves to play with his mouth, he’d do best in a home with older kids who can help him keep up his great progress with manners and boundaries.

If you’re looking for a fun-spirited, affectionate shadow who loves both outdoor adventures and couch snuggles, Brownie is ready to be your new best friend!

Vaccinated for rabies, DHPP, and bordetella, heartworm negative, spayed and microchipped. Current on flea, tick, and heartworm prevention.

To apply for Brownie, visit our website (www.waggytailrescue.org) for an adoption application. It is important to fill out an application as soon as possible for consideration, and we require a complete application for you to meet an animal. We are currently unable to respond to general email inquiries as an all-volunteer run rescue. If the dog is on our website, they are still available.

Waggytail Rescue CANNOT guarantee the exact age of any adoptable dog, or final size of any of our adoptable puppies. We rely on the information provided to us by veterinarians, shelters, owner surrenders and other circumstances bringing the dog into our rescue.

Adoptions: adopt@waggytailrescue.org
