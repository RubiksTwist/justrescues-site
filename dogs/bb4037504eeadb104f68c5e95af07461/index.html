Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

If there is one word that describes Leona best, it is lovely. At just 3 years old and 95 lbs, lovely Leona is a gentle giant with a personality as beautiful as her heart.

Despite her size, Leona is anything but intimidating. In fact, she will quickly show you that all 95 lbs of her are filled with charm. Leona is goofy, sweet, lovable, silly, and endlessly affectionate. She is also remarkably in tune with the expectations of her humans, always eager to please and incredibly receptive to gentle guidance and redirection whenever necessary.

Leona is the type of girl who genuinely enjoys watching the world go by. She likes to take in her surroundings, get a feel for the lay of the land, and eventually surrender to the relaxation of it all. So, don't be surprised if she decides to hop up on a bench beside her favorite people, perfectly content to sit back and enjoy the simple beauty of life.

Leona enjoys a leisurely walk and is known for calmly passing people, dogs, bikes, birds, and all the other sights and sounds that come with life on the streets of New York City. Leona is happy to maintain an average walking pace for the first 10 minutes or so before settling into a slower, more relaxed stroll. She is not necessarily a girl who requires, or would particularly enjoy, intense exercise or lengthy outdoor adventures.

Once the initial excitement of a new place and unfamiliar faces wears off, Leona can confidently be described as a low-energy pup. Her idea of a fulfilling day consists of slow walks, outdoor people-watching, and plenty of time spent relaxing at home. While she may initially show some extra enthusiasm as she adjusts to a new environment, she responds beautifully to calm guidance and gentle redirection. Leona will thrive in a home where she is given the opportunity to decompress, settle into a routine, and simply be the lovely girl she is at heart.

Leona is the kind of dog who has a way of making her humans feel especially loved, because she makes it so clear that she has chosen them, too. Once a friendship is established, Leona will respectfully seek out affection, offer a gentle paw to ask for some love, or gaze deeply into her person's eyes, quietly communicating her desire for a little attention or perhaps a treat! Her personality is an endearing combination of easygoing companionship and unexpected moments of pure silliness. When her playful side makes an appearance, Leona is sure to bring a smile to your face. She is wonderfully treat-motivated and eager to please, responding readily to reminders that all four paws belong on the floor. More than anything, she simply wants to make her people proud.

Leona may be a big girl, but the only thing that rivals her size is the size of her heart. Her training needs are minimal, her daily requirements are refreshingly simple, and her desire to love and be loved is undeniable. She doesn't need a life filled with constant activity or grand adventures to feel fulfilled. For Leona, the greatest happiness comes from a peaceful routine, a comfortable place to rest, and the companionship of people who adore her as much as she adores them.

If Leona sounds like the kind of girl who would fit beautifully into your lifestyle, don't hesitate to submit an application. You may just discover that sharing your life with lovely Leona is every bit as special as she is.
