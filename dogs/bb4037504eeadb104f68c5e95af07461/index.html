Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

The adjective that describs Leona best is "lovely". At just 3 years old and 95 lbs, lovely Leona is a gentle giant with such a desirable personality.

Despite her size, Leona is anything but intimidating. In fact, in new environments, Leona can be respectful and reserved while she is acclimating and becoming comfortable. Once she does however, she will quickly show you that all 95 lbs of her is full of charm. Leona is goofy, sweet, lovable, silly and so absolutely affectionate. Leona is also highly intuned with humans expectations of her and is always incredibly receptive to guidance and redirection should they ever be necessary.

In her telltale leaisurely way, Leona is a quiet observer who leads with cautious optimism. Leona is the type of girl who enjoys watching the world go by, taking in all of get surroundings, figuring out the lay of the land and then eventually succumbing to the relaxation of it all - so, don't be surprised if she wants to hop up on the bench next to her favorite people and enjoy the beauty of life.

Walks with Leona are also a dream come true. Leona is a walks on such a desirable loose leash and so is extremely easy to handle. Leona is known for quietly and calmly passing by people, dogs, bikes, birds and all the additional stimuli found on the streets of New York City. Leona has absolutely no desire react to every person, animal or sound outside. Leona is happy to keep up an average walking pace for her first 10 minutes outdoors and then slows down for a more leisurely stroll, not necessarily being a pup who requires or is able to tolerate intense activity and outdoor adventures.

Overall, after Leona's initial excitement wears off from being in a new place with new people, she would pretty confidently be described as a low energy pup - with her days being fulfillied by slow walks, outdoor people watching and relaxing at home. During her initial time in a new environment, Leona may appear a bit anxious and struggle to settle or fully relax and this perfect girl is so receptive to calm guidance and redirection when necessary. Leona will thrive in a predictable home where she can decompress and simply be who she is at heart. Leona is a docile soul who looks for safety in her environment and once she finds it, she's happy to share her beautiful temperament and perfect personality with her people.

Leona is the type of dog who makes her human feel so special because she shows how much she has picked them too. With a confirmed friendship, Leona is known to respectfully seek out attention from her people, paw at them for love or stare deep into their eyes - signaling a desire for interaction, or a treat! Leona's vibe is an equal split of casual love and an unforeseen good time. Leona in her silly moods will show you how treat motivated she is and will respond every time you remind her that all four of her paws belong on the floor, simply eager to make her human proud.

Leona may be a big girl however the only thing as big as her size is in fact her heart. Otherwise, Leona's training needs are minimal, her daily requirements are easy going and her desire to love and be loved is warranted. If Leona sounds like a girl who can fit into your lifestyle don't hesistate to submit an application and see for yourself how fulfiling a life with Leoa can be.
