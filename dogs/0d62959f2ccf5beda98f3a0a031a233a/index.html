Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

Hi, I’m Wanda. I arrived in the big city and immediately made myself at home. Traffic? Sirens? General sidewalk chaos? Unbothered. I bounce along on walks with my ears flopping, keeping an eye on the birds and squirrels and trying to make eye contact with everyone who passes. You could be my next best friend. I’d hate for you to miss that opportunity.

At home, I’m a happy, playful girl with a busy schedule of throwing toys around and entertaining myself while my foster mom works. I’m also a big fan of the resident dog, who is older, much larger, and apparently unaware that we’re training for a tug-of-war championship. I have yet to win, but I admire my own confidence. I’m learning fetch, too, complete with a little bunny hop before I catch the ball. Form matters.

My pee pad skills are excellent, but I’ve recently discovered that the outdoors also has bathroom facilities, and I’m doing really well with a consistent schedule of potty walks. In the rare event my foster mom falls behind on our appointments, I still reliably use my pee pads. I’m happy to accommodate the occasional management error.

At bedtime, I settle nicely into my crate after a brief statement for the record. Once I’ve gotten my puppy energy out, though, my favorite place is cuddled up with you on the couch. I lean into every pet like I’ve been waiting my whole life for it, even if the last one was four seconds ago.

I’ve got plenty of happy puppy energy, a very sweet snuggly side, and an opening for a lifelong best friend.
