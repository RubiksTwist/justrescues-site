Meet Lantana!

Lantana is a 2-year-old, 15-lb Jack Russel/Chihuahua mix who is ready to be your little shadow. She absolutely loves people and gets along wonderfully with dogs, even the ones who are 5-times her size!

She gets along great with an 80-lb resident dog and is surprisingly brave for such a little girl. When she first arrived, she was nervous about doors and stairs, but by her second day, she was confidently running up and down the stairs like she had always lived here. She is also very comfortable being handled and even patiently allows her paws to be cleaned after walks.

Lantana has been picking up potty training quickly and is learning to do her business outdoors. She does have one funny little quirk: she prefers to pee in tall patches of grass! She can be a little unsure of loud or sudden noises and may run to you for reassurance. Luckily, she is very food motivated! If she hears a treat bag or anything crinkling, she comes running. Outdoors, she doesn’t always take treats, but verbal praise works just as well. And when you give her some love, her entire butt wiggles with happiness.

Her crate has quickly become her cozy little den. She will often run into it on her own when she wants to relax, and she sleeps wonderfully overnight in her crate. She has also done great when left alone and has not shown any signs of distress or cried while her fosters are away.

And perhaps the best part? Lantana is ready to give her people the cutest, most gentle little kisses. She is sweet, affectionate, and just wants to be close to her humans.

Lantana is currently being fostered in Brooklyn, NY and her adoption fee is $550.
