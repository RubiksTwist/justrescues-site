Dance Break was born at a humane society in Tennessee. He was adopted in NY at 3 months old, but sadly dumped at a shelter just 3 months later, all because he was going through his chewing phase. Young, healthy, and social as can be, he still landed on the euthanasia list — but we swooped in and rescued him in the nick of time!

Dance Break lives up to his name in every way. He's a happy, bouncy boy who loves dancing around the yard just as much as he loves a good snuggle. He's smart, obedient, and an all-around joy — he gets along great with kids, dogs, and cats, is house trained, and walks nicely on a leash. He's still learning the difference between a chew toy and a couch cushion, but even when he slips up, he's quick and easy to redirect.

Dance Break is fostered in Brooklyn, NY, and is available for local or out-of-state adoption as long as the adopter can travel to meet him. To get the adoption process started, please send us an application at https://bit.ly/rosr-adopt-foster.
