Description provided by Animal Lighthouse Rescue

Introducing Ralphie! If you're looking for a little bundle of joy, laughter, and puppy kisses, Ralphie is your guy! This adorable 4-month-old terrier mix weighs in at around 10 pounds and is packed with personality. He's incredibly playful, goofy, and always ready to turn an ordinary day into an adventure! Ralphie loves everyone he meets, including other dogs and cats, making him a wonderful addition to just about any family. He's the kind of puppy who will keep you smiling with his silly antics, playful personality, and endless curiosity about the world around him. Like any puppy, Ralphie is ready to learn, explore, and grow! He'll need all the fun things that come with puppyhood: patience, consistency, training, socialization, and plenty of playtime. In return, you'll get a lifetime of unconditional love, happy tailwags, silly moments, and a best friend who thinks you're the greatest person in the world. Whether it's a family with kids, a home with other furry friends, or someone looking for a little sidekick to share life's adventures, Ralphie is sure to fit right in. He's still just a baby with so much love and personality to give! He was found all alone in a park, wet, crying and hoping someone would rescue him. He was scooped up and taken to our sister shelter where he received so much love and a nice warm bath!

Ralphie is neutered, microchipped, and up to date on his vaccinations. For more information about him, please fill out an application at www.ALRcares.com and email Lori@alrcares.com. We schedule meet and greets for approved adopters only, an application is the first step in our approval process. Thank you!
