Meet Bell Pepper!

Bell Pepper is an 11-year-old sweetheart who came to us as an owner surrender. She has lived with adults and is described as friendly, outgoing, and playful with new people. She enjoys attention but prefers to engage on her own terms. She is the kind of dog who likes to be near her people and soak up the calm moments of the day.

Personality

Bell Pepper enjoys being part of whatever her people are doing. She loves a good cuddle session and is happiest when she can relax close to someone she trusts. She is more of a couch potato than a toy player and enjoys fuzzy blankets. She can be a little anxious at times, so she will benefit from structure and supervision in a home.

Daily Life

Bell Pepper walks nicely on leash with no pulling and does not react to people or other dogs while out. She enjoys a calm, steady routine and tends to keep to herself in new environments until she feels comfortable. Once settled, she is easygoing and enjoys simply being near her person. She is partially crate trained and can stay in a crate for up to several hours as long as it is large enough for her to move comfortably.

Training and Manners

Bell Pepper knows several cues including sit, stay, paw, and go. She is treat motivated and will happily take most foods offered to her. She is not reactive toward people on walks and handles gentle and more active handling well, standing still and tolerating contact. She may be a bit more reserved in unfamiliar situations but recovers quickly once she feels safe.

Social Behavior

Bell Pepper is generally soft and low key in new environments. She may take a moment to warm up and will approach when coaxed. She enjoys calm interaction and prefers gentle engagement over high energy play.

My Ideal Home

Bell Pepper would do best in a calm home with adults who can provide structure, routine, and gentle guidance. She would love a cozy environment where she can relax, enjoy couch time, and be included in daily life. She may benefit from continued support around her anxiety and supervision to prevent unwanted behaviors like counter surfing or getting into the garbage.

Living with Kids

She would do best in a home without young children due to her anxiety. She may be open to older, respectful teens who understand her need for space and calm interactions.

Fun Fact

Bell Pepper is a professional snuggler. Once she trusts you, she is happiest just being your quiet little shadow.
