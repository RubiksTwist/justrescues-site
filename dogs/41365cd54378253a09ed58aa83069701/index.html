Submit an adoption application a t socialteesnyc.org Our Team Says: "This pup was rescued from a hoarding situation in PR - involving about 40 dogs. Due to this these pups are on the shyer side and need patient people who will give them time to get used to normal life. They are very sweet dogs: just need more time and patience. We love these dogs - we've rescued about 15 of them and they have all been a joy." Their Foster Parent Says: " Bo is a 12-pound, 5-year-old sweetheart who spent his early life in a hoarding situation in Puerto Rico, and every day with him feels like watching a little guy discover that the world can be soft. On his very first night he ate, picked out his own patch of rug, and fell fast asleep, and he’s been quietly claiming his space ever since. He’s a shy, gentle soul who comes to you on his own terms. He’ll wander over to the bedroom doorway just to be near you, and those small moments of trust are the best part of my day. Everyday he becomes more trusting, for example he gets zoomies when I get home, he loves dinner time, and let's you give him kisses and hugs. He shares our home with my beagle, Watson, and they go on walks and sleep next to one another in side by side beds. His days are simple and predictable: breakfast, rest, low-key outings, dinner, and an early night in his bed, which is just how he likes it.; we often go to the coffee shop and take a nice walk in the park and he does great, he loves going on walks while equally just lounging in bed. He’s still learning house life. He uses pee pads indoors and is working up to going outside, and the leash is a work in progress, once he is on the leash he will walk forever, but sometimes wants you to chase him to put the leash on. So we celebrate every small step with lots of treats, he loves cheese and freeze dried liver. Bo doesn’t need a big yard or long runs. He needs a patient, calm person who will let him set the pace, and in return they’ll get a loyal little shadow who is learning, one day at a time, what it means to be loved." Up to date on their DHPP, Bordetella, and Rabies vaccines; heartworm tested if coming from Tennessee or Puerto Rico; spayed/neutered; microchipped; treated with dewormer upon intake How did this dog end up with Social Tees? Most of our dogs come via partner groups who help us save them from shelters where they are at risk of euthanasia in Tennessee, Los Angeles, and Puerto Rico. There are very few spay/neuter resources there and many more animals in those areas than there are people to adopt them. Rescue groups like ours bring them to the northeast where the desire for pets like these allows us to save their lives.
