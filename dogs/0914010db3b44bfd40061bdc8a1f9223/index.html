My Boroughbreaker writes: We went on a long walk through Central Park. She loved to roll around in the grass. While she was alert to other dogs on the walk, she did not pull aggressively towards them or bark. Overall, she was very calm and friendly and allowed petting. Sometimes she was just excited and jumped a little to get my attention, but in a friendly manner.

Mishka arrived at MACC as a stray with no history. Mishka's previous owners reported that she was displaying aggressive behavior in the home. Noted to play roughly and growling at the resident children. While in care, Mishka has been observed to be fearful. Noted to growl at handlers and thrash on leash during intake. When handlers approach her kennel, Mishka has been observed to hard bark and lunge at the kennel door. Due to the detailed behavior we recommend an experieced adult only home.

A staff member writes: Mishka is a gentle, affectionate girl who loves spending quality time with her people. She walks beautifully on a loose leash, frequently checks in with her handler, happily accepts treats with a gentle mouth, and enjoys leaning in for pets. She's also a smart cookie who already knows "sit" and loves staying engaged during her walks. If you're looking for a sweet, attentive companion who will happily stay by your side, Mishka is ready to meet you!
