Meet Chalddeok | Apply Now

This boy is the full package - handsome, sweet, and down to Earth~ Chalddeok is 10 months old and 35lbs making him a lanky hunk! He was rescued this past April from a dog meat farm discovered in Seongnam-si. Our partners @we.a.c.t helped us save 22 dogs from rusted crates awaiting the worst.

Even so, Chalddeok is a bright ray of sunshine emerging from the darkness. He is still adjusting to his new stomping ground, but his personality is quickly starting to shine. He’s a lover boy through and through and will lean on you for pets and attention, but all the same he’s perfectly happy napping on his own. If you’re in the kitchen so is he; he’s a budding foodie and loves to watch his fosters cook! He recently started showing his more playful side and got some silly zoomies when one of his foster parents came home.

On walks he can be very enthusiastic about squirrels and potential friends. With his growing size his new family will want to start early with training to help him walk cooperatively! Being easily motivated by food and praise will make training fun and a bit easier. His fosters are already working on his attentiveness while outside and report he’s a very good boy.

If you’re ready for this hunka love, come meet him this Saturday! We strongly encourage submitting your application and interviewing before the event. Pre-approved adopters get 30 minutes of exclusive access before the public.

You don’t have to wait for an adoption event. In-person meet-and-greets are available in the NYC area, with virtual meet-and-greets available for adopters outside NYC.
