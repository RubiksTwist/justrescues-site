Description provided by Rescue City

Oreo is a 9 month old, 34 pound hound/Australian Shepherd mix who is spayed, fully vetted, and ready to find her forever person. She is still very much a baby and is learning about the world, building confidence, and getting love wherever she can get it. Oreo is affectionate to her core and happiest when she’s being held or is right next to her human. She’ll gladly sit tucked between your legs at a coffee shop, ride cozy in a bag or stroller, or nestle with you on the couch. She loves snuffle mats and sniffing out hidden treats like the little hound she is, crunching on ice cubes, and giving LOTS of kisses. Her energy is moderate puppy energy; she can be playful and bouncy, but she settles well too. She’s currently healing from a leg injury, so she’s enjoying low-impact activity while she continues to recover like the champ she is. Like many young pups, Oreo is still learning. She’s working on leash manners, confidence around loud noises, and continued training around food boundaries. She’s smart, eager to please, and thrives with consistency and clear guidance. Oreo LOVES people and attention. Because she can guard food and prefers being the center of attention, she may do best as the only dog in the home, or with an experienced adopter committed to structure and training. She would flourish in a home where someone wants a loyal, devoted shadow and best friend! Oreo is still just a baby with so much love to give, please adopt her at rescuecity.nyc/adopt
