Meet Pepper Porter

Pepper Porter is a sweet senior girl who has already experienced a heartbreaking loss. She came to us from West Virginia after her owner passed away, leaving her without the care and companionship she had always known.

We couldn’t let her end up in a municipal shelter, so we stepped in to give her the chance she deserved.

Pepper is a gentle soul who may need a little time to adjust to her new surroundings. After everything she has been through, we want her to know that she is safe, loved, and finally in a place where she can take her time.

And once she gets comfortable, there is one thing Pepper absolutely loves: a good belly rub. There is something so sweet about watching a senior girl begin to relax and discover that she can enjoy the simple things again.

Pepper deserves a quiet, loving home where she can spend her golden years feeling secure and cherished. A family who will be patient with her, let her settle in at her own pace, and give her all the belly rubs she could ever want.

Please consider opening your heart and home to Pepper Porter. She has already lost so much, and she deserves a future filled with love, comfort, and the feeling of being home again.
