Description provided by Best Friends Animal Society — NYC

Alansa, a 1 1/2-year-old female, is currently in foster care and ready for adoption via our virtual process! Here’s what we know so far about her time in foster care: https://bestfriends.sharepoint.com/:b:/s/GRP_NYC_Foster/IQDjGVfcbsF3T5sDYVpO8Wq0ATngsxwkAg3OXLUe5-maBPI?e=goa20t She is very sweet and gentle and loves to play. Her foster says she gets along with the resident cat and is making great strides toward settling in. "She is very sweet and loves getting pets on her head (so much that she will headbutt you often, demanding them!). She is food motivated and you’ll get on her good side with a treat. She also trills adorably all day long and when she’s running around. She’s very curious and will always be watching what I’m doing and keeping me company. The noises she makes are adorable and remind me of a dog. She is chatty once getting comfortable and will trill and meow throughout the day, especially when she's in a playful mood. She loves head pets! She's extremely friendly with my cat. When she gets energy she will be running around the whole apartment - she's a fast runner! She's very gentle and friendly. It takes time to get her to trust you, but once she does, it's the best feeling! She makes the cutest noises - almost similar to birds and dogs. Especially when she's in an energetic mood, she'll trill whenever she moves or runs around. She comes to greet me at the door when i come home. Very social and playful. She loves attention and gentle pets. She trills constantly and will follow me around like a furry little shadow that she is. When I’m working from home, she’ll always lounge around near my office chair and wait for me to give her attention. She’ll perk up and trill when I take a break from work to hang out with her. Whenever I come out of the bedroom in the morning, she chirps happily and approaches me for pets. She loves playing and hanging out with me, and she loves to take naps on the couch next to me. When she’s in a particularly friendly mood, she’ll follow me around the apartment and is always laying in spots where she can see me." Her foster says "Very shy the first night, but quickly came out of her shell. Very food motivated and will always come when called if you have a treat. She’s very vocal and enjoys exploring the apartment and being pet. When you scratch her neck, she’ll immediately roll over and ask for a belly rub." Alansa has been spayed, vaccinated, microchipped, and is ready for her next steps.
