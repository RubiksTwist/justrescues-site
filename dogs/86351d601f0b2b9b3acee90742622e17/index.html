Spark and Starlight

Description provided by AMA Animal Rescue

Meet Spark and Starlight, two bonded siblings who were found in a park, completely soaked from the rain. Since their rescue, they do everything together such as wrestle, chase toys, and explore new places. They also love spending time with their people.

Spark is a confident, clever, and fearless little kitten. He always needs to know what is going on and loves exploring every corner and investigating anything new. His favorite toys are crinkle balls and toy mice, and he can spend hours chasing and batting them around. Spark is independent and curious, but he also enjoys spending time with his humans and Starlight.

Starlight is a playful, outgoing, and affectionate kitten with a huge personality. He loves wrestling with Spark, chasing toys, and causing harmless chaos wherever he goes. Starlight is especially obsessed with Churu treats and goes crazy for a fish string stick toy. Starlight is social, confident, and full of energy, but he also has a sweet side and loves being around people.

Spark and Starlight are ready to find their forever home together, and they would be happy to come into a home with other feline friends. They cannot wait to continue doing everything side by side. They would love to meet a family who will give them both the happy home they deserve.
