HISTORY

Rebecca Black’s owner left her boarding with our rescue partner in Georgia before moving out of state, saying she would come back for her. She never returned and could not be reached. Rebecca traveled to Brooklyn in mid-October 2026.

DETAILED PROFILE

Rebecca Black has a signature move, and it takes almost no encouragement: one kind word and she drops to the floor, belly up, ready for rubs. First, though, her whole back end gets in on the action, a butt wiggle so animated it seems to have a mind of its own. She’s a goofy, friendly, smart girl with moderate energy who makes friends with people fast and happily accepts pets from anyone offering.

She has lived in a home before, and it shows. Her foster says she's very easy to have around. She’s perfect in her crate, heads in quickly when asked, settles right away, and sleeps peacefully overnight. She’s been accident-free and appears house trained, and in the car she either watches out the window or lies down.

Food is her favorite subject. She gets very excited at mealtimes and whenever treats appear. That motivation makes her a quick study: show her a treat and you have her full attention. She’s pretty good on leash, if a little curious and easily distracted, and practice ignoring distractions will help.

Living with another dog, she spent about a day casually coexisting before the two started playing and wrestling happily. She’s been cat-tested and shared a room calmly with cats, sniffing a bit with no urge to chase. Her experience with kids isn’t known yet but she has seemed comfortable around them outside the home. She’s still settling in and has just moved to a new foster home, so some adjustment and backsliding are normal.

Belly rubs, a full bowl, and a friend to snuggle: Rebecca’s wish list is refreshingly short.
