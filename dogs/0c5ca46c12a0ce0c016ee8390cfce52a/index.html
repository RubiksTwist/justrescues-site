Meet Blue Shell!

Blue Shell is a sweet, gentle senior dog looking for a cozy, loving home to spend his golden years. He adores people and enjoys calm, affectionate interactions.

Blue Shell came from a municipal shelter, where he was at risk due to his age. Now in a safe and caring environment, he’s ready to find a forever family who will spoil him with love, belly rubs, and plenty of warm snuggles. This loyal companion has so much heart to give. Could you be the one to make his golden years truly special?

All dogs will be altered, microchipped, and receive age-appropriate vaccines prior to adoption. Please note that the listed location for this dog may not be accurate due to technical limitations of this database and we have transportation options available within 6 hours of NYC (so don't let location hold you back)!
