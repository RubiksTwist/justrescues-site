Everybody clear the runway for Jeff! At 9 years old, this spunky little guy is confident, full of personality, and overflowing with charm. Jeff knows who he is, what he likes, and how to bring endless joy to everyone around him.

Out on the sidewalk, Jeff is a total superstar! He walks on a leash like he is walking down the red carpet, happily soaking up every single second of his strolls and owning the sidewalk as he does it. He is also mastering his house-training, celebrating big wins and fresh progress every day!

When it comes to new places and friends, Jeff likes to take a moment to soak it all in before joining in on the fun. He loves having patient, loving company who lets him settle in at his own comfy pace. Once he feels right at home, his joyful spirit, fun personality, and adorable quirks come shining through. If you are looking for a little buddy with a big heart and an enormous character, he might be your perfect match!
