Description provided by NYC Second Chance Rescue

Hi, I’m Mousse! My foster parents say I’m a very handsome gentleman with a super silly side. I’m still a little shy when it comes to new things, but I’m also incredibly curious and getting braver every day.

I love exploring new rooms, looking out the window from the highest cat perch I can find, chasing feather toys, and of course, food and treats. I also really love my foster siblings, both cats and dogs. I especially adore my foster dog sibling and follow him everywhere.

My foster family has been teaching me how to play and explore. I’ve even started asking for attention on my own and have discovered that being pet is actually pretty great!

I’d thrive in a home with a patient family that will give me a little time to settle in, and some furry siblings that will keep me company and show me the ropes.
