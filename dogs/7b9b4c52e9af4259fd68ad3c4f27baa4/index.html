Description provided by Wags & Walks

Meet Louie! Louie is as sweet as he is snuggly! This playful puppy came to Wags & Walks with his brother, Larry, from a crowded shelter in the south. Now, they are both in the Hamptons ready to find their forever families! Louie loves spending his days wrestling, romping around, and exploring everything his puppy world has to offer. This gentle boy is expected to be a medium to large-sized dog when he’s fully grown, and will need plenty of love and support in the coming months! Like any puppy his age, Louie is full of curiosity and enjoys making new friends, playing with other pups, and then settling in for a well-earned nap with his favorite people. Louie is looking for a family that's excited to guide him through every stage of puppyhood, from training and socialization to plenty of playtime and cuddles. If you’re looking for a sweet puppy, Louie just might be your match!
