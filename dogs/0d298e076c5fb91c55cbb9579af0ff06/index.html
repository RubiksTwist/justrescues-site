Description provided by Waggytail Rescue

Meet Blue! A sweet 5-year-old, 10-pound female chihuahua mix. Blue arrived from Mexico and is looking for a family, or someone looking for a cuddle buddy and someone who loves couch as much as she does.

Blue is a little nervous and skittish, and she's been through a lot, so she's still building her confidence and learning to trust humans. She's already starting to open up to her foster parents. Underneath that shyness is a huge cuddle bug who just wants to be close to her people.

When she feels safe, she is a loyal little shadow and the ultimate cuddle bug. She loves nothing more than curling up next to you and just being together.

She's doing great with potty training outside and is gaining her bravery on the leash a little more every day. She does best when the street is quiet, and just needs her forever family to keep the consistency going. At night, she sleeps well in her crate.

She would do best with a patient adopter who will give her space and time to keep building trust. She has a few bald spots from burns she had before she came to us. With a little patience and a lot of love, she's going to be the sweetest, most loyal companion.

Vaccinated for rabies, DHPP, and bordetella, heartworm negative, spayed and microchipped. Current on flea, tick, and heartworm prevention.

To apply for Blue, visit our website (www.waggytailrescue.org) for an adoption application. It is important to fill out an application as soon as possible for consideration, and we require a complete application for you to meet an animal. We are currently unable to respond to general email inquiries as an all-volunteer run rescue. If the dog is on our website, they are still available.

Waggytail Rescue CANNOT guarantee the exact age of any adoptable dog, or final size of any of our adoptable puppies. We rely on the information provided to us by veterinarians, shelters, owner surrenders and other circumstances bringing the dog into our rescue.

Adoptions: adopt@waggytailrescue.org
