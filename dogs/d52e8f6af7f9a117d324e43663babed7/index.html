Meet Stitch

Sometimes the world is unkind to the smallest souls, but Stitch is proof that even after unimaginable cruelty, hope can find its way in.

Stitch was found in Harlem, living through a nightmare no dog should ever experience. Emaciated, covered in fleas, wounded, and absolutely terrified, he had endured prolonged abuse and neglect. Thankfully, a Good Samaritan stepped in and helped save him from the situation he was living in. Because someone chose to care, Stitch finally got a chance at a different life.

We don't know everything Stitch has been through, and he cannot tell us how many times he was hurt or how long he waited for someone to help. But we do know what he deserves from this moment forward: gentle hands, a safe place to rest, patience as he learns to trust, and a family who will never let him down.

Stitch's past may have been heartbreaking, but it does not get to write the rest of his story. We want his next chapter to be filled with kindness, comfort, and the unconditional love every dog deserves.

He has survived enough. Now it's time for Stitch to finally live.
