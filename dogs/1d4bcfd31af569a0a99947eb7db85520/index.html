Description provided by Wags & Walks

Meet Luci! Luci is as sweet as can be and has a heart full of love. If you're looking for an active dog who can keep up with your adventures before happily settling in with the family for an evening snuggle, Luci may be your perfect match! ⁠ Luci loves getting outside to explore, whether it's romping through the backyard or joining you for a long walk. One of Luci's best qualities is her gentle and loving nature. She's extremely friendly and patient with children and even young puppies. She plays well with dogs of all sizes and she has also lived peacefully with a cat! ⁠ Luci has had great success with potty training when given structure and routine. She has great house manners and is ready to transition into a forever home with ease. If you're searching for an active, affectionate dog who's equally happy joining family adventures and curling up at the end of the day, Luci is ready to become your new best friend!⁠
