Description provided by AMA Animal Rescue

Meet Croissant, the boldest little pastry in the bakery and the undisputed alpha of his litter! This freshly baked boy has a perfectly puffed personality and is always ready to roll into action.

This brilliant little guy is as smart as they come and twice as funny. He is constantly ready to play and loves engaging with people. He is a natural-born climber; he is always looking for the highest peaks, whether that is the tallest cat tree or your shoulder. Life with Croissant is a non-stop adventure that is absolutely the yeast you could ask for.

Beneath his exterior lies an inquisitive sweetie who is completely fascinated by his humans. He loves to shadow your every move, and getting involved in whatever it is you're doing. He truly wants to be near people, providing all of the love and affection he can. he wishes to find a loving home with another feline friend, as he loves to play with other cats.
