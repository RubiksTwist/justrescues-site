Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

A volunteer writes:
Little Millie was found roaming the streets of New York City and brought to the Manhattan Care Center by a Good Samaritan. The cutie was a bit shy to start with, going in and out her hiding place but gentle words and tasty treats(as well as her natural curiosity) got her to trust us and she is now feeling much better, coming for cuddles and churus. Millie's past is a big unknown but she seems like a sweet little gal who would make a new owner happy to have her as a pet. Millie appears healthy but her SNAP test(done on all our incoming kitties) was positive for FeLV, an immunologic condition that predisposes cats to certain infections and certain types of cancer(this can be discussed with our adoption counselors if you envision adopting Millie). These cats need to be your only cat in the house or live with other FeLV+ cats. It is not transmissible to humans or other animals. The test will be repeated to rule in or rule out Millie's FeLV status. Millie is a sweet little tigress, so worth your attention and love. She is waiting to start a new page in her life with you.
