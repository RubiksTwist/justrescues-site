Description provided by Bideawee

Kima is a sweet, affectionate girl who came to Bideawee after losing her home. She loves meeting new people, making friends, and getting plenty of attention from her favorite humans. She's playful, loving, and has so much to offer someone willing to give her a chance.

Unfortunately, Kima has been struggling with shelter life, and we're hoping she won't have to wait much longer to find a home. She deserves a place where she can feel comfortable, loved, and secure again. Kima would do best in a home with adults or older children and as the only dog, where she can soak up all the attention. After everything she's been through, we're hoping her next chapter will be a happy one.

If this animal is located at our Manhattan Adoption Center, head in to meet them and see if you are a match! We are a same-day adoption center, and an application does not put you on a list or hold an animal for you. If they are in Wantagh or in a foster home, please submit an online application and our team will reach out to further discuss the adoption process. Prior to adoption, all of our animals are spayed/neutered, microchipped, and given their age-appropriate vaccines.
