Her foster says "Cookie was a little shy at first and loves hiding under blankets, but she warmed up so quickly to me and absolutely loves attention. She’s loves her beauty sleep during the day, and as soon as the sun sets she immediately finds you for head rubs. She is so affectionate and comfortable when interacting with people. She loves to find my lap or rub against me when I’m not paying attention to her. She also loves to just sit at stare at me." If you’re open to a little patience and a lot of possibility, come meet Cookie! You might be the person who helps them feel safe, confident, and right at home.
