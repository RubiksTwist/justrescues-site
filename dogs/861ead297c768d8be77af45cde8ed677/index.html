Meet Apollo | Apply Here | Videos

Apollo is a 2 year old Malti-poo weighing just about 8lbs. He was rescued alongside some of the other littles you’ve seen this month at an illegal puppymill in Gyeonggi-do, South Korea.

This little one is a fire cracker! He can be a little shy at first as he gets his bearings. Totally fair with how small he is and how large the world is! Once he’s settled into your home though he’s a hoot. Apollo gets zoomies often and loves a good game of chase. He’s quickly adapted to all of the toys his foster has to offer and seems to know he deserves all of the fun this new life offers.

Apollo is continuing to work on his crate and house training, but he’s a very motivated little dude! A good routine will have him in tip top shape and make him a well rounded and confident pup.

You don’t have to wait for an adoption event. In-person meet-and-greets are available in the NYC area, with virtual meet-and-greets available for adopters outside NYC.
