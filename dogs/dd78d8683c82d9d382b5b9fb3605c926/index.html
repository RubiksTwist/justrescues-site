Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Meet Xola, a sweet, energetic, and easygoing girl who recently entered foster care with Every Last One Rescue. We first met Xola during a Borough Break from the shelter, and she immediately showed us what a fun dog she is to have along for an adventure.

Xola would love a home where she can enjoy a fun outing each day and then happily snooze the afternoon away.

One of the things that makes Xola so special is how unfazed she is by the city! She is alert and curious when she sees other dogs and people. She takes in her surroundings without being overwhelmed and is curious while remaining respectful.

We're excited to learn more about Xola as she settles into foster care. Check back soon for all the updates!
