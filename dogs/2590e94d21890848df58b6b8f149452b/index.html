Description provided by Bideawee

Meet Mitatsu! This sweet, laid-back lady has settled beautifully into foster care and loves spending time with her people. She's happiest snuggling beside you while you read or watch TV, but she also enjoys short play sessions with her favorite wand toys and coils. She's even been known to give a little paw tap when she's ready for a treat!

Mitatsu is affectionate without being overly demanding and enjoys attention on her own terms. She'd be a wonderful companion for someone looking for a relaxed, loving cat to share their home with.

If this cat is located at our Manhattan Adoption Center, head in to meet them and see if you are a match! We are a same-day adoption center, and an application does not put you on a list or hold an animal for you. If they are in Wantagh or in a foster home, please submit an online application and our team will reach out to further discuss the adoption process. Prior to adoption, all of our animals are spayed/neutered, microchipped, and given their age-appropriate vaccines.
