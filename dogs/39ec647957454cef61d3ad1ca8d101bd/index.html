Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Heart lives up to her name - she’s a sweet, gentle senior cat with so much love to give. She was surrendered by her previous owner, of no fault of her own, and is now ready for a fresh start in a home where she can feel safe and cherished.

Super friendly and always there to greet you at the door, she loves gentle pets and would treasure a comfy lap to nap on. She has the cutest squeaks you ever did hear and will greet you every morning with the most loving chirps.

Heart does have allergies - and will need a special person who is understanding of her needs and will continue to give her the care she needs. If you’re looking for a loving companion and a best friend for life, Heart is ready to give you hers.
