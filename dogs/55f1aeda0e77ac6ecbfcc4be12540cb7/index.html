Meet Biggs!

Biggs was found abandoned with three other dogs and is now looking for a fresh start with a family who will help him settle into the good life he deserves. He's social, people oriented, and has a big personality that comes out once he feels comfortable.

My Ideal Home

Biggs would do best with an adopter who can provide structure, enrichment, and positive reinforcement training to help guide his enthusiasm. He enjoys attention and engagement from people and will thrive with daily physical and mental exercise like walks, puzzle toys, and interactive play. A home that can continue working on leash manners and impulse control will help him really shine.

Good with Other Pets?

Biggs has shown some interest in other dogs at the care center, but also tends to pull strongly toward smaller dogs on leash. His history with other animals is unknown, so slow, controlled introductions would be important if living with another pet.

Good with Kids?

Due to potential resource guarding tendencies and his strength and enthusiasm, Biggs is recommended for a home without young children at this time.

Things to Know

Biggs is a social and affectionate boy who enjoys being around people and seeks out attention readily. He can be strong on leash and may redirect his energy toward other dogs or exciting stimuli, so he'll do best with continued training and management. He has shown possible interest in guarding valued items, so ongoing trade up games and positive reinforcement around handling and resources will be important. With consistency and structure, Biggs has all the makings of a loyal, fun, and engaging companion.

Volunteer Notes

A volunteer writes: Biggs is giving main character energy from the moment you meet him. He's a social, handsome guy who leans in for attention and happily accepts treats like he's starring in his own feel good rom com. Out in the yard, he's engaged and people focused, soaking up interaction and showing off his affectionate side. You might say he's looking for someone to be his "Mr. Biggs," the steady presence who matches his charm, guides his enthusiasm, and takes him on all the best adventures the city has to offer. With the right home, this boy is ready for his happily ever after.

Fun Fact

Biggs is the kind of dog who believes every walk should come with a little drama, a lot of heart, and maybe a snack break on a park bench.
