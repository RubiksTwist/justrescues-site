Smokey is at risk for behavior reasons. Smokey was initially fearful on intake but warmed up with a considerate approach. He loves to play with toys but has shown moderate resource guarding. Smokey had an incident where he became over aroused with play and bit a staff member's sweater causing it to tear. He had to be slowed down and given some time to recover. Smokey has been in care since early May. Medically, he is apparently healthy.

Smokey is looking for a calm, patient home that will give him the time and space he needs to settle in and build trust at his own pace. Smokey was initially fearful after arriving to the care center back in May. He warmed up quickly with a considerate approach, and quickly became a staff and volunteer favorite.

Smokey LOVES toys - they're extra special to him! Because of this, he should be allowed to enjoy them peacefully without being disturbed. Smokey should be interacted with in a calm manner to avoid any over arousal. After many months in care, he is starting to deteriorate and will require a decompression period.

This shy guy may need a little extra time, but for the right person, watching Smokey grow in confidence will be incredibly rewarding.
