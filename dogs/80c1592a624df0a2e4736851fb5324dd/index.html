Pre-Screener Form

Max was surrendered into care by his previous owner due to landlord issues. Max's previous owner noted that he needs time to warm up to strangers before becoming social. He is noted to resource guard food and treats, and will hard bark and growl at unfamiliar dogs when off leash together. With his family, he was overall described as a social, affectionate, active dog who would spend most of his time in the living room if he wasn't following his people around. During his intake, Max was noted to be fearful and escalated to baring his teeth and snapping when attempting to collar him. Since his intake, Max remains fearful and avoidant of leashing. He has growled and snapped in the kennel at the lead; when out of the kennel, he remains tense and avoidant of handlers. Due to the concerns in therepvious home and during his stay in care, we recommend for him to be placed in an adult only home through a New Hope parnter who can provide any necessary behavior modification (force-free, positive reinforcement-based) and re-evaluate behavior in a stable home environment before placement into a permanent home.
