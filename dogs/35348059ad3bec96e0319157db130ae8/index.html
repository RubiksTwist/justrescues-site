Rocky is on the at-risk list due to behavior concerns. In the care center, Rocky has shown fearful and defensive behavior during handling, including growling and reportedly biting staff when being picked up from his kennel. Rocky has also shown the ability to tolerate handling with a towel, allowing staff to pick him up and return him to his kennel without further issue and is improving with handling slowly, but still is extremely fearful in the care center. Medically, Rocky has moderate dental disease and an ear infection.

Rocky was surrendered to the care center by his previous owner due to their housing situation. They had previously had Rocky for eight years, and describe him as a social, affectionate, playful dog who loves to play fetch with a ball. Rocky has not acclimated well to the care center environment, and had shown highly fearful behavior around handling, escalating to attempts to bite during his medical exam and when being picked up. Due to these concerns, we recommend placement in an adult-only home with a New Hope partner, who can allow for a period of decompression, provide any necessary behavior modification (force-free, positive reinforcement-based) and re-evaluate behavior in a stable home environment before placement into a permanent home.
