Meet Xena!

Xena’s story began in a basement, where she was found just hours after giving birth, in pain and in desperate need of medical care. Despite everything she had been through, she continued to care for her newborn kittens. Thankfully, Xena got the help she needed, and today, she’s ready for a new chapter, one where she gets to be the one who is loved and cared for.

Xena is a playful, independent girl who enjoys her toys, especially a good wand toy session! She has spent time in a loving foster home, where she’s been socialized with dogs, cats, and kittens. She’s comfortable around people but appreciates having her own space, too. She’s slowly learning that human affection can be a wonderful thing, happily accepting nose pets and enjoying when you talk to her. Xena will thrive with a patient family who lets her open up at her own pace. She may not be the type to demand attention right away, but with time, love, and a little patience, she has so much potential to blossom. Could you be the one to give Xena the safe, happy home she deserves?
