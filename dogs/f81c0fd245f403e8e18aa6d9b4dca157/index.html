Meet Shylo!

Found as a stray, Shylo is a young girl who is curious about her surroundings and always keeping an eye out for treats! She readily approaches her people and enjoys exploring, though she can be a little unsure when things feel unfamiliar. Give her some time to get comfortable, and you'll get to see more of her sweet personality shine.

My Ideal Home

Shylo will thrive with a patient family who can give her time to settle in and build confidence. She'll benefit from daily physical and mental enrichment, including walks, food puzzles, long lasting chews, and fun games like hide and seek. Positive reinforcement and letting Shylo set the pace will help her feel safe and successful as she gets to know her new world.

Good with Other Pets?

We don't have much history about Shylo's life before arriving at ACC, and we did not observe a clear response to other dogs during her time in the shelter. Any future introductions should be slow, thoughtful, and supervised, giving Shylo plenty of time to feel comfortable.

Good with Kids?

Shylo would do best with older, dog savvy children who understand how to give a dog space and let her approach on her own terms. Because she can be sensitive during handling and in unfamiliar situations, calm and respectful interactions will be especially important.

Things to Know

Shylo walks with mild pulling and can be a little sensitive to physical handling, so she'll appreciate gentle interactions and plenty of positive reinforcement. She can also be vocal when startled by something unfamiliar and may need a little time to recover, making a calm routine and patient approach especially helpful. She's a young dog with lots to discover, and with the right person beside her, Shylo can grow into a confident and wonderful companion!

A volunteer writes: Breaking news: The volunteer group chat is abuzz about Shylo! With her petite frame (she's only 39 pounds) and speckled ears nearly as tall as the height of her head, one look at her will instantly put a smile on your face. On our walks, strangers comment on how cute she is - it's impossible not to notice. When we sit with her on a ledge, she's content to crawl under our legs and just hang out there for a while, a respite from the tiny kennel in an overflow room she's called home for the last month.

Shylo was dropped off at a police precinct with no information. While her microchip did lead to a previous owner, they were unable to be reached. When Shylo first arrived at the shelter, she was a bit shy and hesitant to walk, but her confidence has grown over the last few weeks as she's learned to make a little more sense of her confusing surroundings. With volunteers and staff she's social and sweet, soliciting attention and often checking in on walks. Shylo is a dog who we can tell is eager to connect, and we think she'll really blossom outside of the chaotic shelter environment. Due to arriving as a stray, we have no information on how she is with other animals in the home. Shylo is already spayed and ready to go home, available to adopters with some dog experience and homes without children under 13.
