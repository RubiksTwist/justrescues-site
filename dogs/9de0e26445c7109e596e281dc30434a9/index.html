Description provided by ASPCA Adoption Center

Is it odd how smitten we are with Oddish? No way! When you see a kitty as cute as this little guy, you fall in love immediately! This social butterfly loves meeting new people and making friends. Who knew so much personality could fit into such a tiny package?

Oddish has thrived in foster care. His foster reports that he adores his feline foster brother and spends his days following him around, initiating playtime, and seeking him out for cuddles. He likes to cuddle with his humans as well, but its playtime that makes him the happiest! If you have a ball of yarn, then you also have a new best friend.
