HISTORY

Siri was picked up as a stray in Georgia in early September 2026 and taken to a local shelter, where no one came forward to claim her. She traveled to Brooklyn in mid-October 2026.

DETAILED PROFILE

Siri has a filing system. Her daily chew goes into the cardboard box in the corner, comes back out to her bed a few minutes later, then goes right back in. It’s one of the clearer signs that this goofy senior boxer is relaxing into home life.

Indoors she follows her people from room to room, eager to help with every chore, then naps happily through the workday. She has moderate energy, a deep love of pillows and blankets, and a habit of singing along with fire trucks. She’s happy to meet new people, and any face within range gets thoroughly licked. She’s met kids at adoption events and seemed fine, though that experience is limited.

Walks are the main project. She pulls hard when she’s fresh and chases birds, squirrels, and anything resembling them, including stray bags. She isn’t very food motivated, but cheese gets her full attention, and she’s starting to learn sit and leave it. Her crate is coming along nicely; after an early protest, she has stayed calmly for a few hours at a stretch. She’s mostly house trained, with a few slip-ups after a change in her foster’s household.

Other dogs need a careful match. She whines and pulls toward them on walks and greets with a lot of intensity, and meetings have gone anywhere fro playful to growly. She’s very curious about cats but has shown no aggression in gated or leashed meetings; cats are still being assessed. She’s still settling in, so expect more to come.

Her next home should stock cheese. A spare cardboard box wouldn’t hurt either."
