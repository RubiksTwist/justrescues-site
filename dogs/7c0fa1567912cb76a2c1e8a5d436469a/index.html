HISTORY

Siri was picked up as a stray in Georgia in early September 2026 and taken to a local shelter, where no one came forward to claim her. She traveled to Brooklyn in mid-October 2026.

DETAILED PROFILE

Siri has a two-setting social life. Outside, people barely register; there’s too much to sniff. Inside, she turns into a full-on face licker, generous with the kisses and the boxer drool. She’s a goofy, clumsy, curious adult boxer with moderate energy, and she’s still settling in, so consider this an early read.

So far, home life agrees with her. She’s curious about everything happening around the house but relaxes a little more each day, eating full meals and sinking into deep, satisfying naps. Toys get mild interest; a couch and a good pillow get her full devotion. When a fire truck goes by, she sings along.

The busy city is a lot for her right now, and she is easily distracted outside. Leash manners and crate training are works in progress, and she doesn’t know any commands yet, so her adopter should plan on teaching the basics from scratch. The good news: she’s had no accidents in her foster home.

Other dogs are case by case. She has met only a few, with mixed results, so any dog sibling would need to be a carefully chosen match. Her fosters’ cats aren’t sure about her yet, but in a calm, leashed introduction she sniffed and licked one with soft, relaxed body language. Cats are still being assessed, and her experience with kids isn’t known.

The couch pillow is already claimed. Your face is next.
