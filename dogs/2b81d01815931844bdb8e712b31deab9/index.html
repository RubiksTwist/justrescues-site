Meet Pixel!

Pixel is a sweet, affectionate cat who is ready to find a loving family of her own. She enjoys attention, loves being pet, and is happiest when she's spending time with her favorite people.

Pixel's journey to our shelter was a heartbreaking one. She was left alone in front of our shelter one afternoon, scared and uncertain about what had happened to her. Despite being abandoned, Pixel has remained gentle, loving, and trusting. She has shown us just how resilient and kind-hearted she truly is.

Since arriving, Pixel has been soaking up affection from staff and volunteers. She enjoys pets and companionship and has a warm, loving personality that makes it easy to fall in love with her. Whether she's relaxing by your side or asking for a few extra scratches, Pixel is always happy to share her affection.

Pixel may also do well in a home with another cat. While at the shelter, she has had several positive interactions when seeing other cats, suggesting she could enjoy a feline companion with a proper introduction.

After being left behind, Pixel deserves a family that will cherish her forever. If you're looking for a sweet, affectionate companion with a lot of love to give, Pixel may be the perfect addition to your home. Please fill out and submit an adoption application today.
