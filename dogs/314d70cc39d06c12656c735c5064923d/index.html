Description provided by NYC Second Chance Rescue

Butters is low-maintenance, wildly affectionate, and equal parts playful and mellow. While Butters was in a shelter, she suffered serious injuries from a dog attack. She has come a long way since then but will be happiest as the only pet in the home. Butters is eager to share her golden years with a special someone who will love her to the moon and back. Had a bad day at work? Butters will greet you with full-body wiggles when you open the door. Apply today to give Butters her happily ever after.

All dogs will be altered, microchipped, and receive age-appropriate vaccines prior to adoption.
