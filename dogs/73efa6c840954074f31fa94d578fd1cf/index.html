He's very friendly with other dogs of all shapes and sizes and loves playing with them. He plays a little bit with toys inside, but he’s slightly awkward and walks carefully on non-carpeted floors. He's also a little skiddish of new places/stores and needs a nudge to explore new stores but he’s making progress quickly. It also took him a couple of days to get used to apartment stairs, but he has now mastered them. He’s a very tall lanky boy so it’s probably just a function of his size, but once he trusts you he follows you everywhere no matter what. He is very agreeable and comfortable with all touch including wiping paws after walks and baths. He is great on walks and follows direction really well, but needs to be guided or held close on busy streets since he takes up so much room! He figured out how to go potty outside immediately so no worries of accidents here. He also eats very well, but isn't particularly food motivated which is nice since he’s so tall (no counter surfing here!)Bruno is an amazing, once in a lifetime pup!" Up to date on their DHPP, Bordetella, and Rabies vaccines; heartworm tested if coming from Tennessee or Puerto Rico; spayed/neutered; microchipped; treated with dewormer upon intake How did this dog end up with Social Tees? Most of our dogs come via partner groups who help us save them from shelters where they are at risk of euthanasia in Tennessee, Los Angeles, and Puerto Rico. There are very few spay/neuter resources there and many more animals in those areas than there are people to adopt them. Rescue groups like ours bring them to the northeast where the desire for pets like these allows us to save their lives.
