Meet Ace of Hearts!

Ace of Hearts was surrendered when his owner could no longer care for him. He previously lived with adults, and is described as a highly social, wiggly, and affectionate dog who loves interacting with people. He comes over readily for attention, leans into touch, and enjoys playtime, often offering play bows and engaging happily with toys.

My Ideal Home

Ace would do best in a calm, patient home that can support him as he settles in and builds confidence. He can be anxious at times and may pace or have trouble settling, especially in new environments, so a consistent routine and a quiet space will help him feel more secure.

Good with Other Pets?

Ace has shown neutral behavior around dogs at the shelter, but he is not a match for homes with cats due to lunging toward them on walks. Continued positive reinforcement training and management will be important when navigating environments with other animals.

Good with Kids?

A home without young children under 5 is recommended due to his sensitivity to handling and tendency to become fearful when startled.

Things to Know

Ace is a sweet and people-oriented dog who enjoys affection and play, but he can be sensitive to loud noises, sudden movements, and handling around his paws. He may also feel anxious when left alone. He responds best to gentle, choice-based interactions and should never be forced into situations that make him uncomfortable. With time, structure, and positive reinforcement, he has great potential to feel more confident and settled in a home.

Fun Fact

Ace of Hearts lives up to his name, he is all heart when it comes to his people and loves being right by your side soaking up attention.
