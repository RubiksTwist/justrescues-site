She's Ginger. She's Spice. She's Everything Nice!

At 4 years old and a tiny 45 lb compct bundle of love, Ginger Spice is a girl who takes your breath away the moment you look at her.

Ginger Spice is a sweet girl who teeters between having a shy and timid personality and an evenkeeled and neutral temperant. Her tender aura matches her demeanor and her beautiful expressive eyes can sometimes seem as though she is yearning for a distant memory that is now no more.

Every so often, if you are lucky enough, Ginger Spice will give you glimpses of a carefree girl who lives to love and loves to live. At her most vulnerable, Ginger Spice has no qualms about showing her people how much they mean to her, often doting on them for hours on end, thanking them for loving her by giving them kisses and showing how much she appreciates them for making her feel safe by rolling over for belly rubs trusting that they care for her too. Once you've made your way into Ginger Spice's heart, you're there forever and you'll know you've made your best friend.

Ginger Spice is game for any time thats spent along side her person. Wether that time be spent taking a leisurely stroll where she shows off being a housebroek, loose leash walker or wether that time be spent watching movies while curled up in your lap. Once Ginger Spice realizes you've accepted her as a welcomed part of your life, she will definitely enmesh herself amongst your social circle regardless if that means hanging out with your friends, family members or simply meeting strangers on the street. Ginger Spice's goal seems to make the entire world jealous of the bond she has with her person by winning them over with the friendly, playful, affectionate girl she blossoms into once she's found her stride in your home.

With other animals, especially dogs, Ginger Spice can often be wary and seems to want to hide from them. Should an adopter be interested in introducing Ginger Spice to other dogs or hoping for her to co-exist amongst a resident sibling, Ginger Spice would need an adopter willing to work on her confidence, support her becoming brave, keep her safe and ensure that her experiences remain positive which will set her up for the most success.

Ginger Spice is the girl that deeply wants to share her deepest, darkest, secrets with you on first meet but her lived experiences now requires her to make sure you don't run away after she shows you her soul. The more time Ginger Spice spends with her person, the more it reinforces that they choose her too and soon enough she will develop the skills to understand that the world has multiple safe places - sometimes its with her person, sometimes its in the crate and sometimes its being left alone as long as she's learned that her people will always come back for her.

Ginger Spice is a little girl who has spent too much time looking for the person she can trust with her big emotions. Once she finds that person, the bond will be unbreakable, the love will be immeasurable and the joy she will bring to them will be unfathomable.
