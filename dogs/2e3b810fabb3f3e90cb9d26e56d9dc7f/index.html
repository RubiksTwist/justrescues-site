My Foster writes: Looking for a best friend who will absolutely adore you? Gepetto is your girl. 💜 She's the sweetest mix of playful and cuddly-always up for a walk or play, but equally happy curled up beside you. She's house-trained, gentle, knows sit and how to give both paws, respects furniture boundaries, and is incredibly loving and trusting. She forms such a special bond with her person and is looking for someone who will give her the consistency and reassurance she deserves. She's still building confidence around other dogs on walks, but with her person by her side, this sweet girl has so much love to give and is ready to finally find her forever home.

My foster writes: Gepetto is an angel amongst us. She's the type of dog you fall in love with immediately (and she returns the affection tenfold). This girl loves hugs and kisses, and just wants to be in the same room as you. Well, that and as many stuffed toys she can find.
She is a great walker, good in her crate, and an excellent treat taker. She's understandably still a little anxious, so on walks she's wary of other dogs and people. But we just walk right by them no fuss no muss.
Gepetto I think would like another dog in the household to help with her confidence and loneliness. She would also be very happy being solo and the center of your world.
I can't say enough good things about Gepetto. Snap her up, she's a keeper, no lie!

My foster writes: Geppeto is a sweet girl who loves to give kisses and enjoy snuggling up next to you. She's great with children and doesn't chew on furniture. She has separation anxiety and is a little skittish sometimes when she is being walked- but with a little time and effort she will be ok in no time.
Although she pulls on a leash a little when being walked (because she's so curious of the world!) she comes right back to your side if called. Gepetto also knows sit, paw and up. She's an amazing dog and will be anyone's best friend !

My foster writes: Gepetto is super friendly and sweet! She gives so many kisses and loves to cuddle! Shes perfectly potty trained and doesn't bite on things in the house. Gepetto is great around children and will be your best friend!
