Say hello to Bahama Mama - a little taste of paradise waiting for her forever home!

If you’ve been dreaming of a sweet, affectionate companion who can bring a little sunshine into your life, look no further than Bahama Mama. This beautiful calico girl is as lovely on the inside as she is on the outside, and she has all the ingredients for the perfect feline best friend.

With her gorgeous calico coloring and adorable personality, Bahama Mama is truly a girl who stands out from the crowd. But what makes her extra special is her sweet and affectionate nature. She came into ARF as an owner surrender, of no fault of her own, but that hasn't dampened her spirits.

Come meet Bahama Mama - skip the plane ticket and bring home your own little piece of paradise.
