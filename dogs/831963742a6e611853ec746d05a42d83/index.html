HISTORY

Jeremy Allen White was born into rescue in early 2024 after his mother was rescued, pregnant, in the U.S. Virgin Islands. He was adopted as a puppy in mid-2024 and was returned to Badass in October 2026 when his adopters were unable to give him the time he needed.

DETAILED PROFILE

Jeremy Allen White has one move he never gets tired of: the roll. Grass, couch, floor, his own toys, it doesn’t matter. If there’s a surface, he’s on his back, wriggling with total commitment. Add a car ride with his head out the window and you’ve basically planned his perfect day.

He’s a goofy, curious, clever young guy with moderate energy and playful bursts, and he loves exploring outdoors. He’s very food motivated, knows his basic commands, and has the rare skill of coming reliably when called off leash. He’s friendly and outgoing with people, so friendly that he tends to jump when greeting someone new, a habit he’s still working on. He’s done well with older kids, and a home with school-age or older children would be a good fit.

He’s lived with dogs and cats and gets along with both, and he’s spent plenty of time socializing at a dog camp. A canine sibling is welcome but not required.

On the practical side, he settles quietly in his crate, especially with a bone to work on. House training needs a fresh start: he does best on a consistent schedule with a trip outside soon after meals. He walks pretty well on leash, but city sidewalks will be new, so expect some practice. Stuffed toys are off the menu, since he prefers to eat them over playing with them. Like any dog changing homes, he may need a refresher on the basics as he settles in.

His ideal person keeps a steady routine, a pocket full of treats, and a car window that rolls down.
