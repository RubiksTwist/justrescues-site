Description provided by AMA Animal Rescue

Meet Gus, your future adventure buddy, professional ball fetcher, and the best-dressed dog in town!

Gus is a goofy, affectionate guy with a big personality and a whole lot of love to give. After being found wandering the streets alone, he has come a long way and is ready to find a family who will make him part of their world. He loves a good adventure, chasing after tennis balls, showing off his latest outfit, and soaking up belly rubs. And when it comes to dressing up, nobody does it better! Gus has the best costumes year after year, and he's always ready to steal the show.

Gus is an incredibly smart boy who loves learning new things and making his people proud. He will need a family committed to consistent training, patience, and structure so he can continue building good habits and becoming the best version of himself. He forms strong bonds with his people and is an extremely loyal companion once he trusts you.

Gus would thrive in a home with a single owner who can give him the consistency, attention, and companionship he needs. In return, you'll have a devoted sidekick who will happily join you on adventures, make you laugh with his silly antics, and never say no to a good belly rub.

If you're looking for a loyal best friend with a goofy personality and a wardrobe better than yours, Gus might just be your guy!
