Meet Bankai!
Bankai is a sweet, gentle girl who seems to have mastered the art of being a perfect little lady! Found as a stray, she's still getting comfortable with her new surroundings, but she's already shown us what a kind and affectionate companion she can be. She's a little timid at first, but with some patience, encouragement, and tasty treats, she begins to warm up and enjoy attention from her people. Bankai is hoping to find a home where she can finally take a deep breath and enjoy the good life!

My Ideal Home
Bankai would love a peaceful home where she can settle in at her own pace and enjoy life's quieter moments. She's a lovely walking companion who enjoys leisurely strolls and keeping pace with her person. Daily walks, food puzzles, long lasting chews, and gentle enrichment will give her fun ways to explore and build confidence. Most of all, she needs someone who understands that the best friendships sometimes take a little time to blossom.

Good with Other Pets?
Bankai's history with other dogs is unknown since she came to us as a stray. During a recent introduction, she showed some interest in another dog and returned to say hello several times, although she was a little unsure throughout the interaction. Slow, thoughtful introductions with plenty of breaks will help her feel comfortable getting to know potential furry friends.

Good with Kids?
Bankai would do best in an adult only home where she can enjoy a calm environment and build confidence without too much hustle and bustle. She's a sensitive girl who appreciates patience, gentle interactions, and having the freedom to approach people when she's ready.

Things to Know
Bankai may be a little hesitant when meeting new people or having her collar put on, but she warms up with gentle encouragement and tasty treats. Once she feels comfortable, she happily accepts affection and follows along with her handler. She walks nicely with only mild pulling noted during her assessment, and she's been a quiet, polite walking companion for our volunteers. Give this sweet girl a little time and a loving home, and you may just see her blossom into the most wonderful companion.
