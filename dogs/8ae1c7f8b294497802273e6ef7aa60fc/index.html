Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Meet Paulie! This handsome little hunk recently joined Every Last One Rescue.

Paulie is a small guy with a whole lot of charm. He's loose, wiggly, and always has that tail wagging. Paulie is incredibly affectionate and is happy to go wherever you go! Whether that be on a walk, exploring the city, or cuddled up next to you on the couch. He walks wonderfully on the leash and has no problem zig-zagging through the busy NYC streets! Paulie has seamlessly entered into his foster home, just happy to be loved and doted upon.

He arrived at the shelter alongside four other dogs and a cat before making his way into foster care. One of those dogs is actually a familiar face around here, our very own Princess Buttercup, who's also in foster care with ELO! If you are looking for an easygoing, cuddly, spunky boy then Paulie is perfect for you. He will give you as much love as his little compact body can!
