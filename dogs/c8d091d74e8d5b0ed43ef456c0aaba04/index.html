Meet Callie!

Callie is an adorable 8-year-old Chihuahua/Dachshund mix weighing about 25 pounds. She's a sweet, affectionate girl who forms strong bonds with her people and loves nothing more than being close to her favorite person. If you're looking for a loyal little companion to share your days with, Callie could be your girl!

Callie has plenty of wonderful qualities. She's house-trained, walks nicely on a leash, and is comfortable staying home alone. She has a moderate energy level and is happiest enjoying the simple things in life—spending time with her person, going for leisurely walks, and relaxing at home.

Callie does, however, have some specific needs when it comes to her environment and interactions with unfamiliar people. She can be protective of her home and her person, and has a history of biting when unfamiliar visitors enter her space. She also displays resource-guarding behaviors. For this reason, Callie will need an experienced, adult-only home with someone who understands canine reactivity and resource guarding and is committed to maintaining appropriate boundaries and continuing to work with her.

Her ideal home would be a quiet, predictable environment with no other pets, preferably outside the hustle and bustle of a busy city. She isn't looking for a home with lots of visitors or a hectic social calendar. She's looking for someone who appreciates a devoted companion and understands that every dog has their own comfort zone.

Callie has so much love to give to the person she trusts. With the right adopter, a calm environment, and an understanding of her needs, she has the opportunity to be a wonderful, loyal companion.
