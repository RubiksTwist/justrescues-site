Meet Miss Independent!
Miss Independent was found as a stray, and this curious girl is ready to find a home where she can be her own kind of wonderful! She's a social, food motivated pup who loves exploring her surroundings and is always happy to say hello, especially when treats are involved. She's got a little independent streak, but she's also more than happy to make new friends along the way!

My Ideal Home
Miss Independent would love an active home where she can enjoy daily walks, sniff out new adventures, and spend time with her favorite people. Food puzzles, long lasting chews, and fun training games will help keep her clever mind busy. She's a bright girl who will enjoy learning new skills, especially when there are tasty rewards involved!

Good with Other Pets?
Miss Independent's history with other dogs is unknown. During a recent introduction, she approached another dog for a brief sniff before deciding she'd rather explore on her own. She may prefer having a little space when meeting new canine friends, so slow and thoughtful introductions will be important.

Good with Kids?
Miss Independent would do best in a home without children under 5. She's an enthusiastic girl who can get excited during greetings and sometimes uses her mouth when interacting, so she'll appreciate a family who can help her practice gentle greetings and good manners.

Things to Know
Miss Independent loves treats and can get a little enthusiastic when taking them, so practicing gentle treat taking and rewarding calm behavior will help her polish her manners. She may also jump up or use her mouth when she's excited, but positive reinforcement and consistency will help her learn more appropriate ways to say hello. She's a curious girl with plenty of personality, and while she may like doing things her own way, she's ready to find someone who will happily be by her side for the adventure!
