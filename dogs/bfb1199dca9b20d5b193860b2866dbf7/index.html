Meet Lightning McQueen!
Lightning McQueen definitely lives up to his name! This strong, energetic boy loves being on the move, whether he's exploring the city, sniffing every bit of interesting pee-mail, or showing off his tricks. He's also VERY affectionate and will happily climb into your lap for some quality cuddle time.

My Ideal Home
Lightning would love an active home with someone who can keep his body and brain busy. He is incredibly treat motivated and already knows Sit, Lay Down, and Shake, so he's ready to keep learning with positive, reward-based training. He can get pretty excited around other dogs and will need a family who is ready to help him slow down, focus, and make good choices.

Good with Other Pets?
Lightning's history with other dogs is unknown since he came to us as a stray. During a recent introduction, he showed interest in the other dog but remained tense and highly focused. He'll need thoughtful, controlled introductions and continued training around his canine friends.

Good with Kids?
We recommend a home without young children under 5. His new family should be ready to help him practice calm greetings and reinforce behaviors like Sit and Off when he's feeling extra excited.

Things to Know
Lightning is a loose, wiggly, highly social boy who loves attention and happily seeks contact from his people. He can jump up when he's excited, but he's very responsive to treats and takes them gently, making positive reinforcement a great way to help him learn. With the right person, Lightning is ready to put the pedal to the metal on becoming his best self.
