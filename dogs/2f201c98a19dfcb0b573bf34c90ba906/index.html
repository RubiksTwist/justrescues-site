Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Bruce is a 1 year-old beagle-mix weighing around 25 lbs, who wants to say hi to everyone he passes on his walks, stopping for a quick sniff and a hello before happily moving along.

Bruce is also wonderfully quiet. He never barks which makes him a great fit for apartment living. He’s crate-trained and settles right in, and he does well on leash. He’s still adjusting to life in the city, so he’s been curious about all the other pups he sees on the street. He’s making steady progress on house training and is doing great learning the routine of heading outside.

Bruce would thrive with an adopter who’s ready to help him finish settling in. With some patience and consistency, and you’ll have a sweet, social, easygoing companion by your side.
