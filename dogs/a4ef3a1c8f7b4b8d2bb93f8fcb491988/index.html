Description provided by Waldo's Rescue Pen

Looking for a loving companion who is equal parts playful, brilliant, and sweet? Meet Younger! This 6-month-old sweetheart is an absolute dream pup who wants nothing more than to connect with her humans.

Younger is remarkably intelligent and emotionally tuned-in. She watches your facial expressions, listens intently when you talk to her, and immediately understands what you’re asking. Even when she’s excited, all you have to do is pause, and she’ll calmly settle herself down and wait patiently. Because she is so eager to please (and very food-motivated!), she picks up new commands with ease.

When she’s not out exploring the world with her curious nose, Younger is a total velcro dog and cuddle bug. The moment you sit on the couch or the floor, she’ll happily climb right into your lap! She gets along with other dogs, cats, and kids, and rarely barks. While city noises and sudden movements used to startle her a bit, she’s been adjusting quickly and gaining confidence every single day.
