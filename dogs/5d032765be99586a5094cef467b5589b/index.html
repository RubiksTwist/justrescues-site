Meet Yahtzee

Sometimes life takes an unexpected turn, and little Yahtzee knows that all too well. This handsome boy was surrendered to a hospital after being hit by a car. Through no fault of his own, his life took a difficult detour, but we hope that from here on out, his story will be filled with nothing but love and happy beginnings.

Just look at that face! Those beautiful eyes, adorable ears, and sweet expression are enough to make anyone fall in love. Yahtzee deserves a family who will see past his difficult start and recognize the wonderful little soul waiting to be loved.

His journey may have started with an accident, but it doesn't have to define his future. He deserves a safe home, a cozy bed, someone to share life's little adventures with, and a family who will never give up on him.

Let's make sure Yahtzee's next big roll is a winning one: a forever home and a lifetime of love!
