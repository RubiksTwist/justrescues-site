🩶 Meet Nora! 🩶

Beautiful Nora is a 5-year-old grey tabby girl with the sweetest soul. She came to us as an owner surrender, and understandably, the transition into rescue life was a little overwhelming for her.

During her first few weeks at our adoption center, Nora seemed confused and shut down. We knew she needed a quieter, cozier environment where she could decompress and feel comfortable again, so we arranged for her to move into a foster home.

And what a difference a little comfort and security have made! Nora is now thriving, showing everyone just how incredibly sweet she truly is. Sometimes all a cat needs is the chance to feel safe again.

But as wonderful as foster life has been, it’s only temporary. Nora wants what every cat deserves: a home of her very own, someone to love, and a place where she never has to wonder where she belongs again.

Nora has already had to start over once. Let’s make sure her next home is her forever one. 🐾
