Midnight Sun is on the at-risk list due to behavior concerns. In the care center, Midnight Sun has shown significant fearful behavior, including trembling, a tucked tail, walking low to the ground, balking, freezing, and refusing to walk through doorways, hallways, and elevators. She frequently requires coaxing or being carried due to her fearfulness. Midnight Sun, with staff, becomes loose and wiggly, seeks attention from handlers, accepts petting, and loves to be outside with staff. Medically, Midnight Sun is apparently healthy.

Midnight Sun was surrendered to the care center as a stray and we have no history on her behavior in a home environment. Midnight Sun has not acclimated well to the care center environment, and has been highly fearful when handlers have attempted to walk her, trembling, baulking on leash, and flattening herself to the ground, having to be carried through the care center. In calmer settings with known handlers she has shown social behavior, soliciting petting with a loose body, but due to her high levels of fear and stress around walking we recommend placement with a New Hope partner who can allow for decompression, provide any necessary behavior modification (force-free, positive reinforcement-based), and re-evaluate behavior in a stable home environment before placement into a permanent home.
