Description provided by PupStarz Rescue

Meet Building Toys! A 9-week-old, 9-pound Hound/Beagle mix with a whole lot of puppy love packed into one adorable little package!

Building Toys is sweet, playful, curious, and always ready for his next adventure. He loves his toys, treats, exploring his surroundings, and snuggling up with his favorite humans. He's also a big fan of soaking up the sunshine and catching a good nap whenever the opportunity presents itself!

This little guy is already taking city life in stride! He's been doing great on the subway (who knew he was such a good commuter?!), where he's often quiet and happy to snooze along the way.

Like any puppy, Building Toys is still figuring out the world. He's teething, discovering what is and isn't a chew toy, and learning his puppy manners. He's not house-trained or crate-trained just yet, so his new family should be ready for all the fun, patience, and occasional puppy mischief that comes with raising a little one!

Building Toys has been around adults and is friendly with both men and women. He's reported to do well with other dogs, while his experience with cats and children is unknown.

Whether you're looking for a walking buddy, a playmate, or someone to keep your couch warm, Building Toys is happy to apply for all three positions!

He's got a lot of growing, learning, and loving ahead of him. Could Building Toys be the little sidekick you've been waiting for?

If you would like to adopt this Rockstar, please visit www.pupstarzrescue.org for an application!
