Meet Madame Jenny!

Madame Jenny, also known as MJ, is a long stay pup with a reduced adoption fee and a whole lot of love to give. She has been in foster care for a few years now, and during that time her foster has watched her grow into a confident, social, and deeply affectionate senior dog who is truly ready for her forever home.

Personality

MJ is playful, smart, and full of charm. She enjoys engaging with people and has a joyful, affectionate nature that makes her very easy to fall in love with. She can be a little cautious with new people at first, but it does not take her long to warm up and show her loving side. Once she feels safe, she is social, engaging, and very people oriented.

Home Life

MJ is an active senior who still enjoys play and daily walks. She loves games like tug of war and enjoys going out for walks where she can trot happily alongside her person. She is also very affectionate and enjoys quiet time close to her people.

She is intelligent and enjoys learning, especially when training is paired with patience and positive reinforcement. She continues to benefit from structure and consistency in her routine.

Social Behavior

According to her foster, MJ can do well with other dogs and even cats when introduced slowly and at her own pace.

She may be a bit reserved when meeting strangers, but she warms up and becomes affectionate once she feels safe. She enjoys connection with people and thrives in environments where she feels secure and understood.

My Ideal Home

MJ will do best in an adult home where she can be the center of attention and enjoy a calm, stable environment. She would thrive with someone who appreciates a loving companion who enjoys both playtime and quiet bonding moments.

She needs continued guidance around resource guarding, as she does not always like to share food, toys, or bedding. A patient, experienced adopter who can provide structure and positive reinforcement will help her continue to succeed.

Fun Fact

MJ's foster says one of her most special traits is her intuition. She always seems to know when her person needs comfort, whether they are feeling down or just in need of a little extra affection. She is the kind of dog who naturally shows up at exactly the right moment, offering love, presence, and a calming energy that makes her truly unforgettable.
