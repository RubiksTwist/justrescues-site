Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Did Ozzy Osbourne write tge “I'm just a dreamer, who dreams of better days” lyrics specifically for our sweet boy Ozzy? It's likely!

3 year old Ozzy, is a muscular, compact little 50 lb tank who embodies love and joy. Not only does Ozzy'a big begging eyes, bowling ball shaped head and goofy personality make people fall in love with him instantly, but his joy for life is absolutely contagious.

When Ozzy isn't being everything happy in the world, you'll be hard pressed not to find him curled up into a tiny ball on any soft surface - preferably your lap but if your lap is unavailable he's happy to hang out in his crate solo.

Some of Ozzy’s quriks include running up to your feet and immediately flopping down, belly up, for a serious belly rub session, and offering his paw if you dare to stop and asking his people for a hug before smushing his snoot into their face. The way Ozzy treats his people makes it impossible not to feel loved by him.

In addition to an awesome personality, Ozzy is a wonderful house guest! Ozzy is fully crate trained throughout the day and overnight, waiting patiently for you to wake up and start your day. Ozzy is fully housetrained outside, does great with 3 daily walks and his learning the cue "go pee pee". Ozzy is a wonderful equal medium between chill and playful making him super adaptable to many households regardless of if some days are spent curled up watching movies or playing fetch and tug and taking leisurely strolls. Ozzy is also dog friendly and has been so sweet and gentle with his furry foster sister and takes cues from other dogs beautifully - understanding when they need space or no longer want to play. Ozzy will also never miss an opportunity to cuddle with a furry friend for a nap. Lastly, Ozzy is so smart! Ozzy has mastered his "leave it" and "Sit" commands and even knows to drop a toy by trading with an "out" cue. Ozzy is currently working diligently on his "Stay" and "down" commands as well.

If you are looking for an easy going, fun, affectionate, low maintenance turn key ready pup - look no further, Ozzy is definitely your guy!
