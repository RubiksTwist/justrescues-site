Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

“I'm just a dreamer, who dreams of better days” - Ozzy Osbourne, written for our adoptable Ozzy (probably). Ozzy dreams of his best days that are yet to come with his forever family!

50lb, 3 year old Ozzy is best described as the cross between a piglet and a baby seal. With a big bowling ball head, snorts that would make his namesake proud, and the most charming personality you could ever meet, Ozzy is the definition of perfection!

If there is one thing this world Ozzy loves, it's tennis balls. Chasing them, catching them, squeaking them... just seeing one makes his eyes light up. If there is one thing Ozzy loves more than tennis balls, it's his people. Ozzy adores bonding with people and will happily spend most of his day cuddling your lap or snoozing nearby. He isn't demanding for constant attention, he just loves to be around his people!

Ozzy has a few signature moves that garnered him love and adoration at the shelter that have carried over into foster care! He loves to run up to you and quickly flop down, belly up (on your feet) for pets! He will very gently paw for more if you try end the rub down too soon. He also LOVES to give hugs and will smush his snoot directly into your face or neck to maximize the snuggles.

Some of Ozzy’s greatest hits:

  • Crate trained! He loves to sleep in it overnight (even knows the cue "crate" to go to bed!) and more than happily chills in their during the work day; snoring and belly up!
  • House trained! Ozzy knows to do all his business outside. Give him 4 walks a day and he will be very happy!
  • Equal parts calm and playful! Ozzy is good for both a movie marathon weekend on the couch and accompanying you on an adventure or long walk!
  • Snoring and snorting! Yes, this is a greatest hit - perhaps Ozzy’s best quality is his non-stop piggy noises. His snoring can be so consistent and calming you may find yourself tossing your white machine and letting the soothing sounds of Ozzy's breathing rock you to sleep.
  • Dog friendly! Ozzy absolutely adores his foster sister and spends all day following her, playing with her, or cuddling her. He can get excited when he sees dogs on walks as well, so his foster works on paying more attention to her than them outside, and he does great! Who can blame him for wanting to be friends with everyone, anyway!
  • Car companion! Ozzy absolutely LOVES a car ride and snores the whole time!

Ozzy is young and playful and therefore is learning a few house manners, mostly just around mouthiness. When Ozzy gets excited, he can get mouthy. His foster redirects with bones, chews, or other toys and she is so proud of his progress!

Ozzy came into the shelter system with a torn CCL in his back leg, and is currently recovering from his TPLO surgery in his foster home. While he is on bedrest now, Ozzy will be back to his bouncy, playful, goofy self in no time - ready to charm his family the same we he has made all of us fall in love him!finitely your guy!
