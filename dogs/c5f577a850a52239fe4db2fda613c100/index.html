DETAILED PROFILE

Lamine Yamal is a smart, alert, entertaining young dog with a lot to say. When he wants something, he makes a funny little sound that's remarkably close to talking, and he'll keep the conversation going until you catch on. He's cautious for about a second with new people, then tail-wagging and curious.

He's still settling in, but his personality is coming through fast. Walks are his favorite thing, and a good scratch is a close second; toys don't do much for him. His energy is moderate and climbing, and he bonds closely with his people. He's found a good routine in foster and speaks up when it's time to go out.

He's friendly with other dogs, though bigger dogs' rowdier play can intimidate him. He's lived with kids. He needs a home without cats.

He's crate trained and house trained. Leash manners are his current project; he's an enthusiastic puller, so an adopter who enjoys training and long walks will make a great teammate.

Medium-sized, quick-witted, and full of charm, he has breakout-star potential written all over him.

MEDICAL HISTORY
