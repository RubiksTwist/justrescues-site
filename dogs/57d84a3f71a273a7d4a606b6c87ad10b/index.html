Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

Minnie is a sweet, smart, and endlessly curious pup who is looking for her forever family. She settled into foster life so quickly you’d think she’d always lived here.

She’s a quick learner, too. In just a week, she picked up crate training, sit, stay, paw, and down. She’s very food motivated, which makes teaching her new tricks a breeze, and she’ll thrive with an adopter who enjoys training and keeping her mind busy.

Minnie is playful and loves a good game, but she’s also a pro at napping throughout the day, so she knows how to balance fun and rest. When it’s time to wind down, she’s a total cuddle bug who will happily curl up beside you and watch TV, so she’s always up for movie night. Housebreaking is going well, and she’s doing great with her crate.

Like any puppy, Minnie is in a big chewing phase. She’s a fan of stuffing-free toys, so stock up on durable chew options and she’ll be happy.

Her ideal home: an active family who can get out and play with her, ideally someone who works from home or has a hybrid schedule so she can get the time and attention a growing puppy needs.

If you’re looking for a loving, trainable companion who will keep you smiling (and keep your lap warm), Minnie is ready to meet you!
