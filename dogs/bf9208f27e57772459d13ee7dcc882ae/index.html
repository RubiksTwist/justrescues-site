Description provided by NYC Second Chance Rescue

Meet Marco, a one-of-a-kind cat with a BIG personality and an endless love for play. If you’re looking for a cat who will make you laugh, keep you entertained, and become your little sidekick, Marco is your guy.

Marco is deaf in both ears and has a mild case of cerebellar hypoplasia (CH), but neither slows him down. He experiences the world through movement and vision and is quite possibly one of the most playful cats you’ll ever meet. Pull out a wand and he’s immediately ready to go. Toss him a spring and he’ll chase it across the room, kick off his zoomies, and come right back for more.

Underneath all that energy is an incredibly loving and affectionate cat. Marco loves being around his people. If you’ve been apart, he’ll come looking for you just to curl up against you and purr. He’ll keep you company while you work at your desk and even join you while you brush your teeth so he can play with the running sink water.

Marco is curious, goofy, energetic, affectionate, and always wants to be part of what you’re doing. You’ll never be bored with Marco around. He’s the kind of cat whose presence fills a home.

Because Marco is deaf and can miss the vocal cues other cats use to set boundaries, his excitement can sometimes be overwhelming for another cat. He would thrive best as the only pet in his home, where he can soak up all the playtime, attention, and companionship he loves.
If you’re looking for a playful, affectionate companion with a personality you won’t forget, Marco is truly something special ❤️
