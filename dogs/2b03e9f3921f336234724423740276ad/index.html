Meet Noise Cancelling Headphones! This handsome 10-year-old, 60-pound gentleman is a wonderful dog with so much love to give! Theo is a sweet, easygoing guy who gets along well with both dogs and cats, making him a great addition to a home with other furry friends.

Theo is also housetrained and has great manners! Don't let his age fool you—this boy still has plenty of life and adventure ahead of him! He would absolutely love a home with a yard where he can stretch his legs, explore, and enjoy the fresh air. He would also make a fantastic companion for someone looking for a walking or running buddy!

At 10 years young, Theo is ready to find a forever family who will give him all the love, attention, and adventures he deserves. Whether it's spending time outdoors or relaxing at home with his favorite people, Theo would love nothing more than to be part of a family.

If you're looking for a loyal, wonderful companion, Theo might just be your perfect match!
