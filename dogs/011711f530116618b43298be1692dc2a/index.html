Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Nelson is an 8-year-old Black Lab mix with a heart full of love and so much life left to share! Originally from the beautiful Bahamas, Nelson was adopted as a puppy and spent many years in a home. Sadly, through no fault of his own, he recently found himself back at the shelter when his owner could no longer care for him.

Now, Nelson is hoping for a second chance at a forever home. At 8 years young, he has so much companionship, affection, and happiness to offer his next chapter of life. Nelson loves the beach, tug of war, and carrots (he might just be part bunny)!

Nelson's story deserves a happy ending. He's already known what it means to be loved, and we're hoping someone special will open their heart and home to him once again.
