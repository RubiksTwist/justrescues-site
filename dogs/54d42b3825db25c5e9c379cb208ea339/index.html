Daisy is a sweet, friendly, and affectionate three-legged dog who is full of loyalty and love for her human friends. She is an incredibly smart, dignified, and gentle dog who loves nothing more than to be included in all family activities. She is quiet, brave, and independent, but also loves to be a couch potato and receive kisses. Daisy has had a challenging road that caused her to lose one of her legs before joining our rescue, and is now looking for a forever home where she can spend her days as a loved companion. She is an incredible athlete and, being a tripawd, she has extra strong and healthy legs! Unfortunately, Daisy is not good with other dogs, so she is looking for a forever home where she will be the only four-legged friend. Does Daisy sound like the perfect dog for you?
