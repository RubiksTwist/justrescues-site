Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Minion has had a rough journey, but she’s finally getting her chance to find the right home. She was originally brought to ARF after being found trapped in a garage with no food or water. After being adopted and spending almost a year in a loving home, she sadly found that the busy environment was too overwhelming for her, and she has now returned to ARF in search of a more peaceful forever home.
Minion would thrive in a calm, quiet home where she can truly relax and feel safe. She will do best as an only pet, with someone patient and understanding who is willing to let her come out of her shell at her own pace.
With time, consistency, and a gentle hand, Minion has so much love to give—she just needs the right person to help her feel secure enough to share it.
