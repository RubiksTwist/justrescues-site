Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Have you ever met a puppy who seems to run on his own unlimited battery pack? That's Carter!

This Terrier mix puppy was rescued from Georgia, and we're pretty sure he brought enough energy with him to power the entire state. Carter is an energizer bunny with four legs, a wagging tail, and absolutely no plans to slow down anytime soon!

His daily schedule? Run, play, investigate, bounce, repeat. And when he's not busy keeping himself entertained, he's busy making sure everyone around him gets some love too. Because underneath all that bouncing, wiggling, nonstop puppy energy is a very affectionate little guy who loves all the attention he can get.

Carter is not looking for a quiet life of afternoon naps and early bedtimes (well, maybe he'll nap eventually!). He's looking for a family who enjoys a little silliness, has room for puppy adventures, and doesn't mind having a furry sidekick who thinks every day is the best day EVER.
