Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

Zuessey has been a great foster dog so far, he was able to acclimate to his new home immediately. He is a very friendly dog when meeting new people and dogs. On the leash he will sometime tug but is easily redirectable, loves to chew on things so it is important to be monitoring him when going on walks to see what he puts in his mouth. Other than that, he requires a lot of love and attention as he will follow you everywhere and want to cuddle and sleep with you while on the couch or on the bed.

﻿Zuessey is approximately 7y 2m 27d old and is fully grown at 74 pounds. They are microchipped and up to date on all age-appropriate medical care. Their breed is unknown as we have not had a DNA test done.
