Meet Latte!

Latte is a 5-month-old kitten with a playful spirit, endless curiosity, and just the right amount of sweetness. Since arriving at Waggytail Rescue, he's grown into a confident little explorer who finds joy in every new toy, corner, and adventure.

Like most kittens his age, Latte loves chasing balls, batting at feather wands, play-fighting with his friends, and racing through the house during his daily zoomies. He's always ready for fun and can turn almost anything into a game.

While Latte enjoys affection, he likes to ask for it on his own terms. He'll happily wander over for head scratches and pets before heading off to investigate his next exciting discovery. His balance of independence and affection makes him an easy, fun companion to have around.

Latte has a gentle nature and gets along wonderfully with other cats. If you're looking for a playful kitten who will make you smile every day and stop by for cuddles when the mood strikes, Latte may be the perfect addition to your family. He shares a wonderful bond with his best friend Mocha, and the two would love to find a forever home together.

Latte is up to date on shots, dewormed, and microchipped.

Latte will need to be adopted into a home with another cat or a cat-friendly dog. If you are looking for a pair of Waggytail kittens - note this in your application!

Please apply using the following instructions if you are interested in adopting one of our Waggytails: Visit our website (www.waggytailrescue.org) for an adoption application. It is important to fill out an application as soon as possible for consideration, and we require a complete application for you to meet an animal. We are currently unable to respond to general email inquiries as an all-volunteer run rescue. If the dog is on our website, they are still available.

Waggytail Rescue CANNOT guarantee the exact age of any adoptable cat, or final size of any of our adoptable kittens. We rely on the information provided to us by veterinarians, shelters, owner surrenders and other circumstances bringing the cat into our rescue.
