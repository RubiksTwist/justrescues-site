My Boroughbreaker writes: Unlike math, everyone loves our pup Geometry. He is the most affectionate dog I have ever met. He loves sitting on your lap or on you in whatever way he can. He is happiest when with his human. He is a playful pup, but prefers your company more than anything. He is an angel on leash, not pulling too hard or reacting when another dog passes by. He is treat motivated and takes it gently from your hands, though he is picky about what treats he likes and doesn't. He was on his best behavior in starbucks, waiting for his pup cup. He can get mildly anxious about loud noises, but who isn't? Any issues he had in the kennel were non-existent on his borough break.
The only way to get an A+ is by taking your Geometry home for snuggles a lifetime of snuggles and cuddles.
