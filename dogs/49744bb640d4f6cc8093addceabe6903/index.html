My Boroughbreaker writes: Unlike math, everyone loves our pup Geometry. He is the most affectionate dog I have ever met. He loves sitting on your lap or on you in whatever way he can. He can get mildly anxious about loud noises, but who isn't? Any issues he had in the kennel were non-existent on his borough break.
The only way to get an A+ is by taking your Geometry home for snuggles a lifetime of snuggles and cuddles.
