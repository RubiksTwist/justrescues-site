Looking for a new man? Bart is your guy. He is the most snuggly, loyal, playful love bug you’ll ever meet. He is always down to relax and watch TV but don’t let his chill-ness fool you - he is also a master at fetch. He may rip up and attack what you have him fetch but he’ll bring it right back! He is smart and learns quick however he does not like to be left alone and will find ways with his smartness to keep you home such as escaping his play pen or eating the bottom of his crate. If left in the open he fares okay just put anything away you don’t want attacked.

﻿Bartholomew is approximately 3y 4m 14d old and is fully grown at 30 pounds. They are microchipped and up to date on all age-appropriate medical care. Their breed is unknown as we have not had a DNA test done.
