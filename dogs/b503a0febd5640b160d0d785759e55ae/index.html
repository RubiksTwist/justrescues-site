Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Aris is a gentle soul who is learning what it means to finally be safe.

Rescued from the Chinese meat trade by Hope4Fur, with support from the Andrew Sabin Family Foundation, Aris has been through more than any dog should ever have to experience. Now as he adjusts to his new surroundings, he is understandably very shy and quiet. He can be nervous and sometimes withdrawn, often keeping to himself as he takes in the world around him.

But behind that understandably timid exterior is a sweet dog with so much love waiting to come out. He is looking for a patient, understanding family who will let him blossom at his own pace and show him just how wonderful life can be.

Aris has already survived so much. Now he deserves the chance to simply be a dog - and to finally be loved.
