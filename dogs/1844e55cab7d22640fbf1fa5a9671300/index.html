Description provided by ASPCA Adoption Center

Meet Boggle, the treat-loving charmer who is blossoming every day!

Boggle is a 2-year-old brown tabby boy with a big heart and an even bigger love for yummy treats! Food is his favorite way to make friends, and it helps him feel confident as he gets to know new people and explore his surroundings. He is a quick learner who is becoming more vocal and adventurous, and he loves to explore.

Boggle is also the first to jump in on playtime! He loves a game of wand play, whether he is perched on his cat tower or racing down to see what is happening. He enjoys the company of other cats, relaxing nearby and keeping an eye on their adventures, and he likes to lounge near his people after play. Are you ready to experience the joy of having a cat in your life with Boggle? To learn more about Boggle, click the “Adopt me” button.

*You can meet animals located at the ASPCA Adoption Center during our walk-in hours (daily, noon to 6 p.m.). We encourage a $150 suggested adoption fee when adopting an animal. This adoption fee allows the ASPCA to continue our lifesaving work and help even more animals in need. We are grateful for any amount given at the time of adoption.*
