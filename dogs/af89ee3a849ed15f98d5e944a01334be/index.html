Description provided by AMA Animal Rescue

Meet Bambi, a sweet and loving girl in need of a fresh start after a rough beginning.

At just 4 months old, Bambi was found abandoned at a precinct, visibly malnourished and scared. We knew we couldn't leave this desperate puppy, so we proudly took on her case and provided her with the necessary care.

Despite everything she’s been through, Bambi is full of love and is ready to find her forever home. With her big ears and gorgeous coat, she’s got a look that’s hard to forget. She is beautiful inside and out!

Bambi is looking for a patient and caring family who can help her feel at home. Once she settles in, she’s the best loyal and affectionate companion.

Bambi is playful and energetic. She enjoys playtime with other sweet and friendly dogs.

If you’re ready to give a sweet, deserving dog the chance she deserves, Bambi might be the one for you. She’s hoping to find a family that will help her thrive and give her the love and attention she deserves. If that could be you, please fill out and submit an adoption application today!
