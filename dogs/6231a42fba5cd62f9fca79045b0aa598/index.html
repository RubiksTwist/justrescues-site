Description provided by NYC Second Chance Rescue

Zigrietta is a beautiful girl with a heart as big as she is. She came into rescue after a difficult start in Georgia sustaining injuries that required medical attention and a period of recovery. Through it all, this sweet girl has shown just how resilient she is.

Zigrietta is a special girl who deserves someone willing to look past what she has been through and see the wonderful dog underneath. She’s ready to put her difficult days behind her and start the next chapter of her life surrounded by love, patience, and plenty of TLC.

At just two years old, Zigrietta has so much life ahead of her, and we can’t wait to see her find the family she deserves.

As always, an adoption bio is just a snapshot of who an animal is. For more information about Zigrietta, including her personality, needs, and what type of home may be the best fit, please reach out to our Adoptions Team.
