Meet Mozz!

Mozz came to ACC after her owner passed away, and while her past is unknown, she's shown herself to be a social, playful, and people-oriented dog who enjoys attention and treats. She's a happy greeter who leans into interactions and is eager to engage with handlers.

My Ideal Home

Mozz would do well in an active, structured home that can continue teaching her polite manners and impulse control in a positive, consistent way. She thrives with engagement, training, and clear boundaries, especially around excitement and food rewards.

Good with Other Pets?

Mozz ignores other dogs in leash observations. Slow and careful introductions with resident pets are always recommended.

Good with Kids?

Due to her mouthiness and high arousal when taking treats, Mozz is recommended for a home without young children under 5. She may be open to older, respectful children after a meet.

Things to Know

Mozz is an affectionate, playful dog who gets very excited during interaction and can jump up frequently when seeking attention or treats. She takes treats with a very hard mouth at times, so continued positive reinforcement training around impulse control and gentle mouth behavior is important. She is otherwise tolerant of handling, enjoys engagement, and walks nicely on leash with only mild pulling. With consistency and structure, Mozz has strong potential to channel her enthusiasm into great manners and reliable behavior.

Fun Fact

Mozz is the kind of dog who always shows up with enthusiasm. She just needs a little guidance to make sure all that excitement turns into good manners.
