Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

Mocha's the sweetest country girl who just wants to cuddle with you all day long. Though she's originally from the South, she's adapting to NYC at lightning speed and we're sure she'll soon be a bona fide city girl :)

She is already crate trained and is sleeping through the night, but may howl or whine when she's initially put in. As for walks, she pulls a bit as she's still acclimating to the sounds of the city and tends to pull harder when she's startled or generally feeling anxious. However, she's adjusting very quickly and even handled her first subway ride like a total pro. She loves to lounge around, whether that's on the couch or in her crate, and especially loves to lounge with another person (invading your personal space). She does great at bath time despite being a bit nervous about it. She is incredibly well-behaved and doesn't even jump on counters or show slight interest in chewing up anything in the apartment (besides her squeaky toy).

Mocha is guaranteed to be a great, affectionate companion and is looking for someone to shower her with love, treats, and cuddles.

﻿Mocha is approximately 2y 0m 5d old and is fully grown at 34 pounds. They are microchipped and up to date on all age-appropriate medical care. Their breed is unknown as we have not had a DNA test done.
