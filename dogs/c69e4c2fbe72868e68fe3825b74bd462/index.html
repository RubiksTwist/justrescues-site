Description provided by Rescue City

This Cane Corso is still very much a puppy (11-12 months old) just in a very big body. She’s been working on impulse control, boundaries, settling in the home, handling, and learning that she can notice everything happening around her without feeling like she has to react to it. And she is doing SO well. In a home experienced with her breed she has even both best friends with a baby, she’s great with most people after a proper meet with some good treats, and quickly learns and adjusts in newer environments. Cora would be an amazing dog for someone who loves and understands guardian breeds and is excited about continuing the work that has already been started. Her next foster or foster-to-adopt home will continue working with Georgia, so you will have professional training support and guidance as Cora settles in and keeps building these skills. If you have experience with large or guardian breeds and can give Cora the consistency and structure she thrives with by opening your home to foster or foster to adopt, please email foster@rescuecity.nyc.
