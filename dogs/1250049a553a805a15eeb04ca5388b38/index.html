Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

With tiny bear ears and the cutest cinnamon roll tail you’ve ever seen, Bon Bon is a little bear in dog form. This 1 year old shiba and shar-pei mix has been impressively brave in his first days exploring NYC, taking in the whole city through his nose.

Inside, Bon Bon is calm and mellow. His favorite pastime is sunbathing and napping in a spot where he can keep an eye on his people. He’s house-trained and even lets you know when he needs to head out, and he sleeps soundly in his crate for a solid 8-9 hours a night. Out on walks, he’s a dedicated sniffer who loves to stop and investigate every tree, lamppost, and fence along the way. Once he’s had his fill of sniffing, he settles into a nice walking rhythm.

Bon Bon is a thoughtful guy who likes to get to know people at his own pace. Once he’s had a chance to sniff and say hello, he’s happy to accept some attention. He’d do best as the only pup in the home, with an adopter who has some dog experience and is excited to keep working on his leash skills, especially around other dogs and the occasional squirrel. For the right person, he’ll be a loyal, laid-back companion.
