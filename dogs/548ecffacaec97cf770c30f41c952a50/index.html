Hi! I'm Boogie, and I'm so excited to meet you! I just moved to NYC and can't wait to explore the city. People tell me I'm adorable, and I'm happy to prove them right. I might also be the friendliest dog you'll ever meet: within seconds of saying hello, I'll probably try to give you a hug or ask for a belly rub.

I love walking around the city and sniffing out new places, but I'm just as happy hanging out while you cook or work. I'm still getting used to the big-city noises and sights, like trucks, busy intersections, and subway grates, so I'm not the best listener outside yet. With a little time and patience, I know I'll settle in.

A few of my favorite things: peanut butter (especially in my Kong or on a lick mat), venison treats, and bully sticks. I get along with other dogs and kids, so I'd be a great fit for a family, but I'd also make a wonderful companion for someone living solo.
