Description provided by Waldo's Rescue Pen

Lenore (or Lennie as we call her) is very sweet, energetic, confident, and loves to be with people. She has been largely un-phased by her move from Georgia to NYC and isn’t bothered by all the new sights and sounds, only showing excitement and curiosity.

She loves to go exploring on long walks, playing with her foster dog sibiling, and snuggling with us on the couch. She really has the sweetest personality and is a little love bug. We've really enjoyed fostering her and know she would be an excellent companion to any family.

Personality & Characteristics:

My foster dog is good with: dogs and kids, unobserved with cats

My foster dog is: mostly crate trained and house trained, low shed, good with being picked up, no signs of guarding

3 Keywords for your pup: cuddly, confident, & curious

Energy Level (1= calm, 10= high energy): 6 - enjoys a good walk but can also sleep all day

Barker? Or Quiet Pup?: Mostly quiet, only barks slightly when wanting to play

Walking Manners: Not bad but likes to stop and sniff often

Appetite: Eats twice a day, normal appetite

Favorite Things: Chewing on toys, going on walks, food motivated

Commands/Tricks: We've been working on the “Sit” command but she gets too excited and squirms around. She eventually gets it.
