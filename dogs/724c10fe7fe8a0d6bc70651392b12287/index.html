Slider Is Ready to Slide into Your Life!

One-year-old Slider is a happy, social girl who is always excited to get out of her kennel and spend time with people. She loves attention and enjoys being petted, and she still has the wiggly and bouncy body movements of a puppy! She also enjoys playing with toys and was recently spotted having a great time tugging with a toy, bouncing after it and proudly carrying it around afterward.

Slider is a smart girl who is eager to learn, especially when tasty treats are involved. She walks nicely on a leash, and treats help her stay focused. She is easily redirected and has been described as “no concerns” on walks! This intelligent pup is a prime candidate for learning some tricks! Enrichment and opportunities to get out and interact with people are especially helpful for her.

What a great companion Slider will be! To learn more about Slider, click the “Adopt me” button.
