From September 18th-30th, part of my adoption fee is sponsored! Thanks to the Bissell Pet Foundation's Empty the Shelters campaign, my new family can take me home for $70!

Meet Brand! Brand is a 2 year old, 85 pound mixed breed.

Brand is a bundle of happy energy who’s always ready to make a new friend. This playful boy loves meeting people and brings a joyful enthusiasm wherever he goes. Whether it’s playtime in the yard or simply hanging out with his people, Brand is happiest when he’s part of the action.

He does great with other dogs of all sizes and genders and enjoys having canine companions to play with. Cats, on the other hand, make him a bit nervous, so a cat-free home would likely be the best fit for him.

Brand is both crate trained and housebroken. Like many dogs, he may occasionally have an accident during bad weather when he’s less excited about going outside, but overall he has great house manners. Brand does have a visual impairment and can only see about 4 feet in front of him, but it does not slow him down at all, especially when he has a canine buddy to follow around!

Affectionate, social, and full of playful spirit, Brand would thrive with a family that’s ready to keep him active, engaged, and included in daily life. If you’re looking for a fun-loving companion who will greet you with excitement and keep you smiling, Brand might just be your perfect match.

Brand is currently being fostered in Ewing, NJ. His adoption fee is $500.

All of our cats come spayed or neutered, up to date on vaccines, dewormed, microchipped, up to date on flea and tick preventative, and treated for any ailments found upon veterinary examination. All adults are combo tested; for some litters of kittens who have not been exposed to other cats, only one kitten in the litter may be tested. The adoption fee covers a portion of these services and helps us continue rescuing animals.

For more information on the adoption fees and the adoption process, please complete our adoption application here: https://www.louieslegacy.org/adopt - an application allows us to get to know your family better so we can assess whether the animal you are interested in is a good match for your home. An application does not obligate you to adopt. Please understand that due to the volume of applications some animals receive, we are unable to contact all applicants. If your application is considered a good fit for the pet you are interested in, or even a different pet, a volunteer will give you a call as soon as possible.

We are a foster based rescue and do not have a shelter location where animals can be seen during the week. Please see our Facebook page at https://www.facebook.com/LouiesLegacy for our weekly adoption album if you are interested in meeting an animal in person. Not all animals attend every adoption event, please check our Facebook page for animal event times and locations before you make a trip to meet a specific animal.
