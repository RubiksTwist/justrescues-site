Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Toby is a young Belgian Malinois who came to us from animal control and is looking for a very special person to call his own!

Like many Malinois, Toby is an intelligent, driven young dog who needs structure, consistency, and a job to do. He is still learning how to navigate the world around him and can be sensitive to unfamiliar people and certain environmental triggers, including passing vehicles. He will need an experienced Malinois owner who understands the breed and is prepared to provide the patience, guidance, structure, and continued training he needs to succeed.

Toby has so much potential, but he isn't looking for just any home. He's looking for someone who appreciates what makes this breed so special and is ready to put in the time and commitment to help him thrive.

If you have Malinois experience, a passion for training, and room in your life for a dog who needs a little extra guidance, Toby might just be your guy!
