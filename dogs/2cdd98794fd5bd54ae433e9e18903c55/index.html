Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Toby is a young Belgian Malinois who came to us from animal control and is ready to find his perfect match! Like many dogs of his breed, Toby is incredibly intelligent, energetic, and eager to have a purpose. He's a young dog with lots to learn and will thrive with someone who enjoys working with their dog and making training part of everyday life.

Toby is still building confidence as he experiences the world around him. He can be a little unsure in new situations, so he'll benefit from a patient, experienced owner who can help him navigate the world and continue building his confidence.

An experienced Malinois home is a must for Toby. He'll need structure, consistency, plenty of mental and physical enrichment, and someone who understands the unique needs of this incredible breed. In return, you'll have a bright, driven young dog with so much potential and a whole lot to offer the right person.

Toby isn't looking for just any home; he's looking for someone who sees his potential, enjoys a good training project, and is excited to help him become the very best version of himself. Could that be you?
