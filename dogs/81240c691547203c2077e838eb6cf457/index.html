Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

My foster writes: Moona is a vocal guy who will not hesitate to raise his voice to communicate with you. He keeps chirping whenever he comes and goes or you go past by him. He is very affectionate and longs for your company - he usually lounges in your close proximity and will get up every hour or so to show his affection: snuggles and headbutts you then victoriously stands on your chest and starts kneading. Once done, he will curl up on you and purr. During the night, Moona is mostly chill as he prefers sleeping in your bed rather than wreaking havoc in your apartment. He loves his cat tower and during the day he mostly lounges there occasionally hopping off to use the scratchpad. And he WILL strut in front of your laptop at the worst possible video call moment - just like a cat should. Oh and he also likes to give you love bites.

My foster writes:
Moona has a very gentle and affectionate soul. Being close to you is everything for him: he is low energy and is less entertained by his toys and is more concerned with being in the same space with you. He will follow you everywhere, gently chirping along the way. He is very vocal and will not hesitate to let his thoughts, wants or needs be known to you with relentless and monotone meows. Instead of causing chaos in your apartment, he will choose to chill and sleep at your bedside and is up for cuddles (on his own terms of course).
