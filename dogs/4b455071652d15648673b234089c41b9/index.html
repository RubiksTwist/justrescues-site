Please note: Any profiles with a "Pawling, NY" icon are located at the ASPCA Recovery & Rehabilitation Center, which is a 90-minute drive outside of the city. All other dogs are located in New York City. Meet Romeo, who believes every good hello deserves a full-body wiggle! Romeo has an enthusiastic way of greeting his friends you might notice the wiggles before anything else! Once the greetings are complete, he’s ready to have some fun. Romeo enjoys playing fetch and working on his skills during training, especially when there’s something tasty involved. But Romeo isn't always on the move. Give him a favorite toy and he’s happy to settle down at your feet, enjoying his toy while keeping you company. It’s a quieter side of Romeo that our team loves just as much as those signature wiggles. From enthusiastic hellos to relaxing together at the end of the fun, Romeo has plenty of ways to make time together memorable. Click the “Adopt Me” button to connect with Romeo’s team in Pawling, New York! *We encourage a $150 suggested adoption fee when adopting an animal. This adoption fee allows the ASPCA to continue our lifesaving work and help even more animals in need. We are grateful for any amount given at the time of adoption.*
