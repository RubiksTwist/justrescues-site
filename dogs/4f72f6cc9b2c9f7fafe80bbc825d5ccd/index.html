Meet Ezra - who's ready to bring a bit of magic and lots of love into your home!

Ezra is an incredibly sweet and affectionate gentleman who has a loving personality and a way of making you feel like you’ve known him forever. He was left in a carrier in front of our shelter with nothing but a note, along with his friend Asher - which we only imagine how confusing and frightening that must have been for him.

But despite that he's kept his loveable personality - he has so much love to give and is searching for that forever home where he can feel safe and cherished. Will you be his special someone?
