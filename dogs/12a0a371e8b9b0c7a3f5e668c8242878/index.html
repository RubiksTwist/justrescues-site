Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Meet Miss Pretty! A little beauty with a big personality and an even bigger heart. 💕🐾

At just 1 year old and 36 pounds full grown, Miss Pretty is the perfect pint-sized package! This sweet girl recently joined Every Last One Rescue, and she's already making herself right at home. Despite being in a brand-new environment, she's been friendly with everyone she's met and is taking city life completely in stride. All the NYC hustle and bustle? No big deal for this girl!

Miss Pretty is a social butterfly who seems to think every dog she passes is a potential new best friend. She's dog-friendly, loves to play, and has the kind of puppy enthusiasm that makes every day a little more fun. We think she'd absolutely love opportunities to socialize and play, and doggy daycare could be right up her alley!

She's also quite the little lady around the house. Miss Pretty is wonderfully well-mannered, crate-trained, and loves cozying up in her dog bed. She's happy eating her meals in her crate and has already shown that she can settle in nicely. She doesn't jump on the couch or bed, and when she needs a little entertainment, she'll politely let her people know she's ready for something to do.

When it comes to walks, she's a dream! She doesn't pull on leash and even checks in with her walker along the way. She's been enjoying hour-long morning adventures in Prospect Park on a long lead, which give her the perfect opportunity to explore, burn off some puppy energy, and then spend the rest of the day relaxing.

And speaking of puppy energy... Miss Pretty definitely still has a playful, goofy side! She can get a little mouthy and enthusiastic when she's running around, sometimes bumping into her people in the excitement. It's all playful puppy behavior, and she's still learning her own strength and how to channel all that fun energy.

She's also already had a successful short stay home alone in her crate, where she was perfectly behaved. She's off to a wonderful start!

Miss Pretty really is the best of both worlds: a sweet, social, well-mannered girl with plenty of playful puppy spirit. She's looking for a family who will keep her active, give her opportunities to make doggy friends, and help her continue learning as she grows into the amazing companion she's meant to be.

If you're looking for a walking buddy, a built-in playdate, and a sweet little shadow to make you smile every day, Miss Pretty might just be your girl! ❤️
