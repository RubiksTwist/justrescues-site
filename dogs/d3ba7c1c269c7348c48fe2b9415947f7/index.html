Meet Marvel, the world's biggest lap dog!

This sweet boy originally came to ARF from Georgia last fall, was adopted as a puppy, but recently came back to the shelter to no fault of his own. Marvel is a big goofball who gladly soaks up every ounce of the attention he is sure to get on outtings. Still a puppy at heart, Marvel would love to play in the yard all day or go on hikes with his person before ultimately curling up on the couch (and you) for a relaxing evening. Come meet Marvel and fall marvelously in love!
