Sparky has been so lucky to spend this time in foster homes that have helped him grow into the dog he is today, after he was surrendered by a family who couldn’t commit to him. He’s calmer, more confident, and has made progress with the support of his foster family and trainer, Louis at @magneticdogtraining. He’ll do best as the only pet in a quieter suburban or rural home where he isn’t constantly surrounded by the things that make him feel overwhelmed, at this time he cannot live in a home with other dogs or cats. For the right person, though? He’s an absolute gem. A loyal best friend, professional blanket burrito, expert cuddler, and a dog that’s happy with the simple things. To help him find that perfect match: His adoption fee has been WAIVED. + His adopter will receive FREE lifetime training support through @magneticdogtraining. We’ve expanded our adoption radius because we’re focused on finding the right home, no matter where it is. He just needs someone who sees how special he is and is willing to meet him where he is.
