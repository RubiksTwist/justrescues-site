Meet Melon!

Melon was found running loose on the street and is now looking for a safe place to call home. She's a very gentle and fearful girl who is still figuring out that the world can be a kind place. She may be a little shy at first, but with patience, a quiet environment, and someone willing to let her take things at her own pace, we think this sweet girl can really come into her own.

My Ideal Home

Melon would do best with a patient adult who can give her plenty of time to decompress and feel safe. A calm home, gentle routines, and lots of positive experiences will help her build confidence and show her that she's finally in a place where she can relax. She's just waiting for someone to give her the chance to have a melon-choly beginning turn into a much sweeter ending.

Good with Other Pets?

Melon's history with other animals is unknown, and she was too fearful to complete her dog assessment. For now, we recommend giving her space to settle in before considering any introductions.

Good with Kids?

Melon would do best in an adult only home because she is currently very fearful. Her family should let her choose when she is ready to approach and never force her into petting or interactions that make her uncomfortable.

Things to Know

Melon walks with mild pulling and may need some coaxing when she's feeling unsure. She can be quite fearful with new people and surroundings, so her new family should give her plenty of time to decompress and always allow her the option to walk away. With patience, kindness, and a little time, we're hoping Melon will find her confidence and become one happy little melon!
