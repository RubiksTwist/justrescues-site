Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Judy is a 3-year-old mixed breed who weighs around 15 lbs and comes with two especially charming features: an adorable little snaggle tooth and a habit of giving actual hugs. She’ll put her front paws right on your shoulders and settle in like that’s exactly where she belongs.

She’s a serious cuddler, incredibly sweet, and the kind of loyal little companion who wants to be close to her person. Judy is house-trained, good on leash, cat-friendly, and has beautiful coloring to go with that very expressive face. She tends to feel comfortable with women right away and can be a little more cautious around men, but give her a couple of hours to get acquainted and she warms up nicely.

The crate hasn’t won her over just yet, but otherwise Judy has a lot of the important stuff figured out. She’s affectionate, portable, very cute, and fully prepared to hug her way into someone’s life.
