Description provided by Pawsome Squad

Cooper had a family that unfortunately was evicted and had to surrender him on Dec. 7, 2025 to the NYACC shelter due to the fact dogs are not allowed in a homeless shelter. We saved Cooper on January 30th, 2026 from the NYACC's euthanasia list.

Cooper was a scared pup when we first got him. But now has flourished!

A very social pup, Cooper also loves walks, play, eager to learn new things, and laying on the couch with you.

Please give this mushy fellow a chance to brighten up your home!
