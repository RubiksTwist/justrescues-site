Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

If you grew up loving The Fox and the Hound, the name Copper may already bring a little warmth to your heart - and this handsome brown puppy is ready to make the name his own.

With his rich copper-colored coat and sweet puppy face, Copper has the kind of charm that makes you want to follow him wherever his little paws take him. He’s at that wonderful stage where everything is worth investigating, every new smell is exciting, and every person might just be a potential best friend.

Like the beloved Copper whose name he shares, there’s something especially endearing about this little guy. But this Copper isn’t here to live in anyone else’s story - he’s ready to write his own. And after being rescued from Louisiana, thanks to our rescue partner BISSELL, his story is just beginning anew in the Hamptons.

Copper is currently receiving treatment for a manageable skin condition and is available for adoption while he recovers. His new family will need to continue his prescribed treatment, and our team is happy to walk them through everything they need to know!

Copper may share his name with a favorite Disney pup, but his next chapter is waiting to be written - and he’s hoping you’ll be part of it.
