Description provided by Best Friends Animal Society — NYC

Thor, a six-year-old male weighing a bit over 65 lbs., is currently in foster care and ready for adoption via our virtual process! Here’s what we know so far about his time time in foster, copy the link below into your browser: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1MUZWqXgOCElJ9il34Xn2V77uX8Q86fPA/view?usp=drive_link Thor is playful and well behaved. He is a great companion. He is house-trained. He is a very intelligent dog. He understands the word "toy" and will bring you one to play when you ask him to. His foster says "When Thor came in, he explored the house for a little bit, and then he got straight to what became his favorite spot on the couch and curled up there and put his head on our lap. Over time, he has discovered many more favorite places and is still just as curious whenever we take him around a new block on his walks. He is still just as affectionate and will follow you everywhere around the house. He loves to play and will bring his toys to you and he loves a toy that squeaks the most in the world. When playing with them, he will either take them to his corner and lick them, or he likes to play tug of war sometimes with some of them. Thor has been a gentleman! He is house trained and crate trained. He loves to go for walks and is happy to go slow and treat as a "sniffari" where he smells the roses along the way. He enjoys playing fetch too. He's still getting used to city noises and will sometimes bark. He is happy to rest on his own and always loves to take a walk with you. My favorite thing about Thor is when he smiles and melts my heart every time. He loves to come cuddle in the bed, right in between me and my fiancé, so I can just put my arm around him and hold him while I sleep.". He is neutered, vaccinated, microchipped, and has had preventative treatments to ensure he is ready for his next steps.
