Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

Arabelle arrived to New York City during a nor’easter but our time together has been anything but stormy!

Arabelle has a favorite grassy spot to pee just steps away from our front door. She occasionally uses a pee pad in the house in between potty breaks.

Quick to learn, and food motivated, she has learned a couple of commands already. She knows “sit” and “touch.” She’s close to mastering “drop it.”

Since she’s still a puppy we are gifted with zoomies and puppy antics. Mostly, Arabelle is a chill girl. She sat by her foster’s side for hours of college football just happy to snuggle and be included.

She is super sweet and cuddly. Her fosters suspect her bread to be 98% sweet angel baby, 2% super mutt. (A DNA test has not been conducted).

She will bring joy to any home.
