Description provided by Best Friends Animal Society — NYC

Meet Calvin and his BFF Hobbes! Calvin, a one-year-old male, is still getting settled in, so we’re just beginning to learn who they are. Right now, everything is new—new people, new sounds, new routines—and we’re giving them time to relax, decompress, and show us their true personality at their own pace. Here’s what we know so far about his time in foster care: https://bestfriends.sharepoint.com/:b:/s/GRP_NYC_Foster/IQBQ6rXplXLiR65SWYcyyiFEAfHj9P1eB6ogQW6_vvD4C7I?e=0yW8fP Their foster says "The boys have totally warmed up to me, and love getting affection! During meal time, they both run through my legs as I’m preparing their food. They love to play, especially with mouse toys. Calvin specifically loves getting pets on his face and back, and Hobbes often joins! It took them some time, but the boys warmed up into the sweetest pair." "They love to get pets when I am sitting with them on the floor, and rub up against each other. They are very playful and love playing with my mouse toy, and absolutely love their meal time. Such sweet and gentle boys, and are continuing to get more and more comfortable each day!" Calvin has settled into his foster home nicely. He is a bit on the shy side and will benefit from some extra support as he adjusts. He is very cute and playful. His foster says "Very shy but eager to explore, I can tell he is a curious boy ...VERY ADORABLE IM DYING FROM CUTENESS OVERLOAD. very smart, he knows that if he meows at me cute enough I cave in and give him treats." Calvin is in foster with his buddy Hobbes. They love to play and cuddle together. Calvin is the bolder of the two when it comes to exploring. They are so lovely and adorable. They are good friends and always walk side by side. They have big bright eyes and smooth fur. They’re shy, but very kind and gentle. They are affectionate, loving companions. "Calvin is more confident and curious about the new environment. He is comfortable staying near me, while Hobbes is more sensitive to sounds and movements and prefers to stay alone. I’ve been fostering them for only one week, so I haven’t fully built trust with them yet. They only let me pet their heads and backs while I’m feeding them. Every time I’m preparing their food, they get excited, especially Hobbes — he shows his social side by rubbing his body against my legs. They both love to play, and Calvin is the more playful one. He’s always the one finding new toys and inviting Hobbes to join. They prefer playing with each other rather than with people. They love grabbing and scratching at little mouse toys, and sometimes play with balls too. They can be very talkative while playing! Calvin uses the scratching boards a lot, and he seems to prefer the fabric type. So I’d recommend providing an alternative scratching surface near any fabric furniture to avoid damage." He is neutered, vaccinated, microchipped, and has had preventative treatments to ensure he is ready for his next steps. If you’re open to a little patience and a lot of possibility, come meet Calvin! You might be the person who helps them feel safe, confident, and right at home. Check back for updates—or stop by to say hello and discover who Calvin is becoming.
