Meet Mellow Boy!

Mellow Boy is a laid back guy who enjoys taking in the sights and smells around him while staying close to his people. He may be a little aloof at first, but he is receptive to treats and comfortable with gentle handling. Once he's comfortable, you'll find a sweet boy with a wagging tail who is happy to hang out by your side.

My Ideal Home

Mellow Boy would love a patient family who will give him time to settle in and get to know his new surroundings. Daily walks, enrichment, and fun activities like food puzzles and long lasting chews will help keep his mind and body happy.

Good with Other Pets?

Mellow Boy has not shown a reaction to other dogs during his time in care, but his history with other animals is unknown. Any introductions should be slow and supervised so everyone can get comfortable.

Good with Kids?

His history with children is unknown. Families should allow Mellow Boy to warm up at his own pace and make sure everyone understands how to give a dog space when needed.

Things to Know

Mellow Boy walks with some moderate pulling and can get distracted by his surroundings, but he follows his handler readily. He is still getting to know the world around him, so positive reinforcement and a little patience will help him feel right at home.
