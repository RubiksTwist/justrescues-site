Meet Roxanne!
Roxanne is a playful girl with a big personality and lots of love for life's little adventures! Found as a stray, she's still getting comfortable in her new surroundings, but she already enjoys exploring, accepting treats, and spending time with people. Bring out a toy and you'll get to see her fun side come alive! Roxanne is looking for someone who will appreciate her spirited nature and help her make the most of all that potential.

My Ideal Home
Roxanne would love a home where she can enjoy daily walks, playtime, and plenty of opportunities to explore the world around her. She's an energetic girl who will enjoy food puzzles, long lasting chews, and fun training games that keep her mind busy. She's still learning how to manage her excitement, so a patient person who can help her practice calm greetings and reward her good choices will be a wonderful match.

Good with Other Pets?
Roxanne's history with other dogs is unknown, and she can be a little unsure when she spots them on walks. She has also shown a strong interest in pigeons, and her previous history includes chasing cats and other small animals, so a home without cats or small pets would be best. Slow, thoughtful introductions and positive reinforcement will help her learn to navigate the world around her.

Good with Kids?
Roxanne would do best in a home without children under 13. She's an enthusiastic girl who can get carried away when she's excited, so an adult home will give her the space and consistency she needs to practice calmer interactions.

Things to Know
Roxanne enjoys attention and can get especially bouncy when she's having fun. Sometimes that excitement comes with jumping up or using her mouth during play, so she'll benefit from positive reinforcement and guidance toward more appropriate ways to interact. She can also get a little carried away on walks, and bringing a favorite toy along may help her redirect that energy. With patience, consistency, and plenty of fun things to do, Roxanne has so much potential to blossom into a wonderful companion. She's ready to find someone who will help her turn all that enthusiasm into something wonderful!
