Meet Chapo!

Chapo's story could have ended before it truly began.

This incredibly handsome boy was being given away on Craigslist in Georgia while bravely fighting the deadly parvovirus. Neglected, vulnerable, and in heartbreaking condition, he deserved so much more than the hand he had been dealt. Thankfully, he was given the chance to rewrite his story.

And Chapo? He is ready to make the most of it.

Chapo is deaf, but he doesn't let that slow him down one bit. He experiences the world in his own special way, and what he may miss in sound, he more than makes up for with his heart. He's sweet, affectionate, and incredibly loving, with a gentle spirit that will melt your heart. He loves being around his people, soaking up attention, and showing off that irresistible smile.

His deafness is simply one small part of what makes Chapo unique. He is smart, adaptable, and eager to connect with his humans, and with a little patience and the use of hand signals and visual cues, he can communicate beautifully with his family. In return, you'll have a devoted companion who has already proven just how much he has to give.

Chapo truly is the whole package: handsome, loving, resilient, and ready to be someone's loyal best friend. After everything he's overcome, he deserves a family who will celebrate him for exactly who he is and give him the kindness, security, and unconditional love he's always deserved.

Could you be the person Chapo has been waiting for? Apply to adopt this special boy today and let his smile, and his enormous heart, steal yours forever.
