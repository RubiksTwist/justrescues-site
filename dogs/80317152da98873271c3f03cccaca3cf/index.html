Meet Ms. Caramel Jelly!

Ms. Caramel Jelly may be a little shy at first, but underneath that cautious exterior is a sweet girl who enjoys gentle attention. She accepts pets, gives slow tail wags, and is starting to show us that she can feel comfortable with a little patience. She's looking for someone who will let her take things one step at a time.

My Ideal Home

Ms. Caramel Jelly would do best in a calm, quiet home where she can settle in at her own pace. Her new family should give her plenty of time to get comfortable with new people and surroundings and let her choose when she's ready for attention. A patient adopter who can help her build confidence will be a wonderful match.

Good with Other Pets?

Ms. Caramel Jelly has shown no reactivity toward other dogs while in our care. Her history with other animals is unknown, so slow introductions are recommended.

Good with Kids?

Because Ms. Caramel Jelly is currently fearful and needs time to feel comfortable, ACC recommends an adult-only home.

Things to Know

Ms. Caramel Jelly is still getting her bearings, but she's already showing us that gentle encouragement goes a long way. She's not looking to rush into anything. She's simply hoping to find someone who will give her the time she needs to feel safe and discover just how sweet life can be.
