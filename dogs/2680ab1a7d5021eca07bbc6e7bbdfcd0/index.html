Meet Marley, a valiant and lionhearted pup!

With a playful spirit and heart full of love, Marley is ready to find a safe, loving home of her very own. While Marley may be shy when meeting new people, her goofy and fun personality shines brightly as she opens up.

Marley is a courageous gal who loves exploring life! Whether she's pouncing after a bouncy ball, conquering her fears, learning new tricks or running around outside with her best human and canine friends, Marley is always ready for her next adventure. After all the fun, Marley is perfectly happy to settle in for snuggles and soak up affection from her people.

If you're looking for a loyal companion who will provide you with endless love, playful adventures and cozy cuddles, Marley just might be a match for you. Click the “Adopt Me” button to connect with Marley’s team in Pawling, New York!

*We encourage a $150 suggested adoption fee when adopting an animal. This adoption fee allows the ASPCA to continue our lifesaving work and help even more animals in need. We are grateful for any amount given at the time of adoption.*
