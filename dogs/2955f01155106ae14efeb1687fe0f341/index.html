HISTORY

Jane was found as a stray in Selma, Alabama. While her full history is unknown, it was evident that she had experienced neglect and needed significant medical attention and TLC. She arrived in Brooklyn in late January 2026.

DETAILED PROFILE

Jane Goodall (the dog) is not out in the field conducting research. She's on the couch. Her head is in your lap. She would like to stay exactly like this for as long as you'll let her, and she has the patience to wait you out.

At nine years old, Jane has arrived at the life she was meant for: soft, quiet, full of people who appreciate her. She came to us from a shelter in Alabama, where she'd been found as a stray in rough shape. She's emerged from it all with a sweetness and ease that feel less like resilience and more like her actual personality.

She's genuinely easy to have around. She doesn't jump on furniture for snuggles; she waits for an invitation, and when it comes, she settles in with quiet gratitude. She's gentle and friendly with people of all ages, including young children. She reads the energy of a room and matches it: calm when calm is called for, happy when happiness is in the air.

Her social skills extend to other animals. She's currently cohabiting peacefully with dogs in her foster home, and she's given the household cat its space with dignified, observant patience. She's house trained, crate trained, and lovely on leash, and she is, in every measurable way, an easy dog. Whatever comes next, Jane is ready for it. She has been for a while.

MEDICAL HISTORY

Jane is up-to-date on required vaccinations, spayed and microchipped. She tested positive for heartworm and has not been treated.

She has received care for medical issues that will require ongoing maintenance care. If you think Jane might be a good fit for your home, we can share a more detailed medical history and potential future care needs.

Complete veterinary records, dating back to the time of rescue, are typically sent via email after an adoption. They can also be sent in advance of an adoption, upon request.
