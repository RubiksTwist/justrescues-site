Description provided by Bideawee

Cole is currently being fostered while we look for an adopter for him- while his family can't keep him forever, they have nothing but awesome things to say about him! We are lucky enough to have insight on what he'd be like in your home!

He is highly intelligent, very loving, and loves a routine (knows exactly when its time to eat and go for walks). He loves to be outside, and play in water in the summer time. He has a lot of energy for a senior dog. He's very friendly with people and loves getting pets and scratches and cuddles all the time.

Toys: Cole does not have a favorite toy. He will destroy any type of plushy/squeaky toy immediately. He prefers tug of war with thick rubber toys or frisbees. We also play with a football that he really likes.

Routine: Cole is pretty set in his ways and really enjoys having a routine. He knows he eats once at 8am and once at 3mp (1cup each) He will definitely remind you when its time to eat!

Does Cole sound like he could be a great fit for your home? Adopt this sweet boy and give him the opportunity to live out his golden years in a furrrrever home.
