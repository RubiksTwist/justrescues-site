Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

Rambo is at risk for behavior reasons. In his previous home, Rambo has escalated to biting and holding 5+ times. Rambo will guard random items, or escalate when people hug or touch. In the care center, he has been highly fearful often shaking in fear. He is allowing for handling at this time. Medically, he is apparently healthy.

This pet is available only through ACC New Hope partners. To adopt or foster, please complete the pre-screener form below. Inquiries sent directly to ACC will not receive a response.

Pre-Screener Form

Rambo is a 5 year old dog with a documented history of multiple bite incidents. He may exhibit generalized resource guarding behaviors, potentially guarding a wide range of resources. He has also been highly fearful while in the care center, which may impact his ability to feel comfortable, build trust, and safely navigate new environments.

Due to his behavioral history and current presentation, Rambo will require placement with an experienced rescue organization equipped to support dogs with significant behavioral needs. His ideal placement would be with a foster or adopter who has demonstrated experience managing fear-based behaviors, multiple bite histories, and generalized resource guarding. A knowledgeable, committed placement that can provide appropriate management, structure, patience, and behavior support will be essential to setting Rambo up for success.
