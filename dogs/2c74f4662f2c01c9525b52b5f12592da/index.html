Description provided by Animal Lighthouse Rescue

Introducing Terry! He is a 6-year-old, 42-pound Lab mix with a heart full of love and resilience. He adores all humans and dogs, always eager to spread joy and affection to whomever comes his way. His favorite activities include playing with squeaker toys, going on long walks, and wrestling with his kennel mates. Terry also thrives on attention, especially when it comes to belly rubs and head scratches—his all-time favorite! This brave and loving boy is ready to find a forever home where he can leave his past behind and bask in the love and care he truly deserves. Despite a tough past of being chained outside 24/7 and suffering chemical burns that left bald spots on his skin, Terry's spirit remains unbroken and he cannot wait to find his forever home. Give this adorable boy a chance to show you how amazing he is!

Terry is neutered, microchipped, and up to date on his vaccinations. For more information about him, please fill out an application at www.ALRcares.com and email Rachel@alrcares.com. We schedule meet and greets for approved adopters only, an application is the first step in our approval process. Thank you!
