Meet Lemon Ginger!
Lemon Ginger is a sweet girl who is still finding her feet in a world full of new sights, sounds, and experiences. Found as a stray, she's ready for a fresh start with someone who will let her settle in at her own pace. She's shown us that she has a curious side, too, happily sniffing around and approaching people when she feels ready. This lovely girl may need a little time to warm up, but she's worth the wait!

My Ideal Home
Lemon Ginger would love a peaceful home where she can get comfortable without feeling rushed. Her ideal person will be patient and gentle, letting her take the lead as she explores her new surroundings and builds trust. Daily walks, food puzzles, long lasting chews, and fun scent games will give her enjoyable ways to explore and stay engaged. Most importantly, she'll need someone who celebrates the little victories and understands that confidence grows one step at a time.

Good with Other Pets?
Lemon Ginger's history with other dogs is unknown, but she's shown that she can approach another dog for a brief sniff before choosing to move away and take some space. She has been thoughtful about these interactions, so any future introductions should be slow and relaxed, with plenty of room for her to step away when she needs a break.

Good with Kids?
Lemon Ginger's history with children is unknown. Since new experiences can feel overwhelming for her, calm and respectful introductions will be important, and she'll appreciate having a quiet space where she can retreat whenever she needs some downtime.

Things to Know
Lemon Ginger can feel nervous in unfamiliar situations and may need some encouragement before she's ready to explore. Once she's more comfortable, she has shown that she can approach people, accept touch, and follow along with her handler. Letting her choose when to interact, moving at her pace, and pairing new experiences with tasty treats will help her feel more secure. With a little patience and a loving home, Lemon Ginger has every chance to add a little sweetness and zest to someone's life!
