Meet Fozzie Bear!
Fozzie Bear was found as a stray, and this sweet girl is still getting to know the world around her. She's a little shy at first, but she has already shown us that a few tasty treats can help bring her out of her shell. With a little patience and plenty of love, Fozzie Bear is ready to find someone who will help her feel right at home.

My Ideal Home
Fozzie Bear would love a peaceful home where she can settle in at her own pace. She's looking for someone who will give her plenty of time to get comfortable, celebrate her little steps of progress, and use positive reinforcement to help her build confidence. Walks, enrichment, and tasty treats will give her lots of ways to have fun while she gets to know her new family.

Good with Other Pets?
Fozzie Bear came into care with three other adult dogs, although we don't know much about their relationships. She has had a brief introduction to another dog here and showed some interest before choosing to move away, so any future introductions should be calm and gradual. For now, she would be happiest getting to know new dog friends in a quiet, controlled setting.

Good with Kids?
Fozzie Bear would do best in an adult-only home where she can enjoy a little extra peace and quiet while she settles in. She's a sensitive girl who appreciates having the choice to approach new people and experiences when she's ready.

Things to Know
Fozzie Bear can be a little unsure when she's being handled, but she has shown that she will come back for high-value treats once she feels more comfortable. She's a gentle soul who simply needs someone willing to let her take things one paw at a time. Give this Fozzie Bear a little time, and you may discover a wonderful new companion ready to share her happily ever after.
