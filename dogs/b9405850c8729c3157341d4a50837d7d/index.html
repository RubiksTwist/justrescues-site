Hailing from a southern shelter, Justin Bieber is 2 Years Old, a tiny 10lbs - and a FANTASTiC little guy. At first, this shy little guy was scared to step outside. Now, he’s happily trotting along on morning walks, following his foster human and doggy foster sibling around, and has figured out a potty routine outside. His confidence is growing, his adorable smile is shining through, and it’s hard not to be obsessed with him. Justin would thrive in a home with another dog who will continue teaching him the ropes and how to be a real dog! He loves cozy naps, snuggling up in bed, following his favorite people around, and having a doggy friend to learn from. He’s a low to moderate energy little guy who would much rather spend his afternoons cuddling than running a marathon. He has been calm around cats and kids. He is neutered and fully vetted!
