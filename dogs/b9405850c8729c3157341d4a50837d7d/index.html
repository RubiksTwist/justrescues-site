Description provided by Rescue City

Sometimes all you need is a 10lb scruffy little teddy bear named Justin Bieber to make life a whole lot sweeter. Our little JB has been BLOSSOMING in foster care, and we couldn’t be prouder of how far he’s come and thankful to his foster! Just a few weeks ago, this shy little guy was too scared to even step outside. Now he’s happily trotting along on morning walks, following his foster mom and doggy foster sibling around, and even starting to figure out that potty breaks happen OUTSIDE! His confidence is growing every single day, his adorable personality is shining through, and it’s hard not to be obsessed with him. Justin Bieber is a 2 year old, 10lb terrier mix with the sweetest little face, the cutest scruffy curls, and SO much love to give. His favorite things are cozy naps, snuggling up in bed, following his favorite people around, and having a doggy friend to show him the ropes. He’s a low to moderate-energy little guy who would much rather spend his afternoons cuddling than running a marathon. JB is doing wonderfully with other dogs, has been calm around cats, and has done well with children once he warms up. He’s also crate trained and becoming quite the little walking buddy! He’s still a shy boy who needs patience with handling, being picked up, and potty training, but the progress he’s made with a little love, consistency, and routine has been AMAZING. And that’s the thing about Justin Bieber. He just needs someone to give him the time and love to become the confident, happy little dog we already know is in there. We would LOVE to see JB find a calm, loving forever home, ideally with another friendly dog to help him continue building his confidence. Someone out there is going to be SO lucky to call this little guy theirs.
