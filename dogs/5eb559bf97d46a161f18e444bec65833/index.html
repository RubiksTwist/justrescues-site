Meet Pup Tart! 🐾

Pup Tart is a sweet, friendly 40-pound brindle boy who loves meeting people and getting attention. He's incredibly social and greets everyone he meets with enthusiasm. He's still working on his basic manners and can be a little exuberant when he's excited, but he's eager to connect with his people and would thrive with someone ready to help him learn.

Pup Tart is stressed in his kennel, so shelter life can be overwhelming for him. He was found as a stray alongside another dog, and now he's hoping to find a loving home where he can decompress, settle in, and show off his affectionate personality.

If you're looking for a friendly, attention-loving pup with plenty of potential, Pup Tart may be your guy! ❤️

On my #BoroughBreak, we... took Pup Tart out of the shelter for a little adventure. We walked, explored the park and beach, enjoyed some treats, and watched the world go by. He was curious about everyone we met and clearly loved being outside. He was a little anxious at times, but with some patience and love, I think this sweet guy has so much potential.
