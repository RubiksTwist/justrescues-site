Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

A volunteer writes:
Poppy Beans, a friendly stray, was fed in the street by a Good Samaritan for about one week before being brought to the Manhattan Care Center as she was soaking wet with rain. Poppy Beans is a pretty cow print kitty who was a bit shaken up in her first days at the care center(giving a little hiss/low growl here and there). Time and help from staff and volunteers did their job and Poppy Beans is now quite a pleasant, friendly and sweet cat to spend time with(see her video)after some initial shyness. Poppy Beans is making the best of her stay in a kennel, often standing at her door, out of curiosity and trying to meet some visitors as she so much enjoys the attention and the petting. Poppy Beans would love to meet you, charm you and spend the rest of her life loving and being loved. Ask about Poppy Beans when you visit us at the care center!
