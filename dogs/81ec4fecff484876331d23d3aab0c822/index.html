Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Meet Chevron AKA Chevy! The softest little sweetheart with the cutest personality and a whole lot of love to give. 🐾❤️

Chevy is a special girl who’s learning that the world isn’t such a scary place after all, and she’s making wonderful progress! Now that she’s settling into foster life, her sweet personality is shining through. She’s got the softest fur (thanks to a little at-home spa treatment from her foster nanny!) and makes the most adorable little trilling tribble noises when she gets excited or wants something. Seriously, how can you resist that?!

Chevy is a smart cookie who already knows sit, wait, come, and up-up (for hopping into the car or onto her favorite furniture). She’s fully housebroken AND pad-trained, and she’s perfectly happy hanging out in her crate while her people are away, especially when she has some fun enrichment to keep her occupied.

When it comes to her favorite things, Chevy is a girl of simple pleasures: soft treats, chicken, belly rubs, ear scritches, tiny squeaky toys (Lamb Chop is her absolute favorite!), car rides, and people-watching from the window. Basically, she’s a little princess who knows how to enjoy the finer things in life.

She’s also working on her leash manners and getting better every day! She may occasionally get excited and try to lead the way or find herself on the wrong side of a lamppost, but she’s learning to stick close to her nanny. With other dogs, she does wonderfully with slow introductions, especially with pups around her own size. She’s still building confidence around bigger, barkier dogs, so thoughtful introductions and a little patience go a long way. Chevy would also do best with calm, gentle children; she’s happy to accept treats and pets from little ones but prefers a peaceful household over a chaotic one.

One thing Chevy is still working through is her sensitivity to loud or unexpected noises. Slamming doors, shouting, alarms, and even the sound of trash cans rolling down the street can startle her. The good news? She’s already made great progress! Her foster is helping her build confidence through gentle exposure, reassurance, and positive reinforcement. She’s learning that the world can make some funny noises and that she’s safe, even when it does.

Chevy is looking for a patient, loving family who will continue helping her blossom and build her confidence. In return, you’ll get a loyal little companion who can join you on car rides, keep you company at home, and fill your days with belly rubs, squeaky-toy antics, and the sweetest little happy noises.

Her dream home? A person who believes in her, keeps the chicken treats coming, and reminds her every day that she’s safe and loved. Bonus points if you have a cozy couch and an endless supply of belly rubs!

Chevy’s come a long way, and we can’t wait to see just how far she’ll go with a family of her very own. Could that be you? ❤️
