Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Diesel still thinks he’s a puppy — full of exuberance, playfulness, and love. He’s a big, strong boy who doesn’t quite know his size (he may try to be your lap dog!) and thrives on affection.

Diesel is looking for an adopter who can provide gentle guidance, structure, and continued training to help him be his best self. He’s dog-selective, needs a cat-free home, and will do best with someone experienced and confident handling larger dogs.

To help set Diesel up for success, his adopter will receive a free course of ARF obedience classes!
