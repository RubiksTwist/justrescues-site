Cori is an 8-month-old Jindo Mix weighing 20.2 lbs. She may take her time warming up to new people, but don't mistake that for fear. Cori is simply thoughtful about who she trusts, and once she decides you're worth it, she gives you everything. Walking is her happy place, and the smile she gets on those walks will stop you in your tracks. She can't wait to find her forever home in NYC!
