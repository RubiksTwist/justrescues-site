Fika has officially discovered his good side. The only problem? He seems to have several.

From above? Strong. Straight on? Impressive. Side profile? He knows what he’s doing. This boy works ALL the angles, and he’s clearly ready for his close-up.

And while Fika may have started out a little shy, he’s been coming out of his shell and showing us more of his personality. These days, he’s happy to come over for pets, enjoys a good rub, and is discovering that getting attention from people is actually pretty great! We’re still getting to know the true personality of this handsome guy, but every bit of confidence he gains makes us more excited to see what’s underneath that gorgeous exterior.

Fika is looking for a calm, loving home where he can continue to blossom at his own pace. Give him some time, affection and plenty of attention, and we have a feeling you’ll have a very sweet companion who is more than happy to keep you company.

For now, he’ll be over here working the angles and waiting for his close-up.
