Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Gwen is already proving that a big personality can come in a puppy package! This adorable California girl came to ARF after being found by local animal control and is ready for a family of her very own. Sweet, affectionate, adventurous, and full of puppy sass, Gwen loves people and is always ready for her next adventure. She is a confident little lady who will thrive with a family ready to give her the training, guidance, and structure every growing puppy needs. She is already working on her leash skills and learning more every day. Gwen is growing fast and will likely be a big girl, but she is still very much a puppy at heart. If you are looking for a fun loving pup with a little extra spunk, Gwen could be your girl!
