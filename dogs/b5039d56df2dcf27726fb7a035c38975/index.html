Chipies *ELIGIBLE FOR FOSTER-TO-ADOPT*

✈️ Chipies is arriving to NYC on Wednesday, November 18 and needs a special medical foster!

He's making his cross-country journey all the way from San Diego, California to the East Coast to start his next chapter—and as you can see by that giant, sunny smile, nothing can dampen his joyful spirit!

Chipies is a sweet, 5-year-old, 12-pound chihuahua mix who is officially joining the tripod club! He recently underwent a rear limb amputation and is looking for a cozy, loving home where he can recover in peace. As if his resilient, happy-go-lucky attitude isn't enough, those giant radar ears, frosted sugar-dusted muzzle, and beaming grin are guaranteed to melt your heart.

Because he is healing from surgery, Chipies will require strict R&R until his sutures are removed. He will need a calm environment with no running or jumping on furniture, and since he only weighs 12 pounds, he'll just need a gentle lift or assistance with stairs while his incision heals and he adjusts to his new three-legged normal! Like any pup settling into a new routine, he may also need a little patience with house, leash, and crate training as he gets his bearings.

No additional photos or information are available at this time.

By applying for Chipies, you will participate in our foster / foster-to-adopt program!

Let’s give this brave, smiling boy a soft place to land and heal—apply to foster Chipies today!

Vaccinated for rabies, DHPP, and bordetella, microchipped and neutered. Heartworm negative and current on flea, tick, and heartworm prevention.

To apply for Chipies, visit our website (www.waggytailrescue.org) for an adoption application. It is important to fill out an application as soon as possible for consideration, and we require a complete application for you to meet an animal. We are currently unable to respond to general email inquiries as an all-volunteer run rescue. If the dog is on our website, they are still available.

Waggytail Rescue CANNOT guarantee the exact age of any adoptable dog, or final size of any of our adoptable puppies. We rely on the information provided to us by veterinarians, shelters, owner surrenders and other circumstances bringing the dog into our rescue.
