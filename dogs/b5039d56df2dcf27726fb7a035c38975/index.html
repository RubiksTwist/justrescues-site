Chipis | 5 yrs | 10.5 lbs | Chihuahua Mix (Tripod)

Chipis is a 5-year-old, 10.5-pound Chihuahua mix with a luxurious black-and-white tuxedo coat and sweet little spots underneath. His left rear leg was amputated at the hip, and you’d never know it slowed him down. He gets where he’s going, and he leads the pack while doing it.

His foster’s favorite things about him:

  • His little extra bit of skin at the neck
  • His white-tipped tail
  • The way he sleeps curled up like a little bean 🫘
  • His kisses! Chipis will pucker up like a little fish and cover your entire mouth with his. 😘

Personality & Highlights:

  • Velcro, if you allow it: Chipis will happily stick to your side, but he’s also independent and content on his own. He hasn’t shown any separation anxiety.
  • Funny and enthusiastic: He gets so excited for walks, and the excitement doesn’t fade when you return. He’s known to nibble on toes and shoelaces to say hello.
  • A true Chihuahua: He’ll give an alert bark to people on the street or a knock at the door. He shivers when it’s below 60°F (a cozy hoodie or a jacket is a must!) and loves to burrow and cuddle under blankets. 🧥
  • Easy to live with: He is fully house-trained. He’s walking well on leash, though it’s clearly new to him, so he’ll appreciate a patient handler.
  • Good with dogs: He’d be great as a single dog or with a smaller dog companion. He hasn’t been cat tested, but he’d likely do fine with a dog-friendly cat and proper introductions.

What Chipis needs: A patient, loving home where he can keep learning. He’s new to leash walking and will need to learn basic commands. Bonus points if you speak Spanish, since it’s his native language! 🇲🇽 With a warm blanket, a good jacket, and someone to cuddle with, he’ll be in heaven.

Ready to meet your little bean?

Vaccinated for rabies, DHPP, and bordetella, microchipped and neutered. Heartworm negative and current on flea, tick, and heartworm prevention.
