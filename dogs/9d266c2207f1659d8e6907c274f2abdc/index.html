Meet Tyson!

This charming gentleman came to ARF as an owner surrender, of no fault of his own, and is now awaiting his forever home. Sweet as can be and always demanding attention with gentle taps, Tyson is the kind of cat who will curl up beside you after a long day, greet you with soft purrs, and remind you how special the bond between humans and animals can be. He'll also gladly hop onto your shoulder if that means being closer to you for more attention!

This affectionate kitty does have special medical needs, as he is positive for Feline Leukemia (FeLV), which means he’s looking for an adopter who can give him extra care and understanding. But what he gives in return is immeasurable - unconditional love, endless affection, and the quiet companionship only a truly grateful cat can offer. He is currently making friends with our other Feline Leukemia positive kitty, Edmond, and ideally we'd love to send them home together!

Tyson deserves a home filled with patience, kindness, and love - and in return, he’ll be your loyal best friend every single day.
