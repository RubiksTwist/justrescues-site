Meet George!

George is a 5-month-old, 29-lb Catahoula Leopard mixed breed with an absolutely wonderful temperament and a whole lot of sweetness to share! This handsome boy is the kind of puppy who loves being part of the action but is also perfectly happy relaxing nearby. He enjoys getting pets and attention, but he doesn’t need to be the center of attention every minute—he’s just happy to be close to his people.

George loves going for walks and is always ready for a little adventure. He’s playful, curious, and especially enjoys exploring outside, where he can often be found happily chewing on a stick. He is crate-trained and housebroken, which is a fantastic start for a young puppy! George is also great with children and has shown himself to be a gentle, affectionate pup who enjoys company.

After some playtime or a nice walk, George is content to settle down and relax nearby. His sweet personality and easygoing nature make him a wonderful companion for a family looking for a puppy who can enjoy both fun and downtime. George is still a puppy, so he’ll need a family ready to continue guiding him, giving him plenty of love, and helping him grow. But with his great temperament, playful spirit, love of people, and sweet nature, George is already showing just how special he is.

This lovable little guy is ready to find his forever family—and he can’t wait to meet them! George is currently being fostered in Roselle, NJ and his adoption fee is $575.

If your application is considered a good fit for the animal you are interested in, or even a different pet, a volunteer will give you a call as soon as possible.

We are a foster based rescue and do not have a shelter location where animals can be seen during the week. Please see our Facebook page at https://www.facebook.com/LouiesLegacy for our weekly adoption album if you are interested in meeting an animal in person. Not all animals attend every adoption event, please check our Facebook page for animal event times and locations before you make a trip to meet a specific animal."
