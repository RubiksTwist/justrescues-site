Meet Romeo! 💕 Romeo is a sweet, affectionate, and smart 11-month-old boy who absolutely loves being around people. Romeo is still learning his new routine, but he’s already showing us what a wonderful companion he can be. If you’re looking for a loving buddy to share your everyday life with, Romeo might be your guy!
