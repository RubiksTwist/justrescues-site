Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Sherri Ann is a sweet and smart Shepherd mix, expected to stay medium-sized when fully grown, who's really coming into her own. She's a total cuddle bug who wants to be wherever her people are, and she's made huge strides since coming into foster care.

She's fully potty trained and crate trained, happily eating her meals in her crate and settling in for quiet naps throughout the day. She's playful and high-energy, especially when tug toys or chews are involved, and she'd happily play all day if you let her!

Sherri Ann can be a little shy with new faces at first, but she warms up faster than you'd think, she made a new friend after just one visit! She's still working on her leash manners in the big city, learning to stay focused and confident around the sights and sounds of NYC. With a patient adopter willing to keep building her confidence and continue her training, Sherri Ann is ready to become the best version of herself.
