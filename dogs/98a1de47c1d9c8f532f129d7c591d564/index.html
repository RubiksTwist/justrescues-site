Meet Calavera!
More sweet than spooky, Calavera is a sensitive boy who just needs a little time to feel comfortable in this big, busy world! Found as a stray, he's still getting used to new people and surroundings. He can be shy when first meeting someone and may need a little coaxing before he's ready to say hello. Calavera is hoping to find a patient person who will let him settle in at his own pace and show him just how lovely life in a home can be.

My Ideal Home
Calavera would love a quiet, cozy home with someone who understands that good things take time. A gentle routine, plenty of encouragement, and the freedom to explore at his own pace will help him feel safe. He's still discovering the world, so fun enrichment activities, sniffing adventures, and long lasting chews will be great ways to keep him busy as he settles in.

Good with Other Pets?
Calavera came to us as a stray, so his history with other dogs is unknown. He's shown some interest in saying hello to another dog but ultimately preferred to keep his distance. He'll need slow, thoughtful introductions, and dog parks aren't recommended for him.

Good with Kids?
Calavera would do best in an adult only home without children under 13. He's a shy boy who appreciates a calm environment and people who can give him the space he needs to feel comfortable.

Things to Know
Calavera can be unsure about new people and handling, so it's important to let him approach when he's ready rather than rushing into pets or cuddles. He may also need a little encouragement to get going on walks while he builds confidence outside. With patience, gentle reassurance, and plenty of time to settle in, this sweet boy can start to discover his own little world of happiness. He's ready to find someone who will help him feel safe enough to blossom!
