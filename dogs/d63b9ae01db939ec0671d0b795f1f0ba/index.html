Description provided by Humane Society of New York

Say Hello to 34 pounds of sweetness, Julian! (Born 8/30/2024) he is a Labrador mixed with Miniature Australian Shepherd and Border Collie. Julian is an amazing, very smart, alert, loving young dog who really enjoys his treats, playing with his toys and with the other dogs and of course you!

Julian came to us from a situation where nervous energy surrounded him, and like the sensitive guy he is, he absorbed some of that. But good news! With patience, consistency, and plenty of encouragement (especially when treats are involved), he’s been making wonderful progress since arriving here. This wonderful, sweet, loving pup, now enjoys hanging around with us in the office, playing in the roof run and going for walks in the neighborhood. Understandably, city sounds and the hustle bustle are overwhelming to Julian, so we are looking for a home in a quieter area where he can stretch his legs and enjoy the fresh air.

We really appreciate that you want to give a shelter animal a home. The Humane Society of New York was founded in 1904. The Vladimir Horowitz/Wanda Toscanini Horowitz Adoption Center is a no-kill shelter. Our on-site clinic and staff of skilled Veterinarians looks after the health and well-being of every animal here. We also have a great adoption matchmaking team who will gladly take your profile and match you or your pet with just the right cat or dog companion. We start the process once you have submitted an application to us.

FOR AN APPLICATION, please visit http://humanesocietyny.org/adoptions/ and download then email your application to hsny.adopt@gmail.com.

Our one and only location is:

306 East 59th Street (between 1st & 2nd Avenue) in Manhattan.

If you'd like to sponsor Julian, please email amk.hsny@verizon.net. You can give as much or as little as you like. Your donation is tax deductible. Thank you!
