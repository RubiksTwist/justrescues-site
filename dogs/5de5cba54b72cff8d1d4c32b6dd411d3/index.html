Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

My foster writes: Looking for a cat who can be your cuddle buddy, personal bathroom attendant, and midnight entertainment all in one? Meet Evergreen!
Evergreen is a curious little soul with a playful side, a sleepy side, and just enough sass to keep things interesting. His favorite hobbies include chasing pom-pom balls, getting the zoomies, and finding the most ridiculous positions imaginable to take a nap. He also enjoys sitting between the shower curtains to quietly observe your daily routine. Privacy? He's never heard of it.
He may be a little shy when you first meet him, but give him time to settle in, and you'll discover his sweet side. When he's in the mood for affection, he absolutely melts in your arms! He's also learning to communicate when he's had enough attention. When overstimulated, Evergreen may nip or scratch, although this behavior has become gentler over time. Redirecting him to his toys can help, and he's still learning that hands are for love, not play. He'll do best with someone who can recognize his cues, respect his boundaries, and let him enjoy affection on his own terms.
Evergreen is the perfect mix of playful and peaceful, affectionate and independent, sweet and just a tiny bit mischievous. He's looking for someone who will let him come out of his shell at his own pace, appreciate his silly little quirks, and continue helping him learn and grow.
Could Evergreen be the missing piece of your home?
