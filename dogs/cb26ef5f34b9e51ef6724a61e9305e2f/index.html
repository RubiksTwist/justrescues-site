✨ Meet Kiki

Meet Kiki — 8 years young and full of personality. 🧡

Kiki is a sweet, curious girl who loves attention on her own terms. She enjoys being petted, absolutely loves a good full-body brushing, and will rub against you when she wants affection — especially if back scratches are involved.

Don’t let her age fool you. Kiki still loves to play and will happily chase a wand toy. She’s also a little explorer who enjoys checking out every mysterious corner of the apartment and seeing what’s going on.

Kiki is affectionate, playful, food-motivated, and knows how to communicate her boundaries. When she’s had enough attention or starts feeling overstimulated, she’ll let you know — and once you understand her signals, she’s very easy to read.

She would be a wonderful match for someone who appreciates an independent cat who also genuinely enjoys companionship.

8 years young. Loves food. Loves brushes. Loves back scratches. Still ready to play. 🧡

Kiki is spayed, up to date on vaccines, microchipped, and FELV/FIV negative.
