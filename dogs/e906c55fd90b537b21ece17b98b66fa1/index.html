Description provided by Waldo's Rescue Pen

Hi, I’m Primrose! I’m a 1-year-old mutt with a whole lot of love to give. I'm a little shy at first, but once I'm comfortable, I’m kind, curious, and gentle!

I love getting to know the world around me: When I go on walks, I sniff absolutely everything. I'm also very good at making new friends at the park. My favorite thing to do is play and run around with any dog who is willing to match my energy! When I'm at home, my favorite activities are chewing a bone and a midday nap.

I never bark, haven't chewed or eaten anything I’m not supposed to, and haven't had any potty accidents in the house. I’m also low-shedding, so I won’t leave too much of my fur around your home. When it’s time for bed, I settle in nicely and sleep peacefully in my crate through the night. I'm so good at sleeping that I don't wake up before the sun rises!

Overall, I’m a sweet girl who loves spending time with my owners and soaking up all the love I can get. I’m still young and have so much to learn and experience, so I can’t wait to find my forever home.
