Handsome Bo loves making friends!

Bo is a 2-year-old, gray-and-white pup who has plenty of enthusiasm for spending time with people. He greets visitors with a wiggly body and happily accepts attention, leaning into pets and soaking up love! Yummy treats are also one of Bo’s favorite things, including anything cheese-flavored!

When it’s time for a walk, Bo has been a good sport about getting fitted with his harness. He enjoys exploring and generally likes to stay alongside his person, though an interesting sight or smell may catch his attention. A little baby talk and gentle encouragement are all it takes to help him refocus and keep moving. While on walks, he has been able to pass other dogs and has shown interest in meeting canine companions, proving he is a friend to humans and dogs alike!
