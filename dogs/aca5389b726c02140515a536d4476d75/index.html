Meet Jupiter | Apply Now

Jupiter is 3 years old and 15lbs of strawberry blonde curls~ He was rescued from an illegal puppy mill in Gyeonggi-do and hasn’t looked back.

He may be a celestial being but Jupiter enjoys sunbathing and anything cozy. He’s a boy of simple pleasures through and through. When he’s feeling goofy, you might find him curiously perched on top of something - atop his crate, the top of a couch - he likes to see the world from different angles what can we say.

Jupiter is doing well on his continued crate and house training, and enjoys his walks as much as his naps. With a good routine and guidance he’ll be talk of the town quickly! He also is completely disinterested in his feline foster sibling.

Meet this nugget on Saturday - it’s his grand debut!
