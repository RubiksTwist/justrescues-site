Description provided by PupStarz Rescue

Holy meow, look how pretty this girl is! Give a warm welcome to Sunny, a 2.5 -year old, 7.5 pound cat who is energetic, litter-box trained, and loves every kitty toy known to mankind!

Quite the active sort, Sunny has the ability to independently play with her toys for what seems like hours on end. This gal sure knows how to amuse herself. If you toss a toy for her to run and fetch, she looks at you like you’ve lost your mind. Sunny prefers inventing her own games! Funny Sunny!

Initially shy, it takes Sunny a while to warm up to humans, but once she’s does, her curiosity and sociability emerge. She enjoys sharing space with her humans and keeping a close eye on them while they work, watch TV, or sleep. She’s not ready for pets and cuddles just yet, but with a very gentle approach, she’s beginning to consider a head stroke and a chin scratch. You can see she kinda wants it; she just needs more time to trust.

Sunny is a good eater and never refuses an offer of a treat. She is sweet, she is graceful, she is quiet, and she keeps her lush coat clean as a whistle. All she needs is the right environment to flourish in, so she can live her best life and live with folks who will love and respect her.

If you would like to adopt this wonderful cat, please email rescue@pupstarzrescue.org or visit www.pupstarzrescue.org for an application!
