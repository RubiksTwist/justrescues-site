Meet Caspian!

Caspian is a handsome boy with a curious heart and a sweet personality! He enjoys taking in his surroundings and discovering all the interesting things the world has to offer. Every day is a new opportunity for this handsome guy to explore and make little discoveries!

Caspian appreciates gentle pets and, of course, some yummy treats! With kindness and a little quality time, you can help him continue discovering all the wonderful things life has to offer. Watching his curiosity grow and seeing him enjoy the little moments is part of what makes getting to know him so special.

If you’re looking for a sweet companion to share new adventures, tasty treats, and gentle moments of affection with, Caspian would love to meet you!

*You can meet animals located at the ASPCA Adoption Center during our walk-in hours (daily, noon to 5 p.m.). We encourage a $150 suggested adoption fee when adopting an animal. This adoption fee allows the ASPCA to continue our lifesaving work and help even more animals in need. We are grateful for any amount given at the time of adoption.*
