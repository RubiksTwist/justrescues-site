Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

I'm a sweet, affectionate girl with lots of puppy energy and an even bigger heart. My favorite thing is heading out for long walks in Central Park, where I can keep a close eye on pigeons, squirrels, and all the exciting things happening around me. I love meeting people, soaking up attention, and collecting as many pets as possible from my admirers. I am a great hiking companion and love getting in the water and swimming, and getting in the car to go for new adventures! But I can also sit calmly in the bar wile my human family has a meal.

While I'm always ready for an adventure outside, I'm actually very well-behaved at home and know how to settle down when it's time to relax. I'm also a bit of a foodie. Dry kibble by itself? Not exactly my favorite. But add a little wet food, and you've got yourself one very happy customer.

I do have a few things I'm still working; being left alone is especially hard. I love my people so much that I can get anxious when they leave, so I'm looking for a patient adopter who can help me continue building confidence and feeling secure. In return, I'll reward you with endless love, loyalty, and companionship.

If you're looking for a playful adventure outdoorsy buddy, professional squirrel chaser, and world-class cuddle enthusiast, I think we'd make a pretty great team!

﻿Maple is approximately 1y 2m 3d old and is fully grown at 58.6 pounds. They are microchipped and up to date on all age-appropriate medical care. Their breed is unknown as we have not had a DNA test done.
