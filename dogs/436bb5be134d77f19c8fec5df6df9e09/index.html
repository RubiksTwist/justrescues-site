Description provided by PupStarz Rescue

Dishwasher is an utterly adorable 3-and-a-half-month old Beagle/Terrier mix, weighing in just shy of 20 pounds right now, and eating enough to easily double her weight by the one-year mark! Yeah, food is a major draw for this hungry hungry hippo!

Although rather young, Dishwasher already shows signs of being highly intelligent. Housebroken, pad trained, and crate trained, Dishwasher is a quick learner and eager to please. She responds to sit and paw, and is capable of sooooo much more. Dishwasher loves spending time outdoors, rain or shine, and is busy at improving her leash manners with every single walk. If you wanna run an errand with Dishwasher, she will hop right into the car and sit calmly while you drive to your destination. Way to go, Dishwasher.

Dishwasher loves absolutely everyone, from kids to old folks to adults to other dogs. She has a blast playing with her canine pals and naturally respects the boundaries of others. When she’s done horsing around with either toys, people or pooches, Dishwasher is all about cuddles and kisses. And she’s so gentle, there’s no nibbling when kissing is involved. This sweetheart is as tender, sweet, and soft as they come.

The real deal, Dishwasher is nothing short of amazing, and she’s 100% ready to become a beloved member of your family. Friendly and fun-loving, Dishwasher will have you at hello!
