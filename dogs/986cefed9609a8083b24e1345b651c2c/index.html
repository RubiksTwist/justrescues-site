Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

My foster writes: Special Day really is such a special girl. She has plenty of energy to keep up with all of your athletic hobbies AND she's a genius! She picks up on word cues in minutes and learns new tricks in hours. She loves to play tug-of-war and fetch (and don't even get her started with bouncy balls!). Once she's gotten that energy out though, she becomes the cuddliest, sweetest little nap buddy. She's not a barker, she's potty trained, and she's SO friendly. I know she's going to make the perfect companion for someone special and I can't wait for her to meet her forever family.

My Boroughbreaker writes: One of my favorite things about my morning with Special Day was watching her come out of her shell. When we started our walk, she was a little bit shy; she startled at loud noises, was attentive toward people and dogs, and wasn't sure how she felt about me. Soon, though, after unwinding by sniffing all the smells in the grass of Central Park and practicing a few tricks (she knows sit and shake!), she began to open up. When we stopped a bench to rest for a bit, she sprawled at my feet and enjoyed her first belly rub of the day--unconcerned about the passing joggers, cyclists, and dogs.
Special Day has plenty of puppyish energy (I think she would make the perfect running buddy) but is still able to settle when needed. She responds extremely well to positive reinforcement, including praise and food rewards. If I got to see a more confident side of Special Day emerge in a few short hours, I know there's even more of her personality just waiting to be discovered!

What my friends at ACC say about me:

My history is a mystery and my friends here do not know much about me yet!

I would appreciate slow introductions to new people and places to help me feel safe.

I would do best in a home with only adult humans.

I love to be loved, but on my own terms! Let's brush up on some canine body language together!
