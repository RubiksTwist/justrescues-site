Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

Sosa is a sweet, affectionate cuddle bug who loves being around his people. He is happiest when he can stay close to you and will gladly curl up by your side for as long as you'll let him. Sosa rarely barks and tends to take on the energy of the people around him, making him a loving and easygoing companion.

Sosa enjoys spending time in the yard and going for short walks, where he does very little pulling on leash. He knows "sit" and was previously crate trained, although he no longer needs to be crated. He enjoys chewing and loves toys he can really sink his teeth into, especially wood and plush toys.

Sosa is comfortable with his family bathing and brushing him and is also used to going to the groomer. Nail trims are more challenging for him, as he becomes fearful and uncomfortable. He is also nervous during car rides. Although he will get into the car on his own, he struggles with his balance and pants throughout the ride.

Above all, Sosa is a loving companion who wants to be close to his people. His affectionate, cuddly personality and love of companionship make him a special dog who would thrive with a family that can give him plenty of love, patience, and reassurance.
