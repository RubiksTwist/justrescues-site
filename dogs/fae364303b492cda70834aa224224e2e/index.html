Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

If you know Jack Russell Terriers, you know they come with a little extra spice - and Jack is no exception. This handsome boy has the energy, enthusiasm, and zest for life that make his breed so special. He’s always ready for action and would love a family who can keep up with his busy little self!

But behind all that energy is a very sweet boy who has plenty of love to give. Jack has the best of both worlds - a lively, spirited personality and a lovable side that makes him hard to resist. He’s looking for a home where he can stay active, explore, and be included in all the fun.

Jack is not the pup for someone looking for a couch potato - he’s looking for his partner in crime! Someone who will enjoy his playful nature, give him outlets for all that classic Jack Russell energy, and appreciate the big personality that comes in his little package.
