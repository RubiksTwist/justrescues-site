Meet Ash!

Ash is a 2-month- old 11-lb German Shepard Mix who loves kisses, cuddles and playing.

His superpowers include being great in the crate/pen, learning basic commands like "sit and paw", loving everyone he meets, and getting along great with other dogs. He loves his naps and snuggling up close!

Ash uses pee pads because he is still a puppy, but we are optimistic that with time and patience, he will figure it out! He is learning how to play with toys and love them! Some of his favorite things are his kong (stuffed with peanut butter and frozen!), his meal times, and exploring his surroundings.

If you're looking for the biggest love bug who loves adventures and snuggling at night and at naptime, he might be your perfect match.

Ash is currently being fostered in River Edge, NJ and his adoption fee is $575.
