Abby was surrendered to the care center as a stray, and we have no history on her behavior in a home environment. Abby has shown highly fearful behavior at the care center; she has been hesitant to leave her kennel, and when in novel environments is tense, with a tucked tail, and reluctant to move far. During her medical exam, she startled and hard stared when a person entered the room. Given time and in calm environments, she has started to warm up and show social behavior with handlers, but due to her level of fear we recommend placement with a New Hope partner who can allow for decompression, provide any necessary behavior modification (force-free, positive reinforcement-based) and re-evaluate behavior in a stable home environment before placement into a permanent home.
