HISTORY

Immortelle is from the island of Dominica; no information about her history before she was rescued is available. She traveled to Brooklyn in late June 2026.

DETAILED PROFILE

Immortelle is a gentle, intuitive girl who shares her personality on her own schedule. Once she trusts you, she's a goofball with so much puppy energy that strangers regularly ask if she's a puppy. When she's thrilled, she launches straight up like a pogo stick. Her favorite people get two-paws-up hugs, though she's selective about handing them out.

She has two modes: playful and sleepy. Mornings bring zoomies and a stuffed toy thrashed until it's empty; afternoons are for naps. Lately she's more into long-lasting chews and food puzzles than toys. She'll snuggle in bed but prefers her own spot on the couch, and laps are not her thing. She needs real daily engagement, a good walk, park time, and play, or she'll invent her own entertainment, including barking for attention.

New people take time, and she warms up to women faster than men. She has started barking at some men who pass or try to say hi, and she announces hallway noise at home, sometimes at night, so she'll do best with a person who lets her approach new people on her own terms. She's been sweet with children who approach calmly. She adores other dogs, especially ones her size or smaller who will chase her; big, boisterous players can spook her, and a confident canine companion could help her shine. Street cats get no reaction.

She's house trained and a dream on leash most of the time, though she can pull near the park and stall on unfamiliar routes. She sleeps in her crate overnight and is still learning to head in and settle on her own.

Once she's decided you're hers, she'll plant herself by the door and wait for her goodbye pets.
