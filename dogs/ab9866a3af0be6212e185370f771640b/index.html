Description provided by AMA Animal Rescue

Introducing Josie, a sweet and quiet companion ready to warm up your home.

Josie is such a gentle and loving girl. She can be a little shy at first when meeting new people or exploring new spaces, but her true self shines through the moment she feels safe.

Her absolute favorite place in the world is her plush tent bed. She finds comfort in its soft blankets and private little hideaway, making it her go-to spot for cozy naps and peaceful afternoons. Give her a quiet corner, her beloved tent, and a little bit of patience, and you will see her relax into the sweetest, most loving companion.

Josie is looking for a peaceful, calm home with patient humans who will let her adjust at her own pace. Because she prefers a quiet environment without any furry competition. She'd prefer to be the only cat in the household, so she can soak up all the love you have to give!

If you have a peaceful home and plenty of love to give a tender-hearted girl, Josie is ready to be your one-and-only! 🐱✨
