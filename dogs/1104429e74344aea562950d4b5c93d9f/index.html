Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

Looking for a snuggle buddy who believes the best things in life are naps, snacks, and affection? Meet Luna Tuna! This beautiful 8-year-old black kitty is a big girl with an even bigger heart!

Luna Tuna is an exceptionally sweet, mellow lady who loves nothing more than spending quality time with her favorite people. Her purr motor starts running the moment you approach, and she happily leans into cheek scratches, chin rubs, and plenty of pets. She's even been known to roll onto her side to soak up a little extra attention! This laid-back lady would be perfectly content spending her days lounging beside you and enjoying the comforts of home.

Luna Tuna has a history of litter box accidents in her previous home, but there's encouraging news: she hasn't had any reported accidents during her time at ACC! Our Behavior Team believes her previous litter box may have been too small for her, and recommends providing multiple extra-large, easily accessible litter boxes to help her maintain her good habits.

Luna Tuna is spayed, vaccinated, and FeLV/FIV negative. She'll need a family committed to helping her achieve a healthier weight and addressing her dental disease. She's also receiving treatment for an upper respiratory infection.

With her gentle personality, endless purrs, and love of snuggles, Luna Tuna has so much affection to share. All she needs is someone willing to give this wonderful girl the comfortable, loving home she deserves!

A volunteer writes: Luna Tuna is eight years young, but we have her baby picture as she was brought to us and adopted as a tiny kitten. We've welcomed her back with open arms as we set about trying to find the forever home she's searching for. Luna Tuna often sits right at the front of her condo, patiently watching the comings and goings of everyone as she waits to get invited herself. Quick to reciprocate the type of welcome she hopes to one day receive, Luna Tuna said hello by headhunting directly into my hand and big stretching herself out right at the front. Peering over her shoulder as she tried to keep tabs on the room, Luna Tuna would love to be the star of the show if only she could. previous owners stated their favorite things about her are how sweet and affectionate she is and we couldn't agree more.
