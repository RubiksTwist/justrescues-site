Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Looking for a little bundle of joy to brighten your life? Meet Qixi, a 3-year-old Bichon Frise with a big personality, an even bigger heart, and plenty of goofy charm!

Thanks to Hope4Fur and the Andrew Sabin Family Foundation, Qixi has been given a second chance at life after being rescued from the Chinese meat trade, and now he's ready to find the loving forever home he deserves. Despite his difficult past, Qixi is full of happiness and love. He's incredibly friendly, playful, and goofy, and he has a special way of making everyone around him smile.

Whether he's showing off his silly personality, making new friends, or simply enjoying being loved, Qixi is sure to bring endless laughter and companionship to his future family.

He's come such a long way, and now all he needs is someone to show him what it means to be truly home
