💙 Meet Smurf! 💙

Smurf is a 1-year-old boy who has spent practically his entire kittenhood growing into adulthood in our foster homes. We first took him in when he was just 2 months old, and even back then, he was a naturally timid little guy who needed extra time to feel comfortable in the world around him.

Smurf isn’t your typical lap cat, and he won’t be the one demanding cuddles or climbing into your arms. He’s a little different, and that’s exactly why we hope someone will give him a chance.

He needs a home that understands that love doesn’t always look like constant affection. Sometimes it’s watching a cat become more confident, seeing him find his favorite spots around the house, and knowing you’ve given him the security to finally just be himself.

One thing we’ve learned about Smurf is that he really comes out of his shell around other cats! A home with a friendly, confident resident kitty looking for a companion could be exactly what he needs.

Smurf has spent far too much of his young life waiting for someone to choose him. He doesn’t need someone to change him or turn him into a lap cat. He just needs someone willing to love the cat he already is.

Please consider giving Smurf a chance. The quieter, more reserved cats deserve forever homes, too. 💙🐾
