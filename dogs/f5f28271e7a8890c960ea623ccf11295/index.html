HISTORY

Jon Snow was found in Georgia tied up and left without food or water at an abandoned house. He had mange and was in very poor condition, and X-rays showed he had been shot in three places, with the wounds healed but a bullet still lodged in his front leg and fragments in his torso. He travels to Brooklyn in late October 2026.

DETAILED PROFILE

Jon Snow has solved the Kong. Handed one filled with peanut butter for the first time, he wedged it under the corner of his crate to hold it steady and cleaned out every bit. So much for knowing nothing.

This big, chill guy is about as low-drama as dogs get. While in Georgia, he earned a reputation for being remarkably easy. He heads back into his kennel without fuss and returns to his bed. When it’s time to come out, he waits at the back until he’s invited, then strolls up to the gate. He loves his crate and hangs out in it by choice.

Other dogs seem to like having him around. He shared a yard with two of them with no trouble at all and became good buddies with another. People get a friendly hello, and he’s happy for attention without being pushy or needy about it.

He arrived in rescue with a hard history and shows little interest in dwelling on it. He has a slight limp on one front leg, though he walks comfortably, and he’s missing some teeth.

These are early impressions from Georgia. His energy at home, house training, leash manners on city streets, and how he does with kids and cats are all still unknown. Like any dog landing somewhere new, he’ll need time to adjust.

In the meantime, stock up on peanut butter. He has a system.
