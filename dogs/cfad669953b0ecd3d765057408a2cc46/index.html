Submit an adoption application a t socialteesnyc.org Our Team Says: " Becky needs a home outside of the city, she's not meant for the city life. We really want her with another dog buddy that can help her show the ropes." Level of dog ownership experience required: JUST A REALLY PATIENT SOUL – Becky is a special fluff. She's extremely shy and is learning how to be a dog. She needs a lot of time to get comfy and a lot of grace. She's very gentle. We know there's a very special lady inside of her you're just not going to see it for a while. If you're okay with that, then this girl is for you. If you want a golden retriever personality on day one - not the girl for you. Energy level: Lower – Needs a daily 30 minute walk or window of quick paced exercise (minimum) in addition to potty break strolls (10-30 min each). Also needs significant socializing opportunities outside the home and mental stimulation at home like tricks training practice, chew items, and puzzle toys. (This is regardless of dog size – many small breeds were designed for lots of activity and will exhibit unwanted behavior when they’re understimulated.) Energy Level - She is very chill but will come by for some affection. Most of the time she is happy in her doggy bed. Tolerance to City- Nope. Needs a home in the burbs. Potty Training - Slowly getting the hang of it. She's decompressing still. But we know she'll get there. She's starting to pee outside. She only goes twice a day so she's predictable and wants to be a good girl, just feeling a little upside down. Comfort Level w/ Strangers- While she's still scared and shy, she is not an aggressive dog. Even in her panic mode, she didn't try to nip or bite at anytime or showed any aggressive behavior. She is very sweet. Separation Anxiety - Nope, no sep anxiety/ She will sometimes follow me into other rooms when I move, but also fine on her own. She has been left alone without much hovering the first few days so she can decompress. She has not barked yet! Leash Manners- She just shuts down and sits and starts shaking or lies down. Can not get her to walk on the leash yet. Cuddle Bug Level - Medium- Loves snuggles and belly rubs. Very Sweet. Crate Training - Loved the Crate. It gives her a sense of safety. She also loves her doggy beds. She sleeps well in a crate at night. Also in the soft carrier in the car. Medical status: Healthy as far as we know. Up to date on their DHPP, Bordetella, and Rabies vaccines; heartworm tested if coming from Tennessee or Puerto Rico; spayed/neutered; microchipped; treated with dewormer upon intake How did this dog end up with Social Tees? Most of our dogs come via partner groups who help us save them from shelters where they are at risk of euthanasia in Tennessee, Los Angeles, and Puerto Rico. There are very few spay/neuter resources there and many more animals in those areas than there are people to adopt them. Rescue groups like ours bring them to the northeast where the desire for pets like these allows us to save their lives. PLEASE NOTE: We cannot guarantee any breed mix, exact age, or full grown size (if the dog is under a year old). We do our best with very educated guesses.
