Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Meet Perla! A tiny queen with a big personality and opinions of her own. 👑🐾

Perla is the perfect mix of independent lady and devoted little shadow. She has a mind of her own, plenty of personality, and a very clear idea of how she thinks things should be done. And honestly? We love a girl who knows what she wants!

When Perla is feeling brave and confident, she loves to explore her surroundings, sniff out every nook and cranny, and make sure she knows exactly what's going on. She's especially interested in kitchen activities and insists on supervising all your culinary endeavors. Just a friendly reminder: check the fridge and oven before closing the doors, because this little inspector takes her job very seriously! She's also a total cuddle girl.

When she's not busy overseeing household operations, Perla has two favorite pastimes: demanding affection and burrowing into blankets for a cozy snooze. She'll happily crawl into your lap, rest her little head on your leg, and let you know when it's time for pets. But don't mistake her sweetness for a lack of boundaries! Perla is a girl who knows her mind, and if you try to move her from her favorite couch spot or remove her adorable outfit before she's ready, she'll make her objections known. Consider yourself lovingly warned: Perla's personal space and fashion choices are not up for debate!

This little lady is also a true New Yorker. The hustle and bustle of city life doesn't faze her one bit. She's just happy to be out and about with her person, taking in all the sights and smells. Her tiny legs do have their limits, though, and when she decides she's walked quite enough, she'll politely request a lift so she can continue her adventures from the comfort of your arms. After all, even a city girl deserves VIP treatment!

Perla is a one-of-a-kind little companion with a whole lot of character packed into one adorable package. She's affectionate without being clingy, independent without wanting to miss out, and endlessly entertaining in her own uniquely Perla way.

If you're looking for a little sidekick with main-character energy, a soft spot for snuggles, and opinions about absolutely everything, Perla might just be your girl. ❤️
