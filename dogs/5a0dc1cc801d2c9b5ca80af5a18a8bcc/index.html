Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

My foster writes: Clove is a goofy, big-hearted pup who loves a good zoomie followed by belly rubs in your lap. He's a relaxed couch buddy for work-from-home days and patient when you're out. Give him a little daily exercise and mental stimulation, and he'll be your perfect companion.

My foster writes: Clove is so ready to be a full time loyal, dedicated companion!
As a five year old gent he is in that sweet spot of calm shadow buddy around the house but has energetic puppy bursts at playtime! He also loves attention but not needy and can go full couch potato after a long walk!
Easy breezy on the leash while walking, can go long distance without issue and still curious when out & about, he even checks in with you for a pet while on longer walks!
He's so silent around the apartment; the only noises you hear are of him readjusting his lying down position!
Friendly to new people; open to petting saying hi!
Clove's personality is really engaged & enthusiastic while being chill the majority of the time; he can really match the energy of the room so I could see him being suited to a variety of different types of house holds.
Despite being in the shelter long term he is so bright, interested and full of affection.
Clove will bring so much to a home and make someone fantastically happy as you companion.

Another foster writes:
Clove is an incredibly affectionate and goofy guy who just wants to be by your side in whatever you do! His favorite thing to do is lean into you for scratches (his ears are silky soft), even falling to the floor and flipping upside down in order for you to get his belly. He's incredibly smart (knows to sit, shake both paws, down), is housebroken, and loves to sploot around the house.
He's a quiet companion in the home and will check in on you during the day. When I have been in the office, he has been able to settle for a few hours and is immediately ready for a cuddle session once I come back! When it's time to go out, Clove sits super politely for you to put his harness on.
He still has plenty of playful puppy energy, always ready for a round of tug with a rope. Clove is also good about boundaries -- he knows what toys are his and can easily be redirected with treats and play!
