Meet Clove!

Clove arrived to us as a stray with no ID. He's a sensitive, shy sweetheart who prefers to take his time connecting - he often approaches on his own terms and enjoys coming for pets when he feels ready. Clove is thoughtful and steady, and he's participating in our Shelter Scholars mat work program to help him decompress and build confidence in busy or new settings.

My Ideal Home

A calm, predictable adult-only home where a patient adopter can continue confidence-building work with slow, gentle handling. Clove does best with predictable routines, gentle enrichment, and gradual introductions to new people and situations. Training that focuses on cooperative care, impulse control, and loose-leash skills (and use of appropriate equipment like a front‑clip harness) will help him thrive.

Good with Kids?

No - Clove would do best in a home with only adults. He can be fearful and has nipped when frightened, so we recommend no children (under 13) while he continues to build confidence.

Good with Other Pets?

Unknown history with household pets. In shelter assessments he has ignored dogs in crates and remained calm during some introductions, though he can hold a tense frame during new encounters. Slow, controlled, supervised introductions are recommended and may help him feel comfortable around other dogs.

Leash Manners

Clove can be strong on leash and is known to mouth or leash-bite at times. He would benefit from loose-leash training, impulse-control exercises, and redirection with high-value treats or toys. Techniques like stop-and-go, direction changes, and a front‑clip harness will be useful while his handler builds engagement and focus.

Overall Health

Healthy overall; neutered and microchipped, and receiving routine veterinary care while in our care.

Behavior Determination

Experienced Adopters - Clove is sensitive and can show fearful or anxious reactions when stressed. He needs an adopter who understands fear-based behavior, can move slowly, avoid sudden approaches, and follow a behavior plan that includes desensitization, enrichment, and cooperative handling. This approach will help reduce anxiety and build his confidence over time.

Other Fun Facts

Clove is soft-bodied and affectionate once he trusts you - he often leans into gentle touch and takes treats nicely. He enjoys mat work to settle his body and mind, and he can be playful with toys (he sometimes grabs a toy and keeps it). Because he prefers to call the shots, many of his best moments come when you let him come to you for attention. His adoption fee has been reduced to help him find the home he deserves.
