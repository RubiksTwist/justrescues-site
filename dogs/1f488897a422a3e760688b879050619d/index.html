Description provided by Waldo's Rescue Pen

Millie is truly a dream foster. At just 8 months old, she is fully crate trained, house trained, and rarely barks. She knows the commands sit, stay, lay, and paw. She's incredibly affectionate and loves to cuddle, but she's also an athletic, intelligent puppy who thrives on both physical and mental enrichment. Her favorite activities include fetch, puzzle games, outdoor naps, and learning new tricks. Millie is a true "Velcro dog" who loves being with her people. She's wonderful with adults, children, and other dogs, and her playful, eager-to-please personality makes her a joy to have around. As a young working-breed mix, she has plenty of energy and does best in a home that can provide regular exercise, training, and attention. She'll happily settle in for cuddles once she's had a chance to play and engage her mind.
