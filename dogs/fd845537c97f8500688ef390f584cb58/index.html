🌼🐾 Meet Quinn! 🐾🌼

Quinn is an active girl with lots of energy! 🏃‍♀️ She needs at least one hour of vigorous exercise daily, beyond regular potty walks. She’ll thrive with an active family who can give her plenty of attention, exercise, and new adventures!

Her natural instincts really shine outdoors! 🐦 She loves sniffing everything and enthusiastically jumping toward birds. On walks, she can be perfectly calm or pull quite a bit, depending on her mood and surroundings. Loud noises, especially trucks, can scare her, causing her to freeze or pull toward walls for safety. With patience and consistent training, she’ll keep building confidence! 💪

She’s doing well with crate training, sometimes whining for 5–10 minutes before settling down for a nap or playing with her toys. 🧸 She sleeps through the night without accidents! 💤 She’s not fully house trained during the day yet and can occasionally have accidents, especially when excited. With patience and a consistent routine, she’ll improve!

Sweet, curious, and incredibly friendly, Quinn loves everyone she meets, humans and dogs alike! She’s always excited to explore the world around her. 🌍

Quinn is looking for an active, patient family ready to help her grow and explore the world together! 💛
