Meet Kiddie - a fun-loving and loyal lady! We're still learning more about Kiddie's personality, but here’s what we know so far from her time in foster care: https://bestfriends.sharepoint.com/:b:/s/GRP_NYC_Foster/IQAzsy1b6Iu3TL2CLsPHlj2QAXFj1cO0J5zLKX8bLH9vKKo?e=67QnMn Her foster says "Kiddie has been sweet, gentle, and affectionate since day one. She settled in almost immediately and was comfortable enough to stretch out and sleep peacefully. She loves food but listens well to commands, even when tempted. She is also very sensitive to human emotions and comes over to comfort me when I’m upset. While she has always been calm and well-behaved at home, she gets very excited outdoors. Over time, she has become more responsive to commands and redirection during walks. My favorite thing about Kiddie is how sweet and affectionate she is. It’s adorable when she wags her tail and looks at me, hoping I’ll give her some pets." Kiddie is spayed, vaccinated, microchipped, and has had preventative treatments to ensure she is ready for her next steps. If you’re open to a little patience and a lot of possibility, come meet Kiddie! You might be the person who helps them feel safe, confident, and right at home.
