Meet Major!

Major is a handsome Alabama boy with his whole life ahead of him, and he is ready to make sure the best part of his story is still to come.

Major and his siblings, Story, Palmer, and Booker, were found on the streets of Alabama without their mom. These puppies should have been growing up surrounded by the safety and love of a family, but instead they were left to fend for themselves far too young.

Thankfully, Major was given a second chance. Now he is safe, cared for, and ready to discover all the wonderful things puppyhood should be about. He deserves a family who will help him learn, grow, play, and become the happy, confident dog he was always meant to be.

Major's journey may have started with uncertainty, but his future can be filled with everything he has been missing: a warm home, a loving family, fun adventures, and someone who will never give up on him.

Could you be the family Major has been waiting for? Apply to adopt this special boy today!

Please remember that adoption bios are only snapshots of who an animal is. For more information about Major, including his personality, needs, and what type of home may be the best fit, please reach out to our Adoptions Team.
