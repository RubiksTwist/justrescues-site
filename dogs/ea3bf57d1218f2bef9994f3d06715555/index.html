Meet Jewel – A Sweet Southern Gem Looking for Her Forever Home!

Straight from Kentucky, Jewel is a lovely 2 year-old sweetheart with a gentle soul, a playful spirit, and so much love to give!

Jewel may be a little shy when you first meet her, but give her a little time and you’ll quickly discover what a sweet, loving girl she truly is. Once she feels comfortable, her adorable personality begins to shine!

Jewel is a thoughtful and observant pup who picks up on her surroundings. She’s been wonderful with her canine foster brother, knowing when to give him space but also enjoying some fun playtime together! She loves playing with toys and happily joins in a good game with her doggy companion. She’s the perfect mix of gentle and playful!

Working from home? Jewel might just be your perfect coworker! She loves quietly hanging out by your side while you work, keeping you company without demanding constant attention! But when it’s time for a break, she’s always happy to have a little fun!

And there’s more to love about Jewel! She’s already house-trained, crate-trained, and has a great appetite. She’s settling beautifully into her foster home and showing everyone what a wonderful companion she can be.

Jewel has so much to offer—a sweet disposition, a playful side, and a loving heart. All she needs is a family willing to give her a little time to settle in and let her true personality blossom.

Could Jewel be the missing gem in your family?
