Meet Sheppard (Shep)!

Sheppard is an 8-year-old mix and quite possibly the sweetest old man you'll ever meet. He's gentle, affectionate, wonderfully mellow, and happiest simply being near his people. Think of him as a giant loaf of bread with legs, with the occasional burst of enthusiasm when something particularly exciting (usually a squirrel) crosses his path.

Shep came to New York from Kentucky, and he's had quite the journey since arriving in foster care. Between pneumonia, significant arthritis, dental surgery, and a handful of other medical hurdles, the poor guy has been through the wringer. But through all of it, he's remained remarkably sweet, patient, and trusting.

And the best part? As he's gotten healthier, he's really started to come to life. With treatment for his arthritis, including injections, he's found a new pep in his step. The sleepy old gentleman who first arrived is still very much a sleepy old gentleman, but these days there's a little more spring in his stride and a lot more personality coming through.

One thing Shep doesn't seem to understand is his own size. Despite being a very large dog, he is firmly convinced he's a Chihuahua. He will happily attempt to climb into your lap, wedge himself against you on the couch, or snuggle as close as physically possible. Personal space is not a concept he has much interest in learning.

Shep is housebroken and excellent at letting you know when he needs to go outside. He loves a good walk, although he firmly believes that every tree, lamppost, and patch of grass deserves a thorough sniff and possibly a little pee. His favorite hobby is investigating other dogs' pee, and he approaches this work with the seriousness of a forensic detective. He's generally an easygoing walking companion, but if he spots a squirrel, he can suddenly remember how to move at a speed you didn't know he was capable of.

He's also a fantastic companion for the quieter parts of life. Bring him to a café, sit with him in the park while you read a book, or take him along for a leisurely afternoon, and he'll happily settle in for a nap beside you. He loves car rides, is content to simply be included, and doesn't need constant entertainment to be happy.

He's wonderful with cats, generally gets along well with other dogs, and has an incredibly gentle disposition. Occasionally, he likes to appoint himself the Fun Police when other dogs are playing a little too enthusiastically, but for the most part, he's content to mind his business and enjoy the company.

Shep does get very nervous in unfamiliar environments. At his foster home, he's made wonderful progress and has become increasingly comfortable when left alone. He's also perfectly happy being left with another person. But being left completely alone somewhere new can be genuinely frightening for him, and when he panics, this otherwise slow-moving gentleman can become a surprisingly determined escape artist. He has a documented history of opening doors and crates, which may have something to do with his double dewclaws. We're not saying he has opposable thumbs, but the evidence is mounting. His forever family will need to be patient as he settles in, take precautions to keep him safe, and help him build confidence in his new surroundings.

After everything he's been through, what stands out most about Sheppard is just how sweet he remains. He's the kind of dog who asks for very little and gives so much in return. He doesn't need constant entertainment or a packed social calendar. He just wants his walks, his naps, his people, and the comfort of knowing he's home.

Shep has spent the last few months getting a second chance at feeling like himself again. Now he just needs someone to give him his second chance at a forever home.
