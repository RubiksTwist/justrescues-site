Meet Maple, whose favorite plans are whatever you’ve got planned!

Going for a walk? Count Maple in! Taking a car ride? She’s ready to tag along! Planning a cozy afternoon on the couch? Maple would love that too. This affectionate girl loves spending time with people and is always happy to be included.

Maple also enjoys squeaky toys, tasty treats and plenty of cuddles. She even has an adorable morning routine of rolling onto her back for a belly rub!

Whether it’s a little adventure or a quiet day together, Maple loves being part of the moment. Click the “Adopt Me” button to connect with Maple’s team in Pawling, New York.

*We encourage a $150 suggested adoption fee when adopting an animal. This adoption fee allows the ASPCA to continue our lifesaving work and help even more animals in need. We are grateful for any amount given at the time of adoption.*
