Meet Pidge!

Pidge is a happy, curious girl with a whole lot of love for life! Found as a stray, she's ready to leave her days of wandering behind and find a family to call her own. She loves exploring, sniffing out new things, and coming over for attention when you call her. Pidge has a big, bouncy personality and is always excited to see what fun lies around the corner. If you're looking for a companion to keep you smiling and make every day a little more exciting, Pidge might just be your girl!

My Ideal Home

Pidge would love a family who enjoys staying active and keeping her busy! Daily walks, playtime, and fun little training sessions will help her shine. She's still learning her manners, so she'd appreciate a family who can make learning fun with plenty of praise, treats, and patience. Food puzzles, hide-and-seek, and yummy long-lasting chews are some great ways to keep that busy mind entertained!

Good with Other Pets?

Pidge is still figuring out how she feels about other dogs. She can be a little conflicted during introductions, so she'll need slow, thoughtful introductions and a family willing to help her build positive experiences. Dog parks aren't recommended, but she may enjoy getting to know other dogs in a more controlled setting.

Good with Kids?

Pidge is a very enthusiastic girl who can get a little carried away when she's excited to meet people. Because of this, she would do best in an adult-only home or a home with children over 13 who can give her space and understand her boundaries.

Things to Know

Pidge can be a little unsure about handling and may bark when she hears someone at the door, but she does better when given time to settle in. Let her come to you at her own pace, and she'll appreciate having a family who respects her boundaries. She's also working on her leash manners and learning to keep all four paws on the ground when excitement takes over!
