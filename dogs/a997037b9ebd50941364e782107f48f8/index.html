Meet Clementine

Clementine is a little Frenchie with a big personality, a sassy side, and enough quirky charm to keep you smiling every single day.

This adorable girl was surrendered to a shelter in Georgia, and now she is ready to leave that chapter behind and find a family who will love her for exactly who she is.

Clementine is funny, loving, and wonderfully full of herself in the best possible way. She has that special kind of personality that makes you laugh without even trying. A little sass here, a quirky moment there, and suddenly, this little lady has completely stolen the show. But beneath all that spunky charm is a loving girl who deserves a family to call her own.

Life with Clementine is sure to come with plenty of laughs, lots of affection, and a whole lot of personality packed into one adorable little package. She isn't just looking for a home. She's looking for her people, the ones who will appreciate her funny little ways, adore her sassy spirit, and love her endlessly.

Please consider opening your heart and home to Clementine. This little girl has so much love and laughter to bring into someone's life, and we hope her next chapter is filled with all the happiness she deserves.
