Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

A volunteer writes:
Penny let us know right away that she did not like what was happening to her. She was quite vocal upon admission and in the medical room although she never tried to hurt anybody. Penny lived a quiet life with her family until now and for sooo many years. From one day to another, everything and everyone changed...No wonder her distress...Penny does not stay in hiding. She sits in plain sight, behind her door, ready likely to go back to her previous life. She meows, gives a little hiss but does not swat or try to bite. She welcomes some offering of wet treats that she eats with gusto, accepting pets on her neck and back and even slightly leans into pets after her meal. Penny is a thin tigress, thirteen years of age who came to us as her owner's health was declining. She is said to be a sweet, friendly and talkative lapcat who likes to stay around people. She would not be comfortable with other cats and never mingled with kids of dogs. Penny is an endearing senior cat with declawed(front paws only), already spayed and who did well on her behavior evaluation. She has quite a personality and a desire to be be a loving and beloved pet still for many years to come! Ask about Penny when you visit us at the Manhattan Care Center!
