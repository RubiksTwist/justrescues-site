Description provided by AMA Animal Rescue

Meet Russell, a silly little guy with a wiggly butt and a whole lot of love to give!

Russell is a Chihuahua mix bursting with personality and energy. He’s the kind of dog who greets his favorite humans with nonstop tail wags, happy dances, and full-body wiggles. If you enjoy a dog who’s excited to see you every single time, Russell will steal your heart fast.

Russell may experience some separation anxiety, so he is looking for a home where someone is home more often (ideally someone who has a flexible schedule or even works from home). Russell thrives on companionship and just wants to be near his people.

Russell will also benefit from a home that’s open to training. He’s eager, smart, and with consistency and patience, he has tons of potential to grow in confidence. He loves being the center of attention and would rather be the only pet in his home.

If you're interested in adopting this sweet boy, please fill out and submit an adoption application today.
