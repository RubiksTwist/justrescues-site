Meet Koozie

Some dogs come into this world knowing nothing but kindness. Others, like Koozie, have to wait a little longer to discover it.

Koozie was brought into a municipal shelter in Georgia, in heartbreaking condition. He was severely emaciated, nothing but skin and bones, and clearly in desperate need of help. We can only imagine how difficult his life must have been before someone finally stepped in. But despite everything, Koozie deserves the chance to leave those days behind and discover what it feels like to be safe, comfortable, and loved.

Just look at that face! Those soulful eyes, those adorable wrinkles, and that wonderfully squishy face are impossible to resist. And when you look at Koozie, we hope you see more than the dog who came in so desperately needing help. We hope you see the family member, best friend, and loyal companion he still has the chance to become.

Koozie's past should never define his future. He deserves a full belly, a soft place to sleep, gentle hands, and someone who will make him feel like he matters every single day.

He's already been through enough. Now, we want the rest of his story to be about all the good things he has been missing.

Could you be the person who gives Koozie his happily ever after?
