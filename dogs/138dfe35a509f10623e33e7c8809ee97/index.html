Meet Lazlo!

Lazlo is a sweet girl who is looking for a fresh start after being surrendered when her family could no longer care for her. She can be quite fearful in the shelter, but she accepts gentle petting, wags her tail, and is curious enough to explore her surroundings. Her previous family describes her as friendly and outgoing with dogs she knows, so we think there's a much more confident Lazlo waiting to emerge once she feels safe.

My Ideal Home

Lazlo would love a calm, patient home where she can take her time getting comfortable. She is a high energy girl who will enjoy daily walks, enrichment, and plenty of positive activities with her new family. Giving her time to decompress and build trust will be especially important.

Good with Other Pets?

Yes, with familiar dogs! Lazlo lived with another dog and her previous family says she is friendly and outgoing with dogs she knows. She has limited experience with unfamiliar dogs, but she showed interest in a new dog at ACC by approaching the gate and happily sniffing and following along the fence line. Any future introductions should be gradual and supervised.

Good with Kids?

Lazlo would do best in an adult only home because she is currently very fearful. She will need people who can respect her boundaries, let her choose when to approach, and give her the time she needs to feel comfortable.

Things to Know

Lazlo is a great loose leash walker and has shown no reactivity toward people or dogs while walking. She is partially house trained, so her new family should continue using positive reinforcement as she settles in. Most importantly, Lazlo should never be rushed into interactions or handling that make her uncomfortable. Give this sweet girl a little patience and a quiet place to land, and she may surprise you with just how much personality she has to share.
