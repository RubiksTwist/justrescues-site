Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

This Alabama gentleman has already been on quite the adventure! James hitched a ride on a transport, thanks to the BISSELL foundation, that flew pups out of harm’s way ahead of Hurricane Isasis, helping make room for more four-legged friends in need. Now he’s traded those stormy skies for safer shores, and we’re mighty glad this little fella landed right here!

James is curious and wants to be loved! He just needs a little time, patience, and a gentle approach to feel safe enough to let his guard down. This Southern gentleman may be bashful for now, but with the right person by his side, he’ll find his courage one small step at a time.

James is still learning the ropes, and he’ll benefit from a family who can give him continued encouragement, patience, and plenty of opportunities to build his confidence. The winds may have blown James out of Alabama, but we’re hoping love will lead him right into your heart here in the Hamptons!
