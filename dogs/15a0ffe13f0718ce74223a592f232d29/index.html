Meet Narwhal!

Narwhal is a senior gent who has spent time living life and now holds the secrets of the world.

At about 8-10 years old and a mid 55 lbs, Narwhal is arguably the best tempered boy that Every Last One Rescue has ever had the privilege of caring for.

Narwhal is respectful, affectionate, social and despite his age, his zest for life is admirable. Everywhere Narwhal goes, the world swoons over him because he is as turnkey ready as they come.

Narwhal is a welcomed houseguest in his foster home. He is incredibly polite even when he eats, keeping his space tidy. Narwhal walks beautiful on a leash, making outings enjoyable for all. Narwhal loves animals of all shapes and sizes being curious yet eventempered with both dogs and cats.

Narwhal is solidly potty trained which only adds to him being such an easygoing pup. Narwhal is also fully crate trained, happily hangs in his crate throughout the day, sleeps in his crate overnight, even sleeping in late in the mornings without extenuating or demanding needs.

Narwhal is at the stage in his life where low maintenace is the plan. Narwhal is happy to self entertain by picking toys from his toy box, partner play short stunts of tug with his humans, pop out for walks as short as long as his person desires and simply exist in a reciprocal companionship with his person.

If you are looking for a dog who was obviously loved throughout his life and has not let losing that love define him - Narwhal is the sweet boy for you.
