Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

My Boroughbreaker writes: Rosebud is a cute little shadow and would follow me around the house wherever I went. She responds well to praise and is such a good girl. She did not paw at any furniture and would only get on the couch if she was encouraged to come on it.

Rosebud is a friendly, affectionate girl who came to the shelter as a stray alongside a large male mixed-breed dog. Since arriving, she has been sweet, social, and eager to interact with staff, winning everyone over with her loving personality.

During her behavior assessment, Rosebud showed that she can become easily over-aroused when she's excited. With continued positive reinforcement, consistent routines, and appropriate outlets for her energy, she has the potential to thrive in a home that understands how to help her settle and succeed.

If you're looking for a happy companion and are willing to invest a little time in helping her channel her enthusiasm, Rosebud could make a wonderful addition to your family. Come meet this sweet girl and see if she's the perfect match for you!
