Meet Runner!

Runner is an adorable 6-month-old, 6-pound brown tabby (as of 8/26) with the sweetest little face and so much personality waiting to shine. He is a playful, curious kitten who is learning that people are pretty great—especially when Churu and treats are involved!

Runner is still building his confidence and would do best with a patient family who will give him a little time to settle in and show off his fun, playful side. He absolutely loves other cats and really enjoys having feline friends around. As he becomes more comfortable, his playful personality is coming out more and more.

Runner is also comfortable around dogs and does well sharing his space with them. He is litterbox trained and ready to find a loving home where he can continue to grow into the confident, affectionate cat we know he can be.

With a little time, plenty of playtime, and some yummy treats, Runner is sure to make a wonderful companion!

If you would like to give this wonderful cat a home, please email rescue@pupstarzrescue.org or visit www.pupstarzrescue.org for an application!
