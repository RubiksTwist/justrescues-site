Jessica Rabbit Hi! I’m Jessica Rabbit - my foster parents renamed me Jackie. I'm a nine month old brindle and white puppy who loves nothing more than romping, playing with my toys, snuggling with my foster family, and falling asleep wrapped up around my sibling puppy Jaq or with my foster dad. I was found abandoned in Georgia and taken to a shelter, but now I’m loving my life in New York. I’m currently sharing my house with 2 cats, 2 dogs, my brother Jaq, and another foster dog, and I am absolutely having the time of my life. I make my foster family laugh nonstop with my silliness, then I crawl into their laps for well-deserved naps. I love every animal who crosses my path and want immediate snuggles from every person I meet. I love to play and wrestle, know sit and stay, am good on a leash, am pee pad trained, crate trained, and getting the hang of housetraining. I've been spayed, had my rabies vaccine, and am up to date on all my shots. I’m looking to bring the same joy, sunshine, laughter , and love to my forever home that I’ve given to my foster home.
