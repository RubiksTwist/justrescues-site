Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Cetus is a 2-year-old mixed breed who weighs around 17 lbs, and he is just the sweetest little guy. He has a quiet, easy presence and isn’t one to demand attention, but he absolutely loves affection and is happiest when he’s close to his people.

He’s house-trained, crate-trained, and very polite about being handled. Cetus is the kind of dog who seems to take everything in stride and just wants to be loved. He’s still figuring out the whole leash thing and can be a little zig-zaggy on walks, but that’s a pretty manageable quirk for such an otherwise easy companion.

Cetus would be a great match for someone looking for a sweet, affectionate dog with a softer personality. He may not be the loudest dog in the room, but he has a very endearing way of quietly making himself part of your day.
