Description provided by Animal Lighthouse Rescue

Meet Axel! This handsome 4-month-old, 8-pound tri-colored wire-haired boy is an absolute showstopper—and he knows it! Wherever Axel goes, people can't help but stop and admire him, and he is more than happy to soak up every bit of attention they have to give. Axel is a happy, playful little guy who loves life and loves having fun. He'll play until he literally passes out on the floor, splash around in a kiddie pool, and wrestle with his brother and sisters until he's completely worn out. He's also busy learning all those important puppy skills, including good puppy manners and walking on a leash. They're still a work in progress, but he's eager to learn and ready to keep practicing! When the day is finally over, Axel shows off his softer side. He's incredibly snuggly and would love nothing more than to wag his little tail, curl up, and melt right into your arms after a long day. With his adorable wire-haired scruff, happy personality, and love for everyone he meets, Axel is the kind of puppy who makes every day a little more fun. We have a feeling his forever family is going to be completely smitten!

Axel is neutered, microchipped, and up to date on his vaccinations. For more information about him, please fill out an application at www.ALRcares.com and email Kate@alrcares.com. We schedule meet and greets for approved adopters only, an application is the first step in our approval process. Thank you!
