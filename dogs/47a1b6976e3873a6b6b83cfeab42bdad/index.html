Meet Crown!

Crown arrived at ACC as a stray and is a sweet, energetic pup who loves to engage with people. He can be a bit exuberant when excited, showing his affectionate nature through enthusiastic greetings.

My Ideal Home

Crown would thrive in a home with experienced guardians who can provide consistent training and positive reinforcement. He benefits from a calm and patient approach, especially when getting ready for walks. His ideal family will understand his need for continued guidance to help him channel his excitement and build good manners.

Good with Kids?

Due to his tendency to jump up and his enthusiastic play style, Crown would do best in a home with older children or adults.

Things to Know

Crown is an active dog who enjoys exploring his surroundings on walks. He is still building his leash manners and can get excited around other dogs he sees, benefiting from continued training to help him focus. He enjoys playtime, especially with a ball, and is learning to play gently. Crown is generally healthy and ready for his forever home.

Crown is available to a Level 3 adopter. His previous foster noted that he is a loving companion who is eager to learn and connect with his people.
