Meet Mocha Cabana!
Mocha Cabana is a social, enthusiastic girl who is always ready to say hello! This young pup loves being around people and has no trouble making her feelings known, whether she's bouncing over to greet you or happily leaning in for attention. She was found as a stray, so we're still getting to know her, but one thing is already clear: Mocha Cabana has plenty of personality to share.

My Ideal Home
Mocha Cabana would love an active home where she can enjoy plenty of walks, playtime, and fun adventures with her people. She's a busy girl who will benefit from enrichment like food puzzles, long-lasting chews, and games that give her something fun to focus on. A family who can help her practice calm greetings and celebrate those quieter moments will bring out her very best.

Good with Other Pets?
Mocha Cabana's history with other dogs is unknown, but she showed no response to dogs while walking at the care center. Any introductions with resident pets should still be slow and thoughtful so everyone gets a chance to start off comfortably.

Good with Kids?
Mocha Cabana would do best with older children who are comfortable around an enthusiastic dog. She's a big fan of jumping up to say hello, so a little help practicing polite greetings will be important.

Things to Know
Mocha Cabana can get pretty excited during interactions, but she's also very affectionate and loves staying close to her people. She walks with only mild pulling and happily accepts handling, especially when she can lean in for attention. With some positive-reinforcement training and plenty of outlets for her energy, this sweet girl is ready to turn every day into a little vacation.

A Volunteer Wrote: The (human) love of your life won't look at you as often, and with such soulful adoration, as this amazing girl does when she's out on a walk with you. Mocha, who has the sweetest, calmest energy, adores being pet and leans in for snuggles, she never pulls on the leash, she has zero reactivity to other dogs on the street or in the park, and she makes her walkers feel like they're the most special people on earth. That's because she is among the most special dogs! Don't miss out on this remarkable dog.
