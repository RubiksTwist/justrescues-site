🛏️ Meet BEDDING! 🛏️

Everyone deserves a comfortable place to land, and Bedding is hoping to find his forever mattress... we mean, HOME! 😂❤️

This sweet senior gentleman has mastered the art of relaxation. His favorite activities include finding the coziest spot in the house, enjoying a good nap, and keeping a watchful eye on what's happening in the kitchen.

But don't let his couch potato status fool you. Bedding has recently discovered that being close to his humans is actually pretty wonderful! He's become much more affectionate, enjoys a good rub, and is happily showing his softer, snuggly side.

And speaking of important responsibilities, Bedding has officially appointed himself Ultimate Sous Chef! 👨‍🍳 His foster mama reports that he is always politely checking in to see if there might be any available kitchen scraps. He's VERY food motivated, and although he doesn't have any teeth, he certainly hasn't lost his appetite for life (or dinner)!

Bedding is a gentle, considerate houseguest who gets along with other dogs and is especially respectful of the other pup in his foster home. He's crate-trained, doesn't shed, has no resource guarding, walks okay on leash, and handles car rides and subway trips like a seasoned New Yorker.

He's still working on his house-training, so he'll need a patient family willing to establish a consistent routine.

Bedding isn't looking for a fast-paced lifestyle, endless adventures, or a packed social calendar. He's looking for a calm home, a comfy bed, someone who appreciates a distinguished senior gentleman, and maybe a little kitchen supervision.

So if your home is missing a professional napper, a polite kitchen assistant, and a whole lot of heart, Bedding (Bramble) might just be your perfect fit! ❤️

All he needs is a soft place to land and someone to remind him that he's home for good.

If you would like to adopt this Rockstar, please visit www.pupstarzrescue.org for an application!
