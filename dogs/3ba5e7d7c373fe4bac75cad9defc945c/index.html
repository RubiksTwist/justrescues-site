Nova Star was surrendered by her previous owner due to conflict with the other resident dog in the home. Nova Star's previous family described her as a very affectionate dog who enjoyed playing with balls and squeaky toys, loved vegetables as snacks, and wanted to be anywhere her people were in the home. She is reported to have initiated several altercations with the other resident dog, which has resulted in bites to both dogs that have broken the skin and drawn blood. During her stay in care, Nova Star has been highly fearful and becomes conflicted with handlers when interacting. We recommend placement with a New Hope partner who can provide any necessary behavior modification (force-free, positive reinforcement-based) and re-evaluate behavior in a stable home environment before placement into a permanent home.
