Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

This precious Alabama girl has already been on quite a journey. Molly was flown out of harm’s way ahead of Hurricane Isasis on a special transport, thanks to the BISSELL foundation. This in turned helped make room for animals in need in the storm's aftermath. While she’s made it to safety, this little gal is still trying to find her footing in a world that feels a bit too big for her right now.

Molly may not be ready to trot right up and ask for belly rubs just yet, but some little flowers need a little more sunshine and time before they’re ready to bloom. We’re hoping that with patience and a gentle touch, Molly will begin to discover that there’s a whole lot of good waiting for her.

This sweet girl will need a calm, loving home with someone who understands that trust can’t be rushed. She’ll need time to settle in, space to feel comfortable, and a family willing to celebrate the little victories along the way.
