Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Chief is a 9-month-old mixed breed weighing around 38 pounds, and he's the kind of puppy who makes every day a little more fun. Affectionate, playful, and eager to be part of whatever you're doing, Chief has an infectious enthusiasm for life that's guaranteed to put a smile on your face.

He loves meeting people and other dogs, and while he's still learning that not every greeting needs to be quite so exciting, his friendly nature shines through in every interaction. He's already crate-trained, knows "sit," and is incredibly food motivated, which makes training both rewarding and fun. Chief is attentive, learns quickly, and has all the potential to thrive with an adopter who's excited to keep teaching him. He also gets along well with his foster sister and is always hoping she'll say yes to another round of playtime.

Like any young dog, Chief will benefit from continued training and guidance, but he's already proving what a smart, loving pup he is. With his joyful personality, athletic spirit, and eagerness to learn, he's ready to become an amazing companion for a family who will help him reach his full potential.

Registered adopters interested in Chief can email adopt@hbrdogs.com for more info. If you’re not registered yet, you can do it at hbrdogs.com.
