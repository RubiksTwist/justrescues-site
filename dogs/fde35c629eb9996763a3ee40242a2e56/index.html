Description provided by NYC Second Chance Rescue

Jackie is an energetic, sweet Husky who was heartbreakingly listed on Craigslist; given away for free, with no concern for where she might end up or who would take her. No love, no compassion. But Jackie deserves so much better, and we promised her she would never be treated as disposable again.

True to her breed, Jackie is playful, spirited, and full of life. She adores attention, thrives on activity, and has a loving heart that’s ready to devote itself completely to her person. Despite being let down by humans before, Jackie hasn’t lost her joy or her ability to trust.

Now, Jackie is looking for a forever home where she’ll be cherished, protected, and loved the way she always deserved. If you’re ready for a loyal companion and a lifetime of adventure, Jackie is ready for you.

Want to learn more? We'd love to tell you! Our bios are just a snapshot, so please reach out to our adoption team with any questions about this dog's personality, medical history, or ideal home. We'd be happy to help you determine if Jackie is the perfect match for your family.
