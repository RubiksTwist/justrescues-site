Description provided by Waldo's Rescue Pen

Iris, whose foster name is Bracket, is the sweetest little pup and a perfect starter dog. She’s playful without being overwhelming, with a low-key energy level of about 4/10.

Iris loves toys, belly rubs, and spending time with her people. She’s pee pad trained, knows “sit,” has a great appetite, and does well with anyone and everything. She’s also very low-shed and is comfortable being left alone as long as she has some toys to keep her busy.

Basically, Iris is the kind of easygoing, happy puppy who would make an amazing addition to just about any home. 🐾
