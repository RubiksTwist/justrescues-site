Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

When the term 'mans best friend" was coined, they were referencing sweet Jude. Jude is a stunning, 7 year old gentleman whose just under 50 lbs and who sort've gives off the vibes of a dalmation missing his spots. With Jude's stark white, velvety soft fur and typically squinting eyes this sweet, sensitive, empathetic boy is as awkward and endearing as they come. Despite Jude being a dog, he's more of a good human that most humans you may know. Not only is Jude an easygoing, gentle, even-tempered guy by nature, he is also a creature who thrives on deep bonds and safe relationships. When Jude is afraid you will know it and what that also means is that when Jude is safe, you will also know it. Nothing on earth is more rewarding than caring for a living, sentient being in the way they know only you can and Jude is both easy to care for and easy to love. Jude is known to swoon everyone he meets by leaning his weight into their legs and tilting his head up for scratches and likely even offering you his belly for rubs right after you give him a little time. Pretty quickly, Jude's cautious optimism will confirm you are his friend and in that moment you are his friend for life and like any good friend, he will care for you in your time of need and lean on your shoulder in his. Jude is the type of dog who will unequivocally remind you to appreciate the quiet, unassuming beauty of your daily lives - he is the epitome of a soul dog. In addition to being an extremely special, emotional dog, Jude's positive qualities are endless. Jude is fully housetrained and has already mastered his basic commands such as sit, stay, down, paw, touch, and place. Jude walks magnificently on a loose leash completely oblivious to any chaos city life could offer. He is happy and safe to be left alone in a room for up to 7-8 hours, is completely non-destructive, and does not desire a need for a crate, although he will happily eat all of his meals in his crate and is even voluntarily taking daily naps in his crate. Jude is also a studious and observant fella who is happy to spend his days enjoying enrichment by way of new puzzles or watching the city wake up by staring out the window or a local park bench. Despite being a natural outdoors lover (and an excellent running buddy!), Jude is an extremely adaptable companion who is also content to nap by your side on busy days or accompany you on your daily errands. He has been with his foster parents to the hardware store, coffee shops, and the weekend farmer's market and has been easygoing and confident to explore every new place. Jude is the type of dog whose quiet moments make you fall in love with his stable companionship. He has the power to make you wonder how you've ever lived without him - he has privileged his foster family by blossoming in front of their very eyes, and it is a privilege he will share with his forever person with true humility. Overall, Jude is an easy going, mellow, loving, intelligent guy who will make your home finally feel complete and your heart feel full. Jude is one of a kind and he would be happy to remind you how deserving you both are to share that once in a lifetime love.
