Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Meet Pumpkin! 🧡 This 22lbs little girl is the perfect family pet and she's looking for her forever! Pumpkin hasn't been in care long, but what we know, we love!

Manners🥰

  • Pumpkin is a dream on the leash! This brave little adventurer is ready to take on whatever comes her way! She's a loose leash walker and when she hears a loud noise or sees something startling, she always checks in before continuing to take the city by storm! She's also not bothered by other dogs, people, or creatures on her walk, she's much too focused on her explorations
  • Because she's so low to the ground, commands like sit and down can be difficult for her (but she's working hard on sit 😉). However, Pumpkin always comes when she's called and she's the most polite pup, never stealing food or going where she isn't allowed.
  • Pumpkin is also a crate trained queen! She's quiet as a mouse and will sleep happily in her crate until you return and she can get back to her true purpose, giving love!

People, Dogs, and Cats🐾

  • There's perhaps no dog that embodies 'man's best friend' quite as much as Pumpkin; her purpose in life is to give love and be loved! Her favorite place is on someone's lap and she greets strangers as though they were her best friends! Pumpkin loves belly rubs and butt scratches and she doesn't care where they come from, she's everyone's best friend
  • Pumpkin's friendliness doesn't stop at humans, she loves the members of the animal kingdom just as much! Pumpkin loves to play with other dogs, groom them, and nap with them; she would fit well into a home that already has dogs!
  • Cats are perhaps where Pumpkin becomes a bit more blasé, she really doesn't have strong opinions on them, she may give them a sniff or two but then she's off to bigger and better things!

Uniquely Pumpkin🎃

  • Pumpkin is a singer! When this girl has strong opinions on something her little baby 'woo woos' are sure to entertain you without disrupting the neighbors!
  • Pumpkin loves to give kisses! Whenever she is held or interacts with other dogs, she is sure to never leave an interaction without a few kisses.
  • Pumpkin relaxes against you, even when she's standing! Perhaps Pumpkin's favorite way of comfort is to stand on her back legs and rest her chin against you, she'll immediately melt into it and has even fallen asleep like that!

Pumpkin is searching for her forever family where she can achieve her life's mission of giving and receiving all the love in the world. If you think that could be you, apply now!
