Description provided by AMA Animal Rescue

Meet Mickey, a sweet little guy with big ears, an even bigger personality, and so much love to give!

Mickey's story hasn't always been easy. He was severely neglected by his previous owners and left in their backyard without food or water, covered in pounds of painfully matted hair. After being surrendered to AMA, he finally received the medical care and kindness he so desperately needed.

Today, Mickey is showing everyone his sweeter, sillier side! He's a charming little guy with adorable big ears, a playful imagination, and some pretty funny ways of entertaining himself with toys. After 13 years of neglect, Mickey understandably needs a slow, patient approach when getting to know new people. We certainly can't blame him! Give him the time and space to feel comfortable, and you'll get to see more of the sweet personality he's been waiting to share.

Mickey would love a calm, quiet home without small children. He can also get along with small adult dogs who enjoy a more relaxed lifestyle, just like him! After waiting so long for his forever family, we hope his happy ending is just around the corner.

If you have a cozy spot on the couch and room in your heart for a very special little guy, Mickey is waiting to meet you!
