Description provided by AMA Animal Rescue

Need a bit of magic to spruce up your life? Mickey here is ready to make your dreams come true! His big ears aren’t as round as his mouse counterpart, and he barks rather than squeaks, but he’s got the same whimsical spirit!

Mickey’s tale is one full of twists, turns, and touching developments. He was severely neglected by his previous owners and left to die in their backyard with no food or water, he was found with pounds of matted hair covering his body. We convinced the owners to surrender him to us and urgently helped Mickey receive medical assistance. Mickey was frightened, but he understood that we were here to aid him.

Slowly, as we've gained Mickey's trust, he's been showing a more optimistic and sunny side of him. He's very imaginative in discovering creative ways to play with toys! Mickey is still unsure of how to channel these positive, jovial vibes when he's around other animals and new people, so he would appreciate an adopter who can teach him the right ways in exchanging pleasantries with friends. He would prefer a home with no small children, with someone who can help him unwind into chill mode when his anxiety gets too springy. Mickey knows that finding your happily ever after isn't easy, but he promises as your future best pal, he'll light the way there for you! If you're ready to experience a world of magic with Mickey, please fill out and submit an adoption application today!
