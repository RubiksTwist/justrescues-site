Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Phinjamin is a bouncy, playful black lab mix with a big heart and an even bigger personality. This handsome boy arrived from Acadiana Animal Aid in Louisiana at the end of August, thanks to the BISSELL Pet Foundation and the Irving and Phyllis Millstein Foundation for Animal Welfare. Phinjamin is fun-loving, affectionate, and always ready for his next adventure. He already knows sit, down, and stay, and he’s working hard on paw and shake (sometimes with a little extra enthusiasm). ARF will cover his first training class to help him continue building great skills. He recently discovered he has allergies to cats, grass, and peanuts, so he’s looking for a home that leans more city or sandy beach than grassy countryside. With structure, patience, and love, Phinjamin will grow into an amazing companion. This silly boy absolutely loves dogs- he would do best in a home with a canine friend to play with. If you’re ready for a joyful, goofy dog who brings nonstop smiles, come meet Phinjamin!
