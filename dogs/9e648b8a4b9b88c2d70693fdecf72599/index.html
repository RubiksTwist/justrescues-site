My foster writes: Meet Judy!

Judy is a sweet girl with a BIG personality and absolutely no shortage of things to say. She's talkative, playful, affectionate, and loves being the center of your attention. She's always up for a good conversation, a little extra affection, and her favorite show: Cat TV, especially when squirrels and foxes make an appearance!

Judy isn't shy about making herself at home. You'll find her lounging on the couch, perched in the window, stretched out on the floor, or following you from room to room just to keep you company. She loves being petted and adored, and when she's had her fill of attention, she'll happily remind you that it's time to sit on the couch so she can curl up beside you for a nap.

One of the sweetest things about Judy is how quickly she warms up. Give her a little time, and before you know it, she's following you around, rubbing against your legs and arms for pets, and showing you just how affectionate she can be.

Judy has so much love and personality to give. I hope she finds a home that gives her all the affection, attention, and love she so naturally gives to everyone around her. She really is something special.

At just over a year old, sweet Judy has already come a long way! This adorable girl arrived at ACC feeling overwhelmed by all the changes in her life, but with a little patience and encouragement, she's been blossoming into the affectionate, playful kitty her former family knew and loved.

Judy's transformation has been wonderful to watch! Once a shy girl who preferred to keep her distance, she's now greeting her favorite people with soft little chirps, following them around, and leaning into pets with her tail held high. She enjoys tasty treats, loves playing with catnip toys, and has even been known to offer a few happy purrs when she's feeling especially comfortable.

Judy previously lived with many other cats and has recently made encouraging progress in ACC's free-roaming cat room.
Because Judy can be sensitive to unfamiliar environments, she's looking for an experienced cat parent who will give her time and space to settle in. Once she feels safe, her sweet and affectionate side is well worth the wait!

Judy is spayed, vaccinated, and FeLV/FIV negative. She has a history of a heart murmur, although it wasn't detected during her most recent surgical examination and her cardiac screening bloodwork was normal. Routine veterinary follow-up is recommended, with additional cardiac evaluation worth discussing with her veterinarian.

Judy has already shown just how much she can blossom when given the chance. Now all she needs is a patient, loving home where she can continue building confidence and finally enjoy the happy new chapter she deserves!
