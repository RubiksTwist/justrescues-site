Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

If you’ve ever wondered what it’s like to be loved by a cat with extra beans, Twister is ready to show you. This charming guy came to us when his previous owner could no longer care for him, and now he’s looking for a new home where he can share his big personality—and his many, many toes.

Twister is a polydactyl cat, which means he has a whole lot of extra toes. More toes = more toe beans = more love to go around. He seems to think they make him extra talented, too. Whether he’s reaching for pets, kneading biscuits, or just dramatically stretching out like the star he is, Twister makes sure those toes are seen. This silly boy is affectionate, funny, and always happy to hang out with his people. He loves attention and has the kind of personality that keeps you laughing one minute and melting the next.

Twister is diabetic, which means he will need a committed adopter to help manage his care—but don’t let that scare you! Many diabetic cats live happy, comfortable lives with a simple routine, and Twister is more than worth it. If you’re looking for a sweet, goofy companion with an impressive number of toes and an even bigger heart, Twister might just be your perfect match.
