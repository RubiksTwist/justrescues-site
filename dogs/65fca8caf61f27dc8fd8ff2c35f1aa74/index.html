Introducing Bella! Bella is a 3-year-old French Bulldog who found herself looking for a new family through absolutely no fault of her own. While she's leaving her past behind, she's ready to start the next chapter of her life with a family who will love her forever. This happy little lady has already mastered using potty pads and is doing a great job learning that the outdoors is the place to do her business. With a little patience and consistency, she'll have it down in no time! She adores kids, loves playing with toys, and has a personality that's larger than life. Whether she's zooming around the house with her favorite toy or snuggled up next to you after a fun day, she brings joy wherever she goes. Bella is the complete package! She would happily accompany you on every adventure, from coffee runs to neighborhood walks. Just be prepared to add a little extra time to your outings—Bella insists on saying hello to everyone she meets and will gladly stop for a butt rub from any willing person. If you're looking for a loyal companion with a heart full of love, a playful spirit, and enough charm to make everyone smile, Bella is ready to become your new best friend. Inquire about her today!

Bella is not spayed yet but will be shortly and is up to date on her vaccinations. For more information about her, please fill out an application at www.ALRcares.com and email Julie@alrcares.com. We schedule meet and greets for approved adopters only, an application is the first step in our approval process. Thank you!
