Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Waffle is a 2-year-old mixed breed who weighs around 52 lbs and may be a little shy at first, but once she warms up, she is all sweetness.

She loves other dogs, loves being close to her people, and would happily spend the day curled up next to you on the couch giving kisses and soaking up affection. She’s the kind of dog who wants to be nearby without making a big production out of it, just tucked in beside you and very content to stay there.

Waffle also seems to be house-trained and crate-trained. She’s affectionate, easygoing, and an absolute sweetheart who just needs a little time to show you how much love she has to give.
