Meet Snowbean!

Snowbean was brought to the shelter when his owner could no longer care for him, and this affectionate boy is ready to find someone who will adore him right back. He's got a BIG personality packed into one very cuddly package, with plenty of enthusiasm for people, playtime, and exploring his surroundings. Snowbean is basically a love muffin with a little extra zoom!

My Ideal Home

Snowbean would love an active home where he can get plenty of daily exercise, playtime, and opportunities to keep his busy brain entertained. He's an enthusiastic guy who can get carried away when he's excited, so he'll need a family who can help him practice settling down with positive reinforcement and give him appropriate outlets for all that energy. Food puzzles, long-lasting chews, sniffy walks, and fun games will be right up his alley.

Good with Other Pets?

Snowbean showed lots of interest in the other dogs around him, including play bows and plenty of tail wags, but his assessment with other dogs was inconclusive. His history with animals in a home is unknown, so introductions with resident pets should be slow, thoughtful, and supervised while everyone gets acquainted.

Good with Kids?

Because Snowbean can become overexcited very quickly, we recommend an adult-only home. He'll do best with people who can recognize when he's getting a little too wound up and help him take a break before all that enthusiasm turns into jumping, running, and general Snowbean chaos.

Things to Know

Snowbean can get very overexcited, especially when he's interacting with people, and may run around, jump on furniture and handlers, or whip his head around when aroused. During intake he was initially nervous around handlers and was observed hard staring and growling, though he became loose and wiggly as he warmed up, so he'll need people who move slowly, let him initiate interactions, and build trust with treats, toys, and praise. He's also a strong puller when he has somewhere he really wants to go, making continued positive-reinforcement training an important part of helping him become a superstar walking companion.

A Volunteer Wrote:

He closed his eyes whenever I started petting him, completely relaxing into the affection. He also seemed very eager to play with the other dogs, offering play bows and wagging his tail nonstop.
