Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

If you’ve ever wanted a sidekick who’s always up for an adventure, meet Curry! This 9 month old girl has been outgoing and curious about the world since the day she arrived, and her personality is guaranteed to make you laugh.

Curry is the sweetest love bug. She wants to be right next to you or curled up in your lap whenever she can. She’s also a smart cookie who picks things up quickly and remembers what you teach her. Inside, she’s calm and quiet, happy to lounge by your side or play a few rounds of fetch with her favorite tomato toy. She sleeps soundly through the night and hasn’t had an accident since her first day with her foster.

Out on walks, Curry is a social butterfly. She adores other dogs of all sizes, loves meeting new people, and gets just as excited to see kids. She’s still polishing her leash manners, but she checks in when you call her name and is working on her crate skills too.
