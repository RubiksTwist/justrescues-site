Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Sweet Honcho is still searching for his forever home. Originally adopted as a puppy in 2019, he was returned in 2024 and adopted again in 2025—but after 7 months, he’s back through no fault of his own. Shelter life is tough on him, and he’s ready to finally settle in for good.

Honcho is a smart, well-trained boy who knows his commands, walks nicely on leash, and is housebroken. He’s also incredibly loyal and goofy, guaranteed to keep you smiling. His favorite place is right by your side, giving kisses or resting his head in your lap.

Honcho is looking for a home where he can be your one and only. He prefers not to share his space with other pets and would do best in a quieter environment where he can truly relax and bond with his person. Honcho is a typical hound and loves to follow his nose- especially on long walks and hikes! An active owner would be best for this loveable boy.

He takes daily medication to help manage his anxiety, but once he feels safe, his loving personality really shines. If you’re looking for a devoted, fun-loving companion, Honcho could be your guy.
