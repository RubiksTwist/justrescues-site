Meet Ghost! This beautiful boy is one of the most kind-hearted souls you’ll ever meet. He loves every person and every dog and wants nothing more than to have a good cuddle on the couch.

Ghost is still a big puppy at heart with lots of energy who would love a family willing to get him out on some good walks. When he first arrived in NYC, it was clear he was experiencing so many things for the first time: being in a house with a family, walking on a leash, seeing a television, etc! In just a couple of weeks he has made incredible progress adapting to a big new world with enthusiasm. He’s smart, eager to learn, and thrives with a good routine. He's already (mostly) learned "sit", "paw", and "side"!!

Ghost is a sweet, playful, and incredibly affectionate dog looking for a loving family with the time and patience to help him figure it all out!
