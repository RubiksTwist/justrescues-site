Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Looking for a best friend for the rest of your life - look no further than Little Vicky because she is more than you could every ask for.

At about 8 months old Little Vicky is just a puppy who has shown the world what resilience really looks like.

Little Vicky lost her family when they couldn't afford her vet care and found herself alone in the city shelter looking for her forever. The Every Last One rescue teamed laid eyes on Little Vicky one time and knew this girl was as special as they come!

Immediately upon arriving at her foster home, Little Vicky proved us right. She is nothing short of a joy to be around and her sensitive timid yet playful nature is the most wholesome thing you may ever see. Little Vickys sunny energy is infectious to both humans and other dogs and silliness and sweet cuddles will keep you both smiling and swooning over how absolutely precious she is.

Little Vicky is also super smart! She has mastered potty training and her sit command like a champ. Little Vicky is tailoring her young lady leash manners and despite a almost fully healed hip injury, Little Vicky is eager to learn and train on leash and always open for any guidance her handler offers.

Little Vicky's love language is being cozied up especially in her crate which she absolutely loves to just hang out in, sleeps in throughout the day and overnight, brings her tous to play in or simply enjoys some solo time.

At just a few months shy of a year old, Little Vickys life is just starting and honestly, you would be silly to not let her life start with you!
