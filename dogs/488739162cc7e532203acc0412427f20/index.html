Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Saying farewell to summer is much easier with sweet June by your side as the colder months roll in!

At 10 months old, sweet June moves through the world with a contagious zest for life. She is a young, happy, energetic give who loves to take in all the world has to offer and all of the experiences she is offered.

June, who was initially a shy girl, has blossomed beautifully. During her time in foster care June has shown us that she loves her people deeply and there is not much she would not do to learn from them and make them happy.

June is fully housebroken, she knows the commands sit, down, with me and is close to mastering her leave it command. June is also extremely pleasant outdoors, walking confidently on a leash with manners and listening skills far beyong her young age. June is known for checking in with her handler consistenly on walks to ensure the time spent is pleasurable for all involved.

June's silly side comes out during playtime and its such a joy to watch. June is a playful girl who loves fetch, tug of war and zoomies with other dogs. In fact, June currently lives with 3 foster dogs and 2 foster cats all of whom are her best friends.

June's idea of independent play is enjoying all kinds of enrichment during crate time - such as bones, chews and even frozen lickmat. Additionaly, June is happy to sleep in her crate overnight however won't miss the opportunity to inform you that your lap is her preferred resting spot during day time hours.

June is a little girl who wears a lot of hats! June is a best friend, she is an adventure buddy, a caring companion, a welcome comedian yet more than anything, June is a soul dog. If you are searching for the missing piece of your heart to remind you how beautiful life can be and how much purpose we truly have in this world, look no further, you've found your soulmate in sweet June.
