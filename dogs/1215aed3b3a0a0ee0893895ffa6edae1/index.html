Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

Bluey is a really special dog. Definitely mostly cattle breed, so incredibly intelligent and a lot of fun. She's still got that puppy energy and loves to play. She bonded with her foster family immediately; she is fiercely loyal and loving. She appears to be good with other dogs she encounters in the park and on walks. She is good with commands like sit, down, stay and she's great at cross walks and stopping. She does still pull a lot on the leash, we are working with a trainer to improve this. Her personality is playful and sweet, she loves to cuddle on the couch and checks in constantly with her people. She wants to be where the action is! Bluey will thrive with a family that appreciates her high energy and need for physical and mental stimulation. Love, training and attention will allow her to become the incredible dog she is meant to be.
