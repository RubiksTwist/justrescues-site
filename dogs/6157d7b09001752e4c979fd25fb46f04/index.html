Description provided by Waldo's Rescue Pen

Effie is an incredibly sweet, cuddly, playful, and loving 6-month old puppy. She’s obsessed with snuggling and will paw at you to ask for affection. She also loves to play, and has that fun puppy energy! Effie can be shy, but is quickly getting used to being outside. She’s quickly gained confidence with her leash, doors, and stairs, and she’ll thrive with somebody who can help her get even more comfortable with the world around her. She’s been going on walks with her big sister (dog) and getting more brave with them! She’s happy being in her crate for naps and bedtime, and sleeps through the night without a problem.

If there are three words to describe Effie, they are: playful, spunky, and affectionate!

Favorite Things: Snuggles, chewing on her bone, playing with her sister!

Temperament: Effie is great with other dogs! She loves to play with them, but is just as happy snuggling together.

Potty training: She’s still learning to go potty outside, and getting used to pee pads.

Shedding: Effie is low-shed and super soft! She does great in the bath.

Energy Level: 7. Effie loves to play, but she adores snuggles more than anything. Once she’s settled in your lap, she’s happy and calm. She’s learning independent play and is great with grabbing a toy or a bone by herself. She’s also good at fetch and retrieval! She’ll do well in an active house.

Noise level: Effie is learning that she can bark, but exercises her voice pretty rarely!

Walking Manners: Effie is quickly getting used to her leash. She doesn’t pull, but she can be a bit nervous! She’ll be great once she gets more comfortable.

Appetite: Effie has a great appetite and eats regularly, but isn’t food motivated. She’ll have a treat if offered, but doesn’t seek them out. She’s still teething, so she loves chewing on toys and bones.

Commands/Tricks: Effie can sit, but doesn’t quite know the command yet!
