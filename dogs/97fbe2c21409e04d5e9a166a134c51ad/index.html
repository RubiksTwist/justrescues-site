HISTORY

Khaleesi entered a Georgia shelter as a stray in early September 2026. She spent time in a foster home in Georgia before traveling to Brooklyn in mid-October 2026.

DETAILED PROFILE

Khaleesi’s first encounter with her foster’s apartment stairs did not go well. She refused, so she was carried up and down. The next day she tried a few steps, and now she bounds up so fast her legs can’t keep up: gangly, awkward, and enormously pleased with herself.

She’s a Great Pyrenees puppy, which means plenty of fluff now and a lot more dog later. She’s goofy, clumsy, gentle, and attached to her people, with moderate energy and a sharp mind. She picked up “sit” almost immediately, answered to her nickname within days, and is now working on “down” and sitting at crosswalks. She’s still settling in, so more of her personality will keep emerging.

People are a highlight. She tries to get everyone she passes to say hello and was perfectly polite with a service worker in the apartment, though she did bark once at a bearded visitor. She’s playful with every dog she meets outside and did well living with dogs in her Georgia foster home. Indoors she shares space with two cats, following them around out of curiosity before mostly leaving them alone. Her experience with kids isn’t known yet.

On the practical side, she seems largely house trained, with one accident chalked up to timing. She’s happy in her crate with treats and settles at night as long as she can see someone, but she cries when left alone in another room, so alone time is still a work in progress. She’s pretty good on leash, though birds, squirrels, and potential playmates can inspire some excitement.

She’d like a home with a rope toy, a stuffed Kong, and a person in view at all times. Stairs optional; she’s got those handled now.
