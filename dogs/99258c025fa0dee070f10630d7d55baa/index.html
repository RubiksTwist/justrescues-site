Meet Bishop!
Bishop is a young, social guy who is ready to find his way back into a home of his own. He loves checking out his surroundings, happily approaches people, and has a nice loose body when he's feeling comfortable. He's currently being treated for a skin condition, so he's looking for someone who will give him a little extra patience while he gets back to feeling his best.

My Ideal Home
Bishop would love an active home where he can enjoy daily walks, playtime, and plenty of fun ways to keep his young mind busy. Food puzzles, long-lasting chews, and little training games would be right up his alley. He's a nice walker who doesn't pull, making him a great buddy for getting out and exploring together.

Good with Other Pets?
Bishop's history with other dogs is unknown, and he wasn't able to complete his dog assessment. His new family should take introductions slowly and thoughtfully, giving him plenty of time to get comfortable with any potential dog friends.

Good with Kids?
Bishop would do best in a home without young children while he is recovering from his skin condition and getting more comfortable with handling. He's a sensitive guy right now and will appreciate people who let him choose when he's ready for extra attention.

Things to Know
Bishop can be a little sensitive about his collar and certain kinds of touch, so gentle handling and positive reinforcement will help him feel comfortable. He may also need a little encouragement when things around him get noisy or overwhelming, but once he's out and about, he's a happy explorer who enjoys being with his people. This handsome young pup has plenty of potential, and we're excited to see him feeling his best and showing off even more of his personality.
