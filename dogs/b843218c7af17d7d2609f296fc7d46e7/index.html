Description provided by PupStarz Rescue

Meet Watermelon Candy, a 4.5-month old, 4-pound (as of 10/5) soft, sleek, black kitten who gets along beautifully with other cats and dogs. He’s lovely around humans too, but just requires a little time to get over his initial shyness. Once he trusts you, he’s all about pets, cuddles, and caresses. Awww!

Watermelon prefers a calmer-type energy level, rather than the rough-housing sort, but even so, when the feeling is right, this little rascal will enthusiastically run around with other cats, play chase, swat tails, and perform acrobatic feats. Once that’s out of his system, Watermelon will find a sunny spot to nap in. Done…it’s naptime now…see ya!

Watermelon’s favorite toys are furry mice, springs, and feather wands. He’s great at entertaining himself and just hanging out quietly when the mood calls for mellowness. This growing guy is a good eater, an excellent sleeper, and always manages to find the coziest place on the couch to curl up on…preferably right next to you!

Fully litter-box trained, Watermelon has good aim, keeps his bathroom area clean, and strives to keep his gorgeous coat in tip-top condition. He’s an all-around sweet, gentle, and kind-hearted kitty who will be a great addition to any feline-friendly home.

If you would like to adopt this wonderful cat, please email rescue@pupstarzrescue.org or visit www.pupstarzrescue.org for an application!
