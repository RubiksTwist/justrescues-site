Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Dolly Llama is a 3-year-old chug who weighs around 12 lbs and believes personal space is mostly a suggestion. He wants to be near his person pretty much all the time. He’s very sweet, and just a little ridiculous in the best way.

He does well with other dogs and is still working on some of the basics, including house-training, crate-training, and leash skills. Dolly Llama will need a patient adopter who is willing to put in the time and help him keep learning, but in return you get a funny little shadow who is very committed to being part of whatever you’re doing.
