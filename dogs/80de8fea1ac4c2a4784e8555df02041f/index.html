HISTORY

Bridesmaids came into a shelter in Georgia as a stray in August 2026 and was the shelter’s longest resident by the time our team visited. She traveled to Brooklyn in mid-October 2026.

DETAILED PROFILE

Bridesmaids has discovered mirrors, and she is a fan. She’ll pose for photos, gaze happily at her own reflection, and generally carry herself like the star of the picture. Between photo shoots she wants one thing: attention, from absolutely everyone, ideally in the form of belly rubs.

She’s a goofy, friendly young dog with moderate energy. She’s still settling into Brooklyn, so more of her personality will show as she gets comfortable. Her favorite pastime is chasing old soccer balls around her foster’s fenced backyard, and she took her first trip to the groomer in stride.

People are her specialty. She loves meeting new folks and has done well with school-age kids; younger kids are still to be determined. With other dogs she’s curious but sometimes a little hesitant, so calm introductions are the way to go. Her experience with cats isn’t known yet.

The practical stuff looks promising. She’s crate trained and hasn’t had an accident in her foster home. Leash manners are a work in progress. She wants to stop and smell everything, which can turn a walk into a negotiation. An adopter ready to keep up leash practice will suit her well.

Bridesmaid duty is officially over. Her plan now is to be the one in the frame.

MEDICAL HISTORY

The dogs in our care are up-to-date on vaccinations, spayed or neutered (when old enough), microchipped, and given a general health check. Immediate medical needs and medical history will be addressed with the adopter prior to adoption. Complete vet records will be sent shortly after adoption.
