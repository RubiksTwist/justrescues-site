Meet Loki!
Loki is a sensitive girl who is still finding her footing after coming to ACC when her owner could no longer care for her. New situations can feel a little overwhelming, but when she's comfortable with someone, she shows a sweet side by leaning in close and even pressing into laps for comfort. She's also shown us that she has a playful side with other dogs, enjoying games of chase and wrestling with her canine friends. Loki may be keeping things low key right now, but she's hoping to find someone who will give her the time she needs to shine!

My Ideal Home
Loki would love a calm, quiet home where she can settle in without feeling rushed. Her perfect person will be patient and gentle, celebrating the little milestones as she grows more confident. Positive reinforcement, tasty treats, and plenty of opportunities to explore at her own pace will help her feel more secure. She's looking for someone who understands that building trust takes time and is happy to let her set the pace.

Good with Other Pets?
Loki has shown a playful side with other dogs at ACC, enjoying chase, wrestling, and playful bows while also taking breaks appropriately. We don't know how she would feel about sharing a home with another dog, so slow, thoughtful introductions will be important. A patient family can help her get to know potential furry friends without putting too much pressure on her.

Good with Kids?
Loki would do best in an adult only home where she can enjoy a calm environment while she builds confidence. She's still getting comfortable with new people and experiences, so she'll appreciate having plenty of space and the freedom to approach others when she's ready.

Things to Know
Loki can feel very nervous on walks and may need plenty of coaxing and patience before she's ready to move. She can be tense during handling, even when accepting contact, so it's important to let her approach on her own terms and never force her into interactions. With a peaceful home, gentle encouragement, and lots of positive reinforcement, Loki will have the chance to blossom into the wonderful companion she's waiting to be.
