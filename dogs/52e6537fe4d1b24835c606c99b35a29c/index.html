Point Break and his siblings were surrendered to an overcrowded shelter in their golden years because their owner moved and could not bring them along. The first thing youll notice about him is his calm and loving nature. Nothing much bothers him! He will glue himself to any welcoming person, any chance he gets, and watch the world go by their side. He gets along nicely with other dogs, though he prefers snuggling to playing.

Point Break is fostered in Pleasantville, NY and is available for local or out-of-state adoption as long as the adopter can travel to meet him.We can also bring him to NYC for meet-and-greets. To get the adoption process started, please send us an application at https://bit.ly/rosr-adopt-foster.
