Meet Fuchsia, a gentle sweetheart with a whole lot of love to give!

With her beautiful light tan eyes and happy wiggles, Fuchsia has an adorable way of greeting her friends. She loves being around people and will happily stay close, sometimes placing a paw on you to ask for a little extra attention.

Fuchsia has also enjoyed socializing with other dogs and is always happy when tasy treats are involved. Cheerios are one of her favorites!

Looking for a sweet companion to spend your days with? Click the “Adopt Me” button to connect with Fuchsia’s team in Pawling, New York!
