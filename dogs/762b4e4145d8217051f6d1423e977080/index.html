Meet Diana! 🐾

Diana is a sweet medium-sized mixed-breed girl who was found as a stray and is now discovering how wonderful it is to have people looking out for her. She has warmed up quickly to both staff and volunteers, showing just how loving and adaptable she can be.

Diana is a food lover, so tasty treats are definitely one of the ways to win her heart! And with her adorable personality and the cutest ears, she's pretty hard to resist.

Diana is ready to leave her stray days behind and find a loving forever home where she can continue to blossom. Come meet this sweet girl and see if Diana might be your perfect match! ❤️

My Boroughbreaker writes: If you are looking for a gentle, affectionate companion, Diana is your girl. I got to take her out of the shelter for the day, and I fell in love immediately. She is a total hugger-she'll stand right there and lean her body against you the moment you wrap your arms around her.
She was so well-behaved the entire day. When we got home, her chest and paws were muddy, but she stood completely still while I washed her off, looking so relieved to be clean! She's potty trained (walked to the door every time she needed out), unfazed by noisy neighbor dogs, and loved exploring every new room and corner of the yard. She is the perfect size and has so much love to give. She was nervous when she first got in the car, but she had no problem on our way back to the shelter. I think she was just nervous because she has never met us before this day.

My Boroughbreaker writes: Diana is such a sweet girl. She is pretty easy on the leash, does tug a bit when she see's other dogs, but not too bad. Once out of sight it's out of her mind and she carries on. She is very gentle with treats, could use training on sitting etc. She behaves very very well in the car, if I didn't tell my mother she was there she wouldn't have noticed until she seen her. There really is not anything negative I can say about her. Everyone that spent time with us during the day fell in love with her. What's not to love about her. Please consider Diana! If I could, I definitely would!
