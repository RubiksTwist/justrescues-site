Meet Beans N Toast!
Beans N Toast was found as a stray, and this handsome guy is ready to find a home where he can feel right at home! He's a curious boy who loves sniffing around and searching for tasty treats. He's happy to approach people and follow along on adventures, and with his social personality and love of snacks, he's got all the ingredients to become someone's best friend. Who's ready to bring home their favorite breakfast buddy?

My Ideal Home
Beans N Toast would love a home where he can enjoy daily walks, fun adventures, and plenty of quality time with his people. He's a young, enthusiastic guy who will benefit from food puzzles, long lasting chews, and games like hide and seek to keep his mind busy. A family who enjoys helping him explore the world and learn new things will be a great match for this curious boy.

Good with Other Pets?
Beans N Toast's history with other dogs is unknown since he came to us as a stray. He recently met another dog through a barrier and remained relaxed, but the other dog was too nervous to complete a full greeting. Slow, thoughtful introductions will help Beans N Toast get to know potential furry friends at a comfortable pace.

Good with Kids?
Beans N Toast would do best in a home without children under 5. He's still getting comfortable with new experiences, so an older household can give him the space and time he needs to settle in.

Things to Know
Beans N Toast walks on a loose leash and happily jogs along with his handler. He's easily tempted by treats and enjoys exploring his surroundings, though he can be a little unsure of handling at times. Giving him gentle encouragement and letting him approach interactions at his own pace will help him feel comfortable. This sweet guy is ready to find his perfect match, and we think life with Beans N Toast could be the best thing since sliced bread!
