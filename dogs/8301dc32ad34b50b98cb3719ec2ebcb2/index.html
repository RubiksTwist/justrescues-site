Meet Pip!

Pip is a sweet Georgia rescue who is ready to begin the most exciting journey of his life, finding a forever family!

This adorable boy is still discovering the world around him and is looking for someone who will be there to help him learn, grow, and become the best dog he can be. He's got all the wonderful potential that comes with puppyhood and is ready for a lifetime of adventures with his people.

Pip will need the love, patience, training, and guidance that every young puppy deserves. But with the right family by his side, we know this little guy has everything it takes to grow into an incredible companion.

His past may have started in Georgia, but his forever is waiting for him right here.

Come meet Pip and let him steal your heart!
