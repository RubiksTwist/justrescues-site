Meet Bogart!

Bogart is a sweet lady who thrives in an environment that’s as gentle as her soul. That is, unless a toy mouse is around! Bogart loves to hop around, playing with her stuffed mice. She’s great at solo play and can keep herself entertained.

Bogart is an explorer at heart. She’s very curious about enclosed spaces. If you open a cabinet, Bogart would love to do an inspection and get as many sniffs as possible. The same goes for the refrigerator. It is a marvel to behold and smell!

Bogart’s foster parent says that Bogart loves curling up near her humans. In the shelter, she’s been fond of cheek and head scratches. She’s a loving little girl looking for the peaceful life.
