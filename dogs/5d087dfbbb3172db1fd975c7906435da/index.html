Pre-Screener Form

Crystal is on the at-risk list due to behavior concerns. In the care center, Crystal has shown difficulty returning to her kennel, including repeatedly balking, refusing to walk, and attempting to move away from the kennel room. She has also been observed biting and pulling on her water bowl while kenneled. Outside of these moments, Crystal is easy to leash, walks calmly with a loose leash, accepts petting, and explores her surroundings with a neutral body. Medically, Crystal is overweight but otherwise healthy.

Crystal was surrendered to the care center by her previous owner due to their housing situation. Her previous owner describes Crystal as a social, affectionate, calm dog who is very loving towards her family, and enjoys running, most toys she is given, and bones. Crystal has not acclimated well to the care center environment, and has shown fearful behavior, in particular balking on leash, thrashing, and vocalizing when being returned to kennel. In calm settings with known handlers, she has shown social behavior, leaning in for pets. Due to her fearful behavior, we recommend placement with a New Hope partner who can provide any necessary behavior modification (force-free, positive reinforcement-based) and re-evaluate behavior in a stable home environment before placement into a permanent home.
