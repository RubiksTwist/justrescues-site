Meet Nellie!

Nellie is a young, active girl who was found as a stray, but luckily her finder got to see a sweeter side of her. They reported that Nellie was friendly with both them and their dog from the very first meeting and even spent the night in their home. At ACC, she's been enthusiastic about meeting people and loves soliciting attention, so she's ready for a family who can help her channel all that excitement into good things.

My Ideal Home

Nellie is looking for an active family who will keep her busy with walks, play, enrichment, and positive training. Puzzle feeders, food dispensing toys, long lasting chews, and fun games will help give her energetic brain something productive to do. She's still learning her manners, so a family who enjoys helping a young dog learn will be a great match for her.

Good with Other Pets?

Nellie's finder reported that she was friendly with their dog during their time together, which is a promising start. At ACC, however, she has shown significant frustration when passing dogs in kennels, so she'll need careful management around other dogs while on leash. Any future dog introductions should be slow and supervised.

Good with Kids?

Nellie would do best in a home with children over 13. She's an enthusiastic girl who can jump up and use her mouth when excited, so older kids who understand how to give her space and help reinforce good manners will be the best fit.

Things to Know

Nellie can be a little mouthy and jumpy when she's excited and is still working on her impulse control. She's also shown some discomfort with handling, so her family should take things slowly and always give her the choice to step away. With force free, reward based training and plenty of patience, this energetic girl has all the makings of a fun and loving companion.
