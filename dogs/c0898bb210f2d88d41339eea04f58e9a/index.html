About: Meet Haewang | Apply Here | Videos Haewang is a 2 year old and 30lb leggy girl ready to find love. She was found as a wee little puppy along with her mom chained up outside, so she practically grew up at our center in Bundang! This girl is all that and a bag of chips - she’s definitely the type of girl you’d take home to meet the family. Haewang is a dream on her walks, being mindful to stay close and sniff leisurely as she strolls through the town. At home, she’s learning to play with her foster sibling, but also enjoys playing with toys or just taking a good long nap. She is also eligible for our Jindo Journey training stipend! This means her adopters could receive $300 towards their training package to help make the new transition smooth and keep everyone happy long term.
