Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Scooter may be a senior, but this sweet boy still has plenty of pep in his step. After the passing of his beloved owner, Scooter found himself back at ARF, and now he's hoping to find another loving family to call his own. Scooter is a gentle, sensitive soul who takes a little time to warm up to new people, but a few treats and a little patience go a long way toward earning his trust. At 9 years young, he knows his commands and walks beautifully on a leash. Because loud, busy environments can make him feel uneasy, Scooter would thrive in a calm home where his gentle nature is understood and respected. With kindness and consistency, his affectionate personality quickly shines through. If you're looking for a loyal companion with a kind heart and plenty of love left to give, Scooter is ready to meet you.
