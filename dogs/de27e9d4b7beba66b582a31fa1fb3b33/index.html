Meet Butterburr!
Butterburr is a charming, affectionate sweetheart who has two favorite things: being close to his people and getting cozy on the couch! Found as a stray, this handsome boy is ready to leave shelter life behind and find a family to shower with love. He's playful and full of personality, but he also knows how to slow things down for some serious snuggling. With Butterburr around, you'll never be short on cuddles!

My Ideal Home
Butterburr would love a home where he can enjoy daily walks, fun adventures, and plenty of quality time with his favorite people. He's an enthusiastic boy who will benefit from enrichment, food puzzles, and games to keep him busy, but don't forget to save room on the couch for him, too! His dream day probably involves a little exploring, lots of belly rubs, and a cozy nap with his family.

Good with Other Pets?
Butterburr came into care with two other female dogs, and during a recent introduction, he showed off his playful side with another dog. The two enjoyed bouncy play, and Butterburr took breaks before happily rejoining the fun. He can get excited when he sees dogs while on leash, sometimes fixating or barking, but he responds well to redirection. Slow introductions and positive reinforcement will help him make new friends while practicing his walking manners.

Good with Kids?
Butterburr's history with children is unknown. His affectionate nature makes him a lovely boy to get to know, and calm, respectful introductions will help everyone feel comfortable.

Things to Know
Butterburr can pull a little on leash and may get distracted by other dogs, but he can be redirected with some encouragement and rewards. A front clip harness and positive reinforcement training can help him practice keeping his attention on his person. He's a social, loving boy who truly shines when he gets to relax with someone he trusts. This butter soft sweetheart is ready to melt right into his forever family's heart!
