Meet Doodle!

Sweet Doodle is ready to put her past behind her and start the next chapter of her life!

Rescued from Georgia, this adorable girl is now looking for a family who will give her all the love, guidance, and attention she deserves. Like any puppy, Doodle is ready to learn about the world, make new memories, and discover all the fun that life has to offer.

She's got a whole lot of puppyhood ahead of her and is looking for someone who is ready for the cuddles, playtime, training, and adventures that come with raising a young dog. In return, Doodle promises a lifetime of love and companionship.

Her future is wide open, and we can't wait to see just how wonderful it will be. Could Doodle be your new best friend?

Want to learn more? We'd love to tell you! Our bios are just a snapshot, so please reach out to our adoption team with any questions about this dog's personality, medical history, or ideal home. We'd be happy to help you determine if Doodle is the perfect match for your family.

All dogs will be altered, microchipped, and receive age-appropriate vaccines prior to adoption.
