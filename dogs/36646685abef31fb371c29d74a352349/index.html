Meet Stanley! ❤️

At around 1–3 years old and 21 lbs, Stanley is a little guy with a big personality, an even bigger heart, and quite possibly the cutest underbite you've ever seen!

When Stanley is comfortable at home with his people, he's just about the sweetest boy you could ask for. He's a fun-loving, playful pup who especially loves his favorite tug toy, but he also knows how to take it easy. Whether he's entertaining himself with a toy or snoozing nearby while you go about your day, Stanley is happy just being part of the action. He's a great little companion who enjoys being close to his people, often finding a spot right beside you (or even on you!) to settle in for a nap. He's also done well with some time alone and settles nicely overnight after a nice day together. He's shown no signs of resource guarding, hasn't demonstrated any destructive chewing, and is great at communicating when he needs to go outside.

Stanley also loves getting out for walks! He's a nice loose-leash walker who enjoys taking in all the sights and smells and exploring his surroundings. His ideal walking environment, however, is a little different from the typical city sidewalk. Stanley can become overwhelmed when encountering unfamiliar people on leash, and isn't afraid to let people know he needs space! Because of this, we think Stanley would really flourish in a quieter, less populated area where he can enjoy his walks and outdoor adventures without the constant stimulation of busy sidewalks and frequent encounters with strangers.

A home outside of the city with a fenced-in yard would be an absolute dream for Stanley! Having a safe space to sniff, explore, play, and romp around would give him the opportunity to enjoy the outdoors on his own terms. He'll also need an adopter who is willing to continue working with him using patience and positive reinforcement as he builds confidence in new environments.

We're still getting to know Stanley's compatibility with other dogs, but based on his time at the shelter and what we know so far, we don't have any reason to believe he couldn't live with another dog.

Stanley is a pretty wonderful little package: playful when it's time for fun, happy to snooze when it's time to relax, and always eager to be near his people. He's looking for a home that appreciates all of these things and can offer him the quieter lifestyle where he can feel comfortable, confident, and truly be himself.

If you've been looking for a sweet, silly little sidekick with an adorable underbite and a serious love for tug-of-war, Stanley might just be your guy! ❤️
