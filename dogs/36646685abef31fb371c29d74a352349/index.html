Description provided by Every Last One Rescue

Meet Stanley! ❤️

At around 1–3 years old and 21 lbs, Stanley is a little guy with a big personality, an even bigger heart, and quite possibly the cutest underbite you've ever seen!

When Stanley is comfortable at home with his people, he's just about the sweetest boy you could ask for. He's a fun-loving, playful pup who especially loves his favorite tug toy, but he also knows how to take it easy. Whether he's entertaining himself with a toy or snoozing nearby while you go about your day, Stanley is happy just being part of the action. He's a great little companion who enjoys being close to his people, often finding a spot right beside you (or even on you!) to settle in for a nap. Stanley is also an excellent houseguest! He sleeps wonderfully through the night and is just as happy to enjoy a lazy morning snuggling with you as he is to get up and start the day. He's done well with some time alone, and is non destructive.

When it comes to other dogs, Stanley is a total rockstar! He's great with his canine friends and would absolutely love a doggy sibling to play with. Having another playful pup around could be a wonderful addition to his new home.

Stanley also loves getting out for walks! He's a nice loose-leash walker who enjoys taking in all the sights and smells and exploring his surroundings. He's been doing fantastic in quieter, suburban environments, where he can enjoy time outside, play in the yard with other dogs, and explore without the hustle and bustle of city life. Stanley can become overwhelmed when encountering unfamiliar people on leash, and isn't afraid to let people know he needs space! We think he'd flourish in a quieter neighborhood, ideally with a fenced-in yard where he can sniff, explore, play, and enjoy the outdoors at his own pace.

Stanley is a pretty wonderful little package: playful when it's time for fun, happy to snooze when it's time to relax, a great friend to other dogs, and always eager to be near his people. He's looking for a home that appreciates all of these things and can offer him the quieter lifestyle where he can feel comfortable, confident, and truly be himself.

If you've been looking for a sweet, silly little sidekick with an adorable underbite, a love for tug-of-war, and plenty of love to give, Stanley might just be your guy! ❤️
