Meet Andromeda! 🌙

This beautiful 8-month-old, 7-pound (as of 8/20) Bombay mix may be small, but Andromeda—affectionately known as Andi—has plenty of personality!

Andi is a sweet and loving girl who enjoys a good chin scratch and head pets, but she’s also an independent lady who likes affection on her own terms. She’ll happily curl up next to you in the morning, and she’s a big fan of sleeping in—making her the perfect companion for anyone who doesn’t want to be woken up at the crack of dawn by a demanding breakfast crew!

Andi is playful and can entertain herself for hours. She has never met a cat teaser she didn’t love and will happily chase and play, whether you’re holding it or have it attached to a wall or door handle. She’s also great with other cats and enjoys wrestling, playing tag, or simply curling up nearby for a nap.

Andi is not a fan of dogs. She isn’t one to run away, though—she’ll happily let them know she’s the boss! For that reason, she’d probably do best in a dog-free home.

Andi is gentle and patient with her people, even during carrier time or nail trims, and her foster hasn’t seen any aggression toward humans.

If you’re looking for a playful, sweet, and independent little companion who can entertain herself but will still curl up beside you for some quality time, Andi may be your perfect match!

If you would like to give this wonderful cat a home, please visit www.pupstarzrescue.org for an application!
