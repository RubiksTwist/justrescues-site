Description provided by Waggytail Rescue

Bitsy is a 9-year-old, 6-pound Chihuahua/Terrier mix from New York City, and she is ready to become the tiny love of your life.

Bitsy is sweet, happy, snuggly, and convinced that she was put on this earth to be carried around in your purse. She’s settling beautifully into foster life and her foster family has quickly discovered her favorite things: belly rubs, cozy naps, leisurely walks, and being wherever her people are.

This sweet girl is ready to be fall-fashion icon rocking tiny sweaters, peeking out of a kangaroo pouch hoodie, and accompanying you on every adventure. She brings major ride-or-die energy in a six-pound package. Frankly, Bitsy deserves to be chauffeured around in a Birkin.

Despite her princess-sized stature, Bitsy is wonderfully well-mannered. She appears to be house-trained and reliably uses pee pads or goes outside. In fact, she’s so committed to her pee-pad routine that she’ll often take care of business while you’re still making your morning coffee, allowing everyone to ease into the day at their own pace.

On walks, Bitsy is an absolute dream. She strolls politely by your side, stops when you stop, and frequently checks in to make sure you’re both on the same page. She enjoys taking in the sights and smells of the neighborhood, but she’s equally happy to keep moving if you’ve got places to be.

Bitsy is continuing to build her confidence and has made wonderful progress in foster care. She’ll thrive with a patient family that provides a predictable routine and gentle encouragement as she settles into her forever home.

When she’s not out exploring the world, you’ll likely find her curled up in her beloved donut bed, perfecting the art of relaxation. She takes her culinary interests very seriously and is a devoted fan of beef treats and chicken. If there’s a beef stick involved, Bitsy suddenly becomes a highly motivated professional athlete. Bitsy has recently begun discovering her playful side, though she’s still a bit suspicious of squeaky toys. We suspect she’s only a few brave moments away from becoming a toy enthusiast.

She also comes equipped with what may be the cutest, funniest sneeze in the world. Whether she’s excited, wiggly, or simply expressing an opinion, her tiny dramatic sneezes have a remarkable ability to make people laugh and fall in love with her on the spot.

Bitsy would do best in a quiet, low-key to moderately active home. She appreciates a nice balance of downtime and adventure. She could be perfectly happy as your one-and-only dog or alongside another respectful canine companion. She would also be fine with children; she’s not known to nip and would most likely choose to politely excuse herself from any chaos by finding a cozy hiding spot. Otherwise, she is incredibly easy to handle and enjoys affection from her trusted humans.

If you’re looking for a loyal companion, professional cuddler, expert lap warmer, and tiny best friend, Bitsy may be your perfect match.

Vaccinated for rabies, DHPP, and bordetella, heartworm negative, spayed/neutered and microchipped. Current on flea, tick, and heartworm prevention.
