Meet Rogue!

Rogue is a sweet and affectionate dog who arrived at ACC when her owner could no longer care for her due to housing insecurity. She has a wonderfully steady, self-assured personality, and volunteers describe her as calm, independent, and polite, with a sweet side. She loves to lean into staff for affection and will even put her paws on a leg to ask for more pets.

My Ideal Home

Rogue can feel unsure in new situations and does best with a calm, patient approach. She is described as active and enthusiastic, needing daily mental and physical activity to keep her engaged. Long-lasting chews, food puzzles, and hide-and-seek games are recommended to positively direct her energy. She is partially house-trained and benefits from continued training to build calm behaviors. Rogue has some ongoing medical needs that staff are happy to discuss.

Good with Other Pets?

Rogue can get worked up around other dogs on leash and benefits from continued training. She has also shown concerning behaviors towards other dogs and cats, so she may do best as the only pet in the home. She is not recommended for dog parks.

Good with Kids?

Due to her fearful disposition and need for contact on her own terms, Rogue would do best in a home with no children under 13.

Things to Know

Rogue is very treat-motivated and takes treats gently. She knows many tricks, including sit, paw, other paw, and down. She can pull moderately on the leash and benefits from continued training to build good leash manners. Rogue has general anxiety and does not like to be left alone.
