HISTORY

Kung Fu Panda was found as a stray in Georgia and entered a local shelter with his sister in early June 2026. He traveled to Brooklyn in mid-October 2026 and boarded for several weeks before moving into a foster home.

DETAILED PROFILE

Kung Fu Panda has mastered exactly one discipline so far, and it’s cuddling. He’ll curl up in your lap, spoon you, or simply fall on top of you for the best nap in the house. Between adventures, he wants to be as close to his people as possible.

He’s a goofy, friendly puppy with moderate energy and a big appetite for exploring. Everyone he meets is a potential new friend, and he’s been very sweet with the kids he’s met. Like most puppies, he greets people by jumping up, and he’s been caught eyeing the countertops, so polite manners are part of his current curriculum. Hand him a sturdy chew and he’ll entertain himself for hours. Naps are non-negotiable; an overtired Kung Fu Panda is a crankier one.

That shows up with other dogs, too. He passes and meets dogs on walks politely and did well at a daycare introduction, but at home he’s gotten grumpy with his foster’s dog a couple of times, usually when he’s overtired. A future dog sibling will need some patience and should be ready to do some big-sibling coaching while he learns his manners. He’s very interested in birds and rodents, so a home with cats would need a successful cat test first.

The early training report is encouraging. He’s comfortable in his crate, settles quickly when left, and naps there on his own. So far there have been no accidents indoors. On leash he’s pretty good with some corrections, though birds test his focus and sirens and trucks can still make him a little nervous. He knows sit and is learning to use it everywhere.

He’s still new to Brooklyn, so more of him will come into focus. Adopters should plan on puppy training, plenty of naps, and a very warm lap.

MEDICAL HISTORY

The dogs in our care are up-to-date on vaccinations, spayed or neutered (when old enough), microchipped, and given a general health check. Immediate medical needs and medical history will be addressed with the adopter prior to adoption. Complete vet records will be sent shortly after adoption.
