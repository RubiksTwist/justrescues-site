HISTORY

Kung Fu Panda was found as a stray in Georgia and entered a local shelter with his sister in early June 2026. He traveled to Brooklyn in mid-October 2026 and boarded for several weeks before moving into a foster home.

DETAILED PROFILE

Kung Fu Panda has mastered exactly one discipline so far, and it's cuddling. He'll curl up in your lap, spoon you, or simply flop on top of you for the best nap in the house. Between adventures, he wants to be as close to his people as possible.

He's a goofy, friendly puppy with moderate energy and a big appetite for exploring. Everyone is a potential new friend, he's been very sweet with the kids he's met, and strollers and wheelchairs don't faze him. Like most puppies, he jumps up to greet people and has been caught eyeing the countertops, so manners are part of his current curriculum. Hand him a sturdy chew and he'll entertain himself for hours. Naps are non-negotiable.

Other dogs are a growing strength. He meets dogs politely on walks, is earning rave reviews at daycare, and has settled in nicely with his foster's dog after a few grumpy early moments. He did growl once at an unfamiliar dog at a busy event, so thoughtful introductions are still smart. He's very interested in birds and rodents, so a home with cats would need a successful cat test first.

The early training report is encouraging. He loves his crate, settles quickly when left, and naps there on his own. Indoor accidents have been rare, though an excited dribble still happens now and then. On leash he's pretty good with some corrections; birds test his focus, and sirens and trucks can still make him a little nervous. He knows sit, down, and off, and that sit is already cutting down on the jumping.

He's still new to Brooklyn, so more of him will come into focus. Adopters should plan on puppy training, plenty of naps, and a very warm lap.

MEDICAL HISTORY
