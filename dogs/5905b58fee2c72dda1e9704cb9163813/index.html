Meet Bellamy! ❤️

At 2 years old, Bellamy is the definition of an easygoing good boy. He loves lounging around the house, enjoying his treats (he’s VERY food motivated!), and coming over for sweet head-butts when he’s in the mood for some affection. 🥹

Bellamy gets along with other cats and even small dogs! But he’s also perfectly happy being your one and only, soaking up all the human love while entertaining himself with plenty of toys.

Bellamy loves to drink water straight from the sink faucet! Why drink from a perfectly good water bowl when you can have fresh running water?

If you’re looking for a relaxed, affectionate, easygoing companion with a silly side, Bellamy might just be your guy!

Come meet Bellamy and give this sweet boy the forever home he deserves! ❤️
