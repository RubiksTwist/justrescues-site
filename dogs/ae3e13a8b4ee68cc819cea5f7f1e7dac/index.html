Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Pinto is a handsome gray tabby with a story that reminds us just how much love a cat can bring into a home. He and his feline friend, Chief, were adopted together as kittens and spent the next four years happily loved by their owner. Sadly, after the loss of their owner, Pinto has found himself back at the shelter, through no fault of his own.

After knowing the comfort and security of a loving home, Pinto deserves another chance to find a family to call his own. He has already experienced what it means to be loved, and we hope his next chapter will be filled with all the same warmth, happiness, and companionship.
