Description provided by NYC Second Chance Rescue

Kalahari has been an incredible mother to her two precious babies, Mojave and Sahara. Rescued from Mississippi, this devoted mama has spent so much of her life caring for others, and now it is finally her turn to be cared for. Kalahari is gentle, loving, and incredibly deserving of a family who will shower her with the kindness and security she has always deserved. She has overcome so much while protecting her babies, and now she is ready for a future filled with love, comfort, and peace.
