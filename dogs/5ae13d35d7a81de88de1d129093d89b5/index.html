Pixie is at risk due to behavioral reasons. While in care, Pixie has been observed to be fearful with handlers. Note to have a tense body and pinned back ears. During her assessment, Pixie did not allow any handling and sought exit throughout the interaction. With familiar handlers, Pixie has a low tag wag and accepts contact. Medically, Pixie came in underweight. Recently she received treatment for CIRDC and diarrhea, both are now resolved. Pixie has been gaining weight and should continue to be encouraged to do so.

Pixie arrived at MACC as a stray with no known history. While in care, Pixie has been observed to be highly fearful. Noted to have a tense body, pinned back ears, and whale eye. During her assessments when a handler attempted to interact with her, Pixie became tense and frozen. Due to the detailed behavior, we recommend an experincved adult only home.
