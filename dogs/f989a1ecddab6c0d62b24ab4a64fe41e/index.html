Meet Robert! Robert is a fun-loving cuddle bug who wants nothing more than to snuggle by your side and eat peanut butter. He has adjusted well to big city life and does pretty well on the leash. He's learning not to tug and is very treat motivated! He had a couple of accidents in the house on his first day (pee only) but since has been getting plenty of walks and doing plenty of business. Robert is the perfect dog for apartment living as he gets all his energy out chewing on his toys and strolling around the block then is happy to be blissed out on the sofa snoring (quite loudly!) for the rest of the day. If you're looking for a cute and compact companion, Robert is the guy for you.

﻿Robert is approximately 2y 0m 8d old and is fully grown at 24 pounds. They are microchipped and up to date on all age-appropriate medical care. Their breed is unknown as we have not had a DNA test done.
