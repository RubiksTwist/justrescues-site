Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

Meet Isak, the sweetest pup of the pack! He is a very mild-mannered, gentle boy that wants nothing more than to be cuddled up next to you every chance he gets. In fact, better than next to you, his preference is to be on you. Be it on your lap, belly, or even perched atop your chest, Isak wants to be connected and eagerly receives pets and kisses. He is as quiet as a church mouse, not even barking at the door if someone rings or knocks. But, he will bark once or twice with excitement as you're making his food. Isak is very curious about other dogs and doesn't display any aggression. Rather, he wags his tail in excitement at the site of other 4 legged friends like him. In fact, he made instant friends with my resident dogs (both Pomeranians) at introduction happily walking together in unison.

While Isak is brave boy and walks well on leash, he is a very trepidatious about his surroundings often pausing to look around and behind himself while walking. He also pauses to look at oncomers and make sure they keep their distance, and with a little gentle coaching and praise he gets back on track.

﻿Isak is approximately 2y 2m 24d old and is fully grown at 8 pounds. They are microchipped and up to date on all age-appropriate medical care. Their breed is unknown as we have not had a DNA test done.

Although our dogs are listed as unknown breed, buildings with specific dog restrictions typically enforce their rules based on what breeds a dog appears to resemble. It's always important to keep that in mind and know your building's policies before adopting a dog.
