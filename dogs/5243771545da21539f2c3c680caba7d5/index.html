Oh Scruffy! From the moment we met him, he has been the sweetest boy. Since coming into our home, he’s been slowly coming out of his shell, showing us more and more of his sweet personality each day.

Scruffy loves going for walks, playing around, and, most importantly, cuddling! 🥹 If you’re lying down, don’t be surprised if he scoots closer and closer until he’s practically on top of you. Personal space? He doesn’t know her! 😂 He just wants to be close to his people and soak up all the love he can get.

He’s a calm, gentle soul who has been quiet in the house and has shown no reactivity toward other dogs we’ve encountered on walks. He’s also quite the professional sleeper, so much so that it can take him a little extra time to notice someone coming through the door! 😂💤
