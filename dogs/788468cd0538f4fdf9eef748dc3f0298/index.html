Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Oreo is a 5-year-old doodle who weighs around 46 lbs and has the whole city dog thing pretty much figured out. She’s sweet, mellow, incredibly polite, and has settled into city life like she’s been doing it forever.

She’s house-trained, crate-trained, and a fantastic walking buddy, whether you’re heading out for a quick stroll or a longer walk. Sometimes she even grabs her own leash to give you a little help along the way! Oreo loves people, is friendly with every dog she meets, and is always happy to be around her humans, but she’s equally comfortable relaxing in her crate or on her designated spot when it’s time to unwind.

She’s also great in the car and has a very low-shedding coat, making her a potentially good fit for people looking for an allergy-friendly companion. Oreo is an easygoing, affectionate girl who’s ready to make herself right at home with her new family.
