Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Balyhoo is a very special puppy with a whole lot of personality packed into one adorable little body. Found as a stray in Maryland, this sweet boy made his way to New York in search of the loving home he deserves.

Due to a believed congenital neurological condition, Balyhoo has a wonderfully goofy walk that is impossible not to smile at. He can be a little unsteady on his feet and is more prone to taking an unexpected tumble than the average puppy.

Balyhoo would thrive best in an adult-only household where his parents understand the needs of a rescue pup with an unknown past. This sweet boy is looking for a patient and understanding adopter who is willing to work on training and structure.

If you’re looking for a puppy who will make you laugh, melt your heart, and remind you that being a little different can be pretty wonderful, Balyhoo may be your guy!
