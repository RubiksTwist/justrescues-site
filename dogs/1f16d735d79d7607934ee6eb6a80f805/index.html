Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Balyhoo is one wonderfully unique puppy with a huge personality and an unforgettable look. Found as a stray by a friend of ARF, this sweet boy is ready to find someone who will see just how special he is. Balyhoo has a suspected congenital neurological condition that gives him a delightfully goofy walk and can make him a little unsteady on his feet. The neurologist believes he may grow out of it as he matures. In the meantime, he is simply busy being his happy, lovable, one of a kind self. Balyhoo would do best in an adult only home with a patient adopter ready to give him the structure, training, and understanding he deserves. If you’re looking for a puppy who will make you laugh, melt your heart, and remind you that being a little different can be pretty wonderful, Balyhoo may be your guy!
