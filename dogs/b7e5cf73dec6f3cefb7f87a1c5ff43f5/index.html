Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Orsino is a 4-year-old mixed breed who weighs around 24 lbs and comes with a truly excellent pair of butterfly ears.

He’s house-trained, very sweet, and always happy about the possibility of making a new friend. Orsino greets people politely and calmly, soaking up attention without making a whole production out of it. Dogs are a different story, mostly because he gets so excited to see them and wants to head straight over, so he’s working on keeping those emotions in check when the moment calls for it.

He’s an affectionate little guy with a lot of love to give and a very cute face to deliver it with.
