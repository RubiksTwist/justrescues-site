Meet Matlock (aka Mady)! 🐾

Mady joined the household after her original foster had to drop out — and with a litter of pups just a month younger already at home, it was an easy yes. Unlike her foster siblings, who were born in foster care, Mady was picked up as a stray down in Georgia along with her own littermates. She's a lovely chocolate brown with a longer face, and she's highly food motivated — a trait that makes her a natural when it comes to training.

While she'll happily play with her foster siblings, Mady's true love is snuggling — with them or with her foster parents. She has the sweetest habit of walking up to you on the couch and leaning her whole body into you, like she's giving you a hug. Add in a love of scratches and belly rubs, and she's about as content as a pup can be curled up close.
