Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

My foster writes: Meet Natasha, a 4-year-old sweetheart with a heart full of love and an adorable way of asking for affection! This lovely cat takes her head scratches very seriously, sometimes even standing on her hind legs to meet your hand halfway! She'll happily weave between your legs, rub against you, and then flop onto her back to ask for even more pets. Her favorite spots for scratches are her head, cheeks, and neck.
Natasha may be a little shy when exploring unfamiliar places, but give her a quiet space and a little patience, and her sweet personality will quickly shine through! She's curious, friendly, and even enjoys meeting new people. She was already asking for pets on her very first day in foster care! She's incredibly gentle, has never hissed or swatted at anyone during her foster stay, and is wonderful with kids!
While Natasha enjoys short play sessions with wand toys, she might even surprise you with a game of fetch! Most of the time, though, she'd rather soak up affection, especially if Churu treats are involved! She prefers keeping all four paws on the ground rather than being picked up.
Since it's unknown whether Natasha has ever lived with other animals, we're looking for a home where she can be the only pet. She would thrive in a loving home where she can feel safe, comfortable, and adored.
