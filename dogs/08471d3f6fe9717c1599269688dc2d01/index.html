Coco Couture is on the at-risk list due to behavior concerns. In the care center, Coco Couture has shown intense fearful and defensive behavior, including shaking, hiding at the back of her kennel, flattening herself against the kennel wall, growling, and snapping at handlers during kennel care. Coco Couture continues to struggle to acclimate to the shelter environment, and interactions have been kept brief to avoid increasing her stress. Medically, Coco Couture is apparently healthy.

Coco Couture was surrendered to the care center by her previous owners due to their housing situation. They describe Coco Couture as an affectionate, active, and playful dog who loves water and knows several cues like sit, jump, and paw. Coco Couture has not acclimated well to the kennel environment and has allowed only minimal handling since intake. She has been extremely reluctant to leave her kennel, flattening herself against the back when handlers have attempted to leash her. While she seems to choose to avoid or retreat when given the opportunity, she has growled when interacting with staff and her previous owners also report that she is fearful of unfamiliar people, and if she does not hide may lunge, snap, and growl. For these reasons, we recommend placement with a New Hope partner who can provide any necessary behavior modification (force-free, positive reinforcement-based) and re-evaluate behavior in a stable home environment before placement into a permanent home.
