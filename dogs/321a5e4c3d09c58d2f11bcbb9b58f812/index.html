Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Originally adopted as a puppy, this sweet boy has been waiting at ARF since 2024 for another chance at a home of his own. A recent foster stay showed just how special Oden is outside the shelter. His foster describes him as a perfect gentleman with impeccable manners who settles easily into a routine. He patiently waits for his meals, keeps you company while you work, and quietly stays by your side. He loves his walks, whether exploring the neighborhood or sniffing out all the latest news at the beach. His foster shared, he really is “the cutest, sweetest boy in the world.” Oden would thrive as your one and only pet in a calm home with a fenced yard to explore. He can be unsure around other dogs on walks, so he needs someone patient who will help him feel safe. His foster so perfectly put it, “He really is a total star.” Oden has so much love to give. All he needs is a chance to shine in a home of his own.
