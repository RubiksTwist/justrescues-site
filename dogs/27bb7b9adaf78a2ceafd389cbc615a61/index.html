Meet Jasper! Professional Couch Potato, Neighborhood Stroll Expert, and Full Time Best Friend! 🐾

If you've been searching for a dog whose greatest adventures involve finding the comfiest spot on the couch and taking walks around the neighborhood, Jasper is your guy!

Jasper is a big, lovable boy who has never met a stranger. Everyone is his new best friend, and if you happen to have a dog who enjoys playing, even better! Jasper absolutely loves doggy friends who are ready for a game or two before everyone settles in for a nice nap.

Jasper is deaf, but don't let that fool you, he's incredibly smart and eager to connect with his people. With hand signals, patience, and lots of positive reinforcement, he's ready to keep learning and thriving.

What Jasper wants most isn't nonstop adventure, it's you. He's a true velcro dog who wants to be wherever his people are. Whether you're working from home, watching TV, reading a book, or just relaxing, Jasper will happily be by your side... or more accurately, on your lap. (He hasn't quite gotten the memo that he's not a tiny lap dog!)

Jasper would do best with a family or owner who is home often and can give him the companionship he craves. In return, you'll get a loyal shadow, an endless supply of cuddles and a best friend who will love you with his whole heart.

If you're ready to share your couch, your heart, and maybe a little bit of your personal space, Jasper is ready to be your forever friend. ❤️
