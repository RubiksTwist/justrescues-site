Description provided by AMA Animal Rescue

Lilly is a small but mighty Yorkie with a heart full of love. This sweet girl was found alone on the street, bravely carrying a large mass on her belly. Thankfully, she’s since had surgery to remove it and has made a wonderful recovery. Now, she’s ready to start the next chapter of her life in a loving home.

Lilly loves playtime, enjoys the comfort of her cozy bed and soft toys, and is especially happy when she’s out exploring — whether she’s riding along in her stroller or walking calmly on a leash like a pro. She’s also house-trained and knows to go potty outside.

City life doesn’t faze this curious girl. Busy streets, and the general hustle and bustle? No problem. Lilly stays calm and enjoys taking in the sights and sounds around her. She’s also shown interest in other small dogs and may enjoy having a gentle companion.

Lilly can be a little shy when meeting new people and is still learning that being picked up is safe. She might hesitate at first, but it’s clear she wants to build trust. With patience and kindness, she gradually comes out of her shell — and once she feels comfortable, her sweet and playful personality starts to shine.

If you’re looking for a loving, resilient companion who’s ready to bring joy to your life, Lilly could be the perfect match. Come meet this special little lady and let her melt your heart.
