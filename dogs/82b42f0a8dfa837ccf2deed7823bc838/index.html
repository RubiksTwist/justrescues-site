Description provided by ASPCA Adoption Center

Meet Honeybee, a gentle and brave soul that will wiggle her way into your heart!

Honeybee is as sweet as her name, with crinkly ears and deep soulful eyes that are sure to draw you in, even from afar. Once, you actually meet Honeybee, her big smile will melt your heart.

Our favorite thing about Honeybee is her resiliency and how much love she has to give. Every person she meets is greeted with a full body wiggle to show just how excited she is to have companionship. She absolutely loves full body pets and will lean her entire body weight into your legs for more before flopping over for belly rubs.

With her favorite people, she likes to jump up for hugs and shower you with kisses. While people are Honeybee’s favorite thing in the world, plushies are a close second favorite and in her world the bigger the better! She likes to run around with her plushies and show each person, wagging her entire bottom half with excitement throughout. Eventually, she likes to settle down in a cozy spot and will suckle on the plushie to soothe herself and eventually fall asleep.

Outside, Honeybee is a delight to walk and enjoys a calm stroll where she can take in the scents around her and show off her tricks such as sit and paw for a tasty treat. Honeybee has so much love to give, and she would be the perfect companion for anyone that wants a loyal and affectionate dog to enjoy their days with.
