My Foster writes: Hi, I'm Oak!
I'm a sweet, curious girl with endless bouts of energy. I can be a little shy at first, but once you earn my trust, I come right out of my shell. Every morning, I'll greet you after waking up with a big stretch, then come over with a wiggly, vibrating butt for endless head bumps and my favorite scritches on the side of my neck. I love following the Swiffer around like it's my job, and I get random bursts of zoomies where I'll chase absolutely nothing across the room. I'm also a dedicated hunter! Feather toys are my favorite catch, and once I've got one in my mouth, I am not letting go. I'll also do the most adorable bunny kicks ever. When playtime's over, I turn into a tiny shrimp, curled up in a ball for the coziest naps.
I love following my person around, but once I've had my fill, I'll usually keep a respectful foot of distance since that's my comfort zone for now. But I'll always find a comfy spot close by, and I'm always eager for another round of playtime! One thing to know about me is that I can still spook if you move too quickly near me, so I'd do best with a patient person who lets me set my own pace. I'm doing great with my litter box habits, and love a good Churu snack and cardboard scratcher box
