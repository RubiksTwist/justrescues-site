Description provided by NYC Second Chance Rescue

Meet Saffron, a gentle, affectionate girl from North Carolina whose future was uncertain due to shelter space limitations. When her life was at risk, we stepped in and gave her the safe place she deserved.

Saffron loves people, soaks up affection, and blossoms with every bit of kindness she receives. Now that she’s safe, she’s ready for the next chapter: a forever home where she can love and be loved.

If you’re looking for a warm, loving companion to brighten your days, Saffron is your girl. She’s waiting to meet you!

All dogs will be altered, microchipped, and receive age-appropriate vaccines prior to adoption.
