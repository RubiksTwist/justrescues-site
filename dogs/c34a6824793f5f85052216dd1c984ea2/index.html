Meet Jordan

Sometimes, life takes an unexpected turn, and through no fault of their own, our beloved pets find themselves needing a new place to call home. That's what happened to Jordan. This sweet little boy was surrendered by his owner when changing circumstances made it difficult to give him the time and attention he deserved.

Making the decision to say goodbye is never easy, but Jordan's next chapter is waiting to be written. Just look at that adorable face! With his fluffy white coat, sweet expression, and those precious little paws, he's impossible not to fall for.

Jordan deserves a family who has the time to include him in life's everyday moments, give him all the love he deserves, and make him feel like an important part of the family. Sometimes all a little dog needs is a second chance and someone who can promise to be there for the long haul.

His previous family couldn't give him the time he needed, but maybe you can give him the forever home he's been waiting for.

Could Jordan be your new best friend?
