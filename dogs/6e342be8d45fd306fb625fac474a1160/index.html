Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

Pete is a spunky, lover boy who enjoys nothing more than to be by your side. Originally from Queens ACC, Pete has settled right into his foster home and loves to follow his foster mom all around like her little shadow. He loves to be around his people and is thrilled when new people come over to visit him. Pete loves exploring new places, taking long neighborhood walks with plenty of sniff breaks, and investigating everything the world has to offer. He loves to sit at the park and watch the world go by and graciously lets anyone and everyone come over to pet him, young and old! He’s great with children at the park, going slowly and learning alongside them. He is working on potty training (but it’s going great so far!), knows “sit”, “paw”, and “down”, loves other dogs, and is nearly a perfect walker. Pete is a beautiful pup (he has the most gorgeous eyes!) looking for his forever family who he hopes will have an endless supply of treats!

﻿Pete is approximately 4y 0m 14d old and is fully grown at 48 pounds. They are microchipped and up to date on all age-appropriate medical care. Their breed is unknown as we have not had a DNA test done.
