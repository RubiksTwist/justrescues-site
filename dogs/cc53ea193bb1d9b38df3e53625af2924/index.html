Meet Munchkin!

Found as a stray, Munchkin is a quiet and independent girl who enjoys taking in the world at her own pace. She likes exploring interesting scents and calmly checking out her surroundings, and she's perfectly happy doing her own thing while she gets comfortable. This petite pup may need a little time to warm up, but there's something special about letting her personality unfold at her own pace!

My Ideal Home

Munchkin will do best in a calm, patient home where she can take her time getting settled. She'll appreciate having the freedom to explore her new surroundings without pressure and a family who understands that trust is built one small step at a time. Let Munchkin set the pace, and she'll have the opportunity to become more comfortable and confident in her new home.

Good with Other Pets?

Munchkin's history with other animals is unknown, and she was fairly reserved during her time in care. Any future introductions should be slow, thoughtful, and supervised, allowing her plenty of space to decide when she is ready to interact.

Good with Kids?

Munchkin would do best in a home without children under 5. Young children may feel a little overwhelming for her, so she'll appreciate older, respectful family members who understand how to give her space and let her approach when she's ready.

Things to Know

Munchkin walks with mild pulling and is comfortable remaining still during handling, but she is not yet a social butterfly with new people. She should never be pressured to interact, and instead should be given the choice to approach and initiate contact when she feels comfortable. For an adopter who appreciates a quieter companion and is willing to let trust grow naturally, Munchkin could be a wonderful little friend.
