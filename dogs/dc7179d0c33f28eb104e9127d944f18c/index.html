Description provided by Hearts & Bones Rescue

Meet Cassie, a sweet 10-month-old retreiver mix weighing 30 pounds. With her oversized ears and coat pattern that gives her the perpetual appearance of wearing an off-the-shoulder top, this little lady is as cute as they come. Cassie is described by her fosters as the quintessential family dog. She has been a great sibling to her human foster sister and is sweet as can be with her foster pawrents. She walks well on a leash and has taken to her crate like a champ. Cassie is working on being a confident pup when meeting new people, but always maintains her sweet nature. If you're looking for a gentle, playful and curious companion, Cassie is the perfect puppy pal for you!

Registered adopters interested in Cassie can email adopt@hbrdogs.com for more info. If you're not Registered yet, do it today at hbrdogs.com.
