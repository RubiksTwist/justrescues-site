Meet Argus aka Danny

Argus has been through a lot, and he’s still waiting for the forever home that’s truly right for him.

This handsome young boy came to us from Georgia after spending months neglected in a backyard alongside his siblings. He was adopted after coming into our care, but unfortunately, the placement didn’t work out, and Argus is now back with us and looking for his forever home.

Argus is sweet, affectionate, goofy, and incredibly playful with the people he trusts. He loves toys, running, exploring, and being close to his favorite people. He’s also a smart boy who has worked hard on his training and has shown just how wonderful he can be in calm, structured environments.

Argus is looking for an experienced, patient adopter in a quieter setting, ideally outside the city, where he can enjoy a predictable routine and plenty of space to decompress. With the right person and environment, we believe Argus has so much love and happiness to give.

He’s been through enough. Now he just needs the right person to give him the fresh start he deserves. Could that person be you?

Want to learn more? We'd love to tell you! Our bios are just a snapshot, so please reach out to our adoption team with any questions about Argus's personality, medical history, or ideal home. We'd be happy to help you determine if Argus is the perfect match for your family.
