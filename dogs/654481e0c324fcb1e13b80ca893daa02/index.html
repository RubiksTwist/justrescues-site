Meet Chai!

Chai is a little puppy with a big story.

His journey started when his mama was rescued as a pregnant stray. Sadly, shortly after giving birth to Chai and his siblings, their mom passed away in the hospital after suffering from severe mastitis and complications. Left without their mother, these puppies needed someone to step in, and their foster family in Alabama did just that.

Chai and his siblings arrived needing care after battling a severe flea infestation, but these little ones showed incredible resilience. With plenty of love and attention, Chai grew into a happy, playful puppy ready to experience all the wonderful things life has to offer.

Now this sweet boy is under our care and is searching for a forever home where he can be loved, spoiled, and cherished. Chai is ready for puppy adventures, belly rubs, and a family who will help him grow into the amazing dog he is meant to become.

If you’re looking for a sweet companion with a brave little heart, Chai might just be your perfect match.
