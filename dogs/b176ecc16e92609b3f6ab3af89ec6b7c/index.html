Description provided by Animal Rescue Fund of the Hamptons

Poppy is a beautiful young Australian Shepherd/German Shepherd mix looking for a very special home that understands her needs. She is incredibly intelligent, affectionate with her people, and has been making excellent progress while working with ARF's trainers. To help her continue succeeding, we'll provide her adopters with ongoing training at our facility at no cost.

Poppy is a high-drive dog who thrives on structure, routine, and consistency. She is looking for an experienced, adult-only home with someone who is committed to continuing her training and helping her build confidence.

Poppy isn't the type of dog who will enjoy busy outings, crowded beaches, or spending the day exploring town. She is happiest with a predictable lifestyle, clear expectations, and an owner who understands working breeds and appreciates the rewarding process of helping a smart dog reach her full potential.

For the right person, Poppy will be an incredibly loyal, devoted companion. If you're looking for a dog to train, bond with, and make part of your daily routine, Poppy may be your perfect match.
