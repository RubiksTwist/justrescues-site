Leo (Leonardo) — 1-year-old, 10 lb Chihuahua mix, NYC

With his fox-like face and big personality, Leo is a young dog who's already been through more than his share — bounced between foster placements due to availability, then two surgeries in under a week. It's left its mark, but with the right person, Leo has everything it takes to become a devoted, exceptional companion.

Leo's love language is physical touch — if it were up to him, he'd be glued to your side 24/7. That affection is real and deep, but it comes with a catch: because of what he's been through, Leo can be skeptical of handling until trust is earned, and he has a tendency to resource guard things he values — furniture, laps, and his people. This isn't him being "bad" — it's a trauma response, and one that improves with consistency, structure, and a handler who knows how to set calm, confident boundaries.

Leo needs an adopter who:
🐕 Has experience with boundary-setting and structure (not a first dog, and not a "let him win" household)
🏠 Can offer an only-animal home — Leo does best without other pets to compete with
🧸 Won't be swayed by his adorable face into skipping the training he needs
❤️ Has patience for a dog who needs to build trust before he shows his full, snuggly self

What's already going great:
🚶 Walks like a champion — no pulling, no reactivity
🧻 Fully potty trained, no accidents, no pads needed
📦 Settles well in his pen after a brief protest
🎓 Working hard on his commands and improving daily

Leo isn't food motivated, so winning him over takes patience, not treats — but once he trusts you, he becomes the affectionate, snuggly gentleman his foster family already sees glimpses of. He does best without young children in the home, as he can be easily startled.

Leo isn't the right fit for every home — but for the right one, he'll be everything.

Vaccinated for rabies, DHPP, and bordetella, heartworm negative, neutered and microchipped. Current on flea, tick, and heartworm prevention.
