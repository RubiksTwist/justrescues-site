Meet Silly Goose!
Silly Goose was found as a stray, and this young girl is ready to flap her way into someone's heart! She can be a little shy when first meeting someone, but once she starts to feel comfortable, her affectionate side comes out. She loves leaning in for attention and being close to her people. With a name like Silly Goose, you can probably expect a few goofy moments along the way!

My Ideal Home
Silly Goose would love an active home where she can enjoy daily walks, playtime, and plenty of fun adventures. She's a young girl with lots to learn, so food puzzles, long lasting chews, and little training games will help keep her mind busy. She'll also benefit from positive reinforcement training to practice polite greetings and learn that keeping all four paws on the floor can be just as rewarding as jumping up for attention.

Good with Other Pets?
Silly Goose's history with other animals is unknown, but she showed no reaction to other dogs during her assessment. Any introductions with resident pets should be slow and thoughtful to help everyone get comfortable.

Good with Kids?
Silly Goose would do best in a home without children under 5. She's an enthusiastic girl who can get bouncy when excited and sometimes uses her mouth during interactions, so she'll benefit from a family who can help her practice gentle greetings and give her appropriate outlets for her energy.

Things to Know
Silly Goose walks with only mild pulling and can warm up to new people with a little time and encouragement. Once she feels comfortable, she enjoys attention and may lean right in for more pets. She's still learning how to manage all that excitement, but with patience, consistency, and tasty rewards, she's ready to spread her wings and become someone's very best friend. Life with Silly Goose is sure to be a little silly in the sweetest way!
