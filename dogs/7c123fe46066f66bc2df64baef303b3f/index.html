Description provided by Muddy Paws Rescue

Gloria, or Gigi as we call her, is pretty much perfect. A Native New Yorker, Gigi was found as a stray in Brooklyn and made her way to the ACC before coming into foster care with Muddy Paws. You would never know she had a rough start though. She is sweet, affectionate, cuddly, silly, playful, tolerant, and curious, and she genuinely loves everyone she meets. She has never met a stranger and seems to assume that every new person is just another friend she hasn’t gotten to know yet. And somehow, everyone who meets Gigi, loves her right back.

She is always excited to head outside and explore and is ready to be part of whatever adventure comes her way. But as much as she loves getting out and about, her absolute favorite place is curled up on the couch next to her person.

Gigi is one of those dogs who is really easy to fall for. She has such a sweet, happy personality and just wants to be around her people. After starting out as a Brooklyn stray, she has definitely earned her happily ever after. We have a feeling whoever gets to take her home is going to feel pretty lucky!

﻿Gloria is approximately 0y 7m 19d old and is fully grown at 42 pounds. They are microchipped and up to date on all age-appropriate medical care. Their breed is unknown as we have not had a DNA test done.
