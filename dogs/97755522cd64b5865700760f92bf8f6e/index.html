Ansel loves his humans and is a very loyal friend!

Ansel loves having his people to go on walks with him and give him yummy treats! When it comes to his humans, Ansel is a friendly little love muffin. He solicits pets every chance he gets.

You can try to ignore his cute little face as he leans into you for pets, but we don’t think you’ll be very successful. We give in every time! Who can say no to this sweet boy?

Come meet Ansel at the Adoption Center during walk-in hours (daily, noon to 5 p.m.), or click the “Adopt Me” button to learn more!
