Cindy - 3 years & 17lbs, Chiweenie, Spayed Please click here to see vidoes of sweet Cindy! Our Team Says: Cindy was treated for Heartworm. Unfortunately it can be common in PR - where she was rescued from. But all of the hard work has been done. She just needs to stay UTD on her prevention, which is recommended for all pups anywyas! Her foster says: "From the moment she arrived, it's been clear that Cindy genuinely loves being around people and wants to be part of whatever you're doing. She's not especially cuddly or the type to climb into your lap, but she absolutely wants to be near her people. If you're working from home, she's perfectly content curling up at your feet while you work, quietly keeping you company. She has a calm, comforting presence and seems happiest just being close by. With that said, when you want to get her excited she's all for it and is ready to play! She's an adaptable little adventurer who has already explored so much—from the dog beach to busy city streets and the waterfalls at the park—and she has loved every new experience. She's incredibly curious and loves meeting other dogs. While she's a bit shy during those first introductions, she actively seeks them out and is steadily growing more confident with each new encounter.Watching her settle into this new life has been so rewarding. Every day she seems a little braver and a little more comfortable." This dog is eligible to join our foster-to-adopt program. Up to date on their DHPP, Bordetella, and Rabies vaccines; heartworm tested if coming from Tennessee or Puerto Rico; spayed/neutered; microchipped; treated with dewormer upon intake How did this dog end up with Social Tees? Most of our dogs come via partner groups who help us save them from shelters where they are at risk of euthanasia in Tennessee, Los Angeles, and Puerto Rico. There are very few spay/neuter resources there and many more animals in those areas than there are people to adopt them. Rescue groups like ours bring them to the northeast where the desire for pets like these allows us to save their lives.
