Robinson is a 5 Month Old 30lb Husky/Lab Mix who is gentle, cuddly, loving and literally couldn’t be any sweeter. He’s not destructive, his potty training is going well, and he greets all dogs and humans like they’re his long lost BFFS. He’s curious and playful outside and especially interested in other dogs - he would ideally LOVE to find him a foster home with a friendly dog sibling to play with and learn the ropes from! Robinson is still working through crate training and spending time alone, and will do a terrific job learning with someone home often or has a schedule that allows him plenty of company and time to train. He truly is the kind of pup that will light up anyone's world! Robinson is fully vaccinated and neutered.
