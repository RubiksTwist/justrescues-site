Melody is a very fearful and shut-down dog who came from the same home as Marilyn, where she lived with many other animals and may not have received adequate socialization. Since arriving in our care, Melody has been extremely overwhelmed by her surroundings and spends most of her time barely moving while in her kennel. At this time, she is too fearful for us to safely bring her out for socialization, so we have had limited opportunity to assess her personality and behavior outside of her kennel. Melody will need a patient and experienced person who understands fearful dogs and is prepared to give her significant time, space, and support as she adjusts. A quiet, predictable home and a very gradual approach to handling and new experiences will be important for helping Melody feel safe and begin to build confidence.
