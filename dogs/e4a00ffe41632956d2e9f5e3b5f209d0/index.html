A volunteer writes:
A Good Samaritan brought very unkempt Carpenter to us after finding him in the street, limping and bleeding from the mouth. Poor Carpenter was underweight, dehydrated and had painful sores on his pads and in his mouth. What could have happened to him ? Hungry, he might have foraged in an area contaminated with a caustic substance. After five days at the care center, Carpenter feels much better, is eating and up and about. He is a most welcoming kitty sooo happy to see a visitor. He leans into pets, , drops on his kennel floor, head bunts and purrs galore. Carpenter is really a sweet and nice cat who by the way, aced his behavior evaluation. . Now, time to find him a home, so ask about Carpenter, meet the friendly chap, fall in love and take him home!
