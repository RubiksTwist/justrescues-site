Description provided by Rescue City

Reese is a 6-7 year old Poodle mix and a textbook lap dog, she just happens to weigh 62 pounds! If there’s a lap available, she’ll happily climb into it, and if there’s even the tiniest bit of space next to you, she’ll find a way to squeeze herself in.

Reese came to us with several other Poodle mixes in desperate need of grooming, and years of matting meant most of her coat had to go. Her curls are already starting their comeback, but Reese’s best feature is easily her personality. She is gentle, affectionate, social and completely devoted to her people. A good cuddle can be even more rewarding to her than a treat, and wherever her people are, Reese would like to be there too.

She has moderate energy and loves going for walks, sniffing, exploring and meeting new people. She is curious and friendly with other dogs she encounters and has been calm and friendly around children. Reese appears to be fully house trained and goes outside for all of her bathroom needs. She is generally good on leash, knows “sit” and “hand,” and her playful side is starting to emerge as she discovers toys and simple toss games. Reese is still working on crate training and becoming more comfortable when left alone.

Her ideal home is a loving household that wants a true companion to be part of everyday life! She is very eager to be someone’s devoted 62 pound shadow.
