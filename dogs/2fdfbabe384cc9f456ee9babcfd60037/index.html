Meet Metzy.

Some dogs arrive with a story written all over them.

When Metzy was found as a stray, she weighed just 32 pounds; she was heartbreakingly underweight, and her little bowlegged stance suggests she may have spent years without the nutrition and care every dog deserves. We may never know exactly what she has been through, but it's clear that life hasn't always been kind to her.

The remarkable part? Metzy decided to be kind anyway.

Despite everything, she greets people with a wagging tail, a gentle heart, and so much love to give. She doesn't hold grudges. She simply wants to be close to her people, soak up affection, and finally experience what it feels like to be cherished.

Metzy is the kind of dog who reminds you that resilience is real. Every meal, every soft bed, every kind touch is something she embraces with quiet gratitude. She deserves a family that will continue showing her the love she's always deserved.

If you've been looking for a loyal companion with a heart bigger than her past, Metzy is waiting. Help give this incredible girl the happy ending she's been missing all these years.

All dogs will be altered, microchipped, and receive age-appropriate vaccines prior to adoption.
