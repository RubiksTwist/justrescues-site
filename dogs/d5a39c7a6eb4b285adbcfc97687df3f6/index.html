Meet Mermaid!

Mermaid was found wandering the Coney Island boardwalk, and now this bouncy girl is ready to make some waves of her own! She's a young, social pup who eagerly approaches new people with a wiggly body and plenty of enthusiasm. She's definitely got a big personality, and with the right guidance, this boardwalk beauty could be swimming straight into your heart.

My Ideal Home

Mermaid would love an active home that can give her plenty of exercise, enrichment, and positive training. She's still learning that not every exciting moment requires jumping up or grabbing with her mouth, so she'll need someone who can help her practice calm, polite ways to say hello. With consistency and patience, Mermaid can learn to keep all four paws on the floor and show off her best self.

Good with Other Pets?

Mermaid's history with other animals is unknown, so her new family should take introductions slowly and thoughtfully. She'll need calm, supervised introductions with any resident dogs after she's had time to settle into her new home.

Good with Kids?

Mermaid would do best in a home without young children under 5. She's very enthusiastic and persistently jumps up when excited, so older children who can follow guidance and give her space may be a better fit.

Things to Know

Mermaid is an energetic girl who zigzags on leash and can get extra bouncy when she's excited. She also mouths hands and fingers and takes treats with a hard mouth, so she'll benefit from force-free, reward-based training that teaches gentle treat taking, impulse control, and alternative behaviors. Give her the right guidance and this Mermaid will be ready to make a splash in her forever home!
