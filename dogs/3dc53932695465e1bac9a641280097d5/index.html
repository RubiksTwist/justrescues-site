Description provided by Animal Care Centers of NYC

Ghost arrived at MACC as an owner surrender. His previous owner describes him as affectionate and independent. While in care, Ghost has been observed to be highly fearful. Noted to show whale eye and growl when handled. During his assessment, he did not allow handling and would freeze when handlers attempted to interact with him. Due to the detailed behavior, we recommend an experienced adult only home.

A staff member writes: Ghost is a sweet, gentle pup who loves spending time with people he knows and trusts. He greets familiar faces with a wagging tail, enjoys pets and treats, and is always ready for a chance to stretch his legs. Outside, he walks nicely on leash with only mild pulling and loves to zoom around the yard before checking in with his handlers. Ghost can be a little sensitive to sudden noises or fast movements, so he'll thrive with a patient adopter who can help him continue building confidence. He's also participated in dog introductions and playgroups, making him a great candidate for slow introductions to canine friends. If you're looking for a kind-hearted companion to grow with, Ghost may be your perfect match.
