Description provided by Waldo's Rescue Pen

This is Lucy! She is the most loving, fun, and adaptable little lady that we have ever fostered. From the moment she arrived, she snuggled up to us, played with our resident dogs, and explored her new home and neighborhood with unwavering confidence.

Personality and Characteristics:

Lucy is undeniably adorable. Her exuberant wagging tail and gentle kisses melt our hearts. On her first night home, she nuzzled her way under my blanket and curled up on my lap for cuddles. Lucy is an affable and confident pup who effortlessly follows our daily routines, whether they’re familiar or new. She greets dogs and people with boundless optimism and without any discrimination. She even loves me and my husband equally! Lucy is a playful pup who enjoys going on walks, but she’s also content to stay home for hours without any issue. We’ve been amazed to see how Lucy embraces every moment like a dream dog!

Sociability with people and dogs: Great!

Lucy has met a diverse group of adults, children, and dogs, and she’s done great with them all. Whenever someone greets her, she responds with a wagging tail and friendly little licks. Her interactions with dogs are also balanced and positive. Lucy is quite comfortable with dogs we encounter during walks. She politely walks past them when necessary, and when they stop to say hello, she greets them politely regardless of their size. However, she often lets new dogs know that she is the boss by making herself as adorably tall as possible.

When we first brought Lucy home, she introduced herself to our resident dogs as the new pack leader. After some playful greetings, they tried different status configurations, and the group quickly settled into a new hierarchy without any issues. Now, they all cuddle together, feeling secure and affectionate towards their tiny pack member.

Lucy is highly trainable when we correct her during interactions. For instance, when she made a few ill-placed territorial growls at our resident dog and a family member, she promptly submitted and moved on. Lucy is a bit wary when a new person approaches us or our dogs at home. However, she was easily reassured that she didn’t need to guard us when we let her know that she shouldn’t police the situations. I think it would be good for her future adopter to be proactive in preventing family resource guarding.

Potty Training:

Lucy has made excellent progress. For the first two days, she had zero accidents inside, which is a remarkable sign for becoming an a very well potty trained dog once fully settled. She has had a few minor accidents, but overall she shows a lot of potential. She enthusiastically goes potty outside without any hesitation or nerves, even in the bustling city. She also uses pee pads when needed. We have also been very impressed with her ability to comfortably sleep in for up to 9 hours before requesting a walk!

Crate training:

Lucy enjoys our crate, but we have not yet locked her in it. Currently, she sleeps in our bed and absolutely loves being curled up under the blanket with us.

Keywords for Lucy:

CUTE, secure, loving, smart, fun, and highly adaptable to change.

Energy levels: 5 (1= calm, 10= Lucy is a high-energy dog.)

She is a 1 when we’re relaxing on the sofa and a 9 when it’s time to play or go for a walk.

Barker? Or Quiet Pup?: Normal

She’ll give some warning-bark/growls at things she thinks are questionable, but it feels more like communication than anxiety or defensiveness. She’s been silent when home alone.

Walking Manners:

Lucy is an enthusiastic walker and gets very excited to go on a walk when we get the leash. She’s pretty brave in the city and walks at a spunky little trot. She does pull when she walks, so that needs training, but it’s pretty manageable.

Appetite: Excellent.

She arrived underweight but eats great.

Favorite Things:

She adores people and hanging out. While she loves to get attention, and will happily play or curl up with you whenever possible, she is also great at being independent. She likes beef cheek rolls and seems interested in squeaky toys.

Commands/Tricks:

Lucy is excellent at recall. She’s the first dog we’ve had who always comes when called. It’s great! She’s also pretty good with leave it, back, stay and other directional commands. She is a genius on walks at stepping over the leash to keep it from tangling with her legs.
