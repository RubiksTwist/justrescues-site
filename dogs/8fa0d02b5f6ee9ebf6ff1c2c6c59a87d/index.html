Say hello to Cheese - because every Mac needs his Cheese!

Meet Cheese, the other half of the most delicious sibling duo around. His name may have come from everyone’s favorite comfort food, but there’s nothing ordinary about this little guy. He was brought in with his brother by local animal control after no one claimed him - and he's hoping you'll be his forever person.

Cheese is a sweet little pup - he loves being part of the action and is curious about the world around him, while still having that soft, lovable puppy charm that makes you want to scoop him right up. Give him a little time, some kindness, and a person he can trust, and Cheese will happily show you just how much love he has to give.

Cheese is ready to be someone’s favorite little slice of happiness - because every family could use a little more cheese in their life.
