Meet Remi | Apply Here

Are you an active adventurer ready to take on whatever life throws at you, as soon as it hits you? Do you like jumping in ponds, climbing steep hiking paths, or galloping through fields of wildflowers? Remi and you will be two peas in a pod then!

Remi is a happy go lucky 2 year old mix-breed and weighs 25lbs (and we can confirm that’s all muscle).Originally pulled from the local shelter system, Remi has come leaps and bounds (literally) from where she started.

Though we had a DNA test done, we’re still sure this girl is part frog despite it not showing up. The way she leaps into open water like a graceful toad is Olympic level and her doggy paddle is as cute as it is efficient. She’s totally happy to go wherever you want to go, provided you bring her favorite ball!

This go-getter is available through our FTA VIP program~ VIP pups may need a little extra care in placement to make sure they’ll truly thrive, so with this potential adopters get a 30 day foster trial period before signing the official adoption agreement! For 30 days we’ll support you with food, supplies, and even a training session to make sure everyone is set up with the best for success.

Does all of this sound like your dream dog? Submit your application! We can not WAIT to meet you and get this fun loving gal into her forever home.

Remi’s adoption fee is $300. To learn more about Remi's rescue story, click here.
