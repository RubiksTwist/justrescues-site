A volunteer writes:
Eleven month old Poppy Seed was found abandoned in a parking lot, in a carrier hidden behind a car....Poor little girl. Nothing more is known about her...At the care center, no wonder Poppy Seed is soo subdued, making herself as small as a poppy seed, hoping that she will be invisible....She makes pitiful and barely audible sounds as I talk to her and try to pet her..She leans away, shrinks in place and waits motion less than I "walk" away...Being in a shelter does not help of course, even that we are trying our best to make Poppy Seed feel safe and loved...It would be wonderful for Poppy seed to go to a loving home with an experienced and patient owner who could make her feel safe, loved and able to grow into a well balanced and healthy pet. Poppy Seed is waiting for her hero at the Manhattan Care Center!
