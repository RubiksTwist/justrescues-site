Description provided by PupStarz Rescue

Meet Kids Tablet (a 3 months old 18lb as of 11/14) , a sweet, playful and curious girl with lots of love to give! Kids Tablet is happy to do just about anything with her people. She loves cuddles, pets, toys and treats, and she always enjoys getting outside. She has plenty of energy and a playful spirit, so she’ll keep you entertained and then happily snuggle up with you.

Kids Tablet gets along with other dogs and loves to play with them. She’s also great with kids and adults of all ages, though she hasn’t been tested with cats. She’s house trained, walks well on a leash, doesn’t shed, rarely barks, and has no resource guarding or bad habits. She knows “sit” and “no,” and her foster is continuing to work on her training. She isn’t crate trained, and she hasn’t ridden in a car yet.

Kids Tablet would be a wonderful fit for an active, loving family, maybe one with a playful dog for her to pal around with. This lovable girl is ready to bring lots of joy to her forever home!
