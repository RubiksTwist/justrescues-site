Description provided by Waldo's Rescue Pen

Poker Face (aka “Roo”) is a super friendly, lovable pup who loves meeting everyone on her walks. She’s a little shadow at home and likes to follow you from room to room, curl up next to you, and soak up all the belly rubs she can get. She has lots of fun puppy energy, but also sleeps well and is doing great with crate training. She walks nicely on leash and is doing really well with learning basic commands and potty training. Roo would be a great fit for someone looking for a sweet, social, affectionate young dog who loves being close to her people and is eager to learn.
