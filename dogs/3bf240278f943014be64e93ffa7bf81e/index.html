Meet Mikey!

Mikey is a 10 month old 12 lb sweetheart who loves cheese, affection, and following you around the house. No task is complete without Mikey's supervision!

Mikey is potty trained and taking to his crate well. He's settled into his foster home quickly and loves playing with his foster big brother. When he's not playing, Mikey wants to be near you. Whether he's on your lap, sitting next to you on the couch, or licking your toes, Mikey loves affection. And when he's scared, the only place he wants to be is in your arms.

Mikey wakes up every day happy. When he's excited, he bunny hops around! He's still working on "down" and "sit" but his foster family learned quickly that Mikey will do just about anything for a piece of American cheese.

Mikey is great a leash, and is curious and eager to explore the neighborhood. Mikey is very good at letting you know when he has energy (or potty) to let out. Whether it's licking your palms or gentle finger nibbles, Mikey will let you know that he's got to go.

Mikey's confidence builds every day, and he's quickly found his voice. This little boy takes his big boy protector job very seriously. He will most certainly let you know when danger is afoot (and yes your neighbors bringing in their groceries counts as danger).

Mikey is eager and ready for his forever home, could that be you?
