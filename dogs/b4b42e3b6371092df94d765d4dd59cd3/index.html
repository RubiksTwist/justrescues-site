Hi, I’m Tink. I’m a tiny, affectionate sweetheart with a curious little spark that comes out as soon as I feel safe. I’m still learning the world — some things are new, some things make me pause — but I adapt quickly. New places, new routines, new adventures… I take them all in gently, and before you know it, I’m moving through them like I’ve been there forever.
I’m playful and cuddly, the kind of girl who lights up when my people give me attention. I love leaning in close, soaking up affection, and trotting around to see what you’re up to. But when you’re busy, I’m perfectly content doing my own thing — playing with my toys, curling up for a nap, or just quietly watching life go by. I don’t need constant entertainment; I just like knowing you’re near.
I don’t ask for much: a safe place, kind humans, and the chance to keep discovering life one small, brave step at a time. I’m easy, sweet, and quietly charming — a little dog who’s ready to blossom in a home of my own.
If you’re looking for a gentle companion with a soft heart, a playful streak, and a curious soul, I’d really love to meet you. 💛
